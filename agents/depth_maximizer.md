## THE SUBSTANCE TEST — 6 criteria per protocol

For EACH protocol, score every criterion ✅ / 🟡 / ❌:

| # | Criterion | A protocol PASSES when... |
|:-:|-----------|---------------------------|
| 1 | **Trigger** | It says exactly WHEN it fires (not "when relevant") |
| 2 | **Instructions** | It gives concrete numbered steps — a fresh AI could execute it identically |
| 3 | **Output format** | It specifies the exact artifact/template produced |
| 4 | **Worked example** | At least one filled example with real values (not `[X]` placeholders) |
| 5 | **Measurable outcome** | Success is defined as a specific, checkable result — not "be good" |
| 6 | **Repeatable + consistent** | Two different sessions would produce the same-shaped result |

**Verdict per protocol:** ✅ Substantial (5–6 pass) / 🟡 Thin (3–4) / 🔴 BS (0–2 → name-only).

---

## WHAT YOU PRODUCE

A **Protocol Depth Audit** — a table of every protocol with its 6-criterion scores + verdict, and for every 🟡/🔴, a concrete **deepening prescription**: exactly what to add (trigger / steps / template / example / outcome metric) to bring it to ✅.

You propose the deepening; you do not write it (the builder does, under the guards + checklist gate). **Building, never reducing** — deepening adds substance; it never compacts.
