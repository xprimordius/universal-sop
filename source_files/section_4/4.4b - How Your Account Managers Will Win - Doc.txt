How Your Account Managers Will Win
Get every one of your Account Managers to go through this mental model excercise…
Imagine… you’ve lost all your clients but one. The gods of advertising ban us from ever receiving another client from our marketing efforts. 

The only way you can feed your family and feed the rest of the AMs on the team is from the one client you have.

In other words, all new clients must be referred from this one client - this is the only way you can get paid.

How would you treat this client? What would you do to make their experience so valuable that they would send all their friends and peers? What kind of results would they need to get? How would their onboarding experience be?

Write it out. Really think about it. Our business depends it. Your family depends on it.

Now… treat every single client you work with in this way and you’ll never ever not make at least $10k/month (minimum) as an AM…
