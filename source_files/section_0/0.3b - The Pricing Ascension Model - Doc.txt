Pricing Ascension Model


There’s a Pricing Model we use that took us from $100k - $200k/month to $1M/month+ in NEW cash collected and more importantly, many of our clients have used it to go from $10k - $20k/month to $100k/month and hundreds have used it to go from 0 with no experience in business or in their respective niches and immeidately start closing clients on Day 1 and grow quickly to $10k/month+.


In other words, no matter what stage your business is in (especially if you’re a complete beginner), the pricing model I’m about to show you is the easiest way to sign a high velocity of clients with extremely profitable economics.


Let me first tell you a few of the problems with the usual “Pricing Structure”:


Most people slap a high-ticket price onto their product or service ($5k, $10k, $15k+) and expect a complete stranger to pay up without any objections.


1. They have many objections
2. They are probably speaking with 5-10 of your competitors who all offer something very similar for far less (and in the prospect's eyes, they see you as exactly the same)
3. You need extreme proof and justification as to why you're charging that much (which is hard to show when you’re a beginner with no case studies)
4. IF you close them, their expectations are SO high that it’s almost impossible to make them happy.
5. IF you close them, because there’s a lack of trust (given they’ve only known you a few days), there is no “leeway” or “forgiveness” if something goes wrong in your fulfilment.


I could go on and on…


The point is, trying to charge a high upfront fee to a cold prospect who has come through your ads is NOT the way to scale profitably on cold traffic (SCIO DFY Example // Opt Digital Example).


Ascension Pricing Model
The key to scaling is through what I call the “Ascension Pricing Model”.


Imagine a world where you could “manufacture referrals”? i.e., you could click your fingers and generate instant referrals who already loved you, already knew what you do works, and were immediately willing to pay you whatever price you tell them ($5k, $10k, $15k+)?


That’s exactly what the Ascension Pricing Model via ads does.


Let me explain:

Instead of trying to pitch a high upfront cost, you want to decrease the barrier to entry and offer a “trial-like-pricing-structure” for 2 - 4 weeks so that your entry point becomes almost an impulse buy.

You want it to be high enough to recoup your adspend (so you still get the effects of Client Financed Acquisition) but low enough that the client doesn’t need to think twice about purchasing.


Then, after you’ve completed a 2 - 4 week trial, you then ascend them into a high-ticket-backend offer ($10k+) or a higher ticket MRR offer ($2k - $5k/mo+).


What this does (on the front end) is:


1. Makes it MUCH easier to sell (your sales skills don’t need to be as expert to sell a $1k offer as they do to sell a $10k offer).
2. It immediately positions you as different from everyone else your prospects have spoken to (since no one else is doing this).
3. Builds immediate trust since you’re showing that you’re happy for them to trial your services before committing to the full package
4. Makes you WAY more money because:
   1. You close WAY more clients (given more people can buy, and it’s easier to sell).
   2. By the time you ask for the “high-ticket” sale, the client will already love you, highly trust you, and they will know your service works since they’ve seen it with their own eyes (how much easier do you think it would be to sell that kind of client on a high-ticket offer vs someone who’s never seen you before?)
Here’s The Math:


Let’s assume: 


* $10k Adspend
* $300 Cost Per Qualified Booked Call (CPQBC)
   * 33 sales calls ($10,000 / $300)
* 70% Show Rate
   * 23 LIVE calls (33 * 70%)


One-Time-Offer Example


Standard Pricing Model ($5k Offer):


* 23 LIVE Calls
* 20% Close Rate
   * Note: This is an extremely good close rate and very difficult to achieve if you’re a beginner, but for the example, let’s assume you’re a very good salesperson…
* 4.6 New Clients


4.6 New Clients @ $5k Price Point = $23k Cash Collected


2.3x Cash ROAS (for every $1 you spend on ads, you’re making $2.30 back)




Acenscion Pricing Model ($500 2-week Trial → $7.5k Offer):


* 23 LIVE Calls
* 50% Close Rate
   * Note: This is very conservative given the extremely cheap front-end price point.
* 11.5 New Clients


11.5 New Clients @ $500 Price Point = $5,750 Cash Collected (for only 2 weeks' worth of work).


But then…


After the 2 weeks, we will show you how to “Ascend” those clients into, in this example, a $7.5k “Backend” offer…


On average, across all our clients, we see a 50% Ascension Rate from the Trial into the higher-ticket offer…


11 New Clients w/ 50% Ascension Rate = 5.5 High-Ticket Clients @ $7.5k


= $41,250


TOTAL Cash Collected = $47,000 ($5,750 + $41,250)


So from the same $10k adspend, using the Ascension Pricing Model, you will collect $47k as opposed to $23k, which means for every $1 spent you will collect $4.70 back… pretty good.


And remember:

* These are MUCH easier sales to make (making it more enjoyable).
* You can start closing clients much faster (because you don’t need as many skills or “justification” / “proof”).
* You’re working with clients who inherently trust you, rather than them constantly doubting you.




Quick Retainer Example




Standard Pricing Model ($3k/Month Offer):


* 23 LIVE Calls
* 15% Close Rate
   * Note: This will naturally have a lower close rate (assuming the same skill set) given it’s a far higher commitment.
* 3.45 New Clients


3.45 New Clients @ $3k/mo Price Point = $10.3k in new MRR


1.03x Cash ROAS (for every $1 you spend on ads, you’re making $1.03 back before the recurring kicks in)


Remember: 


Given these prospects are much colder and have less trust in you being able to deliver, they must see an ROI on their investment with you within their first month; otherwise, they will cancel, and you’ll receive no recurring revenue.


Although adding $10k/month in new recurring revenue sounds great, if you also churn $10k/month in revenue, you stagnate. When working with clients who don’t inherently trust you, it’s much more difficult to retain them past the first 1-2 months (which puts a massive strain on your fulfillment since you’re forced to deliver massive results ASAP, otherwise they will churn).


Acenscion Pricing Model ($500 2-week Trial → $3k/Month Offer):


* 23 LIVE Calls
* 50% Close Rate
   * Note: The close rate here will stay the same (although it decreased when switching to a retainer structure in the standard pricing model) with the APM given the front-end offer/commitment is still a $500 2-week trial. 
* 11.5 New Clients


11.5 New Clients @ $500 Price Point = $5,750 Cash Collected (for only 2 weeks' worth of work).


Let’s assume only a 40% Ascenion Rate (given the commitment is slightly higher):


= 4.6 Ascended clients (11.5 * 40%) @ $3k/month = $13,800 / month in new recurring revenue


TOTAL Cash Collected = $19,550 ($5,750 + $13,800 in recurring).


So again, from the same $10k adspend, using the Ascension Pricing Model, you will collect $19.5k as opposed to only $10k which means for every $1 spent, you will collect $1.95 back BEFORE any recurring revenue kicks in. Remember, this is what you collect “on Day 1”.


Plus, all your recurring clients, with this model, will already love and trust you by the time they “Ascend” to your $3k/month retainer offer which means their retention rate will be significantly longer, which means you not only make more money on the front-end but also much more money on the “backend” (and you have way more fun while doing so)... not bad ;)

How Do You Know Whether To Do a 2-Week or 4-Week Trial?
It’s very simple.


If you think you can deploy your product or service and earn the trust of your clients (meaning, prove your service works) within 2-weeks, then you should do 2-weeks. This is the holy grail as the commitment and price point is much lower, which means… You guessed it… more clients and more profit.


However, if you can’t achieve this in 2-weeks, then there are zero issues with extending it to 4-weeks in order to give yourself more time to prove what you do works.
