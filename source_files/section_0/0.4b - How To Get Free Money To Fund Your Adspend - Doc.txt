1. Wrap up your offer with an OTO (One-Time-Offer):


All this requires you to do is add a discount (through a price increase, not a % off), add an end/closing date, and add limited spots.


Here’s an example:


“I’m bringing on 5 clients to (benefit #1, benefit #2, and benefit #3) and I will GUARANTEE you (Offer) - this will be closing on (date) or once the 5 available spots are filled (whichever comes first)... and when doors close, our price will be increasing by $X.”


The benefits should speak to EXACTLY what your ideal clients want.


For example, if you have a lead gen agency, an easy benefit you can add (that I guarantee your ideal client wants) is that you’ll call and pre-qualify every lead before booking them in for an appointment.


Ask yourself: “If I could click my fingers and give my clients anything they wanted, what would they ask for?” 


Usually, when you ask yourself this question (without any bias), it’s extremely clear what you should offer as deliverables/benefits.
