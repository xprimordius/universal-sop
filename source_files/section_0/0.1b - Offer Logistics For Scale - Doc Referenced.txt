Here’s Our Philosophy/Process On Creating Offers

Step 1: Focusing The Ads On Your Most Ideal Top Of The Market Clients.


The reason why is because if you speak to the top, you naturally get the top and everyone below. If you speak to the bottom, you only get the bottom:
   *   

This concept also ties into your offer.


We don’t want to focus your offer around what you can deliver to the whole market but rather what is the outcome (result) you can achieve for your most ideal, qualified, high-end client who has every resource you require.


Often times these resources refer to:
   * Adspend
   * Location size (if local)
   * Team size
   * Call leads immediately
   * Do everything you require them to do
   * Make any changes you recommend/require
   * Get assets/changes/access/creatives, etc turned around ASAP (not weeks or months)
   * Search volume (if Google Ads)
   * Traffic volume onto the website
   * x%+ conversion rate
   * You must spend at least $X on these creatives/funnels/chatbots, etc


Literally any requirement you need, we assume in your ads they have them all and THAT is who we create the offer and the outcome for NOT people who have less adspend or don’t call leads immediately, etc. 


This is how you will always get the highest volume + quality at the cheapest cost through your ads at the highest scale.


Hence why this is the number one key to unlocking massive scale for your business.


And most likely the reason you’re a client right now is because you saw one of our ads with a big claim (that’s only for people who have a certain amount of adspend) and what you ultimately bought is a more tailored solution that fits exactly what you need (even though it’s different from what you saw in the ad).


90% of people who buy our offer do not buy the offer they saw in the ad. They buy our downsell.
Step 2: Downselling The Guarantee


The question I know you’re asking right now is:


“What if they don’t have (requirements you need)? I can only achieve that outcome (referring to the offer) if they have (requirements).”


This is where the idea of “downselling the guarantee” comes into play.


Before I explain this concept, you might be thinking “why would you still try to sell someone who isn’t a perfect, top of the market fit?”


Well here’s the thing, if for example your ideal client is someone making $100k/m… but you have case studies of clients at $500k/m then we’re always going to focus the offer around the people at $500k/m (top of the market)…


Naturally, this offer will attract people at 200k/m or 100k/m, so in this instance they’re still a qualified client which you would still want to sell (just not on the offer they saw in the ad as that’s centered around people at $500k/m - so you would downsell them)...


But also the offer in this example would attract people at $50k/m or $80k/m, etc and while this may be a complete no go for you, that’s fine… but for most offers and what I lead my sales and marketing team on (internally) is the following:


We should be closing anyone who:
   * Is in our niche
   * Has the problem we solve
   * Has the money to pay us


Why do we have this mindset even though ideally we only want to work with people above $25k/m or so?


Simple. At the point of recording this, our immediate marketing goal is to get to $10k/day spend and the only way we can get there is to close anyone who is in our niche and is closable and that we can 100% help (even if they’re not at our ideal $25k+/month revenue range).


However, this completely depends on how aggressive you want to be. You could set the “bottom” of your market (refer to image above) as your qualified client and the top would be your hyper qualified client (use example from above) and this would scale well to $2-3k/day adspend…


Or you could broaden your market to essentially be anyone who has the problem you solve and has the money to pay you who’s also in your niche.


We can of course dive deeper into this on your onboarding call.


Either way, you will still have the following happen where a qualified prospect will see an ad that has a great offer so they book a call and show up… but on the call, they realize they don’t have the resources required to be able to receive the guarantee/outcome they saw in the ad because they don’t fit that “top of the triangle” criteria…


But, they do have the problem you solve, they do have the funds to pay you, they are in your niche and they are at a level in which you can help (going back to the $100k vs $500k/m example)…


This is where we Downsell The Guarantee.


Here’s the “how to”:


To effectively downsell a guarantee and get every prospect to say yes to the downsell, you need 2 things:

   1. A logical explanation as to why they’re not a good fit for the main guarantee (the one they saw in the ad)...
   1. “Yeah that makes sense”
   2. Then you downsell to something the prospect still wants…
   1. If you downsell to no offer or guarantee (as an example) no one will ever close.


For example:


   * SCIO:
   * Ad says 90 qualified sales calls per month or you don’t pay…
   * 90% of our clients buy our downsell guarantee which is 50 qualified booked calls in 90 days…
   * Logical explanation:
   * “As you know, guarantees are always based on worst case scenario, and so in this case, our absolute worst-case scenario would be a $200 cost per booked call… and so to guarantee you 90 qualified calls per month, or in other words, 3 qualified calls per day, we require a $600/day ad spend. Now I know you mentioned your ad budget wasn’t at $18k/month and so here’s what we can do… we can guarantee you 50 qualified sales calls in 90 days which only requires a $100/day ad spend.”
   * If you run ads then usually “adspend” is your logical explanation for why they aren’t a good fit for the main offer, however, if you have a different service that isn’t ads, ask yourself:
   * “What’s the thing that if they did have it, they would be eligible for the offer?” 
   * Inverse and that becomes your logical explanation.
   * Example: One of our clients had a requirement of needing a 2M population service area in order for him to deliver the results mentioned in his ads. So his logical reasoning became:
   * In order for us to do XYZ (offer stated in ad), we require you to be servicing a population area of at least 2M, now given you’re in X area with only 700,000 people in it, here’s what we can do (insert your downsell that they still find desirable and WANT which you can deliver given their resources).
   * We can help you with this logical explanation + downsell offer on your onboarding call, I just want you thinking about it for now.
Step 3: Determining The Terms
Remember: you are in complete control over the terms you set for your guarantees. If you have a bigger guarantee that is more difficult for you to deliver then simply increase your price on that particular offer. 


Here’s how we structure it (again, this is what took us from $30k/m to $700k/m in 18 months and is what helps us deliver the best client results in our industry).


ScaleClients.io:
   * The guarantee almost all (90%) of our clients buy is the 50 qualified calls booked in 90 days offer. However, if we ran this guarantee in our marketing (ads) it wouldn’t generate any calls because 1. It’s not desirable and 2. It’s completely saturated.
   * So instead, we run the 15 clients/month guaranteed offer.
   * However, this is a lot more time intensive and has a lot more requirements in order for us to deliver it. So here are the terms we set:
   * We charge $40k for 4 months and…
   * The client must have at least $30k - $50k/month in adspend for the full 3 months - depending on the price of their offer ($90k - $150k in total adspend)
   * Or, they can take our downsell - 50 qualified calls in 90 days - (a result they still want) which has the following terms:
   * We charge $17.5k for 4 months and…
   * They can get started with $100/day in adspend and we can scale spend up or down whenever they would like...
   * See how based on the terms we set, most clients take our downsell offer.


Keep in mind, at no point do we ever bait and switch the prospect. The offer they saw in the ad, they can 100% buy. They just need to be able to meet the terms we set.


You never want to lie in your ads and/or bait and switch. This will come back to bite you. I see so many of our competitors trying to copy us but they don’t understand this backend structure so they end up outright lying and they get taken down and never build a real, long lasting business that scales.
Step 4: Conditional Refund Guarantee Stipulations
Ask yourself: “In order for a client to be able to generate (outcome) what exactly do they need/need to do?”


Or, you can look at your biggest clients who have gotten the biggest results with you, and retrace exactly what they did to get those results.


In either case, whatever your clients need to do to get the result, that’s exactly what you need to put in your contract stipulations in order for your clients to be eligible for the refund.


These stipulations can be as specific as:
   * You must call all leads within the first 3 hours of them opting in
   * You must spend (minimum) $500/day consecutively for 90 days
   * You must send through a call recording of every sales call you take
   * You must implement on every ammendment/change we say
   * At the end of each day you need to send us a full breakdown on every lead you spoke with and exactly what the outcome of the call/conversation was (this must be done at the end of every day consecutively NOT at the end of weeks or months)
   * You must return all new creatives to us within 7 days of us sending them over to you


Again, remember, you are in complete control over the stipulations you set. You can put any requirements you need into your contract.
