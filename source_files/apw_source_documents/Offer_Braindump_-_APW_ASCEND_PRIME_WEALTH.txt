Main overview
   * 1) establish fundable credit profile + fundable business entity 
   * 2) create funding sequence via compatible banks & where they pull from
   * 3) begin opening up accounts and developing banking relationships
   * 4) apply and await results - adjust based on results / feedback
5) continue to complete the funding round wave across the bureaus if successful, if not, request for consultation, reconsideration line, etc depending on the result in writing
   * 6) remove hard inquiries from the personal side, as the credit is for the entity which is a separate person, and not the natural individual person
   * 7) Prepare for next round / wave(s) - repeat 1-6
   * 8) With newly acquired funding via credit cards, loans, and lines of credit; decide how you would like to best make the most of the promotional periods such as 0%, and leverage that to your success. Liquidation of credit into cash is also available as an option
   * 9) ensure to budget out 1-3% per month to put towards servicing the debt (minimum payments) - recommended 6-12 months dedicated in a SEPARATE account to pay  the minimums for the 6-12 months to remain in good standing and be eligible for future credit limit increases & additional financial products
