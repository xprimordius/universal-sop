**What I wrongly made it:** an emoji→brief→detail *table style* (a visual cosmetic).

**What it actually is** — Alan, verbatim, PROMPT 5 (`cache/legacy/USER_PROMPTS_VERBATIM.md` lines 45-47):

> *"Maybe for ease for this prime first layer to have priority details that CANNOT be missed; for 100% accuracy and consistency of intended outputs always. While also containing **macro directional caches / pointers to the details of all of the heavier things; that further break down into more cached reference points where relevant**; But this way we are able to maintain coherence on an operational level; before getting completely lost with everything that needs to be expanded upon and verified in the deeper work."*

**Door Scan is a CACHING / REFERENCING ARCHITECTURE — the hallway of doors:**

| Concept | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| 🚪 The hallway | The operational surface — briefs + pointers you scan to know *what is what* |
| 🚪 Each door | A cache of deeper data for one protocol/operation/topic |
| 🔓 Opening a door | Loading the deeper cached detail — **only when relevant** |
| 💾 The point | **Stop approximating.** Instead of guessing, open the right door (read the right cache) for the precise detail. Save tokens by *not loading* irrelevant depth — never by *deleting* it. |

**This is the literal antidote to the approximation problem.** The system is dense; Door Scan makes density navigable by caching depth behind doors and scanning briefs in the hallway. It maps directly to the Prime's **SOP HIERARCHY** (Layer 0 priority details ← briefs; Layer 1/2 detailed SOPs ← behind the doors) and the **cache architecture**.

**Restoration:** Door Scan becomes a real referencing mechanism + agent (see Phase 3), not a table style. Depth is preserved in caches; the hallway holds pointers.
