# 03 · ASTROCARTOGRAPHY · Full Line Analysis

## Methodology (Computed From First Principles)

ACG converts natal celestial coordinates to terrestrial longitudes using:

| Step | Formula |
|---|---|
| 1. Right Ascension from ecliptic longitude | RA = atan2(sin λ · cos ε, cos λ), where ε = 23.4393° (1995 obliquity) |
| 2. Declination from ecliptic longitude | Dec = asin(sin ε · sin λ) |
| 3. MC line longitude | MC_lon = RA_planet − GMST (normalize to -180° to +180°) |
| 4. IC line longitude | IC_lon = MC_lon + 180° |
| 5. AC line at latitude φ | longitude = (RA − arccos(−tan φ · tan Dec)) − GMST |
| 6. DC line at latitude φ | longitude = (RA + arccos(−tan φ · tan Dec)) − GMST |

**GMST at birth (Greenwich Mean Sidereal Time):** 185.65°
