## Mayan / Tzolk'in Calculation

### Calculation method

Reference: December 21, 2012 = 4 Ahau = Kin 160 (classical Maya date, GMT correlation)

Days from July 25, 1995 to December 21, 2012:
- 17 full years × 365 = 6,205 days
- + 5 leap day adjustments (1996, 2000, 2004, 2008, 2012) = 6,210 days for year span
- + 149 days from Jul 25 to Dec 21 within 2012
- **Total: 6,359 days**

6,359 mod 260 = 119
