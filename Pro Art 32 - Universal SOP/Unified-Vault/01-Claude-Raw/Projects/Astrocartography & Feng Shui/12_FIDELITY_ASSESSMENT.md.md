## How to Use This Export with Maximum Lucidity

1. **For decisions involving Kua directions, Western planetary positions, ACG lines, Chinese zodiac year, Bazi pillars, and numerology core numbers:** Trust the values at face value. These are anchor-grade.

2. **For interpretive readings (what the Mars-ASC conjunction means, what Life Path 11 means, etc.):** Read as archetypal patterns, not literal predictions. Apply where they fit your lived experience.

3. **For Mayan, Celtic, Native American systems:** Treat as supplementary archetypal lenses. They add color where they converge with other systems; ignore where they conflict.

4. **For Forma Miami unit recommendations:** Treat the analysis as a starting framework. Compass-verify everything on-tour before signing any lease.

5. **For location decisions:** ACG math is anchor-grade; weighted scoring depends on your priorities (tax, network, climate, etc.). Adjust weights to fit your actual decision criteria.

6. **For Bazi useful gods (Water, Metal):** These are practitioner interpretations. Useful for understanding constitutional balance but not deterministic life rules.

7. **When this export disagrees with itself across systems (e.g., Bazi suggests Pacific NW vs ACG suggesting Florida):** Weigh by fidelity tier. Anchor-grade math (ACG line locations, Kua directions) wins over interpretive synthesis (Bazi regional element matching).
