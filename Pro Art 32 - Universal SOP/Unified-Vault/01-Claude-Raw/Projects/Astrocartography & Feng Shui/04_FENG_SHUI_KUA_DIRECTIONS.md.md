## Form School (Luan Tou 巒頭) Priorities

Classical doctrine ranks placement factors:

1. **Solid backing** (Black Tortoise / Xuan Wu 玄武) — most important
2. **Command position** (visible door, NOT directly aligned)
3. **Direction** (Kua alignment)

A directionally-perfect setup with weak backing UNDERPERFORMS a directionally-decent setup with solid backing.

### Bathroom Form rules
- Bathroom door NOT directly facing bedroom, kitchen, or front door
- Toilet not visible from bedroom or living area

**Cure for missing sectors:** Place tall plant or floor lamp at the corner to symbolically "complete" the sector.
