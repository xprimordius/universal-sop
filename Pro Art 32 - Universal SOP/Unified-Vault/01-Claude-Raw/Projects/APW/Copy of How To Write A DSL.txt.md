How To Write A DSL
Here’s My LIVE DSL That Is Currently Generating 250+ Qualified Appointments Per Month (And Closing 30-40 New Clients/m)
The Ultimate Goal
* Your DSL should create the following epiphanies in your prospect:
   * This is what I’ve been missing
   * This is what I need to get me to my goal
   * I realize why everything I’ve tried in the past has failed and why this is going to be different
   * I can see this is a NO BRAINER
   * This CLEARLY works
   * This is what I NEED
DSL Framework:
Market Call Out (0-5s):
* Call out your MOST ideal client (be specific):
   * Who they are SPECIFICALLY:
      * If you’re a realtor closing at least 1 deal per month on homes above $515k…
      * If you’re a coach or agency owner between $10k-$50k per month…
      * If you’re a {ideal client}...
   * Problems/desires/current pain:
      * If you cannot generate more than 30-50 calls per month for your DFY or coaching offer…
         * This is subtle. I use this a lot because it calls out my audience but also subtly qualifies them because I’m implying they are currently generating 30ish calls
      * If you want to go from 40% of your P&L going to marketing to only 20% whilst SCALING your ad spend, listen up // here’s what you need to know…
      * If you’re losing sales to common objections of “xyz”, listen up…
   * The more specific you can go here, the better. The better your DSL will perform and more importantly, the more qualified calls you’ll generate (especially when running ads)
State Offer + New Opportunity/Mechanism (5-15s):
* The offer needs to ONLY highlight the ONE most emotionally-compelling detail of your offer
   * This should speak to THE ONE BIG THING your market wants and NOTHING else
      * I can’t stress this enough. I don’t care if in your backend you help clients with more things. In your marketing, you must always speak to the one big thing they actually want.
         * Take us as an example, we help our clients with sales teams, offer creation // repositioning, recruitment for all departments, marketing, booking calls, outbound, leading teams, etc etc. But in our ads and DSLs? I don’t talk about ANY of that (not even the 1-1 coaching, slack channels, community calls, nothing). The only thing I speak to in our marketing is the DFY booked calls element to our offer. Why? Because that’s all my market wants. They need much more than this which is why we provide much more than this. But what they want is to just have a filled calendar. I can’t stress this enough.
* Once you’ve stated your offer, touch on the mechanism as to how you’re going to achieve that outcome.
   * IMPORTANT: only mention the mechanism if it’s truly unique and is something tangible and real (not an XYZ system). If it’s the latter, then rely on your offer to do the heavy lifting early on and only expose the mechanism later in the DSL.
      * Example: one of our clients offer is “We’ll guarantee to generate you an extra $7k/day in organic sales or you don’t pay”.
      * Her mechanism is “AI Chat Bot” or “Social DM Funnel”
Guarantee (15-20s):
* Right after stating the ONE BIG THING your market wants… eliminate the downside // answer the question of what will happen if they don’t achieve that outcome by providing a guarantee
   * “{One Big Thing} OR you don’t pay a cent”
   * “{One Big Thing} and if you don’t at LEAST {get the desired result} we’ll refund you in full, no questions asked”
   * “{One Big Thing} OR we’re legally obligated to refund what you paid to join // initially, in full, no questions asked”
   * “{One Big Thing} OR we’ll pay you $10k”
   * “{One Big Thing} OR we’ll BUY your company from you”


Combined example: Ecomm brands above $100k/month: We’ll guarantee to generate you an extra $7k/day in organic sales through installing an AI Chat Bot into your social DMs or you don’t pay

This First Section Of The DSL Should Immediately Answer These 2 Questions:


* Question 1: “How is this different from everything else I’ve seen?”
   * If what we present in these first 10-15 seconds is not perceived as new or novel, it’s useless.
      * When we see something new, we see it has a potential for rewarding us in some way. This potential that lies in new things motivates us to explore our environment for rewards (the DSL). The brain learns that the stimulus, once familiar, has no reward associated with it and so it loses its potential.
      * Only completely new objects activate the midbrain area and increase our levels of dopamine.
* Question 2: “What’s in it for me?”
   * On cold traffic, people are savages. They only care about themselves and no one else. Unless we answer this question immediately, we’ll lose the prospect.
   * Don’t forgot - people don’t have to read your shit. They’re not sat at home waiting for your ad or DSL to show up so they can read it. In fact, they’re looking for every reason to NOT watch your DSL.
      * If you don’t answer this question within seconds, you immediately give them that reason.
   * How you do this is by making an offer so good they can’t say no. In other words, you get them salivating at the sight of the outcome you’re guaranteeing.
      * This shows exactly what’s in it for them.


________________


Proof of Claim (30-45/50s):
* The third question you must answer in your DSL is “How do I know this is real?”
   * Naturally, after making a huge claim, people will default to “BS” or skepticism. We must counteract that immediately by proving our claim beyond any doubts.
* The easiest and most natural transition from the offer/guarantee section into the results/proof of claim section is via a compelling “reason why” you’re offering that guarantee.
      * Examples:
         * “Now you may be wondering, why am I offering such a crazy guarantee like this?”
         * “Now you may be wondering, why am I so confident to offer such a crazy guarantee like this?”
         * “Now why am I offering such a crazy guarantee like this and really putting our neck on the line?”
            * “It’s simple. Just look at our track record…”
      * Side note: You can also advertise the real catch to the offer (if there is one). But the catch should always be a net positive
            * For example, I sometimes will use “Now, the catch is that once you {recieve huge benefit}, we hope you’ll become a private client.” → as an example.
               * This provides a “reason why” and also acts as a net positive because it implies they’re gonna recieve the huge benefit // the one big thing they want
         * When stating results, you can also subtly handle objections
         * Leading on from the “Transition”
            * “It’s simple. Look at our track record:
               * We helped {Name} to {ONE BIG OUTCOME} in {timeframe} without {Objection 1}
               * We helped {Name} to {ONE BIG OUTCOME} in {timeframe} without {Objection 2}
               * {Name} went from {common current state} to {ONE BIG OUTCOME} in {timeframe} without {objection 3}”
         * Cover 7-10 client results
            * The more the better, however, make sure these are always read in rapid fire mode.
First CTA (Inside 60s):
* State the beliefs that your market needs to believe to be true in order for them to buy from you
   * These beliefs can be:
      * This works
      * This specific approach is best
      * Practicing what you’re preaching
      * If you don’t get results, you don’t pay
         * Any other big beliefs your market needs to believe to be true to buy. These beliefs should stem from your market research plus from all the sales calls you’ve taken.
* “Now if this is all you need to hear and you’re thinking, okay:
   * I know this works
   * I know I NEED to book more calls every month in order to ultimately scale my biz
   * And I know you’ve done this yourself and it works!
      * Then click the link below and book a call…
         * And again, we’re not gonna hard sell you on this thing because if you don’t {one big benefit} we will have to {insert your guarantee}...”

Unique Mechanism (5mins)
To create a high-converting Unique Mechanism, we need to answer 2 questions (from the prospects perspective):


1. “Why hasn’t this worked for me so far?”
2. “How does it work?”


Both of these questions tie in together. Here’s how you answer them.


1. “Why hasn’t this worked for me so far?”


The way you answer this is by revealing the real problem that’s been holding them back from achieving their desired outcome (the desired outcome being your offer).


Why haven’t your ideal clients been able to get the result you’re promising them?


What is most of the market currently doing that’s stopping them from getting the result?


Another way to figure this out is by looking at what is the thing you do that’s different from what everyone else does and you take that answer and inverse it. Usually, that inverse is the answer to what’s currently holding them back.


Example (from 16-word sales letter):
* “How is this different from anything else I’ve seen?”
   * It’s the only indicator in the world that helps you get in the trade before the news is published
* “Why hasn’t this worked for me so far?”
   * The real problem is that you get into the trade after the news is published. By the time you get in it’s already too late and the big gains have already been made.


The key here is you take the blame off your prospect's shoulders and put it onto the old opportunities they’ve tried.


SCIO example:


How are we different?


We create a DSL which is built off the back of how the brain is structured. This way our marketing message bypasses the usual “walls” we put up and ultimately pushes your prospects to book a call.


“Why hasn’t this worked for me so far?”


The real reason why you haven’t been able to consistently scale your new client volume is because you’ve been saying the same thing as every other agency which as soon as your ideal client consumes your message they immediately bucket you as “another agency” or “I’ve seen this before” and they immediately ignore you, sub-consciously.


Literally, your prospects don’t even give you a chance. They subconsciously ignore your message because it can’t bypass their reptilian brain.
Here’s the language pattern to use:


“Now, if you want to know more detail on WHY this works so well…

In the next 90 seconds, I’m gonna give you a FULL inside look into HOW we’re able to routinely generate 3-5 LIVE calls per day for our client's offers which allows them to simply focus on taking calls and closing deals without the headache and stress of not knowing when they’re gonna sign their next client, which ultimately means they can focus on the consistent growth of their business because they KNOW they have a filled calendar with qualified booked calls…

See, right now you’re unable to (insert specific core desire), which isn’t your fault because (Insert real problem)...”


Now we need to answer the second question…


2. “How does it work?”


Here we need to explain why your approach/your option is EXPONENTIALLY BETTER than ANYTHING else on the market. Think direct competitors + indirect competitors.


Think of ALL the other ways they could achieve the result and show WHY you are exponentially better than those options.


The explanation must be clear and make the prospect think ‘Oh yeah, that makes sense’.


The way you make the prospect think “yeah that makes sense” is by making them understand exactly how your new mechanism gives them the end result you’re promising and at the same time explain how it’s different from what they’ve seen/tried in the past.


If you can’t make this distinction then your prospects won’t book a call as they won’t have any hope that this is different from that last thing they tried which didn’t work.


Show proof on WHY it works (not just THAT it works).


Show your evidence. Think like you’re presenting your case to a grand jury.


This isn’t results or facts. It’s the reason WHY your approach works (your UM)


If you’re still unsure on exactly what your Unique Mechanism could be continue watching this next part:
Steps To Creating A Mechanism:
1. Get CLEAR on why your method/service works
   1. Examine your competitor's marketing
      1. We’re examining our competitor's marketing to know what NOT to say
         1. What is the mechanism behind their offer?
         2. How does their mechanism/method work?
            1. How does it ACTUALLY work?
         3. What are positives and negatives about their approach?
         4. Does their mechanism solve a symptom of the problem or the root cause?
            1. UNDERSTANDING what your prospects are hearing from competitors is CRITICAL → if you don’t know what they are already hearing then you have no way of knowing whether you’re presenting something different or not
   2. Interrogate your delivery:
      1. Taking your product or service and putting it under the spotlight:
         1. What is my approach?
         2. How does my approach work?
         3. Why does it work? (Does it address the symptom or root cause?)
         4. Why do I do it the way I do it?
         5. Why do I do it in the order I do it?
         6. What have I added or subtracted from what other people normally say to do?
            1. What have I combined?
         7. Why is my way easier?
         8. Why does my way work faster?
         9. Why does my way produce greater results?
         10. Why does my way produce more consistent results?
         11. Why does my way cause fewer problems?
         12. Why is my way better?
2. The wrapping paper
   1. Don’t get too hung up on the name of your UM. The HOW behind your UM is more important than the name. However, below are some key practices for naming:
      1. Make it sound scientific
      2. Sounds REAL
      3. They understand what it is just not in the context you’re using it in
      4. Don’t be cringey
      5. Stay away from mnemonics (especially if you’re in a saturated market)
      6. Ensure it sounds ‘legit’, ‘credible’ and something to where the prospect goes ‘Yeah, that sounds like it could work’
         1. Example: P90X
            1. Their UM wasn’t ‘the HUGE bicep domination method’
            2. Their UM was ‘The muscle confusion method’
Here’s the language pattern to use:
(leading on from the first question…)


“So instead what we do is MECHANISM NAME and here’s how it works… (insert factual/objective explanation that makes the prospect think ‘yeah, that makes sense’)”
Results:
* After explaining how this new approach works this acts as an indirect claim so we want to immediately go into another set of results which prove what we just claimed to actually be true/work.
   * IMPORTANT: these results must not be the same as the ones you stated in the beginning. If the client has less than 10 total results, then frontload them all in the first “Proof Of Claim” section and skip this part. If they have more than 10, put 7-10 in the beginning and introduce another 5-10 in this section.
* Example:
   * “And thats exactly how one of our clients (name) went from (common current state) to (ultimate desired state), it was directly by (insert mechanism you’ve just explained)…”
   * “And another one of our clients ‘x’...”
   * “And another…”
      * The same ‘best practices’ discussed in the “Proof Of Claim” section apply here.
Strong CTA:
* The sixth question is “What do I have to lose?”
* If the prospect is still watching by this point in the DSL is means all of their questions have been answered (if you did the previous sections correctly) and they’re most likely sold on our solution. Now all that’s left is to really hone in on them booking a call and showing how they have nothing to lose by doing so.
   * Example:
      * “And again, if you don’t see these types of results in your (insert their business // whatever you help them with), then you don’t pay…

As I previously mentioned (restate the explanation of your guarantee which you alluded to in the previous “Guarantee” section)...

So if this sounds interesting and you want (insert ultimate desired goal), then click the link right below this video and schedule in a call…

And remember, we’re not gonna try to incentivize you to do anything that you don’t wanna do or doesn’t make sense for you to do because otherwise, we’ll have to refund you anyway…

So click the link below and schedule a call…”
