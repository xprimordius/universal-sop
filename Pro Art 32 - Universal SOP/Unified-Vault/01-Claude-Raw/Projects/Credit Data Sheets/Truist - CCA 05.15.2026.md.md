## **How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

### **Path A — 0% Business Credit Card (cooling; $25k max exposure)**

1. Confirm the **entity \+ owner's personal address are in a Truist footprint state** (required; foreign filings often fail).  
2. **Unfreeze Equifax** — only bureau pulled; hard pull on business apps.  
3. Open a **Simple Business Checking** account **in-branch** and deposit **$5,000–$10,000**. Do not mention lending intent; let the banker "offer" the card during onboarding.  
4. Let the account **season \~30 days** (direct banker guidance). Newer entities or no-seasoning apps are currently seeing $2,500 starter limits.  
5. Apply for the **12-month 0% Business Credit Card**:  
   * Strong profile → **online** (working again as of March 2026).  
   * Decent profile / newer entity → **in-branch** with the onboarding banker.  
6. If first-card approval is **under $25k**, wait **7 days** and apply for the **9-month 0% card** on the same LLC to double-dip up to the $25k cap (e.g., $15k \+ $10k).  
7. If first-card approval is **$25k**, **stop**. A second card will trigger tax-return requests or limit-splitting.  
8. If denied, **call to follow up** and add context (revenue, income, relationship) before reapplying.

### **Path B — Business Line of Credit (active; phone \+ RM)**

1. Confirm footprint state and that Equifax is unfrozen.  
2. Optional but strongly recommended for rate and approval: open business checking and build **90–120 days of activity with bi-weekly deposits** (deposit behavior weighted up to \~3x credit metrics).  
3. **Cold-call local Truist branches** to find a Relationship Manager willing to run a BLOC.  
4. Apply **by phone with the RM**. Expect a DocuSign and a **7–12 hour decision**.  
5. Request **≤ $50k** to target a **no-doc** outcome. Above $50k (or at some branches even at/under $50k), be ready with **2 years business \+ personal tax returns**.  
6. If the first branch demands tax returns at ≤$50k, **call additional branches** — willingness varies by banker.

## **What to Say / What to Enter (application scripting)**

* **Business description:** Operating LLC in a Truist footprint state; keep the industry consistent and underwriting-friendly (e.g., marketing consultant, professional services).  
* **Revenue / income (card):** **$500,000** business revenue / **$225,000** personal annual income as standard well-performing inputs. Phone referrals at **$350k+ rev / $150k+ income** have hit $20k–$25k limits.  
* **Personal credit:** **700+ FICO** (banker minimum for BLOC).  
* **Purpose of funds (card):** Working capital / business operating expenses. **Do not** mention interest in lending while opening checking.  
* **Purpose of funds (BLOC):** Working capital / cash-flow smoothing. If asked, disclosing an **existing $50k+ business credit line elsewhere** has helped secure no-doc BLOCs.  
* **Footprint rule:** Both personal address and LLC registration must be in a Truist footprint state. LLC can be registered in-footprint even if the owner lives elsewhere.  
* **Must avoid:** Applying for a 2nd card after a $25k first-card approval; frozen Equifax at application time; mentioning lending intent at checking onboarding; BLOC phone apps without an RM.
