## **🧠 How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

### **0\) Pre-checks (must pass before applying)**

1. **Address check (CMRA / virtual address).**  
   * Run the business address through USPS lookup. If it flags as CMRA or virtual, Chase will flag it.  
   * The business address, phone, and email on the Ink application **must match** the Chase business checking profile. If using a virtual address, open the Chase business checking under the **residential address** and match both.  
2. **LLC name check.**  
   * If the LLC has sensitive words (e.g. "Investments"), expect friction. A SOS name change is **not accepted** by Chase — only **IRS name-change docs** are accepted.  
3. **5/24 awareness.**  
   * Over 5/24 \= online approvals unlikely. RM path can sometimes bypass, but don't bank on it.  
4. **Active-Chase-biz-card count (popup test).**  
   * Up to **3 active Chase business cards at 4/24** is still inside the approval window.  
   * If over that, **close excess Inks before applying** (active card count at the moment of application drives the popup, not lifetime Inks).  
   * Stagger closures — \~1 per month, don't close multiple at once.  
5. **Freeze TransUnion** (and optionally Equifax) to funnel Chase to a single Experian pull.

### **1\) Open / season Chase business checking**

1. Open **Chase Business Complete** or, for RM access, **Business Premier / Platinum Checking** (\~$99/mo).

   * Platinum upgrade script at branch: "We're opening the business platinum account because we're moving all our business to Chase and expecting \~$1.1M revenue."  
2. Deposit **$10,000+** (10k is the current sweet spot; 5k is underperforming).

3. Season **10+ days** minimum with small recurring transactions ($20–$30/wk). Longer seasoning (1–5 months with $15k balance) has produced $50k+ approvals.

4. For Premier/Platinum path: once opened, ask to be connected to a **BRM** using this script:

    "I'm interested in $80–120k in lending. Can you connect me with a BRM or manager to move that forward? I have too many new clients and need capital to hire, invest in marketing, and keep growing."

### **2\) Apply for Card 1 — Ink Cash (preferred) or Ink Unlimited**

1. Apply **online** for **Ink Cash** — \~10% higher approval rate than Unlimited in our data.  
2. Submit **after 7pm** has been a successful datapoint.  
3. If it goes pending, call acquisitions at **1-888-609-7805** and be ready to verify EIN, address, and business details.  
4. If no prior Chase relationship, expect a possible **Zoom ID verification**.

### **3\) Card 2 — double-dip / Preferred logic**

1. **If Card 1 limit \> $30k:** immediately apply for **Ink Preferred** in the same session.  
2. **If Card 1 limit ≤ $25k:** wait **30 days** before the Preferred app.  
3. **Double-dip flow:**  
   * Wait for Card 1 to arrive and activate.  
   * Remove the inquiry.  
   * Apply for the second card from inside the client's Chase portal.  
   * Expect \~½ the limit of Card 1 on the second card.  
4. If the second card doesn't come with 0% intro, **call and request the 0% intro APR** — this works.  
5. **Moving Preferred limit → Ink Cash/Unlimited** after approval lets you use 0% intro on both. After that, wait **6–12 months** before the next 0% card app.

### **4\) Multi-LLC sequencing**

* **1 LLC:** 1 RM-submitted app, then 1 manual app from the client's device (fast, no waiting).  
* **2 LLCs:** RM submits for LLC 1, wait for approval, then RM submits for LLC 2\.  
* Only stack multiple apps same-day when: (a) strong revenue is already showing in the business account, or (b) one card each on two businesses that each have strong revenue. Otherwise do 1 card, then wait **3–6 months** and keep util below 50% before the next.

### **5\) Post-approval limit increases**

1. Get into **Card Member Services**.  
2. Request a **large** bump (not a safe small one).  
3. Let it go pending, then ask to **expedite via the Acquisitions department**.  
4. Pitch: "I have $X in the bank, my business does Y, I need $Z because ABC."  
   * Worst case: $10k bump. Best case: $25k → $50k, $50k → $75k.  
5. RM-driven recon increases have also worked (e.g., $10k → $20k, $20k → $30k).  
6. Via RM, **personal → business limit transfers** are now possible (datapoint: Jan 2026).

### **6\) BLOC path (through NYC BRM III banker — J. C.)**

* **No-doc BLOC:** up to **$149k**.  
* **With 3 months statements:** up to **$249k**.  
* Requires **730+ credit** minimum.  
* Real business preferred but not strictly required.  
* All Chase BLOCs **come with a UCC filing** regardless of size.  
* Note: this BRM is strong for BLOCs, **not** for business credit cards.

## **🗣️ What to Say / What to Enter (application scripting)**

### **Profile datapoints (match revenue to target limit — revenue should exceed annual spend)**

* **3-month-old business or newer (RM path):** $600k rev / $350k income / $85k spend / 4 employees.  
* **1yr+ business:** $1.2M rev / $450k income / $85k spend / 6 employees.  
* **2yr+ business:** $1.6–2.0M rev / $550k income / $85k spend / 9 employees.  
* **Targeting $75k+ limits:** $1.0–1.3M revenue \+ $250–500k personal income (keeps DTI believable).  
* **Platinum BRM docusign standard:** Revenue $1.05–1.25M / Income $450k / Employees 4–9 / Monthly spend $75k.  
* **Conservative safe set:** $1.0M rev / $450k income / $75k spend / 9 employees (used for a $57k Ink approval; also used $1.0M on a Legacy aged corp second-LLC $55k Ink Unlimited approval).

### **Business description / NAICS**

* Strongest NAICS for consulting-type files: **541611 — Administrative Management and General Management Consulting Services**. NAICS can be changed by calling in.  
* Real-estate-related: **531390** has approved with strong business-credit profile.

### **Purpose of funds**

* Growth/operational: hiring, marketing, inventory/working capital — keep it tied to revenue growth. Avoid any language that sounds personal or speculative.

### **Must-say / Must-avoid**

* **Must say** (if BRM route): explicit capital need tied to growth ($80–120k range) and intent to consolidate banking at Chase.  
* **Must avoid:** understating revenue. Low stated revenue \+ 2 Inks opened under 12 months has triggered recon denials.  
* **Must avoid:** high utilization running on existing Inks — it shows on Experian Small Business and has caused denials on other business lending (e.g., QuickBooks term loan).
