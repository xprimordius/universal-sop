### **🧭 How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

1. **Confirm branch eligibility** — client must be within \~100 miles of a BMO branch (e.g., Dallas-based businesses qualify via the Oklahoma branch).  
2. **Open business checking in-branch** and deposit **$5,000** (guidance: $5k–$10k improves approval size).  
3. **Season the account \~60 days** before applying for the BLOC. Recent network datapoints show **$30k–$50k BLOC approvals** after this timing.  
4. Return **in-branch with banker support** and apply for the business credit card and/or BLOC.  
   * Some bankers allow same-day application after opening checking ("15k every time"), but seasoned accounts consistently produce stronger recent outcomes.  
5. Have **2–3 months of bank statements** ready even for "no-doc" products — underwriting has been requesting them lately.  
6. For BLOC up to **$100k**: plan on **3 months of statements** and be prepared to demonstrate **\~3+ years in business**.

### **📝 What to Say / What to Enter (application scripting)**

* **Business description:** Real, operating business in \[industry\]. Describe normal operating activities (e.g., trucking → fuel, maintenance, payroll). Bank wants to see it's a genuine operating business doing what it says it does.  
* **Purpose of funds / product:**  
  * **Business credit card** — short-term operating expenses (0% intro for 12 months).  
  * **BLOC** — working capital, equipment/vendor payments, cash-flow smoothing.  
* **Revenue / income framing:** State real numbers. Reference datapoint: $1.8M revenue \+ $400k personal income → $50k approval.  
* **Must say:**  
  * Frame statements as evidence of **real operating expenses tied to industry** — not projections.  
  * If eligible, mention the **minority/women-owned small business program** (reportedly as low as \~617 FICO for Black/Hispanic/Native American/women-owned businesses; bank does not verify). Confirm current terms with banker.  
* **Avoid:**  
  * Pitching projections instead of actual operating activity.  
  * Applying cold without opening checking first.

* **Bureaus:** EX and TU (both pulls observed; assume either/both possible on a single app)  
* **Checking relationship:** Yes — open in-branch first  
* **Deposit:** $5,000 (guidance $5k–$10k)

* **RM required:** No, but banker support materially improves outcomes

  * ≤ $50k: often "no-doc" but expect 2 months statements  
  * up to $100k: low-doc, 3 months statements, \~3+ years TIB
