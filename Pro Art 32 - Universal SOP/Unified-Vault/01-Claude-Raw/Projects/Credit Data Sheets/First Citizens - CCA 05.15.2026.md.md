### **🧭 How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

1. **Eligibility gate:** Confirm client is within \~70 miles of a local First Citizens branch and that First Citizens operates in their state (see Footprint below). The BRM must be in-state.  
2. **Open business checking** with the local BRM — phone-based is fine once the RM relationship is established; otherwise in-branch.  
3. **Fund the new checking with \~$10K via a third-party wire** (e.g., card liquidation proceeds). A third-party wire reads like revenue; a self-transfer does not.  
4. **Season the deposit \~2 weeks** before applying. Some branch managers prefer a full 30 days of deposits — ask the BRM which standard their branch uses and default to 30 days if unsure.  
5. **Apply for the business card through the BRM** (0% intro, \~12 months). Target $15K standard; BRM can approve up to $25K based on deposit size. Requests above $25K typically trigger statements or low-doc.  
6. **For BLOC (\<$150K, low-doc lane):** provide 1-year tax return \+ YTD P\&L. Ask the BRM to size at 10% of annual revenue; a strong BRM can push to 20%.  
7. **If the branch insists on tax returns for a card and client wants no-doc:** call nearby branches — requirements vary by branch manager.

### **🗣️ What to Say / What to Enter**

* **Business description:** Operating corporation with consistent revenue and recurring receivables. Keep language underwriting-friendly (established operations, in-state footprint).  
* **Purpose of funds:**  
  * Card: "Expense management and smoothing accounts payable."  
  * BLOC: "Working capital and supplier float."  
* **Requested product:** Business checking \+ business credit card (0% intro, \~12 months) and/or BLOC (\<$150K no-doc tier).  
* **Must say:** Emphasize local footprint, intent to maintain the deposit relationship long-term, and in-state operations.  
* **Must avoid:**  
  * Don't ask to exceed $25K on the card without offering statements or a larger deposit.  
  * Don't request BLOC \>$150K in the low-doc lane.  
  * Don't describe the seed funds as a self-transfer — frame as business inflow when accurate.  
* **Reference profile that has worked (adjust truthfully to the client):** Corporation, \~$310K annual revenue, \~$9,400 estimated monthly spend, \~$225K total annual income.  
* **Heads-up for the client:** Minimum payments on First Citizens business cards are typically 3–5% (not 1%). A $10K balance \= $300–$500/month minimum.
