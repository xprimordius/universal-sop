## **🛠️ How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

### **Path A — Quick BCC (recent wins, $15–20k target)**

1. Open a **PNC business checking** account (in-branch preferred; online also works).  
2. Deposit **$1,000+** and let it sit **1 night**.  
3. Next day, apply **in-branch** for the **"PNC Visa Business Credit Card"** by name.  
   * Ask for it by name up front — RMs will steer into doc-heavy products (BLOC/loan) if you don't.  
4. Target limit request: **$25k** (recent wins consistently land $15–20k).  
5. If approved low, call the number on the back of the card to request a limit increase (commonly \+$10k).

### **Path B — Larger BCC/BLOC with liquidity match ($50–100k target)**

1. Open PNC business checking.  
2. Deposit **the amount you want to be approved for**. Current no-doc threshold has slid from $100k → $75k → **\~$50k** (2024–25).  
3. Let funds sit **\~60 days** before applying. Faster timelines work but success rates drop materially.  
4. Apply for **BCC \+ BLOC** (both can be no-doc with sufficient liquidity).  
   * BLOC **≤ $50k**: typically no-doc.  
   * BLOC **$50–100k**: have **3 months of bank statements** ready.  
   * **\> $100k**: expect 3 years of business \+ personal taxes, debt schedule, and personal financial statement.  
5. For lines **\> $250k**: PNC offers an **asset-backed path** (inventory/AR collateral; e.g., $950k line at \~8.57%).

## **🗣️ What to Say / What to Enter (application scripting)**

* **Product request (RM/branch):** Say *"I'd like the PNC Visa Business Credit Card"* right at client introduction. Do not let the RM reframe it as a loan or BLOC application unless that is the goal.  
* **Stated business revenue:**  
  * Keep **\< $500k** to stay inside the current no-doc threshold.  
  * On the business checking application: **$100k minimum** (or actual revenue if higher). Reference datapoint: $375k annual revenue used successfully.  
* **Annual (total) personal income:** \~$225k (reference datapoint).  
* **Estimated monthly spend (checking app):** \~$10,500 (reference datapoint).  
* **Company structure:** Corporation or LLC. For no-doc lending, **LLC should be 2+ years old** (under 2 years routes to SBA).  
* **Limit request / expected spend:** **$25k** for BCC; up to **$100k** for BLOC (match to liquidity deposited).  
* **Inquiry expectations:** **Experian hard pull \+ Equifax soft pull.** Plan EX inquiry windows accordingly.  
* **Must avoid:** Vague product asks (triggers doc requests); applying with less than $1k in the account; mentioning hotels or auto-industry operations (PNC will not lend).
