## **🧾 How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

1. Confirm the client is in Valley's footprint: AL, CA, FL, IL, NJ, NY, PA. If outside, skip Valley.  
2. Confirm time-in-business:  
   * Business credit card: available earlier.  
   * Line of credit (LOC): business must be **2+ years old**.  
3. Open a Valley **business checking account** first (relationship-first — required before applying).  
4. **Apply in-branch** for the business credit card (and LOC if eligible). Valley applications here are done through the branch, not online.  
5. Immediately after application, request the **inquiry be removed** (legacy team note).  
6. When eligible, stack **business card \+ LOC** together. Target combined exposure up to \~$100k non-doc (needs verification — see below).

## **🗣️ What to Say / What to Enter (application scripting)**

* **Business description:** Established operating business in Valley's footprint; seeking a banking relationship plus working capital tools.  
* **Purpose of funds / product requested:** Business credit card for operating expenses; business line of credit for working capital and inventory / cash-flow smoothing (if 2+ yrs).  
* **Profile template (from latest datapoint):**  
  * Business revenue: $375k–$425k  
  * Household income: $150k–$175k  
* **Must say:** You are opening a business checking relationship and want to apply for the business card (and LOC if eligible) in-branch.  
* **Must avoid:** Speculative or high-risk use of funds; don't imply cash-out or personal use.  
* **Credit pull:** Expect a **TransUnion (TU)** inquiry.
