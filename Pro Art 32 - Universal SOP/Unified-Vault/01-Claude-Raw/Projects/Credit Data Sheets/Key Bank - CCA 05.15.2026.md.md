### **🧭 How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

1. **Confirm eligibility before you walk in**  
   * KeyBank in-footprint branch nearby (AK, CO, CT, ID, IN, ME, MA, MI, NY, OH, OR, PA, UT, VT, WA).  
   * If out of state, foreign-file the entity into a footprint state (WA and PA reported; KeyBank has honored the foreign-filing date) and/or add a personal address in-footprint by updating the billing address on a personal card so it joins your address history.  
   * For LOC/BLOC, target **2+ years in business** and **700+ personal score**.  
2. **Make every identifier match (April 2026 scrutiny)**  
   * KeyBank is actively denying files with mismatches between business name, business address, and personal ID.  
   * Align business name, business address, personal address, and ID across the application, supporting docs, and KeyBank records before submitting.  
3. **Open the relationship in-branch**  
   * Walk into the branch and open business checking with a branch manager or RM.  
   * Initial deposit: **$5,000 minimum ($10,000 preferred)**.  
   * If the first branch is not cooperative, call other in-footprint branches until you find an RM willing to work the file.  
4. **Season the relationship**  
   * Wait **30 days** after the opening deposit before applying for credit products.  
5. **Apply for the combo (in-branch, with RM)**  
   * Ask for **Business credit card (\~$25k)** \+ **Business line of credit ($25k–$50k, 0% intro reported)**.  
   * LOC has historically required 2+ years in business; the newer no-doc lane may relax documentation but not branch/RM dependence.  
6. **No-doc LOC lane (ask the RM directly)**  
   * Reported pattern: application \+ phone number \+ website only — no financials, no tax returns, no bank statements.  
   * Bureau pull: **Equifax only (soft)** on newer datapoints.  
   * Typical approval: **$50k BLOC**. Cap is banker-dependent; some branches are now asking for 6 months of business bank statements, so keep them ready as a fallback.  
7. **EIN-only business credit card lane**  
   * Requires in-branch application \+ a complete business plan \+ a pre-existing KeyBank relationship.  
   * Not available online and not available without an existing relationship.  
8. **Multi-entity stacking (aged corps)**  
   * Members have reported 3–4 separate KeyBank BLOCs at **$50k each** across different entities. Repeatable with clean entities and fully consistent documentation across each file.

---

### **🗣 What to Say / What to Enter (application scripting)**

* **Business description:** Marketing consulting / marketing agency. If asked about clients: real estate professionals (agents, investors), plus PPC, SEO, and other paid advertising.  
* **Purpose of funds / requested product:** Business credit card for operating expenses, and a business line of credit for working capital and ad spend.  
* **Company structure:** Match your filings exactly (Corporation used on recent approved file; LLC also works if that matches your records).  
* **Sweet-number profiles used on approved files:**  
  * Business revenue: **$375k–$425k** (most common); other approved files used $250k, $300k–$350k, $450k–$600k, up to $860k.  
  * Personal income: **$150k–$225k** (higher-end file used $246k).  
  * Estimated monthly spend: **$7,500–$30k** depending on revenue profile.  
  * Employees: **2**.  
* **Must do:**  
  * Make business name, business address, and personal ID perfectly consistent across the application, documents, and KeyBank records.  
  * Apply in-branch with a branch manager or RM whenever possible.  
* **Must avoid:**  
  * Online-only applications for the EIN-only card lane.  
  * Submitting with any name or address mismatch.  
* **If asked for docs:** Be ready with 6 months of business bank statements (increasingly requested in late 2025 / 2026).
