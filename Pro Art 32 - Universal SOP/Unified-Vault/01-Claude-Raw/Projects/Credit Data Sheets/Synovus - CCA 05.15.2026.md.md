### **🧭 How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

1. Decide the product: a Synovus business credit card (0% intro APR for 6 months) **or** a BLOC of **$50k or less** (no-doc lane).  
2. Apply directly with Synovus — **no RM required** and **no checking relationship required**.  
3. For a BLOC, keep the request at **≤ $50k** to stay in the no-doc path. Have statements ready in case underwriting asks, but do not volunteer them.  
4. For a card, pick one of the three Synovus business Visas (see scripting below). Expect **Pinnacle Bank** branding on disclosures — cards are reportedly issued by Pinnacle.  
5. Stage bureau freezes for **Equifax or Experian** (pull varies between the two).

### **🗣️ What to Say / What to Enter (application scripting)**

* **Card options (pick one):**  
  1. **Business Visa Credit Card** — no annual fee, 0% intro APR 6 months on purchases, balance transfers, and overdraft protection.  
  2. **Business Rewards Visa** — no annual fee, 0% intro APR 6 months.  
  3. **Business Travel Rewards Visa** — $0 first year, then $50/year, 0% intro APR 6 months.  
* **BLOC request amount:** **≤ $50k** for the no-doc path. Requesting above this kicks the file into full-doc underwriting.  
* **Business description:** Clean, verifiable operating business consistent with state filings and online footprint.  
* **Purpose of funds:** Working capital / inventory / operating expenses.  
* **Must avoid:** Asking for \>$50k on the BLOC if you want to preserve the no-doc lane.  
* **Issuer note:** Do not be confused if Pinnacle Bank appears on card disclosures — Synovus business cards are issued by Pinnacle.
