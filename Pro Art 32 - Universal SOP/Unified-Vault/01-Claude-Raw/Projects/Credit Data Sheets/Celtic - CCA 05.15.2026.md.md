### **🚀 How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

1. Prepare documents before applying:  
   * **1 year business tax return** (required for Celtic BLOC underwriting)  
   * Supporting P\&L (recommended, not required)  
2. Confirm the financial bar is met:  
   * Strong **revenue** — Celtic underwrites BLOC on revenue, not net  
   * **$20k–$40k net profit** shown on the return  
3. If the bar is met: submit the BLOC application with the tax return as the primary underwriting doc.  
4. If the bar is NOT met: hold off. Either improve financials or redirect to another lender before retrying.
