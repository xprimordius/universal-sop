### AMEX — limit transfer rules \+ relationship strategy • Limit transfers: when moving credit limit off an Amex card, you must leave at least $2,000 on the card you’re moving from (can’t transfer the full limit). • Unique capability: Amex is the only bank discussed that allows transferring personal credit limit to a business card. ◦ Chase \+ Bank of America: allow cross-LLC/business reallocations, but not personal-to-business limit moves. • Relationship/starting limit risk: without an existing Amex relationship, applicants may get only \~$2,000 limits on Blue Business Plus / Blue Cash-type products. • Relationship-build workaround: get Business Gold first, or open a personal Delta Gold, then transfer that limit to build/seed higher limits on the business side.

### **✅ How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

**Hard constraint:** 2 hard-limit business cards per 90 days. If client opened another Amex in the last 90 days, wait.

**Timing that helps:** apply **2–4 PM PST** or **5–7 PM EST** (post-7 PM also cited). Use the **same email** as any existing Amex profile.

#### **Path A — No existing Amex relationship**

1. Start with a **relationship builder**:  
   * Preferred: **Biz Blue Cash** first.  
   * Alternate: **Biz Delta Gold** — ⚠️ recent approvals trending low ($3k in March 2026 even with 3 mo of $12–17k/mo statements). Consider **Amazon Biz** or **Marriott Biz** as builders instead.  
2. Wait **30 days**.  
3. Apply for the **2nd Biz card** (the other Blue product).  
4. Wait **30 days**.  
5. Apply for **Amex Business Line of Credit (BLOC)** — 1+ yr LLC required; 2+ yr LLC sees $25–50k instant approvals; expect Plaid at higher stated revenue.

#### **Path B — Existing Amex personal relationship**

1. Refresh personal income on file if \>12 months old (\~30% bump).  
2. Apply **Blue Business Plus** — BBP limit tracks the matching personal Blue Cash Preferred ceiling.  
3. Same session or next day, apply **Blue Business Cash** (or another biz card).  
4. Optional: **reallocate personal → business** limit (Amex is the only issuer that allows this; must leave $2,000 on the source card).  
5. 30 days later, apply for **BLOC** if eligible.

### **After approval**

* Request CLI **\~35 days** after funding (some data points cite 60–90 days — verify per product).  
* Multi-year 0% loop: Y1 Blue Business Plus → Y2 Blue Business Cash → Y3 cancel BBP → \~6 mo later re-invite → combine limits.

### **🗣️ What to Say / What to Enter (application scripting)**

* **Industry type:** Other  
* **NAICS:** low-risk — "Other Management Consulting (541618)" or "Marketing Consulting"  
* **Company structure:** Corporation (or LLC as applicable)  
* **\>25% ownership:** YES for applicant; **NO** for others (adding another \>25% owner triggers a pull on that person's credit)  
* **Years in business:** enter **2** (min 1 required; safe to enter 2 even for a fresh LLC)  
* **Employees:** 2  
* **Revenue:**  
  * Safe no-doc lane: **≤ $300k**  
  * Plaid risk triggers above **\~$350k**  
  * When targeting a $35k limit, common input is **$350–450k** (expect potential Plaid)  
* **Personal income:** ≤ $250k keeps no-doc; common inputs $90–150k  
* **Monthly spend:** $10–25k  
* **Business description:** "Professional services / consulting. B2B clients. Low returns/refunds. No regulated products."  
* **Purpose of funds:** "Working capital to smooth cash flow between invoices, operating expenses, and vendor payments."

"Growth is accelerating — hiring and scaling spend. A higher limit lets us channel all expenses to Amex and keep them on one 0% line. Can you review for the maximum CLI without another hard pull?"

**Must avoid:** cash-advance framing, debt consolidation, "emergency funding" language, high-risk industries, adding extra \>25% owners unless required.
