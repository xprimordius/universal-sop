# **California Bank & Trust \- CCA 05.15.2026**

# 

## **How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

1. Confirm eligibility before applying:  
   * Have **1 year of business tax returns** ready (required for BLOC).  
   * Return should show **strong revenue** and **$20k–$40k net profit**.  
2. If the tax return does not support the profit range, **hold** — do not submit. Improve profit or route to a different lender.  
3. If eligible, submit the BLOC application with the tax return as the primary underwriting document.  
4. Frame the request as revenue-based (underwriting is revenue-based), but expect them to verify net profit on the return.

## **What to Say / What to Enter**

* **Product requested:** Business Line of Credit (BLOC).  
* **Business description:** Keep concise and underwriting-friendly; emphasize recurring revenue and operating history.  
* **Purpose of funds:** Working capital / smoothing receivables (avoid speculative or high-risk uses).  
* **Must say:** Revenue figures consistent with the tax return; highlight profitability in the $20k–$40k range.  
* **Must avoid:** Claiming net profit outside what the tax return will show.
