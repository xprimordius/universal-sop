### **🚀 How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

1. Go to Blue Vine's online application (no RM, no branch visit).  
2. Connect your business operating account(s) via **Plaid**.  
3. Submit; decisioning is fast once Plaid is linked.  
4. Expect approval to size roughly **1:1 with deposits** shown (latest datapoint: **$45k deposits → $50k approval**).  
5. To target a higher line, increase deposits visible through the linked account(s) before applying.

---

### **💬 What to Say / What to Enter (application scripting)**

* **Business description:** Operating LLC in \[industry\]; consistent monthly revenue through \[core product/service\].  
* **Purpose of funds:** Working capital — inventory, payroll, operating expenses.  
* **Requested product:** Line of Credit.  
* **Must do:** Link the primary operating account with the strongest deposit history via Plaid. Make sure recent months show the deposit level you want the approval sized to.  
* **Must avoid:** Linking thin, new, or irregular accounts that dilute the average-deposit signal Blue Vine keys on.
