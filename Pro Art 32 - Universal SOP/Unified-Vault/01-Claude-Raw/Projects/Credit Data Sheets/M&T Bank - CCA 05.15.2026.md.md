## **🔑 How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

### **Path A — In-branch with banker (recommended, highest approval odds)**

1. **Confirm footprint eligibility.** Business must be in a state with physical M\&T branches: NY, MD, NJ, PA, DE, CT, VA, WV, DC, VT, MA. If outside footprint, stop — M\&T will not approve.  
2. **Open an M\&T business checking account** at a local branch.  
3. **Season the relationship.** Maintain \~$2,000+ average balance for at least **90 days** (longer is materially better — the strongest recent datapoint was a 3-year relationship with $2k–$4k avg balance that produced $37.5k at 0% for 12 months).  
4. **Book an appointment with a branch banker/RM** where you opened the account. Apply **in person** for the M\&T Business Rewards Credit Card.  
5. **Ask banker about no-doc BLOC exposure** (commonly up to $50k, up to \~$100k with a supportive RM — banker-dependent).  
6. **LOC/BLOC lane:** only apply once the business is **2+ years old**.  
7. **Request inquiry removal** after the application is processed (some bankers will do this on request).

### **Path B — Phone application (no existing relationship)**

1. Confirm footprint eligibility (see Path A, step 1).  
2. Call **1-866-236-1219** and apply for the M\&T Business Rewards Credit Card.  
3. Application link: [M\&T Business Rewards Credit Card](https://www3.mtb.com/business/business-financing/business-credit-cards/business-credit-card-options/m-and-t-business-rewards-credit-card)  
4. Be ready for underwriting follow-up; a branch visit may still be requested.

## **📝 What to Say / What to Enter (application scripting)**

* **Business description:** Operating corporation/LLC with a clear, clean industry description. Avoid high-risk or speculative wording.  
* **Purpose of funds / requested product:** Business Rewards Credit Card for working capital and recurring operating expenses. For LOC/BLOC: general business operations and cash-flow management.  
* **Datapoints that have worked recently:**  
  * Gross business revenue: $350k–$550k  
  * Net business income: \~$220k  
  * Personal / household income: $150k–$330k  
  * Estimated monthly card spend: \~$9,000  
  * Company structure: Corporation or LLC  
* **Must say:** Mention the existing M\&T checking relationship and its length (in years, if possible). Emphasize in-footprint operations.  
* **Must avoid:**  
  * Applying from a state outside the M\&T footprint.  
  * Requesting LOC/BLOC before the business is 2+ years old.  
  * Overstating requested exposure on the first application — stage it.  
* **Unknown / needs verification:** whether Experian or Equifax is the current primary pull (both reported in recent datapoints).
