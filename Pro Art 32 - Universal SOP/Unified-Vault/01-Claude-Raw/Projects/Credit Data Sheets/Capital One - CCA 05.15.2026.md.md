## **🧠 How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

1. **Sequence last.** Apply to Capital One only after all other bank pulls in the stack are complete. Capital One pulls **Experian \+ Equifax \+ TransUnion** on every business application — a single app burns all three bureaus.  
2. **Enforce a \~30-day new-account buffer.** Do not apply if the client has opened any new accounts in the prior \~30 days. Recent account velocity triggers auto-denials.  
3. **Apply online** via Capital One's business application portal. No RM or branch required. **No reconsideration line** — a denial is final, so submit clean the first time.  
4. **Product selection (why Capital One is used at all):**  
   * **Spark Cash Plus** — primary target. $25,000 starting limits seen at mid-800 FICO with instant approval. **Does not report to personal credit.**  
   * **Venture X Business** — secondary target. **Does not report to personal credit.** Cannot be held simultaneously with Spark Cash Plus.  
   * Avoid other Capital One business cards — they **do** report to personal credit.  
5. **If Venture X Business is denied for "too high a Spark Card credit limit":** cancel the existing Spark Business card, then re-apply for Venture X Business (approval has followed).  
6. **If denied for "too many accounts of this type":** this is portfolio-level management, not a credit issue (seen even at 830 FICO). Do not retry immediately; move on in the stack.  
7. **Bonus eligibility:** Any prior Capital One business cardholder — even with closed accounts — is blocked from welcome bonuses. Do not target Cap One for bonuses for clients with prior Cap One business history.  
8. **Personal Venture / Venture X (only if relevant):** apply **after** a funding round, not before. 1 card per 6 months rule applies across personal \+ business combined.

## **💬 What to Say / What to Enter**

* **Business description:** Describe active, documented business operations — not a shell or pre-revenue entity. Capital One looks for documented business activity; sole-prop charge-card approvals are tougher without it.  
* **Purpose of funds / product requested:** Request the specific card by name (Spark Cash Plus or Venture X Business). Keep stated revenue realistic but supportable — $1,000+ stated revenue has supported pre-approvals on lower-tier products (VentureOne Business, Spark Cash Select) for sole-prop EINs.  
* **Must avoid saying/entering:**  
  * Do **not** apply as a prior Capital One business cardholder expecting a welcome bonus.  
  * Do **not** apply with new accounts opened in the prior \~30 days.  
  * Do **not** sequence Capital One early in the stack under any circumstances.

## **📇 Contacts**

* None required. Capital One business applications are self-service online; no RM, branch, or underwriter contact is part of the flow.

## **⚠️ Needs verification**

* **Deposit amount / Deposit time:** Capital One business cards do not appear to require a deposit relationship, but these fields are currently unset. Confirm whether the deposit columns should remain blank for card-only banks or be explicitly set to 0\.  
* **Seq Order (Aged corp):** currently 0 (not in the aged-corp sequence). Confirm whether Capital One should occupy the final aged-corp slot as well, mirroring its last-in-stack rule for the normal sequence.
