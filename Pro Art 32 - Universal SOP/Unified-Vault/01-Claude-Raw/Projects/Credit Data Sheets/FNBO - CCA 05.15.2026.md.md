### **🚀 How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

1. **Apply for the FNBO 0% business card first.** Reported promo: 6 months 0% on purchases. Expect a soft ceiling around **$10k–$15k**. Once issued, FNBO reports limits **≥ $10k to Dun & Bradstreet**, which boosts Paydex quickly and becomes a tradeline lever for later steps.  
2. **Open FNBO business checking.** Seed across accounts to reach **$2,500 total** (working DP: $500 personal \+ $1,000 into each of two business accounts). Use a real, non-virtual address — FNBO rejects virtual addresses.  
3. **Season the relationship for 3–4 weeks.**  
4. **Email the banker who opened your account** and say you want to apply for a business line of credit. They will route you to a business banker (phone call).  
5. **Complete the two forms the business banker sends:** Application \+ Personal Net Worth Statement.  
6. **Keep the BLOC request at $50k or less** to stay in the reported no-doc lane. Above $50k triggers full underwriting, which is noted as stricter lately.

---

### **🗣️ What to Say / What to Enter (application scripting)**

* **Requested product:** Business line of credit (BLOC), **$50k or less** to stay in the no-doc lane. If you need more, prepare for full-doc underwriting.  
* **Business description:** Real operating business at a real (non-virtual) street address.  
* **Purpose of funds:** Working capital / inventory / receivables financing — keep it underwriting-friendly. Avoid speculative or startup language.  
* **Financial datapoints that have worked recently:**  
  * Gross $725k / Net $375k → approved $15k card, then **$100k BLOC** after \~3–4 week seasoning.  
  * Annual revenue $1.4M / personal income $270k → $12k limit (reference DP via Flagstar LOC app context).  
* **Must avoid:**  
  * Virtual / CMRA addresses.  
  * Applying for the BLOC cold online — always route through the banker after seasoning.
