### **🚀 How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

1. Confirm the current active Elan bank link from the Credit Stacking Business Card Locator. As of April 2026, **Bank of Texas** is the active link (Bank of Utah was deactivated Feb 18, 2026). Links rotate every 3–4 months.  
2. Call a local branch of the currently active Elan bank and ask the banker to email the application link.  
3. Verify the link is **banker-coded** before applying — the URL should contain `bankercode` (not `offercode`) after the .com, e.g. `branchoffer.com/XYZ`.  
4. Have the **client** fill out and submit the application from their **own IP**. Elan is fraud-sensitive; do not submit from the funder's or a shared IP. If assistance is needed, use screenshare \+ remote control so the submission still originates from the client's device (screenshare detection has caused at least one denial — proceed carefully).  
5. Expect a **TransUnion** pull. Card lane is low-doc. Low-doc BLOC lane requires 3 months of business bank statements; no checking relationship required.  
6. If text verification is triggered, reply `1` twice to route to a direct callback (faster than the hold queue). The March 2026+ verification wave may also request Driver's License, physical Social Security card, and EIN confirmation letter.  
7. Wait for approval. Most decisions come within 24 hours; some applications go to a 7–10 day review.  
8. If the first card approves with a solid limit, remove the inquiry and apply for a 2nd card at a **different** Elan bank (same entity) or at another LLC. 2 cards per entity is confirmed.  
9. **Do NOT run Elan and US Bank back-to-back** — they share the same underwriting team and Elan balances are visible to US Bank underwriting.

#### **Multi-entity stacking sequence**

* Bank 1 / LLC 1 → if good, continue (no wait)  
* Bank 2 / LLC 2 → if good, continue (no wait)  
* Bank 2 / LLC 1

### **📝 What to Say / What to Enter (Application scripting)**

* **Product requested:** Business card (0% intro APR, 18 months) is the primary ask. The low-doc BLOC (online, 3 months of statements, no checking required) is a secondary lane.  
* **Purpose of funds:** business expansion / working capital. Do not mention credit stacking or balance-transfer intent.  
* **Underwriting datapoints that have been approving** (scale to the client's real profile):  
  * Business revenue: $600k–$1.1M  
  * Total annual income: $300k–$400k  
  * Checking / savings / money market: $200k–$275k+  
  * Investments and retirement: $200k–$275k+  
* **Credit profile that has been approving:** 760+ personal, minimal inquiries (0 inquiries in the last 12 months preferred). Recent $20k approval profile: 793 EX / 787 TU / 790 EQ on a 3-year Texas LLC with no prior Elan relationship.  
* **Must do:** client submits from the client's own IP; confirm banker-coded link before applying.  
* **Must avoid:** submitting from a funder/shared IP; applying right before or after US Bank; mentioning balance-transfer intent.
