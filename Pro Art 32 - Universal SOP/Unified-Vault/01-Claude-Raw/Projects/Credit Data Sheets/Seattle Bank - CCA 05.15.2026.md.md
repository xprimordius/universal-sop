1. **Eligibility check** — If out-of-state, file a **Washington foreign entity registration** first. This is what unlocks eligibility to apply here.

3. **Deposit $5,000** into the new checking account.  
4. **Wait 5 days** for seasoning.  
5. **Apply by phone** after seasoning. Request the BLOC.
