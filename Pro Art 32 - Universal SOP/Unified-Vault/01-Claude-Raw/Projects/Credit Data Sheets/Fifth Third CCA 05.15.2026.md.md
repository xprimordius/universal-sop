## **🏦 How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

1. Open a Fifth Third business checking account and seed it with a **$5,000–$10,000 deposit** (higher opening deposits have been linked to better approvals).  
2. **Wait \~90 days** to season the relationship. Run regular activity through the account (automating transactions via a service like [getsequence.io](http://getsequence.io) can help establish flow).  
3. Prepare **3 months of business bank statements** showing strong deposits:  
   * **$10k+ monthly deposits** → targets up to **$100k BLOC**.  
   * **$50k+ total deposits across 3 months** (statements from another bank are acceptable) has produced **$100k BLOC** via in-person submission.  
4. Submit the **BLOC** through a Fifth Third **Business Relationship Manager (BRM)**:  
   * No Fifth Third checking relationship required for the BLOC itself.  
   * Can be done **100% virtually over email \+ DocuSign**, or in-person for the strongest push on higher limits.  
   * BRMs can process BLOCs across all 19 states (may refer to a local rep).  
5. **Sizing the ask:**  
   * Under **$50k** \= easiest "no-doc" path (statements still required).  
   * **$50k–$100k** \= expect possible tax returns; some BRMs have approved up to $100k on statements alone when deposits clearly support it.  
6. Eligibility floor: **2+ years time in business** and good personal credit. Bureau pulled: **Experian (EX)**.  
7. **Business credit card is a separate flow** — it requires **\~90 days of Fifth Third business checking history** and depends on transaction activity. Do not bundle this ask with the BLOC.

## **🗣️ What to Say / What to Enter (application scripting)**

* **Requested product:** Business Line of Credit (BLOC). Keep BCC as a later, separate application once checking history is built.  
* **Purpose of funds:** Working capital / short-term operating needs (payables smoothing, inventory, payroll).  
* **Business description:** Stable operating business with consistent monthly cash flow; emphasize recurring deposits shown on statements.  
* **Requested amount:**  
  * Stay **under $50k** for a no-doc path.  
  * Only request up to **$100k** if 3 months of statements clearly support it ($10k+ monthly or $50k+ across 3 months).  
* **Reference application data (network datapoint, Corporation):**  
  * Estimated Monthly Spend: $9,800  
  * Annual Business Revenue: $340,000  
  * Total Annual Income: $225,000  
* **Must avoid:**  
  * Applying before the 90-day seasoning when you opened the account to build the relationship.  
  * Requesting above $50k without statements that visibly support the number.
