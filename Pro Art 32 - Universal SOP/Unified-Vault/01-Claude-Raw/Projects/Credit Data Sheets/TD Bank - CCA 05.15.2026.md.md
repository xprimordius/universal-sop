### **🧾 How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

1. **Walk into a TD branch in person** (East Coast footprint) and open a **TD business checking** account with the banker.  
2. **Deposit $5k–$10k** at account opening.  
3. **Same visit, ask the banker to apply for a Business Line of Credit (BLOC)** — request **$25k–$35k** (non-doc lane is under $25k; up to $100k is possible on stronger files).  
4. **In parallel, apply for the TD business credit card** with the same banker.  
5. Tell the banker **you have filed your taxes** and hand over any requested bank statements.  
6. For **brand-new LLCs (\<1 year)**: request up to **$25k** BLOC — this is the documented non-doc hack.  
7. For **established businesses**: you can alternatively apply **online** for up to **$250k**.  
8. Final approval for sub-$25k BLOCs is handled by a **local branch call** — answer promptly.

### **🚚 Optional — Truck/Auto Loan Lane**

1. Source a truck from **Arrow Truck Sales** or **Ryder**.  
2. Bring the **invoice** to your TD banker.  
3. Expect **25% down**; new businesses can get up to **$100k**.

### **🗣️ What to Say / What to Enter (application scripting)**

* **Business description:** Established operating business (corporation/LLC) with consistent monthly deposits and recurring client revenue.  
* **Purpose of funds:** Working capital for inventory, payroll smoothing, and short-term operational needs (BLOC). For the card: day-to-day business expenses.  
* **Requested product:** Business Line of Credit $25k–$35k \+ Business Credit Card.  
* **Reference financial profile (if asked, align to file):**  
  * Company structure: Corporation / LLC  
  * Estimated monthly spend: \~$9,600  
  * Annual business revenue: \~$290,000  
  * Total annual personal income: \~$225,000  
* **Must say:** “I have filed my taxes.” Mention the **active TD business checking relationship** and opening deposit.  
* **Must avoid:**  
  * Do not request over $50k unless file supports it — **TD files a UCC above $50k** (sometimes lower).  
  * Do not rely on the TD **business credit card for personal credit protection** — **biz CCs report to personal credit** (BLOCs do **not** report).  
* **Thin-file backup:** If client has a TD relationship but a thin profile, a **TD personal loan up to $15k** is available and useful for building comparable loan tradelines.

### **📇 Contacts**

* No verified direct contacts on file. Use the **local branch banker** at account opening as the execution contact; capture their name, direct line, and email at the meeting.

### **📌 Working datapoints snapshot**

* **Footprint:** East Coast only  
* **Channel:** In-branch with banker (primary); online for established businesses up to $250k  
* **Checking relationship:** Required (open business checking)  
* **Deposit:** $5,000 minimum recommended ($5k–$10k ideal)  
* **Deposit time:** 0 days (apply same visit)  
* **RM needed?:** No (branch banker, not a dedicated RM)  
* **BLOC limits:** Non-doc under $25k; typical walkouts $25k / $35k / $50k; up to $100k on stronger files  
* **UCC:** Filed above $50k (sometimes lower)  
* **Reporting:** Business credit card **reports to personal**; BLOC does **not** report  
* **Truck/Auto lane:** Up to $100k new biz, 25% down, invoice required
