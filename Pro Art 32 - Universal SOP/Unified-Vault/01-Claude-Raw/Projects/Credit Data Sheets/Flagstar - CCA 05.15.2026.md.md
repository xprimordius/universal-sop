## **How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

### **Path A — Card first (standard, most recent wins)**

1. Confirm fit: business is in Flagstar footprint; clean background (no bankruptcies, no criminal record); biz revenue typically $270k+ (wins observed up to $1M+); total personal income $170k–$225k.  
2. Apply online for the Flagstar business credit card (online app confirmed in 10/30 and 11/24 datapoints).  
3. No personal or business checking is required on this path (confirmed 12/31).  
4. Enter Corporation as the company structure and keep stated numbers consistent with statements.  
5. Expected outcome: standard approvals $11k–$15k (12k CC win reported 11/24). No UCC lien.

### **Path B — BLOC (statement-driven, up to $100k)**

1. Open a Flagstar relationship account and deposit \~$1,000.  
2. Season the relationship \~28–30 days.  
3. Prepare 3–4 months of bank statements showing $20k+ monthly deposits. Personal statements may be requested.  
4. Apply for the BLOC **through a banker/RM** — relationship \+ account required for BLOC (12/31 datapoint).  
   * Treat underwriting as equivalent to Citizens: verification and documentation sensitivity is high.  
5. Budget for a $250 origination fee on the BLOC.

## **What to Say / What to Enter**

* **Company structure:** Corporation.  
* **Business description:** Operating business located in Flagstar footprint.  
* **Estimated monthly spend:** \~$7,900 (benchmark datapoint; adjust to reality).  
* **Annual business revenue:** Use true figures. Wins observed at $270k, $420k–$450k, and $1M+. Make sure everything lines up across stated income, statements, and application.  
* **Total annual income (personal):** Wins observed between $170k and $225k.  
* **Purpose of funds:** Working capital / operating expenses (CC or BLOC as applicable).  
* **Must avoid:** Any bankruptcy history or criminal record — will be declined.  
* **Pre-submission check:** Expect a very detailed application; sanity-check numeric consistency (revenue, spend, income) before submitting.
