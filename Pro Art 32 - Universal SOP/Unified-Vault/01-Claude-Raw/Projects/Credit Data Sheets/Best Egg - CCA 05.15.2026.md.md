## **🏦 How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

1. Apply online at [BestEgg.com](http://BestEgg.com) — no RM, no branch visit, no-doc per datapoint.  
2. Check for a pre-approval offer first (soft pull). Prosper (sister brand) also offers pre-approval — can be checked in parallel.  
3. Complete the online application with clean, verifiable identity \+ address details.  
4. If prompted, upload a recent bank statement for address verification.  
5. Accept the offer. Reported outcomes land in the $10k–$30k range depending on profile strength.

## **🗣️ What to Say / What to Enter (Application scripting)**

* **Employer:** Enter the employer exactly as it appears on the credit report — matching this has been tied to a $30k approval datapoint.  
* **Income:** Use a realistic figure. Datapoints show approvals at $30k with income under $120k, so high income is not required.  
* **Purpose of funds:** Best Egg is a personal loan — keep the purpose simple and standard (e.g., debt consolidation, home improvement).  
* **Address verification:** Have a recent bank statement ready with the matching current address.  
* **Must avoid:** Inflated income, or an employer entry that does not match the credit report.
