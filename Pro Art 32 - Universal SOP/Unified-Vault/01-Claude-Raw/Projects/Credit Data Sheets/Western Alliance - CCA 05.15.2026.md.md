## **How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

1. Confirm in-footprint location (reported: CA, NV, NY, NJ — verify current footprint before engaging).  
2. Open/establish an RM relationship at a local Western Alliance branch. Do **not** apply online — this is a banker-driven BLOC play.  
3. Prepare docs (expectation is tightening):  
   * Minimum: most recent business tax return (at least 1 filed; can be under 2 years old).  
   * Have additional docs (bank statements, P\&L) ready — docs are reportedly being requested more frequently.  
4. Build the file before applying:  
   * Use an aged corp where possible.  
   * Establish basic business credit (NET30 tradelines) — \~60 days of buildup has been enough in past datapoints.  
   * Keep all accounts consistent (same legal name, same address, same EIN across filings, bureaus, and bank).  
   * Ensure a clean business credit report with a verified address.  
5. Ask the RM to submit for a Business Line of Credit (BLOC).  
6. Size expectations (verify case-by-case):  
   * \~$100k: low-doc path (last tax return). Historically referenced with 180+ SBSS.  
   * Up to \~$250k: 1 year tax return \+ 190+ SBSS.  
   * Underwriting sizes at \~20–25% of annual gross revenue.

## **What to Say / What to Enter (Application scripting)**

* **Business description:** Established operating business in \[industry\]; \[X\] years in operation; consistent monthly revenue of $\[amount\]; clean books with filed tax return(s).  
* **Purpose of funds / product requested:** Business Line of Credit for working capital, inventory, and operational cash-flow smoothing.  
* **Revenue framing:** Anchor the BLOC request at roughly 20–25% of annual gross revenue — do not ask materially above that band on a low-doc file.  
* **Must say:**  
  * Emphasize the RM relationship and desire to grow the banking relationship.  
  * State that the most recent business tax return is available on request.  
* **Must avoid:**  
  * Do not pitch this as a stated-income file — not tested with lower-quality clients, and docs are trending tighter.  
  * Do not apply online or cold-call the 800 number.
