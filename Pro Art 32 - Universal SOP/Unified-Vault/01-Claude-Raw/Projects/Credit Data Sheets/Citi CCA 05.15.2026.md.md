### **🛠️ How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

**Product focus:** AAdvantage Business card (primary) \+ personal loan (secondary). No 0% business cards currently. Low-doc BLOC is available but requires 3 months of bank statements.

#### **Path A — AAdvantage Business card (primary lane)**

1. Confirm eligibility before applying:  
   * 3+ years personal credit history (thin files are getting denied).  
   * No large Barclays balances on file (Barclays portfolio is migrating to Citi in 2026 — high Barclays balances have cut starting limits from \~$16k down to \~$1,500).  
2. Apply **online** through Citi's business card application — no RM required.  
3. Enter **stated revenue $100K–$200K** ($1,000–$2,000/mo range). This has been producing a consistent **$16,000 starting limit** Jan–April 2026\.  
4. Use **SSN only** when possible — $20k SL datapoint confirmed at 838 score with no EIN.  
5. Expect an **Equifax-only** pull for the business application.  
6. Card arrives in \~**3 business days** (improved from 2+ weeks). Set up Apple Pay immediately.

#### **Path B — Citi personal loan (secondary lane)**

1. Must already be a Citi member (Citi card **or** Citi checking account) — non-members cannot apply.  
2. Seasoning: need **12+ months** of card/checking history to qualify. $40k approval datapoint was on a 3-year personal history.  
3. Run **pre-approval first** — no credit pull at this stage.  
4. Accept the offer only if terms work. **Hard inquiry (Experian) hits after acceptance.**  
5. Typical sizing: up to **$35k**, with $40k achievable on stronger files.

#### **Stacking note (same-day personal \+ business)**

* Clean bureau split works: **Equifax on business, Experian on personal** — apply both same day for one-day hit.

---

### **🗣️ What to Say / What to Enter (application scripting)**

* **Business description:** Keep it straightforward and underwriting-friendly (e.g., consulting / services / e-commerce as applicable to the client). Avoid anything that reads as high-risk or startup-stage.  
* **Stated revenue (AAdvantage Business):** $100K–$200K annual (equivalent to $1,000–$2,000/mo). This is the band currently producing $16k SL.  
* **Identification:** Prefer **SSN-only** where the form allows; EIN is not required for the $20k+ SL datapoint.  
* **Purpose of funds (personal loan):** Debt consolidation or home/business improvement — standard Citi-friendly phrasing.  
* **Must avoid:** Applying for the personal loan without an existing Citi relationship (it will be blocked). Applying with a thin (\<3 yr) credit file. Applying if client carries a large Barclays balance (expect a gutted SL).
