The 5-card play is stronger than we had documented. Real datapoint showed 5 BofA biz cards on one LLC for $48K total, then 10 days later 5 more cards on a second LLC for $25K total. Came out to $73K across 10 cards. The key part is BofA only hit with 1 hard inquiry on TransUnion across both rounds because they were done inside the same 30-day window. So if you have multiple entities, you can stack applications within that window and minimize bureau damage. Way better than the "3 different 0% cards" language we had before. Limit transfers work but carry some risk. Confirmed that limit transfers have been done successfully, but in one case BofA messed it up and part of the credit didn't land correctly on the destination card. The takeaway isn't "never do it" but consolidate selectively, not recklessly, and keep some cards open if you're still trying to hit all the signup bonuses.

## **🧠 How to Apply (Step-by-step)**

1. Open **BoA business checking in person** at a branch. In-person openings consistently outperform online (immediate Platinum status, more approvals, ability to move limits).  
2. Deposit **$5,000+** and let it season **14+ days minimum**. Stronger outcomes with **60–90 days** of reasonable balances. Higher tier: $15k deposit. BLOC tier (rare): ≥$100k parked.  
3. Ideal profile: **LLC aged 2+ years**. Sole prop with EIN can work for some cards (e.g., Alaska Atmos auto-approved).  
4. Use BoA's **online scheduler** to book an RM appointment (you can book on behalf of the client) OR apply in branch. Avoid applying online for LLC entities — online LLC apps trigger requests for tax returns and business license.  
5. **Apply for all cards in the same session / same 30-day window** to minimize bureau hits. Confirmed datapoint: 5 cards on one LLC \+ 5 more on a second LLC inside 30 days produced only **1 TransUnion hard inquiry** across both rounds.  
   * Card sequence: Travel Rewards → Customer Cash Rewards → Unlimited Rewards → Platinum → Alaska.  
   * Historical internal rate: if card \#1 approved → 92% on \#2, 84% on \#3. If \#3 approves, nearly all clients get \#4 and \#5.  
6. **Do NOT apply for BLOC within 6 months** of opening the checking account — denials reported.  
7. **Post-approval:** consolidate limits to one primary 0% card. **Stagger closures** — BofA is stricter than Chase (roughly one closure per month over \~3 months). After the 0% term ends, reapply for a previously closed product to reset 0%.

**Velocity rules (active):**

* 2 new BofA cards / 2 months  
* 3 / 12 months (3/12 soft wall)  
* 4 / 24 months  
* 7/12 hard wall  
* 24-month rule for welcome bonus eligibility on business cards
