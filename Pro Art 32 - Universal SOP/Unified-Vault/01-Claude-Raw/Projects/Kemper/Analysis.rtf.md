1	"We can't cover non-related claim expenses, especially 6-7 months after the accident"	Cal. Civ. Code § 3333 — all detriment proximately caused. Time gap is irrelevant; causation chain is unbroken.	
2	"Florida trip is just a new travel, same as Florida — not related to the accident"	Foreseeability locked in April 24 email; pre-disclosed Miami relocation + cross-country drive. Causation is direct from carrier-induced delay.	
3	"Your vehicle is your business — you pay your own work trips"	Irrelevant to liability analysis. Who normally pays doesn't matter — what matters is who caused the trip to require substitute mode.	
4	"You have an actual physical address — your car isn't your home"	Pre-loss configuration test under Reynolds — the vehicle's NORMAL USE included integrated mobile-residence function (custom platform + UV covers). Doesn't require it to be primary residence.	
5	"We just replace transportation utility — like seatbelts, not Ferrari with Tahoe"	Their own analogy proves the comparability standard. Reynolds requires comparable class/value/use — not generic transportation. A $70K Tesla doesn't get a $25K economy substitute.
