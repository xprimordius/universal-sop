# 6.13 - APW Constantine's Sales Structure.txt

> [!info] From project: [[_Project Info|8 Figure Sales Training - SCIO]]

Intro


Welcome, location, source, why us 
Strong frame (pressure-off agenda)




Discovery (Purpose: to get 
a) the information we need that is relevant to what we will pitch, 
b) reveal emotional leverage for the prospect to take action now, 
c) build rapport, and 
d) eliminate objections before the close) 


Main challenge (label with specific problem)


Background* (gather info/criteria for positioning/differentiation)
* Finances


Efforts* (current and past attempts to resolve) 
- what worked &didn’t work


How long?* (stretching pain)


Goals (monetary and non-monetary vision) 
- short term and long term


Partner


Escape (future pace current)
Timing/Logistics
Commitment (future pace future)
 
Prompt for missed information
Transition: summary -> certain statement (why us)  -> permission to pitch




Pitch (Purpose: to 
a) show the prospect, by linking all our deliverables to solving their pains/goals, how this is ideal 
path to their outcome, and 
b) give them clarity and certainty on the process)


Summarize high level outcome
Give background/social proof to create certainty


Step 1 (callback, refer back to discovery) - solution - benefit (“so that what that means is…”)
Prompt for questions -  (”What questions do you have on that specifically”)


Step 2 (callback, refer back to discovery) - solution - benefit (“so that what that means is…”)
Prompt for questions -  (”What questions do you have on that specifically”)


Step 3 (callback, refer back to discovery) - solution - benefit (“so that what that means is…”)


Confidence social proof statement/wrap-up


Thoughts on process / rank on scale / why / buy-in / prospect self-sells
Next steps / future pace (x->y->z)


Investment (value stack -> price drop -> silence)


Close


Questions: Answer all + assume the sale
Objections: Isolate + solve
