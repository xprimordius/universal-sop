The beginning
Small Talk
This is the first part in every call:
“XXXX, a pleasure to meet you!”

paper, something to take notes with?”

Setting the tone
Here is where we set the tone of the conversation by communicating the
purpose of the call and how it will be divided:
"Alright - we’ll divide this call in 3 parts."
“First I’ll focus on you and ask you some questions about your business and
where you guys are at the moment. Mainly I want to understand if and how we
can help you achieve your goals."
"Second, if everything looks good and I feel like we can help, I’ll walk you
through all the fancy stuff you might want to see in terms of how it all works,
what we do and how we can help you achieve your goals.”
“Lastly, If you like what we have and want to move forward then we can discuss
potential next steps, sounds good?"

North Star
Once we have communicated the purpose and set the tone of the
conversation, we we’ll start with the first part of the conversation: The North Star.
Goals
First, we will ask questions to understand the what the prospect wants. What I
want to get from this question is quite simple — a logical detail (it has to be
measurable) and its emotional context.
“Excellent - tell me, what exactly/specifically/ are you looking to achieve by
working with us?”
 [Narrow down your questions to always know what you’re looking for and to help you
keep control of the frame through the call].
Here, they will either start giving you all of their life story OR nothing at all and
say something along the lines of…
“Nothing really, just curious about what you offer.”
When you bump into this, there’s 3 options. They either:
1. Talk too much and you have to loop them back to what exactly are they
looking to achieve by working with you.
2. Don’t want to open up and start asking you to go straight to the point, in
which case you’ll want to reframe them with something like this:
“Got you.”
“We’ll get into the needy greedy stuff in a bit, but first I need to understand
whether or not we can help and then I’ll be more than happy to walk you through
a personalized step by step on how we can help you achieve what you want.”

Future pacing
The whole purpose of future pacing in the sales call is to get them to believe it
is POSSIBLE. We will take the prospect to a place where they can feel and see their
goal being achieved and make it as real as it can get:
“Let’s assume we accept you in today and we can get you to XYZ… What would
you be able to do differently on your business that you can’t do today?”
[Your naturality and tonality will make this a smooth conversation rather than a robotic
and scripted one.]
OR
“What would you like to do that you might not be able to do today?”
Guess what? 90% of the time, you will get a bunch of generic answers such
as…
“It will be great! / It would be a blessing! / I haven’t even thought about it yet… /
I would be so relieved… / and many other filler/generic answers”
Go deeper! Every generic answer you get is a door that you must open to
discover what’s beneath the surface.
Clarify!
“Help me understand here, ‘cause “great, blessing, relief” can mean a world apart
for a lot of people — for example, for some business owners it could mean
throwing a team retreat somewhere in the Caribbean, for someone else it could
mean getting their family a nicer home), so, for you…”
“What would it mean to achieve XYZ? How would your family/team/partners feel
about you achieving XYZ?”
Loop them back to:
“For you personally, what kind of things would you like to do that you might not
be able to do today?”

Pre-Pitch
Note: We will work on this section of your pitch in our weekly group sessions
and also on 1on1 calls if need be.
The reason is that this will be custom-made for your own market and target
audience within your market.
The whole purpose of a pre-pitch is to build certainty and familiarity in the
prospect's mind in regards to your “process/systems/methods”, not your offer, but
in general, the place it all takes in the market as the top option for them.
As a rule of thumb, you always want to bring up a brief explanation of the
context of your service for people who are new to it.
Nothing too deep, but, just a bit of info to get them on the same page with
you.
Then, we always want to mention/show social proof to back up your
explanation and make it make sense.
Here’s an example of how we do it at Scaleclients.io:
“Cool — so, are you open to my feedback?”
“And look… btw this isn’t coming from some random theory, this is based on
over 100 clients who right now we are averaging:
67 calls in the first 30 days of working with a new client,
at a 25% close rate,
on an average offer price point of $3500/month,
This equates to about $56k in revenue in the first month…”

The Only Outcome Of Your Sales Calls
Your goal on every sales call is to guide people to make a DECISION by the
end of it. It doesn't matter if the decision is to not work with you BUT do make sure
they can give you a clear decision by the end of the call.
There are only 4 real outcomes:
1. You closed the deal! — They had no objections and wanted to go all in. You
take payment on the call and proceed to get them onboarded. It’s not a
closed deal until you have taken a payment.
2. Hard “No” — The prospect specifically tells you,
“No, this is not for me” .
OR
“I don't like you guys at all and there are better options out there”.
In which case, you need to go back to the drawing board and optimize your
offer.
A clear NO is infinitely better than a wishy-washy:
“Let’s revisit this is a couple of weeks/months/years”
or
“Let me think about it”.
Both of which are smokescreens to the REAL objections that you need to
diagnose and handle on the call.
3. Follow-up Call Booked — Genuine logistical issue, then you proceed to work
out a clear action plan with them and book a Follow-Up call to get them on
board.
‣ Only book a follow-up call if the prospect has a genuine reason why they
cannot move forward today. Any other excuse they give you is not a reason to

decision on the spot.
People have a natural tendency for running away from uncertainty/fear and

OBJECTION HANDLING
The exciting part of the call starts now! If there is anything I can guarantee you
it is that you are going to be presented with objections, otherwise, we would be
able to just sell our product/service on a website and get people to buy without
needing to talk to anyone.
The whole reason why we have sales calls is because people need help and
guidance in making big and important decisions. Which means that what you do
can potentially change their lives and/or businesses! Which is great!
So, buckle up and get ready to dive deep into the depths of human behavior
and learn how to HELP people overcome their own fears and excuses to finally
achieve what they truly want and need to level up.

These are the 2 smokescreens that you’ll get 99% of the time:
1. I need to think about it.
2. Let me speak with my partner.
The 4 steps to diffusing a smokescreen and getting to the real objection:
1. Isolate it — Identify which one is it (“partner” or “let me think about it”).
2. Neutralize it —
 “I understand and that’s not a problem!”
3. Put it on the side —
“Before you go away and talk to your wife/think about it…”
4. Discover the real objection —
“Can I ask you, what exactly is making you want to go and do that? Just to see if I
can help while we’re still on the call.”

3. LOGISTICS
Diagnosis: They want to move forward, BUT, they need to move funds around
