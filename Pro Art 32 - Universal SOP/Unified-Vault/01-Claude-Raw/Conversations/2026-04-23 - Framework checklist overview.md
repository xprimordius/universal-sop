## 🤖 Claude

**FRAME-SETTING · FIRST 60 SECONDS**

*Set this up front and the whole call runs easier. Skip it and you're chasing them the rest of the call.*

---

**THE FRAME (deliver right after the opener, before discovery)**

→  "So here's how this is gonna go real quick…"
→  "I'll ask you a few questions to understand your business and what you're trying to hit"
→  "If it sounds like we can actually help, I'll get you on a deep-dive with [closer] who builds the custom game plan"
→  "If not, I'll tell you straight up — no pressure either way"
→  "Sound good?"

---

**WHY EACH LINE WORKS**

→  "How this is gonna go" → you're in control, not them
→  "Few questions" → permission to qualify
→  "If it sounds like we can help" → pullback built in
→  "Deep-dive with [closer]" → next step is earned, not assumed
→  "If not, I'll tell you straight" → builds trust + lowers guard
→  "Sound good?" → micro-commitment, sets compliance tone

---

**THE SCRIPT (natural delivery)**

"Cool — so real quick, here's how this works: I'm gonna ask you a few questions about your business, what you're trying to hit, what's getting in the way. If it sounds like we can actually help you, I'll get you on a deeper call with [closer] who'll build a custom game plan for your specific situation. If it's not a fit, I'll tell you straight — no pressure. Cool?"

---

**THE 4 THINGS THE FRAME INSTALLS**

→  **Authority** — you're running the call, not them
→  **Permission** — to ask direct questions
→  **Pullback** — "if it's a fit" lets you disqualify freely
→  **Compliance** — the "cool?" trains them to agree

---

**WHAT IT KILLS**

→  "What do you guys do?" objections
→  Feeling like a sales call
→  Pushback on personal questions
→  Dead-end "I'll think about it" at the end

---

**RULE**

→  Frame delivered casually, not scripted
→  Under 20 seconds
→  Always end with a micro-yes ("sound good?" / "cool?")
