**On the question itself:** The card doesn't say. Nothing on it indicates insurance, bonding, or licensing — just name, services, and contact info.

**To find out, you have to ask her directly.** Text or call and request:

- **General liability insurance** — covers damage to your home (broken items, water damage, etc.). Ask for a Certificate of Insurance (COI) naming you as certificate holder.
- **Bonding** — protects against theft by her or her workers. Ask if she carries a janitorial/cleaning services bond and the bond amount.
- **Workers' comp** — if she has employees (not just solo). Protects you if a worker gets injured on your property and tries to sue you.

**Suggested text to send:**

> "Hi Alejandra, before booking I wanted to confirm — are you insured and bonded? If so, can you send a copy of your Certificate of Insurance and bond? Thanks!"

A legitimate pro will send the COI within a day. Hesitation, excuses, or "I'll get back to you" is the answer.

**Next move — pick one:**
- **A.** Draft a fuller vetting checklist (insurance, references, scope, pricing, key handling)
- **B.** Draft the text message ready to send
- **C.** Move on — you've got what you need
