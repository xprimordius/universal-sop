# Funny accounting and finance terms


---

## 👤 You

What’s a funny known accounting term

---

## 🤖 Claude

"Dead cat bounce" — when a stock drops hard, bounces briefly, then keeps falling. Named for the idea that even a dead cat will bounce if dropped from high enough.

A few other good ones: "jingle mail" (mailing keys back to the bank on a walkaway mortgage), "big bath" (a CEO dumping every possible loss into one brutal quarter so future quarters look great), and "ZZZZ Best" — not a term, but the name of Barry Minkow's fake carpet-cleaning empire, now a permanent fraud-case punchline.

## 🤖 Claude

**"Goodwill"** — the premium paid over a company's actual asset value when acquiring it. Essentially the accounting line for *"we paid extra because we liked the vibes."*

Other contenders:
- **"Double-entry bookkeeping"** — sounds vaguely scandalous, is actually 500 years old
- **"Amortization"** — nobody outside accounting knows what it means, everyone pretends they do
- **"Accruals"** — sounds like a skin condition
