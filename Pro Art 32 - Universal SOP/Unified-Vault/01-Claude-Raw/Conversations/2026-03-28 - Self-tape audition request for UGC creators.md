> [!info]- Conversation summary
> **Conversation Overview**
> 
> The person is building out a UGC (user-generated content) creator recruitment workflow, likely for a business funding/financial services client or campaign targeting medical practices. > 
> For the audition receipt response message, the person wanted appreciation, professionalism, clear next-step communication, and a soft rejection cushion that protects candidate dignity and the company's long-term reputation. Claude flagged that a specific sentence about non-selection was structurally awkward, and offered three cleaner rewrites for that section, with the person selecting their preferred option independently.
