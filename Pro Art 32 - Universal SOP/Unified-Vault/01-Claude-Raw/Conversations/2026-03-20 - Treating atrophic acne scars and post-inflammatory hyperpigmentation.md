Alan — here's a straight breakdown.

**What I'm seeing:** A mix of two things — (1) **atrophic acne scars** (rolling and boxcar type, the textured indentations) on forehead and cheeks, and (2) **post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH)** — the flat brown/pink marks where breakouts healed.

These are two different problems with different fixes.

---

**PIH (the discoloration) — faster to fix**

This is surface-level pigment that fades over time. You can accelerate it significantly:

**At-home (daily):**
- **Vitamin C serum** (L-ascorbic acid 15-20%) every morning — speeds pigment turnover
- **Niacinamide 5-10%** — reduces melanin transfer, calms redness
- **Retinol or tretinoin** at night — gold standard for cell turnover. Tretinoin (prescription, 0.025-0.05% to start) is stronger and faster than OTC retinol
- **SPF 30+ daily, non-negotiable** — UV re-darkens every mark and undoes all progress

Timeline with consistent use: visible improvement in 4-8 weeks, major improvement by 3-4 months.

**In-office accelerator:** A series of **chemical peels** (glycolic or TCA) can cut that timeline significantly.

---

**Atrophic scars (the texture/indentations) — requires professional treatment**

Topicals alone won't fix these. The collagen is physically lost. You need to stimulate deep collagen remodeling:

**Most effective options (ranked by impact):**

| Treatment | What It Does | Sessions | Downtime | Results |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Microneedling + PRP** | Stimulates collagen via controlled micro-injury + growth factors | 3-6 sessions, 4-6 wks apart | 2-3 days redness | Significant improvement over 3-6 months |
| **Fractional CO2 laser** | Ablative resurfacing, strongest single-treatment option | 1-3 sessions | 5-7 days | Dramatic improvement, most aggressive |
| **RF Microneedling** (Morpheus8, Vivace) | Heat + needles = deeper collagen remodel | 3-4 sessions | 2-3 days | Great for rolling scars specifically |
| **TCA CROSS** | High-concentration TCA acid applied directly into individual scars | 3-6 sessions | Minimal | Best for deep boxcar/ice pick scars |
| **Subcision** | Breaks up scar tissue tethered underneath | 1-3 sessions | Bruising 5-7 days | Releases rolling scars to allow skin to flatten |

**Fastest realistic path:** Combine **subcision + microneedling/RF microneedling** for the textured scars, while running a daily retinoid + vitamin C + SPF protocol for the pigmentation. A dermatologist can do both in the same visit cycle.

---

**Immediate action steps:**

1. Book a consult with a **board-certified dermatologist** (not a med spa aesthetician — you want someone who can prescribe tretinoin and perform subcision/laser)
2. Start **SPF 30+** daily right now — this alone prevents the marks from getting worse
3. Add **vitamin C serum AM + niacinamide** while you wait for the appointment
4. Ask the derm about **tretinoin 0.025%** to start

This is very fixable. The scarring pattern you have responds well to treatment — rolling scars especially improve with microneedling and subcision. Most people see 50-70% improvement over a 6-month treatment cycle, with continued improvement for up to a year after.
