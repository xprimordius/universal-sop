What actually saves money at Zara:
- **Special Prices section** on zara.com — year-round markdowns 30–50%
- **Free shipping** on orders $70+ (non-discounted items), or free pickup at any Zara store
- **Semi-annual sales** — June and December, discounts up to 80%
- **App/email signup** — first access to sale events; some report a small birthday-month discount
