**Bottom line:** meaningful but not the biggest lever. Real commercial > residential > virtual/CMRA flagged.

**Rough impact on unsecured business funding (cards, LOCs, no-doc products):**

- **Real commercial address** (verifiable, not flagged): +20–35% approval probability, +25–40% on initial limits vs residential
- **Residential**: baseline — works fine at many banks, especially with strong personal credit and deposits
- **Virtual office / CMRA / flagged Regus-type**: *worse than residential.* Triggers auto-declines at Chase, Amex, US Bank, BoA business. LexisNexis and Experian Business databases tag these.

**For your 30–60 day Blitz sprint, the bigger levers are:**
1. EIN seasoning + 411 listing
2. Business bank deposits / relationship age
3. Personal credit utilization at app time
4. Industry NAICS code
5. Stacking sequence (soft-pull banks first)

**Practical call given your time crunch:** if you can't lock a clean non-flagged commercial in the next 7 days, **run the sprint from residential.** Don't take a virtual office to "look better" — it actively hurts you. Add real commercial in wave 2 once you find one.
