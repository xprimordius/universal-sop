**MCA § 32-1-211 — Examination and Supervision**
DBFI's core mandate. ## Administrative Procedure Standards

**MCA § 2-4-704 — Standards of Review (Judicial Review)**
If DBFI's closure were challenged in court, a reviewing court can reverse if agency findings are: (i) in violation of constitutional or statutory provisions, (ii) in excess of statutory authority, (iii) made upon unlawful procedure, (iv) affected by other error of law, (v) clearly erroneous in view of the reliable, probative, and substantial evidence on the whole record, or (vi) arbitrary or capricious. **MCA § 2-4-623 — Final Decisions**
