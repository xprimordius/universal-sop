# Tony Robbins' 10-minute morning priming routine

> [!info]- Conversation summary
> **Conversation Overview**
> 
> The person shared two Tony Robbins video transcripts about his daily "priming" routine and asked Claude to build a guided morning ritual that requires zero thinking or memorization. The core request was to make the morning as effortless as possible, with everything pre-decided and guided so no mental effort is needed in the moment.
> 
> The conversation worked through confirming that the Tony Robbins priming video (the second transcript, titled "Priming: the daily habit Tony Robbins uses to boost his brain") serves as a complete, fully guided Step 1 of the morning ritual. The person initially questioned whether the video covered prayer and gratitude, and Claude confirmed after re-reading the transcript that it explicitly covers all three segments: breathwork, gratitude, and a prayer/blessing/light-sending sequence, plus the "3 to Thrive" victory visualization and heart stacking. The video runs approximately 15 minutes and requires nothing from the person except pressing play and following Tony's voice.
> 
> The person then shared important lifestyle preferences that shaped the final ritual structure: they prefer slow mornings, enjoy working from bed as it makes them feel productive and comfortable, and do their walking on a walking pad mid-day at 2.2–2.6mph for two hours to reach 10,000 steps, noting it produces sweat and feels like deep intensive work. Claude validated this approach and structured the updated ritual accordingly: priming video first thing in the morning (before opening phone or laptop to work), then comfortable bed-based work, then the walking pad session mid-day, then physical workout later. The one sequencing rule established was that the priming video must happen before work begins, even from bed.
