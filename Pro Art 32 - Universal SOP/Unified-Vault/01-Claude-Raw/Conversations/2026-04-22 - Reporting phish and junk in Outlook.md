**Desktop (New Outlook / Web)**
- Select the email → **Home** tab → **Report** dropdown → choose **Report phishing** or **Report junk**
- Or right-click the email → **Report** → **Phishing** / **Junk**

**Desktop (Classic Outlook)**
- Select the email → **Home** tab → **Junk** dropdown → **Junk** or **Phishing**

**Mobile (iOS/Android)**
- Open the email → tap the **⋯** (three dots) top right → **Report Junk** → choose **Junk** or **Phishing**

Phishing = malicious/scam. Junk = unwanted/spam. Reporting either moves it out of inbox and trains the filter.
