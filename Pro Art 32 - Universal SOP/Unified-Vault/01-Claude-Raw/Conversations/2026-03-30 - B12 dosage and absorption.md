> [!note]- Claude's thinking
> Alan is asking about the Solaray Liposomal Multivitamin for Men, specifically why it contains 1,000 mcg of B12 (41,667% DV).


Two reasons:

**Low absorption rate.** Even liposomal B12 (methylcobalamin) has limited bioavailability — your body typically absorbs only a small fraction of oral B12. Mega-dosing ensures enough actually gets into circulation.

**No toxicity risk.** B12 is water-soluble — your body excretes what it doesn't need. There's no established upper limit (UL) from the IOM/NAM, so manufacturers dose aggressively without safety concern.

1,000 mcg is actually standard across most quality multivitamins and standalone B12 supplements. Some go to 5,000 mcg. The 41,667% DV looks alarming but it's based on a Daily Value of only 2.4 mcg — which is the *minimum to prevent deficiency*, not the optimal therapeutic dose.

**For you specifically:** Given your dextroamphetamine use, high-output lifestyle, and performance goals, robust B12 status supports methylation, energy metabolism, and neurotransmitter synthesis. This dose is a positive, not a concern.

## 🤖 Claude

> [!note]- Claude's thinking
> Alan is pointing out that since it's liposomal, the absorption rate should be much higher than standard oral B12 — so the mega-dose argument based on poor absorption doesn't hold as well. He's right to push back on that.


Good catch — you're right to push back.

Liposomal delivery bypasses the normal absorption bottleneck (intrinsic factor limitation, gut transport). Absorption rates jump significantly — some estimates put liposomal at 3-5x standard oral bioavailability.

So the "you need to mega-dose because absorption is poor" argument weakens considerably here.

**The real answer:** 1,000 mcg is just the industry-standard dose that's been used for decades, and since there's zero toxicity risk, manufacturers have no reason to lower it. It's convention, not optimization. You'll urinate out what you don't use.

**Bottom line:** It's not harmful, but it's also not giving you 41,667% of your actual needs. Your body will take what it wants and flush the rest. Non-issue.

## 🤖 Claude

> [!note]- Claude's thinking
> He's asking what B12 does. Keep it concise, relevant to his goals.


**Core functions:**

**Energy production** — essential cofactor in converting food to ATP. Low B12 = fatigue, brain fog.

**Methylation** — drives the methylation cycle, which controls detox, neurotransmitter production (dopamine, serotonin), DNA repair, and gene expression. Critical for you given the amphetamine use — your methylation demand is elevated.

**Nervous system** — maintains myelin sheath (nerve insulation). Deficiency causes neuropathy, tingling, cognitive decline.

**Red blood cell formation** — prevents megaloblastic anemia (oversized, inefficient red blood cells that carry less oxygen).

**Mood & cognition** — directly supports dopamine and serotonin synthesis. Deficiency linked to depression, memory issues, poor focus.

**DNA synthesis** — needed for every cell your body makes, including muscle repair and immune cells.

**Why it matters for your stack:** B12 is the engine behind methylation. Methylation is upstream of almost everything you care about — energy, cognition, mood stability, recovery, detox. It's foundational, not flashy.
