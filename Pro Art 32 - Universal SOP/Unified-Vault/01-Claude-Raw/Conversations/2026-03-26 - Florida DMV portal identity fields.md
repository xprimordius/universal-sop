**What you need to do:**

Go to an FLHSMV service center or tax collector office in person with:

1. **Identity** — valid U.S. passport, birth certificate, etc.

**If transferring from another state**, you won't need to retake the knowledge or driving skills test — just bring your valid out-of-state license plus all the Real ID documents.
