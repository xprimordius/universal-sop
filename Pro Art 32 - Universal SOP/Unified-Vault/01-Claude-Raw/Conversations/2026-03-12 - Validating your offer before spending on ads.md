## 👤 You

I’m concerned that once I start running ads I’m not gonna make sales and I’m just gonna waste a bunch of ad spend and be broke

---

## 🤖 Claude

That fear is real — and it's worth addressing directly before you spend a dollar.

## The Core Risk You're Actually Facing

You're not afraid of ads. You're afraid of an **unvalidated offer hitting cold traffic** with no feedback loop and a ticking clock.

That's a legitimate concern. Here's how to think about it clearly.

---

## What Actually Causes Ad Spend to Burn With No Sales

1. **Offer-market fit isn't proven** — the message doesn't hit the pain hard enough
2. **VSL or call hook doesn't convert** — people opt in but don't show or don't close
3. **Sales process leaks** — closes aren't happening on calls
4. **Wrong audience** — targeting creative isn't pulling the right people
5. **Too little data before optimizing** — killing ads before they have a chance

---

## What's Actually in Your Control Right Now

| Variable | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Offer clarity | Strong | Lock the pitch, don't keep tweaking |
| Sales ability | Your strength | Be on every call yourself first |
| VSL / hook | Needs traffic to test | Start with smallest viable spend |
| Show rate | SCIO's job | Confirm their follow-up sequence is dialed |
| Close rate | Your job | Prep objection responses cold |

---

## The Real Protection

**Don't fund ads you can't afford to lose.** Set a hard test budget — say $3K–$5K — treat it as market research, not guaranteed revenue. If you close 1 of 10 calls at $10K, you're profitable before Month 1 ends.

Your break-even isn't far. The math is forgiving if your close rate is even moderate.

---

## Honest Bottom Line

The fear is valid but it's also **a signal to get sharper on calls, not to wait longer.** The market will tell you what's broken faster than any planning session will.

What feels most uncertain right now — the offer, the sales process, or the traffic?
