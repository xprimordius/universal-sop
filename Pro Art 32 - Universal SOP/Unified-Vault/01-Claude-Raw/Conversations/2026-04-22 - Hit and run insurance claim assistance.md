**Page 1, "Costs and other expenses" paragraph — specifically this sentence:**

> *"If a recovery is obtained, Client shall pay from the settlement **after the fee is calculated** all costs incurred by Attorney…"*

The trigger is the four-word phrase **"after the fee is calculated."** That locks the fee to the **gross** settlement, not the net.

**Math tied directly to that language** — $100K settlement, $8K costs:

| Step | Amount |
|---|---|
| Settlement | $100,000 |
| Fee calculated *first* (40% of gross, per the contract) | −$40,000 |
| Remainder | $60,000 |
| Costs paid *after* (also per the contract) | −$8,000 |
| **You net** | **$52,000** |

If the clause instead read *"after costs are deducted"* (client-favorable):

| Step | Amount |
|---|---|
| Settlement | $100,000 |
| Costs deducted first | −$8,000 |
| Net recovery | $92,000 |
| Fee (40% of net) | −$36,800 |
| **You net** | **$55,200** |

**Delta: $3,200 on this case. It scales linearly with costs.** $20K costs → $8K loss. $40K costs (typical post-deposition, pre-trial) → $16K loss.
