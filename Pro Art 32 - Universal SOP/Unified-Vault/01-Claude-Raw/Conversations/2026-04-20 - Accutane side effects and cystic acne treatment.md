Hi Josephine — quick note first: happy to help on this, though medical decisions like Accutane should ultimately be made with your dermatologist. Here's a grounded overview so you can have a sharper conversation with them.

**Short answer:** For cystic acne that's failed spironolactone + doxycycline, isotretinoin (Accutane) is the standard next step and is often the right call. It's the only treatment that can produce long-term remission. Most people tolerate it well, but the side effect profile is real and needs respect.

**Why it likely fits your situation**
- Cystic/nodular acne causes permanent scarring if untreated
- Long-term antibiotics (doxy) aren't sustainable — resistance, gut microbiome impact
- Spiro + doxy is typically the line right before isotretinoin
- ~85% of patients get lasting clearance after one course

**Common side effects (most people get these)**
- Severely dry lips — universal, plan on Aquaphor constantly
- Dry skin, eyes, nose (nosebleeds common)
- Initial flare in first 4–6 weeks (can get worse before better)
- Muscle/joint aches, especially with exercise
- Sun sensitivity
- Elevated triglycerides, cholesterol, liver enzymes (monitored monthly via bloodwork)

**Serious but less common**
- **Teratogenicity — absolute.** Zero tolerance for pregnancy. iPLEDGE program requires 2 forms of birth control + monthly pregnancy tests
- Mood changes/depression — signal is debated but real for some; worth tracking
- Night vision changes
- Temporary hair thinning (usually reverses)
- Rare: pseudotumor cerebri (brain pressure)

**Critical for you specifically**
- **You must stop doxycycline before starting isotretinoin.** Combining tetracyclines with isotretinoin increases pseudotumor cerebri risk. This is non-negotiable.
- Spironolactone can usually be continued alongside isotretinoin and sometimes helps
- Expect 5–7 month course, dosed by weight
- Monthly derm visits + bloodwork

**Questions to bring to your derm**
1. Given spiro + doxy haven't cleared the cysts, are we at the isotretinoin threshold?
2. What's the washout plan for doxy before starting?
3. Continue or taper spiro during the course?
4. Starting dose and target cumulative dose for my weight?
5. Mental health monitoring plan?
