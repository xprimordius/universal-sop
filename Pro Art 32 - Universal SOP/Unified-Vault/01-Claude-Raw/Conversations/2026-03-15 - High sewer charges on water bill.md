## Bill Breakdown

---

## Why Sewer Is the Biggest Line Item

**Sewer: $96.34** (16,030 gal × $0.00601)

Sewer is billed on **total water consumed** — the assumption is whatever goes in comes out through the drain. So you're paying full volume twice: once for water in, once for wastewater out.

It's the largest single charge because it applies to **all 16,030 gallons**, while water is split across two tiers at lower rates.

---

## Is the Usage High?

16,030 gallons ÷ 56 days = **~286 gallons/day**

Average US household: ~80–100 gal/person/day. That implies roughly **3 people** at normal usage — or potentially a **slow leak** somewhere (toilet running, irrigation, etc.).

---

## The Bill Isn't Just Water/Sewer

You're also paying for:
- Garbage + recycling + organics: **~$63**
- Storm drainage: **$19.66**
- Taxes/fees: **~$12**

Nearly **$100 in non-water charges** bundled into one bill — that's why it feels inflated.

---

## Bottom Line

Not outrageous for a ~2-month Bloomington utility bill. The sewer feels high because it mirrors full usage. **Check for a running toilet or dripping fixture** — that's the most common culprit if usage seems high.

## Organics Recycling = Food Waste Program

Bloomington's organics recycling is a **separate bin (usually green or brown) for food scraps and organic waste** — things like vegetable peels, meat, leftovers, coffee grounds, etc. It gets composted instead of going to a landfill.

**$11.74/month** on your bill means you're subscribed to it.

---

## "We just have 1"

If you only have **1 person** in the household — the organics bin is probably overkill and generates very little. You can **cancel it** through Republic Services (952-941-5174) and save ~$11.74/mo.

If you meant you only have **1 bin total** — double-check which color it is. If it's not green/brown, you may be paying for a service you're not even using.

---

**Quick action:** Call Republic Services at **952-941-5174** and ask to remove organics recycling from your service if you don't actively use it.
