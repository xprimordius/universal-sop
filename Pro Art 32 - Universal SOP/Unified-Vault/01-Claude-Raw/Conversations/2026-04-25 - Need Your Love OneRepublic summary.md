**"Need Your Love" — OneRepublic (released April 3, 2026)**

Ryan Tedder's anthem about rejecting materialism in favor of real connection. The verses list what he *doesn't* need — credit-card buzz, the hilltop house, his name on the boulevard, a million dollars, a drink to fill the cup — and the chorus answers with the only thing that matters: "I need your love." Verse 3 turns reflective: aging gracefully, owning past mistakes, choosing one person for the long haul.

Core message: fame and money are hollow without someone to share them with. Co-written by Tedder with James Essien (BTS, ZAYN) — second single from their upcoming 7th studio album.
