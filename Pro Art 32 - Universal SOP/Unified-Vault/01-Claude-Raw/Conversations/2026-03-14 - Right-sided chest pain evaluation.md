## 🤖 Claude

Right, so the main possibilities for sharp right-sided chest pain that comes and goes are: musculoskeletal stuff like a strained muscle or inflamed rib cartilage, which is actually pretty common and usually harmless. Then there's pleurisy, which is inflammation of the lining around your lungs—that typically hurts more when you breathe or cough. You could have a pinched nerve or something spine-related. Less commonly it could be gallbladder issues, though that's usually more towards the upper right and often comes with other symptoms.

The thing is, without examining you and knowing more context—like whether it's worse when you move or breathe, if you've had any recent injury, fever, cough, or other symptoms—I genuinely can't narrow it down. Sharp chest pain that's new and intense enough to concern you really does deserve a professional look, even if it turns out to be nothing serious. When can you get to your doctor?
