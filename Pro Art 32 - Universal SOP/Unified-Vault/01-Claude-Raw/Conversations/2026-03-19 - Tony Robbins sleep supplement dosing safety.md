**Short answer: I'd hold off on the second dose tonight.**

The official Peak Rest dosing is start with ½ scoop, increase to a full scoop after 2-3 days if well-tolerated — with no mention of doubling beyond one full scoop.

A couple of things to consider:

**It's been ~2 hours and you're still up** — Peak Rest contains ashwagandha, Venetron, and Zylaria, which work on relaxation pathways rather than being a hard knockout like a sedative. They can take time, and stacking a second dose won't necessarily speed things up — it could just increase the chance of GI discomfort (the most commonly reported side effect).

**What might actually help right now:** dim all screens or switch to red-light mode, do a few minutes of slow box breathing (4-4-4-4), and let the first dose do its thing. Given your dextroamphetamine schedule (hard cutoff 2:30–3pm), if you took your last dose on time today, stimulant clearance shouldn't be the issue — but if it ran late, that could be the real blocker.

If this becomes a pattern where one scoop consistently isn't enough, that's worth flagging with your Lifeforce clinician when you do your bloodwork consult.
