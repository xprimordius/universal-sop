**Skip for budget-adult trips**

- Kalahari and Great Wolf Lodge — premium-priced, family/kid-heavy, day passes limited and expensive.
- Wilderness Resort — day passes exist but minors must be accompanied by a paid adult and passes are limited quantities, usually not the cheap play.

**Cheapest overall strategy**

Book a midweek (Sun–Thu) stay at Mt. Olympus or Grand Marquis — the free waterpark passes + low room rate beats buying day tickets separately, and gives you a home base.
