## APW — Offer Overview for Growth Partners

**Company:** Ascend Prime Wealth (APW)
**Category:** Business funding education — high-ticket coaching program
**What clients get:** The knowledge, strategy, and execution support to access $100K–$500K+ in unsecured business funding — without tax returns, income documentation, or paying a funding company.

---

## The Core Offer

**$10K / 4-month program**

Three pillars:
1. **Business Profile & Entity Setup** — LLC, EIN, business credit profile, banking deposits (Day 0)
2. **Personal & Business Credit Optimization** — fast-track dispute system (Experian: 24 hrs, full profile: ~1 week)
3. **Funding Sequence Execution** — bank research, data extraction, sequenced applications by round

**The engine:** Multi-round funding model. After each round, inquiries are removed, profile re-optimized, and the next round begins — each one larger than the last.

---

## Delivery Tracks

| Track | What It Is |
|-------|-----------|
| **DIY** | Client executes everything using course content |
| **DWY** (Done With You) | APW guides and reviews at each stage |
| **DFY** (Done For You) | APW executes the funding sequence on client's behalf |

DFY is the premium upsell. DWY is the core delivery track.

---

## What Makes It Different

- No tax returns or income docs required
- Credit optimization happens in days, not months (industry says 3–6 months — APW does it in 1 week or less in most cases)
- Banking deposits start Day 0 — relationship-building starts immediately, no time wasted
- Repeatable — clients can run multiple rounds, growing funding capacity each cycle
- No funding company fees — APW teaches clients to go direct

---

## KPIs (for ad/funnel context)

- **Target:** CPQBC ≤ $300 | 60%+ show rate | 25%+ close rate
- **Paid traffic via:** Meta ads (managed by ScaleClients.io / SCIO)
