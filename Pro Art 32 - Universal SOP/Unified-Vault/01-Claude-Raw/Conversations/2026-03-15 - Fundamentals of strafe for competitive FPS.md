## Fundamentals of Strafe — Master Summary

**Author:** Sam (@400apm) | Written for competitive FPS players (Apex/Quake context)

---

**Core Philosophy**
Strafing is concept-driven, not mechanical spam. The "artful dodger" — calm, intentional, lazy-looking — outperforms the reactive ad-spammer because lower variance = better prediction = better decisions.

---

**Key Concepts**

| Concept | Core Idea |
|---|---|
| **Strafeaim** | Match or oppose enemy direction to maximize damage — mirror when you have HP advantage, anti-mirror when not |
| **Global Bias** | Drift in one direction over time. Removes the "aim at the middle" exploit. Enables map relocation. |
| **Geometry** | Use space to limit your enemy's options. Corners, corridors, cover proximity — all create predictable behavior you can punish. |
| **Control** | Probe for weaknesses. Force your enemy to react predictably by making your movement hard to track. |
| **Simplification** | Your enemy isn't moving that fast. Your target isn't that small. Panic distorts perception — calmness reveals reality. |

---

**Practice Approach**
- One concept at a time, deliberately, in intensive environments (LG duel, KovaaK's, r5reloaded)
- Use a fake name to remove ego — experiment freely
- Don't copy better players mechanically — absorb their *feel*
- Ingame: no conscious thought. Theory must become intuition first.

---

**Source Priority**
This doc is explicitly an *intro* to AIMER7's guides (Strafe Aiming 101, Geometric Positioning, Tracer Guide). Read those for full technical depth.

## Lurch Strafing — Supplemental Summary (Zetto FPS)

**Source:** Video transcript by Zetto FPS, paired with the 400apm strafe mindset doc

---

**What is Lurch Strafing?**
A strafe technique where **0.2–0.5s after jumping**, you input directional keys to shift your hitbox using momentum. Goal: take less damage, spend less time healing, apply more pressure.

---

**Core Input Sequences**

| Direction | Input |
|---|---|
| Left → Right | A-jump + WDS |
| Right → Left | D-jump + WAS |

> Called the **WASD Strafe** — doable with or without tap strafe (tap strafe = much faster)

---

**Progression Ladder**

| Stage | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Beginner** | Action timing, no tap strafe — build clean reps |
| **Intermediate** | Combo lurches — understand distance/height/time before chaining |
| **Advanced** | Add crouch at lurch end → momentum boost toward shifted direction |
| **Advanced+** | Stay crouched → re-lurch with WAS or WDS = **figure-8 effect** |

> Figure-8 forces disciplined aim the average player doesn't have. Can commit to one side or reverse across their FOV.

---

**Key Rules**
- Only press the **necessary sequence** — extra inputs break the chain
- Don't lurch predictably — situational use only at advanced stage
- Master timing before comboing — misclicks kill your momentum entirely

---

**Coming in Series**
Yuki strafing, jump fatigue lurching, circle vs. ras-strafing, 1-key vs. 2-key lurches, movement penalty recognition

---

**Synthesis with 400apm doc:**
Lurch = the *mechanical tool*. Strafe mindset = *when and why to use it*. Neither alone is enough — technique without concept = predictable; concept without technique = slow.
