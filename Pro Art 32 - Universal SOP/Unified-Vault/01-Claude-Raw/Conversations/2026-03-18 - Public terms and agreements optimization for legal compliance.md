## APW-Specific Considerations

**What makes APW's position unique vs. these companies:**

1. **You're education + coaching on credit/funding** — not a lender, not a financial advisor, not a credit repair organization (CRO). This distinction matters for FTC and state AG compliance. You need to clearly disclaim CRO status to avoid triggering CROA (Credit Repair Organizations Act), which imposes specific requirements including a mandatory 3-day cancellation right.

2. **FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule + Business Opportunity Rule** — if APW's offer could be construed as a "business opportunity" (teaching people to access funding to start/grow businesses), there are FTC disclosure requirements. Public terms should preemptively address this.

3. **Minnesota governing law** — MN has strong consumer protection statutes (MN Stat. §325D, §325F). Your terms need to be reasonable enough to survive scrutiny under MN AG enforcement.

4. **Meta ads + special ad category** — your public terms page is the first thing a regulator, payment processor, or ad platform reviewer will check. It needs to be clean, professional, and fully compliant.
