## Selling Real Estate Without a License

The key legal distinction: **you can sell property you own, or sell your contractual interest in a property** — you just can't represent someone else for compensation without a license.

---

## The Main Strategies

### 1. Wholesaling (Assign the Contract)
- Get a property under contract (you = buyer)
- Sell/assign your **equitable interest** (the contract) to an end buyer for an assignment fee
- You never own the property — you own the **right to buy it**
- Assignment fee = your profit ($5K–$50K+ per deal)
- ⚠️ Some states are tightening laws on this — verify your state

### 2. Double Close (Back-to-Back)
- Close as the buyer (A→B), then immediately resell (B→C)
- You briefly own the property
- Cleaner legally than assignment in strict states
- Requires transactional funding (short-term lending, often 1–2 days)

### 3. Seller Financing / Subject-To
- Acquire property creatively, then resell on your terms
- No license needed — you're the owner selling your asset

---

## Pre-Market — How to Move It Before MLS

| Method | How It Works |
|---|---|
| **Cash buyer list** | Build/buy a list of active investors; pitch deals directly via text/email |
| **Facebook Groups** | Real estate investor groups — post deals directly |
| **Direct to investor network** | Relationships with flippers, landlords, developers in your market |
| **Driving for Dollars apps** | Find distressed properties, contact owners before they list |
| **Pocket listing** | Quietly market to your network before any public listing |
| **BiggerPockets / Connected Investors** | Marketplace platforms for off-market deals |
| **Cold outreach (SMS/email)** | Market to motivated sellers → get under contract → sell to buyers |

---

## The Stack That Works

**Motivated seller** (distressed, tired landlord, probate, divorce)
→ **You lock it up** at a discount via purchase agreement
→ **Market to your cash buyer list** before closing
→ **Assign or double close** for profit

No license. No MLS. No realtor fees.

---

## Key Risk to Know

If you're marketing a property you don't own or have under contract — **that can cross into unlicensed brokerage**. The contract is what protects you legally. Always be under contract before marketing the deal.
