## Nanny Tax — Plain Answer

**What it is:** Federal payroll taxes you owe when you hire someone to work in your home. "Nanny tax" is shorthand for three combined taxes:

| Tax | Rate | Who Pays |
|-----|------|----------|
| **FICA** (Federal Insurance Contributions Act — Social Security + Medicare) | 7.65% employer + 7.65% employee = **15.3%** | Both, split |
| **FUTA** (Federal Unemployment Tax Act) | 0.6% on first $7K | Employer only |
| **SUTA** (State Unemployment Tax — MN: ~1–2%) | ~1–2% on first ~$40K | Employer only |

**2026 thresholds (IRS Pub 926):**
- **$3,000+/yr** to one household employee → FICA triggers
- **$1,000+/quarter** → FUTA triggers
- Reported on **Schedule H** of your personal Form 1040

**Your nanny ($7,776 summer pay):** crosses both thresholds. Your true cost:

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Wages | $7,776 |
| Employer FICA (7.65%) | $595 |

| MN SUTA (~1.5% × $7K) | ~$105 |
| **All-in employer cost** | **~$8,518** |
| (Plus you withhold $595 from her checks for her FICA share) | |

## Can You Run Nanny Through Your LLC? **Short answer: No.**

**Why:** IRS treats childcare for your own kids as a **personal expense**, not a business expense. Running it through Skyward USA or APW = audit risk, deduction disallowed, penalties + back taxes.

**Two narrow exceptions:**
1. **Nanny actually works for the business** — answers phones, does admin, handles ops. Must have W-2 hours separate from childcare hours, with real timesheets. Childcare hours still personal.
2. **DCAP** (Dependent Care Assistance Program — Section 129 written plan): your LLC can sponsor one, employees (including you if you take W-2 wages) get up to **$5,000/yr pre-tax** for childcare. Requires formal plan doc + non-discrimination testing.

**Better legitimate strategies (Alan-specific):**

| Strategy | Tax Benefit | Fit |
|----------|-------------|-----|
| **Child & Dependent Care Credit** (personal 1040) | 20–35% credit on up to $3K (1 kid) or $6K (2+) | Easy, modest |
| **DCFSA** (Dependent Care FSA via Amanda's W-2 employer if she has one) | $5K pre-tax | Best if she has W-2 |
| **DCAP through Skyward USA** | $5K pre-tax to you | Requires plan setup, CPA work |
| **Hire your kids in the LLC** | Pay child-rate wages, lower bracket | Separate strategy, ages 7+ ideal |
