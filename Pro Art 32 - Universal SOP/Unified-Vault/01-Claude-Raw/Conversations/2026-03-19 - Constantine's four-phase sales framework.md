## The Framework: Constantine's Sales Structure

The immutable 4-phase architecture every call runs through:

---

### Phase 1 — INTRO
Set the frame. Agenda in 3 parts. Max 60 seconds small talk. Doctor mindset — diagnose, don't chitchat.

### Phase 2 — DISCOVERY
10 sequential sub-phases:
1. Main Challenge
2. Background
3. Efforts
4. How Long (stretch the pain)
5. Goals (measurable + emotional)
6. Partner (surface decision-makers early)
7. Escape / PUSH (future pace the bad path)
8. Timing / Logistics
9. Commitment / PULL (future pace the good path — get verbal commitment)
10. Prompt for missed info → Transition to pitch

> **80% of the sale is done here — before you ever pitch.**

### Phase 3 — PITCH
Callback → Solution → Benefit per step. Simplicity over education. Anchor value, drop price, then **shut up**.

### Phase 4 — CLOSE
Handle in this exact order:
1. Smokescreens (Isolate → Neutralize → Set Aside → Discover)
2. Money
3. Fear
4. Logistics
5. Partner / Responsibility Close

---

**3 Objections:** Fear | Money | Logistics
**2 Smokescreens:** "Need to think" | "Need to talk to partner"
**3 Limiting Beliefs:** Possibility | Capability | Worthiness
