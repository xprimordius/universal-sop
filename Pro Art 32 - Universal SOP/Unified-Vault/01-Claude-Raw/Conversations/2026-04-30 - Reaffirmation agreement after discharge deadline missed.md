**The hard reality of your situation — three separate problems:**

1. **§ 524(c)(1) timing rule.** A reaffirmation agreement *(a contract where the debtor agrees to remain personally liable on a debt that would otherwise be discharged)* must be **"made before the granting of the discharge."** This is statutory — courts can't waive it. If discharge already entered and the bank hasn't signed, the agreement arguably isn't "made" yet. That's your biggest exposure.

2. **Bank's missing signature.** A reaffirmation isn't binding until **both** parties sign and it's filed. Mom signing in time + bank dragging feet + discharge entered = § 524(c)(1) problem regardless of the attorney error.

3. **Attorney error (wrong judge).** Doesn't auto-revive the deadline. But it supports a Motion to Reopen under § 350(b) for "cause" and may support **excusable neglect** under FRBP 9006(b) — *(rule allowing late filings when missed for reasons beyond reasonable control)*.
