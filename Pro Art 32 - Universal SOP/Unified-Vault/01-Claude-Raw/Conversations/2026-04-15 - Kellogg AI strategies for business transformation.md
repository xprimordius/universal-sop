# Kellogg "AI Strategies for Business Transformation"

**What it is:** An 8-week online executive education program from Northwestern's Kellogg School of Management, delivered via Emeritus, focused on how business leaders (not engineers) deploy AI — including Generative AI and Agentic AI — to drive enterprise value.

**Who it's for:** Executives, functional heads, tech leaders, consultants, and investors. No coding required.

**Faculty:** Led by Professor Mohanbir Sawhney, Kellogg's McCormick Foundation Chair of Technology.

**Core focus areas:**
- Customer experience, talent productivity, new product development, personalized marketing
- Evolution from prediction → creation (GenAI) → autonomy (agentic AI)
- Business use cases over algorithm theory

**Frameworks taught:**
- AI Canvas 2.0
- AI Radar 2.0
- AI Capability Maturity Model (CMM)
- Customer Experience DNA (CxDNA)
- Six Principles of Responsible AI

**Format:** Cohort-based, weekly live webinars (recorded), case studies (Prairie Community Bank, Enerwind), and a **capstone project** where you draft an AI strategy for your own org.

**Credential:** Digital certificate from Kellogg Executive Education (80% pass grade). Stackable toward an Advanced Certificate in AI and Product Strategy, Digital Marketing and AI, or Sales and AI Business Strategies — and toward Kellogg Executive Scholar status.

**Official page:** https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/executive-education/individual-programs/online-programs/ais.aspx
