# Funding timeline breakdown: blitz vs prime protocol paths

> [!info]- Conversation summary
> **Conversation Overview**
> 
> Alan is the founder/operator of Ascend Prime Wealth (APW), a business funding education and coaching company that teaches clients how to access multi-six-figure unsecured business funding from banks and financial institutions. The conversation centered on building a comprehensive suite of visual and reference materials for the APW program, including interactive timelines, a detailed quick-start checklist, a branded intro image, and a complete program knowledge base document.
> 
> The session began with Alan providing detailed program architecture and correcting/refining Claude's initial timeline outputs. Claude built a series of interactive HTML widgets covering the full client journey: a pre-execution prep phase, three execution paths (Blitz, Prime Protocol, and Hybrid), and post-funding management. Alan confirmed his identity as Alan partway through the session. Key program details established include: the Blitz Path delivers $50K–$200K+ in 30–60 days with a 6–24 month cooldown; the Prime Protocol requires 8 weeks of bank account setup, $30K–$100K+ in liquid capital, and delivers $300K–$500K+ over 3–10 months with repeatable cycles every 6 months; the Hybrid Path uses two separate entities to run both simultaneously.
> 
> Alan provided specific time corrections to Claude's initial estimates, which were running too high. Corrected figures: credit verification 1–3 hours, business entity setup 1–5 hours active (DUNS 1–4 weeks background), FTC packet 1.5–3 hours with 24 hours to 1.5 weeks system processing, strategy planning approximately 1 hour, application execution 15 minutes to 1 hour, and post-approval setup 15 minutes to 1 hour with 2–14 days for card delivery. Alan also added bank account setup in-person as a distinct phase: 1 hour per institution, 3–8 institutions total, paced at 1 per week or every other day. Claude flagged DUNS number processing (1–4 weeks), Google Business verification (1–3 weeks), and NAP consistency auditing as non-obvious time sinks beyond what Alan had already identified. Data extraction calls were confirmed as the single largest active time investment in the entire program, with Wave 1 of 10–20 institutions taking 1–10 hours and a full list of up to 50 institutions potentially reaching 25 hours total.
> 
> Alan requested that heavy credit optimization be framed as a separate preliminary track before the main program, with light optimization (24 hours to 1.5 weeks, up to 4 weeks outer range) remaining inside the main course. The main program assumes a fundable profile as its starting point. For the intro image, Alan was dissatisfied with Claude's HTML-based visual output and asked instead for a detailed ChatGPT image generation prompt with full brand context, which Claude produced covering brand colors, logo description, scene layout, milestone flow, path fork structure, text hierarchy, and explicit guardrails against common generation failure modes. The session concluded with Claude producing a comprehensive program knowledge base document formatted for input into Gamma AI, covering all program phases, funding paths, offer tiers, credit scoring tiers, utilization impact tables, competitive positioning, course structure, and Gamma-specific styling notes.
