## Recommendations for Entertaining + Karaoke

### 🖥️ TV Upgrade
- Current placement above the fireplace is fine for a **75"–85" TV** — the vaulted ceiling gives you visual room
- Consider a **full-motion articulating mount** so you can angle it down for viewing comfort
- Hide the visible wires — in-wall cable management kit, clean look

### 🎤 Karaoke Setup
- Mount **two bookshelf or satellite speakers** flanking the fireplace surround (those side columns are perfect anchor points)
- Add a **subwoofer** tucked beside the fireplace or inside the cabinet
- Use the **existing cabinet above** for a receiver/soundbar or karaoke machine storage
- A **wireless mic system** (e.g., Shure or UHF dual-mic) keeps the floor clear

### 🎨 Space Refresh
- Paint the wall behind the surround a **deep accent color** (navy, charcoal, or dark plum) — makes the wood pop and signals "entertainment zone"
- Add **LED strip lighting** behind the TV or under the mantel for ambiance
- Clear the toy zone 😄 — even a storage ottoman keeps it functional but clean for guests

### 💡 Bonus
- That vaulted ceiling is **perfect for a projector + retractable screen** if you ever want a cinema night option alongside the TV
