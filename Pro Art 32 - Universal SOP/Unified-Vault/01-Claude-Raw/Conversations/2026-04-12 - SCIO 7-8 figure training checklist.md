# VSL Sequential Build Checklist — SCIO Framework (Verbatim Fidelity)

Source: `How_To_Write_A_VSL` reference doc. Order, timing markers, and language patterns locked to source. Check sequentially top-to-bottom.

---

## PHASE 0 — Ultimate Goal (Mental Lock Before Writing)

VSL must create these 6 epiphanies in the prospect:
- [ ] 1. "This is what I've been missing"
- [ ] 2. "This is what I need to get me to my goal"
- [ ] 3. "I realize why everything I've tried in the past has failed and why this is going to be different"
- [ ] 4. "I can see this is a NO BRAINER"
- [ ] 5. "This CLEARLY works"
- [ ] 6. "This is what I NEED"

---

## PHASE 1 — HEADLINE

- [ ] 7. Lead with the most emotionally-compelling detail of the offer
- [ ] 8. Speak to THE ONE BIG THING the market wants (not clever, original, or shocking — Drayton Bird principle)
- [ ] 9. Underscore the most valuable benefit (typically the guarantee / "what happens if they don't get the result")
- [ ] 10. Pick a guarantee form: "...Or You Don't Pay" / "...Or You Pay Nothing" / "Don't Pay A Penny Until {ONE BIG thing}" / creative variant ("…Or We'll Buy Your Store From You")

---

## PHASE 2 — PATTERN INTERRUPT (first 10–20 sec)

- [ ] 11. Do NOT open sat at a desk / boring location
- [ ] 12. Draw attention to one of: an object, a shocking statement, impending danger, or something that forces a second look
- [ ] 13. If you open a visual/object loop, you MUST close it later in the VSL
- [ ] 14. Minimum baseline: fast-paced intro walking toward camera

---

## PHASE 3 — MARKET CALL OUT (0–5s)

- [ ] 15. Call out the MOST ideal client specifically (role + qualifier numbers)
- [ ] 16. Name their problem / desire / current pain
- [ ] 17. Use subtle qualifying language (e.g. "If you cannot generate more than 30–50 calls per month…" — implies they're at ~30)
- [ ] 18. Maximize specificity — more specific = more qualified calls

---

## PHASE 4 — STATE OFFER + NEW OPPORTUNITY/MECHANISM (5–15s)

- [ ] 19. State ONLY the ONE most emotionally-compelling detail of the offer — NOTHING else (do not list backend deliverables)
- [ ] 20. Touch on the mechanism for how you achieve the outcome
- [ ] 21. Mechanism rule: only name it here if it is **truly unique, tangible, real** (not "XYZ system"). Otherwise let the offer carry early, expose mechanism later
- [ ] 22. Combined model: "{Ideal client qualifier}: We'll guarantee {one big thing} through {mechanism} or you don't pay"

---

## PHASE 5 — GUARANTEE (15–20s)

- [ ] 23. Immediately after the ONE BIG THING, eliminate the downside
- [ ] 24. Use one of: "OR you don't pay a cent" / "refund in full, no questions asked" / "legally obligated to refund" / "OR we'll pay you $10k" / "OR we'll BUY your company from you"

---

## PHASE 6 — FIRST-SECTION VALIDATION GATE

The opening MUST already answer:
- [ ] 25. Q1 — "How is this different from everything else I've seen?" (must register as new/novel — only novel stimulus activates dopamine/midbrain)
- [ ] 26. Q2 — "What's in it for me?" (cold traffic is savage; the salivating outcome answers this)

---

## PHASE 7 — EXPAND ON GUARANTEE (20–30s, optional but recommended)

- [ ] 27. Transition word: **"Meaning…"**
- [ ] 28. Use framework: *"Meaning, if you don't at LEAST {ONE BIG THING} we will {guarantee}. In other words, if you {tangible restatement of one big thing}, we will {guarantee}."*
- [ ] 29. Keep benefit-driven; make the tangible restatement as concrete as possible

---

## PHASE 8 — PROOF OF CLAIM (30–45/50s)

- [ ] 30. Answer Q3: "How do I know this is real?"
- [ ] 31. Transition via "reason why" you're offering the guarantee. Approved openers:
  - "Now you may be wondering, why am I offering such a crazy guarantee like this?"
  - "…why am I so confident to offer such a crazy guarantee like this?"
  - "…why am I really putting our neck on the line?"
  - Close with: **"It's simple. Just look at our track record…"**
- [ ] 32. Optional: state the catch — but the catch must be a NET POSITIVE (e.g. "the catch is once you {huge benefit}, we hope you'll become a private client")
- [ ] 33. Deliver 7–10 client results, **rapid fire**
- [ ] 34. Result format: *"We helped {Name} to {ONE BIG OUTCOME} in {timeframe} without {Objection}"* — bake objection-handling into each result
- [ ] 35. Vary objections handled across the 7–10 results

---

## PHASE 9 — FIRST CTA (inside 60s)

- [ ] 36. State the beliefs the market must hold to buy (this works / this approach is best / you practice what you preach / no results = no pay / etc.)
- [ ] 37. Use language pattern: *"Now if this is all you need to hear and you're thinking — I know this works, I know I NEED {one big thing} to scale, and I know you've done this yourself and it works — then click the link below and book a call…"*
- [ ] 38. Reinforce no-hard-sell: *"…we're not gonna hard sell you on this thing because if you don't {one big benefit} we will have to {guarantee}…"*

---

## PHASE 10 — SECOND TRANSITION

- [ ] 39. Answer Q4: "What's holding me back?" / "Why hasn't this worked for me so far?"
- [ ] 40. Re-hook with another headline/promise
- [ ] 41. Take blame OFF prospect; put it on old opportunities they've tried
- [ ] 42. Construct the "real problem" as the **inverse** of your "how is this different" answer (the real problem is one ONLY your new mechanism solves)
- [ ] 43. Language pattern opener: *"Now, if you wanna know more detail on WHY this works so well… In the next 90 seconds, I'm gonna give you a FULL inside look into HOW we're able to {core promise restated tangibly}…"*
- [ ] 44. Land with: *"See, right now you're unable to {specific core desire}, which isn't your fault because {real problem}…"*

---

## PHASE 11 — UNIQUE MECHANISM (60s – 4min)

- [ ] 45. Answer Q5: "How does it work?"
- [ ] 46. Single goal: simultaneously explain WHY everything they've tried failed AND why yours is different
- [ ] 47. Position your option as **exponentially better** than ALL direct + indirect competitors
- [ ] 48. Target ONE big reason why; **maximum 2–3 reasons**
- [ ] 49. Reason must double as your business moat
- [ ] 50. Explanation must trigger "Oh yeah, that makes sense" — clear cause→effect from mechanism to desired outcome
- [ ] 51. Marketing principle to enforce: **"Marketing is the art of meaningful differentiation"** — different AND superior

### Mechanism creation steps (do offline before scripting):
- [ ] 52. Get clear on WHY your method works
- [ ] 53. Examine competitor marketing — identify their mechanism, how it works, positives/negatives, whether it solves symptom or root cause (so you know what NOT to say)
- [ ] 54. Interrogate your own delivery: What is my approach? How does it work? Why? Why this order? What did I add/subtract/combine? Why is mine easier / faster / greater results / more consistent / fewer problems / better?
- [ ] 55. Name the mechanism ("wrapping paper"): scientific, real-sounding, credible, non-cringey, no mnemonics, prospect thinks "yeah, that could work" (model: P90X → "Muscle Confusion Method," not "Huge Bicep Domination")

### Delivery language pattern:
- [ ] 56. *"However, the reason our clients are achieving {core desire} consistently is because of ONE reason…"*
- [ ] 57. Reason 1 → Explanation (must explain superiority + clear path to goal)
- [ ] 58. (If used) Reason 2 → Explanation
- [ ] 59. (If used) Reason 3 → Explanation
- [ ] 60. End-state check: prospect now WANTS the mechanism (not the product), and the only access to the mechanism is through your offer ("all roads lead to us")

---

## PHASE 12 — RESULTS (Second Set)

- [ ] 61. Treat the mechanism explanation as an indirect claim → immediately back it with proof
- [ ] 62. **Must be different results from Phase 8.** If client has <10 total, frontload all in Phase 8 and SKIP this phase. If >10, use 7–10 in Phase 8 and 5–10 here
- [ ] 63. Format: *"And that's exactly how {Name} went from {current state} to {desired state} — directly by {mechanism just explained}…"*
- [ ] 64. Stack with "And another…" rapid fire

---

## PHASE 13 — STRONG CTA

- [ ] 65. Answer Q6: "What do I have to lose?"
- [ ] 66. Restate the guarantee explanation alluded to earlier
- [ ] 67. Language pattern: *"And again, if you don't see these types of results in your {business}, then you don't pay… As I previously mentioned {restate guarantee}… So if this sounds interesting and you want {ultimate desired goal}, click the link right below this video and schedule a call… And remember, we're not gonna try to incentivize you to do anything that doesn't make sense for you to do, because otherwise we'll have to refund you anyway… So click the link below and schedule a call…"*

---

Read the finished VSL and confirm each is answered, in this order:

# Ethical Words For "Hack / Loophole"

The trick is finding a word that keeps the *insider-knowledge / shortcut / unfair-advantage* energy of "hack" without the negative connotation (cheating, exploiting, breaking rules).

## Top Tier — Strongest For Marketing

| Word | Connotation | Why It Works |
|---|---|---|
| **Edge** | Insider advantage, fair but uncommon | Sounds earned, not exploited. "The funding edge." |
| **Leverage** | Strategic asymmetry | Implies skill + positioning. "Bank leverage." |
| **Lever** | Singular pull point | Punchier than leverage. "The credit lever." |
| **Unlock** | Opening what was always there | Implies access, not exploitation. "Unlock $500K." |
| **Shortcut** | Faster legitimate path | Direct, honest, no rule-breaking implied |
| **Blueprint** | Proven method | Engineering-credible, no shadiness |
| **Playbook** | Tactical sequence | Sports-coded, ethical framing built in |

## Mid Tier — Useful With Context

| Word | Connotation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Method** | Generic but credible | Safe, slightly bland |
| **Protocol** | Clinical / systematic | Sounds rigorous |
| **Framework** | Structural | Slightly corporate |
| **System** | Repeatable process | Overused but works |
| **Strategy** | Deliberate plan | Honest, broad |
| **Approach** | Neutral | Soft, less punchy |
| **Pathway** | Guided route | Friendly tone |
| **Sequence** | Ordered steps | Mechanical, precise |
| **Maneuver** | Tactical move | Slight military edge |
| **Workaround** | Solving around obstacles | Closest to "loophole" without being shady — very useful for credit/funding |

## Phrase-Level (When One Word Won't Carry It)

| Phrase | Use Case |
|---|---|
| **Insider track** | Implies privileged access without deception |
| **Backdoor** *(use cautiously)* | Edgier, can sound shady — only works if framed clearly |
| **Side door** | Softer than backdoor, implies ethical alternative entry |
| **Open secret** | "Everyone in the industry knows this, you just weren't told" |
| **Little-known method** | Old-school direct response, still works |
| **Banker's playbook** | Borrowed-authority framing — strong for funding |
| **What banks won't tell you** | Same energy, more aggressive |
| **Approval lever** | Specific to funding context |

---

## Closest 1:1 Replacements For "Loophole"

If you want to keep that *exact* feeling (using rules in your favor without breaking them):

1. **Workaround** — most honest direct swap
2. **Provision** — legalistic, sounds like you found something written in the rules
3. **Lever** — implies deliberate use of leverage that exists
4. **Edge** — unfair advantage framing without cheating
5. **Loophole** itself — *contrary to popular belief, "loophole" is technically neutral and just means a legal gap.* You can keep it if you immediately reframe: *"a 100% legal loophole banks don't advertise."* The "100% legal" tag neutralizes the shady connotation.

2. **Edge** — punchy, broadly applicable, zero negative connotation
3. **Workaround** — closest to "loophole" while staying clean
