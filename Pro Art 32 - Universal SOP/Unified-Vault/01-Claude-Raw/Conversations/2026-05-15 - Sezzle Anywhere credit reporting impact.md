**No** — the $20/mo subscription itself is not a credit account and doesn't generate a tradeline. But upgrading exposes you to two reporting paths you should know about.

**What actually reports**

- The **subscription fee** (recurring charge to debit/credit) = no tradeline. Just a recurring merchant charge.
- **Sezzle Up** is the opt-in credit-reporting feature — separate from Anywhere. Standard Sezzle (Pay-in-4) is not reported to any bureau as an account. Your on-time payments are invisible. Only opting into Sezzle Up triggers reporting.
- If Sezzle Up is on: each completed order reports as a short-term installment tradeline. A $100 purchase paid across six weeks becomes a tiny loan on your report that was opened, paid on time, and closed. Currently Sezzle Up reports to Equifax and TransUnion. Experian is not part of the standard reporting setup for all accounts.
- **SezzleCash** advances are governed by a separate Loan Agreement — those can report as installment loans depending on terms.
- The **Multi-Use Card** under Anywhere requires you to "apply for credit" — that path can generate a credit-line tradeline (separate from the subscription itself).

**Critical for your AU tradeline / avg-age work**

Each Sezzle Up order = a brand-new short-term installment tradeline (often closed within weeks). Stacking these will **crush your average account age** — directly works against your 7.5 / 9.5 year targets. For someone optimizing avg age for funding, Sezzle Up is age-poison.

**Recommendation**

- Upgrade to Anywhere if you want the card utility + SezzleCash optionality
- Keep Sezzle Up **OFF** until after your funding rounds close
- Treat SezzleCash advances cautiously — verify the Loan Agreement before each pull (those can hit as installment lines)
