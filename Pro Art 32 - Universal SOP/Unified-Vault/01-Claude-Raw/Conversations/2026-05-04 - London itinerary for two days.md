Two days is enough to hit the icons if you plan tight. Here's a clean route:

**Day 1 — Westminster + the Tower**
- Morning: Westminster Abbey → Big Ben & Parliament → walk across Westminster Bridge for the photo
- Buckingham Palace if you want the Changing of the Guard (11am Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun)
- Lunch in Covent Garden or St James's
- Afternoon: Tower of London (2–3 hrs, book ahead) → walk Tower Bridge
- Evening: West End show, or dinner in Soho

**Day 2 — Culture + the river**
- Morning: British Museum (free, give it 2 hrs — Rosetta Stone, Egyptian galleries, Parthenon Marbles)
- Lunch: Borough Market on the South Bank
- Afternoon: Tate Modern → Millennium Bridge → St Paul's Cathedral
- Evening: Thames sunset walk along the South Bank, or the London Eye if the weather's clear

**Skip unless you have specific reason:**
Madame Tussauds, Harrods (just shopping), London Dungeon. The London Eye is fine but overrated if it's gray out.

**Practical:**
- Tap on/off with a contactless card — no need for an Oyster anymore
- Book Westminster Abbey, Tower of London, and any West End show in advance
- Many pub kitchens stop food at 9pm
