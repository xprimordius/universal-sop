# S5800 vs P4500 — Verdict

**Get the S5800.** It's the right chair for your body (5'10", 198 lbs) and your injury history. The P4500 is a big-and-tall chair you don't need, with older, less ergonomic internals.

|---|---|---|
| Price (your cart) | $444.99 | ~$474.99 |

## Why the S5800 for you specifically

- **Injury history (60ft fall, multiple surgeries):** the adaptive lumbar and pressure-relief seat are doing real work the P4500 doesn't. This is the one feature that actually matters for long sessions in Apex or in the studio.
- **Sizing:** at 5'10"/198, you're squarely in the S5800's target range. The P4500 is engineered for frames the S5800 can't accommodate — you'd be paying more for capacity you don't use and getting *less* ergonomic tech.
- **Heat/airflow:** VertaAir + HygennX matter for long sit sessions.

## One caveat

Tom's Hardware noted the S5800's lumbar is **not height-adjustable** — it's flexible/adaptive but fixed in position. If you've previously needed a manually adjustable lumbar crank (like Secretlab/Steelcase), test-sit one if possible. For most users it's a non-issue.

**Bottom line:** Delete the P4500 from your cart. Keep the S5800.
