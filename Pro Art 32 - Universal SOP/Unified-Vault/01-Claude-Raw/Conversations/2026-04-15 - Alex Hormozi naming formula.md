# Hormozi's Offer Naming Formula

**The "MAGIC" Formula** (from *$100M Offers*):

| Letter | Element | Example |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **M** | Make a Magnetic Reason Why | "Free", "$5", "Kickstart" |
| **A** | Announce Your Avatar | "Bee-eaters", "Boudoir", "Dentists" |
| **G** | Give Them A Goal | "Go from 0 to 6-figures" |
| **I** | Indicate a Time Interval | "in 6 weeks" |
| **C** | Complete with a Container Word | "Challenge", "Bootcamp", "Intensive", "Accelerator", "Masterclass", "Workshop", "Summit", "Program" |

---

**Full Example:**
> "Free 6-Week Lead-Gen Intensive for Real Estate Agents to Add 50 Deals/Year"

- **Free** (Magnetic)
- **Real Estate Agents** (Avatar)
- **50 Deals/Year** (Goal)
- **6-Week** (Interval)
- **Intensive** (Container)

---

**Rules Hormozi Emphasizes:**

1. **Use only 3–5 of the 5 components** — don't force all five or it gets clunky
2. **Rotate components** when response rates decline (he calls this the "ad refresh" — change one variable, relaunch)
3. **The avatar callout is the highest-leverage piece** — specificity beats cleverness
4. **Container words signal format** — pick one that matches the actual delivery (don't call a course a "Challenge")

---

**Application to APW:**

Strong candidates:
- "Free 60-Minute Capital Velocity Workshop for Medical Practice Owners to Unlock $100K–$500K in 30 Days"
- "$0 Funding Intelligence Intensive for 7-Figure Operators"
- "5-Day Blitz Challenge: $50K–$200K in Unsecured Capital for Established Businesses"

## What Makes Messaging Magnetic (Hormozi + Drees + direct-response fundamentals)

**Magnetism = Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood ÷ (Time Delay × Effort/Sacrifice)**

To be magnetic, APW messaging must spike one or more of these against the category default ("get a business loan"):

| Lever | Category Default | APW Magnetic Angle |
|---|---|---|
| **Dream Outcome** | "Get approved" | "$100K–$500K in unsecured capital — no collateral, no income docs, no equity given up" |

## The 5 Magnetic Angles (ranked by likely cold-traffic pull)

**1. The "Hidden Capital Stack" Angle** *(curiosity + specificity)*
> "There's $100K–$500K in unsecured capital sitting in your business's name right now. Most owners never see it because banks don't advertise the stack. Here's the order."

Why it works: Implies *withheld information*, not a sales pitch. Hormozi's "magnetic reason why" baked in.

**2. The "No-Doc Operator" Angle** *(removes the #1 objection)*
> "Funded $X in unsecured business capital without a single tax return, P&L, or bank statement requested."

Why it works: Demolishes the assumption that good funding requires deep doc review. High pattern-interrupt.

**3. The "Velocity vs. Loan" Reframe** *(category creation)*
> "Stop applying for loans. Start running Capital Velocity. One is a transaction. The other is an asset."

Why it works: Reframes you out of the commodity "business loan" category entirely. Positions APW as a *system*, not a service.

**4. The "Bank Intelligence" Angle** *(authority + insider positioning)*
> "What underwriters actually score — and the 14 data points that decide if you get $25K or $250K."

Why it works: Teaches before sells. Builds the "articulate the problem better than they can" trust Carnegie/Hormozi both flag.

**5. The "Operator Tax" Angle** *(loss aversion)*
> "Every month you operate without a capital reserve, you pay an Operator Tax — in missed deals, slower hiring, and emergency-mode decisions. Here's how to eliminate it in 60 days."

Why it works: Converts "I don't need funding right now" into "I'm bleeding by not having it."
