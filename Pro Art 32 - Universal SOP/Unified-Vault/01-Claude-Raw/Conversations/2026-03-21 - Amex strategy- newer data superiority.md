# Amex Strategy Summary — Latest Intel Priority

## Critical Update (March 2026)

Delta Biz Gold as relationship-starter is weakening. Four consecutive approvals in March 2026 yielded only **$3K limits** — even when a client showed $12K–$17K/mo bank statements. Amex also pulled **LexisNexis** on a client with no prior relationship. **Test Amazon Biz Card or Marriott Biz Card** as alternative relationship-starters.

---

## Best Current Sequence

**With no Amex relationship:**

1. Open a **charge card first** (Platinum or Gold) to establish relationship
2. After card arrives → apply **Blue Business Plus** (expect $10–25K)
3. Same session or shortly after → apply **Blue Business Cash** (expect $12–25K)
4. Wait 30 days after 2nd card → apply for **BLOC** (expect $25–50K with 2+ yr LLC)

**Key constraint:** 2 hard-limit cards per 90 days (charge cards don't count).

## Optimal Data Points for Applications

| Field | Recommended Input |
|---|---|
| Revenue | $300–450K (stay under $300K to avoid doc requests if conservative) |
| Personal Income | $93–250K |
| Monthly Spend | $10–25K |
| NAICS | 541618 (Management Consulting) or Marketing Consulting |
| Years in Business | 2+ (minimum 1) |
| Employees | 2 |
| Ownership | 25%+ YES, others 25%+ NO |
| Apply Time | **2–4 PM PST** or after **7 PM EST** |
| Email | Must match existing Amex account |

## Limit Maximization

- **Personal limit sets the ceiling** for Blue Business Plus (e.g., $35K personal → $35K biz)
- Amex is the **only bank** that allows personal-to-business limit transfers (leave $2K minimum on source card)
- CLI requests every 35–90 days post-approval
- Connect all business checking accounts to boost BLOC limits

## Perpetual 0% Loop

Year 1: BBP → Year 2: BBC → Year 3: cancel BBP → 6 months later Amex re-invites BBP → combine limits → repeat.

## BLOC Qualification

- **1 year LLC minimum**, 2+ years ideal
- Plaid connection required if revenue >$350K (must show real deposits)
- Top result: $78K BLOC for a client grossing $1M/yr, 2–3 yr old LLC, no prior relationship
- Consistent range after 2 cards: **$20–50K**

## Bottom Line

The Delta Biz Gold entry point is producing diminishing results ($3K). Shift to testing **Amazon or Marriott Biz Cards** as openers, or go straight to a **personal charge card** (Gold preferred over Platinum for lower annual fee) before hitting the 0% cards.
