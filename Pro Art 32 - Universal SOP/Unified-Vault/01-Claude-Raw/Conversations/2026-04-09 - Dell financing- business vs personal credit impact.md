## How It Shows Up on Credit

**Dell Business Credit / Dell Financial Services (DFS)** typically reports to:

- **Business bureaus:** Primarily **Experian Business (Skyward USA)** and sometimes **D&B / Equifax Business**. DFS is one of the better-known net/installment reporters to business bureaus.
- **Personal credit:** **Only if you personally guarantee (PG)**. Most Dell business financing requires a PG for newer/thin-file entities. If PG'd and you default, it hits personal. Some applications also do a **personal credit pull (soft or hard)** at underwriting — verify on the app.

## Key Distinctions

**APEX (Lease):**
- Not a debt instrument → doesn't add to debt-to-income or business debt ratios
- Hardware returns at end of term unless you buy out
- Best for keeping balance sheet clean

**Technology Ownership (Lease-to-own / $1 buyout style):**
- Functions like financing but structured as a lease until final payment
- Tax treatment can differ (Section 179 rules vary)
- Reports as installment debt

**Payment Agreement (True Installment Loan):**
- Cleanest path to ownership
- Standard installment tradeline — helps build business credit history if it reports
- Most likely of the three to show as a positive tradeline on Skyward/Experian Business

## Recommendation for Your Situation

Given your active **Skyward USA business credit build** and the fact you're trying to add quality tradelines:

→ **Payment Agreement** is likely the strongest play. It's the most transparent installment tradeline, builds business credit history immediately, and you own the asset day one.

→ **Avoid APEX** if your goal is tradeline building — it often won't report as a meaningful tradeline.

## Verify Before Signing

Two things to confirm directly with Dell Financial Services rep:
1. **"Does this product report to Experian Business and/or D&B, and as what type of tradeline?"**
2. **"Is a personal guarantee required, and will this trigger a personal credit pull (hard or soft)?"**

Don't assume — DFS terms vary by entity age, revenue, and approval tier.

# Dell Business Financing → Personal Credit: What You Need to Know

## Bottom Line

**You cannot guarantee Dell Business Credit (DBC) stays off your personal credit unless you qualify for no-PG approval — which is not guaranteed and typically requires an aged, established entity.** Skyward USA at its current stage may or may not qualify.

## Key Facts (Verified)

**1. Dell Business Credit is issued by WebBank** (separate from consumer Dell Pay, which is Comenity). It's the business-side product.

**2. Application almost always triggers a hard pull on your personal Experian.** Per multiple borrower reports, Dell pulls Experian (personal) on business credit applications. This happens regardless of PG status in most cases — they use personal credit history to underwrite.

**3. Personal Guarantee = Personal Credit Reporting Risk.** Straight from Dell's own credit agreement: if the account was established with a Personal Guaranty, Dell/WebBank may report the personal liability of the Guarantor and the status of the Account to credit bureaus. Translation: PG'd account → can report to personal bureaus, especially on default or delinquency.

**4. No-PG approval is possible but conditional.** Historically Dell has approved no-PG accounts, but it's tightened over time. Requirements typically include:
- LLC or Corp (not sole prop) ✅ Skyward USA qualifies structurally
- 2–3+ years time in business
- Established business credit profile (Paydex, tradelines on Experian Business / D&B)
- Sufficient revenue documentation

**5. Dell APEX PC as a Service is structured differently.** It's a subscription/lease, not a financed purchase. Often treated as operating expense, less likely to appear as a tradeline at all — but the *application* may still pull personal credit during underwriting.

## The Three Options Re-Scored for Your Goal (No Personal Credit Impact)

| Option | Personal Credit Pull at App? | Personal Credit Tradeline? | Best for "Stay Off Personal" |
|---|---|---|---|
| **APEX ($202.23)** | Likely yes (unless approved no-PG) | Usually no — operating lease | ✅ Best of three |
| **Technology Ownership ($215.95)** | Likely yes | Yes if PG'd; no if no-PG | ⚠️ Riskier |
| **Payment Agreement ($215.95)** | Likely yes | Yes if PG'd; no if no-PG | ⚠️ Riskier |

## What to Do Before Clicking Continue

**Call Dell Financial Services directly** (not chat, not the website) and ask these exact questions. Get the rep's name and note the time:

1. **"For my entity Skyward USA, Inc., will this application require a personal guarantee?"**
2. **"Will Dell Financial Services pull my personal credit — hard or soft — as part of underwriting?"**
3. **"If approved, will the tradeline be reported to personal credit bureaus (Experian, Equifax, TransUnion), or only to business bureaus (Experian Business, D&B)?"**
4. **"Can I apply using only my EIN, without providing my SSN?"**

If they require SSN + PG → assume hard pull on personal Experian and potential personal tradeline.
If they say EIN-only with no PG → safest path, but get it in writing or screenshot the application.

## Strategic Recommendation

Given your active Experian Business (Skyward USA, BIN 428222169) build and your goal of keeping personal credit clean:

→ **Do NOT submit the application through the cart checkout flow.** That path almost always defaults to PG + personal pull.

→ **Apply for Dell Business Credit separately first**, EIN-only, calling DFS to confirm no-PG eligibility. If approved no-PG, then make the purchase against that line.

→ **If denied no-PG**, weigh whether one more business tradeline is worth a hard pull on Experian (your weakest bureau) and a potential personal tradeline.

→ **Strong alternative:** Use an existing business charge card (Amex Biz Gold–style) for the Dell purchase. Cleaner, no new personal hard pull, and you keep Dell as a future no-PG attempt once Skyward is more aged.

## Honest Caveat

Dell's underwriting practices vary by entity profile, time of year, and approval tier. The only way to know with certainty what happens to *your* file is to call DFS directly before submitting. Do not rely on the checkout flow to protect your personal credit — it will not.

# How to Confirm No-PG Before You're Locked In

## The Only Reliable Method: Call Dell Financial Services Directly

**Phone:** 1-800-456-3355 (Dell Business Credit / DFS)
Ask for the **Business Credit / New Accounts** team, not general sales.

## Exact Script to Use

> "Hi, I'm applying for Dell Business Credit for my corporation, Skyward USA, Inc. Before I submit, I need to confirm a few things in writing or on a recorded line:
>
> 1. Can I apply using only my EIN, without providing my SSN?
> 2. Will this application be underwritten without a personal guarantee?
> 3. Will Dell Financial Services or WebBank pull my personal credit — hard or soft — at any point in this application?
> 4. If approved, will the tradeline report only to business bureaus (Experian Business, D&B), or also to personal bureaus?
>
> I need clear yes/no answers on each before I proceed."

**Get the rep's name, ID, date, and time. Note that the call is being recorded (theirs and yours if legal in your state — Minnesota is one-party consent, so you can record).**

## Signals on the Application Itself

When you start the application, these are the **red flags that mean PG is required**:

| Red Flag on Application | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Asks for **SSN** | Personal credit will be pulled. PG almost certain. |
| Asks for **Date of Birth** | Personal underwriting in progress. |
| Asks for **personal home address** | Personal credit pull. |
| Asks for **personal income** | Personal underwriting. |
| Checkbox: *"I personally guarantee…"* | Explicit PG. Do not check unless you accept personal liability + reporting. |
| Language: *"jointly and severally liable"* | PG. |

**Green flags (no PG path):**

| Green Flag | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Asks **only for EIN** | Pure business underwriting. |
| Asks only for **business address, business revenue, time in business** | Business-only file. |
| No SSN field anywhere | No personal pull possible. |
| No PG checkbox or guarantor language | No personal liability. |

## The Truth About Dell's Current Practice

Dell **used to** approve no-PG accounts more freely. In recent years they've tightened significantly. Most applicants today — especially newer entities — get pushed to a PG'd application even if they start EIN-only. The flow often goes:

1. You start EIN-only.
2. System "can't verify" the business.
3. You're asked to "add personal information to strengthen the application."
4. That's the PG trap.

**If you see step 3 → stop. Do not add SSN. Cancel the application.** Adding SSN converts it to a personal-pull, PG'd file.

## After Approval — Verify It Stayed Clean

Even after approval, confirm reality matches what you were told:

1. **Check Experian, Equifax, TransUnion personal reports within 30 days** for a new Dell/WebBank inquiry or tradeline. Use Credit Karma, AnnualCreditReport.com, or your monitoring service.
2. **Check Experian Business (Skyward USA)** for the new tradeline appearing there.
3. **Pull the actual credit agreement Dell sends you** and search for the words "personal guaranty," "guarantor," "jointly and severally," and "personal credit." If any appear → you signed a PG.

## The Cleanest Possible Path

1. **Do not start the application from the cart checkout.** That flow defaults to PG.
2. **Apply directly at** dell.com/business-credit (standalone application) **after calling DFS first** to confirm no-PG eligibility for your entity profile.
3. **Use EIN only.** If the form demands SSN, exit.
4. **Get written confirmation by email** from DFS that the account is no-PG and will not report to personal bureaus. Save it.
5. **Only after approval and verification**, return to the cart and apply the new Dell Business Credit line as the payment method.

## Honest Reality Check

There is **no 100% guaranteed signal** before submission other than (a) a clean EIN-only application form with zero personal fields, and (b) written confirmation from a DFS rep. Anything else is inference. If you cannot get both, assume PG and personal pull are likely — and decide whether that's an acceptable trade for the tradeline.
