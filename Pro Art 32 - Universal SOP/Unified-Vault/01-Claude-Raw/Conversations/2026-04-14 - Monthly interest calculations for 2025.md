Short answer: **none of the documents you've uploaded contain this information.** The Wells Fargo statements only show the loan amount funded ($61,101.97 on 08/06/25) — they don't show the Tesla purchase price, taxes, fees, trade-in, down payment, rebates, or the federal EV tax credit treatment. ```

## Documents to pull, ranked by importance

| # | Document | Where to find it | What it proves |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Motor Vehicle Purchase Agreement / Buyer's Order** | Tesla account → Manage → Documents (or the original signed PDF Tesla emailed at delivery) | Full price breakdown: vehicle, options, destination, doc fee, taxes, title/reg, trade-in, down payment, rebates, **net amount financed** |
