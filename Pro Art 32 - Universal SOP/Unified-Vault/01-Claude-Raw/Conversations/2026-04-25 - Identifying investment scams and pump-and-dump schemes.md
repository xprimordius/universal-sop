**Verdict: 99% scam. Pump-and-dump or pig-butchering setup.**

The tells, stacked:

- "Only visible to those who like and follow" — engagement bait to harvest follower list before pivoting to DMs
- "I'll only say this once" + hard deadline ("Buy before May 1") — manufactured urgency
- $6.27 → $642+ (≈10,200% return) — no legitimate analyst projects 100x in months
- Anonymous "NVIDIA senior analyst" couple making $8M/yr posting penny-stock tips on Threads — NVIDIA employees are bound by Reg FD + insider trading + securities laws; this would be career-ending and federally prosecutable
- The reply nailed it: *"He'll DM you when he needs exit liquidity."* That's the playbook — get you into a Telegram/WhatsApp "VIP group" where coordinated buying pumps a thinly-traded micro-cap, then the operators dump on followers

**If it were real** — meaning, what's the realistic universe of small-cap public companies pitched as "AI interposer / advanced packaging supplier to NVIDIA / IBM" in the $5–10 range? The category exists, but no legitimate one carries a 100x target:

| Category | Real public names (illustrative, not endorsements) |
|---|---|
| Silicon photonics / optical interposer | POET Technologies (POET), Lumentum (LITE — bigger) |
| Advanced packaging / test | Aehr Test (AEHR), Kulicke & Soffa (KLIC), Camtek (CAMT) |
| Substrate / interposer materials | Onto Innovation (ONTO — bigger), Ichor (ICHR) |
| Private (not investable here) | Lightmatter, Ayar Labs |

Even the closest small-cap match in this category would have analyst targets in the $10–25 range, not $642. Anyone quoting a 100x target in a social post is selling the dream, not the math.

**Don't engage.** Don't like, follow, comment, or DM. Block + report. If you want real exposure to the AI packaging supply chain, the legitimate names trade on NASDAQ with published 10-Ks and analyst coverage you can verify.

Not financial advice — but this one's pattern recognition, not analysis.
