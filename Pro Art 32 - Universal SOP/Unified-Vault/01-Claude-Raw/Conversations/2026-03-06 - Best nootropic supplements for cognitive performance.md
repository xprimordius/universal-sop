---

**The brands you're already running are actually top tier.** Qualia Mind 2.0, Lifeforce Peak Cognition, and Now Alchemy are genuinely among the most sophisticated formulas on the market. That said, here's what to consider adding or swapping:

---

**Worth adding or upgrading to:**

**Thesis Nootropics** — the biggest functional differentiator from what you're using. Rather than a single comprehensive formula, Thesis offers six targeted blends (Clarity, Creativity, Energy, Motivation, Logic, Confidence), each focused on a specific cognitive area at full potency. Great for matching your stack to what the day demands — board meeting vs. creative build vs. deep work.

**Mind Lab Pro** — the only brain supplement independently proven in two double-blind, placebo-controlled university trials. It's the most clinically validated pre-made stack available. Less flashy than Qualia, but the evidence base is stronger.

**Performance Lab Mind** — a clean, stim-free formula. Contains Citicoline (Cognizin®), L-Tyrosine, Maritime Pine Bark Extract, and Phosphatidylserine (Sharp-PS®) in a clean, allergen-free formula. Good for stacking on top of Qualia on lower-stim days.

---

**Cutting-edge tier (for the biohacker CEO):**

**LVLUP Neuro Regenerate** — uses pharmaceutical-grade peptides including Dihexa (supports synaptic growth and memory formation), P21, and N-Acetyl-Semax — one of the only stacks using this class of compounds. High-potency, not mainstream. Worth researching if you want to go deeper than supplement-grade.

**Magnesium L-Threonate** — unique in that it crosses the blood-brain barrier, directly supporting synaptic density and memory formation — something most magnesium supplements can't do. A low-cost, high-impact add-on most people overlook.

---

**One honest flag on Qualia:** Qualia Mind's own study found participants reported improvements across six cognitive categories, but they weren't statistically significant over placebo. The ingredient list is impressive, but the clinical proof is thinner than the marketing suggests.

**Bottom line:** If you want to upgrade, Thesis for targeted daily use + Mind Lab Pro as your base is probably the highest-evidence combination out there. Layer in Magnesium L-Threonate and you've got a seriously optimized stack.
