**Top 3 picks — steaks + live music, all walking distance:**

**Skull's Rainbow Room** (4.6★) — This is the one. Hidden speakeasy in Printers Alley. Prime rib with garlic butter that people can't stop talking about. Live jazz band + burlesque shows. Dark, moody, one-of-a-kind atmosphere. Open til 2am.

**The Twelve Thirty Club** (4.5★) — Literally on Broadway at 5th. JT's supper club upstairs has live jazz, steaks, seafood, and killer cocktails. Best blend of fun energy + elevated food right on the strip.

**Gannon's** (4.7★) — Inside a converted bank vault. Prime rib and NY strip cooked perfectly, live piano trio (piano/bass/drums), old-school 1940s sophistication. Quieter and more refined if you want to actually talk over dinner.

**Honorable mention:** Hand Cut Nashville (4.5★) — more casual steakhouse with a live band playing while you eat. Good steaks, great old fashioneds, more relaxed vibe.

If I had to pick one: **Skull's Rainbow Room.** Steaks are legit, the vibe is unlike anything else in Nashville, and the live music is part of the whole experience — not background noise.
