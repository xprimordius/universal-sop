## 👤 You

Post surgery, will a doctor give you a refill for oxycodone for pain?

---

## 🤖 Claude

Right, so it depends on the surgery and your post-op pain profile. Most surgeons will send you home with an initial prescription — typically 10 to 30 tablets — that's meant to cover the first week or two of recovery. If you genuinely need more after that runs out, your surgeon can absolutely write another prescription, but they'll usually want to see you first or at least have you call to discuss your pain levels.

The key thing is: oxycodone prescriptions are tightly controlled now, so doctors are cautious about refills without direct patient contact. They're not trying to be difficult — they're managing their own liability and following DEA guidelines. Most surgeons will give you a refill if your pain genuinely warrants it, but they won't do it blindly.
