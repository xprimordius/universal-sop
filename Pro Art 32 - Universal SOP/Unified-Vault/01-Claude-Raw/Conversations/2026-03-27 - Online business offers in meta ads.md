Good list you've got already. Here's what else is heavily pushed through Meta ads right now:

**High-Ticket Services & B2B**
- SMMA (social media marketing agency) — still one of the most advertised "start an agency" offers
- Appointment setting / lead gen agencies
- Bookkeeping / accounting automation services
- Tax strategy / entity structuring for business owners
- Insurance sales (final expense, Medicare, life) — huge on Meta
- Solar sales / solar appointment setting
- Debt relief / debt consolidation (heavily regulated but high volume)
- Credit repair services and credit repair business training

**Skill-Based / Freelance Offers**
- High-income copywriting (especially direct response)
- UGC creator training ("get paid to make videos for brands")
- AI prompt engineering / AI automation agency
- Virtual assistant agencies / OBM (online business manager) training
- Bookkeeping as a service (especially Bookkeeper Launch style)
- Medical / legal / real estate virtual assistant training

**Productized & Subscription Models**
- Print on demand (Etsy + Shopify)
- Amazon FBA / Amazon automation / Amazon storefront management
- Subscription box businesses
- Faceless YouTube channels / faceless Instagram theme pages
- Newsletter monetization / paid community (Skool, Circle)
- SaaS reselling / white-label software (especially GoHighLevel reselling)

**Investing & Wealth**
- Options / futures / forex trading education
- Sports betting systems / picks services
- Crypto trading / Web3 / NFT flipping (quieter but still active)
- Automated trading bots
- Note investing / mortgage note buying
- Tax lien / tax deed investing
- Self-directed IRA / alternative asset strategies

**Health, Wellness & Transformation**
- Online fitness coaching certification / training
- Telehealth / health coaching businesses
- Supplement brand / private label wellness
- Biohacking / longevity programs
- Weight loss coaching (GLP-1 adjacent offers are everywhere)

**Marketplace & Arbitrage Plays**
- Retail arbitrage (Amazon, Walmart)
- Car flipping
- Storage unit auctions
- Vending machine routes / ATM placement businesses
- Laundromat investing

**Creator Economy**
- Selling digital planners / Canva templates on Etsy
- Music production / beat selling
- Stock photography / AI-generated stock content
- Ghostwriting services
- Cohort-based courses (live group programs)

The biggest *Meta-specific* trend right now: **"done-with-you" hybrid offers** (coaching + software + community) priced $3K–$10K, running VSL or webinar funnels through Advantage+ campaigns. GoHighLevel white-label reselling is probably the single most advertised "business in a box" offer on the platform right now.
