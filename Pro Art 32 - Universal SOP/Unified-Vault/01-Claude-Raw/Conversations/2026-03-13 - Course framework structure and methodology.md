# Course framework structure and methodology

> [!info]- Conversation summary
> **Conversation Overview**
> 
> The person asked Claude to recap the course framework used for structuring video lessons. Claude provided the five-part framework: WHY, WHAT, HOW, DO NOT, and Action Item. Each lesson is broken into five separate videos, where WHY establishes emotional motivation and stakes, WHAT defines the concept precisely, HOW delivers step-by-step execution, DO NOT covers common failure patterns to avoid, and Action Item gives a single concrete next step. The framework was presented in a structured table format.


---

## 👤 You

what is the course framework for each video again? why what? etc?

---

## 🤖 Claude

**WHY / WHAT / HOW / DO NOT / Action Item**

Each lesson = 5 separate videos:

| # | Section | Purpose |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | **WHY** | Emotional hook — why this matters, stakes, motivation |
| 2 | **WHAT** | Concept clarity — what it is, defined precisely |
| 3 | **HOW** | Step-by-step execution — how to actually do it |
| 4 | **DO NOT** | Failure patterns — common mistakes to avoid |
| 5 | **Action Item** | Single next step — what to do right now |
