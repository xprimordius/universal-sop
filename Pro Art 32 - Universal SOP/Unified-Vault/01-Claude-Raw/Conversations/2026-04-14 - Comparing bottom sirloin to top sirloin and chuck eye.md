## Bottom Line for Your Goals

**Bottom sirloin is noticeably worse** for quick high-protein meals — it's the cut that becomes the "why is this steak chewy" experience if you pan-sear it like top sirloin. It includes tri-tip and flap meat (sirloin flap/bavette), which can be great *if* you cook them right (low-and-slow or thinly sliced against the grain).

**Chuck eye is a sleeper.** It's cut from the 5th rib, right next to the ribeye — nearly identical flavor, much cheaper. Higher fat means slightly less protein per oz, but for body recomp it's still solid and far more satiating and enjoyable than bottom sirloin.

**Rank for your protocol:** Top Sirloin ≥ Chuck Eye > Bottom Sirloin

If Aldi carries chuck eye, it's worth rotating in for flavor variety without blowing macros.
