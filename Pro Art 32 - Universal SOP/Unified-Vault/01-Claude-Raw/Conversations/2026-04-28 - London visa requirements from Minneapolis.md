**Two official options:**

**1. UK ETA app** (fastest — ~10 min, decisions often in minutes)
- iOS: App Store → "UK ETA"
- Android: Google Play → "UK ETA"

**2. GOV.UK website** (if app gives you trouble)
- https://www.gov.uk/eta

**Quick facts:**
- £20 — avoid imitator sites that charge more
- Apply at least 3 working days before travel
- Valid 2 years or until passport expires, multiple entries
- You'll need: passport, card/Apple Pay, email access

Quick correction from my earlier note — fee went up to £20 on April 8, 2026 (I'd said £16). App route is faster than web if your phone has NFC.

London late April / early May is cool and unpredictable — think 50–62°F with surprise drizzle. Layer-friendly is the move.

**Outfit 1 — Work (polished, walks well, handles a tube commute)**

- Tailored straight-leg trousers (black, navy, or chocolate)
- Silk or fine-knit shell in cream or ivory
- Cropped or single-breasted blazer (camel/beige reads more London than black)
- Pointed-toe loafers or low block-heel slingbacks
- Structured tote that fits a laptop
- Trench coat as your topper — classic for a reason

UK corporate skews slightly more formal than US — a blazer + real shoes (not flats) signals you read the room.

**Outfit 2 — Walking the city (cute, weatherproof, comfortable for 15K steps)**

- Trench, barn jacket, or quilted vest depending on temp
- White leather sneakers OR Chelsea boots (cobblestones eat thin soles)
- Crossbody bag worn front (London pickpocketing is real around tourist spots)
- Silk scarf or beret if you want the Notting Hill moment
