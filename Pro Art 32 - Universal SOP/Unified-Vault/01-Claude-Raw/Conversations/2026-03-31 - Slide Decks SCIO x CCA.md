## Unique Mechanism Name — Proposals

The core of what makes APW different: you're teaching people to position themselves the way underwriters actually evaluate borrowers, then sequence applications across dozens of institutions in a specific order and timing — something no funding company teaches because it kills their business model.

**1. The Underwriter Positioning Protocol**
Why it works: "Underwriter" is the real gatekeeper they've never thought about. "Positioning" implies strategy, not luck. "Protocol" sounds medical/systematic — familiar to them. It describes exactly what you do. It's not a gimmick name.

**2. The Approval Cascade Method**
Why it works: "Cascade" is a medical/scientific term (cytokine cascade, etc.). It implies one approval triggers the next in sequence — which is literally how wave-based funding works. "Method" keeps it grounded.

**3. The Prime Positioning Protocol**
Why it works: "Prime" connects to prime borrower status AND your brand (Ascend Prime Wealth). Triple alliteration is memorable. "Positioning Protocol" sounds systematic and credible. Downside: slightly more brand-y, less descriptive of the mechanism itself.

**4. The Funding Sequence Protocol**
Why it works: Most literal description. "Sequence" is the actual term for what you do. "Protocol" = medical familiarity. Clean, simple, impossible to misunderstand. Downside: less intrigue than the others.

**5. The Institutional Stacking Method**
Why it works: "Institutional" sounds serious/financial. "Stacking" describes the multi-bank approach. "Method" is grounded. Downside: "stacking" could trigger skepticism in some audiences.
