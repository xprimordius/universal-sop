**UK ETA — £20, ~10 min, apply now via the official app.**

**Direct sources (only use these — avoid imitator sites):**
- Apply: https://www.gov.uk/eta/apply
- iOS app: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/uk-eta/id6444912481
- Android: search "UK ETA" on Google Play (Home Office publisher)

**What you need before you start:**
- US passport you'll travel with
- Phone camera (passport scan + selfie + biometric chip read)
- Card or Apple Pay for £20
- ~10 minutes

**Steps in the app:**
1. Photo of passport bio page
2. Scan passport chip (NFC — back of phone against passport)
3. Face scan + selfie
4. Answer questions (address, job, criminal history, other nationalities)
5. Pay £20
6. Wait for email with 16-digit ETA reference

**Timing:**
- Most decisions arrive in minutes
- Allow up to **3 working days** — apply now, not at the airport
- Valid 2 years or until passport expires (whichever first)
- Multiple entries, up to 6 months per visit
- Linked to passport — no document to print or carry

**Heads up:** App reportedly has issues reading some passport chips. If it fails repeatedly, fall back to the gov.uk web application.
