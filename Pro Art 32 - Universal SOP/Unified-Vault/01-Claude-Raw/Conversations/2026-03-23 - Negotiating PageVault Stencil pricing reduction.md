# Negotiating PageVault Stencil pricing reduction

> [!info]- Conversation summary
> **Conversation Overview**
> 
> Josephine Literski, Director of Procurement at NL Core LLC, worked with Claude to draft a vendor price negotiation email targeting Page Vault's Stencil product — a trademark prosecution automation tool. The goal was to push back on a current quote for 5 users and bring pricing down to approximately $15,000 annually. Claude conducted web research on Page Vault Stencil's market position, competitive alternatives, and pricing context to build a substantive negotiation case.
> 
> Claude drafted two email variants — a collaborative tone and a firm walk-away tone — iterating through several rounds of refinement based on Josephine's feedback. Key negotiation arguments developed included: the product's recent launch date making enterprise pricing hard to justify, the seat composition of 3 attorneys and 2 administrative users making flat per-seat pricing inappropriate, competitive alternatives such as Corsearch TrademarkNow, Trademarkia AI, Huski.ai, and general-purpose enterprise LLMs offering overlapping functionality at lower cost, and an internal build-vs-buy analysis showing that comparable automation could be developed in-house for less than the annual subscription cost. Josephine directed Claude to remove detailed ROI and billable rate calculations from the email because attorney billing rates were not known, keeping the argument grounded in per-seat economics, seat mix, competitive landscape, and build-vs-buy logic instead. The conversation ended with Josephine requesting a full redraft and being prompted to select a tone direction before Claude proceeded.
> 
> Josephine's preferred working style in this conversation was iterative and direct — she gave short, specific correction instructions and expected Claude to apply them cleanly without over-explaining. She confirmed her identity as Josephine when prompted per the established protocol.
