# Reading and speaking time estimation

> [!info]- Conversation summary
> **Conversation Overview**
> 
> Alan asked Claude to estimate recording and output times for a VSL (Video Sales Letter) project he is working on. The conversation covered two asset types: a VSL with a Thank You segment, and a set of four ad scripts (each containing multiple hooks and a body, likely for Meta advertising based on context). Alan's recording approach involves memorizing phrases, delivering them organically in one long take, and restarting from the phrase when mistakes occur, averaging 1–3 restarts per memorized phrase.
> 
> Claude initially overestimated the ad script recording time by assuming all 14 hook-and-body combinations would be recorded separately. Alan corrected this, clarifying he would record one hook per creative (four total recordings), which brought the raw recording estimate down to approximately 30–45 minutes — consistent with his own estimate of around 30 minutes. This correction is important for future reference: Alan records one hook per creative, not every hook variation as a separate take.
> 
> The final agreed-upon estimates were: VSL finished output of 9–12 minutes with 45–75 minutes of raw recording, Thank You segment at 2.5–3 minutes finished with 10–15 minutes raw, and ad scripts at 16–19 minutes finished with 30–45 minutes raw. Combined, the full shoot day totals approximately 26–31 minutes of finished content from roughly 1.5–2.25 hours of raw recording.
