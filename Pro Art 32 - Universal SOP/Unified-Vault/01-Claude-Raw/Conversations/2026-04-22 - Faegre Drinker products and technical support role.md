# Manager of Product Development — Law Firm Landscape

## The Two-Mode Reality of This Role

A legal product manager takes a launched product from a vendor and creates an internal road map for the piloting, communication, training, adoption and ongoing maintenance and improvement. They must act as a bridge between users of the firm's tools, the vendor, and their development teams.

**Mode 1: Internal-focused (Goodwin-style)**
- Deploy and adopt tools (buy more than build)
- Adoption metrics are the scorecard
- Lower risk, lower upside

**Mode 2: Commercial-focused (Gravity Stack-style)**
- Build in-house, license externally
- Revenue + IP are the scorecard
- Higher risk, higher upside, harder role

**Your Faegre Drinker role sounds like a Mode 2 in early formation** — intake → build internally → eventually commercialize through Tritura or similar vehicle. That's a more strategic, higher-ceiling role than pure adoption management.
