### 🔥 METHOD: Slow Simmer for Tenderness

#### ✅ What You Need:
- 4 lbs chicken (thighs = most tender, breast = leaner, more timing sensitive)
- Large pot  
- Water to cover by 1–2 inches  
- Salt (1–2 tbsp)  
- Aromatics (optional: garlic, onion, bay leaf, peppercorn)

---

### 📋 Instructions:

1. **Prep Chicken:**
   - Trim excess fat. Use bone-in/skin-on for best flavor/tenderness.

2. **Add to Pot + Water:**
   - Place chicken in pot. Add cold water to cover by 1–2 inches.
   - Add salt and aromatics.

3. **Heat Gently:**
   - Bring water **slowly to a gentle boil** over medium heat.
   - As it starts bubbling, **skim foam/scum** from surface.

4. **Lower to Simmer:**
   - Once boiling, reduce heat to **low simmer** (just small bubbles).
   - Cover pot with lid tilted.

5. **Cook Time (internal temp target = 165°F):**
   - **Bone-in thighs/legs:** 30–40 min  
   - **Breasts:** 18–22 min  
   - **Mixed pieces (whole 4 lbs):** 25–35 min  
   - Check thickest part with meat thermometer (165°F/74°C).  

6. **Rest in Liquid (Optional for tenderness):**
   - Turn off heat. Let chicken sit in hot broth for 10–15 min.

---

### 🧠 Pro Tip:
- Don’t *rapid boil*—causes tough, stringy meat.  
- For *super shredded tender*, simmer longer (up to 1 hour) or finish in slow cooker after parboil.

### 🔥 **Boiling 4 lbs of Chicken Thighs (Bone-In or Boneless)**

#### ✅ Goal: Fall-off-the-bone tender, fully cooked, juicy

---

### 📋 **Instructions**

1. **Place in pot:**  
   - Add chicken thighs to a large pot. Cover with **cold water** (1–2 inches above).

2. **Add aromatics & salt:**  
   - Salt: 1.5 tbsp  
   - Optional: 3 garlic cloves, ½ onion, 1 bay leaf, peppercorns

3. **Bring to boil slowly:**  
   - Medium heat → gentle boil (~10–15 min)  
   - Skim foam from top

4. **Simmer gently:**  
   - Reduce to **low simmer** (not rapid boil)  
   - Partially cover pot

5. **Cook Time:**  
   - **Bone-in thighs:** 35–40 min  
   - **Boneless thighs:** 25–30 min  
   - Internal temp: 175–185°F for max tenderness (safe at 165°F, but thighs are better above)

6. **Optional Rest in Liquid:**  
   - Turn off heat, let sit in broth 10–15 min  
   - Keeps moist and ultra tender

---

### 🧠 Pro Tips:
- **Don’t boil hard.** Slow simmer = tender.
- **Save broth** — strain and use for soups, rice, or freeze.

Want a shred-friendly finish? Let simmer for 50–60 min.
