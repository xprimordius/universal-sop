## 👤 You

If I’m taking a steak out of the fridge to leave out to get closer to room temp, do I let it warm up first before salt? Or same time?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Salt Immediately — While It’s Cold**

**Best practice:**  
Salt the steak **right when you take it out of the fridge**, not after it warms.

### Why
1. **Dry brining begins sooner**
   - Salt first draws moisture out, then that brine is reabsorbed.
   - This seasons the interior and improves tenderness.

2. **Better crust**
   - Early salting dries the surface → stronger Maillard browning.

3. **Time efficiency**
   - The steak seasons while it warms.

### Timing Rules
| Time Before Cooking | What Happens |
|---|---|
| **40–60 min** | Ideal quick dry brine |
| **2–24 hrs (fridge)** | Even better if planned ahead |
| **<10 min** | Salt immediately before cooking |

### Simple Flow
1. Remove steak from fridge  
2. **Salt immediately (all sides)**  
3. Leave uncovered **30–60 min**  
4. Pat dry if needed → cook

### Key Source
- entity["book","Salt, Fat, Acid, Heat","Samin Nosrat"] – recommends salting meat **ahead of cooking** to allow salt diffusion and improved browning.

**Bottom line:**  
Salt **as soon as it leaves the fridge.** Let it warm **while salted.**
