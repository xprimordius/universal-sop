Your back soreness and body stiffness the day after eating eggs—especially if you're allergic to egg yolks—likely stems from **inflammatory response** and **immune system activation**.

Here’s what’s likely happening:

### 1. **Allergic Reaction = Inflammation**
- Your immune system flags egg yolk proteins (like **ovomucoid**, **ovotransferrin**) as threats.
- This triggers **histamine** and other pro-inflammatory chemicals.
- Inflammation spreads systemically, often settling in **muscles and joints**—including your **back**—causing soreness and stiffness.

### 2. **Delayed Hypersensitivity**
- Reactions can be **delayed (Type IV)**, meaning symptoms appear **hours or a day later**.
- Common signs: joint pain, fatigue, stiffness, muscle soreness.

### 3. **Leaky Gut or Cross-Reactivity**
- Egg allergies are often linked with **leaky gut** or gut permeability.
- Proteins leak into the bloodstream, triggering immune responses that feel like **autoimmune-style pain**.

### What to Do:
- **Eliminate egg yolks completely**, not just reduce.
- Monitor symptoms with a **food diary**.
- Consider testing for **cross-reactive foods** (e.g., dairy, soy).
- Support gut health (L-glutamine, probiotics, anti-inflammatories).

That response suggests a **digestive issue**, not a calorie issue. Here's the strategic breakdown:

---

### **Why Beef Makes You Feel Bloated + Hungry:**

1. **Low Stomach Acid (Hypochlorhydria):**  
   - Beef needs strong stomach acid to break down.  
   - If underproduced, food sits heavy → bloating, slow digestion, false satiety + hunger.

2. **Poor Fat Digestion:**  
   - Beef (esp. fattier cuts) requires good bile flow.  
   - If bile is sluggish, it leads to bloating, fullness, but poor nutrient absorption → still hungry.

3. **Meal Size or Speed:**  
   - Too much at once or eaten fast = harder digestion.  

4. **Gut Microbiome Mismatch:**  
   - If you’ve been low-meat or had gut issues, your biome may not be optimized for red meat.

---

### **Tactical Fixes:**

- **Smaller Portions:** Try 2–3 oz per sitting, spaced out.  
- **Chew thoroughly** — until it’s basically mush.
