### 🔄 e-OSCAR (Electronic Online Solution for Complete and Accurate Reporting)

- **Definition**: A web-based, Metro 2-compliant system facilitating the processing of credit reporting disputes between data furnishers and consumer reporting agencies (CRAs) citeturn0search1.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**FDCPA** = **Fair Debt Collection Practices Act**

### ✅ Purpose:
Protects consumers from abusive, deceptive, or unfair debt collection practices by **third-party debt collectors**.

---

### 🔒 Key Protections:
- **No Harassment:** No threats, profanity, or repeated calls to annoy.
- **No False Statements:** Can’t lie about the amount owed or pose as government/law enforcement.
- **No Contact at Certain Times:** No calls before 8 AM or after 9 PM.
- **No Contact at Work:** If employer disallows it.
- **Must Validate Debt:** You can request written validation of the debt within 30 days.

---

### 📬 Dispute Rights:
- You can **dispute** the debt in writing within **30 days** of first contact.
- Collection must **pause** until verification is provided.

---

### 🧑‍⚖️ Enforcement:
- Enforced by **FTC**, **CFPB**, and **state attorneys general**.
- You can sue violators for up to **$1,000** plus actual damages and attorney fees.

---

### ⚠️ Applies To:
- **Third-party collectors only** (not original creditors).
- **Consumer debts** (credit cards, medical bills, etc.), not business debt.
