## 👤 You

What is COI for shooting at shows?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**COI = Certificate of Insurance.**  

For shooting at shows, a **COI** is a document provided by your insurance company that **proves you’re insured**—usually required by **venues, promoters, or event organizers** before they let you film or photograph.  

### 🔍 Breakdown:
**Purpose:**  
- Protects the venue and promoter from liability if there’s damage, injury, or legal claims related to your shoot.

**Typical Requirements:**
1. **General Liability Coverage:** $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate.  
2. **Additional Insured:** The venue, promoter, and sometimes the event’s parent company must be listed by name.  
3. **Description of Operations:** States the event name, date, and location.  
4. **Proof of Active Coverage:** Shows policy effective dates and insurer details.

**Example:**
> “Ascend Photography LLC is insured under General Liability Policy #XXXXXX. XYZ Events LLC, Time Nightclub, and Insomniac Holdings are named as additional insureds for the event ‘Audien – 11/2/25.’”

**How to Get One:**
- Contact your **insurance agent** (e.g., Hiscox, Thimble, or Full Frame Insurance).
- Request a **COI with additional insureds** listed.
- They’ll usually issue it **same day** for free if you already have coverage.
