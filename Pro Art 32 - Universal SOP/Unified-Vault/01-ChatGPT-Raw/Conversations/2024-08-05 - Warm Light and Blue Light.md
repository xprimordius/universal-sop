# Warm Light and Blue Light

---

## 👤 You

Does the warm iPhone light eliminate blue light

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

The warm light setting on an iPhone, often referred to as "Night Shift," does not completely eliminate blue light, but it does reduce the amount of blue light emitted by the screen. Night Shift shifts the display colors to the warmer end of the spectrum, which means it reduces blue light exposure, making the screen easier on your eyes in low-light conditions and potentially improving sleep quality if used before bedtime. However, for complete elimination of blue light, specialized blue light blocking screen protectors or glasses would be needed.
