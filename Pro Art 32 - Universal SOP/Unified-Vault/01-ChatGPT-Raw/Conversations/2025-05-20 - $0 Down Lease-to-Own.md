## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Yes**, but **rarely and under specific conditions.** Here’s when a **$0 down lease-to-own** (rent-to-own) home is possible:

### ✅ Scenarios Where $0 Down Is Possible:
1. **Motivated Sellers**  
   - Homeowners struggling to sell may accept **$0 option fee** and roll costs into rent.

2. **Distressed Properties or Markets**  
   - In slow markets, sellers may offer flexible terms to attract buyers.

3. **Creative Financing / Subject-To Deals**  
   - You take over mortgage payments directly (“subject-to” existing financing), often requiring **little or no cash upfront.**

4. **Private Owner Agreements**  
   - Individuals (not companies) might negotiate custom deals, especially if they trust the buyer or just want mortgage relief.

5. **Low Option Fee Waiver**  
   - Some lease-to-own sellers skip the typical **1–5% option fee** if rent is set higher or the contract has other incentives.

---

### ⚠️ Key Conditions to Watch:
- **Higher Rent Premiums** (e.g. $200–$600 extra monthly as future credit)
- **Strict Option Terms** (e.g. lose all credits if you don’t buy)
- **Maintenance Responsibility** usually shifts to you
- **No Refund on Missed Purchase**

---

### 🔎 TLDR:
Yes, **$0 down lease-to-own** is possible, but **rare** and often a trade-off:
- higher monthly rent,
- strict terms,
- or more risk.

Want to pursue this? Focus on **FSBO (For Sale by Owner)** listings, **distressed landlords**, or **creative finance groups**.
