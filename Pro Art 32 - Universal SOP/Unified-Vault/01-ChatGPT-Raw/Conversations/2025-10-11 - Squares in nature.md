If your hair is greasy, **it’s best to wash it before the haircut** — especially if you’re going to a professional stylist. Here’s why:  

- 💇‍♂️ **Clean hair = accurate results:** Stylists can better see your hair’s natural texture, movement, and shape when it’s clean. Oil and product buildup can make it clump or lay differently, which can affect how the haircut turns out.  
- ✂️ **Tools perform better:** Clippers and scissors glide more cleanly through freshly washed hair, reducing tugging or uneven cuts.  
- 💦 **Water alone isn’t enough:** Simply wetting greasy hair won’t remove the oils — it’ll just make them spread. That can actually make cutting harder and less precise.  

**Exception:**  
If your barber or stylist always washes your hair as part of the service (many salons do), then you don’t need to wash it beforehand — just avoid heavy products or excess grease.

**Bottom line:**  
If you’re unsure, wash it the night before or a few hours prior with a light shampoo and no conditioner or styling product. Clean and dry (or freshly dampened at the salon) is the ideal state.
