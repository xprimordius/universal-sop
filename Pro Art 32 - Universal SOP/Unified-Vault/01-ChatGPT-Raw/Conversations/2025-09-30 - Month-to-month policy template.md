## 🔧 Sample Month-to-Month Cancellation Policy

**1. Term & Renewal**  
This agreement is month-to-month. It automatically renews each month unless either party gives notice to cancel at least **30 days before the next billing cycle**.

**2. Cancellation Notice**  
To cancel, the client must submit written notice (email or signed form) **no later than 30 days before** the next renewal date. Otherwise, the upcoming month will be billed.

**3. Final Billing & Pro rata**  
- If cancellation is timely, no further billing occurs after the notice period.  
- If cancellation is submitted mid-month and past the deadline, you may bill for the **full next month**, with no pro rata. (Or you may optionally prorate; choose one approach and state it clearly.)

**4. Refund Policy**  
Any fees already paid for the current month are **non-refundable**. (Or: refundable on a prorated basis if service not rendered — choose your stance.)

**5. Late Cancellation / Early Termination Fee**  
If cancellation notice is not given in time, an **“early termination” fee equal to one month’s service** will be charged.

**6. Exceptions & Goodwill**  
You may waive fees in exceptional cases (serious illness, relocation, force majeure), at your discretion — but mention this to avoid rigid appearances.

**7. Notice Method**  
Cancellation must be delivered via accepted communication channels (e.g. official email or signed notice). Verbal notice is not sufficient.

**8. Acknowledgment of Cancellation**  
You’ll confirm cancellation in writing (email) and specify end date.

---

## 🧠 Strategic Rationale (Why each element matters)

- **30-day notice** gives you time to fill or reallocate resources.  
- **Automatic renewal** prevents gaps and ambiguity (this is sometimes called an “evergreen clause”). citeturn0search9  
- **Non-refundable vs prorated** — your choice, but be consistent and transparent.  
- **Termination fee** deters non-serious cancellations, protecting your cash flow.  
- **Exception clause** keeps you human, which builds trust.  
- **Written notice & confirmation** avoids disputes and miscommunication.
