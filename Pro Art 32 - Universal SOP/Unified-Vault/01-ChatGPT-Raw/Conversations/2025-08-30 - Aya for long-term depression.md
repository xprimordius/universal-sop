Aya (ayahuasca) has shown potential in helping people with long-term depression, but the evidence is still limited and not universally conclusive. Here’s what is known:  

### Research Evidence  
- **Rapid Antidepressant Effects:** Clinical studies suggest ayahuasca can produce rapid reductions in depressive symptoms, sometimes within hours, lasting days to weeks.  
- **Treatment-Resistant Depression:** Some participants with years of treatment-resistant depression (similar to your 15-year case) experienced significant relief after ceremonies.  
- **Mechanisms:** Ayahuasca influences serotonin (like SSRIs), increases brain plasticity (BDNF), and often facilitates emotional release and new perspectives.  

### Cautions  
- **Not a Cure-All:** Relief may not be permanent; integration (therapy, lifestyle changes, community support) is critical.  
- **Risks:** Can interact dangerously with antidepressants (SSRIs, MAOIs, stimulants, etc.). Medical and psychiatric screening is essential.  
- **Setting Matters:** Benefits are strongly tied to safe, structured ceremonies with skilled facilitators.  

### Bottom Line  
- **Yes, it can help with chronic depression**, even long-standing cases, but it’s not guaranteed and not risk-free.  
- The strongest outcomes come when it’s combined with integration therapy, proper preparation, and medical oversight.
