The funding required to start an AI tech company with a custom-built location and cover all expenses for five years depends on several factors, including the scope of the company, the size and expertise of the team, the complexity of the technology, and the location of the custom-built facility. Here’s a rough breakdown of the key components and potential costs:

### 1. **Custom-Built Location**
   - **Land and Building Costs**: Costs vary significantly by location, but a rough estimate for a tech hub in the U.S. could be $5M to $15M for land and construction.
   - **Facilities and Infrastructure**: For a state-of-the-art facility including labs, data centers, and office space, expect $10M to $20M.
   - **Technology Infrastructure**: High-performance computing resources, servers, networking equipment, and specialized AI hardware could add another $5M to $10M.

   **Total for Location**: $20M to $45M

### 2. **Employee Salaries and Benefits**
   - **Core Team**: A small to medium AI startup might start with 20-50 employees, including AI researchers, data scientists, engineers, and support staff.
   - **Salaries**: Average salaries could range from $100K to $250K per year, depending on the role and experience level.
   - **Benefits**: Include health insurance, retirement contributions, and other perks, adding about 20-30% to salary costs.

   **Total for 5 Years**:
   - 20 Employees: $24M to $60M
   - 50 Employees: $60M to $150M

### 3. **Operating Expenses**
   - **Utilities and Maintenance**: $1M to $3M annually for a facility of this scale.
   - **Software Licenses and Cloud Services**: $1M to $5M annually, depending on the needs.
   - **Legal, Accounting, and Administrative Costs**: $500K to $2M annually.
   - **Marketing and Sales**: $2M to $10M over five years, depending on growth strategy.

   **Total for 5 Years**: $20M to $50M

### 4. **Research and Development**
   - **R&D Costs**: For an AI company, continuous R&D is crucial. Estimate $2M to $10M annually depending on the scope of projects.
   
   **Total for 5 Years**: $10M to $50M

### **Grand Total Estimate**
   - **Minimum**: $74M to $120M
   - **Maximum**: $205M to $295M

This range reflects a moderate-sized AI tech company. Large-scale operations or projects requiring cutting-edge technology and talent could push these numbers higher. The variability in costs stems from different operational scales, geographic locations, and specific technology needs.
