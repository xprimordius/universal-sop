## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes, **small dogs can eat cheese** in moderation, but consider the following:

### ✅ Safe if:
- **Low-lactose cheeses** like mozzarella, cheddar, or cottage cheese are used
- Given as a **small treat** (tiny cubes or shreds)
- Dog has **no dairy intolerance** (watch for diarrhea, gas, or vomiting)

### ❌ Avoid if:
- Dog is **lactose intolerant** (common in many dogs)
- Cheese is **high in fat or sodium** (like blue cheese, brie, or processed slices)
- It contains **additives** like garlic, onion, or artificial flavors (toxic)

### Best practices:
- Limit to **<10% of daily calories**
- Use cheese to **hide pills** or as **training treats**
