## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here’s the **quick fix checklist** for when your AirPods say “Connected” but audio plays through your iPhone speakers:

---

### ✅ **1. Force Reconnect**
- Swipe down → open **Control Center**
- Tap **AirPlay icon** (top-right of music panel)
- Manually select your **AirPods**

---

### ✅ **2. Forget and Re-pair**
- Go to **Settings > Bluetooth**
- Tap “ℹ️” next to AirPods → **Forget This Device**
- Reopen AirPods case near iPhone → Reconnect

---

### ✅ **3. Reset AirPods**
- Put AirPods in case → close lid 30 sec
- Open lid → press and hold **back button** on case until LED flashes amber → white
- Re-pair

---

### ✅ **4. Set as Default Audio Output**
- **Settings > Accessibility > AirPods** → Ensure **Audio Routing** is set to **Automatic**

---

### ✅ **5. Disable Automatic Ear Detection**
- **Settings > Bluetooth > AirPods ℹ️**
- Turn off **Automatic Ear Detection** → test playback

---

If still broken: test with **another device** to confirm if it’s an AirPods hardware issue.
