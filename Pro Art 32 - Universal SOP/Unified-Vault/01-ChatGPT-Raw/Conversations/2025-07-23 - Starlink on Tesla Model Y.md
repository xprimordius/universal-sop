🔌 **Why Pure Sine Wave Inverter?**  
To safely power sensitive electronics (like Starlink), you want **clean, stable electricity** that mimics home AC power. Pure sine wave avoids:
- 🧨 Overheating
- 🧠 Glitches in routers/modems
- ⚠️ Long-term device damage

---

⚠️ **About Inverter Wattage vs 12V Outlet Limit**:

| Concept                  | Explanation                                                                 |
|--------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Tesla 12V outlet max** | ~120W (12V x 10A). Hard physical limit.                                     |
| **Inverter rating (300W)** | This is **capacity**, not guaranteed output. It just means it *can* handle up to 300W. |
| **Draw limited by source** | Even a 300W inverter can only **draw 120W max from Tesla’s 12V outlet**.         |

So:
- You won’t **get 300W** from that outlet.
- But a **150–300W pure sine inverter** is good because it's:
  - Efficient at **low loads** (like Starlink at 75W)
  - Overhead protects from spikes
  - Higher build quality (often quieter and more stable)

---

✅ **Bottom Line**:
- **Pure sine wave = stability + safety**
- **Inverter size = headroom**, not overdraw
- Always stay within Tesla’s 120W limit via 12V outlet or use a separate battery for more draw.
