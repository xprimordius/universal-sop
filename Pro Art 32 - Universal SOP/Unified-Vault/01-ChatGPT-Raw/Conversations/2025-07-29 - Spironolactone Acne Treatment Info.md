## 🤖 ChatGPT

If you sit on something rusty and it punctures or scratches your skin, the main concern is **tetanus**, caused by *Clostridium tetani* bacteria that can be present on rusty or dirty objects.  

### Risks:
1. **Tetanus** – bacteria can enter through the wound; causes muscle stiffness, spasms, and can be life-threatening if untreated.  
2. **Skin infection** – redness, swelling, pain, pus.  
3. **Bloodstream infection (rare)** – if bacteria spread from the wound.  

### What to do:
- **Clean the area immediately** with soap and water.  
- **Apply antiseptic** and cover it.  
- **Seek medical care** if:
  - The skin is punctured or bleeding significantly.
  - You’re unsure about your **tetanus vaccination status** (booster needed every 10 years, or 5 years if the wound is dirty).
  - Signs of infection appear (swelling, warmth, pus, fever).  

**If it’s just sitting on rust with no wound:** no significant health risk—rust itself isn’t harmful to intact skin.
