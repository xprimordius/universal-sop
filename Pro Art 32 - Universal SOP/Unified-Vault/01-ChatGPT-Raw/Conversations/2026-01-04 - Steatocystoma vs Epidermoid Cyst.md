## Steatocystoma
**What it is**
- Benign cysts from **sebaceous (oil) glands**
- Often **multiple**, small, soft/yellowish
- Common on **chest, armpits, neck, groin**
- Can be genetic (steatocystoma multiplex)

**Symptoms**
- Usually painless
- May swell or inflame; oily fluid if opened

**How to treat (no true “cure”)**
- **Surgical removal** (best for single/few lesions)
- **Laser therapy (CO₂ / Er:YAG)** for many lesions
- **Isotretinoin (Accutane)** → may shrink/inflame less; **does not eliminate**
- **Steroid injections** for inflamed cysts

**Key reality**
- Chronic tendency. Goal = **control + cosmetic improvement**, not eradication.

---

## Epidermoid Cyst
**What it is**
- Benign cyst from **trapped skin cells**
- Usually **single**, firm, slow-growing
- Anywhere on the body
- Filled with **keratin** (thick, foul-smelling)

**Symptoms**
- Painless lump
- Can become red, painful if infected

**How to treat (curable)**
- **Complete surgical excision** (removes cyst wall) → **permanent**
- **Incision & drainage** → temporary (high recurrence)
- **Antibiotics** only if infected (doesn’t remove cyst)

**Key reality**
- If the wall is fully removed, it’s **done for good**.

---

## Quick Comparison

| Feature | Steatocystoma | Epidermoid Cyst |
|------|---|---|
| Origin | Sebaceous gland | Epidermal skin cells |
| Count | Often multiple | Usually single |
| Contents | Oily/sebum | Keratin |
| Curable? | ❌ Chronic | ✅ Yes (with excision) |
| Best fix | Laser / selective surgery | Full surgical removal |

## Why Someone Gets Cysts All Over the Body

**Short answer:** it’s usually **genetics + oil gland behavior + skin cell turnover**, sometimes amplified by hormones or inflammation.

---

## The Core Causes (Ranked by Frequency)

### 1. **Genetic Predisposition (Most Common)**
- Some people are born with **overactive sebaceous (oil) glands**
- Conditions like **steatocystoma multiplex** or **familial epidermoid cysts**
- Often starts **teens–30s**
- Runs in families

➡️ *This is the #1 reason for “cysts everywhere”*

---

### 2. **Sebaceous Gland Dysfunction**
- Oil glands overproduce sebum
- Ducts don’t drain properly → cyst formation
- Common in **chest, armpits, groin, neck**

---

### 3. **Hormonal Influence**
- Androgens (testosterone/DHT) stimulate oil glands
- Puberty, pregnancy, TRT, PCOS can worsen cysts
- Explains flare-ups during certain life stages

---

### 4. **Abnormal Skin Cell Shedding**
- Skin cells don’t shed correctly
- They get trapped → **epidermoid cysts**
- Can follow acne, friction, or minor trauma

---

### 5. **Chronic Inflammation or Acne History**
- Long-term acne alters follicle structure
- Leads to repeated cyst formation

---

### 6. **Rare Systemic or Genetic Syndromes**
(Uncommon, but real)
- Steatocystoma multiplex
- Pachyonychia congenita
- Basal cell nevus syndrome

Only considered if cysts are **extreme, early-onset, or accompanied by other symptoms**.

---

## What It’s *Not* (Common Myths)
- ❌ Poor hygiene  
- ❌ “Toxins leaving the body”  
- ❌ Infection spreading internally  
- ❌ Cancer (benign in >99%)
