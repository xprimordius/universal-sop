# Example — Goal: **$10,000 / month**

## 1. Subscriptions
Typical Twitch split ≈ **$2.50 per Tier-1 sub**

$10,000 ÷ $2.50 ≈ **4,000 subs**

But most streamers diversify.

Example realistic mix:

| Revenue Source | Monthly Target | Math |
|---|---|---|
| Subs | $4,000 | 1,600 subs × $2.50 |
| Donations / Bits | $2,000 | ~$65/day |
| Ads | $1,000 | depends on avg viewers & CPM |
| Sponsorships / affiliate | $3,000 | 1–2 sponsors |

Total = **$10,000**

---

# Now work backwards again

## Sub math → viewers

Typical Twitch conversion:

- **2–5% of viewers subscribe**

So:

1,600 subs ÷ 0.03 ≈ **53,000 unique viewers/month**

or roughly

**300–800 concurrent viewers**

(depending on stream hours)

# Business System Approach

Track **5 metrics weekly**

| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Avg concurrent viewers | core growth metric |
| Unique monthly viewers | audience size |
| Sub conversion rate | monetization efficiency |
| Revenue per viewer | business efficiency |
| Content discovery sources | growth constraint |

# Constraint-based growth model

Find the bottleneck:

| Constraint | Fix |
|---|---|
| Low discovery | YouTube / TikTok clips |
| High viewers low subs | improve CTAs + perks |
| Low retention | better stream structure |
| Low sponsor interest | niche authority |
