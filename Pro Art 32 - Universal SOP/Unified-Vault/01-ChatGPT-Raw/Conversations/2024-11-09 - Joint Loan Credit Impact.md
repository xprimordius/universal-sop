Yes, a joint access or joint pledge loan typically impacts both individuals' credit scores and appears on both of their credit reports. When two people co-sign or jointly take on a loan, they share equal responsibility for the debt, meaning:

1. **Credit Report Impact**: The loan account will be listed on both credit reports, reflecting the loan balance, payment history, and other details. Each person’s credit report will show the same information for that account.
   
2. **Credit Score Impact**: Any activity on the joint loan, such as on-time payments, late payments, or an increase in the loan balance, will influence both parties' credit scores. Positive behavior (like timely payments) can improve both scores, while missed payments or high balances can negatively impact both.

Because of this shared responsibility, it’s crucial for both parties to communicate and agree on a clear plan for managing payments.
