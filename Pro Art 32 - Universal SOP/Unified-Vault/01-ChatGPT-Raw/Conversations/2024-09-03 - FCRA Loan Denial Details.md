# FCRA Loan Denial Details

---

## 👤 You

Is it in violation of the FCRA for Penfed to not give me specific information to the denial of my personal loan? They are saying something super generic like “no eligible offer due to one fed loan or payment requirement”

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes, it could be a violation of the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) if PenFed is not providing specific information regarding the denial of your personal loan application. The FCRA requires that when a consumer is denied credit based on information in a credit report, the creditor must provide specific reasons for the denial. 

Under the FCRA, you are entitled to:

1. **Adverse Action Notice**: This notice must include the specific reasons for the denial or tell you how you can obtain those reasons. "No eligible offer due to one fed loan or payment requirement" might be too vague, and you have the right to ask for more detailed reasons.

2. **Right to Dispute**: If the denial was based on incorrect or incomplete information in your credit report, you have the right to dispute that information with the credit reporting agencies.

3. **Access to Your Credit Report**: If you are denied credit, you are entitled to a free copy of your credit report from the credit reporting agency that provided the information to the lender.

If PenFed’s explanation is too generic and does not provide the specific reasons for the denial, you may want to request a more detailed explanation in writing. If they refuse to provide this information, it might be worth consulting with a consumer protection attorney to explore your options under the FCRA.
