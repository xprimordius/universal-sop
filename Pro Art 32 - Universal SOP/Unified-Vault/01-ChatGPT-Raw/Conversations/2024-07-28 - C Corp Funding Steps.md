### Steps to Set Up a C Corporation and Access Funding

1. **Accredited Investor Requirements:**
   - **Micro Level:** $1,000,000 to $1.5 million is the target range for an accredited investor.
   - **Loss Tolerance:** Ability to lose $250,000 without significant financial distress.

2. **Establishing a C Corporation:**
   - **Good Standing Letter:** Ensure the corporation is in good standing, meaning it’s compliant with all legal requirements.
   - **Articles of Incorporation:** Document establishing the corporation’s creation, including its purpose and compliance with state laws.
   - **EIN Number:** Obtain an Employer Identification Number (EIN), which acts like a Social Security number for the corporation.

3. **Opening a Bank Account:**
   - Use the EIN to open a corporate bank account.

4. **Accessing Funding:**
   - **Personal Guarantor (PG):** Understand that you may need to act as a personal guarantor for corporate funding.
   - **Initial Funding Source:** Start with credit unions for initial funding.
   - **Tranches:** Borrow in stages (tranches) and manage repayments to build creditworthiness.
     - **Tranche Definition:** Each tranche should be reflected on your credit report within 3 weeks to 1.5 months.
     - **Repayment:** Ensure bills are paid before they are due to build trust and relax lending institutions for future borrowing.

### Summary:
To set up a C Corporation and access funding, ensure compliance with legal requirements, obtain an EIN, open a corporate bank account, and strategically access funding through credit unions in manageable stages, acting as a personal guarantor when necessary.
