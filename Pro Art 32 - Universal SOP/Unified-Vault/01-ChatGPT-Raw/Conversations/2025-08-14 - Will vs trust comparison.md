## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Trust vs. Will — Core Differences**  

| Feature | Will | Trust |
|---------|------|-------|
| **Purpose** | Directs how assets are distributed after death | Holds and manages assets during life and after death |
| **Takes Effect** | Only after death | Can take effect during your lifetime (living trust) |
| **Probate** | Must go through probate court (public process) | Avoids probate (private transfer of assets) |
| **Privacy** | Public record once filed in court | Remains private |
| **Control During Life** | No effect while alive | You can manage or appoint a trustee to manage assets |
| **Incapacity Planning** | Does not manage assets if you become incapacitated | Can manage assets if you’re incapacitated (if structured properly) |
| **Cost & Complexity** | Less expensive to set up | More costly and complex to set up and maintain |
| **Updates** | Simple to amend | Requires formal amendments to trust and asset retitling |
| **Court Supervision** | Court oversees distribution | Trustee follows instructions without court oversight |

**Summary:**  
- **Will** = Simpler, cheaper, but public and must go through probate; only acts after death.  
- **Trust** = More complex and costly upfront, but private, avoids probate, and can manage assets
