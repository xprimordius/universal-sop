Sharp pain in the middle-right to upper abdomen after eating salty snacks like pretzels and Goldfish could be from a few things:

**Possible Causes:**
- **Gallbladder (most likely region):** Pain after eating salty/fatty foods can trigger gallbladder irritation or gallstones. Pain is often sharp, upper-right abdomen, and may radiate to back/shoulder.
- **Stomach/duodenum (ulcer/acid irritation):** Pretzels and Goldfish are acidic/salty; could trigger gastritis or ulcer pain (usually more central or upper abdomen).
- **Gas/bloating:** Crunchy processed snacks can trap gas, causing sharp localized pain.
- **Pancreas (less likely but serious):** Upper abdominal pain after food, especially fatty food, could indicate pancreatitis (would worsen, often with nausea/vomiting).

**Red Flags – Seek Immediate Care if:**
- Pain is severe, sudden, or radiating to back/shoulder.
- You feel nauseous, vomit, or have fever/chills.
- Pain persists beyond a few hours or is getting worse.
- Abdomen becomes rigid, swollen, or extremely tender.

**What to Do Now:**
- Stop eating more for the moment.
- Drink water slowly.
- Apply gentle heat (heating pad) to the area if it feels soothing.
- Track whether it worsens with fatty/salty foods (possible gallbladder trigger).

👉 If the pain is sharp and doesn’t ease within an hour, or if any red flags above occur, it’s safest to **go to urgent care or the ER** — especially since gallbladder attacks can escalate quickly.
