## 🤖 ChatGPT

**How it works:**  
Cryptocurrency uses blockchain, a secure online ledger that records every transaction. Computers around the world verify these transactions (no banks needed), making it decentralized and nearly impossible to hack or fake.

**Why it’s better/different:**  
- **Decentralized:** No single company or government controls it.  
- **Secure:** Blockchain ensures transparency and prevents fraud.  
- **Fast & Global:** Send money anywhere instantly, often with low fees.  
- **Limited Supply:** Many cryptocurrencies (like Bitcoin) are scarce, protecting against inflation.
