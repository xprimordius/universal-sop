### **Key Insights:**
- **<3% utilization** = best for **large approvals**, **business funding**, and **credit limit increases**.
- High utilization even on one card can suppress your overall fundability.
- **Soft pull pre-approvals (Amex, FNBO, Apple Card)** tend to show **higher limits** when utilization is <5%.
