### **Key Insights**:
- **Oxidation Point**: Soybean oil (~300–320°F) is more prone to oxidation at moderate temperatures due to its high PUFA content, making it less ideal for prolonged or high-heat cooking.

If you’re concerned about oxidation and want to minimize harmful byproducts, **avocado oil** is the better choice, especially for high-heat cooking.
