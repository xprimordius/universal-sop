You can make a simple, flavorful **ponzu-style sauce** with what you have:

### **Quick Ponzu Substitute (No Mirin, No Seaweed)**

**Ingredients:**
- 3 tbsp tamari (or soy sauce)
- 1.5 tbsp rice wine vinegar
- 1 tbsp lemon juice (fresh)
- Optional: 1 tsp orange juice or a pinch of orange zest (for balance)
- Optional: 1/2 tsp honey or a few drops of maple syrup (mimics mirin’s sweetness)

**Instructions:**
1. Mix all ingredients in a bowl.
2. Let sit for at least 15 minutes (longer = better flavor meld).
3. Taste and adjust: add more lemon for brightness, tamari for salt, vinegar for tang, or sweetener for balance.

**Use** as a dipping sauce, dressing, or over grilled meats, tofu, or veggies.
