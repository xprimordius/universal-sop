### 🔑 **Top 3 Legit Methods to Remove Late Payments**

#### 1. **Goodwill Adjustment Letter (GWL)**
- Sent directly to the creditor (e.g., Capital One, Credit One).
- Politely requests removal of a late mark due to a hardship or isolated mistake.
- Works best if:
  - Account is current.
  - You have a strong payment history.
  - You're emotionally compelling but professional.

#### 2. **FCRA Compliance Challenge**
- Dispute the late mark with bureaus (EX, TU, EQ) under **FCRA** (Fair Credit Reporting Act) rules:
  - Demand full validation of the date, time, and method of the late mark.
  - Most creditors can’t verify it with Metro 2 compliance under pressure, and the item gets deleted.
- Often used with **Metro 2 dispute letters** or systems like:
  - Credit Repair Cloud
  - DIY Metro 2 kits

#### 3. **CFPB or BBB Complaints**
- File a **Consumer Financial Protection Bureau** complaint with:
  - Inconsistent reporting
  - Failure to respond
  - Violation of dispute timelines
- Creditors sometimes delete to avoid compliance exposure.

---

### 🧠 **Advanced Tactics Some Gurus Use**
> As hinted in this ad:

- Use **escalation departments** (executive or presidential office) like what’s shown with “Credit One Bank, Office of the President.”
- Time disputes after **full payment** or while negotiating.
- “Stack” results—combine methods for multiple accounts.
- Know when **compliance pressure** forces removals (e.g., no valid signature on file, failure to report consistently across all 3 bureaus).

---

### ⚠️ **Warning**
Be cautious of:
- “Credit sweep” scams (illegal full deletions).
- Fake letters or forged documents (federal offense).
- Charging fees before results (illegal under CROA).

---

3. Write a **Goodwill letter** or start a **Metro 2-based FCRA dispute**.
