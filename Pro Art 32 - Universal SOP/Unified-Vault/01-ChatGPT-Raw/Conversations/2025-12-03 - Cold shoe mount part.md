## 👤 You

What’s the part that the cold shoe goes into called to mount stuff

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

That’s called a **hot shoe mount** or **cold shoe mount receiver** — essentially the **shoe mount slot** on the camera body or cage.  

- If it provides electrical contacts (for flashes or transmitters) → it’s a **hot shoe**.  
- If it’s just a mechanical slot with no electrical connection → it’s a **cold shoe**.  

So the **part the cold shoe slides into** is the **hot shoe (or shoe mount slot)** on the camera or accessory cage.

---

## 👤 You

So hot shoe isn’t because it has electrical points?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Correct — the **“hot”** in *hot shoe* means it **has electrical contacts** that transmit signals (e.g. to fire a flash or trigger a mic).  

If it **has no electrical contacts** and is purely mechanical, it’s a **cold shoe**.  

**TL;DR:**  
- 🔥 **Hot shoe** = with power/signal pins (flash sync, etc.)  
- ❄️ **Cold shoe** = no electronics, just a mounting bracket
