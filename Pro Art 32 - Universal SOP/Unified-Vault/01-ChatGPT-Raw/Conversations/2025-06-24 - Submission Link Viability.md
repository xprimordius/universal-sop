## 🤖 ChatGPT

A **pocket listing** is a property that is **for sale but not publicly listed** on the MLS or major platforms like Zillow, Redfin, or LoopNet. Instead, it’s quietly marketed through **private channels**, often within an investor’s or agent’s network.

### Key Traits of a Pocket Listing:
- **Not on the MLS** or major public sites.
- **Shared privately** by agents, wholesalers, or sellers.
- May involve a **"quiet marketing" approach**—only certain buyers are told about it.
- Sometimes comes with a **pre-existing relationship or handshake deal**.

### Why Investors Like Pocket Listings:
- Less competition
- More room for negotiation
- Often better deals due to lack of market exposure
