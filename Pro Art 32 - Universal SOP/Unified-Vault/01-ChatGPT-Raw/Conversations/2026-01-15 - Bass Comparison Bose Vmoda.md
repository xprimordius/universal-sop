## How to quantify bass before buying (fast + reliable)
1. **Frequency response graphs (objective):**  
   - Look at **20–200 Hz** relative to mids (around **500 Hz–1 kHz**). More elevation = more bass “loudness.”  
   - Use measurement sites like RTINGS for Bose tuning notes (they explicitly describe bass emphasis/target compliance). citeturn0search3turn0search0  
2. **Seal sensitivity check (the hidden killer):**  
   - If bass changes a lot when you lightly press cups or move jaw, that model is fit-dependent. (This is why some “bass monsters” feel inconsistent.) citeturn0search7  
3. **Volume-matched A/B (subjective but fair):**  
   - Match loudness first (bass seems “better” when it’s just louder).  
4. **Low-bass vs mid-bass test tracks:**  
   - Low bass (30–60 Hz) = rumble; mid-bass (80–150 Hz) = punch. A headphone can have one without the other.
