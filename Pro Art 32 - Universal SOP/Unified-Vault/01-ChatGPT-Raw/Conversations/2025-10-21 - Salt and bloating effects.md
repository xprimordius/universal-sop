Does salt make you bloated

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes — salt (sodium) can make you **bloat or retain water**, especially when consumed in excess.  

**Mechanism:**  
- Sodium binds to water in your body.  
- High salt intake → increased water retention → puffiness or bloating, especially around the stomach and face.  
- The effect is more noticeable if your **electrolyte balance** (potassium, magnesium, water intake) is off.

**To counter it:**  
- Drink more water.  
- Eat potassium-rich foods (spinach, pumpkin seeds, salmon, avocado).  
- Avoid processed foods high in sodium.  

**TL;DR:** Salt doesn’t cause *fat* gain — just temporary *water retention and bloating*.
