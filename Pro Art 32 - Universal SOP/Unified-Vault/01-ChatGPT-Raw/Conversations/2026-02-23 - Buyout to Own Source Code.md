## 👤 You

In our agreement with atlas ai we have an option to buyout the source code but unsure if we just use it or own it - how do we negotiate a buy out to own it

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here’s the clean, black-letter way to think and negotiate this.

---

## First: **Usage vs Ownership (Non-Negotiable Distinction)**

If the agreement says **any** of the following, you **do NOT own the source code**:
- “license”
- “right to use”
- “non-exclusive”
- “subscription”
- “buyout option” without assignment language

Ownership only exists if there is an **IP assignment**.

---

## What You Want (Exact Legal Outcome)

You are negotiating for **full ownership**, not expanded usage.

That means:

**1. IP Assignment (Core)**
- “Atlas AI hereby irrevocably assigns all right, title, and interest in and to the Source Code to [Your Entity], including all derivatives.”

**2. No Retained Rights**
- No reuse by Atlas AI
- No resale
- No sublicensing
- No portfolio/demo use

**3. Delivery + Control**
- Full source repo
- Build scripts
- Dependencies
- Documentation
- Deployment keys (or clean transfer)

**4. Warranties**
- They own the code
- No third-party infringement
- No open-source copyleft contamination (GPL, AGPL, etc.)

---

## How to Negotiate the Buyout (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1 — Anchor the Frame
Say this explicitly:

> “We are not looking to expand a license. We want to convert this into a full IP assignment so the code becomes a balance-sheet asset.”

This reframes it from *software* → *corporate asset*.

---

### Step 2 — Ask the Kill Question (Yes / No)
Ask this in writing:

> “If we exercise the buyout, does Atlas AI retain **any** ownership, reuse, or licensing rights to the source code?”

If the answer isn’t an unqualified **“No”**, you don’t own it.

---

### Step 3 — Price the Buyout Correctly
Typical ranges (real-world, not theory):

- **2–5× remaining contract value**
- OR **1–3× development cost**
- OR **Fixed number tied to exclusivity loss**

You justify the discount by offering:
- Immediate cash
- No ongoing support obligations
- Attribution removal
- Liability cap

---

### Step 4 — Use This Exact Clause Structure
You don’t negotiate vibes. You negotiate language.

**Core clause you want added:**

> “Upon payment of the Buyout Fee, Atlas AI shall irrevocably assign to Client all right, title, and interest worldwide in and to the Source Code and all related intellectual property, including all derivative works, free and clear of all liens, licenses, and encumbrances.”

---

### Step 5 — Close the Risk Loop
Add:
- **Indemnity survives termination**
- **Escrow optional** (if they push back)
- **Transition period** capped (30–60 days max)

---

## What NOT to Accept (Common Traps)

❌ “Perpetual license”  
❌ “Exclusive license”  
❌ “Buyout of usage rights”  
❌ “Ownership except pre-existing materials” (without a schedule)

Those all leave them with leverage.

---

## Power Move (If They Resist)
Say this:

> “If ownership isn’t possible, then we need a material reduction in price and a clean exit path because we can’t build enterprise value on licensed core IP.”

This forces a fork:
- Sell the IP  
- Or accept reduced economics

---

## Bottom Line
- **If there’s no assignment, you don’t own it**
- A buyout must explicitly transfer **title**
- This is a valuation + risk conversation, not a technical one
