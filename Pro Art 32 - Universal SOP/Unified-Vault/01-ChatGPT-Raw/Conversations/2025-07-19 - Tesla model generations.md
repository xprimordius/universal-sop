### 🔩 What Is **Gigacasting**?

**Gigacasting** = Tesla's breakthrough process of casting huge sections of a car’s frame (like the rear or front underbody) in **one solid piece** using a **giant high-pressure die-casting machine** called the *Giga Press*.

---

## ⚙️ Traditional vs Gigacasting

|                        | **Old Method**            | **Gigacasting**               |
|------------------------|---------------------------|-------------------------------|
| **Parts per section**  | 70+ welded parts          | 1 massive cast part           |
| **Production speed**   | 🐢 Slower (welding, QA)   | ⚡️ Faster (1 shot = 1 part)   |
| **Assembly complexity**| 🔧 Very high               | ✅ Greatly reduced             |
| **Cost**               | 💰 More expensive          | 💸 Cheaper per car            |
| **Precision**          | 🟡 Variable                | ✅ Highly accurate             |

---

## ✅ Benefits of Gigacasting

- 🚀 **Faster production**
- 🔒 **Fewer failure points**
- 📉 **Lower cost per unit**
- 🧠 **Simplifies robot automation**
- 🔧 **Easier to scale globally**
