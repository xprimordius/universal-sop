## Add a Credit Card to **entity["mobile_app","Venmo"]**

### Steps
1. Open the **Venmo app**.  
2. Tap **☰ Menu** (top right).  
3. Tap **Settings**.  
4. Tap **Payment Methods**.  
5. Tap **Add bank or card**.  
6. Select **Card**.  
7. Enter your **credit card number, expiration, CVV, and ZIP**.  
8. Tap **Add**.

Your card is now available when sending payments.

### Fees
| Payment Type | Fee |
|---|---|
| Credit card payment | **3% fee** |
| Bank / debit | **Free** |
