# NDA Limits on Investigations

---

## 👤 You

If I am moving money between entity 1’s different business bank accounts and between entity 2’s bank accounts… am I protected from investigation if I sign a non disclosure non circumvent

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Signing a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) or Non-Circumvent Agreement (NCA) does not inherently protect you from investigations, particularly those conducted by regulatory authorities such as the IRS or other financial regulatory bodies. These agreements are typically used to protect confidential information and to ensure that the parties involved do not bypass each other in a business transaction, but they do not provide legal protection against scrutiny from law enforcement or regulatory investigations.

When moving money between different bank accounts, whether within the same entity or between different entities, it’s important to ensure that these transactions are transparent, well-documented, and compliant with relevant laws and regulations. Authorities may investigate if they suspect money laundering, tax evasion, or other illegal activities, regardless of any private agreements between the parties involved.

To mitigate risks:

1. **Maintain Clear Records:** Ensure all transactions are properly documented, including the purpose of the transfer, and retain all related financial records.
2. **Compliance with Laws:** Make sure the transactions comply with all applicable laws, including tax laws and anti-money laundering regulations.
3. **Consult Legal Counsel:** It’s advisable to consult with legal and financial professionals to ensure that your actions are compliant with the law and that any agreements you sign are enforceable and appropriate for your situation.

In summary, while NDAs and NCAs are useful for protecting business interests, they do not shield you from regulatory scrutiny or legal investigations. Compliance with the law is paramount in such matters.
