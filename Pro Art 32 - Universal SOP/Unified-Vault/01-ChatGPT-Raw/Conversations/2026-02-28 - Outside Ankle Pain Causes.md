### 1️⃣ Most Likely: **Peroneal Tendon Overload**
Pain on the *outside ankle* = often irritation of the **peroneal tendons** (they stabilize your foot).

Triggers:
- Overcorrection from a stability shoe  
- Shoe too wide → foot slides → lateral strain  
- Sudden mileage/speed increase  
- Tight calves  

---

### 2️⃣ Stability Shoe + Your Mechanics Mismatch  
You’re wearing **entity["product","Brooks Adrenaline GTS","running shoe model"]** (a stability shoe).

If you:
- Don’t heavily overpronate  
- Or have a neutral/high arch  

Then the medial support can push you slightly outward → loading the lateral ankle.

**Extra wide** can amplify this if your heel isn’t locked in.

---

### 3️⃣ Supination (Under-pronation)
If your wear pattern is on the *outside* of the sole, you may naturally supinate.  
Stability shoes don’t help that — they may worsen it.

---

### 4️⃣ Heel Lock Issue (Common with Extra Wide)
If the heel moves even slightly:
- Peroneals fire harder  
- Outside ankle fatigues  

Quick test: Try a **runner’s loop / heel lock lacing**.
