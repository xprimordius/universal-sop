In high finance and banking, **MT 199** is a type of message within the SWIFT (Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication) system. Here’s what it represents:

- **MT 199** is a free-format message type that is used for transmitting various forms of communication or instructions between banks. Unlike other SWIFT message types that are structured and have specific purposes (like MT 103 for customer credit transfers), the MT 199 is more flexible and can be used for a wide range of purposes, such as inquiries, confirmations, or general notifications.

- It does not carry any payment or settlement instructions. Instead, it is often used for messages that don't fit into other predefined SWIFT message categories. For example, a bank might send an MT 199 to confirm the receipt of a message, provide additional information related to a transaction, or communicate a problem or clarification needed.

- **MT 199** can be used in various contexts, such as resolving issues with transactions, providing status updates, or sending simple, non-transactional information that needs to be communicated between financial institutions.

In summary, MT 199 serves as a versatile and general-purpose messaging tool within the SWIFT network for interbank communication.

2. **M13 Message**:
