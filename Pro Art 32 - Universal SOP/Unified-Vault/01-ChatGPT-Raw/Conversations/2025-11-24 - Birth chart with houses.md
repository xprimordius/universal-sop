# 🏛 **Different major Western house systems**  
Astrology has *many* ways of dividing the sky into 12 houses. None are “more correct” — they simply reflect different traditions or interpretive styles.

Below are the most commonly used:

---

## **1. Whole-Sign Houses**
- The **oldest known system** (Hellenistic / ancient Greek astrology)  
- House = entire sign  
- Easy to interpret symbolically  
- Ascendant sign sets the 1st house  
- No unequal house sizes

Often used today in modern traditional astrology.

---

## **2. Placidus**
- Currently the **most widely used system** in modern Western astrology  
- Houses vary in size depending on latitude  
- House cusps (exact degrees) matter a lot  
- More “psychological” focus

Most online chart calculators default to Placidus.

---

## **3. Koch**
- A later European development  
- Similar to Placidus but uses time-based calculations  
- Stronger emphasis on the individual’s personal development

---

## **4. Equal House**
- 1st house begins at the Ascendant degree  
- Every house = 30°  
- More geometric and consistent than Placidus/Koch

---

## **5. Porphyry**
- Divides each quadrant into three equal parts  
- Often used for traditional aspect interpretation  
- A middle ground between Placidus and Whole-Sign

---

## **6. Campanus, Regiomontanus, Topocentric**
- These are more technical, mathematical systems  
- Often used by astrologers focused on precision

---

# 🌞 **So what did the Mayans use?**
The **Maya did *not* use the Western zodiac** or Western house systems.

They had an entirely different, rich cosmology:

---

## **✨ The Maya used:**

### **1. The Tzolk’in (260-day sacred calendar)**  
- 20 day-signs × 13 number-tones  
- Each day has a symbolic meaning  
- Used for spiritual cycles, ceremonies, and identity themes

### **2. The Haab’ (365-day solar calendar)**  
- Similar to a solar year  
- Structured differently from Western months

### **3. The Long Count**
- Tracked huge historical cycles  
- Not used the same way as a natal “chart”

### **Mayan astrology focuses on:**
- **Day-sign** (like Imix, Ik’, Manik’, etc.)  
- **Number-tone**  
- **Energy cycles of time**, rather than personality houses  
- Symbolic roles, spiritual themes, and communal identity

**No houses, no rising signs, no planets**—completely different system.
