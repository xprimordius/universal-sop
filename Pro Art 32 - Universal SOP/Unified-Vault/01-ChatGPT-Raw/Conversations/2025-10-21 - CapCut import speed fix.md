Here’s how to make **CapCut import iPhone videos instantly** (fix for slow 39-second video loading):  

---

### ⚡ 1. Pre-Optimize the File
- **Convert to H.264 MP4** (not HEVC/H.265). iPhones record in HEVC by default, which CapCut must decode.  
  → In **Photos › Share › Save to Files › Long-press › Quick Actions › Convert Image/Video**, or use a converter app.  
- **Lower resolution** to 1080 p before import; CapCut mobile chokes on 4 K HDR clips.

---

### ⚙️ 2. Fix iPhone Import Lag
- **Turn off HDR** before filming (Settings › Camera › Record Video › toggle off HDR Video).  
- **Disable Live Photos / ProRes / 10-bit color**; these add processing time.  
- **Free storage** to at least > 3 GB; CapCut needs temp cache space.  
- **Restart CapCut** to clear RAM if it’s been open long.  
- **Keep video in Files app**, not Photos, and import from there—avoids iCloud lag.

---

### 🚀 3. Fastest Workflow
1. **AirDrop** video to **Mac/PC**, convert to 1080p H.264 MP4.  
2. **Send back to iPhone** (or upload to CapCut Cloud).  
3. **Import from Files**, not Photos.

---

### 🧠 Pro Tip
CapCut mobile re-encodes on import. Use **CapCut Desktop** for instant timeline loading (no transcoding delay) or trim the clip in Photos first—shorter duration = less preprocessing.
