Here’s how to view **sent messages** depending on which “Inbox” app you mean:  

---

### 📧 **If you mean Google Inbox (old Gmail app)**  
Google officially discontinued **Inbox by Gmail** in 2019 — it automatically redirects to **Gmail**.  
➡️ Open the **Gmail app → Tap ☰ (menu) → Sent** to see your sent mail.

---

### 📱 **If you mean Apple Mail (iPhone Mail app)**  
1. Open the **Mail** app.  
2. Tap **Mailboxes** (top-left).  
3. Tap the **Sent** folder under the email account you use.  
   - Example: *iCloud → Sent* or *Gmail → Sent Mail*.

---

### 💬 **If you mean an SMS or messaging app**  
- **iPhone Messages:** all messages (sent & received) appear together in each conversation.  
- **Android Messages:** same — open a conversation to see your sent texts (they’re usually right-aligned and colored blue or green).
