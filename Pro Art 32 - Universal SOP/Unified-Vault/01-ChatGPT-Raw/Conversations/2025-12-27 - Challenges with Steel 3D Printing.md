## 👤 You

If steel 3D printed frames for homes are cheaper and better for insurance due to not burning or termites, why would builders not want to build with it

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Short answer:**  
Because *cost savings on paper ≠ profit, speed, or simplicity in reality.*

Here are the real blockers—clean and factual:

---

## 1. **Capital + Workflow Inertia**
Builders are optimized for **wood**.
- Crews trained for wood
- Tools, suppliers, subs, schedules built around wood  
Switching to steel/3D = retraining, new subs, new mistakes → **risk + delay**

Builders hate uncertainty more than inefficiency.

---

## 2. **Up-Front Cost vs Lifecycle Cost**
Steel/3D frames:
- Often **higher upfront** cost
- Savings show up over **30–50 years** (insurance, durability)

Builders don’t own the house long enough to benefit.  
They sell fast → move on → no incentive.

---

## 3. **Permitting, Code, and Inspector Friction**
Most building codes:
- Are written for **wood**
- Inspectors are unfamiliar with 3D-printed steel systems

Result:
- More reviews
- More delays
- More red tape  
Delays kill builder margins.

---

## 4. **Supply Chain Fragility**
Wood = everywhere.
Steel 3D frames = few manufacturers, longer lead times.

If one supplier slips:
- Entire project stalls
- Carrying costs explode

Builders prefer boring reliability.

---

## 5. **Financing + Appraisal Bias**
Banks, insurers, and appraisers:
- Understand wood
- Discount or question “non-standard” builds

That can mean:
- Harder loans
- Lower appraisals
- Slower closings

Builders need smooth exits.

---

## 6. **Labor Politics**
Entire trades depend on wood framing.
- Framers
- Unions
- Local contractor ecosystems

Disrupting that invites resistance—quietly or openly.

---

## 7. **Buyers Don’t Demand It (Yet)**
Most buyers:
- Don’t understand framing
- Buy on price, layout, finishes

Until buyers *demand* fireproof, termite-proof frames, builders won’t lead.

---

## The Bottom Line
Builders don’t optimize for:
- Longevity
- Insurance savings
- Structural superiority

They optimize for:
- **Speed**
- **Predictability**
- **Financing ease**
- **Repeatable profit**

Steel 3D framing **will win long-term**, but only when:
- Codes standardize
- Financing adapts
- Labor retrains
- Buyers demand it

That transition is happening—slowly.
