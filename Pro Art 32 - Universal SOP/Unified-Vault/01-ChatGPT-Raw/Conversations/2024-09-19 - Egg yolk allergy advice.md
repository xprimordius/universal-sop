It’s not advisable to try to overcome an egg yolk allergy by continuously eating it. Allergic reactions can range from mild to severe, and consuming the allergen repeatedly could worsen the response over time. Allergies occur when your immune system reacts to a substance as harmful, and exposing yourself to the allergen repeatedly can increase the risk of more serious reactions, including anaphylaxis.

Given that you experienced soreness and aching after consuming egg yolks, it's essential to consult with an allergist. They can guide you on how to manage the allergy safely, and whether desensitization or other treatments might be appropriate for you. However, self-exposure to allergens is not a recommended approach without medical supervision.
