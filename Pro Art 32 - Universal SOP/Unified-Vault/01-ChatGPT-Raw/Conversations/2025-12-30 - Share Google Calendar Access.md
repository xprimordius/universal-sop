## Share a Google Calendar with another Gmail (fast)

### Desktop (best)
1. Open **Google Calendar** (calendar.google.com)
2. Left sidebar → hover your calendar → **⋮ Settings and sharing**
3. **Share with specific people**
4. **Add email address** (the other Gmail)
5. Set permission:
   - *See only free/busy*
   - *See all event details*
   - *Make changes*
   - *Make changes & manage sharing*
6. **Send**

They must **accept the invite** from email.

---

### Mobile (view-only limits)
- Google Calendar app → ☰ → **Settings** → select calendar → **Sharing**
- For full control, use **desktop**.

---

### Pro tips
- Use **“See all event details”** for assistants.
- Use **“Make changes”** only if they’ll edit events.
- You can **remove access anytime** from the same menu.

Want to share **Gmail inbox** or **delegate email** instead?

## Share Google Calendar on **iPhone** (exact steps)

> **Important:** On iPhone, you can **view** sharing settings, but **adding a new person requires desktop mode** in Safari.

### Option A — Do it in **Safari (Desktop Mode)** ✅
1. Open **Safari** on iPhone  
2. Go to **calendar.google.com**
3. Tap **aA** (address bar) → **Request Desktop Website**
4. Sign in
5. Left sidebar → find your calendar → tap **⋮**
6. **Settings and sharing**
7. **Share with specific people**
8. **Add email address** (other Gmail)
9. Choose permission level
10. **Send**

They must accept the invite.

---

### Option B — Google Calendar App (limitations)
- Calendar app → ☰ → **Settings** → select calendar → **Sharing**
- You can **see who has access** and **remove users**
- You **cannot add new people** here
