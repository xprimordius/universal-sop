Brie cheese is **not inherently unhealthy**, but it’s high in **fat and calories**, so moderation matters.  

**Quick breakdown (per 1 oz / 28 g):**  
- **Calories:** ~95  
- **Fat:** ~8 g (about 5 g saturated)  
- **Protein:** ~6 g  
- **Carbs:** ~0.1 g  
- **Calcium:** ~5% of daily value  

**Pros:**  
- High-quality protein and calcium.  
- Contains beneficial bacteria (if unpasteurized).  
- Provides vitamin A and B12.  

**Cons:**  
- High in saturated fat and sodium.  
- Can contribute to excess calories if eaten in large amounts.  
- Unpasteurized Brie may pose risks for pregnant or immunocompromised people.  

**Bottom line:**  
Occasional Brie is fine — even beneficial as part of a balanced, whole-food diet. It becomes “unhealthy” mainly through **quantity or pairing** (e.g., eating large amounts with bread, wine, or processed meats).
