# Multinational Cybercrime Agencies

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Several multinational investigative agencies specialize in investigating scams and crimes online. These agencies operate across borders and have the capability to handle complex cybercrime cases. Here are some of the prominent ones:

### 1. **INTERPOL (International Criminal Police Organization)**
- **Role:** Facilitates international police cooperation and assists in combating various forms of cybercrime, including online scams, fraud, and cyber-attacks.
- **Website:** [INTERPOL](https://www.interpol.int)

### 2. **Europol (European Union Agency for Law Enforcement Cooperation)**
- **Role:** Supports EU member states in fighting serious international crime and terrorism, including cybercrime and online scams.
- **Website:** [Europol](https://www.europol.europa.eu)

### 3. **FBI (Federal Bureau of Investigation) - Cyber Division**
- **Role:** Investigates and combats cybercrime within the United States and collaborates with international partners on transnational cybercrime cases.
- **Website:** [FBI Cyber Crime](https://www.fbi.gov/investigate/cyber)

### 4. **United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC)**
- **Role:** Provides technical assistance and expertise in combating cybercrime and enhancing the capabilities of member states to tackle cyber threats.
- **Website:** [UNODC](https://www.unodc.org)

### 5. **NCA (National Crime Agency) - National Cyber Crime Unit (NCCU)**
- **Role:** Leads the UK's fight against cybercrime and works with international partners to address global cyber threats.
- **Website:** [NCA](https://www.nationalcrimeagency.gov.uk)

### 6. **GCC High-Tech Crime Unit**
- **Role:** A specialized unit within the Gulf Cooperation Council focusing on cybercrime in the Gulf region and collaborating with international agencies.
- **Website:** Information typically found on respective GCC member state law enforcement websites.

### 7. **Australian Cyber Security Centre (ACSC)**
- **Role:** Works to improve Australia's cyber resilience and collaborates with international partners on cybercrime investigations.
- **Website:** [ACSC](https://www.cyber.gov.au)

### 8. **RCMP (Royal Canadian Mounted Police) - National Cybercrime Coordination Unit (NC3)**
- **Role:** Leads Canada's efforts in investigating cybercrime and collaborates with international counterparts to combat cyber threats.
- **Website:** [RCMP Cybercrime](https://www.rcmp-grc.gc.ca/en/cybercrime)

These agencies often collaborate on a global scale, sharing intelligence and resources to tackle online scams and cybercrime effectively. If you suspect an online scam or cybercrime, you can contact the relevant agency based on your location and the nature of the crime.
