Difference in thc and cbd

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**TLDR:**

- **THC (Tetrahydrocannabinol):**  
  - **Psychoactive** – causes a “high”  
  - Alters mood, perception, appetite  
  - Found in marijuana  
  - Federally illegal (but legal in some states)

- **CBD (Cannabidiol):**  
  - **Non-psychoactive** – no high  
  - Used for anxiety, pain, inflammation  
  - Found in both hemp and marijuana  
  - Federally legal if from hemp (<0.3% THC)

**Key Difference:**  
**THC gets you high. CBD doesn’t.**

## 🤖 ChatGPT

When **THC and CBD are mixed**, the **high is typically smoother, more balanced, and less anxious** than THC alone.

### How it works:
- **CBD modulates THC’s effects** – it can **reduce anxiety, paranoia, and intensity** of the high.
- The **more CBD relative to THC**, the **milder the high**.
- The **more THC relative to CBD**, the **stronger and more euphoric the high**.

### Common experiences with THC + CBD mixes:
- **Relaxed but clear-headed**
- **Euphoric without paranoia**
- **Body high with mild mental buzz**
- **Less “couch lock” or mental fog**

### Ratio guide:
- **1:1 (THC:CBD)** – Balanced, therapeutic, light buzz  
- **2:1 or 3:1 THC:CBD** – Stronger high, with some CBD to ease edge  
- **High CBD, low THC** – Minimal to no high, mostly calming
