# Creditor Reporting Late Payments

---

## 👤 You

Is it true that a creditor cannot report a payment late for any reason via the FCRA

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

No, it is not true that a creditor cannot report a payment late for any reason under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA). The FCRA does not prohibit creditors from reporting late payments. However, it does regulate the accuracy and fairness of the information provided to credit reporting agencies. If a payment is genuinely late according to the terms of the credit agreement, the creditor can report it as such. The FCRA ensures that any reported information is accurate and provides consumers with rights to dispute incorrect information.

Your understanding of the relevant statutes is mostly correct, but there are some nuances that need to be addressed. Let me break down the statutes mentioned:

1. **15 USC 1681 (Section 602) - Congressional findings and statement of purpose:**
   - This section outlines the purpose of the FCRA, which includes protecting consumers' privacy and ensuring the accuracy and fairness of credit reporting.

2. **15 USC 1681b - Permissible purposes of consumer reports:**
   - This section details the permissible purposes for which a consumer reporting agency may furnish a consumer report. Typically, this includes situations like credit transactions, employment purposes, and court orders. The provision you mentioned about requiring written instructions generally applies to certain specific scenarios, not all credit reporting activities.

3. **15 USC 1681 (Section 604A) - Protection of medical information:**
   - Section 604A specifically deals with the protection of medical information in credit reporting and is not broadly applicable to general credit reporting practices.

4. **15 USC 1666b - Late fees:**
   - This section, part of the Truth in Lending Act, restricts how creditors can apply late fees on open-end credit plans (e.g., credit cards). It states that if a payment is received by the due date (or within any grace period), it cannot be treated as late.
