## 🤖 ChatGPT

No — **you can do a lease buyout *anytime*** during the lease, **not just at the end.**

There are two types:

1. **End-of-Lease Buyout**  
   - Buy the car at lease end for the **residual value** (pre-agreed).
   - Most common and straightforward.

2. **Early Lease Buyout**  
   - Buy the car **before lease ends**.
   - Cost = residual value + remaining payments + early termination fees (if any).
   - Requires lender (lessor) approval.

**Key**:  
If you want to refinance into lower monthly payments via credit union → you'd do an **early lease buyout + finance the buyout** with the credit union.

**Steps**:
- Ask leasing company for buyout payoff amount.
- Get pre-approved with credit union for auto loan.
- Compare total cost vs keeping lease.

✅ Doable if numbers make sense.  
📌 Tip: Credit union may fund it only if car value ≥ buyout amount.
