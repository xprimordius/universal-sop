Here’s a well-balanced juice recipe that combines your ingredients for a flavorful and nutrient-packed drink:

4. Incorporate the **apple** as a flavor enhancer, about 10%.
5. Add the **ginger** to taste (start with a small piece and adjust if you want more spice).

### Steps:
1. Juice the celery, beet, apple, and ginger in your Nama juicer.
2. Combine the fresh juice with the orange and pineapple juice.
3. Mix well, taste, and adjust ginger or citrus levels as needed.

This ratio ensures a harmonious balance between sweetness, spice, and freshness while highlighting the natural flavors of each ingredient.

Can I leave on the butts? Or do I need to remove them for the beets, carrots, and celery

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

It's generally best to **trim the "butts" or ends** of the vegetables, as they can carry dirt or have a bitter taste that might affect the flavor of your juice. Here's a breakdown for each:

1. **Beets:** Trim off both ends, especially the root tip and stem base, as they can be tough and earthy.
2. **Carrots:** Trim off the tops and any rough or dry ends for better flavor and cleanliness.
3. **Celery:** Remove the dry or tough root base to avoid clogging your juicer and ensure better juice extraction.

While it's not harmful to leave them on if thoroughly washed, trimming improves the taste and texture of the juice.

1. **Check Assembly:**
   - Ensure all parts (e.g., the auger, strainer, and juicing bowl) are correctly assembled and aligned. Most juicers have a safety feature that prevents them from turning on if improperly assembled.

   - Make sure the lid is properly locked into place. The juicer won't operate unless the lid is securely fastened.

3. **Inspect the Power Source:**
   - Double-check that the outlet is functioning by testing it with another device.
   - Ensure the power cord is firmly plugged into both the wall outlet and the juicer.

4. **Reset the Juicer:**
   - If the motor has overheated, unplug the juicer and let it cool for 10–20 minutes before trying again.
   - Some models have a reset button—check your manual to locate and press it.

5. **Check for Obstructions:**
   - Disassemble the juicer and inspect for any blockages or misaligned parts that could be preventing operation.

6. **Toggle the Power Button:**
   - Ensure the power button is switched to the "ON" position. Test it by switching it on and off again.

7. **Consult the Manual:**
   - Look for troubleshooting tips specific to your Nama juicer model in the user manual.

If none of these steps work, contact **Nama customer support** for assistance. They can help you further or provide warranty support if needed.
