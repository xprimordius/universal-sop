# Chase Business Card Usage

---

It was mentioned not to Max out a chase business card because they report to commercial something? Can you tell me more about this

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Chase business credit cards generally do not report your activity to personal credit bureaus unless your account falls into delinquency. However, they do report to business credit bureaus like Dun & Bradstreet, Experian Business, and Equifax Business. This means if you max out a Chase business card, it could negatively affect your business credit score rather than your personal credit score.

When you carry a high balance relative to your credit limit (i.e., a high utilization rate), business credit bureaus may view this as a sign of financial instability, which can lower your business credit score. Unlike personal credit, where high utilization impacts your score once it exceeds 30%, business credit bureaus typically expect lower utilization, ideally below 15%-20%.

This could make it harder for your business to secure loans or new lines of credit at favorable rates.
