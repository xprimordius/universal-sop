If you consume more than about **40 mg of zinc per day (≈300 %+ of the RDA)** consistently, your body can start showing **toxicity effects** — especially if done for more than a few days.  

### ⚠️ Short-Term Effects
- **Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea**
- **Metallic taste** or loss of taste/smell  
- **Abdominal cramps**
- **Headache and fatigue**

### ⚠️ Long-Term or High-Dose (chronic) Effects
- **Copper deficiency** → anemia, low white blood cells, fatigue
- **Lowered immune function** (ironically)
- **Disrupted cholesterol balance** (↓ HDL)
- **Nerve issues** from mineral imbalance  

### ⚖️ Safe Range
- **RDA:** ~11 mg/day (men), 8 mg/day (women)  
- **Upper limit (UL):** 40 mg/day total from all sources (food + supplements)

### ✅ What To Do
If you’ve taken a one-time dose around 300% (≈30–35 mg), you’re likely fine — just **don’t repeat daily**.  
If you’ve been taking it chronically or experience GI upset or metallic taste, **stop supplements** and **hydrate**; symptoms usually resolve within 24–48 h.
