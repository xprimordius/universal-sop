**Scope of Work:**

- Handle **basic banking services**, such as account openings, credit cards, personal loans, and small business accounts.
- Provide **general financial advice** without complex customization.

**Responsibilities:**
1. Managing day-to-day client needs like deposits, withdrawals, or minor complaints.
2. Cross-selling basic financial products (e.g., savings accounts, auto loans, personal lines of credit).

**Target Market:**

- Clients with net worth <$1M.
- Small loans (up to ~$250k).

**Skills Required:**
- Customer service.
- Product knowledge of retail and basic business banking.
- Sales-oriented mindset.

**Limitations:**
- Cannot handle high-value or complex cases (e.g., commercial lending or sophisticated investments).
- Generally require support from Level 2 managers for escalations.

---

### **Level 2 Relationship Managers**
**Scope of Work:**
- Work with **high-net-worth individuals (HNWIs)**, **corporate clients**, or **commercial banking customers**.
- Provide **tailored financial solutions**, including investment management, commercial lending, and strategic banking advice.

**Responsibilities:**
1. Managing complex accounts, including businesses with revenues >$10M or HNWIs with assets >$1M.
2. Structuring advanced credit facilities (e.g., lines of credit >$1M, syndications).
3. Offering wealth management services or business expansion strategies.

**Target Market:**
- Corporate clients, entrepreneurs, and HNWIs.
- Businesses needing advisory or sophisticated banking products.

**Skills Required:**
- Advanced financial expertise (commercial banking, investment services).
- Relationship-building with corporate executives or HNWIs.
- Strategic advisory mindset.

**Advantages:**
- Access to bank’s senior resources (legal, compliance, underwriting).
- Authority to negotiate terms for large credit or financing packages.
- Deeper understanding of market trends and tailored services.

**Limitations:**
- May not handle smaller, simpler cases efficiently due to higher client thresholds.

| **Focus**                 | Day-to-day banking                      | Strategic growth and relationship management |

---

**Who You Need Depends On Your Goals:**
- For basic accounts or small-scale needs: **Level 1.**
