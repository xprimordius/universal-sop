## 👤 You

I started 80mg TRT per dose at twice a week and didn’t really feel it. I moved up to 90mg on my own and it does feel better. If I work up to 100mg x2 a week will this be okay if it feels good? How will this affect TRT with my doc

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Straight answer:

> 100mg x2/week (200mg total) is *above* standard TRT dosing.  
Most docs prescribe 100-150mg/week total.  
180-200mg/week pushes you closer to a “blast” dose — still safe *short term* if monitored — but carries more risk of:  
- Increased hematocrit  
- Estrogen conversion (E2 sides)  
- Lipid profile worsening  
- Blood pressure elevation  
- Long-term cardiovascular strain  

### If it "feels good" — it's likely because:  
- Higher free T  
- Stable serum levels  
- More androgenic activity  

### Impact on Doc Relationship:  
If you're monitored quarterly — and labs are clean — many docs won't care unless:  
- Total T is superphysiologic (>1500 ng/dL)  
- Hematocrit is high (>53%)  
- Estrogen is out of range  
- Lipids tank  

But — if your doc is conservative — they may:  
- Cut your dose  
- Flag you for non-compliance  
- Drop you as a patient  

---

### Optimal Strategy:  
> Work with your doc transparently — frame it like:  
"I increased slightly because I wasn't feeling symptom relief — I’d like to find the *lowest effective dose* together and keep labs in check."

This shows responsibility + willingness to dial back if labs show issues.

---

### Final Thought:  
> 200mg/week TRT *can* be fine for some — but only *if* labs support it.  
Test -> Analyze -> Adjust.  

Blind escalation without labs = dumb risk.
