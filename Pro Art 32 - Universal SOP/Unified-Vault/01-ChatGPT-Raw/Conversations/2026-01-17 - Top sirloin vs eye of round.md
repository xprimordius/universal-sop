| Cut | Tenderness | Flavor | Best Uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Top Sirloin** | **Medium-tender ✅** | Beefy, balanced | Grilling, pan-sear, steak |
| **Eye of Round** | **Very lean, tough ❌** | Mild | Roast beef, deli meat, jerky |

### Why this is true
- **Top sirloin** comes from the sirloin (less-worked muscle) → **naturally more tender**
- **Eye of round** comes from the round (hard-working leg muscle) → **dense, chewy**
- Eye of round has **almost no intramuscular fat**, which directly reduces tenderness

### Verdict (no debate)
- **Steak night / pan or grill → Top sirloin**
- **Low-fat slicing / slow cook / jerky → Eye of round**
