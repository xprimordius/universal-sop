**Elite Salmon & Dill Scrambled Eggs (Fast, Perfect)**

### 🧂 Ingredients (1–2 servings):
- 3–4 eggs  
- 1–2 oz smoked or cooked salmon (flaked)  
- 1 tbsp fresh dill (chopped)  
- 1 tbsp butter (or olive oil)  
- Salt & black pepper (to taste)  
- Optional: 1 tbsp milk or cream (for softer eggs)

---

### 🔥 Steps:

1. **Crack & whisk eggs**  
   Beat eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper. Add milk/cream if desired.

2. **Heat pan (low-medium)**  
   Add butter or oil, let it melt without browning.

3. **Cook eggs slowly**  
   Pour in eggs. Stir gently with a spatula as they begin to set.

4. **Add salmon + dill (midway)**  
   Once eggs are half-cooked, gently fold in salmon and dill. Continue stirring until soft and creamy.

5. **Serve immediately**  
   Finish with extra dill on top if desired.

---

### 💡 Tips:
- Low heat = creamy texture  
- Use cold-smoked salmon for luxury flavor; hot-smoked for heartiness  
- Serve with sourdough, avocado, or crème fraîche for an upgrade
