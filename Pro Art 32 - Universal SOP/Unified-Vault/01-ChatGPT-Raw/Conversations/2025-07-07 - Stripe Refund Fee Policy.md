### 🧠 Summary – What Happens on a Partial Refund:

- **Customer gets back** the requested amount.
- **You lose** the refunded amount **plus** the original Stripe fees (e.g., 2.9% + $0.30).
- **Stripe does not** refund any of their processing fees — they keep them. citeturn0search3turn0search6

---

### Why It Works That Way
The fee covers costs already consumed:
- Bank and card network charges
- Fraud protection
- Infrastructure/support already rendered

These costs aren't reversible once the transaction happened. citeturn0search0turn0search3

---

### Example:
- You charged **$100**  
- Stripe fees: **$3.20** (2.9% + $0.30)  
- You net **$96.80**

Now refund **$50**:
- Customer receives $50
- You lose $50 in revenue  
- Stripe still keeps **$3.20**

**Net loss to you: $53.20**

---

### Takeaway
- Stripe charges no **extra fee** for processing a refund.
- You **don’t get back** the original processing fees — they are **non‑refundable**.
