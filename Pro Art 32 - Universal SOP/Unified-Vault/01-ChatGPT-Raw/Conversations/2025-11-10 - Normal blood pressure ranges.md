Normal adult blood pressure:  
**Around 120/80 mm Hg** (spoken as “120 over 80”).

**Ranges:**  
- **Normal:** Systolic < 120 and Diastolic < 80  
- **Elevated:** Systolic 120–129 and Diastolic < 80  
- **High (Stage 1):** Systolic 130–139 or Diastolic 80–89  
- **High (Stage 2):** Systolic ≥ 140 or Diastolic ≥ 90  
- **Hypertensive crisis:** Systolic > 180 and/or Diastolic > 120 → seek emergency care.
