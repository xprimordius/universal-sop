# FCRA Dispute Response Time

---

## 👤 You

What’s the FCRA law that says if they don’t respond to my validation request within 30 days, they legally must remove the remark from my credit profile

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

The Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) requires credit reporting agencies and furnishers of information to investigate disputes over the accuracy of information on a consumer's credit report. Specifically, under the FCRA, if a consumer disputes the accuracy of information, the credit reporting agency must investigate the claim within 30 days.

According to **15 U.S. Code § 1681i(a)(1)(A)**:

1. If a consumer notifies the credit reporting agency directly of a dispute concerning the accuracy or completeness of any item of information contained in their file, the agency must conduct a reasonable reinvestigation to determine whether the disputed information is inaccurate.
2. The credit reporting agency must complete this reinvestigation within 30 days from the date it receives the notice of the dispute from the consumer.

If the credit reporting agency fails to respond within the 30-day period, they are required to remove or correct the disputed information on the credit report. This means that if a credit bureau does not respond to your validation request within the specified time frame, they must delete the disputed remark from your credit profile.
