# High pitched car noise

---

## 👤 You

High pitched idling sound coming from my car, like similar to how worn brake pads sound while breaking and  screeching

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

A high-pitched idling sound, similar to worn brake pads screeching, can be caused by several issues unrelated to the brake pads themselves, especially when it occurs during idling. Here are a few potential causes:

1. **Belt Wear or Tension**: The most common cause of a high-pitched screeching noise while idling is a worn or loose serpentine belt or accessory belt. If the belt is old or improperly tensioned, it may slip and produce a high-pitched sound.

2. **Pulley Issues**: A faulty or worn-out pulley attached to one of the engine components (like the alternator, water pump, or power steering pump) can cause a screeching noise. If the bearings in the pulley are failing, it could lead to the noise you're hearing.

3. **Idler or Tensioner Pulley**: If the idler or tensioner pulley is faulty or the tensioner isn’t providing the correct tension on the belt, it can cause a similar screeching sound.

4. **Alternator**: Sometimes, a failing alternator can cause a whining or high-pitched sound due to internal bearing wear.

5. **Vacuum Leaks**: If there's a vacuum leak, it can sometimes produce a high-pitched noise as air escapes, especially while idling.

If you’re hearing this sound primarily at idle or low speeds, it’s recommended to check the belts, pulleys, and potentially the alternator. Replacing worn belts or fixing the pulleys should resolve the issue. If you're not comfortable checking these yourself, a mechanic can quickly diagnose and address the issue.
