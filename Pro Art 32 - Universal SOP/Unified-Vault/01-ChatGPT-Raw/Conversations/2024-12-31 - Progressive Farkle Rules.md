## 🤖 ChatGPT

The rule allowing players to continue their turn using the previous person's dice (or score) is typically found in a variant of **"Progressive Farkle"** or similar house rules. In this variation:

1. **Scoring Continuation**:
   - If a player ends their turn without farkling, the next player has the option to continue rolling using the remaining dice from the previous turn.
   - They must build off the prior player's score (and dice results) to avoid farkling and keep their score.

2. **Risk and Reward**:
   - Continuing off the previous score increases the potential points but also increases the risk of farkling, as fewer dice are available to roll.

3. **House Rule Context**:
   - This is not part of the **standard Farkle rules** but is commonly adopted as a house rule to increase interaction between players and add more risk-taking dynamics.

If you're playing in a group, confirm the rules being used beforehand, as this is not universal to Farkle.
