## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here’s Tony Robbins’ **RPM Method** in a simple, actionable breakdown:  

---

## **RPM = Rapid Planning Method (or Results, Purpose, Massive Action)**  

### 1. **R = Result (Outcome)**
- **Ask:** *“What do I really want?”*  
- Define the specific, measurable result you want—not just tasks.  
- Example: Instead of *“Go to the gym”* → *“Be lean, strong, and energized at 10% body fat by December 1st.”*

---

### 2. **P = Purpose (Why)**
- **Ask:** *“Why do I want this?”*  
- Fuel with emotion—purpose gives power.  
- Example: *“So I feel confident, alive, and unstoppable in my business and relationships.”*

---

### 3. **M = Massive Action Plan (MAP)**
- **Ask:** *“What’s my massive action plan?”*  
- List actions that lead directly to your result.  
- Example:  
  - Meal prep twice per week.  
  - Train 4x/week (progressive overload).  
  - Track macros daily.  
  - Hire coach for accountability.  

---

## **How to Use RPM Daily**
1. Write down your **TOP RESULTS** for the day (not tasks).  
2. Anchor them with your **PURPOSES** (emotional juice).  
3. Build a quick **MAP** (3–5 big actions max).  
4. Focus only on these outcomes—say “no” to what doesn’t serve them.  

---

## **Example RPM Daily Page**
- **Result:** Enroll 3 new clients.  
- **Purpose:** Create impact + financial freedom.  
- **Massive Action Plan:**  
  - Call 10 warm leads.  
  - Send 5 personal follow-up videos.  
  - Host one live Q&A.  

---

⚡️In short: **Don’t do “to-do lists.” Do “Results–Purpose–Massive Action” lists.** That’s how Robbins stays outcome-driven instead of activity-driven.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**JSON Prompting** = structuring your prompts in a **machine-readable JSON format** so the model outputs clean, predictable, parseable responses.  

---

### **Why it’s Used**
- **Reliability:** Avoids messy, free-form text.  
- **Structure:** Ensures answers follow a schema (good for automation, APIs, code, analysis).  
- **Parsing:** Makes it easy for software to process outputs.  

---

### **Basic Example**
Instead of asking:
> “Summarize this article.”

You’d prompt:
```json
{
  "task": "summarize",
  "content": "<<<ARTICLE TEXT>>>",
  "output_format": {
    "summary": "string",
    "keywords": ["string"],
    "tone": "string"
  }
}
```

And the model returns:
```json
{
  "summary": "The article explains the basics of intermittent fasting.",
  "keywords": ["fasting", "nutrition", "health"],
  "tone": "informative"
}
```

---

### **Core Principles**
1. **Define schema up front** → keys, types, structure.  
2. **Be explicit** → “Respond ONLY in JSON with no extra text.”  
3. **Validate** → Models can sometimes drift, so include guardrails.  

---

### **When to Use**
- Automations (API → LLM → API).  
- Data extraction (e.g., pulling names, dates, numbers).  
- Multi-step reasoning with structured outputs.  
- Building agents / tools that depend on predictable format.
