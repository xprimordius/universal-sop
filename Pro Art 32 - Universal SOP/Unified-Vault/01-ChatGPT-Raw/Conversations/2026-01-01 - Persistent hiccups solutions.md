### Why this happens
- **Carbonation** → stomach distension → diaphragm spasm  
- **High sodium / rapid electrolyte shift** → irritates the vagus/phrenic nerve  
- **Cold temperature** → sudden diaphragm reflex  
- **Drank it quickly** → gastric stretch trigger

This is a **common, benign cause**—especially when hiccups start **immediately after** the drink.

1. **Stop electrolytes + carbonated drinks** for 24 hours

### What *not* to do
- Don’t chug water  
- Don’t repeat electrolyte drinks “to fix it”  
- Don’t force breath-holding repeatedly (can worsen it)
