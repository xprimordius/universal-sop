For perfect soft-boiled eggs:  

- **6 minutes** → slightly runny yolk, set whites (classic ramen style)

**Method:**  
1. Bring water to a full boil.  
2. Gently lower cold eggs in.  
3. Start timer immediately.  
4. When time’s up, transfer to an **ice bath for 1–2 min** to stop cooking.
