### ⚡ 3 Main Methods:
#### 1. **Balance Transfer** (Best for Debt Consolidation)
- **How it works:** Transfer balance from Card A to Card B (Card B pays off Card A).
- **Tool used:** Balance transfer offer from Card B (0% APR offers ideal).
- **Fees:** Usually 3–5% of amount transferred.
- **When to use:** You’re paying off high-interest debt with a 0% APR promo.

#### 2. **Cash Advance → Bank Account → Pay Card**
- **How it works:** Take a cash advance from Card B → deposit to checking → pay Card A.
- **Warning:** **High fees + interest starts immediately.** Not ideal unless urgent.
- **Only use:** If you're desperate and have no cheaper alternatives.

#### 3. **Third-Party Payment Services** (Workaround)
- **Example:** Use **Plastiq** to pay Card A using Card B (treated as a purchase).
- **Fee:** ~2.9%.
- **Use Case:** Business expenses or strategic cash flow, not for personal revolving debt.

### ⚠️ Important Notes:
- **You can’t directly pay Card A using Card B’s payment portal.** Issuers block it.
- **Cash advances** hurt utilization and credit score, and they’re expensive.
- **Balance transfers** are the **safest, cheapest**, and most credit-score-friendly method when used properly.
