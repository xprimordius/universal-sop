## 🤖 ChatGPT

Lower abdominal pain has different common causes depending on whether it’s **right side, left side, or central**:  

### 🔹 Right lower abdomen  
- **Appendicitis** → sharp, worsening pain, often with nausea/fever.  
- **Kidney/ureter stone** → sharp, wave-like pain, may radiate to groin.  
- **Ovarian cyst/torsion** (if female) → sudden, severe, one-sided pain.  

### 🔹 Left lower abdomen  
- **Diverticulitis** → steady, aching pain, often with fever.  
- **Kidney stone** → similar sharp, radiating pain.  
- **Ovarian cyst/torsion** → sudden one-sided pain.  

### 🔹 Central/pelvic  
- **Bladder infection (UTI)** → pain, burning urination, urgency.  
- **Irritable bowel / constipation** → cramping, relieved by bowel movement.  
- **Gynecologic issues** (female) → endometriosis, pelvic infection, ectopic pregnancy (emergency).  

⚠️ **Red flags** → go to ER or urgent care if:  
- Pain is **sudden, severe, or worsening**  
- Accompanied by **fever, vomiting, blood in urine/stool, fainting, or inability to pass stool/gas**
