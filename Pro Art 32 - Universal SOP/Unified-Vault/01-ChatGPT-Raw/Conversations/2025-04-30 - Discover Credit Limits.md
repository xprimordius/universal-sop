### **PERSONAL PRODUCTS**

#### 1. **Discover It® Cash Back / Miles / Chrome Cards**
- **Limit:** Up to $50,000 (rare; average $10K–$20K)
- **Notes:** No preset categories for approval; high credit scores + low utilization helps.

#### 2. **Discover Personal Loans**
- **Limit:** $2,500 – $40,000  
- **Uses:** Debt consolidation, home improvement, etc.  
- **Fixed rates:** Typically 5.99% – 24.99% APR  
- **No origination fees or prepayment penalties**

#### 3. **Discover Home Equity Loans (HELOC)**
- **Limit:** Up to $300,000  
- **LTV Requirement:** Typically up to 85% CLTV  
- **Term Options:** 10–30 years fixed  
- **Notes:** Property must be primary residence in eligible states.

---

### **BUSINESS PRODUCTS**

**Discover does *not* directly offer traditional business credit cards or loans** under the Discover brand.  
However, you can use personal products to fund businesses, and some business-like accounts are available through **Discover Bank**:

#### 1. **Discover Business Savings (via Discover Bank)**
- **Limit:** No max deposit cap; FDIC insured up to $250K  
- **Yield:** ~4.25% APY  
- **Use Case:** Passive business reserve account

#### 2. **Business Use of Personal Card**
- **Limit:** Same as personal card (up to $50K)  
- **Caution:** Must separate transactions manually; not ideal for scaling.
