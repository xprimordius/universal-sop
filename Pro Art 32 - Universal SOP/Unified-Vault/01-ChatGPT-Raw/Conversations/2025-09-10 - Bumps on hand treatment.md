### Possible Causes
- **Contact dermatitis** (reaction to soap, sanitizer, lotion, or detergent).
- **Heat rash / clogged pores** (tiny sweat duct blockages, common in warm weather).
- **Dyshidrotic eczema** (small, itchy blisters on hands).
- **Allergic reaction** (food, environmental, or topical trigger).
- **Keratosis pilaris–like rough bumps** (less common on hands, more on arms/thighs).

### What You Can Do
1. **Gentle Care**
   - Wash with mild, fragrance-free soap.
   - Apply a plain, fragrance-free moisturizer (e.g., CeraVe, Aquaphor).
   - Avoid scratching—can worsen or infect.

2. **Reduce Irritation**
   - Limit alcohol-based sanitizers or harsh cleaners.
   - Use lukewarm (not hot) water for washing.

3. **Symptom Relief**
   - OTC hydrocortisone cream (1%) can calm inflammation if itchy.
   - Antihistamines (like cetirizine or loratadine) if allergy suspected.

4. **Monitor**
   - If bumps spread, blister, ooze, or become painful → see a doctor.
   - If not improving within 1–2 weeks of gentle care → dermatologist evaluation.

⚠️ **Seek care urgently** if you develop fever, spreading redness, pus, or severe pain.
