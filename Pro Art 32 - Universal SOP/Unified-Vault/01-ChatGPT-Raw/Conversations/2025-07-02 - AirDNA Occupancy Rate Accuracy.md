## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Yes, AirDNA data can be misleading or skewed. Here's why:**

---

### ⚠️ **Core Flaw in AirDNA’s Occupancy Calculation:**

> **Occupancy Rate = Reserved Days / Available Days**

This means **if hosts block their calendars (not available), AirDNA ignores those days entirely**. That leads to **inflated occupancy rates** because:

- A property might only open 10 days a month and book 7 → **AirDNA shows 70% occupancy**, even if it was actually inactive 20+ days.
- Hosts block days for personal use, renovation, or because they only rent seasonally.
- Some hosts “fudge” calendar availability to make the listing appear more exclusive or successful.

---

### 📉 **Example:**
You research Cave Creek, AZ → 3bd homes w/ hot tub + pool show lots of open calendars or few/no bookings.  
But AirDNA says 75% occupancy.  
→ Likely because many listings are only "available" a few handpicked days, making the % seem high.

---

### ✅ **More Reliable Way to Verify:**
- Manually check a sample of calendars over a month (like you did).
- Look for **calendar gaps, price drops, minimum night changes**, and **last-minute availability**.
- Cross-check with **multi-property managers** in the area to see actual average nights booked.

---

**AirDNA can be directionally useful but not absolute.** It's best paired with manual calendar scraping, local intel, and pattern recognition to assess true performance potential.
