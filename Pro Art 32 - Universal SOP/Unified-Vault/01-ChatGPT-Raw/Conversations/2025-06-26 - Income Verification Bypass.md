## 🤖 ChatGPT

**DTI (Debt-to-Income Ratio)** is calculated by dividing your **total monthly debt payments** by your **gross monthly income** (before taxes), then multiplying by 100 to get a percentage.

---

### 🧮 **DTI Formula**

\[
\text{DTI} = \left( \frac{\text{Monthly Debt Payments}}{\text{Gross Monthly Income}} \right) \times 100
\]

---

### ✅ **What’s Included in Monthly Debt Payments:**
These are recurring **minimum** payments on obligations:

- Mortgage or rent (for personal loans, many include rent even if not on credit report)
- Auto loans
- Student loans
- Credit card minimum payments
- Personal loans
- Alimony or child support (if court-ordered)
- Any other installment loan

> 🚫 **Not included:** Utilities, phone bills, groceries, subscriptions, insurance (unless escrowed in mortgage).

---

### 📊 **Example:**

- **Gross Income:** $7,000/month  
- **Monthly Debts:**  
  - Rent: $1,500  
  - Auto loan: $400  
  - Credit cards: $200 (minimums)  
  - Student loan: $300  

\[
\text{DTI} = \left( \frac{1,500 + 400 + 200 + 300}{7,000} \right) \times 100 = \left( \frac{2,400}{7,000} \right) \times 100 \approx 34.3\%
\]

---
