---

### **1. TRT Takes Time**
- **Testosterone has a build-up curve**: Serum levels rise quickly, but *physiological effects* like mood, energy, libido, and strength take **2–6 weeks**, with full impact seen around **8–12 weeks**.
- You're only on **dose 2**, so your body is just beginning to stabilize.

---

### **2. Your Dose Is Moderate**
- 80mg/week is **on the lower end** of TRT dosing (common range: **100–200mg/week**).
- Depending on your metabolism (CYP450 activity), SHBG levels, and body fat, this dose may still be **below your optimal therapeutic threshold**.

---

### **3. Estrogen May Be Rising Too**
- As testosterone increases, some converts to **estradiol**.
- If E2 is rising too fast, it can **blunt benefits** (mood swings, water retention, fatigue, low libido).

---

### **4. Receptor and Brain Adaptation**
- The body (especially the brain) takes time to **upregulate androgen receptors** and rebalance dopamine and serotonin levels affected by testosterone.

---

### **5. Lifestyle Factors**
- **Sleep, diet, training, stress, insulin resistance** all affect how well TRT works.
- If cortisol is high or sleep is low, benefits may be masked.

---

**TLDR**: You’re early. No red flags. Effects build gradually over **weeks**, not days. Most people begin noticing meaningful change **by week 3–6**. Monitor labs at week 6–8 and optimize dose based on free T, E2, and symptoms.
