- **Self-amplifying banter.** Don’t tell “jokes”—make her stories funnier.  
- **Expressive reactions.** Your facial expressions and timing make her laugh harder than your words.

- **Laugh with her, not at her.** She feels safe.

- **Stop before overkill.** Leave her wanting more laughter, not drained.
