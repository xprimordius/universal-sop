## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Traeger Smoked Pork Loin – Master Guide**

**🔥 GOAL:** Juicy, tender, smoky pork loin with flavorful bark.

---

### **PREP**

**1. Trim:** Remove silver skin and excess fat.  
**2. Brine (optional but optimal):**  
- Mix ½ cup salt + ¼ cup sugar per 1 gal water.  
- Add aromatics: garlic, bay leaf, peppercorns.  
- Submerge 4–12 hrs.  
- Rinse + pat dry.

**3. Season:**  
- Coat with oil or mustard binder.  
- Rub: salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika, onion powder, optional brown sugar or Traeger Pork & Poultry rub.

---

### **COOK**

**1. Preheat Traeger:** 225°F  
**2. Wood:** Apple, cherry, or hickory for balanced smoke.  
**3. Smoke Time:**  
- ~2.5–3 hrs total  
- **Target internal temp: 145°F**  
- Use probe in thickest part

**(Optional Step – Bark Finish):**  
- At 135°F internal, increase Traeger to 375°F for 5–10 mins to crisp up outside  
- Pull at 145°F

---

### **REST + SLICE**

**Rest:** 10–15 min (foil tent)  
**Slice:** Across the grain for tenderness

---

### **Pro Tips**
- Mop with apple cider vinegar every hour for bark + moisture  
- Don’t overcook past 150°F—dries fast  
- Great with: roasted veggies, slaw, or Traeger mac & cheese
