### **Availability Zones in Cybersecurity**  

In **cybersecurity**, **Availability Zones (AZs)** play a critical role in ensuring **resilience, redundancy, and disaster recovery** for cloud-based infrastructure. 

### **Key Cybersecurity Benefits of Availability Zones:**
1. **Fault Tolerance & High Availability** – If one AZ fails due to cyberattacks, hardware failures, or natural disasters, another AZ within the region can take over.
2. **Data Redundancy & Backup** – Data is often replicated across multiple AZs to prevent **data loss from ransomware, outages, or system failures**.
3. **DDoS Mitigation** – Distributing workloads across multiple AZs helps absorb and mitigate **Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) attacks**.
4. **Compliance & Risk Management** – Many security regulations (e.g., **SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR**) require redundancy and disaster recovery, which AZs facilitate.
5. **Zero Trust Security Architecture** – Segregating workloads across AZs aligns with **Zero Trust principles**, reducing the attack surface and limiting lateral movement.

### **Best Practices for Cybersecurity in Availability Zones:**
- **Use Multi-AZ Deployments**: Ensure applications and databases are replicated across at least **two AZs**.
- **Implement IAM & Access Controls**: Restrict access between AZs using **Identity & Access Management (IAM)** policies.
- **Enable Encryption & Secure Communication**: Use **end-to-end encryption** and **secure VPNs** between AZs.
- **Automate Failover & Disaster Recovery**: Use cloud-native tools like **AWS Route 53, Azure Traffic Manager, or Google Cloud Load Balancing** to switch AZs seamlessly in case of an attack.

### **Creamy Cottage Cheese Pasta Sauce**  
A rich, high-protein pasta sauce using cottage cheese for a smooth, velvety texture.

#### **Ingredients:**  
- 1 cup **cottage cheese** (full-fat for best texture)  
- ½ cup **Parmesan cheese**, grated  
- ¼ cup **milk** (or pasta water for a lighter sauce)  
- 2 tbsp **olive oil**  
- 2 cloves **garlic**, minced  
- ½ tsp **red pepper flakes** (optional, for heat)  
- ½ tsp **black pepper**  
- ½ tsp **salt** (adjust to taste)  
- 1 tsp **Italian seasoning** (or fresh basil/parsley for garnish)  
- 1 tbsp **lemon juice** (optional, for freshness)  

#### **Instructions:**  
1. **Sauté Garlic** – In a pan, heat olive oil over medium heat and sauté minced garlic until fragrant (about 1 minute).  
2. **Blend the Sauce** – In a blender or food processor, blend cottage cheese, Parmesan, milk (or pasta water), black pepper, red pepper flakes, and salt until smooth.  
3. **Combine & Heat** – Pour the blended sauce into the pan with garlic. Stir over low heat for 2–3 minutes until warmed through.  
4. **Adjust Consistency** – If too thick, add more milk or reserved pasta water.  
5. **Toss with Pasta** – Mix with your favorite cooked pasta, garnish with fresh basil or parsley, and serve hot.  

#### **Optional Add-Ins:**  
- **Protein boost** – Add grilled chicken or shrimp.  
- **Veggies** – Stir in spinach, roasted cherry tomatoes, or mushrooms.  
- **Richer flavor** – Add a splash of heavy cream or butter.
