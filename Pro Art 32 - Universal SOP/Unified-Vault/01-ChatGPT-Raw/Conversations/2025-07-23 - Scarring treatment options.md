### ⚔️ SURGICAL OPTIONS (For Atrophic Acne Scars)

#### 1. **Subcision (Most Common)**
- **What it does:** Breaks fibrotic tissue under scars to release the tethering.
- **Best for:** **Rolling scars** and some **boxcar scars**.
- **Downtime:** 3–7 days (bruising, swelling).

---

#### 2. **Punch Excision / Grafting**
- **What it does:** Surgically removes individual deep scars (e.g., boxcar).
- **Grafting:** May take skin from behind the ear to fill the excised area.
- **Best for:** **Deep boxcar** or **icepick scars**.
- **Downtime:** Sutures removed in 7–10 days.

---

#### 3. **Fractional CO₂ or Erbium Laser Resurfacing (Advanced Tech)**
- **What it does:** Vaporizes damaged skin, triggers deep collagen remodeling.
- **Best for:** **Widespread scarring** with irregular texture.
- **Downtime:** 5–10 days (redness, peeling, sun sensitivity).
- **Risks:** Hyperpigmentation in darker skin tones—must pre-treat.

---

#### 4. **Fat Grafting / Dermal Fillers**
- **What it does:** Adds volume under scars for smoother surface.
- **Autologous fat transfer** (from your body) can be permanent.
- **Hyaluronic acid fillers** (e.g., Juvederm) are temporary (6–18 months).

- **Combination > single method** = best results.
