## 👤 You

What does polish do

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Polish** is an abrasive product that **levels and refines the paint surface** by removing microscopic imperfections — it’s not the same as wax or coating.  

**What it does:**  
- **Removes defects:** Light scratches, swirl marks, oxidation, and haze.  
- **Levels the surface:** Evens out microscopic peaks and valleys so light reflects more uniformly.  
- **Boosts clarity & depth:** Makes color richer and reflections sharper.  
- **Preps for coating:** Creates a perfectly smooth surface so the coating bonds better and looks its best.  

**Think of it like:**  
- Sanding and smoothing wood before staining — the stain (coating) looks better and lasts longer if the surface is perfectly smooth first.  

**Key point for you:**  
If your car’s paint is already new and defect-free, polishing is optional. But if there are swirls or dullness, a light polish before applying Adam’s Graphene Advanced can dramatically improve the final look.
