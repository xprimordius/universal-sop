> of an owned auto  or non-owned auto  by an independent contractor delivering mail for the United States Postal

> 5. SUBROGATION
> When payment is made under this policy, we will be subrogated to all the insured's rights of recovery against others.

> responsible.
> Our rights do not apply against any person or organization insured under this policy, or any other policy we issue with
