### 3. **Cross-Check Key Details**

   - **Explain Similarities**: Mention the similarities and differences, and ask them to investigate.

### 7. **Document Your Findings**
   - **Keep Records**: Maintain detailed notes and copies of your correspondence with Experian or creditors during the verification process.

### 8. **Seek Legal Advice if Necessary**
