## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Perfect Traeger Pork Loin – Juicy, Smoky, and Simple**

### 🔥 **Overview:**
- **Cut:** Boneless pork loin (not tenderloin)
- **Temp:** 225°F → finish at 375°F (for crust)
- **Time:** ~1.5–2 hours
- **Target Internal Temp:** 145°F (juicy, not dry)

---

### ✅ **Step-by-Step:**

#### 1. **Prep the Loin**
- Trim excess fat/silver skin.
- Pat dry.

#### 2. **Season Liberally**
Choose one:
- **Savory:** salt, pepper, garlic, rosemary, paprika  
- **Sweet/Savory:** brown sugar, garlic, chili powder, smoked paprika

Let sit 15–30 mins to absorb.

#### 3. **Preheat Traeger**
- Set to **225°F** (super smoke mode if available).
- Use **apple**, **cherry**, or **hickory** pellets.

#### 4. **Smoke Low & Slow**
- Place loin fat-side up, center of the grate.
- Insert meat probe.
- **Smoke until 135°F internal** (~1.5 hours).

#### 5. **Finish Hot**
- Crank Traeger to **375°F**.
- Cook to **145°F internal** (USDA safe temp).
- Optional: baste with a glaze (honey mustard, apple BBQ) last 10 mins.

#### 6. **Rest & Slice**
- Rest 10–15 mins, tented with foil.
- Slice against the grain.

---

### 🧠 **Pro Tips**
- **Don’t overcook** – 145°F is juicy perfection.
- **Optional brine:** 4–6 hrs in salt/sugar/water mix = extra moisture.
- **Add sear:** pan-sear or torch for crust if skipping the 375°F finish.
