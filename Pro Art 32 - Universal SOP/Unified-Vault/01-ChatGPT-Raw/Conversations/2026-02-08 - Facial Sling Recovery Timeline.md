**Facial sling recovery — when to run & lift**

> *Assuming a cosmetic facial sling / SMAS or similar soft-tissue facial suspension. If yours was a different procedure (e.g., nerve reanimation sling), say so—the timeline changes.*

### TL;DR Timeline
- **Days 0–7:** ❌ **No running or lifting**  
  Swelling, bleeding, and suture displacement risk is highest.
- **Week 2 (Days 8–14):** ⚠️ **Light activity only**  
  Walking is fine. **No running. No lifting >10–15 lb.**
- **Weeks 3–4:** ⚠️ **Gradual return**  
  • Easy jogging **only if** swelling/pain-free  
  • Light weights, higher reps, **no straining/Valsalva**
- **Weeks 4–6:** ✅ **Full return for most patients**  
  Running + heavier lifting usually OK **with surgeon clearance**

### Why the wait matters
- Running & lifting spike **blood pressure and venous pressure** → risk of:
  - Hematoma
  - Prolonged swelling
  - Sling loosening or asymmetry
  - Delayed healing

### Hard stop rules (do **not** train if any are present)
- Throbbing, pressure, or facial tightness during activity  
- Increasing swelling/redness after workouts  
- New asymmetry or pain at sling anchor points

### Bottom line
- **Running:** ~**3–4 weeks minimum**  
- **Heavy lifting:** ~**4–6 weeks**  
- **Always defer to your surgeon** if they gave a stricter plan—that overrides general guidance.
