## **2. Candida**  
- **Definition:** A genus of yeast (fungus). Most common species is **Candida albicans**.  
- **Normal Role:** Lives in the mouth, gut, skin, and vagina in balance with other microbes.  
- **Overgrowth (“Candidiasis”):**

  - **Vaginal Yeast Infections:** Itching, discharge.  
  - **Gut Candida Overgrowth (controversial diagnosis):** Symptoms may include bloating, fatigue, brain fog.  
  - **Invasive Candidiasis:** Dangerous blood infection in immunocompromised patients.  
- **Causes of Overgrowth:** Antibiotics, high sugar diet, weakened immune system, stress, hormonal imbalance.  
- **Treatment:** Antifungal meds (fluconazole, nystatin), probiotics, reduced sugar/simple carbs, lifestyle support.
