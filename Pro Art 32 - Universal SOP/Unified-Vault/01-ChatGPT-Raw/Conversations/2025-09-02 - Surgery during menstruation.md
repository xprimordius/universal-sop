Yes — menstruation by itself is **not a contraindication** to ovarian cyst surgery, even for a large cyst (like 10 cm).  

**Key points:**
- **Timing:** Surgery is often scheduled based on urgency, surgeon availability, and operating room schedules — not on menstrual cycles.  
- **Menstruation impact:** Being on your period does not usually stop or delay surgery. Hospitals and surgeons are accustomed to operating while patients are menstruating.  
- **Exceptions:** If there are complicating factors (heavy bleeding, anemia, unstable hemoglobin, or if the surgery is elective and not urgent), a surgeon may consider rescheduling.  
- **10 cm cyst:** A cyst of that size is often considered for surgical removal regardless of cycle timing due to risk of rupture, torsion, or malignancy concerns.  

✅ **Bottom line:** If the surgery is necessary, they will still perform it even if you just started your period.
