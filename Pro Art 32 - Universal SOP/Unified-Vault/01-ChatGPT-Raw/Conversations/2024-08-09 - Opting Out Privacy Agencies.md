# Opting Out Privacy Agencies

---

## 👤 You

How to opt out of the Experian privacy area that removes me from 50-60 bureaus? I can’t find it specifically things like Lexxis nexus sagestream

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

To opt out of data sharing with Experian and associated bureaus like LexisNexis, SageStream (now called SageStream, Inc., a part of LexisNexis Risk Solutions), and other similar reporting agencies, follow these steps:

### 1. **Opt-Out of Experian:**
   - **Online Opt-Out:**
     - Visit [Experian Opt-Out](https://www.experian.com/privacy/opting-out.html) to begin the process.
     - Follow the instructions to opt out of receiving pre-screened offers of credit and insurance, which also helps limit the sharing of your data with third-party marketers.

   - **By Mail:**
     - If you prefer to opt out by mail, you can send a written request to:
       ```
       Experian Opt-Out
       P.O. Box 919
       Allen, TX 75013
       ```

### 2. **LexisNexis Opt-Out:**
   - **Online Opt-Out:**
     - Visit the [LexisNexis Opt-Out page](https://optout.lexisnexis.com/) to submit your request.
   - **By Mail:**
     - You can send a request with a copy of your government-issued ID to:
       ```
       LexisNexis Consumer Center
       Attn: Opt Out Request
       P.O. Box 105108
       Atlanta, GA 30348-5108
       ```

### 3. **SageStream (Now part of LexisNexis) Opt-Out:**
   - Since SageStream is now part of LexisNexis Risk Solutions, you can follow the LexisNexis opt-out process mentioned above.
   - You may also visit the [SageStream Consumer Opt-Out page](https://www.sagestreamllc.com/) for specific instructions related to your file.

### 4. **Other Consumer Reporting Agencies:**
   - To further remove yourself from other smaller reporting agencies, consider using services like the [Consumer Reporting Agency List](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-are-consumer-reporting-companies-en-1567/) provided by the CFPB. This list includes detailed contact information for each agency.
   - Submit opt-out requests directly to each agency as required.

### 5. **Freeze Your Credit Reports:**
   - Freezing your credit reports with Experian, Equifax, and TransUnion can also reduce the dissemination of your personal information.
   - You can do this online at each bureau's respective website.

### 6. **Additional Resources:**
   - For further protection, consider placing a **fraud alert** on your credit reports, which requires creditors to take extra steps to verify your identity before extending credit.
   - Visit the **OptOutPrescreen** website at [www.optoutprescreen.com](https://www.optoutprescreen.com) to opt out of pre-screened offers.

This process should help remove your information from multiple databases used by credit reporting agencies, marketers, and other third parties.
