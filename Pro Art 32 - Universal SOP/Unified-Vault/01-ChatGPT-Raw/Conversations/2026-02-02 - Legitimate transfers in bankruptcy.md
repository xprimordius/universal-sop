For someone who just filed or is in the process of filing Chapter 7 bankruptcy and the banks or courts are asking for statements from their bank accounts, specifically for one in January, if they sent $950 to their son and it shows in the statement that they're sending to the court that are requested, what legitimate reasons are they able to be able to send money to someone else like their son without running into legal issues? ---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

In a Chapter 7 bankruptcy, the court scrutinizes transactions to ensure no improper transfers were made to avoid paying creditors. If you sent money to your son, the reason must align with ordinary, necessary, or legally allowed expenses. For example, if it was to cover a legitimate household expense on their behalf, like rent, utilities, or medical needs you were responsible for, it may be seen as reasonable. If it was a repayment of a documented, bona fide loan, that could also be justified. But gifting, repaying informal debts, or simply transferring funds without a clear, necessary purpose can raise red flags. It’s essential to frame it around documented obligations or necessities, not discretionary support. Always consult your bankruptcy attorney to ensure full compliance.
