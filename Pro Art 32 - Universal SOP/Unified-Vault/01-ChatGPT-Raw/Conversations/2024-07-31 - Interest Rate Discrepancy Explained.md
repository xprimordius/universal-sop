# Interest Rate Discrepancy Explained

---

## 👤 You

Why did the company advertised over the phone 14-15% interest but the application results say 22% APR

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

The discrepancy between the advertised interest rate of 14-15% and the application result of 22% APR can arise from several factors:

1. **Creditworthiness:** The advertised rate is often the lowest available rate, typically reserved for customers with excellent credit. If your credit score or credit history doesn't meet the criteria for the lowest rate, a higher rate will be applied.

2. **Loan Terms and Conditions:** The specific terms and conditions of the loan, such as the loan amount, repayment period, and any additional fees or charges, can affect the final APR.

3. **Variable vs. Fixed Rates:** The advertised rate might be a starting variable rate, while the APR reflects the full cost of the loan, including fixed rates over time.

4. **Additional Fees:** APR includes not just the interest rate but also any fees associated with the loan, such as origination fees, administrative fees, or service charges.

5. **Promotional Rates:** The advertised rate might be a promotional rate for a limited time, after which the rate increases to the standard APR.

To address this issue, contact the lending company to request a detailed breakdown of how the APR was calculated and compare it with the advertised rate. Understanding the specific factors contributing to the higher APR can provide clarity and possibly identify any errors or miscommunications.
