## Why It Turned Crumbly (Most Common Causes)

### 1️⃣ **Too Much Flour**
- Scooping directly packs flour → dry loaf.
- **Fix:** Spoon flour into the cup, level it. Or reduce by **2–4 tbsp** next time.

### 2️⃣ **Not Enough Fat or Sugar**
- Fat + sugar = moisture + binding.
- **Fix:** Add **1–2 tbsp oil/butter** *or* use **½ cup brown sugar** instead of white.

### 3️⃣ **Overbaked (Most Common)**
- Even 5 extra minutes dries it out.
- **Fix:** Pull at **200–205°F internal** or when a toothpick has **moist crumbs**, not clean.

### 4️⃣ **Too Little Banana**
- Bananas are the glue.
- **Fix:** Use **at least 1½ cups mashed (3 very ripe bananas)**.

### 5️⃣ **Overmixed Batter**
- Overmixing breaks structure → crumbly, not tender.
- **Fix:** Mix **just until flour disappears**. Lumpy is correct.

### 6️⃣ **Wrong Pan Size**
- Too wide = thin loaf = dry.
- **Fix:** Use **8.5×4.5 in** loaf pan (not 9×5 unless adjusting).

---

## Quick Rescue (If It’s Already Baked)
- Toast slices + add butter/honey  
- Or wrap loaf in foil + warm at **300°F for 10 min**  
- Crumble → French toast or bread pudding (elite save)
