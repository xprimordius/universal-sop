**XQD = older high-speed camera memory card**  

- **Origin:** Developed by Sony & Nikon before CFexpress.  
- **Design:** Same size as **CFexpress Type B**, but uses an older PCIe 2.0 x2 interface.  
- **Speed:** Typically **400 MB/s – 700 MB/s**, slower than CFexpress B (which hits **1,700 MB/s +**).  
- **Use case:** Found in older pro cameras (e.g., Nikon D5, D850, Z6/Z7 Gen 1).  
- **Compatibility:** CFexpress B and XQD share the same **physical slot**, but require **firmware support**—some cameras can use both.  

👉 In short: **XQD = last-gen tech; CFexpress B = modern successor.**

### ⚙️ How HDDs Work
Hard drives store data on **spinning magnetic platters** with a **tiny read/write head** hovering nanometers above them.  
When you’re driving, you’re constantly introducing **vibration, bumps, and G-forces** — all enemies of this mechanism.

---

### 🚨 Risks While Driving
1. **Micro-vibration wear:**  
   Constant motion causes the head to oscillate microscopically, wearing bearings and increasing seek error rates.

2. **Head crash risk:**  
   One sharp bump (pothole, speed bump, hard stop) while spinning can make the head touch the platter → instant, irreversible data loss.

3. **File corruption during movement:**  
   If data is being written and the drive jolts or power cuts (e.g., unplugging, sleep mode, inverter glitch), sectors can corrupt.

4. **Temperature swings:**  
   Car interiors can hit 130°F+ or drop below freezing. HDD tolerances are tight; SSDs handle this much better.

5. **Power irregularities:**  
   Power surges or drops from inverters, USB hubs, or vehicle charging systems can interrupt writes and corrupt sectors.
