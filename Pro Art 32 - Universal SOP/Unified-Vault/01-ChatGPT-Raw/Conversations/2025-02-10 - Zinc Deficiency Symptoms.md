## 🤖 ChatGPT

Zinc deficiency can manifest in several ways:

- **Weakened Immune System:** Increased susceptibility to infections.
- **Hair Loss:** Thinning hair or bald patches.
- **Skin Issues:** Dermatitis or rough skin.
- **Delayed Wound Healing:** Slow recovery from cuts or wounds.
- **Loss of Appetite:** Reduced desire to eat.
- **Cognitive Impairment:** Difficulty concentrating or poor memory.

If you suspect a deficiency, it's best to consult with a healthcare professional.
