## 👤 You

Why do I gar to have ssn proof

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

You must show **proof of your Social Security number (SSN)** because it’s required by **federal REAL ID law** (and Minnesota state law) to verify your identity and prevent fraud.  

Here’s why it’s required:  
1. **Federal mandate:** The REAL ID Act requires states to confirm each applicant’s SSN with the Social Security Administration.  
2. **Identity verification:** It links your license/ID to your federal record so no one else can use your name or SSN to get a license.  
3. **Database accuracy:** It ensures your information matches across government systems (SSA, DHS, etc.).  

### ✅ What you can use as proof
- Original **Social Security card**, **W-2**, or **pay stub** showing full SSN  
- **SSA 1099** or **non-SSA 1099** form  

If you’ve **never been issued an SSN**, you must provide a signed statement (and usually immigration documents if applicable).
