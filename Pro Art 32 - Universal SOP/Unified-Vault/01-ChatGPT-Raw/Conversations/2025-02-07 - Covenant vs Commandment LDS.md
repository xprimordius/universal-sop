Covenant vs commandment LDS

## 🤖 ChatGPT

In **Latter-day Saint (LDS) doctrine**, **covenants** and **commandments** are closely related but distinct concepts:

### **Covenant**
- A **covenant** is a **sacred, two-way promise** between God and His children.
- When a person makes a covenant with God, they commit to obey certain laws, and in return, God promises specific blessings.
- Covenants are **formalized through ordinances** such as **baptism, the sacrament, and temple ceremonies** (e.g., the new and everlasting covenant of marriage).
- Example: The **Abrahamic Covenant**, where God promised Abraham land, posterity, and priesthood blessings in return for his faithfulness.

### **Commandment**
- A **commandment** is a **divine instruction** from God that His children are expected to follow.
- Commandments apply to everyone and are not tied to specific ordinances.
- Obeying commandments brings blessings, but they do not necessarily involve a formalized promise or ordinance.
- Example: The **Ten Commandments**, the **Word of Wisdom**, and **tithing**.

### **Key Differences**
| **Aspect**      | **Covenant** | **Commandment** |
|---------------|------------|------------|
| **Definition** | A sacred, mutual promise between God and an individual or group. | A divine rule or law given by God for guidance. |
| **Two-Way Commitment?** | Yes, both parties (God and the individual) have responsibilities. | No, it is a directive from God that must be followed. |
| **Formalized Through Ordinance?** | Yes, covenants are made through ordinances like baptism or temple endowments. | No, commandments are general laws to live by. |
| **Scope** | Applies to those who enter into them. | Universal for all God's children. |
| **Blessings** | Promised blessings are conditional upon keeping the covenant. | Blessings may come as a result of obedience but are not necessarily tied to a specific agreement. |

### **How They Connect**
- **Obeying commandments is part of keeping covenants.** For example, those who enter into the **baptismal covenant** promise to keep **God’s commandments**.
- **Covenants elevate commandments to a personal level**—when someone makes a covenant, they commit to following specific commandments more deeply.

### **Summary**
A **commandment** is a directive from God, while a **covenant** is a sacred promise that includes commandments as part of the agreement. In LDS doctrine, keeping commandments is essential for **keeping covenants**, which in turn brings **greater spiritual blessings and eternal life**.
