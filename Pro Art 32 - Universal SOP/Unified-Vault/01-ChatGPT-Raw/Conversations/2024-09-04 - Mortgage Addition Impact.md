# Mortgage Addition Impact

---

## 👤 You

If my mom adds me to the already existing mortgage with 150K left out, will it help my credit at 770?

Will it hurt her?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Adding yourself to your mom's existing mortgage may have a minimal impact on your credit if the mortgage is in good standing and has a positive payment history. Here are some key considerations:

### Impact on Your Credit:
1. **Positive Payment History:** If your mom has been making consistent, on-time payments, being added to the mortgage could help maintain or slightly improve your credit score by adding another positive account to your credit mix.
2. **Credit Mix:** Mortgages are considered installment loans and having a variety of credit types (installment loans, revolving credit, etc.) can boost your score slightly.
3. **Debt-to-Income Ratio:** Being added to the mortgage will also show an increase in your debt. If you're planning on taking other loans or credit, this could affect your debt-to-income ratio, which may slightly impact your ability to get additional credit.

### Impact on Her Credit:
1. **Minimal Impact:** Adding you to the mortgage itself shouldn't hurt her credit unless there are issues with making payments. However, if you both take on shared responsibility and payments are missed, it could negatively affect both your scores.
2. **Potential for Better Terms:** Depending on how the mortgage is structured, adding someone with a strong credit score like yours could improve her chances of getting refinancing offers with better terms (if that's considered).

In summary, adding yourself to the mortgage could slightly help your credit if everything remains in good standing, and it shouldn't harm her credit as long as payments are consistently made. However, it's important to consider how this impacts your debt-to-income ratio, especially if you're planning on taking out new loans.
