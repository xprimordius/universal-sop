## 🤖 ChatGPT

To **change the text (Messages) icon** appearance on your iPhone **without changing the call wallpaper**, here's what you can do:

---

### ✅ Option 1: **Use a Custom App Icon via Shortcuts**
This changes the *icon* only (not the functionality):

1. Open **Shortcuts** app.
2. Tap **+** to create a new shortcut.
3. Tap **“Add Action”** → Search and select **“Open App.”**
4. Tap “App” → Select **Messages.**
5. Tap the **(i)** or **settings icon** → **“Add to Home Screen.”**
6. Tap the icon image → Choose **Photo or File** for your custom icon.
7. Name it (e.g., "Text") → Tap **Add.**

> ✅ Result: New custom **Messages icon** appears on your Home Screen.
> 🚫 Downside: It won’t show notifications (badge count). Keep the original app hidden in a folder if needed.

---

### ✅ Option 2: **Use a Third-Party App Icon Pack (with Shortcuts)**
Install a theming app like:

- **Brass**
- **Moloko**
- **Themify**
- **Widgetsmith (limited icon customization)**

Follow in-app instructions—most use **Shortcuts** under the hood. Again, only the icon is changed.

---

### ⚠️ Notes:
- iOS **does not allow separate wallpapers** for Phone and Messages.
- **Changing call wallpaper** (FaceTime/call screen) is separate and optional under:
  `Contacts → Edit → Add Photo → Poster`.

You can **customize icons** without affecting **call wallpapers** if you only modify app icons via Shortcuts or third-party theming tools.

Got it. If you want to **change the text (iMessage/SMS) icon or appearance for a *specific contact*** — **but not their call wallpaper (contact poster)** — here’s what you can do:

---

### 🎯 Goal: Customize the contact’s **Messages appearance only**, not Phone/call.

### ✅ iOS 17+ — You **can’t separate** the photo/icon for texting vs. calling. Apple links both.

However, here’s what’s possible:

---

### ✅ Option 1: **Change Their Contact Photo (Affects Both Text & Call)**

1. Go to **Contacts** app → Select the contact.
2. Tap **Edit** → Tap **Add Photo / Edit Photo**.
3. Choose a **Memoji, emoji, photo, or custom icon**.
4. Tap **Done**.

> 🔄 This updates the icon seen in **Messages app** (top of the text thread) **and** when they call you.

---

### ⚠️ There’s *no official way* to change the **Messages icon/photo** for someone **without also changing their call display** — Apple links them in the contact profile.

---

### ❌ Not Possible (as of iOS 17+):
- **Separate image for Messages only.**
- **Different photos for texting vs. calling.**

---

### ✅ Workaround:
If you *only* want a custom visual for texting:

- **Pin the conversation** (long press the message thread → Pin).
- This creates a **large icon** at the top of your Messages app.
- The **icon is pulled from the contact photo**, so set this photo to whatever you want for visual effect.

> Tip: Use a **custom emoji or design** so the pinned icon feels like “text-only,” while their actual call poster can remain generic.
