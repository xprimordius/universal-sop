# Appendix Rupture Explained

---

## 👤 You

Why does an appendix explode

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

An appendix "explodes," or more accurately ruptures, due to acute appendicitis, which is the inflammation of the appendix. This happens when the appendix becomes blocked, often by stool, a foreign body, or infection. The blockage leads to increased pressure, swelling, and bacterial growth within the appendix. If not treated in time, the appendix can rupture, causing its contents (including bacteria and pus) to spill into the abdominal cavity. This can lead to a serious and potentially life-threatening infection known as peritonitis.
