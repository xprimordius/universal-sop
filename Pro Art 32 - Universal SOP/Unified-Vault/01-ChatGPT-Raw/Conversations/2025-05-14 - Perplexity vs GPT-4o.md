### Verdict:

- **Use Perplexity** if you want:
  - Fast answers with live web results
  - Direct citations for quick research
  - A lightweight tool with minimal depth

- **Use ChatGPT Plus (GPT-4o)** if you want:
  - Advanced writing, editing, and problem-solving
  - Deep reasoning, creative output, or strategic insight
  - Multimodal input (image, file, charts)
  - Memory, customization, and full assistant capabilities

For elite use (business, legal, sales, systems, marketing), **GPT-4o wins on capability, depth, and versatility.** Perplexity is better for speed and simple fact-checking with sources.
