### How to Classify If You Offer Both Massage (Non-Medical) and Nails

1. **Choose Your Primary NAICS:**  
   - If most of your revenue or focus is massage (non-medical/spa-focused), use **NAICS 812199 (Other Personal Care Services)** as the *primary* code.
   - If nails dominate revenue, use **NAICS 812113 (Nail Salons)** as the *primary* code.

2. **Add the Other as a Secondary NAICS:**  
   - Whichever service is *not* your main business activity can be listed under the appropriate secondary NAICS code.  
   - For example, if nails are secondary, list **812113** as secondary; if massage is secondary, list **812199** (or **621399** if you’re licensed as a health practitioner) as secondary.

**Tip:** If you have a *licensed medical* approach to massage therapy, consider **NAICS 621399 (Offices of All Other Miscellaneous Health Practitioners)** for that part of the business. Otherwise, stick to the personal care classification (812xxx codes).
