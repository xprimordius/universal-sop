## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Short answer:**  
For banks like **Bravera**, “**most recent financial statement**” typically means **within the last 90 days**. In some cases they will accept **up to 120 days**, but 90 is the safe standard.

---

## What qualifies as “recent”

### **For a business entity**
Accepted documents (choose **one**, unless they ask otherwise):
- **Balance Sheet + Profit & Loss** dated **≤ 90 days**
- **Year-to-Date (YTD) financials** through the most recent month
- **Most recent filed business tax return** (usually prior tax year)  
  *Used if current financials aren’t available*

### **For individuals / guarantors**
- **Most recent personal tax return** (last filed year)
- Sometimes a **personal financial statement (PFS)** dated **≤ 90 days**

---

## Practical bank standards (what underwriters expect)
- **≤ 90 days old** → ideal, frictionless approval  
- **91–120 days old** → sometimes accepted, may trigger follow-up  
- **>120 days old** → commonly rejected or flagged

---

## Date that matters
The **statement “as of” date**, not the print date.

Example:  
- Statement dated **Nov 30** submitted Feb 1 → ❌ likely stale  
- Statement dated **Dec 31** submitted Feb 1 → ✅ acceptable

---

## If you want to minimize scrutiny
Submit:
- **YTD P&L + Balance Sheet through last month**
- Clean formatting (QuickBooks / accountant-prepared preferred)
- Borrowing resolution dated **within 30 days**
