Yes, a Maltese-Yorkie-Shih Tzu mix (often called a Morkie-Shih Tzu) **can eat pineapple in small amounts**.

### ✅ Safe if:
- **Fresh, raw pineapple only** (no canned or sugary versions)
- **No skin or core** (only the soft, ripe flesh)
- **Small amounts** (a few tiny cubes as a treat)

### ⚠️ Avoid if:
- Your dog has **sensitive digestion** (it may cause gas or diarrhea)
- You’re feeding **too much** (pineapple is high in sugar)

### Summary:
