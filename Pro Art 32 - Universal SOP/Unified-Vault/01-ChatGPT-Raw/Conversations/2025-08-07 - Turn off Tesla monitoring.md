### 🧠 **Driver Monitoring via Cabin Camera**
If you want to disable the in-cabin camera that monitors attention while using Autopilot or FSD:

1. **Open Controls > Safety**
2. **Toggle OFF “Allow Cabin Camera Analytics”**

> This prevents Tesla from recording or analyzing your cabin camera footage.

---

### 👁️‍🗨️ **Driver Drowsiness Warning**
If your Tesla has a "Driver Drowsiness Warning" feature:

1. **Controls > Safety > Driver Drowsiness Warning**
2. **Toggle OFF**

> This disables alerts based on driver behavior.

---

### 🚘 **Autopilot Monitoring**
Tesla **does not allow disabling** driver monitoring entirely while using Autopilot. Hands must remain on the wheel. Cabin camera monitoring may still be active in the background (non-optional for FSD Beta).
