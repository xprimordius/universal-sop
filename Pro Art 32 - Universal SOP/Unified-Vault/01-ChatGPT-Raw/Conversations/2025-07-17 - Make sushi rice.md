## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Sushi Rice Recipe (Perfect Sticky & Seasoned)**

### ✅ Ingredients:
- 2 cups **short-grain white rice** (e.g., sushi rice or Calrose)
- 2 cups **water**
- 1/3 cup **rice vinegar**
- 2 tbsp **sugar**
- 1 tsp **salt**

---

### 🔪 Instructions:

#### 1. **Rinse the Rice**  
- Rinse rice under cold water until water runs clear (removes excess starch).

#### 2. **Cook the Rice**  
- Use a rice cooker or stovetop:
  - **Rice Cooker:** 1:1 ratio of rice to water.
  - **Stovetop:** Boil, then cover and simmer on low for ~18 minutes, then rest for 10 minutes off heat.

#### 3. **Season the Rice**  
- In a small pot, gently heat:
  - 1/3 cup **rice vinegar**
  - 2 tbsp **sugar**
  - 1 tsp **salt**  
  Stir until dissolved (do not boil).

#### 4. **Mix Gently**  
- Transfer hot rice to a large, non-metal bowl (preferably wooden).
- Pour vinegar mix over rice.
- Fold and fan: Use a cutting + folding motion with a rice paddle while fanning to cool the rice and give it a glossy finish.

---

### 💡 Tips:
- Don't mash or stir aggressively—keep grains intact.
- Use immediately once cool (room temp), not cold.
