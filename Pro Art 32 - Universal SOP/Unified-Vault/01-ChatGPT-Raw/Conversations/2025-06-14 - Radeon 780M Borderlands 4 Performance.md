### 🎮 How the Radeon 780M handles Borderlands 4 (predictably similar to BL3 performance)

There aren't any direct benchmarks yet for *Borderlands 4*, but we can use *Borderlands 3* testing as a solid reference point. Here’s what to expect:

---

#### 👍 Likely Performance Levels

- **Low to Medium settings @1080p or @720p**: Expect **30–60 FPS**. The *Borderlands 3* clip shows good performance at around 45–60 FPS (video above).  
- **Higher settings or resolution**: Performance drops significantly. The 780M, even with its 12 RDNA 3 CUs, struggles past medium detail.  
- **Use upscalers**: Enabling FSR (FidelityFX Super Resolution) can boost fps while keeping visuals acceptable.

---

#### 🔥 Hardware Strengths and Constraints

- The 780M is the strongest integrated GPU in its class—closely rivaling laptop RTX 2050 or GTX 1650 in some benchmarks citeturn0search4turn0search2.  
- It shines at lighter settings and benefits from fast dual-channel DDR5 memory (e.g., 5600 MHz) citeturn0search2turn0search8.

---

#### ⚠️ Potential Limitations

- **VRAM via shared system RAM** can bottleneck performance in VRAM-demanding scenes—common in open-world areas citeturn0search10.  
- Some users report performance issues due to throttling or driver misconfiguration—best to tweak BIOS power modes, dual-channel memory, and Radeon settings citeturn0search3.

---

#### 🛠️ Optimization Tips

1. **DLSS/FSR Upscaling**: Use FSR if BL4 supports it to raise effective resolution without fps loss.
2. **Run at 1080p with Medium/Low presets**: Ideal balance for playable frame rates.
3. **Dual-channel DDR5 RAM**: Essential—single-channel memory can halve performance.
4. **Adjust Radeon settings**: Enable Gaming Mode, disable Chill/Boost, set to Maximum Performance.
5. **Keep drivers & BIOS updated**: Ensures stable GPU clocks and avoids throttling issues.

---

### ✅ Bottom Line

Expect **30–60 FPS** on medium/low settings at 1080p (or better fps at 720p) for Borderlands 4 on AMD 780M. With proper tuning (FSR, memory setup, BIOS tweaks), gameplay should stay smooth. Just don’t set your expectations for high/ultra visuals.
