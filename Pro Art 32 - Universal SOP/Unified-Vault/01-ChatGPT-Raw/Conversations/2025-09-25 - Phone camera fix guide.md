# Immediate fixes — try these now
1. **Force-quit & restart**: Close Camera app → Force restart phone.  
2. **Remove case + clean lens**: Wipe lens with microfiber; check for physical cracks.  
3. **Test front/back & other apps**: Open FaceTime/WhatsApp/Instagram camera — does *any* camera work?  
4. **Check storage**: If storage is full, camera can fail — free up space.  
5. **Permissions & updates**: Settings → Camera permissions; update the OS and Camera app.  
6. **Clear cache (Android)**: Settings → Apps → Camera → Storage → Clear cache/data (Android only).  
7. **Try a 3rd-party camera app**: If that works, it’s a Camera app problem — reinstall defaults.  
8. **Safe Mode (Android)**: Boot to Safe Mode to rule out a rogue app.  
9. **Reset settings**: iPhone: Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone → Reset All Settings (keeps data). Android: Reset App Preferences.

# If still broken — next moves
- **Backup now** (important): Transfer any sensitive video/photos to a separate device or encrypted cloud (iCloud/Google with 2FA) *before* anything else.  
- **Factory reset** only after backup and trying the above.  
- **Hardware likely** if camera fails in all apps → get professional repair (Apple/authorized shop or carrier repair).  
- **Check warranty / AppleCare / carrier insurance** — may cover repair/replacement.  
- **Avoid DIY teardown** unless you’re experienced — can void warranty.
