# Resolution Letter Template

---

## 👤 You

How to create a resolution letter to get permission from a corp to open a bank account if you are the only owner

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Creating a resolution letter to get permission from a corporation to open a bank account as the sole owner involves drafting a formal document that clearly states your intentions and provides the necessary details for the bank. Below is a sample resolution letter template you can use:

---

**[Your Name]**  
**[Your Address]**  
**[City, State, ZIP Code]**  
**[Email Address]**  
**[Phone Number]**  

**[Date]**

**[Bank Name]**  
**[Bank Address]**  
**[City, State, ZIP Code]**

Dear [Bank Manager's Name],

**Subject: Resolution to Open a Corporate Bank Account**

I, [Your Name], the sole owner and authorized representative of [Your Corporation's Name], hereby resolve to open a bank account with [Bank Name]. As the sole shareholder and director of the corporation, I am authorized to make financial decisions on behalf of the company.

The following resolutions were adopted by the sole shareholder and director of [Your Corporation's Name] on [Date]:

1. **Resolution to Open a Bank Account**  
   RESOLVED, that [Your Corporation's Name] is authorized to open and maintain a corporate bank account with [Bank Name].

2. **Authorization of Signatories**  
   RESOLVED, that [Your Name], as the sole owner and director, is authorized to sign and execute any documents required by [Bank Name] to open and manage the corporate bank account.

3. **Deposit and Withdrawal Authority**  
   RESOLVED, that [Your Name] is authorized to deposit funds into and withdraw funds from the corporate bank account as needed for business operations.

4. **Account Management**  
   RESOLVED, that [Your Name] shall have full authority to manage the bank account, including but not limited to, making deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and other necessary transactions.

I hereby certify that the above resolutions are true and correct and were duly adopted by [Your Corporation's Name] on [Date].

Sincerely,

_________________________  
[Your Name]  
Sole Owner and Director  
[Your Corporation's Name]  
[Corporation's Address]  
[City, State, ZIP Code]

---

### Instructions:

1. **Customize the template** with your specific details, including your name, corporation's name, address, and bank details.
2. **Print the letter** on your corporation's letterhead if you have one.
3. **Sign the letter** in the space provided.
4. **Provide any additional documentation** the bank might require, such as your corporation's articles of incorporation, proof of identity, and any other relevant documents.

This formal letter should meet the requirements of most banks for opening a corporate account. Make sure to verify with the bank if they have any specific forms or additional requirements.
