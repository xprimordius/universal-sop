My domestic faucet is not pulling the water through the extended hose, how to fix

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Got it. Here’s the most likely fixes, step-by-step:

### 1. Check Diverter
- Most faucets with a pull-out/pull-down hose have a **diverter valve** that switches water from spout to hose.  
- If clogged with mineral buildup → water won’t flow into the hose.  
- **Fix:** Shut off water, remove faucet handle/body, clean or replace the diverter.

### 2. Inspect Hose
- **Kinked or twisted hose** can block water. Pull the hose fully out and check for bends.  
- **Fix:** Straighten or replace if it’s damaged.

### 3. Verify Connections
- Look under the sink where the hose connects to the supply line.  
- Loose or misaligned connection = no suction.  
- **Fix:** Re-seat the hose connection tightly.

### 4. Check for Debris
- Small screen filters (aerators or check valves) can clog.  
- **Fix:** Unscrew the spray head and flush water directly from the hose. If water flows now, clean the spray head.

### 5. Water Pressure / Cartridge
- Weak water flow overall = clogged **cartridge** inside faucet body.  
- **Fix:** Remove cartridge, soak in vinegar, reinstall (or replace if worn).

---

1. Remove spray head → test flow directly.  
2. If no flow → diverter or hose issue.  
3. If flow works without spray head → spray head clogged.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Perfect observation — you’re describing an **air admittance / venting issue**. Here’s the breakdown:

---

### How Dometic Faucets Work
- The pump **sucks water** from the sealed container.  
- For suction to continue, **air must replace the water** inside the container.  
- That’s what the little **air nozzle/vent cap** is for: it lets air in while keeping spills minimal.

---

### If No Air Can Enter
- As water leaves, a **vacuum forms**.  
- The pump keeps trying to draw, but flow slows/stops because nothing replaces the displaced water.  
- That’s why when you twisted the nozzle and let air in, water could flow again.

---

### Solution
- Keep the **air vent open enough** to let air in while drawing water.  
- Some setups use a **special cap with a built-in check valve** so you don’t have to loosen it manually.  
- If the vent is blocked/clogged → clean it or leave it cracked open while using.

---

⚡ **Answer to your direct Q:**  
No, it won’t keep working if the container is fully sealed. The pump needs air replacement, or it’ll cavitate and fail to pull water.
