Ankle and hip pain from running is usually due to one or more of the following:

1. **Overuse Injuries** – Repetitive stress without enough recovery (e.g. tendonitis, bursitis).
2. **Poor Running Form** – Heel striking, overstriding, or pelvic instability can strain joints.
3. **Weak Glutes/Core** – Leads to poor alignment and excess force on hips/ankles.
4. **Tight Muscles** – Especially calves, hamstrings, and hip flexors, which alter gait.
5. **Worn or Incorrect Footwear** – Lack of support or wrong fit affects alignment.
6. **Leg Length Imbalance or Structural Issues** – Can create uneven stress on joints.
7. **Hard Surfaces or Sudden Mileage Increases** – Spike impact load on joints.

**Fix:** Get gait analysis, strengthen glutes/core, stretch, use proper shoes, and increase mileage gradually.

Compression socks hurting the top of your calf usually means:

- **Too much compression** (wrong mmHg rating for your needs).
- **Improper fit** (too tight for your calf circumference).
- **Elastic band digging in**, restricting blood flow or irritating nerves.

### Fix:
- Switch to **15–20 mmHg** light compression if you're using 20–30+.
- Measure your calf at its widest point and get socks sized accordingly.
- Try socks with **graduated compression** and a **wide, soft top band**.
