### Neuropathy Overview:
Neuropathy refers to damage or dysfunction of one or more nerves, leading to symptoms such as pain, tingling, numbness, or muscle weakness. It can affect sensory, motor, or autonomic nerves, depending on the cause and severity. Vitamin B6 (pyridoxine) is essential for nerve health, but excessive amounts can lead to neuropathy.

### How Excess Vitamin B6 Causes Neuropathy:
Vitamin B6 plays a role in neurotransmitter synthesis and nerve signaling. However, **too much B6 can be toxic to nerves**:
1. **Direct Neurotoxicity:** High levels of B6 may overstimulate nerve cells, leading to degeneration of nerve fibers (axonopathy). This is more likely to affect sensory nerves, which carry signals like touch, temperature, and pain.
2. **Imbalance in Neurotransmitters:** Excess B6 disrupts the balance of neurotransmitters like serotonin and dopamine, further impairing nerve function.
3. **Symptoms of B6 Toxicity-Induced Neuropathy:**
   - Tingling or numbness, especially in the hands and feet (peripheral neuropathy).
   - Burning sensations.
   - Difficulty with fine motor tasks, like buttoning shirts.
   - Weakness or cramping in affected areas.

### Why This is Problematic in Your Case:
1. **Combined Excess:**
   - The tolerable upper intake level (UL) for vitamin B6 is **100 mg/day** for adults.
   - With Thorne B-Complex #6 providing **100 mg** and Rho Liposomal Multivitamin adding another **1 mg**, your intake slightly exceeds this threshold.

2. **Long-Term Use Risks:**
   - **Cumulative Toxicity:** Prolonged intake of excess B6 can cause **irreversible nerve damage**, even if the symptoms develop slowly over time.
   - **Sensitivity Variance:** Some individuals may experience neuropathy at levels below 200 mg/day, depending on genetic factors, underlying health conditions, and dietary intake of B6 from food sources.

3. **Potentially Unnecessary Supplementation:**
   - If you're already meeting your B6 needs through diet or multivitamins, taking an additional 100 mg from Thorne B-Complex #6 might not provide further benefit but instead introduces risk.

### Why Exceeding UL is a Problem:
- The **UL is not a "toxicity threshold"**; it's the maximum amount unlikely to cause harm in most people. Consistently exceeding the UL increases the likelihood of adverse effects, especially for vitamins stored in the body like B6.
- **Neuropathy risks are well-documented:** Case studies have shown sensory neuropathy symptoms in people consuming 200–500 mg/day, with some sensitive individuals affected at 100–200 mg/day.

### Other Considerations:
- **Potential Misdiagnosis:** Neuropathy symptoms can mimic other conditions like diabetes or autoimmune diseases, which could complicate diagnosis if excess B6 is the cause.
- **Unnecessary Risk:** While some conditions (e.g., carpal tunnel syndrome, certain nerve disorders) benefit from higher doses of B6, routine use of such high amounts in healthy individuals is unwarranted.

### Key Takeaways:
- **Balance is critical:** While B6 is vital for nerve health, too much can damage the very system it supports.
- **Avoid Chronic Overdose:** Limit your intake to the recommended levels unless advised otherwise by a healthcare provider.
- **Monitoring is Important:** If you choose to combine these supplements, consider monitoring for early signs of neuropathy (tingling, numbness, weakness) and adjusting your intake accordingly.

### Final Advice:
To prevent potential harm, either reduce the dosage of the Thorne B-Complex #6 or stop using one of the two supplements entirely. A consultation with a healthcare provider or nutritionist can provide a personalized plan based on your health needs.

### Signs and Symptoms of Vitamin B6-Induced Neuropathy:
If you're consuming excessive amounts of vitamin B6, you may experience the following **early signs** of neuropathy. These are most likely to appear in the **hands and feet (peripheral nerves):**

#### 1. **Sensory Symptoms** (most common):
   - **Tingling or Numbness:** A "pins and needles" sensation, often starting in the fingertips or toes and spreading.
   - **Burning Sensations:** A feeling of heat or discomfort in the skin.
   - **Reduced Sensation:** Difficulty feeling textures or temperatures, like not noticing hot water or rough surfaces.
   - **Pain:** Sharp, stabbing, or shooting pains in the extremities.

#### 2. **Motor Symptoms** (less common):
   - **Weakness:** Difficulty gripping objects, such as holding a pen or opening jars.
   - **Loss of Coordination:** Struggling with fine motor skills like buttoning clothes.
   - **Muscle Cramping or Twitching:** Especially in the legs or hands.

#### 3. **Other Possible Symptoms:**
   - **Clumsiness:** Dropping objects frequently or losing balance.
   - **Hyperesthesia:** Hypersensitivity to touch, where even light contact feels painful.
   - **Loss of Reflexes:** Reduced reaction in tendon reflexes (e.g., knee jerk).

---

### When to Suspect B6 Toxicity:
- **Chronic High Doses:** Symptoms often arise after prolonged daily intake of **100–200 mg/day or more**.
- **Timing of Symptoms:** If you started high-dose vitamin B6 supplementation recently, symptoms may develop within weeks to months.
- **Symmetry:** B6-induced neuropathy is typically **bilateral** (affects both sides of the body equally).

---

### How to Confirm Neuropathy:
1. **Self-Assessment:**
   - Pay attention to unusual sensations (tingling, burning, numbness) in your hands or feet.
   - Test your coordination and grip strength by performing small tasks (e.g., writing, buttoning).

2. **Professional Evaluation:**
   - **Neurological Exam:** A doctor can assess reflexes, strength, and sensation.
   - **Nerve Conduction Studies:** Measures how well electrical signals travel along your nerves.
   - **Vitamin B6 Blood Test:** High levels of B6 in the blood can confirm excess intake.

3. **Discontinue High Doses:**
   - If you suspect symptoms are related to supplementation, stop taking high doses immediately. Symptoms often improve after discontinuing the excess intake, though severe cases can cause permanent damage.

---

### How to Prevent or Mitigate Neuropathy:
- **Monitor Your Intake:** Ensure you’re not exceeding the tolerable upper intake level of **100 mg/day**.
- **Adjust Dosage Early:** If symptoms occur, stop the supplements and consult a healthcare provider.
- **Gradual Improvement:** Neuropathy from B6 toxicity typically resolves within **6 months** of stopping the supplement in mild cases.

---

### When to Seek Medical Attention:
- If symptoms persist or worsen despite stopping the supplement.
- If you experience severe pain, muscle weakness, or difficulty with daily tasks.
- If you're unsure whether the symptoms are due to vitamin B6 or another condition.

#### **Note:** Early recognition and action can prevent permanent nerve damage, so don’t delay seeking help if you notice symptoms!
