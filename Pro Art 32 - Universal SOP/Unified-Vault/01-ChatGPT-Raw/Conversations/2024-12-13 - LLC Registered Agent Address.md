1. **Physical Address Requirement**: The registered agent address must be a physical street address in Minnesota. It cannot be a P.O. Box or virtual office unless the virtual office provides a physical location where documents can be received during regular business hours.

2. **Availability During Business Hours**: Someone must be available at the address during regular business hours to receive official documents, such as service of process or legal notices.

3. **Compliance**: The address must be compliant with state requirements and capable of handling official correspondence for the LLC.

If your business address meets these conditions, it can be used as your registered agent address. If your current business address is a virtual office or does not meet these requirements, you may need to designate a different registered agent or use a registered agent service.
