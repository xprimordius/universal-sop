Good question — this kind of “free iPad” offer is **not truly free**, it’s a **bill-credit promotion** tied to conditions. Here’s how it really works:  

---

### 🔍 Breakdown of the Offer
- **Retail price:** $499.99 (Apple iPad A16)
- **Advertised cost:** $0.00/mo  
- **Mechanism:** You pay $13.88/mo for ~36 months, but the carrier gives you **bill credits** totaling that same amount.  
  - Example: they charge you $13.88 → apply a $13.88 credit → net $0.
- **Condition:** You must:
  1. **Add a new line of service** (tablet plan, typically $10–$20 per month).  
  2. **Stay on that plan for the full 36 months.**
  3. Remain **in good standing** (no missed payments, cancellations, or downgrades).

---

### 💡 What Happens If You Cancel Early
- The credits **stop immediately**.
- You become responsible for the **remaining balance** of the iPad’s full retail price.
- Example: cancel after 12 months → you owe the remaining ~$333.

---

### 💸 Real Cost Over Time
Even though the device looks “free,” you still pay for:
- The **tablet data plan** ($10–$20 / month × 36 mo = $360–$720 total)
- A **$40 activation fee**
- Taxes/fees on the retail value in some states

---

### ✅ TL;DR
It’s free **only if you keep the new line active for the entire credit term**.
