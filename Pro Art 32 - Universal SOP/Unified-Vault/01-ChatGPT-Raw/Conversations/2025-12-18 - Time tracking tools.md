## 🎯 Best for Simple Start / Stop + Custom Categories

**1) Toggl Track**  
• Hit **Start / Stop** to log any work (no upfront setup required).  
• Tag entries with custom **projects/tasks** (e.g., sales training, hypnosis).  
• Mobile, desktop, web, calendar view, reports + analytics.  
• Free tier available. citeturn0search0turn0search3

**2) Clockify**  
• **Free forever** time tracker with easy start/stop.  
• Create **projects, labels, tags** you can edit later.  
• Visual reports and timeline views.  
• Best ultra-simple option. citeturn0search10

**3) My Hours**  
• Start/stop timers on multiple tasks.  
• Can assign unlimited projects and **custom tags** for later sorting.  
• Clean UI with detailed real-time reports. citeturn0search1

**4) TimeCamp**  
• Start-stop or automatic tracking.  
• Integrates with other tools like Trello/ClickUp.  
• Good for self-analysis and long-term habits. citeturn0search6

**5) TMetric**  
• Simple time tracker + project/task tagging.  
• Useful if you want *money estimates* or cost tracking later. citeturn0search21
