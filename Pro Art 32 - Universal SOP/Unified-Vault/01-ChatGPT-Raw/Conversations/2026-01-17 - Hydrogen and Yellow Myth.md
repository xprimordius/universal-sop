- Its visible emissions are mainly:
  - **Red** (~656 nm)

### Why yellow is often confused here
- **Yellow (~589 nm)** is strongly associated with **sodium**, not hydrogen.
- Many flames look yellow due to **trace sodium contamination**, not hydrogen.
