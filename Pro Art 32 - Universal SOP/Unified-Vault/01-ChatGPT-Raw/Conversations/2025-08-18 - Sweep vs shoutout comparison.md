Here’s the clear breakdown:  

### **Sweep**  
- **Definition**: Paid placement where your product/service is showcased to a creator’s audience, usually as a full feature or integration.  
- **Style**: Feels like part of the content (integrated into a story, vlog, or demo).  
- **Reach**: Often broader and longer-lasting since it’s embedded in evergreen content.  
- **Cost**: Higher because it’s more creative, time-consuming, and “native.”  
- **Effectiveness**: Strong for trust-building and long-term brand association.  

### **Shoutout**  
- **Definition**: Quick mention, tag, or callout from a creator (e.g., Instagram Story, TikTok video mention).  
- **Style**: Short, direct, less integrated.  
- **Reach**: Immediate spike in visibility, usually shorter lifespan (24–72 hours unless saved/highlighted).  
- **Cost**: Lower compared to sweeps.  
- **Effectiveness**: Great for bursts of traffic, followers, or awareness but weaker on deep trust or longevity.  

---

### **TL;DR**  
- **Sweep** = long-form, integrated, higher cost, stronger long-term trust.  
- **Shoutout** = quick, cheaper, short-lived, good for bursts.

## 👤 You

Difference snake draft vs auction draft

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here’s the sharp breakdown:  

---

## **Snake Draft**  
- **Order**: Teams pick in a set order, then reverse next round. (Ex: Team 1 picks first in Round 1, last in Round 2.)  
- **Style**: Like a traditional sports draft.  
- **Fairness**: Balances advantage of early picks with late-round reversals.  
- **Speed**: Faster, simpler, easier for beginners.  
- **Limitation**: You only get players available when it’s your turn.  

---

## **Auction Draft**  
- **Order**: No set turn order. Every player is nominated, and managers **bid** using a fixed budget (commonly $200).  
- **Style**: Like an auction—highest bid wins the player.  
- **Fairness**: Everyone has a shot at every player, not just based on draft order.  
- **Strategy**: Budget management is key (stars vs. balanced roster).  
- **Limitation**: Longer, more complex; requires planning and discipline.  

---

### **TL;DR**  
- **Snake Draft** → fixed order, quick, balanced, beginner-friendly.  
- **Auction Draft** → open bidding, more strategy/fairness, but slower and more complex.
