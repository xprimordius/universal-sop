### 🥕 Maple-Glazed Roasted Baby Carrots (for 3 lbs)

**Prep time:** 10 min **Cook:** 35–45 min **Temp:** 400 °F  

---

#### **Ingredients**
- 3 lbs baby carrots (whole or halved if large)  
- 3 Tbsp olive oil or melted butter  
- ¼ cup pure maple syrup  
- 2 Tbsp brown sugar (optional for extra caramelization)  
- 1 tsp salt  
- ½ tsp black pepper  
- ½ tsp cinnamon or smoked paprika (optional)  
- Fresh thyme or parsley (optional for garnish)

---

#### **Instructions**
1. **Preheat oven** to **400 °F (204 °C)**. Line 2 large baking sheets with parchment.  
2. **Toss** carrots in a bowl with oil, maple syrup, brown sugar, salt, and pepper until evenly coated.  
3. **Spread** in a single layer on the pans (avoid crowding for even browning).  
4. **Roast 35–45 min**, flipping once halfway, until tender and edges caramelized.  
5. **Optional glaze boost:** During the last 10 min, brush with 1 Tbsp extra maple syrup for a shiny finish.  
6. **Finish:** Sprinkle herbs or a pinch of flaky salt before serving.

---

**Tip:** For extra flavor depth, add 1 Tbsp balsamic vinegar or Dijon mustard to the glaze before roasting.
