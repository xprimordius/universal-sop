## 🤖 ChatGPT

Pain between the eyebrows and at the back of the head could point to a few possible causes:

### 1. **Tension Headache**
- **Symptoms:** Dull, pressure-like pain in forehead (including between eyebrows) and back of the head.
- **Cause:** Stress, lack of sleep, eye strain, poor posture.
- **Fix:** Hydration, rest, massage, stretching, magnesium, OTC pain relievers (e.g., ibuprofen).

### 2. **Sinus Pressure**
- **Symptoms:** Pressure or ache between eyebrows, possibly with nasal congestion or facial tenderness.
- **Cause:** Sinus infection or inflammation.
- **Fix:** Steam inhalation, nasal rinse (e.g., Neti Pot), hydration, decongestants.

### 3. **Occipital Neuralgia**
- **Symptoms:** Sharp, electric shock-like pain at the back of the head or neck that can radiate forward.
- **Cause:** Irritation of occipital nerves, often from posture, trauma, or tension.
- **Fix:** Massage, heat, posture correction, anti-inflammatory meds. Severe cases may need nerve blocks.

### 4. **Eye Strain / Screen Fatigue**
- **Symptoms:** Pain between eyebrows, possibly radiating to the back of the head.
- **Cause:** Long periods of screen use or reading.
- **Fix:** Eye breaks (20-20-20 rule), blue light filters, vision check.

---

**If:**  
- Pain is **sudden, severe**, or accompanied by **vision changes, vomiting, or numbness**, seek medical attention **immediately**.  
- It's been **persistent or recurring**, she should see a healthcare provider for proper diagnosis.
