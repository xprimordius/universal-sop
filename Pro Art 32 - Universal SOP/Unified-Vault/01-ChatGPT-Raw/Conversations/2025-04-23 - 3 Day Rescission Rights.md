- Sales **$25+ made in your home** or at a **temporary location** (e.g., hotel, trade show).
- It **does NOT** apply to:
  - **Online or in-store purchases**

- If the contract **expressly waives refund rights** and was **not covered by the Cooling-Off Rule**, you're **likely bound** by its terms.
