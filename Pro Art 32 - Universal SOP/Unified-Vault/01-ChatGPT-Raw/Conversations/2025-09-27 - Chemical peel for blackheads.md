Yes.  

Chemical peels **help loosen and lift out blackheads**, but the effect depends on the type and strength:  

- **Superficial/light peels** (glycolic, salicylic, lactic) → exfoliate dead skin, unclog pores, and gradually bring blackheads to the surface.  
- **Medium/deep peels** → more aggressive, but usually used for scarring, pigmentation, or deeper issues—not just blackheads.  

⚠️ Important:  
- Blackheads may not "pop up" instantly after one peel—it often takes several treatments.

- Extra blackhead extraction is usually done by a dermatologist or esthetician after the peel softens the plugs.
