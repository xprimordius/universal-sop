- **YES – in moderation.**

  - **Black olives**: Neutral
  - **Green olives**: Slightly more reactive but still generally okay in small amounts
  - **Olive oil (extra virgin)**: Highly beneficial

- **Why they’re good:**
  - Rich in **monounsaturated fats** → heart + brain health
  - Contain **polyphenols** → anti-inflammatory, antioxidant
  - Support **hormonal balance** and **immune function**

### 🤔 What About the Bitterness?

- **Bitter taste ≠ danger.**
  - Bitterness is often from **oleuropein**, a powerful antioxidant in olives.
  - Nature uses bitterness to **limit overconsumption**, not necessarily to say *"avoid."*

- Many of the healthiest foods (e.g., arugula, dandelion, turmeric) are bitter.
  - Your body may **detox** better or get stronger bile flow from these compounds.

### ⚠️ Cautions:

- **Cured/salty olives**: Too much sodium can be an issue. Choose:
  - Low-sodium
  - Brined without harsh preservatives (no potassium sorbate)
  - Organic if possible

- **Listen to your body**: If you feel bloated, tired, or inflamed after eating them, reduce or cut back.
