# Link Payment Processor Fees

---

## 👤 You

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes, **Link by Stripe** charges processing fees similar to other payment processors. The fees typically include:

1. **Credit/Debit Card Payments:** Standard fee is usually around 2.9% + $0.30 per successful transaction. This can vary based on the card type and location.
  
2. **ACH Payments:** Often around 0.8%, capped at $5 per transaction.

3. **International Transactions:** Additional fees might apply, such as an extra 1% for international cards.

It's important to review Stripe’s specific pricing details or reach out directly for precise information, as fees can vary based on factors like volume, currency, and region.
