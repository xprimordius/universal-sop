How viable is doing Google PPC ads to get land deals specifically 10+% under market value in high grossing developing areas where all the builders are going

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Google PPC for Land Deals in Builder Hot Zones: Viability Analysis**

**TL;DR:** It can work *very well*—but only with hyper-specific targeting, strong funnel execution, and patient capital. It’s viable, but not beginner-friendly. Cost-per-lead (CPL) is high, but **the margin potential can justify it** if you're dialed in.

---

### ✅ **Why It *Can* Work:**
1. **High Intent Leads**  
   Sellers searching “sell land fast,” “value of my land,” etc., have urgency → great leverage for lowball offers.

2. **Inbound Advantage**  
   In hot markets (e.g., Central TX, Phoenix suburbs, FL coasts), sellers are often overwhelmed by agents/investors. PPC gives you **first-mover advantage** via direct-to-seller.

3. **Arbitrage Opportunity**  
   Builders pay premium. If you can lock land at 10–25% below market and flip to them or assign to agents, **your spread is high enough** to justify PPC costs.

---

### ❌ **Why Most Fail:**
1. **High CPL:**  
   - Rural/low comp areas: $15–$40/lead  
   - Urban/suburban dev areas: $50–$150/lead  
   > You may spend $3,000–$6,000+ before getting one deeply discounted deal.

2. **Unqualified Leads:**  
   Many landowners want top dollar or don't even know zoning/value. You must **prequalify fast** and automate dead leads.

3. **Weak Funnels:**  
   PPC is only half the game. **Your site, credibility, and offer strategy must convert**.

---

### 🔑 **Success Conditions:**
- **Tightly focused zip code/city campaigns** (e.g., “sell land in Leander TX fast”)
- **Custom pages per region with dynamic content**
- **Strong qualification form:**  
  - Parcel ID  
  - Reason for selling  
  - Timeline  
  - Price expectations  
- **CRM & SMS follow-up sequence** (use GoHighLevel or similar)
- **Fast offers & comps engine** (PropStream, PRYCD, etc.)

---

### 🔁 Example ROI Model:
| Metric                   | Estimate              |

> ROI: ~3x–6x **IF** offer process and market targeting are dialed in.

### 🧠 Strategic Tip:
- **Use builders' expansion maps + public permit data** to triangulate where to target.
- Reverse-engineer their acquisitions → PPC into those zip codes only.
