## 👤 You

Is lightly pink ground lamb okay

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes — **lightly pink ground lamb can be safe**, *if*:

1. **Internal Temperature** reached **160°F (71°C)** — verified with a food thermometer.
2. It’s **not raw or cold inside**, and juices run **clear**, not red.
3. It was **cooked evenly** and **stored properly** before cooking.

**Color alone ≠ safety.** Always go by **temperature**, not just appearance.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

If it's been sitting in **boiling fat** (e.g. searing or shallow frying) for **several minutes**, it's likely safe — but here’s a quick safety checklist:

- **Texture:** Firm, not mushy or raw-feeling when pressed.
- **Juices:** Run **clear**, not red or bloody.
- **Smell:** Neutral or savory — no sour or "off" smell.
- **Color inside:** Slight pink is fine if the texture is cooked and it’s hot throughout.

If all of the above check out, it’s likely safe — but next time, a $10 digital thermometer is a great investment.
