### 1. **Western Medicine Perspective**

   - **Common Causes:**
     - **Allergies:** Environmental allergens can trigger redness due to histamine release, causing blood vessels in the eyes to dilate.
     - **Dry Eyes:** Insufficient lubrication can lead to irritation and visible blood vessels. This is especially common in people who spend long hours in front of screens or in dry environments.
     - **Eye Strain:** Prolonged screen time, poor lighting, or lack of sleep can lead to fatigue and strain in the eyes, resulting in redness.
     - **Infections:** Conjunctivitis or other eye infections can cause red veins due to inflammation.
     - **High Blood Pressure:** Elevated blood pressure may increase pressure in the eye blood vessels, causing visible veins.
     - **Irritants:** Smoking, pollution, and chlorinated water exposure can irritate the eyes.

   - **Remedies:**
     - **Over-the-counter Eye Drops:** Artificial tears for dryness, antihistamine eye drops for allergies.
     - **Reduce Screen Time:** Take frequent breaks following the 20-20-20 rule (every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds).
     - **Hydrate:** Proper hydration can help keep eyes moist and reduce dryness-related redness.
