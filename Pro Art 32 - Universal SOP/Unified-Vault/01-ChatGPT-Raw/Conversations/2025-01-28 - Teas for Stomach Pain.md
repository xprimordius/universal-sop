Certain teas can be soothing for stomach pain, depending on the cause. Here are a few teas that are generally helpful:

1. **Peppermint Tea**: Relieves bloating, gas, and indigestion due to its antispasmodic properties. Avoid if you have acid reflux, as it can worsen symptoms.

2. **Ginger Tea**: Helps with nausea, indigestion, and cramping. It has anti-inflammatory properties that soothe the digestive tract.

3. **Chamomile Tea**: Calms the stomach and reduces inflammation. It’s gentle and can also help relax the body.

4. **Fennel Tea**: Reduces gas and bloating and helps relieve cramps.

5. **Licorice Root Tea**: Protects the stomach lining and reduces inflammation, which can help with heartburn and ulcers.

6. **Green Tea**: May help with digestion, but it should be consumed in moderation as it contains caffeine, which can irritate sensitive stomachs.

### Important Note:
- Avoid teas with high caffeine if your stomach pain is severe or due to ulcers.
- If the pain persists, worsens, or is accompanied by other symptoms, consult a healthcare provider.
