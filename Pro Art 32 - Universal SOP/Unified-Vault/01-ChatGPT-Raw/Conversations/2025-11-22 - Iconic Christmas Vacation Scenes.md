## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here’s how to erase or remove **stickers** on an iPhone — depends on what kind:  

---

### **1. iMessage Stickers (on messages or photos)**
**To remove a sticker from a message bubble or image:**
1. Open the **Messages** app.  
2. Find the **message** with the sticker.  
3. **Press and hold** on the sticker until a menu appears.  
4. Tap **Sticker Details** → swipe left on the sticker → tap **Delete**.  

*Alternative:* If it’s layered on a photo or bubble, press and hold the sticker itself and drag it slightly — a **trash icon** will appear; drop it there.

---

### **2. Keyboard / Sticker Packs**
**To delete a sticker pack (e.g., Bitmoji, Memojis, or apps):**
1. Open **Messages** → open a chat.  
2. Tap the **“+” (App Drawer)** icon next to the text box.  
3. Tap **Manage** (top-right).  
4. Turn off or delete the sticker packs you don’t want.  

Or:  
- Go to **Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Messages → Review Large Attachments**, and delete sticker/media data there.

---

### **3. Photo Stickers (iOS 17+ “Lift Subject” Stickers)**
If you added stickers to photos in Photos or iMessage:
1. Open the **Photos app**.  
2. Tap **Edit** on the image.  
3. Tap the **sticker** → tap **Delete Sticker** (trash icon).
