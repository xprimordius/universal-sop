## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Infatuated** means being intensely but *short-term obsessed* or *in love* with someone or something — often driven by excitement or attraction rather than deep understanding.  

**Example:**  
“He’s infatuated with her” → He feels strong passion or admiration, but it might fade once reality sets in.
