## 🤖 ChatGPT

### **Homemade Baked Potato Pizza (Pizza Lucé Style)**

#### **Ingredients:**
- 1 **pizza dough** (store-bought or homemade)
- 2 **medium red potatoes**, boiled and mashed
- 2 tbsp **butter**
- 2 cloves **garlic**, minced
- ¼ cup **heavy cream** (or milk)
- Salt & pepper to taste
- 1 cup **shredded cheddar cheese**
- ½ cup **mozzarella cheese**
- ½ cup **cooked bacon**, crumbled
- ¼ cup **diced tomatoes**
- ¼ cup **chopped broccoli** (optional)
- 2 tbsp **green onions**, chopped (optional)
- Sour cream (for serving)

#### **Instructions:**
1. **Preheat oven** to **450°F (232°C)**. If using a pizza stone, place it in the oven while preheating.
2. **Prepare mashed potatoes:** In a bowl, mash the boiled potatoes with butter, garlic, cream, salt, and pepper. Mix until smooth.
3. **Roll out pizza dough** onto a lightly floured surface. Place on a baking sheet or pizza stone.
4. **Spread mashed potatoes** evenly over the dough, leaving a small crust border.
5. **Add toppings:** Sprinkle cheddar and mozzarella cheese over the potatoes. Add crumbled bacon, diced tomatoes, and chopped broccoli.
6. **Bake** for **12–15 minutes** or until the crust is golden and cheese is bubbly.
7. **Garnish** with chopped green onions.
8. **Serve hot** with sour cream on the side.

Enjoy your **Baked Potato Pizza**, just like Pizza Lucé’s version!
