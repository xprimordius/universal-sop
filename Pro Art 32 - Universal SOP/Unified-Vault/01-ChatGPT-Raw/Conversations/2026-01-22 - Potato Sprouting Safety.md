## 👤 You

Are potatoes sprouting bad? Can I cut them off and eat safely?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Short answer:** *Sometimes safe, sometimes not.*

### When potatoes are **safe to eat**
- **Small sprouts only**
- **Potato is firm** (not soft/shriveled)
- **No green color**

👉 **Action:**  
Cut off **all sprouts plus ~½ inch** of surrounding flesh.  
Peel thickly. Cook thoroughly. Safe for most people.

### When potatoes are **NOT safe**
- **Green skin or green flesh**
- **Bitter smell or taste**
- **Soft, wrinkled, or many long sprouts**

🚫 **Do not eat. Discard.**

### Why this matters
Sprouting increases **solanine** and **chaconine** (toxic glycoalkaloids).  
These **do not break down with cooking**.

### Symptoms if ingested (rare but real)
- Nausea, vomiting
- Diarrhea
- Headache, dizziness

### Bottom line
- **Firm + no green + minimal sprouts = OK after trimming**
- **Green, soft, bitter, or heavily sprouted = trash it**
