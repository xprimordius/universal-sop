Those “hard, pea-sized balls” under the skin on the legs or near the vulva can come from a few different causes—most often **cysts**, **ingrown hairs/folliculitis**, or **hidradenitis suppurativa (HS)**.  
Here’s how to tell them apart and what to do:

---

### ⚕️ Possible Causes
1. **Epidermoid or sebaceous cysts** – smooth, firm lumps under the skin; may have a small pore; non-painful unless infected.  
2. **Ingrown hairs / folliculitis** – from shaving, tight clothes, or friction; usually red, tender, sometimes with a white tip.  
3. **Hidradenitis suppurativa (HS)** – deeper, recurrent painful lumps or abscesses around groin, thighs, buttocks, or armpits; may drain fluid and leave scars.  
4. **Bartholin gland cyst (vaginal area)** – lump near vaginal opening from blocked gland duct.

---

### 🩺 What You Can Do Now
- **Do NOT squeeze or cut** them—this can worsen infection or scarring.  
- **Warm compresses** (10–15 min, 2–3×/day) to help drainage and soothe inflammation.  
- **Wear loose, breathable clothing** and avoid friction or shaving until it heals.  
- **Wash gently** with an antibacterial or benzoyl peroxide cleanser (if not irritated).  
- **Topical clindamycin 1% or chlorhexidine wash** can help with HS or recurrent folliculitis (needs prescription).  
- **Pain/swelling?** OTC ibuprofen may help temporarily.

---

### 🚨 See a Clinician If
- The lumps are **painful, red, draining**, or **keep coming back**.  
- You have **multiple lesions in folds** (groin, armpits, under breasts).  
- You have **fever** or spreading redness.  
- They’ve **been present >2–3 weeks** without change.

A **dermatologist or gynecologist** can confirm the diagnosis and, if HS or cystic, may use:
- **Steroid or antibiotic injections**
- **Incision & drainage**
- **Long-term medications** (antibiotics, retinoids, or biologics for HS)
- **Laser hair removal** to prevent recurrence
