## 👤 You

Is it possible to have two different Apple devices sending audio to one Bluetooth headset at the same time

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

## Short Answer  
**No — not simultaneously.** One Bluetooth headset cannot play live audio from **two Apple devices at the same time**.

---

## Why This Is a Hard “No”
- **Bluetooth Classic audio = single active source**
- Apple devices follow this standard
- Headsets can *pair* with multiple devices, but only **one can stream at a time**

When the second device tries to play:
→ it **steals** the connection  
→ the first device is muted or disconnected

---

### 1️⃣ **Apple AirPods + Automatic Switching**  
- Works with **iPhone, iPad, Mac**
- Audio switches instantly based on activity
- **Still not simultaneous**, but feels seamless

- Two devices → audio interface → Bluetooth transmitter → headset
