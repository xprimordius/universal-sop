## 🤖 ChatGPT

I’m not a lawyer and this isn’t legal advice. Consult a licensed attorney for specific guidance.

Generally:

1. **Formal Written Notice**: Send a clear, written demand to each party, documenting all facts, inconsistencies, and requests for termination/refund.

2. **Misrepresentation/Fraud Claims**: If you can show reliance on false statements or omitted key info, you may argue the contract is void/voidable. Gather all evidence (emails, messages, timeline of interactions).

3. **Duress**: Must prove you had no real choice but to sign. Emphasize any pressure or threat that left no practical alternative.

4. **Communication Records**: Save all attempts to contact them (emails, phone logs). Their lack of response may help demonstrate bad faith.

5. **Chargeback/Payment Dispute**: If you used a credit card or other payment method, consider requesting a chargeback by citing non-delivery or misrepresented services.

6. **Demand Letter**: Through an attorney, send a demand letter threatening legal action if they fail to comply. This often prompts a response.

7. **Small Claims/Court**: If unresolved, speak with a lawyer about filing in small-claims (if under the limit) or other civil court. The burden of proof is on you to show misrepresentation or breach.

Seek legal counsel to tailor actions to your jurisdiction’s contract and consumer-protection laws.
