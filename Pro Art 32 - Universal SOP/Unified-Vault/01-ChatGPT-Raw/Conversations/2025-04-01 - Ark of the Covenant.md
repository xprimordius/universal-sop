The Ark of the Covenant is a sacred, gold-covered chest described in the Bible. It housed the stone tablets of the Ten Commandments and represented God’s presence with the Israelites.

**Key details:**
- **Contents:** Ten Commandments (also said to include Aaron’s rod and a jar of manna).
- **Construction:** Acacia wood overlaid with gold; lid called the **Mercy Seat** with two cherubim.
- **Purpose:** Carried during the Exodus, led battles, and was placed in the Holy of Holies in Solomon’s Temple.
- **Symbolism:** God's covenant with Israel, divine presence, and holiness.
- **Disappearance:** Lost or hidden after the Babylonian destruction of the Temple (~586 BCE); never recovered.

**Modern relevance:**  
Still central in religious, archaeological, and mystical traditions—subject of debate, legend, and pop culture (e.g. *Raiders of the Lost Ark*).
