Companies require an **Incumbency Certificate** and an **Amendment Authorizing Electronic Signatures** for legal and compliance reasons:

1. **Incumbency Certificate:**
   - Confirms the identities and official capacities of key officers (e.g., CEO, CFO, Secretary).
   - Ensures that the person signing contracts or agreements on behalf of the company has the legal authority to do so.
   - Prevents disputes about unauthorized signatories in future legal or financial transactions.

2. **Amendment Authorizing Electronic Signatures:**
   - Explicitly grants permission for the company to use electronic signatures for contracts, agreements, and official records.
   - Ensures compliance with **e-signature laws** (e.g., **ESIGN Act** in the U.S. and **eIDAS Regulation** in the EU).
   - Mitigates legal risks by confirming that electronically signed documents hold the same legal weight as physical signatures.

These documents **protect both parties** by ensuring that:
- The signer has verified authority.
- Electronic signatures are legally binding.
- Future disputes over authenticity and validity are minimized.
