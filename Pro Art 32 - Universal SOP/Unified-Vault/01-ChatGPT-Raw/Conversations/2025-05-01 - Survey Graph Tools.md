## 🤖 ChatGPT

Use **ranking questions** or **Likert scale** questions for easy charting. These work well with tools like Google Forms and SurveyMonkey.

### Best formats for graph-ready survey:

#### 1. **Likert Scale (1–5)**
- *"How satisfied were you with the following?"*  
  (1 = Very Dissatisfied, 5 = Very Satisfied)  
  - Booking process  
  - Communication  
  - Trip options  
  - Pricing  
  - Support

**Graph Type:** Bar chart (average or frequency per item)

---

#### 2. **Ranking Question**
- *"Please rank the following areas for improvement (1 = Needs Most Improvement):"*  
  - Booking experience  
  - Trip selection  
  - Customer service  
  - Price/value  
  - Website usability

**Graph Type:** Bar chart or stacked rank chart

---

#### 3. **Multiple Choice (Single or Multiple Select)**
- *"What should we improve next?"*  
  - Faster support  
  - More travel packages  
  - Better pricing  
  - Simpler website/app  
  - Loyalty rewards

**Graph Type:** Pie or bar chart (response count per option)
