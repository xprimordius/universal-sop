Here are your **best strategies to secure a DSCR or STR-qualified property with little to no money down**, ranked by feasibility and leverage:

---

### 1. **Seller Financing with Subordination (0–10% Down)**
- Negotiate directly with the seller.
- Structure: Seller holds 1st or 2nd lien, allows you to bring a small down payment.
- **Stack with DSCR** loan in 1st position.
- Ideal if seller owns free & clear or has equity.

---

### 2. **Cross-Collateralization (0% Down if assets exist)**
- Use equity in **another property** as collateral.
- DSCR lender uses both properties to satisfy LTV/DCSR requirements.
- Zero new cash out-of-pocket if LTV across both is low enough.

---

### 3. **Equity Partners / Silent Investors**
- Bring in a capital partner for 20–25% down.
- You manage property; partner earns equity/cash flow split.
- Use pitch deck + projections (AirDNA, STR income) to sell them.

---

### 4. **Business Credit Lines / Cards**
- Use 0% APR **business credit cards** or lines (e.g., Fund&Grow, Ramp, Navy Fed).
- Liquidate via **Plastiq**, **MSR platforms**, or **cash advance partners**.
- Use funds for down payment, then refinance to repay later.

---

### 5. **Gap Funding Lenders (Hard/Soft Money 2nd Position)**
- Find **private lenders** offering 2nd position loans for down payments.
- Structure: 80% LTV from DSCR lender + 20% gap loan.
- Higher rate, short-term (3–12 months).

---

### 6. **Lease Option or Master Lease with Option to Buy**
- Control the property **now**, earn Airbnb income.
- Use future profits or refinance proceeds to purchase.
- Best for undervalued/neglected homes with motivated sellers.

---

### 7. **Navy Federal Homebuyers Choice Loan (0% Down)**
- **Only works if property will be primary residence** first.
- Strategy: Live there 6–12 months, then convert to STR and refinance into DSCR loan.

---

### 8. **Subject-To or Wrap Mortgage (Creative)**
- Take over seller's existing mortgage "subject-to."
- Use wraparound financing to add equity payments.
- Zero down deals possible with motivated/distressed sellers.

---

- **Most Effective:** Seller financing + DSCR loan
- **Most Scalable:** Investor partner + DSCR loan
- **Most Creative:** Subject-to / master lease options
