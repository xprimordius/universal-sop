## 🤖 ChatGPT

Pain in the **lower back and right hip during and after running** can stem from several common causes:

---

### 🔍 Likely Causes:
1. **Pelvic Misalignment**  
   - Uneven hips or pelvic tilt can strain the lower back and one-sided hip.

2. **Glute Medius Weakness**  
   - This stabilizer muscle (especially on the right side) helps control hip alignment. Weakness = hip drop = compensation pain.

3. **SI Joint Dysfunction**  
   - Sacroiliac joint irritation (near the lower back/top of the butt) can radiate pain into the hip.

4. **Tight Hip Flexors or Piriformis**  
   - Can compress the lower back or cause sciatic-like pain into the hip/glute.

5. **Uneven Running Form or Foot Strike**  
   - Overstriding, pronation, or leg length discrepancy can shift force into one side of your lower body.

---

### ✅ What to Do:
1. **Stop Running Temporarily**
   - Let inflammation settle; continuing can worsen misalignment.

2. **Foam Roll + Stretch**  
   - Focus on **hip flexors, glutes, piriformis, hamstrings, and lower back**.

3. **Strengthen Core + Glutes**  
   - Add clamshells, single-leg bridges, bird dogs, and planks.

4. **Check Footwear**  
   - Worn-out shoes or wrong support can shift biomechanics.

5. **See a PT or Sports Chiropractor**  
   - Especially for pelvic alignment and gait assessment.
