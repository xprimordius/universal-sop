---

### 🏛️ **“The Republic” vs. The Corporation**
- **The Republic:**  
  Refers to the original constitutional government formed by the Founders — “a Republic, if you can keep it” (Benjamin Franklin). Sovereign states united under a limited federal government. Citizens have unalienable rights protected by the Constitution.

- **United States Corporation:**  
  Conspiracy and legal theorists argue that the **Act of 1871** (District of Columbia Organic Act) created a **corporate entity** called “THE UNITED STATES” — in all caps — separate from the original constitutional republic.  
  - Claim: This turned the government into a **corporate trust** under maritime/admiralty law.
  - Legal reality: The Act did **not** corporatize the U.S., but rather established D.C.'s local government.

---

### 🛡️ **Illuminati & the Dollar Bill**
- **Great Seal of the U.S. (Back of Dollar Bill):**
  - **Eye of Providence (Eye in Pyramid):** Represents divine guidance or God watching over humanity.
  - **“Annuit Coeptis”** – “He [God] favors our undertakings.”
  - **“Novus Ordo Seclorum”** – “New order of the ages.”  
    - Conspiracy theorists read this as “New World Order,” tied to **Illuminati** control.  
    - Historically: Refers to a new American era post-independence, not a secret society.

---

### 👑 **Queen Elizabeth’s Trust & Oaths**
- Some sovereign citizen theories claim:
  - The U.S. (corporation) is a **trust** controlled by the British Crown/Vatican.
  - **Police officers** allegedly take an oath to the “Queen's trust” or the “Crown” (interpreted as City of London Corp.), not the people.
  - No verifiable evidence supports this. Law enforcement oaths are sworn to uphold the **Constitution** and **state laws**, not foreign monarchs.

---

### 🇺🇸 **Pledge of Allegiance: “To the Republic…”**
- “I pledge allegiance to the flag... and to the **Republic** for which it stands.”
  - This affirms loyalty to the **constitutional Republic**, not a democracy or corporate entity.
  - Sovereign theorists emphasize this as proof that the people were meant to serve **a republic**, not a corporation.

---

- **The Republic:** Constitutional government = natural rights + limited federal power.
