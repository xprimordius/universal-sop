# Sex Before Marriage Teachings

---

## 👤 You

What does the Book of Mormon and the 10 commandments say about sex before marriage

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

### **Book of Mormon on Sexual Morality**  
The **Book of Mormon** emphasizes chastity and sexual purity. While it does not explicitly use the phrase “sex before marriage,” it strongly condemns sexual immorality. One key passage comes from **Alma 39:3-5**, where Alma warns his son Corianton about the seriousness of sexual sin:

- **Alma 39:5:**  
  _"Know ye not, my son, that these things are an abomination in the sight of the Lord; yea, most abominable above all sins save it be the shedding of innocent blood or denying the Holy Ghost?"_

This verse frames sexual sin (referred to in this case as “going after the harlot Isabel”) as a grave offense, second only to murder and denying the Holy Ghost. The Book of Mormon aligns with the broader scriptural standard of reserving sexual relations for marriage.

Additionally, **Jacob 2:28** states:
- _"For I, the Lord God, delight in the chastity of women."_

This emphasizes that both men and women are called to live chaste lives, with chastity meaning abstaining from sexual activity outside of marriage.

---

### **The Ten Commandments on Sexual Morality**  
The **Ten Commandments** (found in **Exodus 20:14** and **Deuteronomy 5:18**) contain a commandment that prohibits adultery:

- **Exodus 20:14:**  
  _"Thou shalt not commit adultery."_

While "adultery" technically refers to a married person being unfaithful, the spirit of the commandment in both ancient and modern interpretations extends to **any form of sexual immorality**, including premarital sex. 

Throughout the Bible, sexual relations are understood to be reserved for marriage (e.g., **Genesis 2:24** speaks of two becoming one flesh in the context of marriage). Additionally, the New Testament reinforces sexual purity, with verses like **1 Corinthians 6:18-20** calling believers to "flee fornication" (a term covering sexual relations outside of marriage).

---

### **Summary**  
Both the **Book of Mormon** and the **Bible** emphasize that sexual relations are to be reserved for marriage. The Book of Mormon uses terms like "chastity" and warns against grievous sins related to sexual immorality, while the Ten Commandments forbid adultery, which extends to include sexual purity more broadly. Both teachings promote the sanctity of marriage and call believers to live moral lives in accordance with God's commandments.
