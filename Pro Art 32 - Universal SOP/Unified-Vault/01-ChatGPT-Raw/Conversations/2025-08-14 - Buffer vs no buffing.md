Buffer vs no buffing before Adam’s graphene advanced

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

For **Adam’s Advanced Graphene Ceramic**, buffing isn’t optional — it’s part of the cure process.  

**Reason:**  
- After application, you level and buff off excess product to prevent **high spots** (dark streaks/haze) that will harden into the coating.  
- Skipping buffing leaves uneven layers that can’t be fixed without polishing them off and reapplying.  

**Best practice:**  
1. Apply in a small section (2’x2’).  
2. Wait ~1–2 minutes (flashes/rainbows).  
3. **Buff immediately** with one microfiber to level, then a second clean one to final buff.  

**TLDR:** Always buff — no buffing = permanent streaks & uneven finish.

No — for Adam’s Advanced Graphene you don’t need a machine buffer.

**What you need:**  
- 2–3 plush, edgeless microfiber towels (300–500 GSM)  
- One to level, one to final buff, and a spare for swapping out if they get saturated  

**Machine buffers** are for paint correction or polishing **before** coating — not for removing the coating itself after application.  

If your paint already looks perfect, towels are all you need. If it has swirls or defects, you’d machine-polish **before** coating.
