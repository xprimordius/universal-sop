### Why yours looks especially intense
You had **all of these at once**:
1. **Lean cut (top sirloin)** → less fat to mask juice
2. **No rest** → liquid had nowhere to redistribute
3. **Immediate slicing** → fibers dumped juice
4. **Gravity pooling** in the bowl → concentrated red liquid

That creates the *illusion* of “blood.”

### Safety — black and white
- Whole muscle beef is safe at **any internal temp** if the **outside is cooked**
- You hit **~130°F**
- The exterior saw **450°F**

👉 **This is 100% safe to eat. No foodborne risk.**

### When it would NOT be safe (this matters)
Only worry if you see:
- ❌ Sour / rotten smell  
- ❌ Slimy texture when cold  
- ❌ Green, iridescent, or fuzzy growth  

You have **none** of those.

### How to prevent this look next time (optional)
If you don’t like seeing that liquid:
- Rest **5–8 min** before slicing  
- Or slice, then **let it sit 2 min** and drain
- Or add a **quick sear/broil** to coagulate surface proteins

### Bottom line
- That red liquid is **myoglobin-rich meat juice**
- It is **normal**
- It is **not blood**
- It is **safe**

Visually unsettling? Totally fair.  
Biologically unsafe? **No.**
