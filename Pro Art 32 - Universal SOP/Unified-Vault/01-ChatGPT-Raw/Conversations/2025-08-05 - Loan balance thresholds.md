## 👤 You

At what point does banks consider % of balance too high on installment loans

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**TL;DR:**  
Most banks consider an **installment loan balance above ~80–90%** of the original loan amount **too high** — this can **negatively affect your credit score and funding approvals**.

---

### 🔍 Key Thresholds Banks Use:

| % of Original Loan Remaining | Perception | Impact |
|-----------------------------|------------|--------|
| **90–100%** | **Too new / high risk** | Negative – seen as just opened or barely paid down. |
| **70–89%** | **High balance** | Moderate risk – still considered high. |
| **50–69%** | **Acceptable** | Neutral or slightly positive. Shows active repayment. |
| **<50%** | **Strong** | Positive – indicates lower risk and good repayment history. |
| **<10% or Paid Off** | **Best** | Excellent for credit profile, especially with aged history. |

---

### 📌 Bank Underwriting Considerations:
- **New loan with high % used = red flag** (you haven’t proven repayment capacity).
- **High balances = lower DTI cushion** (less room for new credit).
- **Mortgage vs. auto vs. personal loan**: mortgages are treated more favorably than maxed-out personal or auto loans.

---

### 🧠 Strategy for Best Approvals:
1. **Pay down to <70%** if planning to apply for more funding.
2. **Keep aged, paid-off loans on your credit** for positive history.
3. For business credit funding, **one aged auto loan paid down to <50%** is a strong profile booster.

### 🔢 Estimate Months to Pay Down Below Key Thresholds (e.g. 90%, 70%, 50%)

#### **Formula (Simple Approximation):**

> **Months = (Current Balance – Target Balance) ÷ Principal Paid Per Month**

Where:
- **Target Balance = Original Loan Amount × Desired % (e.g. 70%)**
- **Principal Paid Per Month = Monthly Payment – Interest**

---

### 🧠 Example:

Let’s say:

- Original loan = $60,000  
- Current balance = $58,000  
- Monthly payment = $900  
- Interest = ~5% → ~$250/month initially toward interest  
- Principal paid/month ≈ $650  

**To reach 90% (i.e. $54,000 balance):**

- You need to pay down: $58,000 – $54,000 = **$4,000**
- Months ≈ $4,000 ÷ $650 ≈ **6.15 months** → ~**6–7 months**

**To reach 70% (i.e. $42,000 balance):**

- You need to pay down: $58,000 – $42,000 = **$16,000**
- Months ≈ $16,000 ÷ $650 ≈ **24.6 months** → ~**25 months**
