### ⚠️ Tips & Caveats

- Even if the app *claims* 4K or 60fps, the front camera hardware of your iPhone model might not support 4K / 60fps. Always test.
- The “free” version often includes watermarking, limits on script length, or disabled premium features.
- Use “selfie / front-cam mode” (or “selfie mode”) in apps to position the script close to the lens so your eyes stay centered.
- If full 60fps isn’t supported, 4K at 30fps is often more than sufficient for smooth video to the eye.
