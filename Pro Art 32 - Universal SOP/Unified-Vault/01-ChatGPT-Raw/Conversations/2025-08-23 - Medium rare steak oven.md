- **Seasoning:** Generously salt & pepper both sides.  

---

### 2. Sear First
- Preheat oven to **275°F (135°C)**.  
- Heat oven-safe skillet (cast iron best) on high with a little oil until smoking.

---

- Insert meat thermometer in thickest part.  
- Place skillet in oven.  
- Cook until internal temp hits **125°F (52°C)** (pull early, it rises while resting).  

---

### 4. Rest
- Rest steak **5–10 min** loosely covered with foil.  
- Final internal temp rises to **130–135°F (54–57°C)** = perfect medium rare.  

---

### 5. Optional Butter Baste
- Right before pulling from oven, add butter, garlic, and herbs (thyme/rosemary) to skillet.  
- Spoon over steak for flavor.
