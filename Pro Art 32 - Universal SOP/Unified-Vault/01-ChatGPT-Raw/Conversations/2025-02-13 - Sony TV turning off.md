If your **Sony TV keeps randomly turning off**, but the **LEDs plugged into the TV remain on**, it suggests that the TV is not fully losing power but shutting itself down due to an internal issue. Here’s how to troubleshoot:

### **1. Check Power-Saving Settings**
- Go to **Settings** > **Power & Energy** > **Eco Mode** or **Power Saving**.
- Disable any **Auto Power Off** or **Idle TV Standby** features.

### **2. Disable HDMI-CEC (Bravia Sync)**
- Go to **Settings** > **External Inputs** > **Bravia Sync Settings**.
- Turn off **Bravia Sync Control** (some devices may be turning the TV off).

### **3. Check for Overheating**
- If the TV **shuts down and restarts**, it may be overheating.
- Ensure **good ventilation** and clean **dust from vents**.

### **4. Update Firmware**
- Go to **Settings** > **System** > **Software Update**.
- If there’s an update, install it.

### **5. Check for Sleep Timer or Auto-Off**
- Go to **Settings** > **Timers**.
- Ensure **Sleep Timer** is OFF.

### **6. Unplug External Devices**
- Disconnect all **HDMI** and **USB devices** (except power).
- See if the TV still turns off.

### **7. Check Power Supply & Cable**
- Try a **different power outlet**.
- Ensure the **power cable is secure** and not damaged.

### **8. Factory Reset (if all else fails)**
- Go to **Settings** > **Device Preferences** > **Reset**.
- Select **Factory Data Reset**.

If the issue persists after these steps, there might be a **hardware issue** (faulty power board, capacitors, or main board), and **professional repair or warranty service** may be needed. Let me know if you need specific guidance on any of these steps.
