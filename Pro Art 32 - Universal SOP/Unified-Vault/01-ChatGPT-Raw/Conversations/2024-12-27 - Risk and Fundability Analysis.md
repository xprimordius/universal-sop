#### **1. Marketing Consulting (SIC 87420300)**

  - Focused on revenue-generating activities for clients, such as advertising, branding, and strategic promotions.

#### **2. Management Consulting (SIC 87420000)**
- **Perceived Risk**: **Higher**
  - Broad scope includes strategic advisory services, operational optimization, and leadership coaching.
  - Often seen as a discretionary service, making demand more variable.
  - Harder to quantify ROI compared to marketing, as benefits may be indirect or long-term.
- **Cash Flow**: May rely on fewer large contracts, increasing dependency on specific clients.
- **Fundability**: Moderate but higher risk if the firm cannot demonstrate a consistent client pipeline or tangible results.

---

### **Key Reasons Marketing Consulting is Lower Risk**
1. **Tangible Deliverables**:
   - Marketing consulting services often produce visible, immediate outcomes (e.g., increased sales, website traffic), making them more fundable.
   - Management consulting outcomes are harder to quantify and may not directly impact revenue.

2. **Demand Stability**:
   - Marketing remains a priority for businesses during growth and downturns.
   - Management consulting may be deprioritized during economic challenges as businesses cut costs.

3. **Client Diversification**:
   - Marketing consultants tend to work with a wider variety of clients and industries, spreading risk.
   - Management consultants may have fewer but larger clients, leading to dependency risks.

---

**Marketing consulting** is considered **lower risk** and generally more fundable due to its direct impact on client revenue and broader client base.  
**Management consulting** is seen as **higher risk**, especially for smaller firms, because it often lacks measurable outcomes and depends on fewer high-value contracts.
