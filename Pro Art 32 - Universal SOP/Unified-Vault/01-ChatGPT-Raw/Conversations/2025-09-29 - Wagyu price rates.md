## 🥩 Wagyu Grading in Japan
- **Grade “A”** = Yield (how much usable meat from the carcass)  
- **Number (1–5)** = Quality (marbling, color, texture, fat quality)  
   - **A5** = Highest possible grade (max yield + max marbling/quality)  
   - **A2** = Much lower marbling/quality; closer to regular beef  

So when a restaurant says **A2 or A5**, they’re pointing to the *Japanese grading scale*.  

---

## 🇺🇸 American Wagyu
- The U.S. doesn’t use “A2” or “A5.”  
- American Wagyu is usually **crossbred Wagyu × Angus**, graded by USDA (Prime, Choice, Select) or branded by ranches.
