## 🤖 ChatGPT

If your iPhone alarm keeps turning off by itself, here are the most likely causes and fixes:

---

### 🔍 **Top Causes & Fixes**
1. **Sleep Focus or Do Not Disturb Enabled**
   - **Fix:** Go to `Settings` > `Focus` > `Sleep` or `Do Not Disturb`, and turn it off. Alarms may get silenced if "Sleep Schedule" is active.

2. **Alarm Set to Sound "None"**
   - **Fix:** Open the Clock app > tap your alarm > check "Sound" > ensure it’s set to an audible tone.

3. **Volume Too Low or on Silent**
   - **Fix:** Alarms ignore Silent Mode, but double-check that `Settings` > `Sounds & Haptics` > "Ringer and Alerts" volume is high enough.

4. **Third-Party Alarm App Conflict**
   - **Fix:** Uninstall any third-party alarm/timer apps. They may override or suppress the built-in alarm.

5. **Software Bug or Glitch**
   - **Fix:** Restart iPhone. If issue persists, update iOS: `Settings` > `General` > `Software Update`.

6. **Alarm Deleted Automatically (via Shortcuts or Automations)**
   - **Fix:** Check `Shortcuts` app > "Automation" tab to ensure there are no rules turning off alarms.

7. **Bedtime Alarm Only Going Off Once**
   - **Fix:** Bedtime alarms don’t repeat unless scheduled. Use a standard alarm instead of “Sleep/Wake Up” for daily use.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

To have your iPhone alarm **play through the phone speakers instead of AirPods**, follow this:

---

### ✅ **Fix: Disconnect AirPods for Alarm**
Alarms will play through **AirPods if they’re connected**, even if you're not wearing them. To force the alarm through the phone speaker:

#### Option 1: **Disable Bluetooth Temporarily**
- Swipe down → **Control Center** → Tap **Bluetooth icon** to turn off (before bed).

#### Option 2: **Forget or Disconnect AirPods**
- `Settings` → `Bluetooth` → Tap the **(i)** next to your AirPods → Tap **Disconnect**  
  *(Or tap “Forget This Device” if you want a permanent solution.)*

#### Option 3: **Place AirPods in Charging Case**
- Put both AirPods in the case and close it. This usually disconnects them automatically.

---

**Important Note:**  
iPhone alarms **do not play through both AirPods and phone speakers**—only one output at a time. If AirPods are connected, they take priority.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

If you're **wearing AirPods**, your iPhone alarm **will only play through the AirPods** — **not the phone speaker**. There is **no current way to force the alarm through the phone speaker while AirPods are in and connected**.

---

### 🔒 Apple Limitation (as of iOS 17/18):
- iPhone alarms **follow the active audio output**.
- If AirPods are in your ears and connected, the alarm plays **only through them**.
- Even loud alarms like **Bedtime** or **Clock app alarms** do **not override** this behavior.

---

### ✅ Solutions:
1. **Before Bed:** Put AirPods in the case or disconnect them.
2. **Use a Physical Backup Alarm Clock** as fail-safe.
3. **Use Apple Watch Alarm** (if you wear it), which vibrates and rings independently.
4. **Submit Feedback to Apple:** [apple.com/feedback](https://www.apple.com/feedback/)
