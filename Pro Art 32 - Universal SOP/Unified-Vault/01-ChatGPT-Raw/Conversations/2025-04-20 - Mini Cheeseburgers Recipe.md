**Mini Cheeseburgers in Oven – Quick Method**

**Ingredients:**
- Ground beef (1–2 lbs)
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder
- Slider buns or Hawaiian rolls
- Cheese slices (cut into quarters)
- Optional: onions, pickles, ketchup, mustard

**Steps:**
1. **Preheat oven** to 400°F (200°C).
2. **Shape mini patties** (about 2 oz each) and season both sides.
3. **Place on baking sheet** (use foil or parchment).
4. **Bake for 10–12 mins**, flipping at 6 mins.
5. **Add cheese** in last 1–2 mins to melt.
6. **Toast buns** cut-side up on a separate tray during the last 3–5 mins.
7. **Assemble** with desired toppings.

**Optional Tip:** Bake patties on a wire rack over a tray to keep them juicy but not greasy.

3. **Shaving Cream Paint:**
   - Mix shaving cream with food coloring or washable paint.
   - Use brushes or fingers to paint on paper or windows (easy cleanup).

4. **Shaving Cream Ice Cream Shop:**
   - Use scoops, cones (real or toy), and cups to “serve” pretend ice cream.

5. **Clean Writing Practice:**
   - Great for learning letters/numbers—kids write them in the foam, then smooth it out to start over.

**Tips:**

- Choose **fragrance-free** shaving cream for sensitive skin.
