What happens if a dog accidentally ate a garlic steak

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Short answer:** Garlic is toxic to dogs. What happens depends on **how much garlic** and **the dog’s size**.

### What garlic can do
- Damages red blood cells → **anemia**
- Symptoms can appear **6–72 hours later**

### Possible symptoms
- Vomiting, diarrhea, drooling
- Lethargy, weakness
- Pale gums
- Rapid breathing / heart rate
- Dark urine (serious)

### Risk level (rule of thumb)
- **Small dog + more than a few bites** → higher risk  
- **Large dog + tiny amount (a few bites)** → often mild, but still monitor  
- Cooked garlic is **still toxic**

### What to do **now**
1. **Do not induce vomiting** unless a vet tells you to.
2. **Call your vet or ASPCA Animal Poison Control**  
   - ASPCA APCC: **888-426-4435** (fee-based, very reliable)
3. Monitor closely for **3 days**.

### If it was just a small accidental bite
- Many dogs are okay, but **don’t assume safety**
- Still monitor and call if unsure
