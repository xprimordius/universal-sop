## 👤 You

Can you do trading under an LLC so you can deduct business expenses and lower taxable income ?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Yes.** Trading under an LLC can allow you to deduct business-related expenses and potentially reduce taxable income — **but only if the IRS considers your activity a business**, not a hobby or passive investment.

### Requirements to Deduct as a Business:
- **Material Participation:** You're actively involved in trading (not just passive investing).
- **Frequency & Volume:** High volume, regular trades (e.g., multiple trades per week).
- **Intention to Profit:** You trade with a clear profit motive.

### Common Deductions Allowed:
- Trading platform/software fees  
- Internet, phone, and office expenses  
- Education and research materials  
- Home office (if used exclusively for trading)  
- Accounting/legal fees

### Important Notes:
- **LLC alone isn't enough.** IRS classification matters. Consider electing **S Corp or C Corp** status depending on strategy.
- **Consider Trader Tax Status (TTS):** If you qualify, it allows broader deductions and possibly **Section 475(f) mark-to-market accounting**, which avoids wash-sale rules and allows full deduction of losses.
