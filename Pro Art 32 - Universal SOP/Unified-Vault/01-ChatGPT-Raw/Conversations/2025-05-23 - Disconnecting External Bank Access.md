### ✅ Step-by-Step to Ensure No Ongoing Data Sharing:

#### **1. Remove External Accounts (which you already did):**
- ✅ **You did this** – good first step.

#### **2. Revoke Data Sharing Permissions via Account Aggregation/Linked Apps:**

Each bank may keep a connection via **Plaid** or internal **linked apps** settings. You need to **manually revoke access** on both ends:

---

#### 🔹 **Bank of America (BofA):**
- Log in to BofA Online or Mobile App.
- Go to **Security Center** → **Connected Apps & Websites**.
- Find any mention of **Chase**, **Plaid**, or linked external banks.
- **Revoke or Disconnect** access.

📍 If not visible: Call BofA support (800-432-1000) and **ask them to confirm and revoke any linked third-party data connections or account aggregators.**

---

#### 🔹 **Chase:**
- Log in to Chase Mobile or Web.
- Go to **Profile & Settings** → **Account Settings** → **Linked Accounts** or **Security & Privacy** → **Manage Linked Apps**.
- Remove BofA or any aggregators like **Plaid**, **Yodlee**, etc.
- Alternatively: go to chase.com/secure/third-party-access (if applicable).

📍 You can also call Chase support (800-935-9935) to confirm **no lingering access tokens** are active for BofA.

---

#### **3. Revoke Access via Plaid (If Used):**
- Visit: [https://my.plaid.com](https://my.plaid.com)
- Log in with your email used during linking.
- You'll see all connected apps/banks.
- **Revoke access to Chase/BofA** as needed.

---

### 🔐 Final Check:
- After doing all of the above, neither bank will have ongoing access to the other’s balances or activity unless you relink manually.
- Always avoid giving permission to view balances when setting up transfers — most allow sending/receiving without enabling visibility.
