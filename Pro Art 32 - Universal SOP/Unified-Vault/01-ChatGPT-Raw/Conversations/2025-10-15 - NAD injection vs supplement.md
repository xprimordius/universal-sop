### **NAD Injection vs. Supplement (Precision Breakdown)**

#### **1. Bioavailability (Absorption Efficiency)**
- **Injection (IV or IM):**  
  **90–100% absorption.** Goes directly into bloodstream, bypassing digestion. Immediate cellular uptake and mitochondrial activation.  
- **Oral/Nasal Supplement:**  
  **5–20% absorption.** Most NAD⁺, NMN, or NR forms degrade in stomach/liver before reaching cells.

#### **2. Onset & Duration**
- **Injection:**  
  Rapid effect (within minutes to hours). Elevated energy, mental clarity, and mood can last **3–14 days** depending on dose.  
- **Supplement:**  
  Gradual buildup; effects accumulate over **1–2 weeks** with daily use.

#### **3. Use Cases**
| Goal | Best Option | Reason |
|------|--------------|--------|
| Rapid anti-aging / recovery | **Injection** | Immediate mitochondrial saturation |
| Daily maintenance | **Supplement** | Sustainable long-term elevation |
| Neuroregeneration / addiction recovery | **Injection** | Clinical efficacy proven |
| Energy, focus, sleep, mood | **Supplement** | Easier, lower cost, steady support |

#### **4. Cost & Convenience**
- **Injection:** $150–$600 per session; requires clinic or medical-grade supplies.  
- **Supplement:** $40–$150/month; easy, self-managed.

#### **5. Safety & Tolerance**
- **Injection:** May cause flushing, nausea, or chest tightness if infused too quickly.  
- **Supplement:** Generally safe; mild GI discomfort possible.

#### **6. Optimal Strategy**
**Best of both worlds:**  
→ Use **NAD+ injections weekly or biweekly** for mitochondrial saturation.  
→ Maintain with **daily NMN or NR supplements** (500–1000 mg/day) + **cofactors** (B3, B6, magnesium, resveratrol, CoQ10).
