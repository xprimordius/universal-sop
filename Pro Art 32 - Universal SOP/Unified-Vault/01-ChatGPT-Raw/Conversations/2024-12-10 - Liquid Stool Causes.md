### **5. Check for Underlying Issues**
Low testosterone at **300 ng/dL** despite a strong diet suggests possible underlying issues:
1. **Excess Body Fat**:

2. **Hormonal Imbalances**:
   - High estrogen or SHBG can limit free testosterone.
   - Check hormone levels: total testosterone, free testosterone, SHBG, estradiol, and cortisol.

   - Disrupts REM sleep, lowering testosterone.
   - If snoring or fatigue are present, consider a sleep study.

4. **Micronutrient Deficiencies**:
   - Test levels of **zinc**, **magnesium**, and **vitamin D** to ensure sufficiency.

5. **Thyroid Function**:
   - Hypothyroidism can suppress testosterone.
   - Test TSH, free T3, and free T4.
