## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here's a simple guide to make creamy mashed potatoes:

### Ingredients:
- 2 pounds of potatoes (Yukon Gold or Russet work best)
- 4 tablespoons butter (unsalted)
- 1/2 cup milk or heavy cream (adjust to taste)
- Salt (to taste)
- Pepper (optional, to taste)

---

### Instructions:

1. **Peel and Chop Potatoes**:
   - Peel the potatoes and cut them into even-sized chunks (about 2 inches). This ensures they cook evenly.

2. **Boil Potatoes**:
   - Place the potatoes in a large pot and cover with cold water. Add a generous pinch of salt.
   - Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Cook for 15-20 minutes or until a fork easily pierces through.

3. **Drain and Dry**:
   - Drain the potatoes in a colander. Return them to the pot and let them sit on low heat for 1-2 minutes to evaporate excess water. This helps achieve a fluffier texture.

4. **Mash the Potatoes**:
   - Use a potato masher, ricer, or electric hand mixer to mash the potatoes to your desired consistency.

5. **Add Butter and Milk**:
   - Add the butter first and mix until melted.
   - Slowly add the milk or cream while mashing, adjusting the amount until the potatoes are creamy.

6. **Season**:
   - Add salt and pepper to taste. Mix well.

7. **Optional Add-Ins**:
   - For extra flavor, mix in garlic powder, roasted garlic, chives, sour cream, or shredded cheese.

---

### Serve:
Serve immediately while hot, topped with additional butter or garnished with parsley for a nice finish. Enjoy!
