   - Attach any supporting documents such as proof of travel, unauthorized transaction reports, and any communication with FNB Omaha.

   - Send a certified letter with return receipt requested to FNB Omaha to formally dispute the inaccuracies. This ensures you have proof that they received your dispute.

5. **File a Complaint with the CFPB:**
   - File a complaint with the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB). This adds pressure on the creditor and the credit bureaus to resolve your dispute quickly.

   Use this link to file a complaint: [CFPB Complaint](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/complaint/)
