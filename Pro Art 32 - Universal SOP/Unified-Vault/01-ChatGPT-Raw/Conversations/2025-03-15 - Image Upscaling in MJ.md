## 🤖 ChatGPT

To upload an image and have **MidJourney** expand it into a higher-resolution, larger composition, follow these steps:

### **1. Upload Your Image to MidJourney**
1. **Open Discord** and go to a MidJourney server or your **own DM with the MidJourney bot**.
2. **Upload your image** by dragging it into the chat or clicking the `+` button and selecting **Upload a File**.
3. Once uploaded, **right-click the image**, select **Copy Link**, and keep it for the next step.

---

### **2. Use the /imagine Command with Your Image URL**
1. In the MidJourney chat, type `/imagine`.
2. **Paste the image URL** you copied earlier **before** adding any prompt text.
   - Example:
     ```
     /imagine [PASTE IMAGE URL] Expand to a wide scenic view, ultra-detailed, 8K resolution
     ```
3. **Modify the prompt** based on your needs (e.g., “wider frame,” “higher resolution,” “surrounding details”).

---

### **3. Upscale and Expand**
1. **Select the best image** from the generated options.
2. **Click U1, U2, U3, or U4** (Upscale buttons) to enhance resolution.
3. **Use the “Vary (Region)” or “Zoom Out” Feature**:
   - **Zoom Out 1.5x or 2x**: Expands the canvas while keeping the original content intact.
   - **Custom Zoom**: Lets you add more details to the extended area.
   - **Vary (Region)**: Allows selective adjustments to expanded parts.

---

### **4. Download the Final Image**
1. Once upscaled, **right-click** the image and select **Save Image As** to download the high-res version.
