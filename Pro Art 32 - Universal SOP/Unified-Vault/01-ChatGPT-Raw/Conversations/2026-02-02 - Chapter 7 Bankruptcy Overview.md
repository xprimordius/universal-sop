This packet **does not flag it** — but here’s how it’s typically viewed:

- A **one-time $950 transfer** to a **son** is **not automatically a problem**
- Trustee cares about:
  - Pattern (repeated transfers?)
  - Intent (hiding assets vs normal family support)
  - Timing (right before filing vs ordinary course)

**Single, modest family support payments are commonly acceptable**, especially if:
- It’s for living expenses
- It’s not repayment of an old debt
- It’s not repeated or structured to avoid creditors

If asked, the answer is usually simple and factual.
