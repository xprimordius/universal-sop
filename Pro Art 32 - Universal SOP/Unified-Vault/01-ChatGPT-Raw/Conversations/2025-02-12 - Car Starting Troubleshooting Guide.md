## 👤 You

Trying to start my car with a tack life battery, and it has worked before, but this time it is turning on the engine but instantly dies.

How do I get it to run long enough to keep the car on

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

If your car is starting but immediately dying when using the **Tacklife jump starter**, the issue might not be with the jump starter itself but rather with your car’s **battery, alternator, or fuel system**. Here’s how to troubleshoot and get it running:

### **Step 1: Confirm the Jump Starter is Fully Charged**
- Ensure your **Tacklife jump starter** is fully charged (at least **80%+**).
- Try a **second jump attempt** if the first one didn’t work.

### **Step 2: Keep the Jump Starter Connected Longer**
- After the car starts, **keep the jump starter connected for at least 30-60 seconds** before disconnecting. This may give the alternator time to stabilize voltage.
- If the engine dies **immediately** after disconnecting, your car battery may not be holding a charge.

### **Step 3: Check for Alternator Issues**
- If the **battery warning light** stays on after starting, the alternator may not be charging the battery.
- To confirm, use a multimeter:
  - Battery voltage **before starting**: ~12.4V–12.6V.
  - Battery voltage **while running**: ~13.8V–14.4V.
  - If voltage **does not increase** while running, the alternator may be faulty.

### **Step 4: Use a Manual Rev Boost**
- Try **pressing the gas pedal slightly (2000-3000 RPM) for 1-2 minutes** after starting.
- This can help the alternator recharge the battery and stabilize the system.

### **Step 5: Test the Fuel System (If Alternator is Fine)**
- If the car dies immediately and it's **not a battery/alternator issue**, it could be:
  - **Low fuel pressure** (faulty fuel pump or clogged fuel filter).
  - **Bad sensors** (MAF, crankshaft sensor, etc.).

To test:
1. **Turn the key to ON (without starting)** for a few seconds and listen for a **fuel pump priming sound**.
2. If no sound or the car struggles to stay running, fuel delivery could be the issue.

### **Final Steps**
- If the car **stays running after revving** but dies at idle, check for a **bad idle air control valve**.
- If the car **only runs with the jump starter**, replace the battery ASAP.
- If the **battery and alternator check out**, but the car still won’t stay running, it may need further diagnosis.
