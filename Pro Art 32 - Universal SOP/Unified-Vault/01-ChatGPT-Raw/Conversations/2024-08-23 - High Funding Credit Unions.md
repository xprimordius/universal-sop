# High Funding Credit Unions

---

## 👤 You

What are the highest funding credit unions that give the most money for people like me who are not military

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

When seeking credit unions that offer high funding and aren't restricted to military members, the following options are some of the most recognized for their generous lending practices and broad membership eligibility:

1. **Navy Federal Credit Union (NFCU)**
   - **Note:** Though traditionally for military members, NFCU offers some of the highest lending limits and has recently broadened its eligibility to include family members and household members of military personnel. It's worth checking eligibility if you have any connections.
   
2. **Alliant Credit Union**
   - **Membership:** Open to anyone who joins Foster Care to Success, a nonprofit organization, making it accessible to non-military members.
   - **Funding:** Known for competitive loan limits, personal loans, and credit cards with higher limits.

3. **PenFed Credit Union**
   - **Membership:** Initially military-focused but now open to the general public. They offer a wide range of high-limit loan products and credit cards.
   - **Funding:** PenFed is known for offering competitive rates and high limits on personal loans and credit cards.

4. **First Tech Federal Credit Union**
   - **Membership:** Open to anyone who becomes a member of the Computer History Museum or Financial Fitness Association, making it widely accessible.
   - **Funding:** Offers high-limit personal loans and credit cards with favorable terms, especially for tech professionals and those in related industries.

5. **DCU (Digital Federal Credit Union)**
   - **Membership:** Open to anyone through membership in certain organizations, making it widely accessible to non-military members.
   - **Funding:** Known for offering competitive auto loans, personal loans, and credit cards with substantial limits.

6. **NASA Federal Credit Union**
   - **Membership:** Open to anyone who joins one of their partner organizations, allowing broad access.
   - **Funding:** Offers generous credit limits on credit cards and personal loans, particularly suited for those in scientific or engineering fields.

7. **Connexus Credit Union**
   - **Membership:** Open to the public through the Connexus Association.
   - **Funding:** Known for offering substantial personal loan amounts and competitive credit card limits.

These credit unions are recognized for their strong funding capabilities and inclusive membership policies. Always ensure you review the specific eligibility requirements and consider joining any associated organizations if needed to become a member. Additionally, comparing their loan products, interest rates, and terms will help you find the best fit for your needs.
