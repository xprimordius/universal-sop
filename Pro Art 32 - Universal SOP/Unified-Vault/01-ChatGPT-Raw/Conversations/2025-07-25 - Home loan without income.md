## 👤 You

is it possible to get approved for a home loan without income

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Yes, but rare.** Here's how it’s sometimes possible to get a home loan without traditional income:

---

### 🔑 **Main Approval Scenarios Without Income:**

1. **Asset-Based Lending (Asset Depletion Loans):**
   - Uses your liquid assets (stocks, savings, crypto, retirement) as a substitute for income.
   - Lenders divide total assets by a set number of months (e.g., 360) to calculate a “monthly income.”
   - Typically requires **$500K+ in assets** for significant approval.

2. **Non-QM (Non-Qualified Mortgage) Loans:**
   - Don’t follow standard income/DTI rules.
   - Often used by high-net-worth or self-employed borrowers.
   - May accept:
     - **Bank statements**
     - **Rental income**
     - **401(k)/IRA distributions**
     - **Stated income + strong credit + assets**

3. **DSCR Loans (For Investors):**
   - Based on **property’s rental income**, not your personal income.
   - Approval if the **Debt-Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) ≥ 1.0** (i.e., rent covers the mortgage).

4. **Co-Signer or Guarantor:**
   - A co-borrower with verifiable income and good credit can help you qualify.

5. **Private/Hard Money Lenders:**
   - Asset-based. Don't care about your income or credit.
   - High down payment (30–50%) and high interest (8–12%+).
   - Short-term (6–36 months), often for flips or bridge financing.

---

### ⚠️ Key Considerations:
- **High Credit Score** (700+ preferred)
- **Substantial Down Payment** (20–50%)
- **Liquid Reserves or Assets**
- **Low DTI or alternative cash flow proof**

---

### ✅ Summary:
You *can* get approved for a home loan with no income, but you’ll need **assets, equity, or rental income** as an alternative. Best paths: **asset depletion loans, DSCR loans, or non-QM lenders.**

#### 3. **Creative Financing**
- **Seller Financing**: Seller becomes the lender; no bank needed.
- **Subject-To Financing**: Take over a seller’s existing mortgage.
- **Lease Option**: Rent with the right to buy later using rent credits.

#### 4. **FHA Loan with Gifted Down Payment + Roommates**
- Use **FHA 3.5% down loan**.
- Down payment can be **gifted**.
- Use projected **roommate income** to qualify (some lenders allow it).

#### 5. **Private Money or Hard Money**
- High-cost short-term loans (e.g. 10–12%) based on the property.
- Useful if you plan to refinance or sell quickly.

#### 6. **House Hack Through a Partner**
- You bring the deal, manage the property, and live for free.
- Partner brings credit/income/down payment.
- Split equity or cash flow.
