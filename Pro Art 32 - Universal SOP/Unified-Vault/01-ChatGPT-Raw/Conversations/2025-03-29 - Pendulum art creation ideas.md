How does it work

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Pendulum art** works by using the natural motion of a swinging pendulum to distribute paint in smooth, symmetrical patterns.

### How it Works — TLDR:
1. **Setup:** A container with a small hole is suspended from a fixed point so it can swing freely.
2. **Fill with Paint:** You pour paint into the container.
3. **Release Swing:** Once released, the pendulum swings in circular or elliptical paths.
4. **Gravity + Inertia:** Paint drips through the hole as the pendulum moves, creating flowing geometric patterns (e.g. spirals, loops).
5. **Pattern Control:** The motion decays over time, forming tighter curves—beautiful, physics-based designs.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Carryover heat** is the **residual heat** that continues to cook food **after it's removed from the heat source**.

### TLDR:
- When you remove a steak at 130°F, it may rise **5–10°F** while resting.
- This is why you **pull it early** to hit the perfect doneness.
- Essential for precise cooking (especially steaks, roasts, chicken).
