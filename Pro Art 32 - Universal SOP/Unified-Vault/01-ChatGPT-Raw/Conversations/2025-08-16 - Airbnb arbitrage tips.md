# Airbnb Arbitrage: Find Hidden Wins With Tiny Upfronts

**1) Hunt the Right Markets (fast filters)**
- **Legal first:** Cities/HOAs that **allow STR or 30–90 day MTR** with permits possible.  
- **Math guardrails:** **Occupancy ≥ 60%**, **ADR ≥ 2.5× rent/30**, **RevPAR > $90–$120** for 1BR.  
- **Spillover zones:** Suburbs next to banned/limited cores; near **new hospitals, airports, convention centers, film projects, universities**.  
- **Seasonality edge:** Resorts with strong shoulder seasons; college towns Aug–May; snowbird Oct–Apr.

**2) Data Sniping (cheap or free)**
- **Scrape demand:** Airbnb map → filter “entire place”, note **calendar gaps**, poor photos, low reviews = **underperformers**.  
- **Cross-verify:** InsideAirbnb (free), Google Events calendar, city permit lists, tourism stats.  
- **Rent deals:** Apartments.com/Zillow → **“concessions”**, **“1–2 months free”**, high DOM.  
- **Corporate demand:** **Furnished Finder**, hospital HR pages, travel-nurse FB groups, insurance housing vendors (ALE/Sedgwick), construction bids.

**3) Low/No-CapEx Deal Structures**
- **Co-host/Rev-Share (0 capex):** You fix listing + ops; take **20–35%** of gross.  
- **Master Lease + Owner-Funded Furnish:** Offer **base rent + 10–20% upside**; owner buys furniture (they keep asset/depreciation).  
- **Furniture-as-a-Service:** Cort/Fernish/Feather rentals: **$150–$300/mo per unit** vs. $4–6k cash.  
- **New-Build Concessions:** Use **free months** to fund setup; negotiate **reduced deposit**.  
- **Lease Takeovers:** Acquire **exiting hosts’** furnished units at discount; assume lease with addendum.  
- **MTR Pivot (30–90 days):** If nightly STR is tight, run **corporate/medical stays** to stay compliant and steady.

**4) Hidden-Opportunity Signals (alpha)**
- **Permit slack:** Cities with **unused STR permits** or waitlists clearing.  
- **Calendar anomalies:** High-demand weekends still open on many comps → **pricing incompetence** you can beat.  
- **Event pipelines:** Announced **conventions, tournaments, festivals** (dated); pre-position inventory.  
- **Hospital expansions/temporary closures:** Spikes in **travel staff & patient families**.  
- **Airline/airport projects:** Long crew rotations = **reliable MTR**.

**5) Landlord Pipeline (minimal friction)**
- Targets: **small portfolio owners, tired hosts, new-build leasing offices, Realtors with stale listings**.  
- Offer menu: **(A)** Management-only **(0 risk to them)**, **(B)** Master lease with **corporate housing** use, **(C)** Hybrid **base + %**.  
- Trade-ups for owners: **Guaranteed rent, light wear, hotel-grade cleaning, added insured**.

**6) Compliance & Protection (non-negotiable)**
- **Written permission** to sublet/STR/MTR; city **permit/tax registration**; HOA rules.  
- **Commercial STR insurance** + liability; smart safety gear (detector/decibel cam).  
- **Clauses:** regulation-change **escape**, inspection rights, **quiet-hours** addendum.

**7) Unit Economics (quick math)**
- Aim: **Rent ÷ Gross ≤ 0.35**; **Breakeven occupancy ≤ 45–50%** at conservative ADR.  
- Cost stack thumb-rule: **Platform 3%, cleaning pass-through, utilities/Wi-Fi 6–8%, supplies 2–3%, insurance 1–2%, pricing tool 1%**.  
- **Target net:** **$600–$1,200+/mo** per 1BR in MTR; **$1,200–$2,500+** in strong STR cores.

**8) Launch Fast, Spend Light**
- **Essentials-only** kit + furniture rentals; FB Marketplace for accents; pro-looking photos (phone + tripod).  
- **Automate:** Smart lock, cleaner app, inventory bin system, dynamic pricing (PriceLabs/Wheelhouse), message templates.  
- **Multi-channel:** Airbnb + **Furnished Finder + Zillow Corporate + direct inquiry form**.

**9) Pricing Tactics**
- **Dynamic min-stay calendar:** 2–3 nights midweek, 1 night to **fill orphans**; 7–14 nights for events.  
- **Last-minute discounts** inside **3–7 days**; **length-of-stay discounts** for 14/28+ nights.  
- **Event rate fences:** Load event dates **60–120 days** early; do not underprice.

**10) Outreach Script (paste-and-send)**
- “*I’ll guarantee on-time rent, add you as **additional insured**, and keep hotel-grade cleanliness. You can choose: **management-only** (no risk) or **base rent + % of upside**. I host **travel professionals and executives** with verified IDs, no parties, quiet-hours tech, and a **reg-change escape clause** for your protection. 15-minute walkthrough this week?*”

**11) 10-Step Playbook (checklist)**
1) Shortlist 3 markets (legal + metrics).  
2) Pull comps; compute **RevPAR/rent**; pick 1–2 neighborhoods.  
3) Build owner list (stale rentals, concessions, tired hosts).  
4) Pitch **management-only first**; upsell to hybrid/master.  
5) Lock **written permissions** + insurance.  
6) Furnish via **rental + marketplace**.  
7) List on **Airbnb + MTR channels** day 1.  
8) Automate ops; set pricing rules.  
9) Fill calendar with **mid-term anchors** (nurses/insurance).  
10) Review KPIs at **30/60/90 days**; double down or exit via clause.
