How to change Apple Watch height, and from kjouls to cal

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here’s how to do both quickly and precisely:  

---

### 🕹 **Change Your Height in Apple Watch**
1. **On iPhone:**  
   - Open **Watch app** → tap **My Watch** → **Health** → **Health Details** → **Edit**.  
   - Change your **Height**, **Weight**, or other stats → tap **Done**.  
   *(This updates metrics for calories, distance, VO₂ max, etc.)*  

2. **Directly on Watch:**  
   - Open **Settings** → **Health** → **Health Details** → edit **Height**.  

---

### 🔢 **Switch from Kilojoules (kJ) to Calories (kcal)**
1. **On iPhone:**  
   - Open **Watch app** → **Workout**.  
   - Scroll down to **Units of Measure**.  
   - Tap **Energy Units** → choose **Calories** instead of **Kilojoules**.  

   *(You can also open the **Fitness app → Profile → Units of Measure** if the option isn’t visible in Watch app.)*
