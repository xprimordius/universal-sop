**TLDR:**  
No, **nicotine does not cure Alzheimer's** or any major disease. It may show **neuroprotective effects** in some studies, but it's **not a cure**, and it carries **risks**.

---

### ⚠️ Important Truths:
- **Nicotine ≠ Tobacco**: While nicotine (especially in isolation) may have different effects than smoking, it's still a **stimulant** and **addictive**.
- **Not FDA-approved** for Alzheimer’s, Parkinson’s, or any disease treatment or prevention.

---

### 🧠 Alzheimer’s + Nicotine: What Studies Suggest
- Some research indicates **nicotine may enhance cognitive function**, especially **working memory and attention**, in early stages or models of Alzheimer’s.
- May **modulate acetylcholine receptors** (which degrade in Alzheimer’s).
- Some animal and small human studies show **potential slowing of decline**, but **no reversal or cure**.

> 🧪 Most of this is **experimental, low-sample, or animal-based.**

---

### ☕ Nicotine Tea?
- There is **no widely validated, safe nicotine tea** product.
- If you're extracting nicotine from plants like tobacco or wild relatives: **dangerous**, **unregulated**, **risk of overdose**.
- **Nicotine is toxic in high doses.** Even a few drops of liquid nicotine can be fatal to a child.

---

### ✅ What Nicotine Might Help With (in controlled doses):
- Parkinson’s symptom modulation (still under study).
- ADHD focus/alertness (but other meds are better).
- Appetite suppression, metabolism boost (short-term, but dangerous tradeoffs).

---

### ❌ Risks:
- **Highly addictive**.
- Increases **heart rate**, **blood pressure**, and stroke/heart disease risk.
- Can cause **anxiety, insomnia**, dependency.
- Long-term cognitive benefits are **unproven** and potentially outweighed by **addiction and vascular damage**.

---

### Verdict:
Nicotine is **not a cure**, and using it as tea or supplement is **unproven and risky**. Stick with proven prevention: **exercise, sleep, omega-3s, glucose regulation, mental activity.**

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes — **some studies** have suggested **nicotine may have therapeutic potential** in **Alzheimer’s, Parkinson’s, schizophrenia, ADHD**, and other **neurological disorders**. However:

---

### ⚠️ Critical Context:
- **"Published" ≠ Proven Cure**: Peer-reviewed studies showing *benefit* ≠ clinical evidence of a **cure**.
- Most research is **preclinical (animal models)** or **small-scale human trials** — **not definitive or FDA-approved.**

---

### 🔬 Key Findings (What’s Actually Published):
1. **Alzheimer’s Disease**  
   - **Nicotinic acetylcholine receptor loss** is linked to cognitive decline.  
   - Nicotine **stimulates these receptors**, temporarily improving attention and working memory.  
   - A few studies show **slower cognitive decline**, but **no reversal or cure**.  
   - [Newhouse et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22298119/): Nicotine patch improved attention in patients with mild cognitive impairment.

2. **Parkinson’s Disease**  
   - Smokers have lower rates — possibly due to nicotine’s **dopaminergic protection**.  
   - [Quik et al., 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17395135/): Nicotine shown to protect dopamine neurons in animal models.

3. **Schizophrenia & ADHD**  
   - Nicotine modulates attention, impulse control.  
   - Many schizophrenics self-medicate with nicotine — but not a safe or official treatment.

---

### 🧪 Mechanisms Proposed:
- **Neuroprotection** via anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects.
- **Stimulation of α4β2 and α7 nicotinic acetylcholine receptors**.
- **Enhanced neurotransmitter release** (dopamine, norepinephrine, serotonin).

---

### 🧠 So Why Not Approved?
- **Risks > Benefits** (addiction, cardiovascular harm).
- Safer cholinergic drugs exist (e.g. donepezil).
- Long-term studies are **lacking or inconclusive**.
- **Dose control matters** — too much nicotine is **toxic**, especially outside pharmaceutical supervision.

---

### 🚫 "Nicotine Tea" Problem:
- No **standardized dosing** or **safety profile**.
- DIY extractions = risk of **accidental overdose**, nausea, heart issues.
- **Not backed by published trials** as a safe delivery method.

---

### ✅ Summary:
- **Yes**, nicotine has *published data* supporting **neurological benefits**.
- **No**, it is **not a recognized cure**, and benefits remain **context-specific**, **temporary**, and **outweighed by risks** in general use.

**Nicotine Risks (especially outside medical context):**

---

### 🔥 **1. Addiction (Most Critical)**
- **Highly addictive** — activates dopamine → creates dependency.
- Withdrawal = irritability, anxiety, cravings, depression.

---

### ❤️ **2. Cardiovascular Stress**
- Raises **blood pressure**, **heart rate**, and **vasoconstriction**.
- Increases risk of **stroke, heart attack**, and **arrhythmias**, especially in those predisposed.

---

### 🧠 **3. Neurological Risks**
- Can **worsen anxiety**, trigger **insomnia**, and affect **mood stability** long term.
- May impair **adolescent brain development** and **neuroplasticity**.

---

### 🧬 **4. Cellular + Genetic Damage**
- Promotes **oxidative stress** and **DNA damage** in high or chronic doses.
- May **stimulate tumor growth** in certain contexts, even without tobacco.

---

### 👶 **5. Developmental Toxicity**
- Dangerous for **pregnant women** → birth defects, low birth weight, learning problems in children.

---

### 🤢 **6. Acute Toxicity**
- Especially with **nicotine tea**, e-liquids, or patches:
  - Symptoms: **nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, headache, rapid heartbeat, seizures**.
  - Lethal dose (oral): ~30–60mg for adults; **far less for children**.
  - **E.g. 1 cigarette = ~10–15mg nicotine**.

---

### ⚠️ Summary
Even isolated nicotine (without tobacco):
- **Not safe for casual use or DIY delivery.**
- Use must be **clinically dosed, timed, and monitored** to avoid harm.
