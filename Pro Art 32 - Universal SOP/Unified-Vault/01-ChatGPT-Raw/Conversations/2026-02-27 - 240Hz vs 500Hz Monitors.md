## 👤 You

Is it true that 500hz monitor is faster than 240hz at 240fps because the monitor has less latency / faster response time or something? I heard a reviewer saying it just feels sharper and surreal

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Short answer: **Yes — but only if you’re actually outputting more than 240 FPS.**  
At exactly **240 FPS locked**, a 500 Hz panel does *not* magically reduce latency below what 240 Hz already gives you in a meaningful way.

Let’s break it down clearly.

---

## 1️⃣ Refresh Rate vs FPS (Hard Numbers)

- **240 Hz** = screen refreshes every **4.17 ms**
- **500 Hz** = screen refreshes every **2.0 ms**

That means:
- 500 Hz can display a new frame **~2 ms sooner** than 240 Hz.

That is real. That is measurable.

But here’s the key:

If your GPU is outputting **exactly 240 FPS**,  
then you only have 240 unique frames per second to show.

A 500 Hz monitor cannot invent extra frames.

---

## 2️⃣ Does 500 Hz Reduce Input Lag at 240 FPS?

Slightly — but marginally.

Why?

Because refresh cycles are shorter (2 ms vs 4.17 ms), so the *worst-case wait time* before a frame scanout is smaller.

But:
- The frame still only updates every 4.17 ms at 240 FPS.
- Your total system latency (engine + GPU + queue + display) is usually **20–35 ms**.

So we’re talking about shaving **~1–2 ms at best**.

That’s measurable.
But not night-and-day.

---

## 3️⃣ Why Reviewers Say It Feels “Sharper” or “Surreal”

This is where things change.

When you run:

- **400–500 FPS on a 500 Hz monitor**

You get:
- Lower motion blur
- Less sample-and-hold persistence
- Cleaner motion clarity
- Lower MPRT
- Less perceived smear in fast flicks

It feels:
- More connected
- More immediate
- Visually tighter
- Like the world “sticks” to your mouse

Especially in:
- Valorant
- CS2
- Apex tracking
- Flick-heavy shooters

It’s not placebo. Motion clarity genuinely improves.

But this requires FPS matching refresh rate.

---

## 4️⃣ Important: Response Time ≠ Refresh Rate

Many 500 Hz monitors also:
- Use faster panels
- Have better overdrive tuning
- Lower pixel transition time

So sometimes the “sharper” feel is panel quality, not just Hz.

---

## 5️⃣ The Real Answer

If you are:

- Locked at 240 FPS → upgrade won’t transform you.
- Pushing 400–500 FPS → you will feel it.

The difference from:
- 144 → 240 Hz = huge
- 240 → 360 Hz = noticeable
- 360 → 500 Hz = diminishing returns

It’s performance-elite territory.

---

## Final Verdict

At 240 FPS:
> A 500 Hz monitor is technically faster, but not meaningfully game-changing.

At 400–500 FPS:
> Yes — it absolutely feels sharper and more responsive.
