### 1. What the law says (California / LA County)
- California Health & Safety Code §114259.5: **live animals are not allowed in food facilities** (that includes grocery stores) **except** for:
  - **Service animals** trained to do specific tasks for a person with a disability  
  - Police dogs, etc. citeturn1search1turn1search7  
- Public health departments (like Pasadena, OC, Ventura) all repeat the same thing:  
  - Pets and **emotional-support/comfort dogs are *not* considered service animals** and **are not allowed inside** grocery stores. citeturn1search2turn1search3turn1search6  

So Pavilions, Ralphs, Trader Joe’s, Vallarta, etc. around **Burbank** are **not allowed** to let pet dogs inside, even if some people ignore the rule.

### 2. When a dog *is* allowed inside
You can bring your dog inside a grocery store if:

- It is a **true service dog** (trained to do specific tasks for your disability, including psychiatric tasks like interrupting panic attacks).  
- Staff are only allowed to ask:
  1. “Is this a service animal required because of a disability?”  
  2. “What work or task has the dog been trained to perform?” citeturn1search0turn1search2  

**Emotional-support/therapy/”for anxiety comfort” alone doesn’t qualify** unless the dog is trained for specific tasks.

- **Curbside / pickup:** Use pickup at places like Ralphs, Pavilions, Target, Walmart, etc. and keep your dog with you in the car.
