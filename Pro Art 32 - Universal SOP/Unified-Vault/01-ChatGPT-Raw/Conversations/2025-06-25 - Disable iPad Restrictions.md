## 🤖 ChatGPT

**To turn off restrictions (Screen Time / parental controls) on a child’s iPad:**

---

### 🔐 **If You Know the Screen Time Passcode**  
1. Go to **Settings**  
2. Tap **Screen Time**  
3. Tap **Turn Off Screen Time**  
4. Enter the **Screen Time passcode**  
5. Confirm to disable

This removes all app limits, downtime, content restrictions, etc.

---

### 🚫 **If You Don’t Know the Passcode**  
You have two options:

#### 1. **Using Family Sharing (if device is in your family group):**
- On your iPhone:  
  - Go to **Settings** > **Screen Time**  
  - Tap the child’s name  
  - Tap **Turn Off Screen Time Passcode**  
  - Enter your **Apple ID password** to confirm

#### 2. **Erase and Reset the iPad (if no passcode access and no Family Sharing):**
⚠️ This wipes the device completely.
- **Back up first** (iCloud or computer)
- Go to: **Settings** > **General** > **Transfer or Reset iPad** > **Erase All Content and Settings**
- After reset, **do not restore from backup** that has Screen Time settings.
