## 👤 You

Shockz or beats for running

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Shockz (formerly AfterShokz)** are better for **running** — especially outdoors.

**Why:**
- **Open-ear design** (bone conduction): lets you hear traffic and surroundings = **safer**
- **Lightweight + Secure fit**: doesn’t fall off mid-run
- **Sweat/water-resistant**: built for sports
- **Battery life**: solid for long runs

**Beats** (like Fit Pro or Powerbeats Pro):
- Better **sound + bass**
- Still **good fit** for workouts
- But more **noise isolation** = less situational awareness
- Not ideal if running outside or near traffic

**TLDR:**  
- **Outdoor/safe/situational awareness = Shockz**
- **Indoor/gym/high sound quality = Beats**
