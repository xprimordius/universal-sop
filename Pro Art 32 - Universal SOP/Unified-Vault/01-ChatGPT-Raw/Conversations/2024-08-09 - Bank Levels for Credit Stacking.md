# Bank Levels for Credit Stacking

---

## 👤 You

what are the 4 levels of banks that credit stacking talks about moving 0% interest debt through? big banks, regional banks, credit unions

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

The four levels of banks that credit stacking refers to when moving 0% interest debt through are:

1. **Big Banks**: These are large, national banks with significant assets and a wide range of financial products. Examples include Chase, Bank of America, and Wells Fargo.

2. **Regional Banks**: These banks operate within a specific region or a few states. They are smaller than big banks but still offer a broad range of services. Examples include PNC Bank and Fifth Third Bank.

3. **Credit Unions**: These are member-owned financial institutions that typically offer lower fees and better interest rates. They are often more flexible and community-focused than traditional banks.

4. **Community Banks**: These are smaller, local banks that serve specific communities or small geographic areas. They are known for personalized service and a strong understanding of local markets.

In the context of credit stacking, the strategy involves moving 0% interest debt through these different levels of financial institutions to maximize the availability of credit and minimize interest costs.
