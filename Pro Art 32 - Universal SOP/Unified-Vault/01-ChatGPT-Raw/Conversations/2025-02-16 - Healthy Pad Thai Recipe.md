## 🤖 ChatGPT

For a **healthy and delicious Pad Thai**, you can use the ingredients you have while keeping it flavorful and balanced. Here’s a great combination:

### **Sauce (Key Flavor Base)**
Mix together:
- **2 tbsp coconut aminos** (umami and slightly sweet)
- **1 tbsp fish sauce** (deepens the umami)
- **1 tbsp honey** (balances acidity and saltiness)
- **1 tbsp olive oil or coconut oil** (for a richer sauce)
- **1 tsp garlic powder** (aromatic)
- **1/2 tsp onion powder** (extra depth)

### **Protein & Stir-Fry**
- Cook **shrimp, chicken, or beef** in **beef tallow or butter** for extra richness.
- Stir-fry with some **veggies** (like bean sprouts, bell peppers, or carrots).
- Once almost done, add cooked **rice noodles** (or zoodles for a low-carb option).
  
### **Final Toss & Serving**
- Pour the sauce over the noodles/protein and stir well.
- Add a squeeze of **lime (if available)** for brightness.
- Garnish with **crushed peanuts (or pumpkin seeds for crunch), green onions, or cilantro**.

This will be **savory, slightly sweet, and full of umami** while using your healthiest ingredients!
