## 🤖 ChatGPT

No — **lane assist alone does not keep you centered continuously without steering input.**  

Here’s the distinction:  

---

### 🚗 **Lane Assist (Standard / Safety Feature)**
- **Purpose:** Warns or *nudges* you back if you start drifting.  
- **Hands-On Required:** Always. You must steer.  
- **Behavior:** Brief correction or vibration; not continuous lane-keeping.  
- **Included:** Standard on all Teslas.  

---

### 🧠 **Autopilot / FSD (Paid Upgrades)**
- **Purpose:** Actively steers to stay centered in the lane.  
- **Hands-On Required:** Tesla requires torque feedback on the wheel (you must keep a light hand on it).  
- **Behavior:** Continuous lane centering and adaptive cruise control.  
- **Included:**  
  - **Basic Autopilot** – Often included free.  
  - **Enhanced Autopilot / FSD** – Paid upgrades with more features (auto lane change, traffic light response, etc.).  

---

**Summary:**  
👉 *Lane Assist* = momentary correction.  
👉 *Autopilot or FSD* = active lane-centering (hands still on wheel).
