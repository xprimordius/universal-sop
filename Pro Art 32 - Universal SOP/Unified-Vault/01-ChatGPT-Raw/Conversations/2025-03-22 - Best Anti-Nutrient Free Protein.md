Isn’t plant protein full of anti nutrients because of the plantsv

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes — most **plant proteins naturally contain anti-nutrients**, such as:

- **Phytates (phytic acid)** – block mineral absorption (e.g. zinc, iron, calcium)  
- **Oxalates** – bind calcium and may contribute to kidney stones  
- **Lectins** – may impair digestion and damage gut lining  
- **Saponins** – can irritate the GI tract  
- **Protease inhibitors** – interfere with protein digestion

These are found in soy, pea, rice, and other plant-based proteins.

### TLDR:
**Yes, plant protein powders often contain anti-nutrients by default due to the nature of plants.** Sprouting, fermenting, or certain extraction processes can reduce them — but unless specifically processed or tested, most will still retain some anti-nutrient content.

If avoiding anti-nutrients is critical:
- **Opt for animal-based proteins** (e.g. grass-fed whey isolate or collagen)
- Look for **"low anti-nutrient" certified or sprouted/fermented** labels in plant proteins (rare)
