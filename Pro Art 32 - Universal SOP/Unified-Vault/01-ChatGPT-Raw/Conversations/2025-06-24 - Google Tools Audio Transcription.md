### **1. Google Docs + Voice Typing (Manual Playback)**
- **How:** Open Google Docs → Tools → *Voice Typing* → play your MP3 aloud.
- **Pros:** 100% free, no install.
- **Cons:** Manual, needs quiet environment and decent mic.
- **Best for:** Short or clean audio clips.

---

### **2. Microsoft Word (Online) – Dictate**
- **How:** Word Online → *Dictate* button → play MP3 into mic.
- **Pros:** Similar to Google Docs, free with Microsoft account.
- **Cons:** Manual playback required.

---

### **3. Otter.ai Free Plan**
- **How:** Upload MP3 on [otter.ai](https://otter.ai).
- **Free Tier:** 300 minutes/month, 30 min/recording.
- **Pros:** High accuracy, speaker ID, timestamps.
- **Cons:** Limit of 3 uploads per account.

---

### **4. YouTube + Subtitles Hack**
- **How:** Convert MP3 → MP4 (with blank image), upload to YouTube (unlisted) → Auto-captions → Download subtitles (.srt) → Convert to .txt.
- **Tools:** Use VLC or Kapwing for conversion.
- **Pros:** Bypasses file size/time limits.
- **Cons:** 12–24h delay, not private unless unlisted.

---

### **5. Whisper by OpenAI (Free via Local Install)**
- **How:** Install Whisper on your computer (Python) → run locally.
- **Pros:** Extremely accurate, offline, 100% free.
- **Cons:** Tech setup required. Needs decent GPU or patience.
- **Link:** [https://github.com/openai/whisper](https://github.com/openai/whisper)

---

### **6. Android: Google Live Transcribe**
- **How:** Install from Play Store → play MP3 out loud → copy text.
- **Pros:** Free, real-time.
- **Cons:** Android-only, manual.
