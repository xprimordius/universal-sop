## 🤖 ChatGPT

In **LetterStream**, when you select **"Duplicate Job"** and choose **"First-Class Mail"**, it performs the following:

### ✅ What it does:
1. **Clones the existing job** — same documents, same recipient addresses, same options.
2. **Changes the mail class** to **First-Class Mail** instead of the original class (e.g., if it was previously Certified or Standard).
3. **Generates a new job ID** — it’s treated as a separate mailing.
4. **Prices and processes it according to First-Class Mail rates**.
5. **Speeds up delivery** — typically delivered in 1–3 business days.

### ⚠️ Use case:
This is useful when:
- You want a **faster, cheaper re-send** of the same letter.
- Certified wasn't required, but you want confirmation it was sent.
- You're testing deliverability or following up.
