## 🤖 ChatGPT

On credit reports:

- **DRG** typically stands for **"Delinquent Rating Group"**, which may indicate that the account was delinquent at some point during the reporting period.
  
- **DRG/CLS** likely means **"Delinquent Rating Group / Closed"**, indicating that the account was previously delinquent but is now closed.

This terminology is used by some creditors or credit reporting systems to provide status updates on accounts. If you need clarification or dispute details, you can follow up with the reporting bureau (TransUnion in this case, as indicated on the report).
