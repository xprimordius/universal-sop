## 🤖 ChatGPT

If you're allergic to egg yolks, you can get choline from these alternative sources:

### **Animal-Based Sources (Highest Bioavailability)**
1. **Beef Liver** – ~356 mg per 100g  
2. **Beef (Chuck, Cooked)** – ~110 mg per 100g  
3. **Salmon** – ~90 mg per 100g  
4. **Chicken Breast** – ~72 mg per 100g  
5. **Pork (Lean Cuts)** – ~80 mg per 100g  

### **Plant-Based Sources**
1. **Soybeans (Cooked or Roasted)** – ~107 mg per 100g  
2. **Quinoa** – ~43 mg per 100g  
3. **Broccoli** – ~40 mg per 100g  
4. **Cauliflower** – ~39 mg per 100g  

### **Supplements**
- **Alpha-GPC (Highly bioavailable, crosses the blood-brain barrier easily)**  
- **CDP-Choline (Citicoline, supports brain function)**  
- **Choline Bitartrate (Budget-friendly, lower absorption rate)**  

If you consume beef and salmon regularly, you’re likely already getting a good amount of choline. Adding liver once a week can easily cover your needs.
