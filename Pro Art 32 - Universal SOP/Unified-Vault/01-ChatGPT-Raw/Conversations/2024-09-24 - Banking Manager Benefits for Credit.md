# Banking Manager Benefits for Credit

---

## 👤 You

does using a banking relationship manager actually help with 0% apr approvals for business credit cards with max limits?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Using a banking relationship manager can indeed help with 0% APR business credit card approvals and potentially securing higher credit limits. Here’s how:

1. **Personalized Service:** A relationship manager can advocate on your behalf with the bank, offering tailored advice and ensuring your applications get special attention. They might be able to present your business in a more favorable light to the underwriters.
  
2. **Improved Creditworthiness Perception:** By maintaining a strong banking relationship (business accounts, transactions, deposits), you create a track record that the bank can rely on. This can improve the perception of your business's stability and increase your chances of approval for 0% APR offers.

3. **Access to Exclusive Offers:** Relationship managers often have access to exclusive bank products and promotions, including credit cards with favorable terms such as 0% APR for longer periods or higher initial limits.

4. **Limit Increases:** They can facilitate limit increase requests based on your banking history, cash flow, and demonstrated ability to manage credit.

However, these benefits usually depend on the depth of the relationship and the financial standing of your business. Establishing regular deposits, managing accounts responsibly, and having a solid credit profile will maximize your chances of success.
