## 🤖 ChatGPT

A Morkie (Maltese × Yorkie) coughing and drooling excessively can signal several things — some mild, others urgent.  

### ⚠️ Rule-out checklist  
**1. Choking or throat obstruction**  
- Sudden gagging, pawing at mouth, panic, or unable to swallow saliva → **emergency vet now.**  

**2. Kennel cough / tracheal irritation**  
- Dry “honking” cough, especially after excitement or leash pressure.  
- Usually mild but contagious; needs vet exam for cough suppressant or antibiotics if secondary infection suspected.  

**3. Dental or oral issue**  
- Rotten tooth, gum abscess, or something stuck under the tongue can cause **drool + coughing.**  
- Look (carefully) for redness, swelling, or foul smell.  

**4. Nausea or toxin ingestion**  
- Drooling + gagging + lethargy can mean nausea or poisoning (plants, chocolate, xylitol, meds).  
- If you suspect ingestion → **go to emergency clinic immediately.**

**5. Heart or lung disease**  
- Cough worse at night or after activity, labored breathing, blue-tinged gums → urgent evaluation and chest X-ray.  

### ✅ What to do right now  
- **Check airway**: open mouth, ensure nothing stuck.  
- **Check gums**: should be pink and moist. Pale/blue = ER now.  
- **Keep dog calm and upright.**  
- **Record video** of cough for the vet—it helps diagnosis.  
- **Call a vet or ER** if: persistent cough > 6 hours, heavy drool, gagging, trouble breathing, or no eating/drinking.
