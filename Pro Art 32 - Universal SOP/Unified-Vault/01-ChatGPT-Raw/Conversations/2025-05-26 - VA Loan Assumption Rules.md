## 👤 You

Can you subject to a VA loan property from someone if you are not military

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**No**, you **cannot legally assume or “subject-to” a VA loan** if you are **not military-connected**, *unless*:

### ✅ Conditions Where It *Is* Possible:
1. **The VA loan is assumable** (most VA loans **after 1988** are, with lender approval).
2. **You apply to assume the loan** and get:
   - **Lender approval**
   - **VA approval**
3. **The seller (veteran)** agrees, knowing:
   - Their **VA entitlement stays tied up** unless you are also VA-eligible and substitute your entitlement.
   - They’re still **liable** if you default, unless released from liability (which only VA can do officially).

---

### ❌ If You Just Take Over “Subject-To”:
- You **don’t notify the lender**, and the loan stays in seller’s name.
- Technically possible, but:
   - **Violates “due-on-sale” clause** → lender can call the loan due.
   - **Highly risky** → especially if caught or loan goes into default.
   - **VA seller’s entitlement stays locked**.

---

### Summary:
- ✅ You can **assume** a VA loan **with approval** — even if not military.
- ❌ You cannot take over **subject-to** a VA loan without risking **loan acceleration** and harming the seller’s benefits.

- May take **30–60 days**.

  - **Assumption agreement signed**
  - **Title transfer**

## 👤 You

Do they have to carry the loan for you

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

No — **the seller does *not* carry the loan for you** in a **legal VA loan assumption**.

### Here's the distinction:

#### ✅ **If You Assume the VA Loan Properly (Through Lender):**
- The **loan stays with the current lender**.
- You take over the **existing terms, rate, balance**.
- Seller is removed from payment responsibility *if* a **release of liability** is granted.
- **No seller financing involved** — you're now the borrower.

---

#### ❌ **If You Do a "Subject-To" (Without Lender Approval):**
- Loan stays in **seller’s name**.
- You pay the mortgage on their behalf.
- This is **not a true assumption** and is risky for both parties.
- Lender can call the loan due (due-on-sale clause).

---

### Summary:
> A **true VA loan assumption = you become the legal borrower.**  
> The **seller does *not* carry the loan** for you — the **bank/lender still owns it**, and you just step into the repayment role.
