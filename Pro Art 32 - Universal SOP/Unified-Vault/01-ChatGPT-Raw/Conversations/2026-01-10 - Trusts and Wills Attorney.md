- **Not required:** You can legally create a will yourself.

- **Not required:** You can create a trust without an attorney.

- **Simple life + low assets:** DIY can work.  
- **Anything complex or valuable:** Use an estate attorney.

1. **Bartholin gland cyst**
   - At the vaginal opening (one side)
   - From blocked gland ducts
   - Painless unless infected (then very painful = abscess)

2. **Inclusion cyst**
   - Small, firm bumps
   - Often after childbirth, tearing, or surgery
   - Trapped skin cells

3. **Sebaceous (oil) cyst**
   - Blocked hair follicle or oil gland
   - Can recur
   - May get inflamed

4. **Skene’s duct cyst**
   - Near the urethra
   - Less common

5. **Infection-related**
   - Can enlarge with bacterial overgrowth
   - Sometimes linked with STIs (not always)

---

## Why they recur
- Gland blockage tendency
- Friction (sex, tight clothing)
- Hormonal changes
- Prior trauma or scarring
- Skin conditions (less common)

---

## When to be concerned
Seek medical care if:
- Pain, redness, fever
- Rapid growth
- Drainage with foul odor
- Recurrent cysts in the same spot
- Pain with walking or sex
- You’re over 40 and it’s new (rule out rare causes)

---

## What helps (non-emergency)
- **Warm sitz baths** (10–15 min, 2–3×/day)
- Avoid squeezing or popping
- Loose clothing
- Good hygiene (no harsh soaps)

---

## What NOT to do
❌ Pop it yourself  
❌ Ignore increasing pain  
❌ Assume it’s an STI without evaluation  

---

## Bottom line
> Most vaginal cysts are **blocked glands or skin-related**, not dangerous.  
> Painful, growing, or recurring cysts should be **examined by a clinician**—often easily treated.
