# Business Codes Overview

---

## 👤 You

What are the two types of business codes for federal and state

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

The two primary types of business codes used at the federal and state level are:

1. **NAICS (North American Industry Classification System):**
   - **Federal Level:** The NAICS code is used by federal agencies to classify businesses by the type of economic activity they engage in. It’s a six-digit code that allows for detailed tracking of business activity across North America.
   - **Example:** A business in software publishing might have the NAICS code 511210.

2. **SIC (Standard Industrial Classification):**
   - **State Level (or sometimes still used federally):** The SIC system is an older four-digit code that some states and sectors continue to use for business classification. It categorizes companies by their primary business activity.
   - **Example:** The SIC code for a software company might be 7372.

Some states may also adopt other state-specific classification systems, but these two are the most widely recognized for business classification in the U.S.
