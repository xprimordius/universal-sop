### Why **Candid (esp. CandidPro)** is More Reliable  

**1. Doctor-Led Care (Not Just Techs or Remote Dentists)**  
- Every case is designed and monitored by **orthodontists only** (not general dentists).  
- Orthodontists specialize in moving teeth, bites, and jaw mechanics — higher expertise = fewer mistakes.  

**2. Remote Monitoring Tech (CandidMonitoring™)**  
- You send regular scans via app + special device.  
- Orthodontist checks your progress every 10–14 days instead of months apart like SmileDirect.  
- Problems are caught early → refinements added faster, better end results.  

**3. Hybrid Model (In-Person + Remote)**  
- Initial scan or impressions often in-person at a **Candid Studio or partner dental office**.  
- Provides accurate 3D imaging and professional assessment of gums, bite, roots before starting.  

**4. Case Selection Discipline**  
- Candid is stricter on who qualifies.  
- Complex cases are rejected (sent to braces/Invisalign), so they don’t “force fit” aligners where they won’t work. This increases success rates.  

**5. Professional Refinements Built-In**  
- If teeth don’t track as planned, orthodontist prescribes new aligners at no extra cost (within treatment scope).  
- Many DIY aligner brands make revisions harder or slower.  

---

### TL;DR  
Candid is more reliable because it’s **orthodontist-only**, **monitored frequently**, **starts with in-person precision**, and **doesn’t accept cases it can’t finish well**. That structure prevents small issues from turning into big failures — which is the main risk with cheaper remote systems.

## 🔍 What Drives Cost

- **Severity of misalignment / bite issues** — mild crowding or small gaps are cheaper; big rotations + bite correction + jaw issues cost more.  
- **Number of aligners + treatment duration** — more trays & longer time = higher cost.  
- **In-office vs remote work** — in-person scans, supervision, check-ups add cost.  
- **Attachments, elastics, etc.** — needed in tougher cases, cost more.  
- **Geographic location / provider fees** — big city / specialty orthodontist offices are pricier.  
- **Insurance, payment plans, specials** — can reduce out-of-pocket.
