## 🤖 ChatGPT

Your Minecraft roller coaster cart shaking or being unridable usually comes down to one or more of these issues:

---

### 1. **Misaligned Tracks**
- **Cause:** Curves or slopes aren't connected properly.
- **Fix:** Make sure all rail pieces are smoothly connected — no gaps, mismatched slopes, or sudden transitions.

---

### 2. **Blocks in the Way**
- **Cause:** The minecart is clipping into adjacent blocks (like walls, fences, or blocks too close).
- **Fix:** Leave at least **1 block of space** on both sides of the track. Avoid putting blocks directly next to the rails.

---

### 3. **Too Many Powered Rails / Glitches**
- **Cause:** Overuse of powered rails or improper power setup.
- **Fix:** Alternate between powered rails and regular rails. Avoid stacking too many powered rails together without breaks.

---

### 4. **Lag or Entity Overload**
- **Cause:** Too many entities (mobs, minecarts, etc.) or redstone machines causing lag.
- **Fix:** Reduce entities in the area. Use `/kill @e[type=minecraft:minecart]` if many carts are stuck.

---

### 5. **World/Chunk Corruption**
- **Cause:** Rarely, chunk glitches or corrupted blocks can bug the ride.
- **Fix:** Rebuild that section of track or move the ride to a new area.

---

### 6. **Minecart Physics Bug**
- **Cause:** Some versions (especially modded or snapshot builds) have buggy minecart physics.
- **Fix:** Update to the latest **stable** version of Minecraft.
