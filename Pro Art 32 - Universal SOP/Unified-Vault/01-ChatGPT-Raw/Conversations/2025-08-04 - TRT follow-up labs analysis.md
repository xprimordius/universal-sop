### 🚨 What High Hematocrit/Hemoglobin Does (TRT-Induced Erythrocytosis)

Elevated **hematocrit**, **hemoglobin**, and **RBC count** = **thick, viscous blood** → like trying to pump syrup through your arteries.

---

### 🔬 **What This Actually Does in the Body**

| Effect | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| **Increased blood viscosity** | Thicker blood = slower flow, higher blood pressure, and higher risk of clotting. |
| **Strain on the heart** | Heart must pump harder to circulate thicker blood. |
| **Decreased oxygen delivery** | Ironically, too many RBCs can impair microcirculation, reducing oxygen at the capillary level. |
| **Clot risk** | Higher hematocrit = more likely to develop DVTs, strokes, heart attacks. |
| **Worsened sleep apnea** | If present, high hematocrit makes this worse, increasing fatigue. |

---
