### 1. **What Is TransUnion?**  
TransUnion is one of the **three major credit reporting agencies** (along with Equifax and Experian) in the U.S. It compiles, sells, and maintains consumer credit information used by banks, lenders, employers, and insurers to assess risk.

---

### 2. **Their Legal Responsibility:**  
Under the **Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA)**, TransUnion must:  
- **Maintain maximum possible accuracy** of consumer data.  
- **Correct errors promptly when disputed.**  
- **Report only verifiable and lawful information.**  
Their data directly influences approvals, rates, and terms for credit, loans, housing, employment, and insurance.

---

### 3. **So Who Owns Them?**  
As of recent history:
- **Spartan Acquisition Corp. II**, a **Special Purpose Acquisition Company (SPAC)** affiliated with **Apollo Global Management**, was linked to TransUnion ownership and restructuring.
- **TransUnion is a public company (NYSE: TRU)**, but large institutional investors—including private equity firms like **Advent International** (a previous owner), **Goldman Sachs**, **BlackRock**, and **Vanguard**—hold significant ownership stakes.

Translation: **Wall Street, not the public**, controls the data gatekeepers.
