---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

To be someone else’s *keeper* means:

> **You take responsibility for their well-being, actions, or safety — often when they’re not doing it for themselves.**

It implies:
- **Stewardship or guardianship** (like a parent, protector, or mentor)
- Often **burdensome**, especially if it’s unasked or one-sided
- May involve **moral obligation**, not just physical care

Example: “Am I my brother’s keeper?” — suggests questioning whether we are responsible for others’ behavior or fate.
