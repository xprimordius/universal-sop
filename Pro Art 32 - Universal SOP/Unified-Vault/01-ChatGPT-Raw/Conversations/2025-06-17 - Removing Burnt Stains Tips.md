## 👤 You

How to get rid of burnt remains in a stainless steel pot? Lemon? Baking soda? Vinegar?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Best method to remove burnt remains from a stainless steel pot:**

### **1. Boiling Water + Baking Soda**
- Fill the pot with water to cover the burnt area.
- Bring to a boil.
- Add **2–3 tablespoons of baking soda**.
- Simmer for 15–20 minutes.
- Let cool slightly, then **scrape gently with a wooden or silicone spatula**.

### **2. Vinegar + Baking Soda (for stubborn burns)**
- Cover burnt area with equal parts **white vinegar and water**.
- Bring to a boil, then **remove from heat**.
- Add **1–2 tablespoons baking soda** slowly (it will fizz).
- Let sit 15–30 minutes, then scrub.

### **3. Lemon Method (light burns or polishing)**
- Cut a lemon in half, rub it over the burn.
- Let lemon juice sit for 15 mins.
- Add water and boil.
- Scrub gently.

**Pro tip:** For extreme cases, make a **paste of baking soda + a little water**, apply to burn, and let sit overnight before scrubbing.

Avoid steel wool—it scratches stainless steel. Use a soft sponge or nylon scrubber.

## 👤 You

Can I use rice wine vinegar?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

No — **do not use rice wine vinegar** for burnt stainless steel cleanup.

Use **white distilled vinegar** instead. It’s:
- **Stronger in acidity**
- **More effective at breaking down burnt residue**
- **Cheaper and leaves no residue or scent**

Rice wine vinegar is too mild and slightly sweet — not ideal for cleaning.

## 👤 You

What if I boil with all ingredient and baking soda in there

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Do **not** boil **baking soda and vinegar together** in the same step.

### Why?
- They **neutralize each other** (acid + base = mostly water + CO₂), canceling out their cleaning power.

### Optimal Method (Use Separately):
1. **Boil vinegar + water** (1:1) to loosen the burn.
2. Remove from heat, then **add baking soda** slowly (it will fizz).
3. Let sit 15–30 min.
4. Scrub.

If you want to boil something from the start, just use **water + vinegar**, then add baking soda **after boiling**.
