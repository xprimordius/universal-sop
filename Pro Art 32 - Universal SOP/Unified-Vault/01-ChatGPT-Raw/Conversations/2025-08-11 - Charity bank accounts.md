## 🤖 ChatGPT

You’re probably thinking of a **segregated bank account** or **dedicated business/charity account** — but for donations and protection from scams, the most common terms are:  

- **Escrow Account** – Held by a neutral third party until conditions are met.  
- **Trust Account** – Funds legally separated and managed for a specific purpose (often used by attorneys, nonprofits, or fiduciaries).  
- **Charity/Donation Account** – Opened under a registered nonprofit’s name to keep funds separate from personal finances.  
- **Fiscal Sponsor Account** – If you’re not a registered nonprofit, a fiscal sponsor holds and manages donations for you.  

For scam protection, **never have donations go directly to a personal account** — instead, register a nonprofit or use a fiscal sponsor, then open a bank account under that entity’s name so the money is legally and visibly separate.
