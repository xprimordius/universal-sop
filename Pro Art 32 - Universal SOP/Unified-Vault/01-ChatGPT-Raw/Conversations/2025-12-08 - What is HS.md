- Those lumps may become inflamed, sometimes forming painful nodules or abscesses. Over time, they can rupture, drain fluid or pus, scar, or create tunnels under the skin.  
- It’s not a simple “pimple”; tends to be deeper, long-lasting, often recurring, and can impact quality of life.
