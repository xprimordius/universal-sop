**Short answer:**  
👉 **A small amount of blood in the urine can be normal right after fascial sling surgery — but only briefly.**

### What’s considered **normal**
- **Light pink or blood-tinged urine**  
- **First 24–72 hours** after surgery  
- Often due to **catheter irritation**, bladder manipulation, or swelling

### What is **NOT normal / needs attention**
Seek medical care **urgently** if you have **any** of the following:
- **Bright red urine** or **blood clots**
- Bleeding that **worsens** or lasts **> 3 days**
- **Burning, fever, chills** (possible infection)
- **Inability to urinate**, severe urgency, or retention
- **Increasing pelvic pain or pressure**

### Why bleeding can happen
- Bladder or urethral irritation from surgery
- Temporary catheter trauma
- Post-operative inflammation
- (Less common) bladder injury or infection

### Bottom line
- ✅ **Light blood, short-lived = usually normal**
- 🚩 **Heavy, persistent, painful, or systemic symptoms = call your surgeon or go to urgent care/ER**
