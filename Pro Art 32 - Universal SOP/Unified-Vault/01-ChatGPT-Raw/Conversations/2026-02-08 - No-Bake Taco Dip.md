**No-Bake Taco Dip (No Sour Cream)**  
Fast. Cold. Clean. Zero oven.

image_group{"query":["no bake taco dip","taco dip cream cheese ground beef","taco dip no sour cream","taco dip with salsa"]}

### Ingredients
- 1 lb ground beef  
- 1 packet taco seasoning  
- 8 oz cream cheese (softened)  
- ½–1 cup salsa (drained if watery)  
- Optional: shredded cheese, jalapeños, green onions, cilantro

### Steps
1. **Cook beef**  
   Brown ground beef. Drain fat. Stir in taco seasoning + a splash of water. Cool to warm (not hot).

2. **Mix base**  
   In a bowl, whip cream cheese until smooth. Fold in salsa.

3. **Assemble**  
   Gently mix in seasoned beef **or** layer:  
   - Bottom: cream cheese + salsa  
   - Top: taco beef

4. **Finish (optional)**  
   Add cheese, jalapeños, or green onions.

5. **Chill (optional)**  
   10–15 min for better texture. Serve immediately if needed.

### Serve With
- Tortilla chips  
- Bell pepper strips  
- Celery sticks

**Pro tip:** If it feels loose → add more cream cheese.  
If too thick → add 1–2 tbsp salsa juice.
