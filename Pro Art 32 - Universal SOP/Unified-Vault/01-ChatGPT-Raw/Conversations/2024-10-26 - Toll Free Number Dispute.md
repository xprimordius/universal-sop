### 1. **Gather Evidence**
   - **Document Ownership:** Collect all documents proving your ownership of the business and the toll-free number, such as:
     - IRS documents (EIN confirmation letter)
     - State registration certificates
     - Toll-free number service agreements/contracts
     - DUNS registration documents (if you registered your business with them initially)

### 2. **Contact the Service Provider for the Toll-Free Number**
   - Reach out to your toll-free number provider (e.g., AT&T, Verizon) and explain the situation.
   - Provide proof of your ownership and request them to verify and reassign the number correctly under your business name.
   - They may need to see contracts or invoices to prove that you are the legitimate owner.

### 3. **Correct the 411 Listing**
   - Contact the entity managing the 411 directory (e.g., ListYourself.net or a telecom service provider).
   - Provide business registration documents and proof of ownership to update the listing to reflect your business accurately.

### 4. **Update with Dun & Bradstreet (DUNS)**
   - **Dispute the Information:**
     - Log in to your DUNS account, or create one if you haven’t.
     - Submit a dispute to correct the listing with the accurate toll-free number and business information.
   - **Provide Documentation:**
     - Include proof of business registration, IRS details, and proof of ownership of the toll-free number.

### 5. **File Complaints or Reports if Needed**
   - **FTC Complaint:**
     - If you believe this is an intentional case of identity theft or fraud, file a complaint with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) at IdentityTheft.gov.
   - **State Attorney General or Local Authorities:**
     - Report the issue to the State Attorney General’s office if you suspect malicious intent or if the steps above are unsuccessful.
   
### 6. **Update the IRS and State Records**
   - **IRS:**
     - Contact the IRS to inform them about the issue. Request an update or correction of the business records if necessary.
   - **State Business Registration:**
     - Update your state’s business registration details to ensure the correct toll-free number is on file.

### 7. **Prevent Future Issues**
   - Set up monitoring alerts for your business name and toll-free number with services like Experian Business Credit Monitoring or D&B’s CreditSignal.
   - Ensure all future listings and business records are secured with the correct information to prevent unauthorized changes.
