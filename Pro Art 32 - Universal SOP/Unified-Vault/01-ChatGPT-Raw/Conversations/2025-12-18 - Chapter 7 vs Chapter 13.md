### Chapter 7 vs Chapter 13 — Business Shutdown, Straight Facts

---

## Chapter 7 — **Liquidation**
**Purpose:** Wipe out debt fast  
**Timeline:** ~3–6 months  

**What happens to your stuff**
- **Business assets:** ❌ Sold (liquidated) to pay creditors  
- **Personal assets:**  
  - ✅ Keep **exempt** property (varies by state)  
  - ❌ Non-exempt assets can be sold  

**Best for**
- Shutting down a **sole proprietorship**
- No meaningful assets to protect
- Want a **clean, fast reset**

**Key reality**
- Corporations/LLCs can file Ch.7, but **do not get a discharge** — just liquidation  
- Owners may still need **personal Ch.7** if they personally guaranteed debts

---

## Chapter 13 — **Reorganization (Wage-Earner Plan)**
**Purpose:** Keep assets, repay over time  
**Timeline:** 3–5 year payment plan  

**What happens to your stuff**
- **Business assets:** ✅ You keep them  
- **Personal assets:** ✅ You keep them  
- Creditors are paid **partially** through a court-approved plan

**Best for**
- **Sole proprietors** who want to keep equipment, vehicles, inventory
- Need to stop foreclosure/repossession
- Have **regular income**

**Key limitation**
- **Only individuals** can file Chapter 13  
- LLCs / corporations **cannot** use Chapter 13

---

## Bottom Line (No Fluff)

| Goal | Chapter 7 | Chapter 13 |
|---|---|---|
| Shut down business | ✅ | ❌ (designed to continue) |
| Keep business assets | ❌ | ✅ |
| Fast exit | ✅ | ❌ |
| Repay over time | ❌ | ✅ |
| LLC / Corp eligible | ✅ (liquidation only) | ❌ |

- **Want out + minimal assets? → Chapter 7**
- **Want to keep assets + have income? → Chapter 13**
