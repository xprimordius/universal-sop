**Effects and tiredness considerations:**

1. **Magnesium Glycinate and Relaxation**:
   - Magnesium glycinate is often calming due to its glycine component, which has relaxing effects on the nervous system.
   - At higher doses, you might experience drowsiness or fatigue, especially if you're magnesium-deficient or sensitive to magnesium's effects.

2. **B6 (P5P) at 5000% DV**:
   - A very high dose of B6 could theoretically add to relaxation but is less likely to cause sleepiness than the magnesium itself.
   - Long-term excessive B6 can lead to nerve-related side effects, but short-term high doses are generally well-tolerated.

3. **Tolerance to Magnesium**:
   - If you're accustomed to taking magnesium, the sedative effect might be less noticeable.
   - If you are magnesium-deficient, the relaxing effects could feel more pronounced initially.

4. **Taking 12 Capsules (1440 mg magnesium glycinate)**:
   - This would roughly double your elemental magnesium to ~200–288 mg, which remains within tolerable levels for most people when taken across the day.
   - Splitting the doses between morning and evening can help mitigate excessive tiredness.

### Recommendations:
- **Timing**: To reduce drowsiness, take smaller doses (e.g., 3 capsules) throughout the day instead of all at once.
- **Hydration**: Magnesium has a mild laxative effect for some; stay hydrated.
- **Sensitivity Monitoring**: If you feel overly tired, scale back and assess your tolerance.
- **Sleep Aid**: Use the larger portion of your dose in the evening if you welcome the calming effect.
