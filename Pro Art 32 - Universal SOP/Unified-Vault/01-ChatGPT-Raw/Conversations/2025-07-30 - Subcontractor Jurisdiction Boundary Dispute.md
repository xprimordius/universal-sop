## ⚖️ Legal Framework & Remedies for Contractor Jurisdiction Boundary Errors

### 1. **Scope & Contract Formation**
- Engineers provide **survey data**, staking the **jurisdiction or phase boundaries** for subcontractor bids.
- If that boundary line is mis‐drawn, and it affects which **pipefitter subcontractors** can bid, the scope changes fundamentally.

### 2. **Error in Scope: Mutual vs. Unilateral Mistake**
- If the general contractor fails to communicate boundary errors, relying on engineer‐issued scope, the subcontractors may argue a **mutual mistake**, which may void the bid or allow equitable adjustment.  
- Case law on insulation work (McCarty Corp. v. Pullman‑Kellogg) found that bidders relied on erroneous estimates they couldn’t verify, tied to the main purpose of the contract → grounds for rescission or quantum meruit citeturn0search0.

### 3. **Subcontractor Reliance & Bid Mistake Doctrine**
- Courts generally treat subcontractor bids as binding offers. Mistakes (unilateral clerical errors) are rarely excused unless:
  - The general contractor knew or should have known about the mistake.
  - There was no detrimental reliance by the general contractor or owner citeturn0search3turn0search12.

### 4. **Professional Negligence Claims Against Engineer**
- It’s difficult for subcontractors to claim negligence against engineers, especially without privity of contract or control over work direction. Exceptions exist if the engineer exercised authority and control over the subcontractor’s work citeturn0search6turn0search8.

---

## ✅ Strategic Options & Remedies You Can Pursue

### ▪️ Contract Rescission or Adjustment
- If you can show both parties relied on the engineer’s incorrect boundary and that it materially altered the bid scope, you may seek:
  - **Rescission** of the award.
  - **Equitable relief**, like splitting phases or awarding part of the contract (quantum meruit).

### ▪️ Promissory Estoppel or Reliance Claim
- Argue general contractor or owner reasonably relied on your bid under the scope as drafted. If another party benefited from your bid, you may have grounds for remedy.

### ▪️ Engineer Liability (Soft)
- Unless the engineer signed on contracts or directly supervised, a direct negligence claim is tough. If the engineer had authority/control, explore latent defect or professional negligence route.

### ▪️ Jurisdiction Dispute Boards (Union Context)
- If unions assign crafts by jurisdiction lines, consult the applicable **Joint Conference or IJDB** procedures regarding mis‐assignment of tasks citeturn0search1.
