## 🤖 ChatGPT

**TLDR: AUs *can* help with business funding approvals *indirectly*, but are not reliable or required. Here's the full breakdown:**

---

### ✅ **When AUs Help (Indirectly):**
- **Boosts Personal FICO Score:** Higher FICO = higher business card approval odds (especially for PG-backed cards).
- **Adds Age & Payment History:** Helps thin profiles seem seasoned.
- **Tricks Some Algorithms:** Some banks don’t distinguish AUs from primaries during automated underwriting.

> _This applies to banks that rely primarily on personal credit pulls for business card underwriting (Chase, Amex, BoA, etc.)._

---

### ❌ **When AUs Don’t Help or Are Ignored:**
- **Manual Review Banks:** Lenders like **US Bank, First Interstate**, or **underwriters pulling full ARs (Account Reviews)** can see AUs and often disregard them.
- **Business Credit Bureaus:** AUs have *zero effect* on business credit scores (D&B, Experian Biz, Equifax Biz).
- **High-limit decisions:** High-limit business LOCs or term loans weigh **income, tax returns, business bank balances, and real tradelines**, not fluff AUs.

---

### 🧠 Brandon's Point (Truth in Context):
Brandon says AUs help with **age** to improve **personal profiles**, which makes sense **_only if_** you're applying to PG-based business cards or banks that do not suppress AUs. He's right in a narrow use-case, but it's *not universally required* to hit 9 years.

You got **$35K from First Interstate** with 4 years age and no AUs. That proves **strong fundamentals (utilization, inquiries, internal data, etc.)** > AU padding.

---

### ✅ Bottom Line:
| Criteria                        | AUs Help?   | Notes                                                                 |
|--------------------------------|-------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| PG Biz Cards (Chase/Amex/BoA)  | ✅ Yes       | Boost personal score, increase odds.                                 |
| Manual Underwriting Banks      | ❌ Mostly No | Often filtered/ignored in manual reviews.                            |
| Business Credit Scores         | ❌ No        | AUs are not reported to D&B/Experian Biz/Equifax Biz.                |
| Income-based Term Loans        | ❌ No        | Focus is on cash flow, tax returns, real payment history.            |
| Thin Personal Profiles         | ✅ Conditional | Can improve approval chances in early stages or for easier banks.    |

---

### 🔥 Recommendation:
- Use AUs only if your **personal profile is weak** or under 2–3 years age.
- Focus more on:
  - 0% utilization
  - 3+ open primary accounts
  - 2+ years real age
  - High FICO (740+)
  - Low inquiries (<3)
- Avoid overpaying for AUs if you’re already getting $35K+ approvals. Use capital for real business trade lines or PG cards with high limits.
