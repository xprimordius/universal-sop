## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes — indirectly. Weed itself doesn’t directly “cause” acne, but it can *trigger* conditions that make breakouts more likely.  

**Breakdown:**  
1. **Hormonal Effects:** THC increases cortisol and may slightly disrupt testosterone/estrogen balance → more oil (sebum) production.  
2. **Inflammation:** Smoking (any form) increases oxidative stress → clogged pores, slower healing.  
3. **Lifestyle Factors:** Weed-induced snacking (sugar/fat/salt) + dehydration = acne fuel.  
4. **Sleep & Hygiene:** Poor sleep, dirty pillowcases, or touching your face while high worsen it.  
5. **Topicals/Vapes:** Oils, residues, or contaminants can clog pores.  

**If you smoke and want clear skin:**  
- Hydrate heavily.  
- Eat anti-inflammatory (omega-3s, greens).  
- Clean face after smoking.  
- Prioritize sleep and zinc + vitamin A intake.  

👉 Occasional use isn’t a big deal — chronic, dehydrating, or high-sugar use is.
