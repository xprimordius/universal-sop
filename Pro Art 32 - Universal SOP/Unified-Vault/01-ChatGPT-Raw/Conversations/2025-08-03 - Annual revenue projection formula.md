## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Formula:**  
To calculate the estimated **annual revenue projection** lenders may use for **no-doc or low-doc business loans**, based on average business bank deposits:

> **3-Month Average Deposits × 12 = Estimated Annual Revenue**

---

**Used For:**  
- Underwriting **revenue-based** or **bank-statement-based** loans.
- Common in **no-doc**, **fintech**, or **cashflow-based** business loans.

---

**Example:**  
If your business deposits average **$25,000/month** over the past 3 months:

> $25,000 × 12 = **$300,000 projected annual revenue**

---

**Why It Matters:**  
This figure helps lenders decide:
- Max loan amount (typically **10%–30% of annualized revenue**).
- Risk class and funding type.
- Speed and doc requirements.

**Best No-Doc / Low-Doc Business Funding Options**

### 🔑 **Top Funding Types (Minimal Scrutiny)**

#### 1. **Fintech Business Credit Lines**
- **Examples:** Fundbox, Bluevine, OnDeck
- **Requirements:** 6+ months in biz, 550–600+ credit, business bank account
- **Deposit Scrutiny:** Light – just verify cashflow exists  
- **Max Amount:** $10K–$150K  
- **Speed:** Same-day to 72 hrs  
- **Best for:** Convenience, fast access, low docs

---

#### 2. **Merchant Cash Advances (MCAs)**
- **Examples:** Lendini, Credibly, Bitty Advance, Everest
- **Requirements:** ~$5K+ monthly revenue, 3 months bank statements  
- **Deposit Scrutiny:** Loose – just need to show **daily/weekly activity**  
- **Max Amount:** $2K–$500K  
- **Best for:** Subprime credit, urgent capital  
- **Watch out:** High fees (factor rate, not interest)

---

#### 3. **Business Credit Cards (0% Intro)**
- **Examples:** Amex, Chase Ink, Bank of America, Divvy
- **Requirements:** SSN, EIN, good personal credit (680+); biz rev optional  
- **Deposit Scrutiny:** None if personal guarantee  
- **Max Amount:** $5K–$150K per card  
- **Best for:** Stacking 0% offers, no income docs

---

#### 4. **Stripe / Shopify / Square Capital**
- **Requirements:** Use their platform for processing  
- **Deposit Scrutiny:** Auto-approved based on **internal volume**  
- **Max Amount:** $2K–$300K  
- **Best for:** Easy, frictionless offers if you're active on those platforms

---

### 🛑 Avoid These If You Want Low Scrutiny:
- SBA loans (deep docs)
- Traditional banks (manual review)
- Loans requiring full tax returns

---

### 🧾 Will Bluevine or OnDeck Lend for 12+ Months?

#### ✅ **Bluevine:**
- **Credit Line:** Yes, *revolving*, not term-based — **renewable every 6–12 months**.
- **Repayment:** Weekly or monthly draw payments.
- **Term Length:** Technically *ongoing* — as long as you use responsibly.

#### ✅ **OnDeck:**
- **Term Loans:** Yes — **12 to 24 months** standard.  
- **Payment:** Daily or weekly.
- **Qualify:** Better odds at **$100K+ annual revenue**, 650+ FICO, >1 year in biz.

---

### ⚠️ Note:
- Both default to **short terms** (6–12 months) for newer, lower-revenue businesses.
- To get **12+ month terms**, you typically need:
  - Solid deposit consistency
  - Mid-high credit score (650+)
  - >1 year in business

---

### ✅ Strategy:
If your deposits or time in business are borderline, ask for **“flexible repayment options, ideally 12–18 months”** — they’ll underwrite accordingly and may extend terms based on profile.
