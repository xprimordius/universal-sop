**Doxycycline is used for:**

- **Bacterial infections**
  - Acne
  - Pneumonia, bronchitis
  - Sinus, skin, eye infections
  - Urinary tract infections (some cases)

- **Sexually transmitted infections**
  - Chlamydia
  - Gonorrhea (with another antibiotic)
  - Syphilis (penicillin-allergic patients)

  - Lyme disease
  - Rocky Mountain spotted fever
  - Ehrlichiosis
  - Malaria prevention (travel)

  - Rosacea
  - Periodontal (gum) disease
  - Anthrax exposure (post-exposure)
  - Anti-inflammatory use in dermatology

- Avoid lying down for 30 minutes after taking (prevents esophageal irritation)
- Sun sensitivity ↑
- Avoid in pregnancy and in children <8 unless specifically directed

**Short answer:**  
Yes — **doxycycline and spironolactone can be taken together**. They’re commonly combined (especially for acne). Duration depends on *why* you’re taking doxycycline.

---

## **How long to take doxycycline**
**Typical ranges (doctor may adjust):**
- **Acne:** **6–12 weeks** (sometimes up to 3–4 months, not long-term forever)
- **Chlamydia:** **7 days**
- **Lyme disease (early):** **10–21 days**
- **Sinus / respiratory infection:** **7–10 days**
- **Skin infections:** **7–14 days**
- **Tick bite prophylaxis:** **Single dose** (specific cases only)

👉 If it’s for acne and you’ve been on it **>3–4 months**, doctors usually reassess and taper.

---

## **Taking doxycycline + spironolactone together**
✅ **Safe together**  
✅ **No harmful interaction**  
✅ Often prescribed **at the same time** for hormonal acne

**What to watch:**
- **Spironolactone:** can raise potassium → avoid excess potassium supplements
- **Doxycycline:** sun sensitivity, stomach irritation

---

## **Best practices**
- Take **doxycycline with water**, stay upright 30 min  
- Avoid taking it **with calcium/iron** (wait 2 hours)  
- Use **sun protection**
- Spironolactone is usually **daily, long-term** (months to years if tolerated)
