- **Lexapro (escitalopram) discontinuation** can cause **emotional volatility**, even if used **<1 month**.
- Stopping abruptly → **SSRI discontinuation syndrome**.
- Symptoms often include: **mood swings, anxiety, irritability, crying spells, agitation**, brain fog.

**Timing fits**
- Onset: **2–10 days after last dose** (sometimes sooner).
- Duration: **days to a few weeks**.
- Christmas Eve → now = **right in the window**.

**Why it happens**
- The brain starts adapting **immediately** to SSRIs.
- Sudden stop = **serotonin imbalance** before regulation stabilizes.

**What helps**
- Reassurance: this is **physiological, not character-based**.
- Gentle support, low stimulation, good sleep.
- **Medical guidance** if symptoms are intense:  
  - Sometimes a **temporary low-dose restart + taper** resolves it fast.
  - Or reassurance + monitoring if symptoms are manageable.
