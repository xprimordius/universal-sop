## FTC Law on “Bait-and-Switch” and Misrepresented Deliverables

### 1. **Primary Federal Law**
- entity["law","Federal Trade Commission Act","Section 5"]  
- **15 U.S.C. §45**

**Rule:**  
Unfair or deceptive acts or practices in commerce are illegal.

A practice is **deceptive** if:
1. A **representation or omission** occurs  
2. It is **likely to mislead a reasonable consumer**  
3. It is **material** to the purchase decision

**Source:**  
https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/45

## 2. FTC “Bait-and-Switch” Doctrine
Defined by the FTC as advertising one product/service but **intending to sell a different or inferior one.**

### Governing Regulation
- entity["law","FTC Bait Advertising Rule","16 CFR Part 238"]

**Key provision:**

> “Bait advertising is an alluring but insincere offer to sell a product or service which the advertiser does not actually intend to sell.”  
> — 16 CFR §238.0

Source:  
https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-238

## 3. How It Applies to Programs / Courses / Services
The FTC has repeatedly stated that **misrepresenting program content or deliverables** qualifies as deception.

Examples of **illegal conduct**:

| Conduct | FTC Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Ads promise specific modules / coaching / services but not delivered | **Deceptive representation** |
| Sales call promises bonuses or services absent in program | **Material misrepresentation** |
| VSL advertises a system but product is something else | **Bait-and-switch** |
| Marketing claims “done-for-you” but delivers only education | **False advertising** |
| Testimonials imply outcomes not typical | **Deceptive earnings claims** |

---

## 4. Material Misrepresentation Standard
FTC policy statement on deception:

A claim is **material** if it affects the consumer’s purchase decision.

Examples the FTC considers material:

- program **features**
- **deliverables**
- **services included**
- **results promised**
- **support level**
- **timeframe**

Source:  
https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/ftc-policy-statement-deception
