## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes, **you can raise a baby chick indoors** — but it requires **very specific care**:

- **Brooder:** Safe enclosed area (box, tub, or small pen) to prevent escape and injury.
- **Heat Source:** Use a heat lamp or brooder plate.  
  - **95°F** (35°C) for week 1, then **reduce by 5°F each week** until fully feathered (~6 weeks).
- **Bedding:** Pine shavings or paper towels (avoid slippery surfaces like newspaper).
- **Food:** Chick starter feed (high-protein, formulated for baby chicks).
- **Water:** Shallow dish with clean, fresh water. Add marbles/pebbles inside dish to prevent drowning.
- **Ventilation:** Fresh air but no drafts.
- **Sanitation:** Clean daily to prevent disease.
- **Socialization:** If raising one chick, it needs extra attention (chicks are very social).

**Key:** Temperature control, hygiene, and nutrition are critical.
