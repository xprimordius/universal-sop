**Executive** is better for the following reasons:

---

### **1. Credibility with Underwriters & Lenders**
- Executive roles (e.g., CEO, Managing Member, Partner) suggest ownership, strategic control, and continuity.
- Sales roles are seen as performance-dependent and potentially volatile.

---

### **2. Income Justification**
- $245K from $935K revenue = ~26% compensation. Reasonable for an executive.
- That ratio looks aggressive for a *salesperson* unless you're the *only* sales generator.

---

### **3. Flexibility for Approvals**
- Executive implies you can sign contracts, make financial decisions — key for:
  - Business credit lines
  - Stated income credit cards
  - Leasing & funding approvals
  - SBA loans or PG-backed offers

---

### **4. Tax/Financial Planning**
- Executive role aligns with S-Corp or LLC profit pass-through, allowing flexibility in how $245K is paid (W2 vs distribution).
- Salesperson = typically W2 only, limits structuring.

---

### **Conclusion:**
Use **Executive (e.g., Managing Member or CEO)** — it's strategic, credible, and maximizes flexibility for funding, approvals, and long-term growth.
