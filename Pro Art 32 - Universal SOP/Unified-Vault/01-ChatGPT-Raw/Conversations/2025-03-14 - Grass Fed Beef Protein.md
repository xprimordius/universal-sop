### **Is the Combination Complete?**
- No, because **beef already has all essential amino acids**, but beans alone would be incomplete without a **grain source** (e.g., rice, corn, or wheat) to balance their amino acid profile.

### **Verdict:**

Yes, you can **live off grass-fed ground beef, organic black beans, and northern beans** as your primary meal, but there are a few key considerations for **long-term health and optimal nutrition**:

### **Macronutrient Breakdown**  
- **Protein:** Well covered from beef and beans.  
- **Fats:** Depends on the fat content of the beef. If it's very lean, you may need additional healthy fats (e.g., butter, tallow, olive oil).  
- **Carbs:** Beans provide complex carbohydrates and fiber.  

### **Micronutrient Considerations**  
1. **Omega-3s & Healthy Fats** – Grass-fed beef has some omega-3s, but **not enough for optimal brain & heart health**. Consider adding:  
   - Wild-caught **salmon** or sardines (for EPA & DHA)  
   - **Egg yolks** (for choline & fat-soluble vitamins)  
   - **Olive oil, avocado, or tallow** for more balanced fats  

2. **Vitamins & Minerals**  
   - **Beef covers:** Iron, zinc, B12, choline, selenium  
   - **Beans cover:** Magnesium, potassium, fiber  
   - **Missing nutrients:**  
     - **Vitamin C** (crucial for collagen and immunity) → Add citrus, bell peppers, or berries  
     - **Vitamin K2 & D3** (bone & cardiovascular health) → Egg yolks, butter, or sunlight exposure  
     - **Iodine** (thyroid function) → Seaweed or iodized salt  

3. **Digestibility**  
   - Beans have **antinutrients (phytates & lectins)** that can interfere with mineral absorption. Soaking/sprouting helps.  
   - **Beef is very digestible**, but some may need additional fiber (e.g., fermented veggies) for gut health.  

### **Final Verdict**  
- **Short-term? Yes, you’ll be fine.**  
- **Long-term? Add a few key nutrients for optimal performance & longevity.**  

#### **Minimal Additions for a Balanced Diet:**
