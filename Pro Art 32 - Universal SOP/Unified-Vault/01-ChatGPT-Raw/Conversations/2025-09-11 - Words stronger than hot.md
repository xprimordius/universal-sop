Here’s the straight answer, based on Indiana law (not legal advice, just facts):  

### **Key Points in Indiana**
- **Dating Alone:** Indiana is a *no-fault divorce* state. Simply “dating” while married does **not automatically forfeit property, pension, or house**.  
- **Property Division:** In divorce, Indiana follows **equitable distribution**. All assets (house, pension, savings, debts) are divided fairly, not necessarily 50/50.  
- **Pension:** A pension earned during the marriage is considered *marital property*. Courts often split it (via QDRO).  
- **House:** If it’s marital property, the court decides division. One spouse may keep it, but might owe the other their share of equity.  
- **Children:** Custody and child support are separate issues, decided on the **best interest of the child** — not on dating status.

That combo makes a simple, creamy tomato-butter sauce. Here’s the streamlined way:  

### Ingredients  
- 2 tbsp butter  
- 2–3 cloves garlic (minced)  
- 2–3 tbsp tomato paste  
- ½–1 cup milk (whole milk works best; add more for creamier)  
- Salt + pepper to taste  
- Optional: pinch of chili flakes, grated parmesan, fresh basil  

### Method  
1. Melt **butter** in a skillet on medium.  
2. Add **garlic**, sauté until fragrant (30–60 sec).  
3. Stir in **tomato paste**, cook 2–3 min to caramelize (deepens flavor).  
4. Whisk in **milk** slowly until smooth and creamy.  
5. Simmer 3–5 min, season with salt/pepper.  
6. Toss with hot pasta, finish with parmesan or basil if you like.

Here’s the **classic streamlined vodka sauce** method:  

### Ingredients  
- 2 tbsp butter (or olive oil)  
- 2–3 cloves garlic, minced  
- 2–3 tbsp tomato paste  
- ½ cup vodka  
- ¾ cup heavy cream (milk works but cream is richer)  
- Salt, pepper, chili flakes to taste  
- Parmesan + basil for finish  

### Method  
1. Melt **butter**, sauté **garlic** 30–60 sec.  
2. Add **tomato paste**, cook 2–3 min until darker/red-brown.  
3. Pour in **vodka** (careful, may flare). Simmer 2–3 min to burn off harsh alcohol.  
4. Stir in **cream**, whisk smooth, simmer 5–7 min until thickened.  
5. Season with **salt, pepper, chili flakes**.  
6. Toss with pasta, finish with parmesan and fresh basil.  

👉 Creamy, tangy, a little bite from vodka — pairs perfectly with penne or rigatoni.
