## 👤 You

For credit card i only need to pay the min balance to avoid interest right

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

No — that’s a common misconception.  

To **avoid interest charges**, you must pay the **full statement balance** by the due date.  

- **Minimum payment** → only keeps your account in good standing and avoids late fees, but the remaining balance accrues interest.  
- **Full statement balance** → avoids all interest (on most standard credit cards with a grace period).  
- **Anything in between** → reduces interest but does not eliminate it.  

⚠️ Exception: if you’ve already carried a balance into a new cycle, interest starts immediately and paying the full statement balance won’t erase the accrued interest from the previous cycle.

## 👤 You

So it gets interest if I carry a balance

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Exactly.  

If you **carry any balance** past the due date, interest starts applying to the unpaid portion.  
- Pay **in full** → no interest (grace period applies).  
- Pay **partial** → interest on the remaining balance.  
- Pay only **minimum** → most of the balance accrues interest, often at 18–30% APR.
