## 🤖 ChatGPT

It’s generally possible, but you may face a few considerations:

1. **Bank Policies:** Some financial institutions restrict linking an account that isn’t in the same business name. Confirm with both banks that external business accounts are allowed as payment sources.

2. **Name & Authorization Mismatch:** If your credit card is tied to “Biz 1” and the funding account is from “Biz 2,” the bank might flag a mismatch. They could require additional verification or documentation to ensure authorized use.

3. **Accounting & Compliance:** Paying a Biz 1 expense from Biz 2’s funds can complicate bookkeeping and tax reporting. For clarity, maintain thorough documentation and consult an accountant if necessary.

If permitted by the banks and handled with proper records, it’s safe. Just double-check policies, ensure both accounts are in good standing, and keep clear accounting records.
