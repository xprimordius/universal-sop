## 🤖 ChatGPT

On an iPhone, you can remove or edit zits (blemishes) using the built-in **Photos app** or a third-party app:

### Option 1: Built-in iPhone (Photos App)
1. Open the **Photos** app and select the picture.  
2. Tap **Edit** (top right).  
3. Tap the **healing tool** (bandage icon, appears in iOS 17+ under Retouch).  
4. Zoom in, tap or drag on the blemish — it auto-blends with surrounding skin.  
5. Tap **Done** to save.

*(If you don’t see a bandage/heal tool, your iOS may not be updated to iOS 17 — it’s a newer feature.)*

### Option 2: Free Apps (More Control)
- **Snapseed** (by Google, free) → “Healing” tool.  
- **Facetune** (free/paid) → “Retouch → Heal” or “Patch.”  
- **Adobe Photoshop Express** → “Spot Heal.”

These apps let you precisely tap blemishes and blend skin naturally.
