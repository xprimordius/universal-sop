## 🤖 ChatGPT

Those **numbers with a slash ( / )** in camera gear—like **24-70 mm f/2.8**—break down as follows:

---

### 📷 **Lens Focal Length (e.g. 24–70 mm)**
- **24–70 mm** means the lens can **zoom** from **24 mm wide-angle** (captures more scene) to **70 mm telephoto** (closer, more compressed look).  
- **Smaller number = wider field of view** (good for vlogs, landscapes).  
- **Larger number = more zoom** (good for portraits, detail shots).  
- A single number (e.g. **35 mm**) = **prime lens**, no zoom—usually sharper and brighter.

---

### 🌙 **Aperture (e.g. f/1.4 or f/2.8)**
- The **f-number** measures how wide the lens opens.  
- **Lower number → bigger opening → more light → better low-light & stronger bokeh.**
  - **f/1.2 – f/1.8:** Ultra-shallow depth, creamy background (best for talking heads, raves).  
  - **f/2.8:** Professional zoom standard—good balance of light and range.  
  - **f/4 or higher:** Smaller opening—needs more light, less background blur.

---

| Lens | Translation |
|------|--------------|
| **35 mm f/1.4** | Prime lens, medium-wide view, very strong blur, great for talking heads. |
| **24-70 mm f/2.8** | Versatile zoom for most scenarios; not as blurry as f/1.4 but flexible. |
| **70-200 mm f/2.8** | Telephoto for compressed, cinematic concert shots or far-away stages. |
