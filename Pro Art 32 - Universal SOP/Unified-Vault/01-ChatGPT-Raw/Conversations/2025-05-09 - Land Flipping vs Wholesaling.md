## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Top Auto-Dialer + Voicemail Drop Software (2025)**  
For bulk cold calling + voicemail drops to thousands, these are the most powerful and compliant tools:

---

### 🔹 **1. Slybroadcast** (Voicemail Drop Only)  
- **Function:** Sends **ringless voicemails** directly to inbox (no ring).  
- **Use Case:** Best for **non-intrusive** voicemail drops.  
- **Answer Behavior:** Bypasses ring → no issue if they answer (they won’t).  
- **Cost:** ~$0.10/drop, discounts at volume.  
- **Compliant:** Yes (TCPA compliant if opt-in or B2B landlines).

---

### 🔹 **2. Launch Control + Slybroadcast/REI Rail**  
- **Combo:** SMS/text platform + voicemail drops.  
- **Best For:** Real estate investors.  
- **Answer Behavior:** Use voicemail drops + trigger follow-up SMS or callback funnel.  

---

### 🔹 **3. Mojo Dialer** (Triple Line Dialer)  
- **Function:** Dials 3 numbers at once, auto drops pre-recorded message if no answer.  
- **Use Case:** High-speed calling (e.g. 300+ calls/hour).  
- **Answer Behavior:** Connects live to agent if someone picks up.  
- **Voicemail:** Drops pre-recorded message if no answer.  
- **Cost:** ~$149–$249/mo.  
- **Compliant:** Must manually filter DNC & opt-ins.

---

### 🔹 **4. CallTools** (Power Dialer + Voicemail Drop)  
- **Function:** Cloud-based auto dialer, live answer routing + pre-recorded VM drop.  
- **Answer Behavior:** Live handoff or drop message.  
- **Cost:** ~$99–$200/mo.  
- **Scalable:** Yes – team support.  
- **Built-in CRM.**

---

### 🔹 **5. SmartLine/Ringless Voicemail APIs (for devs)**  
- **Function:** Customizable API to auto-drop voicemail via backend.  
- **Best For:** Programmers or Zapier-style automations.  
- **Compliant:** Use B2B or opt-in lists.

---

### ⚠️ Important:
- **If someone answers,** systems like **Mojo, CallTools** connect you or a rep **live** — they **do not drop the voicemail**.
- **If no answer:** the system drops your **pre-recorded message**.
- **Ringless systems (e.g. Slybroadcast)** never ring — go straight to VM inbox.
- **Legal risk:** Avoid calling mobile DNC numbers unless **opt-in or B2B**. Consider using **consent forms, disclaimers**, or **commercial-only lists**.

### 🔁 **Automated Funnel Logic**
1. **Upload List** → scrub DNC or mobile-only if needed (Twilio + DNC.com API)
2. **Drop Ringless VM** → "Hey it’s Alan, I may have the wrong number, but wanted to ask about a property in [city]...”
3. **If Answered Live:**  
   - Route to you/rep → use a **soft, curious tone**:  
     > “Hey there! Just reaching out about a property in [city] — I may have the wrong number — are you the owner?”
4. **If No Answer:**  
   - Drop VM  
   - Trigger **text sequence** after 5–15 minutes (via GoHighLevel or Launch Control):  
     > “Hey [first name], I just left you a quick message. I might be interested in your property in [city] — is this the right number to text?”

---

### 🧠 **Live Answer Script Flow (Minimal Rejection)**
**Step 1: Disarm**  
> “Hey, sorry to bother — I might have the wrong number. Quick question though — are you the owner of the property at [123 Main St]?”

**Step 2: Qualify (if yes)**  
> “Got it — I help connect cash buyers with owners who might want to sell off-market. No pressure — have you ever considered selling, or getting a no-obligation offer?”

**Step 3: Exit Softly (if no interest)**  
> “Totally understand. I’ll mark you down as not interested. Thanks for your time.”
