The issue isn’t the shirt or tie itself — it’s **tie knot structure and collar spread alignment.**  
Here’s exactly what’s happening and how to fix it fast:

---

### 🔍 Problem:
- The **knot is too wide and loose**, causing it to bulge sideways under the collar.
- The **collar spread is narrow**, while your tie knot (likely a Windsor or similar) is too large for it.
- The **top button gap** and fabric tension make the tie puff out.

---

### ✅ Fix Options:

#### 1. **Use a Smaller Knot**
Switch from a **Full Windsor → Half Windsor or Four-in-Hand**.
- These are tighter and longer, fitting narrow collars perfectly.
- This will remove the “side bulge” and create a clean vertical drop.

#### 2. **Tighten and Center the Knot**
- Hold the narrow end and **pull down slightly** while **pinching the dimple** at the knot’s base.
- Ensure the knot **snugs up to the collar** — no visible gap.

#### 3. **Adjust Collar Fit**
- Button the top button and make sure the tie knot fully fills the space between collar points.
- If your shirt is slightly loose at the neck, consider **collar stays** or a **tie clip higher up** to keep structure.

#### 4. **Press or Steam the Tie**
- Lightly press the knot area with a steamer or low-heat iron to remove any creases from retying.

---

### ⚡Quick Recap:
**Problem:** Knot too big for collar  
**Fix:** Use Half Windsor or Four-in-Hand → tighten snugly → adjust collar
