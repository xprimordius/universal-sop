Make me a divorce plan with road map and plans we want to keep the house and we have 3 kids under the age of 7.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here’s a concise, strategic **Divorce Roadmap & Plan** — designed for clarity, control, and stability while keeping the house and prioritizing the kids.  

---

## ⚖️ Phase 1: Foundation & Legal Strategy
**Goal:** Set a calm, organized, and fact-based foundation.  

### 1. Collect Key Documents  
- Mortgage statements, deed, tax returns (last 3 years), pay stubs, bank/retirement accounts.  
- Household budget: income, expenses, childcare, health insurance.  
- Any prenup/postnup or agreements.  

### 2. Choose Legal Path  
**Options:**  
| Path | Description | Pros | Cons |
|------|--------------|------|------|
| **Collaborative Divorce** | Both hire cooperative lawyers, agree outside court | Keeps peace, saves money | Requires trust and transparency |
| **Mediation** | Neutral mediator helps reach agreement | Fastest, least costly | Only works if both act in good faith |
| **Litigation** | Judge decides | Necessary for high conflict | Expensive, emotionally draining |

→ **Recommendation:** Start with *mediation* or *collaborative* if possible.

---

## 🏠 Phase 2: Home Retention Plan
**Goal:** Keep the family home.  

1. **Determine Equity Split:**  
   - Get a home valuation.  
   - Calculate mortgage balance vs equity.  

2. **Plan for Affordability:**  
   - Show stable income covering mortgage + taxes + insurance.  
   - If needed, refinance under your name alone.  

3. **Negotiate Ownership:**  
   - Offer fair offset (e.g., other assets, 401(k) share) to buy out ex’s interest.  
   - Document agreement in property settlement.

---

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Phase 3: Parenting & Custody Plan
**Goal:** Maintain stability, protect the kids emotionally.  

1. **Custody Framework:**  
   - Aim for **joint legal custody** (shared decisions) + **primary physical custody** (kids mainly live with one parent).  
   - Example schedule: 4-3 rotation or alternate weekends with midweek dinner.  

2. **Parenting Provisions:**  
   - Keep kids in the same school district (stability).  
   - Shared calendars for holidays and breaks.  
   - No disparagement, consistent routines.

3. **Child Support:**  
   - Based on income, custody split, and local guidelines.  
   - Include health insurance, childcare, extracurriculars.

---

## 💰 Phase 4: Financial Structure
**Goal:** Ensure long-term financial safety.  

- Separate joint accounts.  
- Open new checking/savings in your name.  
- Create post-divorce budget.  
- Adjust insurance beneficiaries and estate plans.  
- Document all expenses related to children and property.

---

## 🌱 Phase 5: Emotional & Practical Stability
**Goal:** Smooth transition for everyone.  

- Family therapy or co-parent counseling.  
- Maintain consistent bedtime/school routines.  
- Communicate calmly in writing (e.g., email, apps like *OurFamilyWizard*).  
- Plan personal healing: gym, journaling, support network.
