## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Short answer: Yes — *you* are protected.**

**How Amazon handles this**
- **Amazon self-insures most shipments.** They don’t rely on UPS insurance the way individual shippers do.
- **If a return is damaged in transit, that’s *not on you*.**
- Once UPS marks it **“undeliverable / damaged”**, Amazon treats it as **carrier fault**, not customer fault.

**What this means for your refund**
- You **still get a full refund**.
- You **do not need to file an insurance claim**.
- Amazon handles UPS internally.
- You are **not charged**, penalized, or required to reship.

**What to do (minimal effort)**
1. Do nothing yet — refunds often auto-process once the scan posts.
2. If no refund **7–10 days after the return ETA**, contact Amazon and say:
   > “UPS marked my return as damaged and undeliverable. The tracking shows it’s returning to Amazon. Please process my refund.”

That’s it.  
You’re covered.
