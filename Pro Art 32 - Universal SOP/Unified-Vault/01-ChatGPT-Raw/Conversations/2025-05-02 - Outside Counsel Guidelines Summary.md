## 👤 You

What are outside counsel guidelines

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Outside Counsel Guidelines (OCGs)** are formal documents issued by a company to law firms they hire, outlining the rules, expectations, and requirements for legal services provided. They're used to control legal costs, ensure compliance, and streamline operations.

**Key components usually include:**

- **Billing Guidelines:** Hourly rates, block billing prohibitions, staffing expectations, pre-approval for expenses, invoice submission standards.
- **Conflicts of Interest:** Disclosure and resolution rules.
- **Matter Management:** Use of preferred tools or platforms (e.g., billing portals, document systems).
- **Diversity Requirements:** Expectations around staffing from diverse backgrounds.
- **Confidentiality & Security:** Data protection and information security protocols.
- **Communication:** Points of contact, reporting cadence, strategy alignment.
- **Compliance:** Adherence to company policies, regulatory obligations, and ethics rules.

**Purpose:**  
Reduce friction, prevent overbilling, enforce consistency, and align external law firms with the company’s internal policies and business goals.
