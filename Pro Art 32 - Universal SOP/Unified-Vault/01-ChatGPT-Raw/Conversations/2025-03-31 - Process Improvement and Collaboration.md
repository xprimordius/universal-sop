## 👤 You

What are refined carbs

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Refined carbs** are carbohydrates that have been **processed**, removing most of their **fiber, nutrients, and natural structure**. They're quickly digested, spike blood sugar, and promote fat storage—especially belly fat.

**Common examples:**
- White bread
- White rice
- Pasta (non-whole grain)
- Pastries, cookies, cakes
- Breakfast cereals (especially sweetened)
- Crackers, chips
- Sugary drinks and candy

**Better alternatives:**
- Whole grains (quinoa, brown rice, oats)
- Vegetables
- Legumes (beans, lentils)
- Fruits (in moderation)

**TLDR:** Refined = stripped + spiked insulin. Stick to whole, unprocessed carbs for fat loss and energy.
