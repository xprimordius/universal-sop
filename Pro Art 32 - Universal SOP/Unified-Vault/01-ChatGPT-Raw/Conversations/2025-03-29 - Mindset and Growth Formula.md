**"Hard work doesn’t create success. Alignment with success creates success. Hard work only gives you more chances to get into alignment."**

### 🧠 1. Mindset is the Operating System
- Your **mindset is a catalog of past experiences** that forms your default response to life.
- When you're operating in the *known* (things you've done before), experience is an asset.
- When in the *unknown* (new business, new venture), **mindset is the biggest limiting factor**.

---

### 🧭 2. Alignment Is Everything
- **Alignment = Certainty.**
  - If you're 100% certain the outcome is happening → you're aligned.
  - 99% certainty = misalignment.
- **Frame > Mindset > Action:**
  - Your **frame** (energetic frequency you hold) creates your mindset.
  - Your **mindset** (thoughts + beliefs) drives your actions.
  - Your **actions** then shape your external reality.

---

### 🔄 3. Frame Shifting Process
- Step into the **future version of yourself** who already solved the problem.
- Hold the *feeling* of that version, not the plan.
- Let the answers and strategy surface from that place of inner certainty.

---

### 🎯 4. Three-Question Goal-Setting Framework
1. **What do you want?**  
   (Based on past experience.)
2. **What is possible?**  
   (Based on what you've seen others do.)
3. **What feels impossible but would be fun anyway?**  
   (This is where intuitive growth lies—your soul's goal.)

---

### 🧱 5. Why Long-Term Goals Don’t Work (Anymore)
- **We’re evolving too fast.**
- Long-term (5–10 year) goals limit growth by anchoring to old frames.
- You can't set accurate long-term targets when you're growing fast.
- **Set 90-day “rocks”** instead. Fast feedback loops build confidence.

---

### 🔥 6. Burn the Boats Mentality
- Success is a result of **burning the backup plan**.
- “Dabblers don’t win.” Go all-in. Decide → Commit → Figure it out.

---

### 🧬 7. Build the Identity, Not Just the Plan
- Don’t try to build the plan before deciding.
- **Decide who you are becoming**, then act like it now.
- Identity-level changes > Strategy-level changes.
