## 🤖 ChatGPT

### FHA Loan Approval – Documentation Requirements

**1. Income Verification (Tax Returns & Pay Stubs)**  
- Typically **2 years of federal tax returns** (W-2s, 1099s if self-employed, plus business returns if applicable).  
- Recent **30 days of pay stubs** to show current income.  
- Lenders use this to calculate your **debt-to-income (DTI) ratio**.

**2. Bank Statements**  
- Usually **2 months of bank statements**.  
- Purpose: verify down payment source, reserves, and no undisclosed debt.  
- Large deposits must be documented.

**3. Employment Verification**  
- A written or verbal confirmation of employment from your employer is common.  
- Self-employed borrowers must show at least **2 years of consistent income** with tax filings and possibly a CPA letter.

**4. Stated Income**  
- FHA **does not allow true “stated income” loans.** Income must be documented and verified.  
- Only exception: in rare non-traditional situations, alternative documentation may be considered, but lender still must prove “ability to repay.”

**5. Credit Requirements**  
- **580+ FICO** = eligible for 3.5% down payment.  
- **500–579 FICO** = possible but requires 10% down.  
- Review of credit history, not just score.

**6. Assets & Down Payment**  
- Down payment can come from borrower savings, gifts from relatives, or approved assistance programs.  
- Must be sourced and documented.

---

✅ **Bottom line:** FHA loans require **full documentation** — 2 years tax returns, 2 months bank statements, pay stubs, W-2s/1099s, and verification of employment. No true “stated income” FHA loans exist.

### FHA Loan Without Tax Returns  

**General Rule:**  
- FHA **requires tax returns** if you’re self-employed, own rental properties, or rely on non-W2 income.  
- For **W-2 employees with straightforward income**, some lenders may approve with just:  
  - Recent **W-2s (2 years)**  
  - **30 days of pay stubs**  
  - **Verification of employment**  

**When Tax Returns Are NOT Required:**  
- You’re a W-2 employee with no significant side income.  
- You don’t own a business, rental property, or claim large deductions that affect income.  
- Automated underwriting system (AUS) accepts your file with W-2s and pay stubs alone.  

**When They ARE Required:**  
- Self-employed (≥25% ownership in business).  
- Commission income ≥25% of total income.  
- Rental income or other non-W2 sources.  
- Recent major job changes or inconsistent income.  

---

✅ **Bottom line:** Yes, you *can* get approved for an FHA loan without tax returns if you’re a straight W-2 wage earner, but **not** if you’re self-employed or rely on non-traditional income.

### How to Know if Lender Verifies Your W-2 With the IRS  

1. **Look for IRS Form 4506-C in Your Loan Disclosures**  
   - If the lender includes a **4506-C Request for Transcript** in your paperwork, they have the right to pull your W-2/tax return transcripts directly from the IRS.

   - FHA, Fannie, Freddie → most lenders use IRS transcript verification on **self-employed or complex income**.

3. **Ask Your Loan Officer Directly**  
   - They can tell you whether they run IRS transcript requests on all files or only when flagged. Policies vary by lender.  

4. **Signs They Did Verify**  
   - Delays while “waiting on IRS transcripts.”  
   - Requests to re-sign or correct a 4506-C.  
   - Explicit mention of “IRS transcript” in conditions.
