# Secretary Credit Impact C Corp

---

## 👤 You

If my secretary has bad credit, will that hurt my c corp if I’m doing the PG funding with good credit

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

No, your secretary's bad credit will not hurt your C corporation if you are the one providing the personal guarantee (PG) with your good credit. The personal guarantee is tied to the individual who is guaranteeing the loan or credit line, not to other employees or officers within the company.

In other words, as long as your credit is strong and you are the one signing as the personal guarantor, your secretary's credit score will not impact the corporation's ability to secure funding. The lender will primarily assess your creditworthiness, not that of your secretary or other employees.
