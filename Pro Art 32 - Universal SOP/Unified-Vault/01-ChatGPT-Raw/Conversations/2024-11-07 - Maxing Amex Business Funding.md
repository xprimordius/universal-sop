To invoice yourself between different LLCs with minimal fees across various bank accounts, consider the following tactics:

1. **Internal Invoicing Setup:**
   - **Invoice Structure:** Clearly define the transaction's purpose in your invoices. For example, use terms like "Management Fees," "Reimbursement for Expenses," or "Inter-Company Loan Repayment," depending on the nature of the transaction.
   - **Frequency:** To minimize transaction fees, consolidate multiple transactions into fewer, larger payments rather than frequent small ones.

2. **Bank Selection for Low Fees:**
   - **Same-Bank Transfers:** Open bank accounts for each LLC within the same bank or bank group. Transfers between accounts within the same bank often incur minimal or no fees.
   - **Banks with Free ACH Transfers:** Look for banks offering free ACH (Automated Clearing House) transfers, such as Novo, BlueVine, or other business-focused online banks. ACH transfers are generally free or very low cost.
   - **Avoid Wire Transfers:** Wire transfers tend to be expensive, so opt for ACH when possible, which can take 1-3 business days but is cost-effective.

3. **Use of Payment Platforms with Low Fees:**
   - **Bill.com or Veem:** These platforms provide a way to manage inter-company invoicing and typically have low ACH fees (or even free ACH options). They can also automate payments between different LLCs.
   - **Zelle:** If both accounts accept Zelle and it’s available for business use, Zelle allows for immediate transfers with no fee between linked bank accounts. Verify that your business accounts support Zelle, as it’s more common in personal accounts.

4. **Credit Union or Digital Bank Accounts:**
   - **Credit Unions:** Many credit unions offer lower transaction fees than traditional banks, especially for business accounts.
   - **Digital Banks:** Consider using digital banks (e.g., Mercury, NorthOne) that have low or no fees for intra-company transfers.

5. **Tax Considerations and Record-Keeping:**
   - **Document Each Transaction:** Ensure that each transfer is properly documented with an invoice and payment record. This is essential for tax reporting and audit purposes, especially if transactions are significant or frequent.
   - **Consult a CPA:** Ensure your approach is tax-compliant, as transactions between LLCs can impact taxes, especially if they’re used for loans or expense reimbursements.

By leveraging ACH transfers, using payment platforms that reduce fees, and maintaining clear documentation, you can transfer funds between LLCs with minimal costs effectively.
