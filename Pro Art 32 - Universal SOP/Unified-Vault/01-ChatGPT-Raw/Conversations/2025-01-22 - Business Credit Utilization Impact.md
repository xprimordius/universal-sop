## 👤 You

What percentage utilization on the business credit card side will start to negatively affect your business score

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

For business credit cards, **utilization above 30%** of your credit limit generally starts to negatively impact your business credit score. However, the ideal utilization rate is typically below **10%**, as this demonstrates strong financial responsibility and keeps your business credit score in optimal range.

Key considerations:
1. **Dun & Bradstreet (D&B Paydex):** Utilization isn't a direct factor for D&B, as payment timeliness has more weight.
2. **Experian & Equifax Business Credit:** These agencies factor in utilization, and exceeding 30% can lower your score.
3. **Credit Card-Specific Impact:** Some business credit card providers report usage to **personal credit bureaus**, so high utilization could also affect your personal score.

### Recommendation:
- Aim for **10% or less utilization** to maximize your score and financial reputation.
- Monitor reporting policies to understand how individual issuers affect your score.
