**FASTEST & MOST EFFECTIVE WAY TO REMOVE HARD INQUIRIES FROM TRANSUNION (2025)**

### ⚖️ LEGALLY VERIFIED METHOD — Highest Success Rate (Especially if Unauthorized)

#### ✅ 1. **Submit a *Permissible Purpose* Challenge**
- **Authority**: FCRA § 604(a), 15 U.S.C. § 1681b
- **Core Argument**: A hard inquiry requires a *permissible purpose*. If the company cannot **prove written authorization**, it must be removed.
- **Steps**:
  1. **Get a full report** from TransUnion with exact dates and sources of inquiries.
  2. **Send dispute via mail or online**, referencing:
     > *"Under FCRA § 604(a), I am requesting verification of the permissible purpose for the inquiry dated [XX/XX/XXXX] by [Creditor Name]. If you cannot provide documentation showing I gave written consent, this must be removed immediately."*

  3. Attach ID + proof of address.
  4. Track all communications.

#### ✅ 2. **File an Identity Theft Claim (Section 605B)**
- **Best for truly unauthorized inquiries (e.g., no account, no application)**
- **Authority**: FCRA § 605B (15 U.S.C. § 1681c-2)
- **Steps**:
  1. File FTC identity theft report: [https://www.identitytheft.gov](https://www.identitytheft.gov)
  2. Create police report (if willing—boosts success).
  3. Mail Section 605B dispute package to TransUnion:
     - Cover letter invoking 605B
     - Copy of FTC affidavit
     - Police report (optional but powerful)
     - 2 forms of ID
     - Copy of your credit report highlighting the inquiries

  4. TransUnion must block within **4 business days**.

#### ✅ 3. **Direct Creditor Challenge**
- Send a certified letter to each creditor who pulled the inquiry:
  > *“Under FCRA, please provide documentation showing I authorized this inquiry. If you cannot provide signed authorization, I request immediate deletion from all credit bureaus.”*

- Most companies drop the inquiry to avoid liability if it wasn't properly documented.
