## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here’s how to set your phone to automatically switch to **grayscale at 8pm every night**:

---

### **iPhone (iOS)**
1. Go to **Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Color Filters**.
   - Turn on **Color Filters** and select **Grayscale**.
2. Go to **Settings → Accessibility → Accessibility Shortcut**.
   - Choose **Color Filters** (triple-click side button to toggle grayscale).
3. Automate it:
   - Open **Shortcuts app → Automation → Create Personal Automation**.
   - Select **Time of Day → 8:00 PM → Daily**.
   - Add Action: **Set Appearance → Turn Color Filters On**.
   - (Optional) Add another automation for **8:00 AM → Turn Off**.

---

### **Android (Digital Wellbeing)**
(Steps vary by phone brand)
1. Go to **Settings → Digital Wellbeing & Parental Controls**.
2. Select **Bedtime Mode** or **Wind Down**.
3. Under **Screen options**, enable **Grayscale**.
4. Set schedule: **Start at 8:00 PM → End at your chosen time (e.g., 7:00 AM)**.

If your phone doesn’t have Bedtime Mode:
- Go to **Settings → Developer Options → Simulate color space → Monochromacy**.
- Use a **third-party automation app** (like Tasker) to schedule it at 8 PM.
