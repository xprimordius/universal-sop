## 🤖 ChatGPT

### Dangers of Eating Raw Eggs  

**1. Bacterial Risk (Salmonella):**  
- Raw eggs can contain *Salmonella enteritidis*.  
- CDC estimates ~1 in 20,000 eggs carry it.  
- Risk is higher in raw vs. cooked. Food poisoning = diarrhea, fever, cramps.  

**2. Biotin Deficiency (Avidin):**  
- Raw egg whites contain **avidin**, a protein that binds to biotin (vitamin B7).  
- Large/raw intake over time can cause biotin deficiency → skin, hair, nervous system issues.  
- Cooking deactivates avidin.  

**3. Reduced Protein Absorption:**  
- Studies show raw egg protein absorption ≈ **50%** vs. ~90% when cooked.  
- So raw = less efficient for muscle growth.  

**4. Allergic Reactions:**  
- Egg allergy (especially to whites) can cause hives, swelling, or worse.  

**5. Cholesterol Sensitivity:**  
- Yolks are high in cholesterol. Usually fine for healthy people, but if you have genetic lipid issues (e.g., familial hypercholesterolemia), raw yolk intake could worsen markers.  

---

✅ **Safer Ways if You Still Want the Benefits:**  
- Use **pasteurized eggs** (heated just below cooking temp — bacteria killed, nutrients intact).  
- Or swap for cooked eggs (better absorption, no Salmonella risk).  
- Supplement biotin if eating raw whites regularly.
