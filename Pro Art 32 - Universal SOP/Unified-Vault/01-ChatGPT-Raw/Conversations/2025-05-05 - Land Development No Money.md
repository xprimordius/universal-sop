**Yes, it's true — but only under very specific conditions.** Here's how it’s done:

### **Key Strategies to Earn 6–7 Figures with No Money Down in Land Development:**

#### 1. **Control, Not Ownership (Option Contracts or LOIs):**
   - Lock up land with **option agreements**, **purchase contracts with long due diligence**, or **letters of intent** (LOIs).
   - No upfront capital, just legal control.

#### 2. **Entitlement Arbitrage:**
   - You get the land **rezoned, permitted, or subdivided**.
   - This alone can **10x the land value**.
   - You then **flip the contract** or bring in a builder/developer who pays for the uplift.

#### 3. **Joint Venture with Builder/Capital Partner:**
   - You bring the deal; they bring the capital.
   - Split profits **(often 10–50%)**, depending on your role and value.

#### 4. **Assignment of Contract:**
   - Assign your right to purchase the land for a **six-figure assignment fee** after securing development approval or builder interest.

#### 5. **Land Assemblage Play:**
   - Tie up multiple parcels (e.g. rural → suburban) and **bundle for a national builder** or commercial developer.
   - You profit off the aggregate value.

---

### **Critical Factors for Success:**
- **Zoning knowledge**
- **High-growth area**
- **Builder or investor demand**
- **Development timeline risk management**
- **Strong negotiation and legal protection**

---

### **Examples:**
- Lock 10 acres under option for $1M → rezone → assign to developer for $2M → **$1M assignment fee**.
- Control a parcel for 6 months → entitle → JV with builder → exit at $10M valuation → **$500K–$2M profit share**.
