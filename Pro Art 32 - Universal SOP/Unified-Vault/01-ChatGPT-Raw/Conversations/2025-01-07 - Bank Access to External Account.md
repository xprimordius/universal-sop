## 👤 You

If I have an external account linked from one bank to another, can the bank see daily and current balances / activity etc

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

No, banks cannot typically see your daily or current balances, activity, or detailed transaction history in an external account, even if it is linked for transfers. They only have access to:

1. **Linked Account Info:** Basic account details required for the link (e.g., account number, routing number).
2. **Transfer Activity:** Transactions initiated through their platform (e.g., incoming/outgoing transfers).
3. **Balance at Transfer Time:** Some banks may request and display the external account balance at the time of setting up the link or during transfers for verification purposes, but they don't have continuous access to real-time balances or activity.

If you’ve shared login credentials through third-party services (e.g., Plaid), only then might detailed balance and transaction data be accessible to those services, not the bank directly.
