**The longest river in the world** is the **Nile River**, based on most measurements:

- **Length:** ~6,650 km (4,130 miles)
- **Location:** Northeastern Africa (flows through 11 countries, including Egypt and Sudan)
- **Mouth:** Mediterranean Sea

However, some argue the **Amazon River** in South America is longer:

- **Length (contested):** ~6,400–7,000 km (depending on source and tributary considered)
- **Amazon has the highest volume of water** and **largest drainage basin**.

**Summary:**
- **Nile = longest (most sources)**
- **Amazon = largest (by volume)**  
Both are top contenders depending on how length is measured.
