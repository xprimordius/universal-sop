# Fractional Reserve Banking Explained

---

## 👤 You

How does a bank loan out money to you and make 9x?

If they loan us $1000 they make $9000?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

The idea you're referring to is related to the concept of **fractional reserve banking**, but the interpretation of a bank making "9x" on a loan is often misunderstood. Here’s a breakdown of how it actually works:

### Fractional Reserve Banking:

1. **Initial Deposit**: When someone deposits $1,000 into a bank, the bank is required to keep only a fraction of this deposit as reserves. Let's assume the reserve requirement is 10%. This means the bank must keep $100 in reserve and can loan out $900.

2. **Loan Creation**: The bank loans out $900 to another customer. That customer might deposit this $900 into their bank account (either in the same bank or another bank). The bank (or another bank) then has $900 in new deposits.

3. **Repeat Process**: This new $900 deposit allows the bank to loan out 90% of it ($810), keeping $90 in reserve. This process repeats itself multiple times.

4. **Money Multiplication**: Through this repeated process, the banking system can theoretically create up to $9,000 in new money from the original $1,000 deposit, assuming the reserve requirement is 10%. This is known as the **money multiplier effect**.

### Bank’s Profit from Lending:

- **Interest Earnings**: Banks earn money by charging interest on loans. For example, if a bank loans out $1,000 at an interest rate of 5%, the borrower pays back $1,050. The bank's profit is the $50 interest minus any costs associated with the loan.

- **Loan Portfolio**: While the fractional reserve banking system allows the banking system to create additional money, the bank's profit primarily comes from the interest it earns on loans, not from "creating" 9x the money in profit. The "9x" refers to the potential increase in the money supply, not the bank's profit on a single loan.

### Clarification:

- **Banks Do Not Make 9x Profit on a Single Loan**: The concept of "making 9x" refers to the potential expansion of the money supply in the economy due to fractional reserve banking, not the bank’s profit margin on a loan. The bank earns from the interest on the loan, which is typically a percentage of the loan amount.

In summary, the bank does not directly make 9x the amount of the loan in profit. Instead, the money supply in the economy can multiply through the process of fractional reserve banking, while the bank earns interest on the loans it issues.
