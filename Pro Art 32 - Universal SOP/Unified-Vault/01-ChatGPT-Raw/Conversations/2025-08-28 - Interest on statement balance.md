---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

No—if you pay the **statement balance** in full by the due date, you will **not** be charged interest on purchases.  

Here’s the breakdown:

- **Statement Balance:** The amount shown on your statement. Paying this in full by the due date gives you the *grace period* benefit → no interest on new purchases.  
- **Current (Total) Balance:** Includes charges made after the statement closed. Paying only the statement balance means those new charges carry over, but they won’t accrue interest as long as you keep paying statement balances in full each month.  
- **Partial Payment (less than statement balance):** You lose the grace period and interest is charged on the *entire* remaining balance (including new purchases).  

✅ Rule of thumb: Pay at least the **full statement balance** each month to avoid interest.
