The Bible contains several references to astrology, generally portraying it in a negative light. Astrology, as the practice of interpreting celestial bodies to predict or influence events, is often associated with divination, which the Bible warns against. Key passages include:

1. **Warnings Against Divination:**
   - *Deuteronomy 18:10-12*: “Let no one be found among you… who practices divination or sorcery, interprets omens, engages in witchcraft, or casts spells.” These practices are called detestable to God.

2. **God's Sovereignty Over the Heavens:**
   - *Isaiah 47:13-14*: God rebukes Babylon for relying on astrologers and stargazers: “Let your astrologers come forward, those stargazers who make predictions month by month... Surely they are like stubble; the fire will burn them up.”

3. **Recognition of Celestial Order (but not for Divination):**
   - *Genesis 1:14*: God created celestial bodies “for signs and seasons,” indicating their purpose as markers of time, not tools for fortune-telling.
   - *Psalm 19:1-2*: The heavens declare God’s glory, suggesting that the stars are meant to point to God’s majesty, not to guide human decisions.

4. **Dependence on God, Not Stars:**
   - *Jeremiah 10:2*: “Do not learn the ways of the nations or be terrified by signs in the heavens, though the nations are terrified by them.” This emphasizes trust in God rather than celestial phenomena.

### Conclusion:
The Bible recognizes the celestial bodies as part of God's creation, meant to glorify Him and mark time. However, it consistently discourages using them for astrological purposes, associating such practices with idolatry and misplaced trust. Instead, it calls for reliance on God's guidance and wisdom.
