Why are homes built out of wood instead of steel

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Headline: Wood Wins for Cost, Speed, and Insulation**

**1. Cost Efficiency**  
Wood is much cheaper than steel. Lumber is abundant in North America, easy to transport, and requires less specialized labor or tools.

**2. Ease & Speed of Construction**  
Wood is lightweight and simple to cut, nail, and assemble—ideal for rapid framing and on-site adjustments. Steel requires welding, special tools, and skilled labor.

**3. Insulation Performance**  
Wood naturally insulates heat and sound better than steel, reducing energy costs and condensation issues. Steel conducts heat, demanding extra insulation.

**4. Flexibility & Resilience**  
Wood flexes under stress (like wind or minor earthquakes), reducing cracking. Steel can warp or buckle with heat and corrosion if not properly protected.

**5. Tradition & Building Codes**  
The U.S. housing industry evolved around timber framing—codes, materials, and labor markets are optimized for it. Steel framing is more common in commercial or high-rise builds.

**TL;DR:**  
Wood is cheaper, faster, more insulating, and easier to work with—making it the dominant choice for residential construction.
