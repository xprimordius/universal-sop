Liquidating a credit card balance into cash without fees can be challenging due to card issuer policies, but these methods may help reduce costs:

### Methods:
1. **PayPal or Square Transfer to a Trusted Person:**
   - Link your credit card to a **PayPal** or **Square** account.
   - Send funds to a trusted friend or family member who can withdraw it and give you cash.
   - **Risk:** Some platforms charge fees for credit card transactions (2.9% or more).

2. **Purchase a Money Order:**
   - Use your credit card to buy a **money order** at a vendor that accepts cards (e.g., Walmart, some grocery stores).
   - Deposit the money order into your bank account.
   - **Tip:** Ensure the vendor does not process it as a cash advance to avoid fees.

3. **Gift Cards to Resale:**
   - Purchase **high-resale-value gift cards** (e.g., Visa or Mastercard) using your credit card.
   - Sell the cards for cash on platforms like **Raise** or **CardCash**.
   - **Risk:** May lose a small percentage during resale.

4. **Venmo or Cash App:**
   - Link your credit card to **Venmo** or **Cash App**.
   - Transfer funds to a trusted recipient who can withdraw it for you.
   - **Check:** Confirm if the app allows transfers without fees, as policies may vary.

5. **Bank Direct Deposit via Plastiq:**
   - Use **Plastiq** to make a payment to yourself or a trusted account.
   - Deposit the transferred funds into your bank account.
   - **Note:** Some payments may be categorized as cash advances.

6. **Check Credit Union Policies:**
   - Some credit unions may allow card-funded **cash advances** with waived fees for members.
   - Explore membership at local credit unions with low/no-fee cash advance options.

7. **Refund Trick (High Risk):**
   - Purchase a refundable item using your credit card.
   - Return the item for a refund in cash or to a debit card.
   - **Warning:** This could violate card issuer policies and pose risks.

### Key Considerations:
- Always verify whether the transaction will be treated as a **cash advance**, as it incurs higher fees and interest rates.
- Maintain a trustworthy relationship with any intermediary.
- Avoid tactics that could breach your card's terms or result in account closure.
