What’s RMES framework

## 🤖 ChatGPT

The **RMES Framework** typically refers to four key principles in responsible AI and data use:

### **RMES = Responsible, Measurable, Explainable, Secure**

1. **Responsible**  
   - Ethical AI development and use  
   - Avoids harm, bias, and discrimination  
   - Complies with laws and respects rights

2. **Measurable**  
   - Outcomes and performance can be quantified  
   - Metrics are defined for accuracy, fairness, and impact  
   - Supports auditability and accountability

3. **Explainable**  
   - Outputs and decisions can be understood by humans  
   - Transparent logic or models behind results  
   - Enables trust and regulatory compliance

4. **Secure**  
   - Protects data and systems from breaches  
   - Ensures privacy and integrity  
   - Follows security best practices and encryption standards

This framework is used in AI governance, legal agreements, and compliance policies to ensure AI systems are safe, ethical, and trustworthy.

**Covenants** are **binding promises or commitments** made in a legal agreement. In contracts, they impose **obligations or restrictions** on one or both parties.
