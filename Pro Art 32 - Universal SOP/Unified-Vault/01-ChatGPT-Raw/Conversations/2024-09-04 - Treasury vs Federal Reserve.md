# Treasury vs Federal Reserve

---

## 👤 You

Does the U.S. treasure make the money?

My mentor says this from the U.S. government, but  thought they loan it from the fed who actually make it

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Your mentor is correct in that the U.S. Treasury plays a role in the creation of money, but it works in conjunction with the Federal Reserve (the "Fed").

Here’s the breakdown:

- **The U.S. Treasury** physically produces coins and paper currency. The Bureau of Engraving and Printing (a part of the Treasury) prints paper money, and the U.S. Mint produces coins.
- However, the **Federal Reserve** (a central bank, not part of the U.S. government in the same way) is responsible for managing the money supply and ensuring economic stability. The Fed controls the actual issuance of currency, which includes how much is circulated in the economy.

Essentially, the Treasury prints the money, but it is the Federal Reserve that decides how much money should be created and distributed to banks through lending and other financial activities.

In summary:
- **Treasury** = Prints and mints physical money.
- **Federal Reserve** = Controls the creation and flow of money in the economy.
