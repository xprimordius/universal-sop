## 👤 You

what are the top secondary bureaus that would hold information that would result in credit denials, as well as data furnishing that would tell banks about prior charge off history that is not on the credit report

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

The following secondary credit bureaus and data furnishers are key players that can hold information impacting credit decisions, including prior charge-off history that may not appear on the main credit reports from Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion:

---

### **Secondary Bureaus to Review for Credit Denial Data**
1. **LexisNexis Risk Solutions**  
   - **What They Hold:** Public records (bankruptcies, liens, judgments), prior charge-offs, address history, and identity verification.
   - **Impact:** Banks often check LexisNexis for fraud prevention, charge-offs, and public records even if they don't appear on primary credit reports.

2. **Innovis**  
   - **What They Hold:** Alternative credit history, past delinquencies, and prior credit inquiries.
   - **Impact:** Innovis data is often used in underwriting decisions, especially by smaller lenders and utility companies.

3. **SageStream (Now Part of LexisNexis)**  
   - **What They Hold:** Alternative credit scoring, past delinquencies, and financial behaviors.
   - **Impact:** Often used for credit card approvals, installment loans, and auto financing.

4. **CoreLogic Credco**  
   - **What They Hold:** Tenant screening data, eviction records, property records, and past charge-offs.
   - **Impact:** Used heavily in mortgage underwriting and tenant screening.

5. **ChexSystems**  
   - **What They Hold:** Banking history, including overdrafts, negative account closures, and charge-offs from bank accounts.
   - **Impact:** Affects new bank account approvals and impacts how lenders view financial responsibility.

6. **Early Warning Services (EWS)**  
   - **What They Hold:** Information on checking and savings account abuse, negative balances, and charge-offs.
   - **Impact:** Often used by banks to assess risk for opening accounts or approving credit.

7. **Teletrack (Owned by Equifax)**  
   - **What They Hold:** Alternative financial data, including subprime lending, payday loans, and past delinquencies.
   - **Impact:** Focuses on non-traditional credit and subprime credit activity.

8. **FactorTrust (Owned by TransUnion)**  
   - **What They Hold:** Short-term loan activity, payday loans, rent-to-own, and prior defaults.
   - **Impact:** Primarily used by subprime lenders.

---

### **Data Furnishers Tracking Prior Charge-Offs**
1. **National Consumer Telecom & Utilities Exchange (NCTUE)**  
   - Tracks utility, telecom, and subscription service accounts, including prior charge-offs.

2. **Clarity Services (Owned by Experian)**  
   - Focuses on subprime financial data and alternative credit histories, including charge-offs not reported to major bureaus.

3. **The Work Number (Equifax)**  
   - Employment and income data that might be factored into credit decisions.

4. **PRBC (Payment Reporting Builds Credit)**  
   - Tracks rent, utilities, and other non-traditional payment histories.

---

### **Steps to Mitigate Risks**
1. **Obtain Reports:**
   - Request your reports from these bureaus using their respective consumer portals to identify any negative data.
   
2. **Dispute Errors:**
   - Review the data for inaccuracies and dispute through the specific bureau if errors are found.

3. **Freeze Files:**
   - Freeze files with secondary bureaus to prevent unauthorized access by lenders (e.g., LexisNexis, ChexSystems, SageStream).

4. **Monitor Reports:**
   - Regularly monitor reports to catch new data that could negatively impact credit applications.

5. **Clean-Up Strategies:**
   - Work with creditors or use dispute templates to address prior charge-offs and request removal of inaccurate data.

---

These secondary bureaus can significantly impact your creditworthiness, especially in niche lending or financial situations. Reviewing and managing this data is essential for mitigating denials.

is the lexis nexus report the same thing as sage stream

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

No, the **LexisNexis** report and **SageStream** report are not the same, although they are closely related because LexisNexis acquired SageStream in 2021. Here’s the distinction:

---

### **LexisNexis Report**
1. **Data Scope:**
   - Contains comprehensive public records such as bankruptcies, liens, judgments, criminal records, and property ownership.
   - Includes identity verification data, prior addresses, and some alternative financial data.
   - Often used by lenders, landlords, and insurers for background checks, fraud prevention, and credit risk assessments.

2. **Focus:**
   - Broad, covering legal and public records, and alternative data.
   - Used in underwriting, fraud detection, and compliance.

3. **Access:**
   - Can be requested directly from LexisNexis via their **Consumer Portal**.

---

### **SageStream (Now Part of LexisNexis)**
1. **Data Scope:**
   - Focuses on alternative credit scoring and data, including subprime lending activity, payday loans, rent-to-own agreements, and telecom/utility payment history.
   - Includes data not typically found in major credit bureau reports, such as past delinquencies or short-term loan activity.

2. **Focus:**
   - Narrower and more specific to alternative financial data and credit behavior.
   - Primarily used for credit underwriting in niche markets, such as auto loans, credit cards, and subprime lending.

3. **Access:**
   - SageStream reports were historically available via SageStream directly, but since its acquisition by LexisNexis, you may now find this data included in LexisNexis consumer disclosures.

---

### **Key Differences**
| **Feature**              | **LexisNexis**                   | **SageStream**                     |
|--------------------------|----------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| **Primary Use**           | Public records and fraud checks | Alternative credit and risk scoring|
| **Data Type**             | Legal/public records, ID data   | Subprime credit behavior, niche data|
| **Scope**                 | Broad                          | Niche financial behavior           |
| **Access**                | LexisNexis Consumer Portal      | Historically SageStream, now LexisNexis|

---

### **Post-Acquisition Impact**
Since SageStream was acquired by LexisNexis, some SageStream data may now be folded into **LexisNexis Risk Solutions**. However, the **types of data** they focus on remain distinct, so requesting reports from LexisNexis should include SageStream-related data if it is applicable to your profile.

If you are concerned about how these reports affect you, it’s best to **request your full LexisNexis disclosure** to verify the complete scope of information held on you.
