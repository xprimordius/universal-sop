Here’s a quick **Taco Deviled Eggs** recipe that fuses classic deviled eggs with taco flavors:

### Ingredients
- 6 large eggs (hard-boiled)
- 3 tbsp mayonnaise (or Greek yogurt for lighter option)
- 1 tsp taco seasoning
- 1 tsp lime juice
- 1 tbsp finely chopped cilantro
- 1 tbsp finely chopped onion (optional)
- 2 tbsp shredded cheddar or Mexican blend cheese
- 1 tbsp salsa or pico de gallo (optional, for topping)
- Crumbled tortilla chips or crushed Doritos (for garnish)

### Directions
1. Hard boil eggs, peel, and cut in half. Remove yolks.
2. Mash yolks with mayo, taco seasoning, lime juice, cheese, and cilantro until smooth.
3. Spoon or pipe filling into egg whites.
4. Top with salsa/pico, crushed chips, or a sprinkle of extra taco seasoning.
5. Garnish with fresh cilantro or jalapeño slice for spice.
