If a suspension bar (sway/stabilizer bar) isn’t fixed:  

- **Reduced Stability in Turns** – More body roll, harder to control in sharp turns or evasive maneuvers.  
- **Increased Risk of Losing Control** – Especially in emergencies, high speeds, or wet/icy roads.  
- **Uneven Tire Wear** – Suspension stress shifts, wearing tires faster on one side.  
- **Extra Strain on Suspension Parts** – Bushings, shocks, and struts wear out faster.  
- **Possible Noise/Vibrations** – Clunking or rattling over bumps as broken links or bushings move.
