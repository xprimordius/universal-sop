# Hypoallergenic Golden Retriever Mixes

---

## 👤 You

hypoallergenic golden retriever mix no shed

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

If you’re looking for a hypoallergenic, low-shedding Golden Retriever mix, the following breeds and mixes are great options to consider:  

### 1. **Goldendoodle**  
- **Mix:** Golden Retriever + Poodle  
- **Shedding:** Minimal to none (depends on coat type, favoring the Poodle’s genes).  
- **Why:** Poodles have curly, hypoallergenic coats, making this cross a popular choice for families with allergies.  
- **Temperament:** Friendly, intelligent, and great with children.  

### 2. **Golden Wheaten (Soft-Coated Wheaten Terrier Mix)**  
- **Mix:** Golden Retriever + Soft-Coated Wheaten Terrier  
- **Shedding:** Low shedding and hypoallergenic.  
- **Why:** The Wheaten Terrier contributes a silky, minimal-shed coat.  
- **Temperament:** Affectionate and energetic.  

### 3. **Goldador-Poodle Mix (Double Doodle)**  
- **Mix:** Labrador + Golden Retriever + Poodle  
- **Shedding:** Low to minimal shedding (depends on Poodle genetics).  
- **Why:** The Poodle influence reduces shedding, making it allergy-friendly.  
- **Temperament:** Loyal, playful, and social.

### 4. **Golden Schnauzer**  
- **Mix:** Golden Retriever + Schnauzer  
- **Shedding:** Low to no shedding (Schnauzers are hypoallergenic).  
- **Why:** The Schnauzer’s coat makes it an appealing low-shed companion.  
- **Temperament:** Protective, intelligent, and friendly.  

### 5. **Goldendoodle x Cavapoo (Mini Double Doodle)**  
- **Mix:** Golden Retriever + Poodle + Cavalier King Charles Spaniel  
- **Shedding:** Very low to none (ideal for allergy sufferers).  
- **Why:** A small-sized hybrid with a soft, curly, hypoallergenic coat.  
- **Temperament:** Sweet-natured, social, and good with kids.  

When selecting a hypoallergenic mix, look for one with strong Poodle or Terrier genetics to ensure minimal shedding. Be sure to check with breeders about the puppy's coat type and allergy-friendly traits, as genetics can vary. Regular grooming is essential to maintain these breeds’ coats and reduce allergens.
