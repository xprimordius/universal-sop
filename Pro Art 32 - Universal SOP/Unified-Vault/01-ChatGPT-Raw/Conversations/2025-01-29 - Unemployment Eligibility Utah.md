1. **Compelling Personal Circumstances:**  
   - If the travel was necessary due to **a family emergency, medical care for a dependent, or other compelling reasons** beyond your control, you may have a case.

   - If your employer **refused reasonable alternatives**, this strengthens your case.

3. **Burden of Proof on You:**

     - Attempts to retain employment before quitting.
     - Any employer correspondence denying reasonable accommodation.

### **Potential Outcome:**

- If it is seen as a **personal choice without sufficient justification**, you will likely be **denied benefits**.
