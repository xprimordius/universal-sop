**Baseball Game Drinking Games (Live at the Stadium)**  
Fun, low-key games you can play while watching without drawing too much attention:

---

### 1. **"Beer Batter"**
- Every time a player with a low batting average comes up, finish your drink if they get a hit.

---

### 2. **"Strikeout Sip"**
- Take 1 sip for every strike your team gets.
- Finish your drink if your favorite player strikes out looking.

---

### 3. **"Foul Ball Shot"**
- If a foul ball lands near your section, take a shot or big chug.

---

### 4. **"Booze for the Bullpen"**
- Every time the bullpen starts warming up, everyone drinks until the pitcher is officially changed.

---

### 5. **"7th Inning Stretch Slam"**
- Slam a drink during or immediately after the 7th inning stretch song.

---

### 6. **"Mascot Mayhem"**
- Drink if the mascot appears on the screen or comes near your section.

---

Keep it fun, safe, and stadium-appropriate—pace yourself, and drink responsibly!

**Fun Drinking Games While Watching Pro Baseball**  
Perfect for game day with friends — fun, easy, and tailored to the natural rhythm of a baseball broadcast.

---

### 1. ⚾ **"At-Bat Action"**
**Rules**:  
- 1 sip for each pitch.
- 2 sips if it’s a strike.  
- 3 sips if it’s a foul ball.  
- Finish your drink if a home run is hit.

---

### 2. 🍻 **"Commentator Clichés"**
**Drink when the announcer says**:  
- "He's due."  
- "Small ball."  
- "He’s a five-tool player."  
- "Web gem."  
- "Can of corn."  

Bonus: Shot if they mention a player’s high school or family.

---

### 3. 🧢 **"Base Hit Bingo"**
Create a 5x5 bingo card with common baseball events:  
- Walk  
- Double play  
- Pitching change  
- Steal attempt  
- Mound visit  
**Take a sip when you mark a square.**  
**Win = everyone else drinks.**

---

### 4. 🍺 **"Beer Inning"**
- Each person is assigned an inning.  
- If something big happens in your inning (home run, triple, error), you drink.  
- Switch innings every 3 outs.

---

### 5. 🎤 **"Fan Cam Slam"**
- Drink when the Kiss Cam or Dance Cam appears.  
- Bonus: Take a shot if your group gets on TV.

---

### 6. 🍻 **"Bullpen Roulette"**
- Every time a relief pitcher is called in, pick a random drinker to finish their cup.  
- Use cards or a spinner to randomize.

---

### 7. 🥴 **"Umpire Rage"**
- Drink if the crowd boos a call.  
- Double drink if it results in a manager yelling or getting ejected.
