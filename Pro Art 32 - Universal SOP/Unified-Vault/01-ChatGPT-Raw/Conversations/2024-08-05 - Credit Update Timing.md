# Credit Update Timing

---

## 👤 You

If my creditor sent out a credit balance update to my bureau today, how long will it take to reflect back on Experian

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Credit reporting timelines can vary, but generally, it takes between 30 to 45 days for updates to reflect on your Experian credit report after a creditor sends an update. This timeframe can depend on the creditor's reporting schedule and the processing time of the credit bureau.

---

## 👤 You

How long does it take for Experian to process? Isn’t it daily?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Experian updates credit reports as they receive information from creditors, which can happen daily. However, the time it takes for a creditor's update to appear on your Experian credit report can still be influenced by when the creditor submits the update and the specific processing time involved. If a creditor reports daily and submits your update promptly, it could reflect on your credit report within a few days.
