### **How to Become an Apostle or a Member of the First Presidency in the LDS Church**

In **The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints**, apostles and members of the **First Presidency** are not elected or campaigned for; they are chosen through a process guided by **revelation and seniority** within Church leadership.

---

## **1. Becoming an Apostle**
### **Selection Process:**
1. **Revelation & Calling** – The President of the Church, with input from the **First Presidency** and the **Quorum of the Twelve Apostles**, seeks inspiration and revelation to choose a new apostle when a vacancy occurs (usually due to death).
2. **Sustaining & Ordination** – The new apostle is **sustained** by the Church membership in **General Conference** and then **ordained** by the First Presidency and Quorum of the Twelve.
3. **Lifetime Calling** – Apostles serve until they pass away, with no retirement.

### **Typical Background:**
- Apostles are often chosen from **seasoned General Authorities** (e.g., members of the **Seventy**) or from other **trusted Church leaders**.
- Many have **extensive experience** in missionary work, administration, and teaching the gospel.
- They are typically **well-known among Church leadership** due to their faithful service.

---

## **2. Becoming a Member of the First Presidency**
The **First Presidency** is the highest governing body of the Church, composed of:
- **The President of the Church (Prophet)**
- **Two Counselors**

### **Selection Process:**
1. **Presidency Dissolution & Reorganization**  
   - When the **President of the Church dies**, the **First Presidency is dissolved**, and leadership reverts to the **Quorum of the Twelve Apostles** (led by the most senior apostle).
   
2. **New President Chosen by Seniority**  
   - The **most senior apostle** (the one who has served the longest in the Quorum of the Twelve) is usually sustained as the next **President of the Church**.
   - He chooses two apostles (typically from the Twelve) to serve as his **counselors** in the First Presidency.

3. **Sustaining & Ordination**  
   - The new **First Presidency** is **sustained** by the general membership of the Church and then **set apart**.

### **Key Points About the First Presidency:**
- The **President of the Church is considered a prophet, seer, and revelator**, leading the entire Church under divine direction.
- The **counselors** assist in governance and administration but do not succeed the President upon his passing.

---

## **Summary of the Process**
| **Position** | **How Selected** | **Key Criteria** | **Who Selects** |
|-------------|----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Apostle** | Chosen by revelation when a vacancy occurs | Experienced leader, faithful service, inspiration | President of the Church & Quorum of the Twelve |
| **First Presidency (President + Counselors)** | Most senior apostle becomes President; he selects his counselors | Seniority in apostleship, trusted leadership | New President of the Church |

Since apostles are **called for life**, this system ensures continuity, stability, and unity in Church leadership.

### **Conclusion: Faith-Based vs. Historical Perspective**
