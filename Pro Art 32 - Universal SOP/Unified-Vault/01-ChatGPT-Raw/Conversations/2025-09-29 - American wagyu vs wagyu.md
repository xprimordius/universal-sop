Here’s the clear breakdown:

### **Japanese Wagyu**
- **Origin:** Purebred cattle from Japan (Kobe, Miyazaki, etc.).
- **Standards:** Strict genetic lineage, raised under tight regulations.
- **Marbling:** Extremely high, with fine texture and melt-in-your-mouth quality.
- **Price:** Very expensive due to rarity and import restrictions.
- **Labeling:** True “Wagyu” must be 100% Japanese-bred and verified.

### **American Wagyu**
- **Origin:** Crossbreed of Japanese Wagyu (usually Tajima/Kobe line) and American Angus.
- **Standards:** Less strict than Japan; regulated by USDA, but not as rigorous.
- **Marbling:** More marbling than standard U.S. beef, but typically less than pure Japanese Wagyu.
- **Taste:** Rich, buttery flavor with a balance of Wagyu tenderness and Angus beefiness.
- **Price:** Premium, but cheaper than authentic Japanese Wagyu.
- **Labeling:** “American Wagyu” usually means a crossbreed, not 100% Wagyu.
