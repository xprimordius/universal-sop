Feeling like you're about to pass out (near-syncope or lightheadedness), especially in someone with a history of **low iron**, could indicate:

### Most Likely Causes (esp. with low iron history):
1. **Iron-deficiency anemia** – Not enough oxygen delivery to the brain.
2. **Low blood pressure** (hypotension) – Can be triggered by standing up too quickly (orthostatic).
3. **Low blood sugar** – Common if she hasn’t eaten in a while.
4. **Dehydration** – Low fluid volume reduces blood flow to the brain.

### Other Possible Causes:
5. **Heart-related issues** – Irregular rhythms or structural problems.
6. **Vasovagal response** – Triggered by stress, pain, or standing long.
7. **Medication side effects** – Especially diuretics or blood pressure meds.

### Immediate Steps:
- Have her sit/lie down immediately.
- Elevate her legs.
- Hydrate with water or electrolyte drink.
- Eat something with iron + vitamin C if anemia suspected.
- Seek medical evaluation **urgently** if:
  - Chest pain, shortness of breath, palpitations.
  - Episode lasts more than a minute or is frequent.
  - She fully passes out or injures herself.
