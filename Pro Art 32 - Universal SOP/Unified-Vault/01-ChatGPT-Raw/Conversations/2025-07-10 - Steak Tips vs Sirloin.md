Can I cook tips just like normal steak medium rare salt pepper

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Yes, if they're from sirloin or a tender cut.**

### **Key Rule:**
- **Sirloin Tips:** Yes — Treat like steak.  
- **Flap/Other Cuts:** Sometimes tougher — May need marinade or quick sear + slice thin.

### **How to Cook Like Steak (Medium-Rare):**
1. Pat dry → Salt + pepper generously.
2. Sear on high heat (cast iron or grill) — ~2-3 min per side.
3. Check temp: **130–135°F** internal.
4. Rest 5 mins → Serve.

### **Tip:**  
If unsure of tenderness, test one piece first. If chewy, quick marinate or slice thin post-cook.
