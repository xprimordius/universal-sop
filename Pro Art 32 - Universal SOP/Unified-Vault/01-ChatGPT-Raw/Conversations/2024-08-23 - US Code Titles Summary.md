Title 31 and Title 15 of the United States Code (U.S. Code) cover different aspects of federal law, each focusing on specific areas:

### Title 31 - Money and Finance
- **Content Overview:** Title 31 of the U.S. Code governs federal financial matters, including the Treasury, public debt, and general financial management. It covers the creation, handling, and disbursement of public funds and the management of federal accounts.
- **Key Areas:**
  - **The Department of the Treasury:** Functions, powers, and duties of the U.S. Treasury, including currency and coinage.
  - **Public Debt:** Regulations concerning borrowing by the federal government and managing the national debt.
  - **Accounting and Budgeting:** Rules for federal financial accounting, auditing, and budgeting, including the preparation and submission of the federal budget.
  - **Public Contracts:** Regulations related to financial aspects of federal contracts, grants, and other obligations.
  - **Federal Financial Management:** Oversight and management of public funds, including appropriations and expenditures.

### Title 15 - Commerce and Trade
- **Content Overview:** Title 15 of the U.S. Code addresses laws related to commerce, trade, and various aspects of business regulation in the United States. It covers a wide range of topics, including antitrust laws, consumer protection, and the regulation of financial institutions and securities.
- **Key Areas:**
  - **Antitrust Law:** Regulations that prevent monopolies and promote competition in the marketplace.
  - **Consumer Protection:** Laws designed to protect consumers from unfair, deceptive, or fraudulent practices, including the Fair Credit Reporting Act and the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act.
  - **Trade and Commerce:** Regulations on trade practices, export and import controls, and standards for business operations.
  - **Securities Regulation:** Rules governing the issuance and trading of securities, including the Securities Exchange Act and the role of the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).
  - **Banking and Financial Institutions:** Laws related to the regulation of banking practices and financial institutions, including aspects of credit and loans.

These titles play crucial roles in the regulation and management of financial and commercial activities in the United States, ensuring that there is a structured legal framework for both government financial operations and private sector commerce.
