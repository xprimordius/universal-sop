  \[

  \]

- **Area of two 12-inch pizzas**:  
  \[
  2 \times 36\pi = 72\pi \approx 226.2 \text{ in}^2
  \]

- **Area of one 16-inch pizza** (radius \(= 8\) inches):  
  \[
  \pi \times 8^2 = 64\pi \approx 201.1 \text{ in}^2
  \]

Therefore:
- **2×12" pizzas** total about **226.2 in²**.  
- **1×16" pizza** totals about **201.1 in²**.
