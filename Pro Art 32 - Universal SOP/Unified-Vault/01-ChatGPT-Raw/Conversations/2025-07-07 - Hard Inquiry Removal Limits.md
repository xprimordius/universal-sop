---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Quick Answer:** No, you cannot legally remove *hard inquiries* from *open accounts* using **FCRA Section 605B** (Identity Theft Block) or **human trafficking protections** under FCRA **Section 605C**.

### Here’s Why:
1. **605B (Identity Theft Block):**  
Only applies to **fraudulent accounts, charges, or tradelines**—not hard inquiries tied to *valid open accounts*.  
TransUnion can lawfully deny removal if:
- The account is open in your name.
- The inquiry resulted from an account you authorized (even under pressure).
- You did not file a valid police report showing ID theft for that *specific inquiry*.

2. **605C (Human Trafficking Protections):**
Removes data from reports caused by trafficking but:
- Requires a *certified trafficking victim determination* (by court, government, or authorized entity).
- Only applies to negative items directly stemming from trafficking.
- You must prove *each item* resulted from trafficking—general removal isn’t allowed.

3. **Hard Inquiries on Open Accounts:**
They’re tied to an *existing relationship* (the account itself), which makes them *legally reportable* in most cases.
Even with trafficking status, if you kept the account, they may refuse removal.

---

### ✅ What You *Can* Do:
1. **Close the Account** (if not essential).
2. Re-submit the dispute after closure + trafficking documentation.
3. Demand the *inquiry* be blocked as part of post-trafficking relief (if account tied to trafficking).
4. Consult a consumer rights attorney for case-specific guidance.

---

### ⚠️ Key Insight:
Inquiries are tricky because if the account remains open, bureaus can argue there’s a “legitimate relationship.”  
Removing them generally requires either:
- Closing the account first.
- Proving the entire account stems from trafficking or identity theft.
