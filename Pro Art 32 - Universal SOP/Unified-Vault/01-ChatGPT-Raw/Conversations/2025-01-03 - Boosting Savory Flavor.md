## 🤖 ChatGPT

To elevate the savoriness and create a balanced, addictive flavor profile, you can consider these adjustments:

1. **Add Umami Boosters**:
   - **Soy Sauce or Tamari**: Adds depth and enhances the savoriness.
   - **Miso Paste**: A small amount will provide a rich umami flavor.
   - **Anchovy Paste**: Complements the fish sauce and boosts savoriness.

2. **Acidity for Balance**:
   - **Rice Vinegar**: Balances the sweetness and enhances flavors.
   - **Lime Juice**: Adds brightness and complements the fish sauce.

3. **Aromatics**:
   - **Fresh Garlic (Minced)**: Sautéed for a richer, savory base.
   - **Ginger**: Adds warmth and depth to the flavor.

4. **Depth of Flavor**:
   - **Sesame Oil**: A few drops for a nutty, toasty undertone.
   - **Dark Soy Sauce**: Adds color and a deeper savory taste.

5. **Optional Additions**:
   - **MSG or Umami Seasoning**: For a savory punch if you're comfortable using it.
   - **Shallots**: Crispy shallots add sweetness and depth.

### Method:
1. Sauté garlic (and optionally ginger) in avocado/olive oil until fragrant.
2. Mix fish sauce, soy sauce, and a small amount of miso paste with water.
3. Stir in honey, sugar, and vinegar or lime juice for a balanced sweet-sour note.
4. Combine with the chili flakes mixture and adjust seasoning to taste.

Taste frequently to adjust sweetness, saltiness, and acidity until the desired flavor is achieved.

To deep-fry chicken wings in avocado oil:

1. **Temperature**: Heat the oil to **350°F (175°C)**.
2. **Time**: Fry the wings for **8-10 minutes** until golden brown and fully cooked (internal temperature of 165°F/74°C).
3. **Tips**:
   - Avoid overcrowding the fryer to ensure even cooking.
   - Drain on a wire rack or paper towels to keep them crispy.

Check for doneness using a meat thermometer. Adjust time slightly based on wing size.
