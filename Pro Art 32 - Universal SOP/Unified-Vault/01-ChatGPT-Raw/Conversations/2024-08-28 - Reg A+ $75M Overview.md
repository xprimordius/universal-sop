**Regulation A+ (Reg A+) Overview:**

Regulation A+, often referred to as a "mini-IPO," is a type of offering that allows companies to raise capital from the general public with less regulatory burden than a traditional IPO. Under Reg A+, companies can raise up to $75 million in a 12-month period.

**Key Points About Reg A+ $75M:**

1. **Two Tiers:**
   - **Tier 1:** Allows for raising up to $20 million in a 12-month period.
   - **Tier 2:** Allows for raising up to $75 million in a 12-month period. Companies using Tier 2 must provide audited financial statements, file annual, semiannual, and current event reports, and comply with more rigorous investor protection rules.

2. **Eligibility:**
   - Companies must be U.S. or Canadian entities.
   - Companies cannot be in the business of blank check companies, certain investment companies, or those with a disqualified status.

3. **Advantages:**
   - Access to public capital markets with fewer restrictions than a full IPO.
   - Ability to market to both accredited and non-accredited investors.
   - Reduced disclosure requirements compared to traditional IPOs.

4. **Requirements:**
   - Must file an offering statement on Form 1-A with the SEC, which includes financial statements, risk factors, and other relevant company information.
   - Tier 2 offerings require ongoing reporting, including annual reports on Form 1-K, semiannual reports on Form 1-SA, and current reports on Form 1-U.

5. **Investment Limits:**
   - For Tier 2 offerings, non-accredited investors are limited in how much they can invest, usually up to 10% of their annual income or net worth.

6. **Use of Funds:**
   - Funds can be used for various purposes, such as business expansion, research and development, or debt repayment.

7. **Costs and Timing:**
   - Costs include legal, accounting, and SEC filing fees, which can range from hundreds of thousands to over a million dollars.
   - The timeline from preparation to approval can range from several months to a year.

8. **Liquidity:**
   - Shares sold under Reg A+ can be freely traded after the offering, providing liquidity to investors.

Reg A+ $75M offerings are ideal for companies looking to raise significant capital while maintaining flexibility and access to a broad investor base without the full burden of a traditional IPO.

**Regulation Crowdfunding (Reg CF) Overview:**

Regulation Crowdfunding (Reg CF) allows smaller companies to raise capital from a large number of investors through online crowdfunding platforms. Under Reg CF, companies can raise up to $5 million in a 12-month period.

**Key Points About Reg CF $5M/year:**

1. **Eligibility:**
   - Available to U.S.-based companies.
   - Companies must not have exceeded the $5 million cap in the past 12 months.
   - Companies cannot be non-U.S. entities, investment companies, or entities disqualified under SEC rules.

2. **Funding Limit:**
   - Companies can raise up to $5 million from accredited and non-accredited investors within a 12-month period.

3. **Investor Limits:**
   - Non-accredited investors are limited in the amount they can invest in Reg CF offerings based on their annual income or net worth.
   - Investors can invest up to:
     - 5% of their annual income or net worth (whichever is greater) if either is below $124,000.
     - 10% of their annual income or net worth (whichever is greater) if both exceed $124,000.
   - The maximum investment limit for non-accredited investors is $124,000 per 12-month period.

4. **Disclosure Requirements:**

   - Financial statement requirements vary based on the amount being raised:
     - Up to $107,000: Financial statements certified by the company's principal executive officer.
     - $107,000 - $535,000: Financial statements reviewed by an independent public accountant.
     - $535,000 - $5 million: Financial statements audited by an independent public accountant.

5. **Ongoing Reporting:**
   - Companies must file annual reports on Form C-AR with the SEC, updating financial information and other disclosures.

6. **Platform Use:**

   - The platform handles the regulatory compliance, transaction processing, and communications with investors.

7. **Advertising and Promotion:**

   - If the company fails to meet its funding target, all funds are returned to investors.

9. **Costs and Timeline:**
   - Costs can include platform fees, legal fees, and accounting fees, which can vary depending on the size of the offering and the platform used.
   - The timeline for launching a Reg CF campaign can range from a few weeks to several months, depending on the company’s readiness and the platform’s requirements.

10. **Liquidity:**
    - Shares purchased in a Reg CF offering are subject to resale restrictions, typically locked in for one year, limiting liquidity.

**Strategic Use of Reg CF:**
- Reg CF is ideal for startups and small businesses looking to raise up to $5 million while building a community of engaged investors. It’s also a way to test market interest before pursuing larger funding rounds.

Reg CF provides an accessible way for companies to raise funds from a broad base of investors while adhering to SEC regulations tailored for smaller offerings.
