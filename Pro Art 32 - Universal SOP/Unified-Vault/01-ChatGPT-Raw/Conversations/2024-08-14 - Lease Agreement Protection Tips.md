For extended legal documents that include additional pages or exhibits beyond the main body of the contract, the standard professional notation typically includes the page number along with an indication that these are part of an extended or additional section. Here are a few common practices:

1. **For the Main Document:**
   - "Page 1 of X" (where X is the total number of pages in the main document, including all sections).

2. **For Exhibits or Attachments:**
   - "Exhibit A - Page 1 of Y" (where Y is the total number of pages in the exhibit).
   - "Attachment 1 - Page 1 of Y"
   - "Appendix A - Page 1 of Y"

3. **For an Extended Section of the Main Document:**
   - "Extended Page 1 of X"
   - "Additional Page 1 of X"
   - "Supplemental Page 1 of X"

4. **For Additional or Combined Pages:**
   - "Document Continuation - Page 1 of X"

The key is to make sure that the pagination is clear and consistent, indicating the specific section or type of document (e.g., Exhibit, Attachment, etc.), which helps in ensuring that all parts of the document are recognized as part of the legally binding agreement.
