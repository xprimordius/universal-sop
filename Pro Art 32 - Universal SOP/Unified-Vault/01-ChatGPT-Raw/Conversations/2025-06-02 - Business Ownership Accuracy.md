## 👤 You

is it possible and acceptable to state on a business CC application that the business Years under current ownership older than the establish date of the business? 7 vs 3 years if I've been doing consulting for years?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**No**, it is **not acceptable** to list **years under current ownership (7)** if the **legal business entity was established only 3 years ago**, **unless**:

---

### ✅ **You Meet One of These Conditions:**

1. **The business was a sole proprietorship before** and **you incorporated or formed an LLC later**, **but it was the same business activity and EIN** (or you can clearly document continuity).
   
2. You **acquired** the business from a previous owner **and** have documentation of the **prior existence and continuity of operations** under your ownership.

3. You are asked about **industry experience** or **years of relevant work**, in which case your **7 years consulting** is valid (but that’s **not** the same as "years under ownership").

---

### ⚠️ Risk:
Falsely stating **7 years of ownership** when the **business was legally formed only 3 years ago** can be flagged as misrepresentation, especially if the underwriter cross-checks the **SOS registration date**.

---

### ✅ Alternative (Accurate) Approach:
- **Years in Industry/Experience:** 7  
- **Years Under Current Ownership:** 3  
- **Business Established Date:** 3 years ago

You can also **note in a business profile or explanation**:  
> "The owner has 7 years of experience in this field, including consulting prior to formal entity formation."

This builds credibility **without risk**.

---

TLDR:  
✅ You can show 7 years of experience.  
❌ You **cannot claim 7 years of ownership** if the business entity is only 3 years old unless it's a legal continuation.
