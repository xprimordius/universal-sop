## 👤 You

ASUS IPS 4K vs QDOLED

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**TLDR:**  
**QDOLED > IPS 4K** in contrast, black levels, color vibrancy, and HDR.  
**IPS 4K > QDOLED** in price, static image safety, and bright office settings.

---

### **QDOLED (Quantum Dot OLED):**
- **Pros:**
  - Deep, pure blacks (self-emissive pixels).
  - Extremely vibrant colors with quantum dot enhancement.
  - Superior contrast and HDR experience.
  - Near-instant response time (ideal for gaming/visuals).

- **Cons:**
  - Risk of image retention (burn-in) with static UI.
  - More expensive.
  - Slightly lower peak brightness vs some miniLEDs in daylight.

---

### **IPS 4K (In-Plane Switching):**
- **Pros:**
  - No burn-in risk (safe for productivity, static work).
  - Generally cheaper.
  - More consistent brightness in well-lit environments.

- **Cons:**
  - Lower contrast (washed-out blacks).
  - Less color depth and vibrancy.
  - Slower response time, more motion blur.

---

### **Verdict:**
- **Creative/Gaming/Media:** QDOLED wins (deeper blacks, stunning color, better HDR).
- **Office/Productivity/Static UI Use:** IPS safer and more cost-effective.
- **If money no object & you’re careful with static images:** **QDOLED is best-in-class.**
