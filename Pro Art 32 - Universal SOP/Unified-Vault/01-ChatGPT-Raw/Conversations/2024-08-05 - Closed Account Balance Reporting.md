# Closed Account Balance Reporting

---

## 👤 You

Does a closed credit account with a balance report the balance being paid down each month even if it is not active / open?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes, a closed credit account with a balance will still report the balance being paid down each month. Even though the account is not active or open, the creditor will continue to report the payments made towards the balance to the credit bureaus. This ongoing reporting will reflect the decreasing balance as you make payments, which can positively impact your credit utilization ratio and overall credit score over time.
