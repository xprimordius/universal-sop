---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

There have been discussions and concerns about the presence of microplastics in bottled water, including premium brands like Icelandic Glacial Water. Microplastics are tiny plastic particles that have been found in many bottled water brands due to factors like plastic packaging or environmental contamination.

While Icelandic Glacial Water is marketed as pure and sourced from Iceland’s Ölfus Spring, studies on bottled water in general have revealed that microplastics are nearly ubiquitous in the bottled water industry. A 2018 study published by *Orb Media* found that 93% of tested bottled water brands contained microplastics, though individual brands were not always the focus.

For Icelandic Glacial Water specifically, there hasn't been widely publicized research singling out its microplastic content. However, the overall concern about microplastics in bottled water suggests that it might not be entirely exempt from this issue, particularly if bottled in plastic.

If you're looking to minimize microplastic intake, switching to filtered tap water using high-quality filters or stainless steel bottles might be an alternative.
