# Women Shareholding Requirements

---

## 👤 You

is it true that women need to have majority of shares?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

No, it is not true that women need to have the majority of shares in a business unless there is a specific requirement or incentive tied to ownership, such as certain grants, loans, or certifications designed for women-owned businesses. 

In general, shareholding can be distributed in any way the owners agree upon, regardless of gender. However, if the business wants to qualify as a "women-owned business" for programs like the Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB) certification in the U.S., women must own at least 51% of the business. These certifications can provide access to government contracts, grants, and other opportunities specifically designed for women entrepreneurs. 

The need for women to hold the majority of shares only applies in contexts where gender-based ownership is a condition for certain benefits or opportunities.
