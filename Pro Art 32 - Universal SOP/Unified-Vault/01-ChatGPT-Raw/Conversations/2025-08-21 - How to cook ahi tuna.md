## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here’s the **quick master guide** to cooking ahi tuna:

### 1. **Sear (Most Popular)**
- **Prep:** Pat tuna steaks dry. Rub with olive oil, salt, pepper, optional sesame seeds.
- **Pan:** Heat cast-iron or stainless pan until *smoking hot*.
- **Cook:** Sear **45–60 sec per side** for rare/med-rare (center still raw). Add 15–30 sec if you want more cooked.
- **Finish:** Slice against the grain. Serve with soy sauce, ponzu, or wasabi mayo.

### 2. **Grill**
- **Prep:** Same as above.
- **Heat:** Grill on high.
- **Cook:** **1–2 min per side** for seared, **3–4 min per side** for medium.
- **Pro Tip:** Oil grates well to prevent sticking.

### 3. **Raw / Poke / Sushi**
- **Requirement:** Must be *sushi-grade* ahi.
- **Serve:** Dice into cubes. Mix with soy, sesame oil, green onion, chili flakes, avocado.

### 4. **Oven (Least Common)**
- Preheat to 450°F. Bake 8–10 min for medium. Risk of drying out—best brushed with marinade.

---

🔥 **Key Rule:** Ahi is best *rare to medium-rare*. Overcooking = dry, chalky texture.
