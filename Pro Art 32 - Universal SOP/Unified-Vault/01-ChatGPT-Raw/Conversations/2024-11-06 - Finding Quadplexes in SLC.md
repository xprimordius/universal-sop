Yes, it is possible to secure a $0 down new construction loan for a quadplex, but it requires strategic planning and typically involves one of these methods:

1. **VA Loans**: If you’re a qualified veteran, a VA loan might allow you to finance a multi-family property (up to four units) with 0% down, provided you will occupy one of the units. However, VA loans for new construction can be more restrictive, so you may need to work with a builder who is approved for VA financing.

2. **USDA Loans**: In rural or certain suburban areas, USDA loans can offer 100% financing, though they’re often limited to single-family homes. In rare cases, multi-family properties may be considered if you meet USDA criteria and the property is in an eligible area.

3. **FHA Construction Loans (FHA 203(k) or 203(b))**: These loans can sometimes work for 4-unit properties if you occupy one unit. However, FHA loans require a minimum down payment (typically around 3.5%), but you might be able to offset it with seller concessions, grants, or down payment assistance programs.

4. **Commercial Lenders with Flexible Programs**: Some lenders offer $0 down financing options for quadplexes if you bring additional assets as collateral or a guarantor. While not always common, these lenders may be more flexible with a strong personal or business credit history and financial profile.

5. **Private Investors and Joint Ventures**: Partnering with an investor who funds the down payment and/or construction costs in exchange for equity or a share of rental income can also make a $0 down investment possible.

### Key Considerations:
- **Higher Interest Rates and Closing Costs**: $0 down loans often carry higher rates or fees.
- **Strong Financial and Credit Profile**: These loans usually require excellent credit, consistent income, and/or personal assets to mitigate lender risk.
- **Occupancy Requirements**: Many programs require you to live in one of the units for at least a year.

While $0 down options exist, working closely with an experienced mortgage broker and real estate advisor will help you navigate complex qualifications and maximize your chance of approval.
