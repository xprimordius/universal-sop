# Business Name Consistency

---

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

For legal and official documentation, such as employment records, it’s generally advisable to use the exact name of the business as registered, which in this case would be "SKYWARD USA, INC." This ensures consistency and avoids any potential confusion or legal discrepancies. 

However, for less formal contexts, such as a resume or LinkedIn profile, you might use "Skyward USA" as long as it's clear and recognizable, but it's typically best to match the registered name when possible.
