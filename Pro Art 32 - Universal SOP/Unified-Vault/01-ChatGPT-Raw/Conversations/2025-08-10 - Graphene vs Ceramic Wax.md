Yes — **Adam’s Graphene Spray** can be applied to **glass** (windshield, side windows, mirrors) — it works similarly to Rain-X but with longer-lasting hydrophobicity and slightly better chemical resistance.  

---

## **Why Precise Buff Timing Matters**
Adam’s graphene formula flashes (dries) faster than many sprays. If you:
- **Buff too soon** → You may remove too much product, reducing protection.
- **Buff too late** → It starts to harden, leaving streaks or a rainbow haze that’s harder to remove.

This is especially true in **hot, sunny, or humid** conditions where flash time shortens.

---

## **Proper Application Method**
**Best Conditions:** Shade, cool panel, low wind, moderate humidity.

1. **Prep the Surface**  
   - Wash, dry, and (if possible) decontaminate.  
   - If applying over old wax/coating, clean with an IPA/water mix for maximum bonding.

2. **Light Mist**  
   - Spray **onto a microfiber**, not directly on glass/paint (prevents overspray and high spots).

3. **Spread Evenly**  
   - Wipe in a crosshatch pattern (horizontal + vertical passes).

4. **Wait for Initial Flash**  
   - Watch for a light rainbow sheen or faint haze — usually **30–60 seconds** in cool shade, faster in heat.

5. **Buff to Clarity**  
   - Use a second, clean, plush microfiber to lightly buff until crystal clear.

6. **Cure Time**  
   - Keep the surface dry for **at least 4 hours**, ideally 12–24 hours for full bonding.

---

**Extra Tip for Glass:**  
Use even less product than on paint — too much can cause wiper chatter until it wears in. A *very* thin coat gives all the hydrophobic benefits without residue.
