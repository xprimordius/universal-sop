Here’s how to manually set **aperture** on a **Sony A7 IV (α7 IV)**:  

---

### 🎯 **If Using a Lens with an Aperture Ring**
1. **Set the lens ring** to your desired f-stop (e.g., f/2.8).  
2. Make sure the ring is not on **“A”** (Auto).  
3. The camera will use that aperture value directly.

---

### 🎯 **If Using a Lens Without an Aperture Ring**
1. Turn the **Mode Dial** to **A (Aperture Priority)** or **M (Manual Exposure)**.  
2. Use the **Front Dial** (near the shutter button) to adjust the **aperture (f-stop)**.  
   - If it adjusts shutter instead, go to:
     **Menu → Setup → Operation Customize → Dial Setup**  
     and assign “Aperture” to the **Front Dial** (or Rear Dial if preferred).  

---

### 🔧 **In Manual Mode (M)**
- **Front Dial** = aperture  
- **Rear Dial** = shutter speed  
- **ISO** can be adjusted via the ISO button or control wheel.

---

### 🧠 **Tip**
If the camera doesn’t let you change aperture:
- Ensure the **lens is electronic** and fully mounted.  
- Some lenses (manual/third-party) require switching the camera to **“Release w/o Lens = Enable”** under  
  **Menu → Setup → Release w/o Lens**.
