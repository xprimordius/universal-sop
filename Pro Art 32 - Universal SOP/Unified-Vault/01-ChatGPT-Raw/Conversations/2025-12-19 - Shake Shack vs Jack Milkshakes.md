## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Custard — the clean definition**

**What it is**
- **Custard = dairy + sugar + eggs** (specifically **egg yolks**).

**What makes it different**
- **Egg yolks** are the key. They add:
  - Richness  
  - Creamier, denser texture  
  - Smoother mouthfeel  

**Frozen custard (what Shake Shack uses)**
- A type of ice cream **with egg yolks added**.
- Churned warmer and served fresher → **softer, silkier, richer** than regular ice cream.

**Custard vs Ice Cream (quick contrast)**
- **Ice cream:** milk + cream + sugar (no eggs required)  
- **Custard:** milk + cream + sugar **+ egg yolks**

**Why people like custard**
- More indulgent  
- Less icy  
- Feels “luxury” and dessert-forward  

**Bottom line**
- Custard = **ice cream upgraded with eggs** for richness and smoothness.
