# Credit Reporting Issues

---

## 👤 You

If a credit card isn’t properly reporting to the agencies for 2 billing cycles, hurting consumer credit, is that illegal ?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

If a credit card issuer fails to report accurate information to credit reporting agencies for two billing cycles, potentially hurting a consumer's credit, it may not necessarily be illegal, but it could be a violation of the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA). The FCRA requires that information provided to credit reporting agencies must be accurate and complete. 

Here are steps a consumer can take:

1. **Contact the Creditor**: The consumer should first contact the credit card issuer to notify them of the reporting error and request that it be corrected.

2. **Dispute the Error**: The consumer can file a dispute with the credit reporting agencies (Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion) to have the error investigated and corrected.

3. **File a Complaint**: If the issue is not resolved, the consumer can file a complaint with the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) or the Federal Trade Commission (FTC).

4. **Seek Legal Advice**: In cases where significant harm has occurred due to the error, the consumer may want to consult with a lawyer who specializes in consumer rights and FCRA violations.

Accurate credit reporting is critical, and consumers have the right to dispute and correct any inaccuracies that affect their credit scores.
