## How to Set a Micrometer Click-Style Torque Wrench

1. **Unlock the handle**  
   - At the base, twist the lock ring/collar **counter-clockwise** to loosen.

2. **Choose your target**  
   - Yakima EXO spec = **100 N·m (~74 ft-lb)**.  
   - Wrench body shows **major scales** (e.g. 50, 60, 70 ft-lb).  
   - Handle shows **minor scale** (0–9). Each increment = +1 ft-lb.

3. **Dial it in**  
   - Rotate the handle until the **main scale lines up just below your target**.  
   - Keep turning until the handle’s minor scale adds up to exactly 74 ft-lb.  
     - Example: stop on “70” on the body, then turn until “4” on the handle aligns → 74 ft-lb.

4. **Lock it**  
   - Twist the base collar **clockwise** to lock. Prevents slipping during use.

5. **Use correctly**  
   - Place wrench squarely on bolt.  
   - Pull slowly, steady pressure.  
   - Stop **immediately** when you hear/feel the “click.” That’s the torque limit.

6. **After use**  
   - Always **reset to lowest setting** (but not below it) before storage. Keeps calibration.
