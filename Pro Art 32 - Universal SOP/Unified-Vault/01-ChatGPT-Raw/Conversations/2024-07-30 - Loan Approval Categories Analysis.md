To determine the category with the highest probability of approval, it is important to consider the typical lending criteria for each purpose. Generally, lenders tend to favor certain loan purposes over others based on perceived risk and potential for repayment. Here are some insights on each category:

1. **Pay off Credit Cards:** This is often favored because it consolidates higher-interest debt into a potentially lower-interest loan, improving the borrower's financial stability.

2. **Debt Consolidation:** Similar to paying off credit cards, this purpose is typically well-received as it suggests the borrower is looking to manage and streamline their debt.

3. **Business:** This can be riskier for lenders, especially if the business is new or not well-established, as the success of the business directly impacts repayment ability.

4. **Home Improvement:** Often favored because home improvements can increase the value of the property, providing a form of collateral.

6. **Other:** This is usually the most variable category and can be less favored unless the borrower provides a compelling reason.

Given these considerations, **Pay off Credit Cards** and **Debt Consolidation** are generally seen as the categories with the highest probability of approval due to their focus on financial management and stability.

However, the exact probability can vary depending on the specific lender’s policies, the borrower's credit profile, income, and other factors. It’s advisable to choose the category that best reflects your actual need and to provide any additional information that can support your case.

Can soft inquiries hurt my score? Can banks see soft pull inquiries?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

### Impact of Soft Inquiries on Credit Score:

1. **No Impact on Credit Score**: Soft inquiries do not affect your credit score. They are not factored into the credit scoring models used by major credit bureaus (e.g., FICO, VantageScore).

2. **Purpose of Soft Inquiries**: Soft inquiries occur when you check your own credit, a lender pre-approves you for an offer without your explicit application, or a company checks your credit for background verification purposes. These are considered non-intrusive checks.

### Visibility of Soft Inquiries:

1. **Visible to You**: Soft inquiries are listed on your credit report and can be seen when you request your own credit report.

2. **Not Visible to Lenders**: Banks and other lenders cannot see soft inquiries when they review your credit report for lending decisions. Only hard inquiries, which occur when you apply for credit, are visible to potential lenders and can impact your credit score.

### Hard Inquiries:

1. **Impact on Credit Score**: Hard inquiries can affect your credit score slightly and remain on your credit report for about two years. Multiple hard inquiries in a short period can have a more significant impact, especially if you have a short credit history or few accounts.

2. **Purpose of Hard Inquiries**: These occur when you apply for a new credit card, mortgage, auto loan, or any other form of credit.

### Summary:

- **Soft Inquiries**: No impact on your credit score; not visible to lenders.
- **Hard Inquiries**: Can affect your credit score slightly; visible to lenders.

By understanding the distinction between soft and hard inquiries, you can better manage your credit health and ensure that your actions do not inadvertently lower your credit score.
