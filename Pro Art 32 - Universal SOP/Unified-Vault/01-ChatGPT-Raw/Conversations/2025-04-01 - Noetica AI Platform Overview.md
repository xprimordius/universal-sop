## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Noetica benchmarks deal terms** in complex financial transactions. Specifically, they focus on:

1. **Corporate Credit Agreements**
   - Terms like covenants, interest rates, collateral requirements, maturity, etc.

2. **Bond Indentures**
   - Legal language and protections for bondholders (e.g. call provisions, change of control, negative covenants).

3. **M&A Agreements**
   - Key clauses like breakup fees, reps and warranties, indemnities, earnouts, etc.

4. **Securities Filings**
   - Terms in prospectuses, registration statements, and related documents.
