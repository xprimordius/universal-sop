- **Metro 2 Disputes** signal that you're disputing *accuracy or completeness* of credit information—notify furnishers and CRAs via Metro 2 Compliance Condition Codes (e.g., XB, XC, XH) when a dispute is pending, completed, or consumer disagrees with results. citeturn0search4turn0search13

  1. Proof of identity
  2. Copy of identity theft report
  3. Written statement that the items are fraudulent—not your transaction citeturn0search2turn0search7turn0search8.

## **Metro 2 Disputes on Inquiries (TransUnion)**

### 1. **Understand the Framework**
- **Metro 2** = industry standard reporting format.  
- **Inquiries** are coded under **ECOA Code "I" (Individual)**, and furnishers must ensure accuracy and permissible purpose.  
- Disputes force the CRA to **re-verify permissible purpose** with the furnisher. If they cannot, the inquiry must be deleted.  

---

### 2. **Grounds to Dispute**
You can only remove inquiries that are:  
- **Unauthorized** (no permissible purpose under FCRA § 604).  
- **Inaccurate** (wrong date, duplicated, coded incorrectly).  
- **Obsolete / stale** (should age off after 2 years).  

Legitimate, authorized inquiries (apps you signed for) are not removed via Metro 2 — only challenged if reported wrong.  

---

### 3. **Process with TransUnion**
- **Step 1:** Pull your TU report, identify inquiry section.  
- **Step 2:** Draft dispute letter citing FCRA §§ 604 & 611 and Metro 2 Compliance Condition Code **XB** (consumer disputes accuracy).  
- **Step 3:** Demand CRA verify with furnisher that inquiry had permissible purpose.  
- **Step 4:** If they can’t validate, TU must delete.  
- **Step 5:** TU codes as **XH** (dispute resolved) once closed.  

---

### 4. **Sample Metro 2 Dispute Language**
> “I am disputing the accuracy and legitimacy of the inquiries listed below. Under FCRA § 604, a consumer reporting agency may only include inquiries when a permissible purpose exists. Please provide documentation from the furnisher showing permissible purpose, or delete the inquiries. Code disputed items as Metro 2 Compliance Condition Code XB during reinvestigation.”  

List each inquiry: name, date, company.  

---

### 5. **Tactics for Success**
- **Attach ID** + proof of address.  
- **Send certified mail** for legal paper trail.  
- **Cite Metro 2 Codes** so TU knows you’re precise (XB → disputed, XH → resolved).  
- **Follow up** if TU “verifies” without evidence—demand actual documentation, not automated parroting.  

---

✅ **Key Point:** You don’t “Metro 2 delete” inquiries. You **force permissible-purpose verification**. If TU or the furnisher can’t prove authorization, the inquiry must be removed.
