## 🎚️ **Latency & Accuracy Reality Check for Tribe XR**

### **Meta Quest 3 / 3 S**
- **Latency:** ~20–25 ms standalone; ~10–15 ms tethered (Link Cable / Air Link).  
- **Tracking:** Inside-out tracking is accurate for large hand motions but can drift on *micro-adjustments* like EQ knobs or jog nudges if hands move out of camera view.  
- **Feel:** Good for mixing, beat-matching, and crossfades; *less ideal* for precision scratching or rapid jogs.  
- **Fix:** Use a well-lit environment + controller grip covers (to keep tracking markers visible) + tether via Link for lowest latency.  

### **Valve Index**
- **Latency:** 5–10 ms typical (SteamVR tethered).  
- **Tracking:** Excellent sub-mm precision from Lighthouse 2.0 base stations. No drift, consistent through 360°.  
- **Feel:** Best in class for rotary accuracy—closest to real CDJ jog feel in VR.  
- **Downside:** Wired setup and price, but unmatched responsiveness for serious mixing practice.  

### **Pimax Crystal**
- **Latency:** ~7–12 ms PC-VR mode.  
- **Tracking:** Mixed—controllers are inside-out like Quest, not Lighthouse, so small-motion fidelity isn’t as stable as Index.  
- **Visuals:** Insane clarity for seeing tiny labels and waveforms; hands can occasionally mis-register.  

### **PlayStation VR2**
- **Latency:** 12–15 ms via PS5.  
- **Tracking:** Solid, but limited by camera FOV; small knob turns sometimes get occluded.  
- **Use:** Great visuals, decent latency; not pro-level precision.  

### **Meta Quest 2**
- **Latency:** 25–35 ms; visible delay with quick fader snaps.  
- **Tracking:** Rough for precision work—fine for fun jams but not accurate enough for cue juggling.  

---

## ⚙️ **Pro Setup Tips (To Reduce Drift / Delay)**
1. **Use PC VR (wired Link)** — drops latency by half.  
2. **Bright, even lighting** — inside-out tracking needs contrast.  
3. **Stable room boundaries** — re-scan if controllers lose position.  
4. **Disable Wi-Fi interference** — especially on Air Link.  
5. **Grip aids** — add physical marker tape or controller grips to improve tactile mapping of mixer zones.  

---

- **Best tactile accuracy:** 🏆 *Valve Index* (base-station tracking).  
- **Best balance of freedom + latency:** *Meta Quest 3 tethered via Link Cable*.  
- **Weakest for precise knob turns:** *Quest 2* / standalone mode only.
