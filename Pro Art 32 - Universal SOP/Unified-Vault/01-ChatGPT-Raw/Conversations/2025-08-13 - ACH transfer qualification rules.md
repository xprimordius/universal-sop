## 🤖 ChatGPT

No — per the Wells Fargo terms in your screenshot, an ACH transfer from your own business account to this new checking account would **not** count as a qualifying electronic deposit for the $325 bonus.  

They explicitly exclude:  
- **Transfers from one account to another** (including your own accounts)  
- **Mobile deposits, Zelle®, or ATM/branch deposits**  

A qualifying deposit must be:  
1. A payroll/government payment or other income sent as a **direct deposit via ACH** from an employer/benefit provider.  
2. An **instant payment** through RTP/FedNow.  
3. An **electronic credit** from a third-party service (e.g., Visa/Mastercard Original Credit Transaction).  

So unless your business payroll is processed through a third-party payroll provider and sent as an actual **ACH direct deposit** (coded as payroll), it won’t qualify.

Right — an ACH is technically a different rail than a simple account-to-account “transfer,” but Wells Fargo’s wording here is more about **the source and coding** of the ACH, not just the rail used.  

Even if you initiate an ACH from your business bank to this new account, it will **only** qualify if:  
- It’s processed as a **direct deposit** via ACH from a recognized payroll provider, government benefits source, or similar income source.

If you push funds from your business account manually or as a standard ACH transfer (CCD, WEB), Wells Fargo will likely treat it as a “transfer from one account” and exclude it from the bonus requirement — even though it’s ACH in the technical sense.  

If you want it to count, you’d need to send it through a **payroll service** (like Gusto, ADP, etc.) so it lands coded as payroll/income.
