# Disabling Backup Card Usage

---

## 👤 You

Amazon keeps using my other cards without my consent when the card I chose doesn’t go through. How to disable this

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

To prevent Amazon from automatically charging a backup card when your primary payment method doesn't go through, you can follow these steps:

1. **Review and Update Payment Methods:**
   - Go to your Amazon account and click on **"Your Payments"** under **"Your Account"** settings.
   - Review the list of payment methods associated with your account.
   - Remove any payment methods you do not want to be used as backups by clicking on **"Delete"** next to the card.

2. **Turn Off One-Click Purchasing:**
   - In your account settings, locate **"Your Payments"** or **"Payment Options"**.
   - Find the **"One-Click settings"** option and turn off **One-Click purchasing** for all devices and accounts.

3. **Manage Default Payment Method:**
   - Set the preferred payment method for each order manually.
   - During checkout, ensure that the correct card is selected and there are no backup payment methods enabled.

4. **Subscription Management:**
   - Check for any subscriptions or recurring payments under **"Manage Your Subscriptions"**.
   - Make sure that each subscription is linked to the payment method you intend to use.

5. **Contact Amazon Support:**
   - If Amazon continues to charge backup cards without your consent, consider contacting Amazon Customer Service directly to report the issue and request that they disable this automatic backup payment feature on your account.

These steps should help you control which payment methods Amazon uses for your purchases.
