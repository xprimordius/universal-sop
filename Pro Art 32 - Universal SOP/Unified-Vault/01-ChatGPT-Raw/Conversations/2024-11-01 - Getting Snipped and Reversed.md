When it comes to getting a guy snipped, how does a guy get snipped and unsnipped so he doesn't get a girl pregnant? And if he gets snipped, can he still get a girl pregnant? And how does it get reversed?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Getting "snipped" refers to a vasectomy, a surgical procedure where the vas deferens are cut or sealed to prevent sperm from entering the semen. This is a highly effective form of birth control. After a vasectomy, it typically takes a few months for the remaining sperm to clear out of the system, so another form of contraception should be used until a semen analysis confirms the absence of sperm.

A vasectomy is considered a permanent form of contraception, but it can sometimes be reversed through a procedure called vasovasostomy, where the cut ends of the vas deferens are reconnected. However, success rates vary and decrease over time. Even if reversed, fertility is not guaranteed. Therefore, a vasectomy should only be considered if you're certain you don't want to father children in the future.
