## 🤖 ChatGPT

The **Amazon Prime Rewards Visa Signature Card (Amex/Chase)** is a **credit card**, **not a charge card**.  

- **Credit card:** You can carry a balance month-to-month (though interest applies if not paid in full).  
- **Charge card:** Requires full payment each month (like the Amex Platinum Charge Card).  

So the Amazon Prime Visa allows revolving balances like a traditional credit card.
