Here’s the strength ranking of **PUR Gum flavors** (based on menthol intensity, cooling effect, and flavor longevity):  

### 🔥 Strength Order (Strongest → Mildest)
1. **Peppermint** – **Strongest.**  
   - Highest menthol concentration.  
   - Produces the most intense cooling and nasal-clearing sensation.  
   - Flavor lasts longer and feels sharper.

2. **Wintergreen** – **Medium.**  
   - Milder sweetness with a smooth, medicinal “root beer” tone.  
   - Cooling effect is moderate—less punchy than peppermint but still refreshing.

3. **Spearmint** – **Mildest.**  
   - Lower menthol content; more sweet and herbal than icy.  
   - Softer mouthfeel and dissipates quicker.

### ⚗️ Why
- **Peppermint oil** = ~40–50% menthol → strongest cooling bite.  
- **Wintergreen oil** = methyl salicylate → warm, aromatic, less “cold.”  
- **Spearmint oil** = carvone → sweet, gentle, barely cooling.

**TL;DR:**  
➡️ **Peppermint = sharp + longest lasting**  
➡️ **Wintergreen = smooth + medium strength**  
➡️ **Spearmint = sweet + softest**
