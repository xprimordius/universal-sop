The IRS EIN reference number error 101 typically occurs when there's an issue with the information provided in the EIN application. Common causes include:

1. **Duplicate Entity Information**: If an EIN application was previously submitted with similar information (such as the same responsible party or business name), the IRS might flag this as a duplicate. 

2. **Incorrect SSN or ITIN**: Any mismatch in the SSN or ITIN for the responsible party can trigger this error.

3. **System Glitch or Downtime**: Occasionally, the IRS online system may have issues or experience downtime that generates this error even when there’s no actual problem with the application data.

### Steps to Resolve Error 101:
1. **Contact the IRS EIN Support**: Call the IRS Business & Specialty Tax Line at **1-800-829-4933**. Explain the error code and have your information ready (such as the SSN/ITIN of the responsible party, business name, etc.) so the IRS can verify and resolve the issue.

2. **Verify Information**: Double-check that all information is accurate, including the responsible party’s details and business structure. Incorrect entries in the application can often lead to this error.

3. **Submit a New Application**: If the IRS confirms no EIN was assigned and there are no issues with the initial application data, you may need to submit a new application, possibly via mail or fax, if the online option continues to fail.

4. **Check for Previous EIN Applications**: If you've applied for an EIN for this business before, the IRS might have already issued an EIN. Confirm by checking any previous communications or EIN documentation.

This should help clear up the issue and allow you to receive your EIN.
