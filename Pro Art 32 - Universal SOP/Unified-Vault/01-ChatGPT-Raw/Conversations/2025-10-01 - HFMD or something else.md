Hand, foot, and mouth disease (HFMD) usually shows up as:  
- Small red spots or blisters on the hands, feet, and sometimes buttocks/genital area.  
- Sores in the mouth.  
- Fever, sore throat, and cold-like symptoms (runny nose is possible).  

**Key points:**  
- Bumps on the butt + new runny nose could be HFMD, but other causes are also common: heat rash, folliculitis (infected hair follicles), irritation from sitting/sweating, or other viral rashes.  
- HFMD rashes are usually blister-like, sometimes painful, not just random bumps.  
- In adults, HFMD is less common but possible.  

⚠️ **Seek medical care if:**  
- The bumps spread quickly, blister, or are painful.
