Here’s the **TL;DR master method** for sous vide steak:  

**1. Prep Steak**  
- Season with salt, pepper, optional garlic/herbs.  
- Place in vacuum-sealed or zip-top bag (air removed).  

**2. Water Temp & Time** *(based on doneness)*  
- Rare: **120°F / 49°C**, 1–2 hrs  
- Medium-rare: **129°F / 54°C**, 1–3 hrs *(most popular)*  
- Medium: **135°F / 57°C**, 1–3 hrs  
- Medium-well: **145°F / 63°C**, 1–3 hrs  

**3. Cook**  
- Fully submerge bag in preheated water bath.  
- Hold temp steady for the set time.  

**4. Finish** *(critical for flavor & crust)*  
- Remove from bag, pat completely dry.  
- Sear in very hot cast-iron pan or grill, **30–60 sec per side** with a little high-smoke oil (e.g., avocado oil).  
- Optionally add butter, garlic, herbs while searing for aroma.  

**5. Rest & Serve**  
- Rest 2–3 min, slice against the grain, enjoy.
