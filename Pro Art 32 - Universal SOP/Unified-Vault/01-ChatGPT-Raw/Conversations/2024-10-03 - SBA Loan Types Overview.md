# SBA Loan Types Overview

---

## 👤 You

What different kinds of SBA loans are there by $ lent, and which ones do 1M+

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

The **Small Business Administration (SBA)** offers several loan programs with varying loan amounts and purposes. Here’s a breakdown of the most common SBA loans, focusing on the maximum amounts lent and those that can exceed $1 million:

### 1. **SBA 7(a) Loan Program**
   - **Max Loan Amount:** Up to **$5 million**.
   - **Use:** General purposes such as working capital, expansion, equipment purchase, and real estate.
   - **Eligibility:** Flexible, for most small businesses.
   - **Loans Over $1M:** Yes, can go up to $5 million.

### 2. **SBA 504 Loan Program**
   - **Max Loan Amount:** Up to **$5.5 million** per project.
   - **Use:** Primarily for purchasing large fixed assets like real estate or equipment.
   - **Eligibility:** Businesses needing long-term financing for assets.
   - **Loans Over $1M:** Yes, can exceed $1 million and typically used for large purchases like commercial real estate.

### 3. **SBA Express Loan**
   - **Max Loan Amount:** Up to **$500,000**.
   - **Use:** For quicker access to funding with streamlined approval, typically for short-term working capital.
   - **Loans Over $1M:** No, capped at $500,000.

### 4. **SBA Microloan Program**
   - **Max Loan Amount:** Up to **$50,000**.
   - **Use:** For startups or very small businesses needing smaller loans.
   - **Loans Over $1M:** No, capped at $50,000.

### 5. **SBA Export Working Capital Program (EWCP)**
   - **Max Loan Amount:** Up to **$5 million**.
   - **Use:** For businesses involved in exporting goods and services.
   - **Loans Over $1M:** Yes, can exceed $1 million, designed to support export transactions.

### 6. **SBA International Trade Loan Program**
   - **Max Loan Amount:** Up to **$5 million**.
   - **Use:** For businesses expanding export activity or dealing with import competition.
   - **Loans Over $1M:** Yes, can go up to $5 million.

### 7. **SBA CAPLines Program**
   - **Max Loan Amount:** Up to **$5 million**.
   - **Use:** Revolving credit lines for short-term and seasonal working capital needs.
   - **Loans Over $1M:** Yes, depending on the line of credit needs.

### Loans That Exceed $1M:
- **SBA 7(a)**: Up to $5M.
- **SBA 504**: Up to $5.5M per project.
- **SBA Export Working Capital**: Up to $5M.
- **SBA International Trade**: Up to $5M.
- **SBA CAPLines**: Up to $5M.

If your goal is to access more than $1M in SBA loans, the **7(a), 504, Export Working Capital, International Trade, and CAPLines** programs would be the primary options.
