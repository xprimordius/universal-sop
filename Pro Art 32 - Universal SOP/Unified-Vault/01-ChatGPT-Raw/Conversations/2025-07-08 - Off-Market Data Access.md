## 👤 You

how do places like propstream get access to off market land ? doesn't this make it on market?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Key Distinction: “Off-Market” ≠ Private or Hidden**  

PropStream and similar platforms pull **public data** from:
- County Assessor Records (ownership, land details)
- Tax Records (delinquency, liens)
- Recorder’s Office (mortgages, deeds)
- MLS + Non-MLS Data (where available)
- Other Data Vendors (skip tracing, demographics)

**Why It’s Still Called “Off-Market”:**
- “Off-market” means **not actively listed for sale** on MLS or public listing services.
- These properties are *not actively for sale*—they’re simply **visible via public records**.
- PropStream aggregates and organizes the data—owners haven’t expressed intent to sell (yet).

**Key Insight:**  
Off-market = no public active listing.  
It’s about status, not privacy.

**Bonus Tip:**  
Once *you* or someone markets it, *then* it’s on-market—even if it wasn’t listed initially.

## 👤 You

what is a county assessor

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**County Assessor = Property Value & Tax Authority**

**Purpose:**  
- Determines property value for **tax purposes**.
- Maintains property records: land, homes, commercial, vacant lots.

**Duties:**  
1. **Assess Property Value:** Estimates *market value* for taxation.
2. **Track Ownership:** Keeps records of who owns what.
3. **Record Property Characteristics:** Lot size, square footage, improvements, etc.
4. **Tax Roll:** Provides data to calculate property taxes.

**Key Insight:**  
Their records fuel most “off-market” databases like PropStream.

**Example Data From Assessor:**  
- Owner’s name & mailing address  
- Lot size, zoning  
- Property type (vacant, residential, commercial)  
- Last sale price/date  
- Assessed value (taxable value)  

**It’s Public Record**—that’s how investors find hidden deals.

## 👤 You

what are liens

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Liens = Legal Claims Against Property (Debt Attached to Real Estate)**  

When someone owes money, a **lien** can be filed against their property as collateral for the debt.

---

### **How Liens Work:**
- Debt is recorded at the county level.
- Owner *cannot* sell or refinance without resolving the lien.
- Liens “cloud the title,” meaning the property can’t transfer cleanly.

---

### **Common Lien Types (Each Signals Distress):**
| **Lien Type**   | **Meaning**                                    | **Why It’s Motivating**                  |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| **Tax Lien**    | Unpaid property taxes                          | Risk of losing property via tax sale     |
| **IRS Lien**    | Owed federal income taxes                      | Strong pressure; affects all assets      |
| **HOA Lien**    | Unpaid homeowners association dues              | HOA may foreclose in some states         |
| **Mechanic’s Lien** | Unpaid contractor, supplier, or builder fees | Can’t sell/refi until paid               |
| **Judgment Lien**  | Result of lawsuit/creditor judgment          | Court-ordered debt; very limiting        |
| **Utility Lien**   | Unpaid water/sewer/utility bills (some areas) | Risk of service shutoff or foreclosure   |

---

### **Why Investors Target Liens:**  
- Indicates financial hardship.
- Owner often needs fast cash or debt relief.
- Great for **creative deals** (subject-to, seller finance, discounts).

---

### ⚡ **Key Insight:**  
**Liens ≠ Sale**, but they create *pressure* → owners may become motivated to sell fast.
