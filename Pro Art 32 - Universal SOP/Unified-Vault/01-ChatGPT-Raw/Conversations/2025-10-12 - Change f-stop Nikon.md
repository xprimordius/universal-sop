---

- **Rotate the main command dial** (rear dial by your thumb) to adjust aperture.

- Watch the f-number on the **screen or viewfinder** — smaller number = wider aperture (more light, blurrier background).

- In **A or M mode**, **rotate the rear command dial** to adjust aperture.
- If in **M mode**, the **front dial** may control aperture and the **rear dial** shutter speed (can be swapped in settings).
- You can also **assign aperture control** to custom buttons in the menu.

---

### **3. Lens With Aperture Ring (older Nikon F lenses)**
- Rotate the **aperture ring** directly on the lens barrel.
- On modern bodies, you may need to **enable “Aperture ring control”** in the custom settings menu.
