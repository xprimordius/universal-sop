### 1. Identify the Platform
- **PC/Mac/Switch/Mobile** – Name = Epic Games display name.

---

### 2. Epic Games (for non-console platforms)
1. Go to Epic’s website → log in.
2. Click your username (top-right) → **Account** → **General**.
3. Click the pencil **✏️** next to Display Name.
4. Enter new name → **Confirm**.
5. Check your email and verify change.  
   • Cooldown: 14 days before next change citeturn0search0turn0search4

### 3. PlayStation (PS4/PS5)
- Fortnite name = PSN Online ID.
- First change = **free**, subsequent = ~$10 (or $5 with PS+). citeturn0search4  
1. Go to Settings → Account Management → Profile → Online ID.
2. Change ID → confirm → sync.
3. Relaunch Fortnite to see change.

---

### 4. Xbox (One/Series X/S)
- Fortnite name = your Xbox Gamertag.
- First change = **free**, then ~$10. citeturn0search4turn0search8  
1. On Xbox website → sign into Microsoft account.
2. Go to Profile → Customize → Edit Gamertag.
3. Enter new Gamertag → confirm → launch Fortnite to update.
