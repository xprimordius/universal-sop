**Virgin vs. Non-Virgin Coconut Oil — Key Differences**

| Feature | **Virgin Coconut Oil (VCO)** | **Non-Virgin (Refined) Coconut Oil** |
|--------|-------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| **Source** | Fresh coconut meat | Dried coconut (copra) |
| **Processing** | Cold-pressed or expeller-pressed, unrefined | Refined, bleached, and deodorized (RBD) |
| **Smell/Taste** | Strong coconut aroma and flavor | Neutral (no coconut smell or taste) |
| **Color** | Clear or slightly cloudy | Typically pure white or clear when melted |
| **Nutrients** | Higher in antioxidants and polyphenols | Fewer nutrients due to heat processing |
| **Uses** | Best for skin, hair, eating raw, smoothies | Better for high-heat cooking (higher smoke point) |
| **Shelf Life** | Shorter | Longer (due to lack of organic matter) |

### TLDR:
- **Virgin = Pure, aromatic, nutrient-rich.**
- **Non-Virgin = Neutral, more processed, longer shelf life.**

Use **virgin** for raw use, health, and beauty. Use **refined** for **high-heat cooking** or when flavor neutrality is needed.
