If you need to check your own credit score, you can use the following legitimate methods:

3. **Bank or Credit Card Issuer**: Many banks and credit card companies offer free credit score access to their customers as part of their services.

For any other needs, ensure you have the proper authorization and use legal channels to obtain credit information.

2. **Inspect the Fan**:

3. **BIOS Settings Adjustment**:

     - Navigate to the `Monitor` or `Hardware Monitor` section.
     - Look for `CPU Fan Speed Lower Limit` and consider lowering it or setting it to `Ignore`. This can sometimes prevent the error from appearing if the fan operates at a lower RPM than expected.
  
4. **Update BIOS**:
   - Make sure your BIOS is up-to-date. Sometimes, updating the BIOS can resolve detection issues.

5. **Replace or Test Different Fans**:
   - If possible, test with a different CPU fan to see if the issue persists. This can help determine if the problem is with the fan itself or the motherboard.
