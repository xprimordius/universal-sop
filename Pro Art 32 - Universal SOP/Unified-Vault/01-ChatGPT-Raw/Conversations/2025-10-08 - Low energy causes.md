### Low Energy Period — Core Causes

**1. Physiological:**
- **Sleep Debt:** Even mild deprivation wrecks mitochondrial energy output.  
- **Blood Sugar Swings:** Irregular eating or caffeine-only mornings cause insulin crashes.  
- **Adrenal Fatigue / Cortisol Dysregulation:** Chronic stress → flat energy curve.  
- **Thyroid or Testosterone Suppression:** Common from stress, low calories, or micronutrient deficits (iodine, zinc, selenium).  
- **Liver Congestion:** Fatigue, eye redness, brain fog—especially after heavy meals—signal impaired detox and sluggish bile flow.  
- **Dehydration / Electrolyte Imbalance:** Low sodium, magnesium, or potassium = instant energy dip.

**2. Nutritional:**
- Low **protein** or missing **B vitamins, iron, magnesium, or carnitine** reduces ATP production.  
- Overconsumption of **seed oils or refined carbs** dulls insulin sensitivity → mitochondrial slowdown.

**3. Energetic / Emotional:**
- **Resistance or misalignment:** Doing what drains vs. excites you collapses life-force (Bashar’s “not following your highest excitement”).  
- **Suppressed emotion or overwhelm:** Stored emotion = energetic stagnation, felt as fatigue or heaviness.

**4. Environmental:**
- **Low sunlight or grounding time:** Mitochondria sync with light cycles.  
- **EMF overload, poor air quality, or static environments** drain bioelectric charge.

### Strategic Fix

**Immediate (24–48h):**
1. Hydrate with electrolytes + lemon + sea salt.  
2. Eat clean proteins + greens + healthy fats (avoid seed oils).  
3. Move → brisk walk + sunlight + 5 mins breathwork.  
4. Sleep 8–9h, blackout room, no screens 1h prior.  
5. Caffeine only *after* food, not on empty stomach.

- Liver flush foods: beet, dandelion, turmeric, garlic.
