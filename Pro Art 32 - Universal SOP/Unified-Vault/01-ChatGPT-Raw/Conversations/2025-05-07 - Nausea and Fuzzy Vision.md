### **Possible Causes (based on symptoms)**
- **Vasovagal response**: sudden nausea, vision dimming, ears plugging = possible fainting.
- **Inner ear issue** (vestibular): dizziness, nausea, plugged ears.
- **Low blood pressure or hypoglycemia**: can trigger nausea + fuzzy vision + faintness.
- **Migraine onset**: some migraines start with visual/auditory symptoms + nausea.
- **Toxin ingestion / food poisoning / dehydration**: if recent suspect meal or low fluid intake.
- **Anxiety/panic episode**: can present with these exact symptoms.

---

### **Immediate Actions (at home)**
1. **Lay her flat** **with feet elevated** (unless vomiting starts).
2. **Cool, quiet, dim environment** – no screens.
3. **Loosen tight clothes**, support her neck.
4. **Give sips of water or electrolyte solution** (unless actively vomiting).
5. **Monitor consciousness and breathing**.
6. **Cold compress to forehead/neck.**
7. **Do NOT** let her walk alone – she could faint.

---

### **Check These Immediately**
- **Has she eaten?** Low blood sugar?
- **Is she overheating or dehydrated?**
- **Any medications, supplements, or unusual foods today?**
- **Does she have a history of fainting, migraines, or vertigo?**

---

### **When to Call Emergency Services**
- Loss of consciousness
- Slurred speech or confusion
- Chest pain
- Weakness in arms/legs
- Not improving within 10–15 minutes
