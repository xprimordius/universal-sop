### Key Points:
1. **FCRA Protection**: Under the FCRA, consumers have the right to:
   - **Dispute inaccurate information** on their credit report.
   - Demand that credit reporting agencies **investigate and correct errors** within 30 days.
   
2. **Soft Inquiries**: Although soft inquiries typically don’t impact credit scores, if they were made without proper authorization or contain inaccuracies, you can still dispute them. The law mandates that only **authorized entities** can perform inquiries on your report.

3. **Your Rights**:
   - You have the right to dispute any item, including soft inquiries, if you believe:
     - They were unauthorized.
     - They contain inaccurate information.
     - They are a result of identity theft.
   
4. **Action Steps**:
