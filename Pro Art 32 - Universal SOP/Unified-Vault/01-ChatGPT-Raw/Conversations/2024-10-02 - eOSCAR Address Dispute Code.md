The e-OSCAR (Electronic Online Solution for Complete and Accurate Reporting) system is used by data furnishers (like banks, creditors, and debt collectors) to process credit disputes electronically with the credit reporting agencies. When disputing addresses on your credit report using e-OSCAR, specific codes are used to indicate the type of dispute. These codes help categorize and communicate the nature of the dispute. 

For an **address dispute**, the most relevant e-OSCAR code would typically be **112** which stands for "Address Dispute - Incorrect or Outdated Address." This code is applied when a consumer disputes an address on their credit report as inaccurate or no longer relevant.

If you are managing this yourself or through a service, ensuring that the dispute is clearly communicated, providing proof of current and accurate addresses can enhance the success of getting the outdated or incorrect information removed.
