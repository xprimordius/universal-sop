- **Traditional format** (landscape).
- Best for cinematic or long-form content.

### 🔹 **2:3** = Vertical (Mobile-First)
- **Used for:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Snapchat.
- Prioritizes **mobile viewing**.
- **More native to social scrolling**.
- **Takes up more screen real estate**, improving engagement.

---

## 🤖 Why is **Sora defaulted to 2:3**?

Because:
- **Short-form, vertical video is dominating attention.**
- 2:3 format is **optimized for TikTok/Reels/Shorts** virality.
- **OpenAI is positioning Sora for mobile-native creators**, not desktop filmmakers.
- It's also a compromise between 9:16 (full vertical) and 3:4—gives flexibility in cropping.

---

- **16:9** = best for cinematic, YouTube, desktop.
- **2:3** = best for short-form, viral, mobile-first content.
