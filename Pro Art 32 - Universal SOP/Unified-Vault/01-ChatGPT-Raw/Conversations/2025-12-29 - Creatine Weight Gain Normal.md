**Yes — this is normal.**  
Especially in the **first 2–6 weeks** of lifting + creatine.

**Why the scale jumped (~8 lb):**
- **Creatine = intracellular water** → muscles pull in water (2–5 lb common).
- **Glycogen refill** → every 1g glycogen binds ~3g water.
- **Early muscle gain + inflammation** from new training.
- **Hormonal cycle** can add temporary water weight.

**What it is NOT (usually):**
- Not fat gain (that would require a large calorie surplus over weeks).

**What to watch instead of scale:**
- Waist/hip measurements
- How clothes fit
- Strength increases
- Mirror/photos

**Timeline:**
- Water weight stabilizes in **2–4 weeks**.
- Body recomposition shows by **6–12 weeks**.

**When to reassess:**
- Weight keeps rising **after 6–8 weeks**
- Measurements increase significantly
- Calories clearly exceed needs

**Bottom line:**  
Creatine + lifting often makes the scale go up **before** your body tightens. Stay the course.
