what happens if my TRT is in my car room temp and in the sun

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Leaving your TRT (testosterone replacement therapy) vial or pens in a hot car at room temperature or in the sun is risky:  

- **Heat sensitivity:** Testosterone injections (cypionate, enanthate, etc.) are oil-based, but prolonged exposure to heat (above ~77°F/25°C, especially >86°F/30°C) can degrade potency and stability. Direct sunlight can raise temps inside a car well past 120°F (49°C).  
- **Potency loss:** The hormone can break down faster, making the medication less effective. You might not notice immediately, but blood levels could drop.  
- **Sterility risk:** Repeated heating and cooling cycles can compromise sterility and increase the risk of contamination.  
- **Manufacturer guidance:** Storage is usually recommended at **controlled room temperature (68–77°F / 20–25°C), protected from light and excessive heat**.  

**Action:**  
- If it was only a brief exposure (e.g., <1–2 hours), it’s probably fine.  
- If it sat in the sun for several hours or a whole day, best practice is **not to use it**—contact your pharmacy or doctor for replacement.
