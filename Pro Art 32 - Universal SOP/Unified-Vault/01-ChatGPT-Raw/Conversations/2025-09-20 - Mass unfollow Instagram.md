Instagram doesn’t have a native “mass unfollow” feature, and third-party automation tools that do it automatically can get your account flagged or banned. Here are the safe, practical options:  

### 1. Manual + Sorting
- Use apps like **Followers Insight**, **FollowMeter**, or **Unfollowers for Instagram** (iOS/Android) — they show who doesn’t follow you back.  
- You still have to tap unfollow one by one, but it’s faster with sorting.  

### 2. Browser Extension (Riskier)
- Chrome extensions (e.g., “Superpowers for Instagram,” “IG Follower Export”) let you export your follower/following lists to a spreadsheet.  
- You can then compare who you follow vs. who follows you, and manually clean up.  
- Direct automation (auto-click unfollow) risks temporary blocks.  

### 3. Safe Daily Limits
If you unfollow manually, stay under **50–100 unfollows per day**. Going higher risks Instagram action.  

### 4. Pro Workflow
- Export lists (spreadsheet).  
- Filter out people not following back.  
- Unfollow in batches each day, keeping under safe limits.  

⚠️ **Avoid “mass unfollow” apps that promise automation** — they usually break Instagram’s Terms of Service and can lock your account.
