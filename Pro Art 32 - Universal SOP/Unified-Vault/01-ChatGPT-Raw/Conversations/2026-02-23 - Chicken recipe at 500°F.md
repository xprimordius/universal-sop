## The controlling law

If an employer uses a **background check** (criminal, credit, employment history) **and it influences a hiring decision**, they must follow a **strict process**.

---

## What an employer MUST do (non-optional)

### 1. **Pre-Adverse Action Notice (BEFORE rejecting you)**
They must send you:
- A notice saying they are *considering* adverse action
- A **copy of the background report**
- A **Summary of Your Rights under the FCRA**

⛔ If they rejected you **without this**, that’s a violation.

---

### 2. **Reasonable time to dispute**
- Usually **5–7 business days**
- They must wait before final rejection
- You have the right to:
  - Dispute inaccuracies
  - Provide context or corrections

⛔ Rejecting you immediately = violation.

---

### 3. **Final Adverse Action Notice**
Only AFTER the waiting period, they must send:
- Notice you were not hired
- Name + contact info of the background check company
- Statement that the company did **not** make the decision
- Notice of your right to dispute the report

⛔ Missing or vague notices = violation.

---

## What employers CANNOT do
- ❌ Reject you based on **incorrect, outdated, or expunged info**
