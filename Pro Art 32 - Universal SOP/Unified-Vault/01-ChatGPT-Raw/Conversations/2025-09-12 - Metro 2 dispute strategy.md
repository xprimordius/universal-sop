---

## 🔑 Metro 2 Inquiry (J-Segment) Codes
- **J1 Segment Identifier** = “J” (flags the inquiry segment).  
- **J2 Inquiry Date** = Date inquiry was made.  
- **J3 Inquiry Subscriber Number** = Code identifying the data furnisher (e.g., US Bank, Huntington).  
- **J4 Inquiry Type Code** = Shows what type of inquiry it is:  
  - `01` = Individual  
  - `02` = Joint  
  - `03` = Authorized User  
- **J5 Inquiry Subscriber Name** = Name of furnisher (e.g., “US BANK”).  
- **J6 Inquiry Amount** = Amount associated (usually left blank for credit pulls).  
- **J7 Inquiry Permissible Purpose Code** = 🔥 the key compliance field — identifies the **reason** the inquiry was made. Examples:  
  - `A` = Auto loan  
  - `C` = Credit card  
  - `M` = Mortgage  
  - `O` = Other  
  - `U` = Unknown  

---

## Why This Matters
- If TransUnion (or the bank) cannot show the **J-Segment fields fully populated**, the inquiry is **non-Metro 2 compliant**.  
- Most bureaus cannot provide **J7 Permissible Purpose** tied to **signed authorization**, which gives you leverage.  
- Example: If Huntington reports a hard inquiry but doesn’t include the **exact permissible purpose code** (`C` for credit card, `M` for mortgage, etc.) → it’s **inaccurate reporting**.

## 2. **Compliance Condition Codes (CCC)**
- These are the official **Metro 2 compliance codes** that furnishers/bureaus must use to mark the *status* of any tradeline or item.  
- They tell the bureau how to handle the account/inquiry under the law.  
- Examples:  
  - **XB** = Account/inquiry in dispute (meets FCRA requirements).  
  - **XC** = Consumer disputes, but furnisher believes info is correct.  
  - **XH** = Previously disputed, no longer in dispute.  
  - **XP** = Disputed by consumer, meets FCRA requirements.  
- These must be attached whenever an item is **in dispute**.  

---

## ⚡ Why It Matters for Hard Inquiries
- If you dispute an inquiry, the bureau must tag it with **XB** (or equivalent) while under investigation.  
- Many times they fail → **Metro 2 non-compliance violation**.  
- If they respond “verified” but never coded the inquiry properly with XB/XP, they’ve violated **FCRA §607(b)** (reasonable procedures) and **FCRA §611** (failure to investigate).
