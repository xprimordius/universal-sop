To use your iPhone camera as a webcam for Discord on Windows, follow these steps:

Download a dedicated app that allows your iPhone to function as a webcam:
- **EpocCam** (popular and reliable)
- **Reincubate Camo** (premium features, great quality)
- **iVCam** (simple and effective)
- **DroidCam** (supports iPhone and Android)

---

### **Step 2: Install the Companion Software on Windows**
1. Go to the app's official website and download the **Windows companion software** for the app you chose:
   - [EpocCam](https://www.elgato.com/en/epoccam)
   - [Reincubate Camo](https://reincubate.com/camo/)
   - [iVCam](https://www.e2esoft.com/ivcam/)
   - [DroidCam](https://www.dev47apps.com/)

2. Install the software and follow the on-screen setup instructions.

---

### **Step 3: Connect Your iPhone to Your PC**
You can connect your iPhone to your PC using either:
- **Wi-Fi** (ensure both devices are on the same network).
- **USB Cable** (for better performance and stability).

---

### **Step 4: Configure the Webcam App**
1. Launch the webcam app on your iPhone.
2. Open the companion software on your PC.
3. Follow the app's setup to establish the connection (most apps will connect automatically if both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network or connected via USB).

---

### **Step 5: Set the iPhone Camera as Webcam in Discord**
1. Open **Discord** on your PC.
2. Go to **Settings** > **Voice & Video**.
3. Under **Video Settings**, click the dropdown menu for **Camera** and select the app (e.g., **EpocCam**, **Camo**, etc.).
4. Test the camera to ensure it's working.

---

### **Tips for Best Results**
- Use a tripod or phone stand to stabilize the camera.
- Ensure good lighting for higher video quality.
- For higher resolution, some apps like Reincubate Camo offer premium upgrades.

With these steps, your iPhone camera should now function as a high-quality webcam for Discord on Windows!
