# Pro Forma Paystub Verification

---

## 👤 You

Will a personal loan company like lightstream truist fact check a paystub if it’s pro forma

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Lenders like LightStream, a division of Truist, typically verify the information provided in loan applications, including paystubs. If a paystub is marked as pro forma (i.e., it is a projected or hypothetical financial statement rather than an actual one), the lender may:

1. **Request Additional Documentation:** They might ask for additional proof of income, such as bank statements, tax returns, or an employment verification letter.
2. **Perform Employment Verification:** Lenders often contact employers directly to confirm employment status and income.
3. **Assess Creditworthiness:** They will also look at your credit history, credit score, and overall financial situation to determine loan eligibility and terms.

Providing accurate and truthful information is crucial, as discrepancies can lead to loan denial or other legal implications. If you have a pro forma paystub, it's best to clarify its nature upfront and provide any supporting documents that reflect your actual income.
