## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Perfectly Boiled Eggs (for Deviled Eggs)**  

**1. Choose:** Use large, fresh eggs.  
**2. Boil:**
- Place eggs in a pot, single layer.  
- Add cold water until eggs are covered by 1 inch.  
- Bring to a **rolling boil** over medium-high heat.  
- Once boiling, **turn off heat**, cover, and let sit **10–12 minutes**.  
**3. Cool:** Immediately transfer eggs to **ice water** for **10–15 minutes** to stop cooking and make peeling easy.  
**4. Peel:** Gently crack and roll under water.  

---

**Classic Deviled Eggs Recipe**  

**Ingredients:**  
- 6 boiled eggs  
- 3 tbsp mayonnaise  
- 1 tsp yellow mustard  
- ½ tsp white vinegar (optional, for tang)  
- Salt & pepper to taste  
- Paprika (for garnish)  

**Steps:**  
1. Slice eggs lengthwise, remove yolks.  
2. Mash yolks until smooth.  
3. Mix in mayo, mustard, vinegar, salt, and pepper.  
4. Spoon or pipe mixture into egg whites.  
5. Sprinkle with paprika.  

**Tips:**  
- For creamy texture: add a touch of sour cream or Greek yogurt.  
- For flair: top with chives, bacon bits, or a tiny pickle slice.
