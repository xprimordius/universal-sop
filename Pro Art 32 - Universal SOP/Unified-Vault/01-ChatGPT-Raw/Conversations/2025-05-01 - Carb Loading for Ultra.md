## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Best Carb Loading Strategy for Ultra in 3 Days:**

**Goal:** Maximize muscle glycogen without bloating or GI distress.

---

### **3-Day Carb Load Plan (72-Hour Protocol)**

**Daily Macro Target:**  
- **8–10g carbs/kg body weight/day**  
- Moderate protein, low fat  
- Hydrate aggressively with electrolytes  

---

### **Daily Breakdown:**

#### **Day 1 (T-3):**  
- 60–70% carbs, ~4–5g/kg  
- Moderate fiber  
- Example: rice, potatoes, oats, bananas, bagels  
- Walk or very light activity only  

#### **Day 2 (T-2):**  
- 70–80% carbs, ~7–8g/kg  
- Lower fiber  
- Prioritize fast-digesting: white rice, pasta, fruit, juice  
- No training

#### **Day 3 (T-1 / Day Before Race):**  
- 80–90% carbs, ~10g/kg  
- Very low fiber, no veggies, no red meat  
- White rice, low-fiber breads, applesauce, rice cakes, maple syrup  
- Hydrate with 1/4 tsp salt per 16 oz water (every 2–3 hrs)  
- Small dinner (light, carb-rich)

---

### **Extras:**  
- Avoid high fat and high protein  
- Avoid new foods  
- Monitor for GI issues  
- Add **carbohydrate drink (e.g., Vitargo, Skratch Superfuel)** if appetite lags

## 👤 You

What’s better for energy apple sauce or maple syrup

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Maple syrup** is better for rapid energy due to:

- **Higher glycemic index**: Faster glucose spike and absorption  
- **Liquid form**: Absorbs quicker  
- **Pure glucose/sucrose**: Direct muscle fuel  

**Applesauce** is slower due to fiber and fructose content.

**Use both strategically**:  
- **Maple syrup**: Pre-race or during race for fast fuel  
- **Applesauce**: Earlier in carb load for gentle digestion and volume
