## 🤖 ChatGPT

Vital Farms offers two main types of **pasture-raised eggs**:  

### **1. Organic Pasture-Raised Eggs**  
- **Feed:** Organic, non-GMO, no synthetic pesticides or herbicides.  
- **Certification:** USDA Organic.  
- **Animal Welfare:** Same high standards as their non-organic eggs, with **108 sq. ft. per hen** on pasture.  
- **Price:** Typically more expensive due to organic certification.  

### **2. Non-Organic Pasture-Raised Eggs**  
- **Feed:** Non-organic, may include GMO grains and conventional pesticides.  
- **Certification:** Not USDA Organic but still Certified Humane® Pasture-Raised.  
- **Animal Welfare:** Same **108 sq. ft. per hen** on pasture.  
- **Price:** More affordable than organic.  

### **Key Differences**  
| Feature                  | Organic Pasture-Raised | Non-Organic Pasture-Raised |
|--------------------------|-----------------------|---------------------------|
| Feed Quality             | Organic, Non-GMO      | Conventional, may include GMO |
| Pesticides/Herbicides    | No synthetic chemicals | May contain some residues |
| Certification            | USDA Organic, Certified Humane® | Certified Humane® |
| Animal Welfare          | 108 sq. ft. per hen | 108 sq. ft. per hen |
| Price                   | Higher                 | Lower |

### **Bottom Line**  
- **If avoiding pesticides and GMOs matters**, go for **organic**.  
- **If price is a concern but you still want high animal welfare**, non-organic is still **far superior to cage-free or conventional eggs**.
