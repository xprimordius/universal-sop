## 👤 You

What do bankruptcy trustees through the BK courts do with the money they collect at the finalization of the process

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Short answer:**  
Money collected by a bankruptcy trustee is **distributed to creditors according to the priority rules in the U.S. Bankruptcy Code**. The trustee does **not keep it** (beyond a small statutory commission).

---

## How the Money Is Used (Order of Distribution)

### 1. **Administrative Expenses**
Paid first.

Includes:
- Trustee commission  
- Trustee attorney fees  
- Accountant / professional fees  
- Court administrative costs  

Source:  
11 U.S.C. §503, §507, §326  
https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/11/503

---

### 2. **Priority Debts**
Next in line.

Examples:
- Certain **tax debts**
- **Domestic support obligations** (child support / alimony)
- **Employee wages owed**
- Certain government claims

Source:  
11 U.S.C. §507  
https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/11/507

---

### 3. **Secured Creditors**
Creditors with collateral (cars, houses, etc.) get paid from the **value of the collateral**.

Example:
- Mortgage lender
- Car lender

Source:  
11 U.S.C. §506  
https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/11/506

---

### 4. **Unsecured Creditors**
Whatever remains is divided **pro-rata** among unsecured creditors.

Examples:
- Credit cards  
- personal loans  
- medical bills  

---

### 5. **Debtor (Rare)**
If money remains after all debts are paid, it **goes back to the debtor**.

Source:  
11 U.S.C. §726  
https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/11/726

---

# Trustee Compensation
Trustees get a **regulated commission** based on the money they distribute.

Typical maximum under **11 U.S.C. §326**:

| Amount Distributed | Trustee Max Commission |
|---|---|
| First $5,000 | 25% |
| Next $45,000 | 10% |
| Next $950,000 | 5% |
| Over $1M | 3% |

Source:  
https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/11/326

---

# Important Reality
In many **Chapter 7 cases**:

- There are **no assets to distribute**  
- Trustees collect **nothing**  
- Case closes as a **“no asset case.”**

Source:  
U.S. Courts Bankruptcy Basics  
https://www.uscourts.gov/services-forms/bankruptcy/bankruptcy-basics/chapter-7-bankruptcy-basics

---

✔ **Summary**

Trustee collects money →  
Pays **administrative costs → priority debts → secured creditors → unsecured creditors → debtor (if anything remains)**.
