Here’s how to fill out each section of the **LLC Member Resolution** form. Assume you're documenting a formal business decision:

---

### **Title Line**
**For:**  
> *Enter the subject of the resolution* (e.g., “Opening a Business Bank Account” or “Authorizing a Lease Agreement”)

---

### **First Section**

**Date & Location:**  
> *____ day of _________________ 20___*  
→ Fill in the date the resolution is being made (e.g., “3rd day of June, 2025”)

> *Location*  
→ Fill in the physical address (or city/state) where the resolution is being agreed upon (e.g., “Minneapolis, MN”)

---

### **Resolution Statement**

In the blank lines after *“the members decided unanimously that:”*, describe **what was discussed and agreed upon**, such as:

> The Company will open a business checking account with [Bank Name].  
> Alan Nguyen is authorized to sign, deposit, and withdraw on behalf of the Company.  
> The Company agrees to the bank’s terms and conditions.  

(Use bullet points or short paragraphs if multiple items.)

---

### **Now, therefore, it is resolved, that the Company shall:**  
Reiterate the **action to be taken**. Example:

> Open a business checking account with Chase Bank.  
> Authorize Alan Nguyen as the primary signer.  
> Submit all required documents to the financial institution.

---

### **Signature Section**

Each LLC member signs and prints their name and date. If you are the only member, just fill out one line.

> - Signature  
> - Printed Name  
> - Date (e.g., 06/03/2025)
