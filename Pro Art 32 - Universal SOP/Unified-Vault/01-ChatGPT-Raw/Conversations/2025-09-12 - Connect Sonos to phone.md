## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here’s the fastest way to connect your Sonos to your phone:  

### 1. Install the Sonos App  
- **iOS:** App Store → download **Sonos**.  
- **Android:** Google Play → download **Sonos**.  

### 2. Connect to Wi-Fi  
- Make sure your phone and Sonos speaker are on the **same Wi-Fi network**.  

### 3. Open the App & Set Up  
- Open Sonos app → tap **Set up a new system**.  
- Choose **Standard Setup (Wi-Fi)**.  
- Follow prompts to connect your Sonos speaker to Wi-Fi.  

### 4. Add Services  
- In the app, go to **Settings → Services & Voice**.  
- Add Spotify, Apple Music, etc.  

### 5. Control Playback  
- Once connected, play music directly from the Sonos app.  
- Many services (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music) also let you select **Sonos** under the speaker/airplay options.  

⚡ **Shortcut:** If your Sonos supports **Bluetooth** (e.g., Sonos Roam, Move), just press the Bluetooth button, then pair in your phone’s Bluetooth settings.
