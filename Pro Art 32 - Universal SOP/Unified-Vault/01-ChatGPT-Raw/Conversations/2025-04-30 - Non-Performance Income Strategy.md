### **What Is Non-Performance Income?**  
Income **not tied to time, labor, or effort**. It’s **passive** or **system-driven**.

> “Non-performance income is income that doesn’t require you to perform.” — Derrick Whitehead

---

### **Core Pillars to Build It (Whitehead Methodology):**

#### 1. **Credit-Based Wealth Creation**
- **Leverage strong credit profiles** (personal + business) to access $50K–$500K+
- Fund **income-generating assets** (e.g., eCom automation, real estate, notes)
- Use **O.P.M.** (other people’s money), not your own
- Protect it with **trusts & layered LLC/C-corp structures**

#### 2. **Corporate Structures That Pay You**
- Setup **holding companies** and **operating corps**  
- Issue **stock to yourself** or to **family trusts**  
- Collect **dividends**, **management fees**, or **royalties** without performing

#### 3. **Automated Systems & Digital Assets**
- Invest in systems that run with **VAs, software, or contractors**  
  (e.g., Airbnb automation, digital products, auto-scaling eCom stores)

- **System = owner independence + infinite scale**

#### 4. **Trusts & Estate-Level Structuring**
- Use **irrevocable trusts**, **private trust organizations**, or **non-grantor trusts**  
- Move assets **out of your name** to reduce liability and increase control
- Income can flow to you **as a beneficiary**, not as earned income

#### 5. **Leveraging the Law**
- Enforce contracts and secure revenue via **UCC**, **performance clauses**, and **statutory rights**
- Enforce **non-payment** or **performance** through **commercial affidavits** and **administrative remedy**

**TLDR: TikTok Shop + ClickBank affiliate marketing is *not directly viable*.** Here's why — and the *best way to structure it instead*:

---

### **Why It Doesn’t Work Directly:**
- **TikTok Shop = physical product platform** (ships to buyers)  
- **ClickBank = digital products (courses, ebooks, etc.)**  
- TikTok Shop **doesn't support** external digital product links like ClickBank  
- **Affiliate links in TikTok captions or bios are blocked or flagged**

---

### **How to Make It Work Indirectly (Hybrid Model):**

| Step | Action | Purpose |
|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Create TikTok Shop or U.S. creator account | Native access to shop features |
| 2 | Build niche content (e.g. health, finance, mindset) | Drive trust and followers |
| 3 | Post **physical products** in TikTok Shop relevant to your niche | Monetize with built-in affiliate commissions |
| 4 | Funnel bio traffic to **your own landing page** (Linktree, systeme.io) | Bypass TikTok’s restrictions |
| 5 | Promote **ClickBank digital offers** via that landing page | Capture leads + earn ClickBank commissions |
| 6 | Use email automation to warm and close leads | Builds non-performance backend income |

---

### **Example (Health Niche):**

- **TikTok Video:** “Why you wake up tired every day…”  
- **TikTok Shop Product:** Affiliate link to magnesium supplement  
- **Bio Funnel:** Linktree → Free guide → Email opt-in  
- **Email #1–3:** Educate on health → ClickBank product (e.g., sleep optimization course)

---

### **Conclusion:**
TikTok Shop + ClickBank is viable only **with a bridge funnel + email system**. Pure direct linking is not sustainable.
