**TC Fee = Transaction Coordinator Fee**

► **Definition:**  
A **Transaction Coordinator (TC)** handles the paperwork and administrative tasks to ensure a smooth closing. This fee compensates them.

► **Common Role:**  
- Organize documents  
- Coordinate with escrow/title  
- Track deadlines  
- Ensure contract compliance  
- Communicate with all parties  

► **Typical Cost:**  
Usually **$300–$1,500+**, depending on deal complexity.

► **In Your Deal:**  
It’s an *extra* fee on top of the $50K entry cost and closing costs. Ask for exact amount upfront.
