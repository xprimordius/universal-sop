**No — pro forma bank statements are not legitimate or acceptable** for rental approval and **could be considered fraudulent** if submitted as if they were real.

### ⚠️ Risks:
- **Fraud**: Falsifying financial docs can lead to legal consequences and blacklisting from rentals.
- **Reputation damage**: If caught, property managers often share info with other landlords.
- **Lease cancellation**: Even if approved, discovery can void your lease and result in eviction.

---

### ✅ Legit Alternatives:
- **Brokerage Statements**: Show actual trading history, balance, and withdrawals.
- **Letters of Support**: From a CPA or financial advisor verifying consistent income.
- **Advance Payment**: Offer 3–12 months upfront or prepay the lease.
- **Co-Signer**: Adds a layer of trust for landlords.
- **Proof of Assets**: Show other assets (e.g. crypto wallets, real estate equity, etc.).
