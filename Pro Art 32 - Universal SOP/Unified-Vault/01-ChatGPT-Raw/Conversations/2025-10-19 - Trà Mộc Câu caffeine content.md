### Caffeine Content
Yerba mate contains **moderate caffeine**, usually:
- **30–50 mg per 8 oz (240 ml)** cup — roughly similar to **green tea**, and less than **coffee** (which has ~90–120 mg per cup).  
It also contains **theobromine** and **theophylline**, which act as gentle stimulants, giving a smooth, focused energy rather than jittery caffeine spikes.
