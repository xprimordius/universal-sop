1. **Personal vs. Business Representation**:  
   - **Individuals** can represent themselves (this is called *pro se* representation).  
   - **Businesses** typically cannot represent themselves in court unless they are small claims cases or very specific exceptions apply. Courts require businesses to be represented by an attorney because a business is considered a separate legal entity from its owner.
