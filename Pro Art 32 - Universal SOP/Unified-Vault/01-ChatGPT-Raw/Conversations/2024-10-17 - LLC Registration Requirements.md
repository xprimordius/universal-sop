# LLC Registration Requirements

---

## 👤 You

Do you need primary residence in 
The state you register your LLC in?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

No, you do not need to have your primary residence in the state where you register your LLC. Many business owners register their LLCs in states where they do not reside due to business-friendly laws, such as Delaware, Wyoming, or Nevada. However, if your LLC conducts significant business in a state other than where it's registered, you may need to file as a "foreign LLC" in your state of residence or operation. Additionally, you must have a registered agent in the state where your LLC is registered to handle official correspondence.
