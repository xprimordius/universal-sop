## 👤 You

If a tenant decides after 4 years to do a rent to own option, does the purchase price start there? Or does previous rent paid turn into equity off market value

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

In most **rent-to-own** agreements, **past rent payments do not count toward equity** unless specifically agreed upon in writing. Here's the breakdown:

### 1. **Purchase Price Timing**
- **Starts when the rent-to-own agreement is signed.**  
  The price is usually:
  - Locked in at market value **on the date of the agreement**, or  
  - Pre-agreed upon in advance (e.g. “$250,000 in 2 years”).

### 2. **Previous Rent (Last 4 Years)**
- **Does not count toward equity or purchase price** unless:
  - The original or new agreement **explicitly credits part of rent** toward purchase.
  - The landlord agrees retroactively to apply past rent as a credit (rare and negotiable).

### 3. **How Rent Credits Work**
- Typically only apply to **future rent** under the new rent-to-own contract.
- Example: $1,500/month rent with $300/month rent credit = $300 x 24 months = $7,200 toward purchase.

### Summary:
- **Purchase price** = agreed upon **at time of new rent-to-own deal**, not 4 years ago.
- **Past rent** = generally **not applied** toward purchase unless **explicitly negotiated**.
