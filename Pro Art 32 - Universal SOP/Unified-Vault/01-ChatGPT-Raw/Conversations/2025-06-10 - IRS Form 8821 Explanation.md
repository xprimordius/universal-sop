## 👤 You

What is the 8821 form

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**IRS Form 8821** is the **Tax Information Authorization** form.

### TLDR:
It authorizes someone (like a CPA, attorney, or tax advisor) to **receive confidential tax info from the IRS** on your behalf — but **does NOT allow them to act or speak for you** (i.e., no authority to represent you in tax matters or make decisions).

---

### Key Details:
- **Use case:** Authorize third parties (e.g., lenders, bookkeepers, consultants) to **view IRS tax info** (like transcripts, notices, filings).
- **Authority given:** **View-only access** to your IRS records.
- **Not the same as Form 2848** (Power of Attorney), which gives representation rights.
- **Can be revoked** at any time by filing a new 8821 or writing to the IRS.
- **Common with business credit applications**, especially when banks need access to your business tax return records or transcripts.

---

Yes — **Form 8821 allows the authorized party to see your full tax return details**, including:

- **Total revenue / gross receipts**
- **Expenses and deductions**
- **Net income**
- **Taxable income**
- **Tax paid or owed**
- **Balance due or refunds**
- Any IRS account transcripts, return transcripts, wage/income statements, etc.

### TLDR:
Yes — they can **see all your numbers**, but **cannot act on your behalf** or make decisions for you. It's **read-only access** to your IRS records.
