# iPhone Alarm Volume Issue

---

## 👤 You

Why does my iPhone alarm start loud than get quiet?

I wanna be on a discord call but still have the full effects of the alarm loudness

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

The iPhone alarm often starts loud and then quickly becomes quieter due to the **Attention Aware feature**. This feature uses the front-facing camera to detect if you're looking at your phone, reducing the alarm volume once it senses your attention. Additionally, it adjusts the loudness based on environmental factors or other activities on your phone (such as being on a call or a Discord session).

To maintain full alarm loudness while on a Discord call, you can try the following steps:

1. **Disable Attention Aware Features**:
   - Go to **Settings** > **Face ID & Passcode**.
   - Toggle off **Attention Aware Features**. This will prevent the alarm from lowering when the phone detects you're looking at it.

2. **Do Not Disturb & Focus Mode Settings**:
   - Ensure that **Do Not Disturb** or **Focus Mode** is not automatically adjusting your notifications or sounds during the Discord call.
   - Check **Settings** > **Focus** to customize these settings.

3. **Test Alarm with Discord**:
   - Before relying on the alarm, test it with Discord open to see if any sound adjustments happen while you're on the call, ensuring it works as intended. 

These steps should help keep your alarm loud even while on a call.
