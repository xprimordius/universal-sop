## How your monitor normally shows motion

1 frame appears

That’s ghosting.

ELMB does **not** speed this up.  
The pixel chemistry is unchanged.

## Frame visibility time (motion blur)

Now separate layer:

Even if pixels switch instantly…

A frame is still visible for the entire refresh window.

At 240Hz:

frame sits there 4.2 ms

Your eyes are tracking movement during those 4.2 ms.

Your brain smears the image.

That smear is motion blur.

ELMB shortens:

4.2 ms visible  
→ maybe 1 ms flash  
→ 3.2 ms darkness

Pixels still switch at the same speed.

But your eyes only see a quick flash instead of a long hold.

That reduces smear.
