## 👤 You

how is it possible to get a 100% LTC loan for a fix and flip

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**How to Get 100% Loan-to-Cost (LTC) for Fix and Flip:**

### 1. **Use Gap Funding**
- **Structure:** Hard money lender covers 85–90% of purchase + 100% of rehab → You bring a private lender or partner for the 10–15% down.
- **Common Terms:** 10–12% interest, 1–3 points, 6–12 months.
- **Key:** You stack loans—senior (hard money), junior (private capital).

---

### 2. **Bring the Deal to an Equity Partner**
- **Structure:** Equity partner funds 100% of purchase + rehab.
- **You Provide:** The deal, execution, and resale.
- **Split:** 50/50 profit split or agreed equity structure.
- **Tip:** Show them ARV, comps, and construction plan. De-risk the deal.

---

### 3. **Use a JV with the Seller (Creative Finance)**
- **Structure:** Seller finances the deal or delays payment until after the flip (carryback or subject-to).
- **You Invest:** Just rehab costs (or none if seller funds that too).
- **Exit:** Flip and pay seller agreed share/profit.
- **Key:** Works best with motivated sellers, inherited properties, or free-and-clear homes.

---

### 4. **Lender Offers 100% LTC (Rare but Possible)**
- **Conditions:**
  - Proven experience (5–10+ past flips).
  - Strong credit (680+), liquidity, track record.
  - Extremely discounted property (<65% ARV).
- **Source:** Relationship-based private lenders or elite hard money programs.

---

### 5. **Wholesale to Yourself (Double Close or Assignment)**
- **Structure:** Lock up deal at discount. Assign to LLC you own or double close.
- **Result:** Use end-buyer funds (or lender) to fund acquisition + rehab without own cash.
- **Risk:** Must be very tight with title and closing coordination.

---

### 6. **Transactional Funding**
- **Structure:** Use same-day funds to buy, then immediately resell (A→B→C).
- **Works When:** You have a guaranteed end buyer (pre-sold flip or wholesale).
- **Note:** Not true fix-and-flip, more like arbitrage play.

---

### Final Tip:  
**Leverage your value—execution, sourcing, speed, or network—to attract capital.** No money deals are possible, but you must *bring something exceptional* (deal, skill, or relationship).
