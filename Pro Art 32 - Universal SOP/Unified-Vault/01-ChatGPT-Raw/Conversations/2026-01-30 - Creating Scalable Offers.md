The ideal TAM is more than 30,000 in the US and less than 100,000.

We want more than 30,000 so that it gives us some good scale on ads, however, we want less than 100,000 because otherwise our messaging is too broad and we need to narrow it down.

	2	The Outcome
Once we’ve defined a niche that has a TAM between 30,000 - 100,000, we then need to identify that specific markets (coffee brands making over $500k/year as an example) pre-existing desire.

Every market segment has a pre-existing desire. 

When we’re creating offers we’re not trying to create desire in the market, we simply need to look at the market segment and find the pre-existing desire and channel that into the offer we make.

We want to ask the question:

“If I were to ask every person in this market segment what they want.. what would 80% or more tell me? In other words, what is at the forefront of their mind every day?”

The easiest offer framework is: find the markets “broken arm”, take the inverse of that and guarantee that you will give them a big amount of that thing in the immediate future (90-180 days) or they get their money back.
