### How to Repel Woodpeckers (Effective & Humane)

1. **Visual Deterrents (Scare Tactics)**  
   - **Reflective tape or CDs:** Hang shiny, reflective materials that move in the wind.  
   - **Scare-eye balloons:** Large, colorful balloons with predator-like eyes.  
   - **Fake predators:** Owl or hawk decoys (move them periodically so woodpeckers don’t adapt).  

2. **Noise & Vibration**  
   - Motion-activated ultrasonic devices.  
   - Loud intermittent sounds (radios, wind chimes, or recorded predator calls).  

3. **Physical Barriers**  
   - **Netting:** Bird netting installed 3–4 inches from the surface prevents pecking.  
   - **Metal or plastic sheeting:** Cover frequently damaged spots temporarily.  

4. **Remove Attractants**  
   - Treat wood for insect infestations (carpenter ants, termites, larvae).  
   - Fill holes with wood filler to deter return visits.  
   - Avoid leaving suet feeders near structures (attracts them).  

5. **Chemical / Taste Deterrents**  
   - Apply non-toxic sticky bird gels or bitter-tasting sprays on pecked areas (must be reapplied).  

6. **Address Nesting or Drumming Behavior**  
   - If they’re nesting, wait until fledglings leave, then seal openings.  
   - If they’re drumming (territorial signaling), combine visual + noise deterrents for faster results.  

⚠️ **Legal note:** Woodpeckers are protected under the Migratory Bird Treaty Act in the U.S. You cannot harm or kill them. Focus on deterrents only.
