After a **Total and Permanent Disability (TPD) discharge** through **Nelnet**, your federal student loans are **forgiven**, but the way it reports to credit bureaus varies slightly depending on the stage and timing:

---

- **Status of Loans:** Once discharged, your loans will be updated to reflect **“Paid in full through loan discharge”** or similar language.
- **Balance:** Will be **reduced to $0.**

---

- Credit reports will reflect that the loans were discharged **conditionally**.
- No new balances accrue or are reported unless you're found ineligible during monitoring.

---

### 🔹 **After Monitoring Period Ends**
If no disqualifying events occur:
- The status may update to reflect **final discharge.**
- The discharged debt will **not return to collections** or reopen.

---

### 🔹 **Important Notes**
- **Late Payments:** If your loans were delinquent before discharge, those late marks typically remain unless disputed or removed by goodwill or other means.
- **No Negative Remarks for Discharge:** TPD discharge itself is **not negative** and doesn’t hurt your score—many borrowers see their score improve.
- **Nelnet does not report discharge as bankruptcy, charge-off, or default** if the discharge was approved.

Here’s how **banking institutions typically view a Nelnet TPD discharge** when reviewing **high-limit credit approvals**—for both **personal** and **business (PG)** applications:

---

### 🔹 **1. Personal Credit (PG or Personal Loans)**
**What lenders see:**
- **$0 balance**, account closed, **"discharged" or "paid via government program"** status.
- Full **payment history still visible** (any prior lates or defaults remain unless removed).

**Impact on high-limit approvals:**
- **No negative weight** if the account is marked "paid/discharged" and you’re current on everything else.
- **Key risk factors:**
  - **Recent late payments** pre-discharge can reduce limits or cause denials.
  - **No active installment loans post-discharge** can weaken credit mix/score due to no open-term debt.

**Underwriting view:**
- Most lenders **do not penalize** for TPD discharge if credit utilization is low, scores are high, and payment history is clean.
- If late marks exist, underwriters may flag them unless context is explained or they’re removed.

---

### 🔹 **2. Business Credit (With Personal Guarantee)**
**What banks care about:**
- **Your personal credit report and FICO** (usually 680–740+ for no-doc $25K–$50K cards).
- Clean tradeline history, high limits, and responsible revolving use.

**Impact:**
- **TPD discharge itself is neutral** as long as it's:
  - Marked as **“paid in full via discharge”**
  - **No current defaults or collections**
- If your file shows **strong recent activity**, high personal limits, and low utilization, the TPD won’t matter.
- **Personal derogatories** (like pre-discharge 90+ day lates) can kill or limit approvals unless offset by strong business financials.

---

### 🔹 **3. What to Do to Strengthen Your Profile Post-TPD**
