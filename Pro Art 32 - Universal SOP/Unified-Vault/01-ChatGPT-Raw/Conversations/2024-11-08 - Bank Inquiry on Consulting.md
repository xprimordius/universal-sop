Banks often ask if you do financial consulting for other businesses because certain consulting services—especially those involving financial advice—are subject to stricter regulations and compliance checks. Here are a few key reasons for this:

2. **Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Requirements**: Financial consulting is sometimes associated with transactions that could trigger AML protocols. Banks want to ensure they comply with AML laws by understanding whether you handle sensitive financial transactions for clients.

3. **Compliance with Licensing Requirements**: Certain types of financial consulting require specific licenses (e.g., FINRA for investment advising). By clarifying the nature of your consulting, banks ensure you meet the necessary legal standards and avoid liability.

4. **Enhanced Due Diligence**: If you provide financial consulting, banks may conduct enhanced due diligence, as financial consultants might deal with high-value transactions, client assets, or sensitive financial data.

By understanding if your consulting involves financial advice, banks can appropriately categorize your business, assess potential risks, and ensure compliance with regulatory obligations.
