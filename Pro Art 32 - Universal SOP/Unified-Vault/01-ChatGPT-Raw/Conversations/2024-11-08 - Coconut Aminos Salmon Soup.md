Here’s a straightforward coconut aminos salmon soup recipe:

### Ingredients
- 1 lb salmon fillet, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 tbsp coconut oil or olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1-inch piece of ginger, minced
- 4 cups fish or vegetable broth
- 1 can (13.5 oz) coconut milk
- 2 tbsp coconut aminos
- 1 tbsp lime juice (optional, for brightness)
- 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
- 1 cup baby spinach
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro and green onions, chopped, for garnish

### Instructions
1. **Sauté Aromatics**: Heat coconut oil in a pot over medium heat. Add the diced onion and cook until translucent (3-5 minutes). Stir in garlic and ginger, cooking until fragrant.
2. **Add Broth and Coconut Milk**: Pour in the broth and coconut milk, stirring to combine. Bring to a simmer.
3. **Flavor and Add Salmon**: Stir in coconut aminos and lime juice. Season with salt and pepper. Add the salmon pieces, simmering gently for 5-7 minutes until cooked through.
4. **Add Vegetables**: Add mushrooms and cook for a few minutes until tender. Stir in spinach until just wilted.
5. **Serve**: Ladle into bowls and garnish with fresh cilantro and green onions.

Enjoy your nourishing coconut aminos salmon soup!
