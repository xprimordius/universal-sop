# Chase Ink Business Pull

---

## 👤 You

if I get approved for chase ink business unlimited, does this do a hard pull?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes, applying for the Chase Ink Business Unlimited card will typically result in a hard pull on your credit report. A hard pull, or hard inquiry, occurs when a lender checks your credit as part of their decision-making process. This can temporarily lower your credit score by a few points. It's a standard practice for most credit card applications to perform a hard inquiry.

## 👤 You

if I get approved for this, can I use the same pull to get approved for other products?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

When you apply for multiple credit products from Chase (or any other bank) within a short period, typically within the same day, it's possible that the bank might use the same hard pull for each application. However, this isn't guaranteed and depends on the bank's internal policies. Here are a few things to consider:

1. **Timing**: Applying for multiple products on the same day increases the likelihood of using the same hard pull.
2. **Product Type**: The possibility of using the same hard pull is higher when applying for similar types of credit products (e.g., multiple credit cards) rather than a mix of different types (e.g., a credit card and a loan).
3. **Bank Policies**: Each bank has its own policies regarding hard inquiries, and these policies can change. It's best to check directly with Chase or review recent user experiences for the most accurate information.

If you plan to apply for multiple Chase products, you might consider calling Chase customer service to inquire about their current policy on using the same credit inquiry for multiple applications.

is it common to get approved for a credit card, and then ask for another credit card an line of credit?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

It is relatively common for individuals and businesses to apply for multiple credit products, such as credit cards and lines of credit, within a short period, especially if they have strong credit profiles. However, there are several considerations to keep in mind:

### Timing and Strategy
1. **Same-Day Applications**:
   - Applying for multiple Chase products on the same day can sometimes result in only one hard pull on your credit report.
   - This strategy minimizes the impact on your credit score.

2. **Sequential Applications**:
   - If you apply for a second product shortly after the first, it may still be counted as a single inquiry if it's within a very short time frame.
   - This is not guaranteed, and policies may vary between banks and over time.

### Considerations for Multiple Applications
1. **Credit Score Impact**:
   - Each application typically results in a hard inquiry, which can lower your credit score slightly.
   - Multiple inquiries in a short period can have a more significant impact.

2. **Bank's Internal Policies**:
   - Banks may have internal guidelines regarding the approval of multiple products.
   - Existing relationships and recent account activity with the bank can influence approval decisions.

3. **Approval Odds**:
   - Having a strong credit score, high income, and a good relationship with the bank increases your chances of approval for multiple products.
   - If you have recently been approved for a credit card, you may have a higher chance of getting approved for another card or line of credit, but it also depends on your overall credit profile.

### Practical Steps
1. **Review Your Credit Profile**:
   - Ensure your credit score is strong and your credit report is accurate before applying for multiple products.

2. **Consider Pre-Qualification**:
   - Use pre-qualification tools to gauge your likelihood of approval without impacting your credit score.

3. **Contact Customer Service**:
   - Call Chase customer service to inquire about their policies on applying for multiple products and whether they can use the same hard inquiry for multiple applications.

4. **Space Out Applications**:
   - If you are concerned about multiple hard inquiries, consider spacing out your applications by a few months.

1. **Apply for Another Business Credit Card**: If your business needs align, you could apply for the Chase Ink Business Cash or Chase Ink Business Preferred on the same day.

3. **Personal Credit Products**: If you also need personal credit products, you might apply for a Chase Freedom Unlimited or Chase Sapphire Preferred card.

### Summary
While it is common to apply for multiple credit products, ensure you have a strategic approach to minimize the impact on your credit score and maximize your chances of approval. Always check the latest bank policies and consider pre-qualification options to make informed decisions.
