## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Compression socks should be snug but not painful.** Here’s the ideal:

### 🔒 Proper Fit:
- **Firm pressure** without cutting off circulation.
- **No numbness, tingling, or pain.**
- **No deep marks** left on your skin after removal (mild indentation is normal).
- Should not **roll down** or **bunch up**—this causes localized pressure and reduces effectiveness.

### 📏 Compression Levels (mmHg):
- **15–20 mmHg:** Light compression (mild swelling, travel, daily wear).
- **20–30 mmHg:** Medical-grade (varicose veins, mild DVT risk, long hours standing).
- **30–40 mmHg+:** High medical-grade (prescribed for severe venous issues or lymphedema).

### ✅ Test:
- Slide a finger under the sock at the top. It should be difficult, but not impossible.
- If toes tingle or turn cold/purple → **too tight**.
