## 🤖 ChatGPT

Apple’s official MagSafe charger delivers **up to 15 W** to compatible iPhones (iPhone 12 and later), but only **up to 7.5 W** for older models or when using non-Apple MagSafe-compatible accessories.  

The actual wattage depends on:
- **Power adapter** — Apple recommends a 20 W USB-C adapter or higher to get the full 15 W.  
- **Heat** — Charging slows if the phone gets warm.  
- **Background activity** — High usage while charging can reduce net charge rate.
