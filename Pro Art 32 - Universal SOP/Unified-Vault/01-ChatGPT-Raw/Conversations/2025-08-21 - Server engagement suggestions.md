## 🔑 Core Activity Drivers
1. **Events (Live Energy)**
   - Game nights, tournaments, watch parties, trivia.
   - Small but regular > big and rare.

2. **Roles & Rewards (Gamify It)**
   - Activity = XP = rewards (custom roles, emotes, access to VIP channels).
   - Example: “Top 3 chatters of the week get featured.”

3. **Content Hooks**
   - Daily prompts (memes, polls, “question of the day”).
   - Clips / highlights from games members play.  

4. **Forums & Channels by Demand**
   - Only open channels/categories if at least 5+ members request it.
   - Use polls → “Who wants a horror-games forum?” 

5. **Member Spotlights**
   - Highlight a member each week (interview, achievements, funny moment).
   - Builds belonging → people come back for recognition.

6. **Collab & Cross-Promo**
   - Partner with similar communities for joint events.
   - Guest Q&As or raids (if gaming-related).

7. **Leadership Energy**
   - Mods set the vibe → if mods chat, others follow.
   - Lead with humor, genuine convos, and consistency.

3. **Question of the Day / Prompts**  
   - Bots: *Tatsu, Apollo, or custom bots with scheduled messages*  
   - Automation: Bot posts daily/weekly prompts without manual effort.

4. **Events (Game Nights, Movie Nights, etc.)**  
   - Bot: *Apollo* (event scheduling/reminders).  
   - Automation: Creates events + reminders, tracks RSVPs.

5. **Giveaways**  
   - Bots: *GiveawayBot, MEE6*  
   - Automation: Timed giveaways → winners chosen automatically.

6. **Forums / Channel Creation**  
   - Not fully automated (mods still need to create forums), but…

7. **Welcome & Engagement Pings**  
   - Bots: *Carl-bot, MEE6, Dyno*
