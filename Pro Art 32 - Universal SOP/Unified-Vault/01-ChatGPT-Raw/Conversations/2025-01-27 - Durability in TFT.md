## 🤖 ChatGPT

In **Teamfight Tactics (TFT)**, **Durability** generally refers to a unit's ability to withstand damage and stay alive longer in combat. This concept is influenced by the following factors:

1. **Health (HP):** Higher health means the unit can take more damage before dying.
2. **Armor:** Reduces the amount of physical damage taken from attacks and abilities.
3. **Magic Resistance (MR):** Reduces the amount of magic damage taken from abilities.
4. **Healing and Regeneration:** Some units have healing, lifesteal, or self-regeneration mechanics, increasing their longevity.
5. **Shields:** Temporary health boosts from shields can make units more durable.
6. **Damage Reduction:** Some traits, items, or abilities directly reduce incoming damage (e.g., Bramble Vest or traits like Brawler).

In gameplay, durable units are typically used in the **frontline** to soak up damage and protect squishier, high-damage **backline** units. Building durability into your team comp is crucial for maintaining a balanced and effective strategy.
