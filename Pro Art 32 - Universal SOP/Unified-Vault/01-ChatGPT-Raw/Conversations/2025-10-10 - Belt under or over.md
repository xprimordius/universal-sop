What is is called when the belt goes underneath vs over to keep it hidden

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

That’s called a **“belt loop under tuck”** or **“belt under method.”**  

In fashion and tailoring terms:
- When the belt passes **underneath** the waistband flap or loops to stay hidden, it’s often referred to as a **“concealed belt,” “hidden belt,”** or **“internal belt style.”**
- When it’s worn **over** and visible, it’s just a **standard belt style.**

You’ll see the hidden/under style used in **dress pants, tailoring, or tactical clothing** for a cleaner silhouette with no visible buckle.
