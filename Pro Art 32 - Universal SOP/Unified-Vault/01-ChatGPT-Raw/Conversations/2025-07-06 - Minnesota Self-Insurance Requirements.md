**Key Points from the Official MN DPS Vehicle Insurance Page (Source Verified):**

### ➜ **Insurance Requirements (Minnesota)**
- **Mandatory Liability Insurance:**  
Required for all vehicles driven on Minnesota roads.

- **Minimum Coverages:**
  - **$30,000** per person (bodily injury)
  - **$60,000** per accident (bodily injury)
  - **$10,000** (property damage)

- **Additional Required Coverages:**
  - **Personal Injury Protection (PIP)**
  - **Uninsured Motorist Coverage (UM)**
  - **Underinsured Motorist Coverage (UIM)**

- **Proof Required:**  
Must carry proof of insurance in vehicle; failure may result in:
  - Fines
  - Suspended registration
  - Revoked license

---

### ➜ **Self-Insurance Option (Precise Definition):**
You may qualify for **self-insurance** by applying with the **Minnesota Department of Public Safety (DPS)** and proving financial responsibility.

#### ➥ Requirements:
1. Sufficient financial resources to cover liability risks.
2. Apply through DPS for self-insurance certificate.

#### ➥ Purpose:
Primarily used by large companies or fleet operators—not typical for personal use.

[Official MN DPS Vehicle Insurance Requirements](https://dps.mn.gov/divisions/dvs/vehicle/vehicle-registration#automobile-insurance-requirements-)

**How to Apply for Self-Insurance Certification in Minnesota (Exact Process):**

### ➜ 1. **Contact Minnesota Department of Public Safety (DPS)**
- Division: **Driver and Vehicle Services (DVS)**
- Phone: (651) 296-6911
- Email: dvs.motor.vehicles@state.mn.us

---

### ➜ 2. **Submit a Formal Application**
- Request the **Self-Insurance Application Form** from DVS.
- You’ll need to submit:
  - **Financial statements** (proof of financial ability to cover liability).
  - List of **all vehicles** under self-insurance.
  - Statement of intent and purpose.

---

### ➜ 3. **Financial Proof Required:**
- Demonstrate sufficient financial net worth or assets.
- No specific public dollar minimum listed, but it must cover **liability limits**:
  - $30,000 per person / $60,000 per accident (bodily injury)
  - $10,000 property damage
  - Plus PIP, UM, UIM coverage.

---

### ➜ 4. **Approval & Certification:**
- If approved, you’ll receive a **Certificate of Self-Insurance** from DPS.
- You must carry this certificate in your vehicle(s) at all times.

---

### ➜ 5. **Annual Renewal & Reporting:**
- Annual review required.
- Must update financial documents and vehicle lists yearly.

---

### ⚡ **Key Notes:**
- Typically only approved for:
  - Large fleets.
  - Corporations with strong financial backing.
- Rarely approved for individuals.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Minnesota Penalties for Driving Without Insurance (No Accident, Routine Stop):**

### ➜ **1st Offense (Standard Penalty)**
- **Misdemeanor** (criminal offense).
- **Fines:**
  - Minimum **$200** fine (can exceed **$1,000** with court costs).
- **Additional Penalties:**
  - License suspension **until proof of insurance** + reinstatement fee.
  - Vehicle registration may also be suspended.
  - **$30** reinstatement fee (minimum; may vary by case).
- Must file **SR-22 insurance** (proof of future financial responsibility) in some cases.

---

### ➜ **Court Discretion:**
- Courts may allow **fine reduction** if you obtain insurance before your court date.
- Possible **community service** alternative to fine.

---

### ➜ **Key Statute:**  
Minnesota Statutes § 169.797 — Prohibits driving without insurance.

---

### **TL;DR:**  
Expect **$200+ fine**, license suspension, possible SR-22 filing—even if no accident. Penalties escalate with repeat offenses.
