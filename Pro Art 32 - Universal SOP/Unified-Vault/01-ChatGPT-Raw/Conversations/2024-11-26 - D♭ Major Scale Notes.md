The notes **B♭, E♭, A♭, D♭, and G♭** correspond to the **D♭ major scale**.

The D♭ major scale consists of the following notes:

**D♭, E♭, F, G♭, A♭, B♭, C, D♭**.

is this the same as c sharp major

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes, **D♭ major** and **C♯ major** are **enharmonic equivalents**, meaning they sound the same on most instruments but are written differently. 

The **C♯ major scale** consists of:

**C♯, D♯, E♯ (F), F♯, G♯, A♯, B♯ (C), C♯**.

It uses **seven sharps** (F♯, C♯, G♯, D♯, A♯, E♯, B♯), while **D♭ major** uses **five flats** (B♭, E♭, A♭, D♭, G♭). 

They are typically used in different contexts depending on the musical situation or the preference of the composer.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

If the **Tuner** shows the correct note but the **Piano Roll** displays it incorrectly, it's likely due to one of the following issues:

### 1. **Octave Mismatch**
   - The computer keyboard often maps to a specific octave by default, which might not align with the octave you are using in the Piano Roll.
   - **Solution**: Use the `Z` and `X` keys to shift the octave down and up, respectively, until the notes played on the computer keyboard match the desired range in the Piano Roll.

---

### 2. **Incorrect Scale Mapping**
   - If you've applied a **Scale MIDI Effect**, it might adjust the incoming notes to fit the scale, causing the Piano Roll to display a corrected note.
   - **Solution**: Verify that the **Scale MIDI Effect** is set to **C# Major** and confirm the notes in the Scale matrix match the key.

---

### 3. **MIDI Input Configuration**
   - Ensure your **Computer MIDI Keyboard** is configured correctly.
   - **Solution**: Check the following:
     1. In the MIDI track, click the **Input Monitor (In)** button to hear the played notes live.
     2. Ensure the **Input Channel** is set to "All Ins" or the channel for your computer MIDI keyboard.

---

### 4. **Clip Folding**
   - If the Piano Roll is folded to show only specific notes, any notes outside of this selection may appear "incorrect."
   - **Solution**: Unfold the Piano Roll by deselecting **Fold** and manually check if the notes are being placed in the correct scale and octave.

---

### 5. **MIDI Pitch Effect**
   - If you've accidentally added a **Pitch** effect in the MIDI chain, it might be transposing the notes.
   - **Solution**: Remove or reset the **Pitch** MIDI Effect if present.

---

If these solutions don't resolve the issue, you might want to revisit the **Piano Roll note setup** or reset your MIDI mappings. Let me know if you need further help!
