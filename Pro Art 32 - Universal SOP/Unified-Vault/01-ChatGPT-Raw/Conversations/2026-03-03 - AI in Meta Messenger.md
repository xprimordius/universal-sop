**2) GPT (OpenAI)**
- Balanced **generalist powerhouse** with very high accuracy across language, reasoning, code, math, and multimodal. citeturn0search3

**4) Gemini (Google)**
- Designed for **deep reasoning + multimodal understanding** (text, images, code) with very large context sizes. citeturn0search3
