**Revenue-Based Business Credit & Capital Options — TL;DR with Strategy**

### 1) **Revenue-Based Financing (RBF)** — Flexible, revenue-tied capital  
- Capital in exchange for a **percentage of future revenue** until a cap is met (no fixed monthly payments). citeturn0search7turn0search18  
- Payments rise/fall with business cash flow — good if revenue fluctuates. citeturn0search7  
- Not equity; you **retain ownership**. citeturn0search6

**Use-cases:** growth capital, marketing, inventory, scaling operations. citeturn0search20  
**Pros:** fast funding, flexible repayment, no collateral/equity dilution. citeturn0search7  
**Cons:** can be **higher total cost** than traditional loans. citeturn0search8

**Common Providers in the market:**  
- Biz2Credit  
- Kapitus  
- Credibly  
- Square Capital  
- Shopify Capital  
- PayPal Working Capital citeturn0search11

**Examples of RBF variants / similar products:**  
- **Merchant Cash Advance (MCA):** upfront cash repaid via revenue share; very fast but often costly. citeturn0search14turn0news46  
- **Business cash advance / working capital advance:** similar to RBF but may vary repayment frequency and terms. citeturn0search14

**Who it suits:**  
- Businesses generating consistent revenue.  
- Asset-light businesses (e-commerce, SaaS).  
- Those needing growth capital without fixed repayment pressure.

---

### 2) **Revenue-Based Business Loans** — Cash flow loans  
- Borrow a lump sum based on **past revenue performance**; repay with a % of future revenue. citeturn0search26  
- Similar to RBF but structured more like a loan with a revenue-linked payment mechanism. citeturn0search26

**Position:** More “loan-like” than pure RBF; still tied to revenue streams.

---

### 3) **Business Line of Credit** — Revolving credit  
- A **rolling credit limit** you draw as needed; pay interest only on amount used. citeturn0search2turn0search27  
- Not strictly revenue-based, but approval and limit often depend on **business revenue** and cash flow. citeturn0search16

**Use-cases:**  
- Bridge cash-flow gaps  
- Seasonal expenses  
- Inventory purchases  
- Unplanned costs

**Pros:** flexible draw/repay; reusable credit. citeturn0search2  
**Types:**  
- **Unsecured lines:** no collateral (Bank of America, Bluevine, etc.). citeturn0search30turn0search22  
- **Secured lines:** backed by assets (better rates, higher limits). citeturn0search27

---

### 4) **Invoice Financing / Factoring** — Receivables-backed cash  
- Sell or borrow against unpaid invoices for immediate capital. citeturn0search12  
- Funding tied to revenue accounts receivable rather than future revenue percentage.

---

### Strategic Snapshot — When to Choose What

| Goal | Best Option | Why |
|------|-------------|-----|
| Predictable repayment aligned to cash flow | **Revenue-Based Financing** | Payments flex with sales. citeturn0search7 |
| Flexible, reusable funding | **Business Line of Credit** | Draw/repay as needed. citeturn0search2 |
| Monetize unpaid invoices | **Invoice Factoring** | Get cash fast against receivables. citeturn0search12 |
| Quick bridge for seasonal or short gaps | **Merchant Cash Advance / Working Capital Advance** | Fast access; revenue linked. citeturn0search14 |
