- **Crystal-clear voice** (much better than gaming headsets)
- **No headset pressure** (great for long sessions)
- **Perfect for streaming, Discord, content creation**

---

- **Latency**:  
  - ~20–25 ms  
  - Totally fine for **voice chat**, **streaming**, **casual gaming**  
  - Not ideal for **rhythm games** or ultra-competitive FPS audio sync nerds

- **Monitoring**:  
  - You’ll still need **separate headphones** for game audio  
  - Mic does *not* replace a headset

---

### Best Setup (Recommended)
**PC Gaming**
- Receiver → **USB-C into PC**
- Set **DJI Mic 2** as input in:
  - Windows
  - Discord
  - OBS / Streamlabs

**Console Gaming**
- Plug receiver into:
  - PS5 / Xbox USB port **or**
  - Controller 3.5mm jack (may require TRRS adapter)

- ✅ Rode Lavalier GO

## The Mirror Test (Gold Standard)
**Fast. Accurate. Zero tools.**

### What you need
- A **small mirror**
- A **second person** (or phone on selfie video)
- You sitting at your mic position

### Steps
1. Sit exactly where you record.
2. Have the other person slide the mirror **flat against the wall**.
3. Wherever **you can see the mic capsule** in the mirror from your seated position  
   → **that spot is an early reflection point**.
4. Mark it with tape.
5. Repeat for:
   - Left wall
   - Right wall
   - Ceiling
   - Desk surface (often overlooked)

**Those marked spots = where pads go.**

---

## Primary Early Reflection Zones (Priority Order)

### 1️⃣ Side walls (MOST important)
- Left + right of your head
- At mouth / mic height

### 2️⃣ Ceiling “cloud”
- Directly above mic → your mouth
- Huge clarity improvement

### 3️⃣ Desk reflection
- Flat desks bounce sound straight into mic
- Fix with:
  - Desk mat
  - Felt pad
  - Angled mic position

### 4️⃣ Front wall (behind mic)
- Important if wall is close
- Especially for dynamic mics like SM7dB

### 5️⃣ Back wall (behind you)
- Less “early,” but affects tone
- Thicker absorption or bookshelf helps

---

## Pad Type & Thickness (Critical)
**Do NOT use thin foam tiles for voice authority.**

### Minimum specs for voice
- **2–4 inches thick**
- Rockwool / mineral wool / dense panels
- NRC ≥ 0.8

Thin foam = placebo for low-mid reflections.

---

## Mic-Specific Tips (SM7dB / Broadcast)
- Early reflections still matter even with dynamics
- Dynamics reduce room pickup, **not eliminate reflections**
- Panels tighten voice → “expensive” sound
