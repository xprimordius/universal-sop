How to keep Apple Watch on Strava when flick wrist instead of clicking and unlock

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

To keep your **Apple Watch screen on during Strava** workouts when you **flick your wrist**, and **avoid needing to tap or unlock**, do the following:

---

### **1. Set Apple Watch to “Wake on Wrist Raise”**
- Open **Settings** on Apple Watch  
- Go to **Display & Brightness**  
- Ensure **Wake on Wrist Raise** is **ON**  
- Set **Wake Duration** to **70 Seconds** (max allowed)

---

### **2. Turn Off Wrist Detection (Optional)**  
*(Disables auto-lock, but also disables Apple Pay and some health tracking)*  
- On your **iPhone** → Open **Watch app**  
- Go to **Passcode**  
- Toggle **Wrist Detection** **OFF**

---

### **3. Use “Workout Power Saving Mode” (Optional)**  
This helps battery last longer during GPS runs.  
- On iPhone → Watch app  
- Go to **Workout**  
- Enable **Power Saving Mode**

---

### **4. Use “Always On” Display (for supported models)**  
- Settings → **Display & Brightness**  
- Tap **Always On** → **ON**  
- Disable “Hide Complications” and “Hide Apps”

---

### **5. While Using Strava:**
- Start workout in **Strava app**  
- Raise wrist gently, screen should stay on longer  
- Avoid pressing Digital Crown unless needed
