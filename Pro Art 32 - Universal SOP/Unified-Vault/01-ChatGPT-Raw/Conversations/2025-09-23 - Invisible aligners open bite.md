---

### Why Aligner32 Said No
- **Open bites are complex.** They involve vertical tooth/jaw discrepancies, not just simple crowding or spacing.  
- Many direct-to-consumer or “light aligner” systems (Aligner32, SmileDirectClub, Byte, etc.) limit cases to mild/moderate alignment only.

---

### Options That *Do* Work
1. **Invisalign (Comprehensive / Full)**
   - Widely considered the gold standard for aligners treating open bites.  
   - Uses attachments (small tooth-colored bumps) and elastics if needed.  
   - Can address vertical issues with precision cuts and staging.

2. **CandidPro (dentist-supervised)**
   - More advanced than SmileDirect, but depends on your provider.  
   - Can sometimes handle open bites if attachments/elastics are included.

3. **ClearCorrect (by Straumann)**
   - Doctor-directed like Invisalign.  
   - Capable of treating open bites, again depending on severity.

---

### When Aligners May *Not* Be Enough
- **Severe skeletal open bites** (jaw-related, not just teeth) often require braces with elastics, TADs (mini-implants), or even surgery.  
- Your X-ray suggests an orthodontist is properly evaluating — they’ll know if aligners alone can close your bite.

---

✅ **Bottom line:**
