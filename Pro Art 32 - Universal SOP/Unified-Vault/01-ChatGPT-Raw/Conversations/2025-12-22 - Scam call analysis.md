## 👤 You

Happy Monday, this is Morgan Hale, calling from the account risk resolution department at the tax relief and resolution agency on the 22nd. I'm reaching out because your account remains unresolved and has been flagged for immediate review. If left open, this may result in additional charges or enforced collection measures. Recent reviews show many individuals qualifying for adjusted balances or full resolution, but availability is limited as year in cutoffs approach. Please return my call today at 888-853-1809. Again, 888-853-1809. If this message reached you in error. Press 2 when calling back.is this a scam

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Yes — this is almost certainly a scam.**

### Why it’s a scam (red flags)
- **Generic agency name** (“tax relief and resolution agency”) — no real firm identifies itself this way.
- **Urgent threat language** (“immediate review,” “enforced collection”) — classic pressure tactic.
- **No personalization** — no IRS notice number, tax year, or your name.
- **Voicemail-first contact** — the IRS **never** initiates enforcement by phone.
- **Call-back funnel** with “press 2” — used to route victims to sales closers.
- **“Limited availability / year-end cutoffs”** — fake scarcity.

### What to do
- **Do not call back.**
- **Block the number.**
- **Report it** to the FTC (reportfraud.ftc.gov) and your phone carrier.
- If you’re concerned about taxes, **contact the IRS directly** using the number on irs.gov or a letter you received.

### Bottom line
Legitimate tax enforcement comes **by mail first**, with specific details. This message is designed to scare you into calling. Ignore it.

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Yes — if you don’t renew, you must retake the exam.**

### CIPP/US renewal rules (clear + exact)
- CIPP/US requires **annual renewal** (fees + CPEs).
- If you **let it lapse beyond the grace period**, the certification becomes **inactive**.
- To regain it after lapse: **you must retake and pass the CIPP/US exam**.
- There is **no reinstatement** option once it fully expires.

### Bottom line
Stay current = keep cert.  
Let it lapse = **exam again**.
