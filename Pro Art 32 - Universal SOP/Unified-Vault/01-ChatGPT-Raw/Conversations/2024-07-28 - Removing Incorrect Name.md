# Removing Incorrect Name

---

remove wrong name from credit report

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

To remove an incorrect name from your credit report, follow these steps:

1. **Review Your Credit Reports:**
   - Obtain your credit reports from the three major credit bureaus: Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion. You can get a free report from each bureau annually at [AnnualCreditReport.com](https://www.annualcreditreport.com).

2. **Identify the Errors:**
   - Carefully review each report to identify the incorrect name or any other inaccuracies.

3. **Gather Documentation:**
   - Collect any documentation that proves your correct name (e.g., driver's license, Social Security card, birth certificate).

4. **Contact the Credit Bureaus:**
   - Write a dispute letter to each credit bureau that has the incorrect name listed. Include:
     - Your full name, address, and contact information.
     - A clear description of the error.
     - Copies of documents that support your claim (do not send originals).
     - A copy of the credit report with the incorrect name highlighted.

5. **Send Your Dispute:**
   - Mail your dispute letter to each credit bureau's address for disputes:
     - **Equifax:** 
       Equifax Information Services LLC
       P.O. Box 740256
       Atlanta, GA 30374
     - **Experian:** 
       Experian
       P.O. Box 4500
       Allen, TX 75013
     - **TransUnion:** 
       TransUnion Consumer Solutions
       P.O. Box 2000
       Chester, PA 19016

6. **Wait for a Response:**
   - The credit bureaus typically have 30 days to investigate your dispute. They will notify you of the results and provide a free copy of your updated credit report if a change is made.

7. **Follow Up:**
   - If the incorrect name is not removed, follow up with the credit bureau. You can also file a complaint with the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) if necessary.

8. **Monitor Your Credit Reports:**
   - Regularly check your credit reports to ensure the incorrect name and other inaccuracies do not reappear.

By following these steps, you can effectively address and remove an incorrect name from your credit report.
