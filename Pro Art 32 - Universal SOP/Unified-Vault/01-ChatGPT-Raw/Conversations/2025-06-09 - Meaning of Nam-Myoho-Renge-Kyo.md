## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Nam-Myoho-Renge-Kyo** is a sacred phrase (mantra) from **Nichiren Buddhism**, rooted in the **Lotus Sutra**, and is chanted as a central practice for transforming one's life and accessing inner power.

### **Breakdown & Meaning:**

1. **Nam (南無)**  
   - From Sanskrit "Namas"  
   - Means: **Devotion**, **dedication**, or **to take refuge in**

2. **Myoho (妙法)**  
   - "Myo" = **Mystic / Wonderful**  
   - "Ho" = **Law / Dharma**  
   - Together: **The Mystic Law of life** — the unseen cause-and-effect force governing all phenomena

3. **Renge (蓮華)**  
   - "Ren" = **Lotus**  
   - "Ge" = **Flower**  
   - Symbol: **The simultaneity of cause and effect** (lotus blooms and seeds at once) — also represents purity and enlightenment amid life’s muddy struggles

4. **Kyo (経)**  
   - Means: **Sutra / Teaching / Sound**  
   - Refers to: **The eternal truth or universal teaching**, especially as found in the Lotus Sutra

---

### **Full Meaning (Concise):**  
**"I devote myself to the Mystic Law of Cause and Effect through the Lotus Sutra."**

Or:

**"I awaken and align with the ultimate universal truth that transforms life from within."**

### **Purpose:**  
Chanting it is believed to activate your highest life state — **courage, wisdom, compassion, and clarity** — and create positive transformation internally and externally.
