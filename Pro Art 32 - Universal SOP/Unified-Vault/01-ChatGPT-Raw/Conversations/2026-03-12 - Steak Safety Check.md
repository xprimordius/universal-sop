## 1. The Only Reliable Test — Temperature
The definitive way to know it isn’t raw is **internal temperature**.

For whole beef steaks:

- **130–135°F (54–57°C)** → Medium-rare

- **145°F + 3 min rest** → USDA “safe minimum”

Authoritative source:  
USDA FSIS Safe Temperature Chart  
https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart

Important USDA fact:

> “Color is not a reliable indicator of doneness for beef.”

Source:  
https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/meat/color-meat-and-poultry

---

## 2. Why Steak Can Look “Raw” But Isn’t
The red color comes from **myoglobin**, not blood.

Myoglobin stays red even when cooked at medium-rare temperatures.  
This is why high-quality steak often looks very red inside.

Scientific explanation:  
USDA Meat Color Guide  
https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/meat/color-meat-and-poultry
