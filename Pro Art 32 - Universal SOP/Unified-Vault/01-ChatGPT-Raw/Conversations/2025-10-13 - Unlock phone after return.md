## 🤖 ChatGPT

Headline: Quick checks to confirm your iPhone is unlocked — 60 seconds.

1) Fastest — Settings check (iOS 14.3+)
- Open **Settings → General → About**.  
- Look for **Carrier Lock**.  
  - If it says **“No SIM restrictions”** → **Unlocked**.  
  - Any other message (e.g., carrier name or “SIM locked”) → **Likely locked**.

2) Hands-on test (most reliable)
- Power off, insert a **SIM card from a different carrier**, power on.  
  - If phone connects and makes/receives calls or cellular data works → **Unlocked**.  
  - If you get “SIM Not Supported” or “Invalid SIM” → **Locked**.

3) IMEI lookup (when you want a carrier confirmation)
- Find IMEI: **Settings → General → About** or dial **`*#06#`**.  
- Contact current carrier and ask them to check IMEI status, or use a reputable IMEI-check service (avoid sketchy sites).

4) eSIM note
- If you use eSIM, go **Settings → Cellular** and check if you can add a different eSIM/carrier profile — inability to add may indicate restrictions.

5) If stuck / next step
- Carrier lockout after unpaid balance or carrier finance may require contacting the carrier and requesting an unlock (they’ll tell you eligibility & process).
