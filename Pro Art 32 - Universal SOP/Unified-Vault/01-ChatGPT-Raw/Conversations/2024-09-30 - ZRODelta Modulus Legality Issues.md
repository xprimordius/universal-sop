> 
> “The Modulus was a billet-aluminum-frame style of this pistol,” Eli Duckworth with ZRO Delta’s marketing team told Guns.com. “We went to polymer now, and it still retains all of its modularity. > The gun includes three different dust covers, two different backstraps and front straps, and three different magwells. Grip modules will be available in gray, black, and FDE. Keeping the same slide, which is now a 【57†Gen 5】 design and supports most of those aftermarket parts, you can swap everything else out. The frame itself will also be available as a stand-alone option.

> [Image 1: ZRO Delta The One Handgun Rifle System] The polymer grip frame slides into the PCU much like it would a handgun slide. (Photo: Paul Peterson/Guns.com)

>
