# Secondary Bureau Student Loans

---

## 👤 You

What secondary bureau(s) have student loan information

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Secondary bureaus that commonly have student loan information include:

1. **Innovis:** A credit reporting agency that often has similar data to the major bureaus but may include additional information related to student loans.

2. **CoreLogic Credco:** This bureau collects data on consumer credit information, including student loans, and provides it to lenders for mortgage and other credit-related decisions.

3. **LexisNexis:** They maintain public records and other data sources, including information about student loans, which can be used by lenders and other entities for credit evaluation.

4. **SageStream (Now part of LexisNexis Risk Solutions):** It may also have student loan data, particularly if it’s relevant to lending decisions.

5. **ChexSystems:** While primarily focused on banking history, it may have records if student loan payments have impacted your banking behavior.

These secondary bureaus may provide additional insights or data points not always included in the major credit bureaus (Experian, Equifax, TransUnion). If you're checking or disputing information related to student loans, it may be helpful to review the reports from these bureaus as well.
