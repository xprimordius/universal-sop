## What usually determines camera policy

- **“Professional gear” bans.** Many venues prohibit cameras with interchangeable lenses or long zooms, or restrict “professional film/video equipment.”  
- **Purpose clause.** They may allow cameras *for personal use* but not for commercial/press. (Example: Glastonbury states “Cameras for personal use are welcome; big professional zooms discouraged.”) citeturn0search5  
- **Venue-by-venue rules.** Some venues outright ban any camera or recording device. Others are lax. Ticketmaster says: “Most venues don’t allow cameras or recording devices inside.” citeturn0search3  
- **Security discretion.** Even if the policy allows it, security guards might deny your camera if it “looks professional” or bulky.

## Your plan

To tilt the odds in your favor:

1. **Check the festival’s “what you can bring” / rules page** — many list camera policies explicitly.  
2. **Use compact mirrorless + small prime / short zoom lens** rather than heavy telephoto gear.  
3. **Avoid flash, monopods, rigs** — things that scream “pro.”  
4. **Bring documentation / proof (if possible).** If you emailed the event or band and got permission, bring that.  
5. **Be discreet at security.** Let it look like just part of your personal kit.  
6. **Have a backup plan.** If they refuse, accept it gracefully or store it somewhere safe.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Because **security bans cameras that *look professional*** to protect:  

1. **Artist + venue contracts** — performers often sell or control official photos/videos; outside recording violates agreements.  
2. **Safety and privacy** — large lenses or rigs block views, pose hazards in crowds, and can capture people without consent.  
3. **Liability + media control** — festivals want only approved media producing commercial content, hence *media pass only* for pro gear.  
4. **Piracy + monetization** — prevents people from filming sets for resale or YouTube uploads.  

So: mirrorless = borderline — small ones sometimes pass, large lenses/rigs almost never unless you have a media pass.
