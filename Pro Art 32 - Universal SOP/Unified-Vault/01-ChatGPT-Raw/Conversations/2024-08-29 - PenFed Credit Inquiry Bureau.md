Yes, it's normal for some lenders, like Trust Other, to take some time to review your application before making a decision. While many online lenders can provide instant decisions, others may require additional time to manually review your application, verify information, or assess your creditworthiness.

Factors that could lead to a delayed decision include:

1. **Manual Review**: If your application requires a more in-depth review or if there are any discrepancies in the information provided.
2. **Verification Process**: Lenders may need to verify income, employment, or other details before making a final decision.
3. **High Application Volume**: If the lender is processing a large number of applications, it could slow down the response time.
4. **Credit Report Issues**: If there are issues with accessing your credit report or if the lender needs to pull reports from multiple bureaus.

Receiving an email later with their decision is a standard part of the process for many lenders, especially if they are conducting a more thorough review. If you're concerned, you can always reach out to their customer service for more information on the expected timeline.
