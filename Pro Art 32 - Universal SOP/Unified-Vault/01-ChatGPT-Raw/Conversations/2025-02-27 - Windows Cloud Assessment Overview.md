3. **Cost-Benefit Analysis** – Compares costs of cloud vs. on-premises solutions, including licensing and operational expenses.
4. **Security & Compliance** – Reviews security policies, data protection needs, and compliance requirements for cloud migration.
5. **Performance & Scalability** – Ensures cloud solutions meet performance requirements and can scale with business needs.
6. **User Experience & Accessibility** – Assesses end-user requirements for remote work, device compatibility, and network performance.

### **Purpose:**

### **Policy Restriction Violations in Travel Management**  

A **policy restriction violation** occurs when a traveler books a trip that does not comply with the company’s **corporate travel policy**. In a **law firm setting**, these violations could lead to **increased costs, compliance risks, and billing complications**.  

---

### **Common Types of Policy Violations:**  

#### **1. Unauthorized Travel Booking**  
🚫 **Booking outside the approved system** – Using **personal travel sites** (Expedia, Kayak) instead of the firm’s travel management platform.  
🚫 **Bypassing preferred vendors** – Booking with airlines, hotels, or car rentals that are **not on the firm’s approved list**.  
🚫 **Self-booking when assistant should handle** – Attorneys bypassing assistants or travel managers in firms that require centralized booking.  

#### **2. Class of Service Violations**  
🚫 **Booking first-class or business-class flights** when only **economy is allowed** under firm policy.  
🚫 **Upgrading hotel rooms beyond firm limits** without approval.  
🚫 **Renting luxury cars** instead of standard vehicles.  

#### **3. Excessive Spending & Per Diem Violations**  
🚫 **Exceeding daily meal allowances** or per diem rates.  
🚫 **Booking hotels above the allowed nightly rate** (e.g., choosing a $500/night hotel when the firm caps it at $300).  
🚫 **Expense padding** – Claiming unauthorized upgrades, extra nights, or personal expenses as business-related.  

#### **4. Non-Refundable or Last-Minute Bookings**  
🚫 **Purchasing non-refundable tickets** when policy requires refundable fares for flexibility.  
🚫 **Booking travel too close to departure** without a valid reason, leading to **higher costs**.  

#### **5. Personal Travel Mixed with Business Travel**  
🚫 **Booking personal trips on the firm’s travel system** (e.g., extending a work trip for vacation but charging the firm).  
🚫 **Upgrading family members to business class** on a firm-paid trip.  

#### **6. Non-Compliance with Unused Ticket Policies**  
🚫 **Not applying unused airline tickets** to future firm travel, leading to waste.  
🚫 **Failing to cancel travel in time**, causing unnecessary fees.  

#### **7. Billing & Client Chargeback Violations**  
🚫 **Charging travel to the wrong client/matter number**.  
🚫 **Splitting travel costs incorrectly** across multiple clients.  
🚫 **Billing ineligible expenses to clients**, violating ethical standards.

✅ **Real-time alerts & approval workflows** – Flag violations before booking confirmation.  
✅ **Restricted booking tools** – Lock down unauthorized vendors, fare classes, or rates.  
✅ **Pre-trip approval requirements** – Require manager approval for high-cost trips.
