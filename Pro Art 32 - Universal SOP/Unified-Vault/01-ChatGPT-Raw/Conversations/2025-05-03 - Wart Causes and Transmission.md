What are warts caused from

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Warts are caused by the **human papillomavirus (HPV)**. This virus infects the top layer of skin, usually through small cuts or abrasions, leading to rapid skin cell growth and the formation of a wart.

- **Transmission:** Skin-to-skin contact or contact with contaminated surfaces.
- **Risk factors:** Broken skin, weak immune system, wet environments (like public showers), and direct contact with someone who has warts.

## 👤 You

Are warts from being dirty

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

No, warts are **not caused by being dirty**. They result from infection with the **HPV virus**, not poor hygiene.

However:
- Having **cuts, scrapes, or moist skin** can make it easier for the virus to enter.
- **Good hygiene** helps prevent spreading the virus to other parts of your body or to others.

So while cleanliness doesn't cause or prevent warts directly, it helps limit transmission. Want tips on stopping them from spreading?

## 🤖 ChatGPT

To stop warts from spreading:

1. **Don’t pick or scratch** warts – it spreads the virus to other areas.
2. **Keep them covered** with a clean bandage.
3. **Wash hands** after touching the wart.
4. **Avoid sharing** towels, razors, socks, or shoes.
5. **Wear flip-flops** in communal showers or pools.
6. **Don’t bite nails or cuticles**—open skin is a gateway for the virus.
7. **Disinfect surfaces** (like nail tools or floors) that may contact the wart.

Wart remover help

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes, wart removers can help—most use **salicylic acid** or **freezing (cryotherapy)** to destroy the wart tissue.

**Top Options:**

1. **Salicylic Acid (Liquid/Pads):**
   - Brands: Compound W, Dr. Scholl’s.
   - Soften with warm water, apply daily, gently file dead skin.

2. **Cryotherapy (Freezing):**
   - Brands: Compound W Freeze Off, Wartner.
   - Rapid freeze kills wart cells; may need multiple treatments.

3. **Prescription-strength or In-office treatments:**
   - Used if OTC fails: stronger cryo, cantharidin, laser, or minor surgery.

**Tips:**
- Be consistent—treatment can take weeks.
- Stop if it causes severe irritation or infection.
- Don’t use on face/genitals unless product is labeled safe for those areas.
