### **f/4 vs f/2.4 — Low-Light Photo Comparison**

**1. Light Capture**
- **f/2.4** lets in **≈2.8× more light** than f/4 (exactly 1 ^1⁄3 stops difference).  
- This means lower ISO, cleaner images, and faster shutter speed for the same brightness.  

**2. Image Noise**
- **f/2.4** produces **less noise** in dim scenes since ISO can stay lower.
- **f/4** requires higher ISO → more grain, less dynamic range.

**3. Shutter Speed**
- At f/2.4, you can shoot **2.8× faster** (e.g., 1/60 s vs 1/20 s) for equal exposure—reduces motion blur.

**4. Depth of Field**
- **f/2.4:** shallower depth → more background blur (bokeh).  
- **f/4:** deeper focus → better for group or landscape shots but less light.

**5. Sharpness Trade-off**
- Most lenses are sharpest around **f/4–f/5.6**.  
- f/2.4 gives more light but can be slightly softer at edges.
