### Fluoride and IQ
Several studies have examined the potential link between fluoride exposure and IQ. Some research suggests that high levels of fluoride in drinking water may be associated with lower IQ scores in children. This has raised public concern, though it's worth noting that these studies primarily focus on areas with very high fluoride concentrations, often exceeding recommended safe levels. The scientific consensus remains mixed, with more research needed to establish a definitive causal relationship between fluoride at typical exposure levels and cognitive development.

### Fluoride and the Pineal Gland

One hypothesis is that high fluoride accumulation could impact melatonin production, potentially interfering with sleep patterns or other biological processes. However, there is no conclusive evidence that typical fluoride exposure from dental treatments or fluoridated water has a significant or harmful effect on the pineal gland.

In summary, while fluoride has known dental benefits, its broader systemic effects, particularly regarding IQ and the pineal gland, are still being investigated. The risks at typical levels of exposure remain largely unproven according to current scientific data.
