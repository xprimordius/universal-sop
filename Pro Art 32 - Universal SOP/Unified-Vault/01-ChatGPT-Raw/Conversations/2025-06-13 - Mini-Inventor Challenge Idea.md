Quick, fun, creative invention activity:

**“Mini-Inventor Challenge: Super Gadget Day”**

### Goal:
Let him invent a gadget using household stuff.

### Setup:
- Gather **safe, random supplies**: paper towel tubes, tape, string, LEGOs, bottle caps, cardboard, rubber bands, foil, etc.
- Give him a **theme or challenge**:
  - “Invent something that helps with chores.”
  - “Invent a toy no one’s ever seen.”
  - “Invent something that helps superheroes.”

### Steps:
1. **Draw It**: He sketches his invention idea.
2. **Name It**: Let him give it a cool name.
3. **Build It**: Use materials to make a prototype.
4. **Demo It**: He presents it to you like Shark Tank or a commercial.
5. **Bonus**: Film the pitch. Let him watch himself like a real inventor.

Let me know if he likes a specific theme (space, animals, sports) and I’ll tailor it tighter.
