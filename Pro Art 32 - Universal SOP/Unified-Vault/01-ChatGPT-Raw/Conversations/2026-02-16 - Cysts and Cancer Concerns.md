## What this most often is (non-cancer)
**Much more common causes:**
- **Epidermoid / sebaceous cysts** – benign, can appear suddenly
- **Hormonal shifts** (thyroid, androgens, postpartum, stopping/starting meds)
- **Inflammatory skin conditions** (hidradenitis, folliculitis)
- **Infections** (bacterial, viral)
- **Medication reactions** (steroids, lithium, some biologics)
- **Genetic tendencies** (can surface later in life)

These account for the **vast majority** of cases.

---

## When cancer becomes a concern (rarer)
Cancer is **unlikely**, but evaluation is important if cysts are:
- **Hard, fixed, non-movable**
- **Rapidly multiplying + growing fast**
- **Associated with**:
  - Unexplained weight loss
  - Night sweats
  - Persistent fevers
  - Severe fatigue
  - Enlarged, painless lymph nodes
- **Deep (not skin-level)** or recurring after removal
- Accompanied by **new neurological, GI, or systemic symptoms**

Some rare syndromes and metastatic skin findings exist—but again, **they are uncommon**.

---

1. **Primary care or dermatologist ASAP**
2. Ask for:
   - Physical exam (mobility, depth, consistency)
   - **Ultrasound** of representative lesions
   - Labs if indicated (CBC, inflammatory markers, thyroid)
3. **Biopsy only if features are suspicious** (not routine)

Most cases are diagnosed **clinically + imaging**, not surgery.
