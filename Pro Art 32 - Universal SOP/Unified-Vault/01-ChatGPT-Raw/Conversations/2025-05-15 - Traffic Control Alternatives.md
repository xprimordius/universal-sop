## 🤖 ChatGPT

If your city won’t approve stop signs for traffic control, here are effective alternatives you can push for:

---

### **1. Speed Humps or Cushions**
- Slows vehicles naturally
- Less controversial than stop signs
- Often allowed on residential streets

---

### **2. Raised Crosswalks**
- Combines pedestrian safety with speed reduction  
- Visually prominent and ADA-compliant

---

### **3. Curb Extensions (Bulb-outs)**
- Narrows road visually
- Forces slower turns at intersections

---

### **4. Chicanes or Lane Narrowing**
- Slight curves or offsets that slow down traffic
- Can be temporary using planters or striping

---

### **5. Painted Optical Illusions**
- Visual tricks like converging lines or faux speed bumps
- Low-cost, effective in reducing speed

---

### **6. Traffic Circles (Mini Roundabouts)**
- Keeps flow but reduces speed at intersections
- Lower maintenance than 4-way stops

---

### **7. Flashing Pedestrian Beacons**
- Enhances crossing visibility without a stop sign
- Activated by pedestrians

---

### **8. Radar Speed Signs**
- Displays driver’s speed with flashing warning if over limit
- Often funded by community or grants

---

### **9. Increased Police Presence / Tickets**
- Temporary enforcement to change driver habits
- Can request targeted enforcement during peak hours

---

### **10. Road Diets**
- Reduce lane count or narrow lanes
- Calms traffic and improves pedestrian safety

---
