### 🎥 Best Ways to Capture Clean **Night Footage** (Vlogging or Driving)

---

### 🔑 1. **Use a Camera Built for Low Light**
| Device | Night Performance |
|--------|--------------------|
| **GoPro Hero 13 Black** | Better than 12, but grainy in very dark scenes. Use **Night Effects Mode** and **Night Lapse Video**. |
| **iPhone 14 Pro / 15 Pro** | Excellent Night Mode + computational HDR for ambient lighting. |
| **Sony ZV-E10 / FX3 / A7S III** | Industry-best low light (if you're going pro). |
| **Insta360 Ace Pro** | 1-inch sensor + Leica optics = top-tier night shots for an action cam. |

---

### 🔧 2. **Settings Optimization**
For GoPro Hero 13:
- **Resolution**: 4K or 5.3K
- **FPS**: 24–30 (slower FPS = more light)
- **Shutter**: Set to “Auto” or manually slow (1/30–1/60)
- **ISO Min/Max**: 100–800 (avoid 1600+ unless needed)
- **EV Comp**: +0.5 to +1.0 for brightness boost
- **Color**: Flat (to edit later)

---

### 💡 3. **Add External Lighting**
- **Lume Cube 2.0 or GoPro Light Mod**: Compact, mountable, powerful
- **USB rechargeable camping lights** (magnetic): Clip inside car or on hatch
- **Soft diffused light** = avoids harsh glare, preserves mood

---

### 🚗 4. **Strategic Placement**
- **Inside Car**: Place light at **45° angle** from your face (not direct)
- **Outside**: Light the car from front/side with a suction light or LED wand
- **Dash Cam Style**: Face the camera inward, use cabin light + dimmable LED

---

### 🧪 5. **Test Before Publishing**
- Always **test 10–15s clips** before long sessions
- Watch for: lens flares, windshield reflections, grain

---

### 🛠️ Pro Tools (Optional)
- **ND Filters with IR Cut**: Prevent glare from street lamps
- **Post-production noise reduction** (DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut): Clean up grain
- **Gimbal or digital stabilization**: Prevent motion blur from shaky hands
