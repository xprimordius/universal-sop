**No — bacon left out for 36 hours is not safe to eat.**  
Even if it was covered with a paper towel, it’s been in the **temperature danger zone (40°F–140°F)** far too long.

### Why it's unsafe:
- Bacteria like *Salmonella* and *Clostridium perfringens* multiply rapidly at room temp.
- After **2 hours**, per USDA, cooked meat is no longer safe.
- A paper towel does **not** preserve or inhibit bacteria.

### Exception?
**Shelf-stable, fully dehydrated bacon** (e.g., bacon bits or pre-cooked shelf-stable packs) may be safe if labeled for room temp storage — but this does **not** apply to typical cooked bacon.

**Recommendation:** Discard it. Not worth the risk.
