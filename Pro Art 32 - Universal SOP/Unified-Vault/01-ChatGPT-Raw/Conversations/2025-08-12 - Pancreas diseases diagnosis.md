**Pancreas Diseases – Diagnosis & Causes**  

---

## **1. Main Pancreatic Diseases**
| Condition | Core Problem | Common Symptoms |
|-----------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Acute Pancreatitis** | Sudden inflammation, usually reversible | Severe upper abdominal pain radiating to back, nausea, vomiting, elevated amylase/lipase |
| **Chronic Pancreatitis** | Progressive, irreversible inflammation and scarring | Persistent pain, malabsorption, weight loss, diabetes |
| **Pancreatic Cancer** | Malignant tumor (often adenocarcinoma) | Painless jaundice, weight loss, abdominal/back pain |
| **Pancreatic Cysts / Pseudocysts** | Fluid collections from pancreatitis or other causes | Fullness, pain, infection risk |
| **Exocrine Pancreatic Insufficiency (EPI)** | Inadequate digestive enzyme production | Steatorrhea (fatty stool), weight loss, nutrient deficiencies |
| **Pancreatic Neuroendocrine Tumors (PNETs)** | Hormone-secreting tumors | Symptoms vary by hormone (insulin → hypoglycemia, gastrin → ulcers) |

---

## **2. Common Causes**
### **A. Acute Pancreatitis**
- Gallstones (most common in U.S.)
- Heavy alcohol use
- High triglycerides (>1000 mg/dL)
- Certain drugs (azathioprine, valproate, thiazides)
- ERCP procedure complications
- Pancreatic trauma
- Idiopathic (~10–20% cases)

### **B. Chronic Pancreatitis**
- Long-term alcohol abuse
- Genetic mutations (PRSS1, SPINK1, CFTR)
- Recurrent acute pancreatitis
- Autoimmune pancreatitis
- Ductal obstruction (tumor, stricture)

### **C. Pancreatic Cancer**
- Smoking
- Chronic pancreatitis
- Family history / genetic syndromes (BRCA2, Peutz–Jeghers)
- Obesity, diabetes
- High red meat / processed meat diet

### **D. Exocrine Pancreatic Insufficiency**
- Chronic pancreatitis
- Cystic fibrosis
- Pancreatic resection surgery

---

## **3. Diagnostic Approach**
**A. History & Physical**
- Pain pattern (acute onset vs chronic)
- Alcohol/gallstone history
- Weight loss, jaundice, stool changes

**B. Laboratory Tests**
| Test | Use |
|------|-----|
| Serum **amylase & lipase** | Elevated in acute pancreatitis |
| Liver function tests | Jaundice, bile duct obstruction |
| Fasting triglycerides | Detect hypertriglyceridemia cause |
| Glucose / HbA1c | Check for diabetes |
| CA 19-9 | Pancreatic cancer tumor marker (not screening) |
| Fecal elastase | EPI detection |

**C. Imaging**
| Test | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Abdominal ultrasound | Detect gallstones, duct dilation |
| CT abdomen w/ contrast | Detect pancreatitis, necrosis, tumors |
| MRI / MRCP | Pancreatic duct evaluation, cysts |
| Endoscopic ultrasound (EUS) | High-res imaging, biopsy guidance |
| ERCP | Diagnose & treat duct obstruction (used selectively) |

---

## **4. Red Flags Needing Urgent Attention**
- Sudden severe abdominal pain with vomiting  
- Jaundice + weight loss  
- Persistent unexplained diarrhea or fatty stools  
- Acute pancreatitis symptoms in someone with known gallstones or heavy alcohol intake

**Pancreas – Function & Purpose**  

---

## **Core Purpose**
The pancreas is a **dual-function gland** that acts as both:
1. **Digestive organ** (exocrine function) – makes enzymes to break down food.  
2. **Hormonal regulator** (endocrine function) – controls blood sugar.

---

## **1. Exocrine Role – Digestion**
- **Produces digestive enzymes**:
  - **Amylase** → breaks carbs into sugars.
  - **Lipase** → breaks fats into fatty acids.
  - **Proteases** (trypsin, chymotrypsin) → break proteins into amino acids.
- **Secretes bicarbonate** → neutralizes stomach acid before food enters small intestine.
- **Goal:** Ensure nutrients are fully broken down for absorption.

---

## **2. Endocrine Role – Blood Sugar Control**
- Specialized clusters (**islets of Langerhans**) release hormones into blood:
  - **Insulin** – lowers blood sugar by moving glucose into cells.
  - **Glucagon** – raises blood sugar by releasing glucose from liver stores.
  - **Somatostatin** – regulates and balances other hormone release.
  - **Pancreatic polypeptide** – helps control appetite and digestion.
- **Goal:** Keep blood sugar stable for energy and brain function.

---

## **Big Picture**
The pancreas is like a **control center + supply factory**:
- **Factory:** Creates enzymes for nutrient breakdown.
- **Control Center:** Regulates the body’s fuel supply via blood sugar.

---

If your pancreas fails in **exocrine function** → malnutrition.  
If it fails in **endocrine function** → diabetes.  
If both fail → severe metabolic and digestive problems.
