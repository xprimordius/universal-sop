## Data points that actually predict “best footsteps” (how to quantify)
1. **Imaging accuracy (not just soundstage width):** can you place a sound at **10°, 30°, 60°, 90°** reliably? Wide stage without sharp imaging = worse for Apex.
2. **Tuning that doesn’t mask footsteps:** too much bass/low-mids can bury step transients; a cleaner upper-mid/treble region often helps (but harsh treble can fatigue).
3. **Transient speed + separation in chaos:** multiple sounds at once (gunfire + abilities + steps) and you still isolate steps.
4. **Channel matching / consistency:** small L/R imbalance ruins precision.
5. **Open-back vs closed-back:** open-back often feels wider/more “out of head,” but closed/IEM can sometimes image more “locked-in.” (So test, don’t assume.)

**HRTF** is the **measurable way your head, ears (pinna), and torso filter sound** depending on **direction** (left/right, up/down, front/back).

Your brain combines all three → **3D direction**.
