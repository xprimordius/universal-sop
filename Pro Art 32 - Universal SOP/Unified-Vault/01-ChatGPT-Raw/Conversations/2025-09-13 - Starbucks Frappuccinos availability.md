## 🤖 ChatGPT

No — neither **AirPods Max** nor **AirPods Pro 2** support **true lossless Bluetooth**.  

### Why:
- Both rely on **AAC codec over Bluetooth**, which is *compressed*.  
- Apple’s “Lossless Audio” in Apple Music (ALAC up to 24-bit/192 kHz) only plays fully lossless via **wired connection** (Lightning/USB-C DACs, etc.), not over Bluetooth.  
- Even with the newer **H2 chip**, AirPods Pro 2 (USB-C version) still use AAC — not ALAC, LDAC, or aptX Lossless.  

### Workarounds:
- **AirPods Max wired mode** (with Lightning-to-3.5 mm analog cable + DAC) — but still not *bit-perfect* lossless; the signal is re-converted.  
- **No Apple AirPods model currently delivers end-to-end true lossless over Bluetooth.**  

### Alternatives for true lossless over wireless:
- **Sony WH-1000XM6 / XM5** → LDAC (up to 990 kbps, near-lossless).  
- **Focal Bathys**, **Bowers & Wilkins PX8**, **Audeze Maxwell** → support aptX Adaptive / aptX Lossless with compatible devices.  

⚡ So: if you’re locked into Apple ecosystem, you’ll get *excellent quality*, but not *true lossless*. If you *must have lossless wireless*, you’ll need a non-Apple pair with LDAC or aptX Lossless **and** a device that supports those codecs.
