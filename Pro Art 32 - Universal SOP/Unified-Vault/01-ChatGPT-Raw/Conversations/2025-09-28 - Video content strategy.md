## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here’s the distilled guidance Hilary gave you in that thread:

### Key Takeaways
1. **Top-of-Funnel Wins Virality**  
   Broad, relatable content gets the most reach. Industry jargon narrows your audience and costs you views.  

2. **Viewer Motivation is Missing**  
   Ask: *Why should a stranger care?*  
   - What are they losing without a better score?  
   - What do they gain with one?  
   Without clear stakes, viewers scroll past.  

3. **One Idea = One Video**  
   Don’t overload a single video with multiple angles—each tangent sheds viewers. Break concepts into micro-content.  

4. **Put Viewer at the Center**  
   Frame everything around their desires and fears. Start with what *they* care about, not what you know.  

5. **Compelling Hook + Visual**  
   Pair a text hook (problem/benefit) with a strong visual to instantly connect. That’s the starting point for virality.
