### 1. What actually matters for “quality”

For sildenafil, true quality differences are mostly about:

1. **Regulation & oversight**
   - **FDA-approved (U.S.)**: Must prove bioequivalence to Viagra (same active ingredient, same effect within tight margins), follow GMP, and lot-level testing.
   - **Outside-U.S. generics**: Some countries have strong regulators, some don’t. Enforcement and testing can be weaker or inconsistent.
   - **Counterfeit risk**: Much higher with cross-border or non-verified online sources.

2. **Active ingredient vs fillers**
   - When a generic is **properly approved**, the **sildenafil itself is essentially the same**; differences are in binders, dyes, how fast it dissolves, etc.
   - For most people, that doesn’t change effect; a few notice minor differences in onset or side-effects between brands.

3. **Dose accuracy**
   - FDA-approved 100 mg generics must be within a tight % of label claim across batches.
   - “Street” or questionable generics can be **under-dosed, over-dosed, or mixed with other drugs** (e.g., extra PDE5 inhibitors or stimulants).
