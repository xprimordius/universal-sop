**Blackstone Medium-Rare Steak – Master Guide**

🔥 **Prep:**
- Use ribeye, NY strip, or filet mignon (1–1.5" thick).
- Let steak sit out 30–45 min before cooking (room temp).
- Pat dry and season generously (salt, pepper, garlic powder).

🔥 **Blackstone Setup:**
- Preheat griddle on **high** for 10–15 min.
- Use **avocado oil** or beef tallow on surface.

🔥 **Cook:**
1. **Sear First Side:**  
   - Place steak down and **don’t touch** – 2 to 3 minutes.
2. **Flip and Sear Other Side:**  
   - Another 2 to 3 minutes.
3. **Check Temp:**  
   - Internal should be **130°F** for true medium-rare.
   - Use a meat thermometer.
4. **Optional Sear Edges:**  
   - Hold steak with tongs and sear edges briefly if thick-cut.

🔥 **Rest:**
- Remove and let rest **5–10 min** (carryover heat finishes it).
- Tent with foil for best results.

🔥 **Finish (Optional):**
- Top with butter, herbs, or garlic compound butter while resting.
