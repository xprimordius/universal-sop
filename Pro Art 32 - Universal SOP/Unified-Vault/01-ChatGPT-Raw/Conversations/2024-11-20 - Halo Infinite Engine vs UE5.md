### **What is the Slipspace Engine?**
The **Slipspace Engine** is a proprietary game engine developed by 343 Industries, specifically for **Halo Infinite**. It was designed to modernize Halo's technology stack and enable features like:
- **Dynamic environments**: Large open-world gameplay in Infinite's campaign.
- **Seamless transitions**: Between campaign, multiplayer, and cutscenes.
- **Customization**: Extensive Spartan customization for multiplayer modes.
- **Cross-platform play**: Optimized for Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S, and PC.

It was marketed as a next-generation toolset to power Halo Infinite’s gameplay and visuals, but its development was riddled with delays and internal challenges.

**Halo 5: Guardians** used a heavily modified version of the **Blam Engine**, which has its roots in Bungie’s original engine for the early Halo games. Over the years, 343 Industries iteratively updated it for their projects.
