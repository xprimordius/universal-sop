# Driving Without Insurance Legalities

---

## 👤 You

Can i drive my car across the country with no insurance

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Driving across the country without car insurance is illegal in almost all U.S. states. Every state except New Hampshire and Virginia requires drivers to have at least a minimum amount of liability insurance. Even in those two states, there are specific financial responsibility rules. Driving without insurance can lead to fines, license suspension, and vehicle impoundment, especially if you're caught or involved in an accident.

It's best to ensure your vehicle is properly insured before making a cross-country trip to avoid legal and financial consequences.
