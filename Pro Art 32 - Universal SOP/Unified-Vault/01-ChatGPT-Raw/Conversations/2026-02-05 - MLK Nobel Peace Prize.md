## Vietnamese Fried Chicken Wings (Fish Sauce–Garlic–Chili)

**Flavor profile:** Crispy, sticky, savory-sweet, garlicky heat. Classic street-style.

### Ingredients (2–3 servings)
- 1.5–2 lb chicken wings  
- Oil for frying

**Marinade (10 min)**
- 1 tbsp fish sauce  
- 1/2 tsp sugar  
- 1/2 tsp white pepper

**Crispy coat (optional but recommended)**
- 2 tbsp cornstarch (or rice flour)

**Fish Sauce Glaze**
- 3 tbsp fish sauce  
- 2 tbsp sugar (palm sugar best; brown sugar ok)  
- 1–2 tbsp water  
- 6 cloves garlic, finely minced  
- 1–3 red chili peppers, finely sliced (Thai or Fresno)  
- 1/2 tsp black pepper  
- Optional: 1/2 tsp chili paste or chili oil

### Method
1. **Marinate wings** with marinade 10 minutes. Pat dry. Toss lightly with cornstarch.  
2. **Fry** at 350°F / 175°C until deep golden and crisp (8–10 min). Drain.  
3. **Make glaze:** In a pan, simmer fish sauce + sugar + water until syrupy (1–2 min). Add garlic and chili; cook until fragrant, not browned (30–45 sec).  
4. **Toss wings** in glaze over medium heat until glossy and sticky (30–60 sec).  
5. **Finish** with black pepper. Serve hot.

### Pro tips
- **Balance:** If too salty, add 1–2 tsp water; too sweet, add a splash of fish sauce.  
- **Extra crunch:** Double-fry (first at 325°F, rest 5 min, second at 375°F).  
- **Aromatic upgrade:** Add minced shallot with the garlic.
