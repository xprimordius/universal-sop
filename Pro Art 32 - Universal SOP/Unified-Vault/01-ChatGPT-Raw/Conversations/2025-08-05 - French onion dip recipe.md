**French Onion Dip with Cream Cheese & French Onion Powder**

### 🧄 Ultra-Creamy Flavor-Packed Dip

**Ingredients:**
- 8 oz **cream cheese** (softened)
- ½ cup **sour cream** or Greek yogurt (optional for lighter texture)
- 2–3 tbsp **French onion soup mix** or **French onion powder**
- ½ tsp **garlic powder** (optional)
- ¼ tsp **black pepper**
- 1–2 tbsp **milk or heavy cream** (for desired consistency)
- Optional: chopped chives or green onions for garnish

**Instructions:**
1. **Mix:** In a bowl, combine softened cream cheese and sour cream (if using) until smooth.
2. **Flavor:** Add French onion powder, garlic powder, and black pepper. Mix well.
3. **Thin (if needed):** Add a splash of milk or cream to reach your ideal dip texture.
4. **Chill (optional):** For best flavor, refrigerate 30+ minutes.
5. **Serve:** Garnish with chives. Pair with chips, crackers, or veggies.
