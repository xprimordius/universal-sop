## 👤 You

Is freezing all that it takes to kill parasites?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Freezing is highly effective at killing parasites in fish, but it must be done under the right conditions. Here's a detailed explanation:

### Freezing to Kill Parasites
Freezing fish at very low temperatures for a sufficient amount of time kills parasites like **Anisakis** (roundworms) and **Diphyllobothrium** (tapeworms), which are the most common in raw fish. However, it does not kill bacteria or viruses, so the fish still needs to be handled hygienically.

### FDA Guidelines for Parasite Destruction:
1. **Freeze at -4°F (-20°C) for 7 days** (in a standard freezer)  
   OR  
2. **Freeze at -31°F (-35°C) for 15 hours** (in a commercial deep freezer)

These conditions are sufficient to kill parasites in fish intended for raw consumption.

### Exceptions:
1. **Farmed Salmon:**  
   - Farmed Atlantic salmon is typically raised on parasite-free feed in controlled environments, significantly lowering parasite risk. This makes it safer than wild-caught salmon for raw consumption.
   
2. **Wild-Caught Salmon:**  
   - Wild salmon has a higher risk of parasites and must always be frozen according to FDA standards before eating raw.

3. **Parasite Eggs (Rare Cases):**  
   - Freezing may not destroy eggs that are encased in tissues, though this is rare. Proper inspection and trimming can mitigate this risk.

### What Freezing Doesn’t Do:
- **Does not kill bacteria or viruses:** Freezing doesn’t eliminate bacteria like *Salmonella* or *Listeria*, or viruses like *Hepatitis A*. These risks are minimized by sourcing high-quality fish meant for raw consumption.
- **Does not remove toxins:** Freezing won’t neutralize toxins produced by spoiled fish.

### Key Takeaways:
- Freezing at the correct temperature and duration is sufficient to kill parasites, but it doesn’t address other safety concerns like bacteria or poor handling.
- Always buy fish labeled for "sushi" or "sashimi" grade, or follow the freezing guidelines yourself for wild fish.

If you want to ensure absolute safety and quality, use high-grade, parasite-free farmed fish or fish sold explicitly for raw consumption.
