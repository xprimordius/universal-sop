**TLDR:** Here's how to switch Amazon app back to **Amazon.com (US)** from **Amazon.es (Spain)**:

---

### **Step-by-Step (Amazon App):**
1. **Open Amazon App**
2. Tap the **three horizontal lines** (☰) bottom right
3. Scroll down and tap **Settings**
4. Tap **Country & Language**
5. Tap **Country/Region** and select **United States**
6. Confirm and the app will switch back to **Amazon.com**
