## What it means (simple + exact)

An **investor meeting** = a company meeting with current or potential investors to **raise money, give updates, or discuss strategy/performance**.

For **entity["company","Chisos","Chisos Boots company"]**:

### 1) If they’re raising money
- They’re pitching investors to **get capital (cash)**
- Goal: exchange **equity (ownership)** or sometimes debt for funding

### 2) If investors already invested
- They’re giving **updates**
  - Revenue / growth
  - Operations
  - Future plans
- Keeping investors confident + aligned

### 3) What actually happens in the meeting
- Pitch or presentation (deck)
- Financials + metrics
- Vision / expansion plan
- Q&A (investors challenge assumptions)
- Possible negotiation (valuation, terms)
