**Compression socks** are most beneficial during **long runs and post-run recovery**, not necessarily for speed itself. Here's a breakdown:

### **When to Wear Compression Socks:**

| **Use Case**         | **Recommended?** | **Why** |
|----------------------|------------------|--------|
| **Long Runs**        | Yes              | Increases circulation, reduces muscle vibration, delays fatigue |
| **Speed Workouts**   | Optional         | Can support calves, but benefits for performance are minimal |
| **Post-Run Recovery**| Yes              | Aids in flushing lactic acid, speeds recovery |

### **Bottom Line:**
- **Long run?** Wear them.
- **Speed run?** Optional—won’t hurt, might help with muscle support.
- **Recovery?** Highly recommended.
