# Burning Itching Lips Aligners

---

## 👤 You

Why are my lips burning and itching from my smile direct alligners

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Burning and itching from your Smile Direct aligners could be due to several potential factors:

1. **Allergic Reaction**: Some people may be allergic to the materials used in the aligners (typically plastic or resin). If you're sensitive to certain chemicals, the contact between your lips and the aligners could cause irritation.

2. **Cleaning Solution Residue**: If the aligners were cleaned with a solution that wasn’t rinsed off completely, the chemicals could cause a reaction.

3. **Fit or Sharp Edges**: If the aligners don’t fit properly or have sharp edges, they might be irritating the skin and tissues around your mouth.

4. **Saliva and Dryness**: Wearing aligners can sometimes cause dry mouth or changes in saliva flow, which might irritate your lips and mouth area, causing burning or itching.

5. **Oral Hygiene Products**: The toothpaste or mouthwash you use might be interacting with the aligners, leading to irritation.

If the symptoms persist, consider discontinuing use and consult Smile Direct or a dental professional to evaluate the fit or assess if you're having an allergic reaction.
