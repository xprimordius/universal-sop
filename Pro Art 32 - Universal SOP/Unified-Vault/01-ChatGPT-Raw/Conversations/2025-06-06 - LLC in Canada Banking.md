No, **CIBC (Canadian Imperial Bank of Commerce)** is a **Canadian bank** and does **not offer business banking accounts to U.S.-based businesses** through its Canadian platform unless you have a **Canadian business registration** (e.g., a Canadian corporation, GST/HST number, or physical presence in Canada).

However:

### 🔹 If you're in the U.S. and want to bank with CIBC:
- You can apply through **CIBC Bank USA**, a separate U.S. subsidiary.
- Website: [us.cibc.com](https://us.cibc.com)
- CIBC Bank USA offers personal and business banking, but **availability and services are limited to certain states** (mainly in the Midwest).

### ✅ To open a business account in Canada:
You must typically provide:
- Canadian business registration (Corporation, Sole Prop, etc.)
- CRA business number
- Canadian address
- Two pieces of ID
- Possibly a Canadian phone number
