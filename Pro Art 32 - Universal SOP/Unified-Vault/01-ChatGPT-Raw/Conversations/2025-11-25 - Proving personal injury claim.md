## 🤖 ChatGPT

To **prove a personal injury claim**, you must establish **negligence** with four legal elements:

---

### **1. Duty of Care**
Show the defendant had a **legal responsibility** to act with reasonable care.
- Example: Drivers must follow traffic laws and avoid harming others.

---

### **2. Breach of Duty**
Prove they **failed** to meet that duty.
- Example: They ran a red light, were texting, or speeding.

---

### **3. Causation**
Link their actions directly to your injury.
- **“But for” causation:** The injury wouldn’t have happened but for their actions.  
- **Proximate cause:** The harm was a foreseeable result of their actions.

---

### **4. Damages**
You must show **actual harm** — physical, emotional, or financial.
- Medical bills  
- Lost wages  
- Pain and suffering  
- Property damage

---

### **Supporting Evidence**
Gather and preserve:
- Police reports / witness statements  
- Photos and videos (scene, injuries, vehicle damage, etc.)  
- Medical records and bills  
- Expert testimony (doctors, accident reconstruction)  
- Dashcam or surveillance footage  

---

### **Pro Tip**
If it’s a **hit-and-run or unclear liability**, evidence like dashcam footage, plate numbers, and eyewitnesses can be used to file a claim through **your uninsured motorist coverage** or a **civil suit** once the driver is identified.

## 👤 You

What if I have pre existing spinal problems

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Good question — you can **still win** a personal injury claim even with pre-existing spinal issues. The key is proving the **accident worsened or aggravated** your prior condition. Here’s how:  

---

### **1. Legal Principle: “Eggshell Plaintiff Rule”**
The law says the defendant **must take you as they find you** — meaning they’re liable for any **aggravation** of an existing injury, even if you were more vulnerable.

---

### **2. Proof Strategy**
You must show a **clear difference** between your condition *before* and *after* the accident.  

**Evidence to use:**
- **Medical records before and after** the crash (MRI, X-rays, doctor notes).  
- **Doctor’s testimony** explaining how trauma aggravated the spine.  
- **Pain journals / physical therapy logs** showing increased symptoms or new limitations.  
- **Expert medical opinion** distinguishing pre-existing degeneration from new trauma.

---

### **3. Avoid Common Mistakes**
- Don’t hide past injuries — defense will find them and use them to discredit you.  
- Instead, show consistency: “I had manageable pain before; after the accident, it became disabling.”

---

### **4. Damage Recovery**
You can claim compensation for:  
- The *increase* in pain or disability.  
- Extra medical care, therapy, or surgeries now required.  
- Lost income from worsened mobility or pain.
