## 👤 You

Credit card do you have to pay all off or only the last statement balance to avoid interests

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

To avoid paying interest on a credit card:  

- **You only need to pay the full *statement balance*** (the amount shown on your last billing statement) by the due date.  
- You do **not** need to pay off the entire current balance (which may include new purchases made after the statement closed).  
- If you pay the statement balance in full by the due date, you get a **grace period** and no interest is charged on purchases.  
- If you pay less than the statement balance, or if you carry a balance, interest will apply to the remaining amount (and often to new purchases too, since you lose the grace period).  

⚡ TLDR: **Pay the full statement balance (not necessarily the full current balance) each cycle to stay interest-free.**
