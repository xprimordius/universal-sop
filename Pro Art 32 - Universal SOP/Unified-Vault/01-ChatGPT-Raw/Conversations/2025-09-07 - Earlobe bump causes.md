Likely causes of a **small, pea-sized, painful bump inside the earlobe** (where earrings go) include:  

- **Epidermoid cyst (sebaceous cyst):** Common, feels like a firm pea under the skin, may hurt if inflamed or infected.  
- **Keloid or scar tissue:** Can form after piercing, usually firmer and can grow larger over time.  
- **Infected piercing/abscess:** Especially if the area is red, warm, swollen, or oozing.  
- **Localized pimple/folliculitis:** Can develop if bacteria got into a hair follicle or oil gland.  

⚠️ **When to seek care quickly:**  
- Redness spreading beyond the earlobe  
- Rapid growth in size  
- Significant pain or throbbing  
- Pus or drainage  
- Fever  

👉 For now:  
- Avoid squeezing or picking (can worsen infection).  
- Apply warm compresses (10–15 min, 2–3×/day) to encourage drainage.  
- Keep the area clean, avoid wearing earrings until it heals.  
- If it worsens, see a doctor/urgent care for possible antibiotics or drainage.
