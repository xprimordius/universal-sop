1. Any negligent, reckless, intentional, or unlawful act or omission by the Contractual Operator in the course of performing duties under this agreement.
2. Any violation of applicable laws, regulations, or third-party rights by the Contractual Operator.
3. Any breach of the terms, representations, or warranties made by the Contractual Operator in this agreement.

Furthermore, the Contractual Operator agrees that:

1. **No Liability to the Company:** In no event shall the Company be liable for any acts, omissions, or misconduct of the Contractual Operator. This includes but is not limited to negligence, fraud, or any illegal activities committed by the Contractual Operator.
   
2. **Third-Party Claims:** The Contractual Operator shall bear full responsibility for any claims, lawsuits, or legal actions brought by third parties as a result of the Contractual Operator’s actions, ensuring that no such claims can be made against the Company. 3. **Insurance Requirement:** The Contractual Operator agrees to maintain at their own expense adequate liability insurance coverage to protect against potential claims arising from their actions. Proof of such coverage shall be provided to the Company upon request.

4. **Survival of Clause:** This clause shall survive the termination or expiration of this agreement and shall remain in full force and effect indefinitely.

5. **Governing Law and Dispute Resolution:** This clause shall be governed by the laws of [State of LLC Formation], and any disputes arising under this clause shall be resolved through binding arbitration in [City, State], in accordance with the rules of the American Arbitration Association (AAA).
