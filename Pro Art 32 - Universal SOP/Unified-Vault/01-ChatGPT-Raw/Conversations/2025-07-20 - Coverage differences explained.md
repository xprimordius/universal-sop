## 👤 You

Does declining comprehensive and collision cover me if someone else hits me or is at fault? what is the difference between these two

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**TLDR: No, declining *comprehensive* and *collision* coverage does **not** protect you if someone else hits you. That’s the role of **liability** and **uninsured motorist** coverage.**

---

### 🔍 Breakdown:

#### 1. **Collision Coverage**  
- **Covers:** Damage to *your vehicle* in an accident (regardless of who's at fault).  
- **Example:** You hit a pole, or someone hits you and *they’re not insured*.

#### 2. **Comprehensive Coverage**  
- **Covers:** Non-collision damage to *your vehicle*.  
- **Example:** Theft, vandalism, fire, hail, hitting a deer.

#### 3. **If Someone Else Hits You (They're At Fault):**
- You’re **not covered** by your own *collision* or *comprehensive*.  
- You’re covered by:
  - **Their liability insurance** (if they have it).
  - **Your uninsured/underinsured motorist** (if they don’t have enough or any).

---

### ✅ Best Practice:
- Keep **liability + uninsured motorist** for protection against others.
- Keep **collision + comprehensive** if your car is valuable or you'd want repairs/replacement.
