The term "deep state" refers to a conspiracy theory that suggests the existence of a hidden or shadow government operating independently of elected officials and acting against the will of the people. This idea posits that a network of government officials, intelligence agencies, military leaders, and sometimes private entities operates covertly to control public policy and government actions.

While the term has been used more frequently in recent political discourse, especially in the U.S., it's worth noting that many experts argue that these claims often lack substantial evidence and are driven by political motivations or misinformation.

The term is also used in other contexts to describe government bureaucracies that resist changes in leadership or policy, but this is a more benign interpretation compared to the conspiratorial version.
