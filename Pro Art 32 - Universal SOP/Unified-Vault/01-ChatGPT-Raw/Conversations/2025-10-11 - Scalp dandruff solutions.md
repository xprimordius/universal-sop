---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

If you’re still seeing **visible flakes even when your hair is wet**, that means the issue isn’t just surface dandruff — it’s deeper scalp buildup, inflammation, or fungal imbalance.  
Here’s how to fix it:

---

### 🔍 Root Causes
- **Seborrheic dermatitis (yeast overgrowth / oily scalp)**
- **Product buildup or clogged follicles**
- **Overwashing or stripping natural oils**
- **Fungal resistance to zinc pyrithione (Head & Shoulders)**

---

### 🧴 Corrective Protocol
**1. Rotate actives weekly**
- **Ketoconazole 1% (Nizoral A-D):** 2–3x/week — kills the underlying fungus.
- **Salicylic acid shampoo (Neutrogena T/Sal):** 1–2x/week — dissolves dead skin and buildup.
- **Head & Shoulders or Selsun Blue:** 1x/week for maintenance.

**2. Massage + Dwell Time**
- Apply on **scalp only**, not hair.
- **Leave on 3–5 minutes** before rinsing — it needs contact time to work.

**3. Condition the scalp**
- After rinsing, use a **light, non-comedogenic oil** (a few drops of jojoba or tea tree diluted in carrier oil) on scalp to prevent rebound dryness.

**4. Deep clean weekly**
- Mix **1 tbsp apple cider vinegar + 1 cup water**, pour over scalp after shampoo, leave 1–2 min, rinse.

---

### 🧠 Bonus Tips
- Wash brushes, pillowcases, hats weekly — reinfection source.
- Avoid leave-in oils/creams on scalp.
- If it persists >2 weeks after rotation → likely seborrheic dermatitis → see a dermatologist for a **prescription 2% ketoconazole or topical steroid**.

Why can’t I just use a good anti dandruff shampoo

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Because most “anti-dandruff” shampoos only treat **surface flakes**, not the **root cause** — which can differ per person.  

Here’s the breakdown:  

| Cause | Why basic shampoo fails | What actually works |
|-------|--------------------------|----------------------|
| **Fungus (Malassezia)** | Head & Shoulders’ zinc kills some, but not all strains | **Ketoconazole (Nizoral)** |
| **Oil buildup / clogged scalp** | Regular shampoos can’t dissolve hardened sebum | **Salicylic acid (T/Sal)** |
| **Dry, irritated scalp** | Anti-fungal alone makes it drier | **Add a light oil (jojoba)** |
| **Inflamed skin (dermatitis)** | OTC shampoos too weak | **Prescription 2% ketoconazole or mild steroid** |

So—using one “good” shampoo helps short-term, but flakes return because the root cause isn’t fully addressed.
