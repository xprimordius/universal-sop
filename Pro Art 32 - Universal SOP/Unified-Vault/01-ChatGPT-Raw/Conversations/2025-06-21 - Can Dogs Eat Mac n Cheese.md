## 🤖 ChatGPT

**No, it’s not recommended.** While your Maltese-Yorkie-Shih Tzu mix *can physically eat* Goodles mac and cheese, **it’s not healthy or safe for dogs**, especially small breeds.

### 🚫 Why Avoid It:
1. **Dairy (Cheese):** Dogs are often lactose intolerant. Cheese can cause gas, bloating, or diarrhea.
2. **Salt & Seasonings:** Processed mac and cheese is loaded with sodium and artificial flavors — harmful for a dog’s kidneys and heart.
3. **Wheat Pasta:** Not toxic, but not ideal — can trigger food sensitivities or digestive upset.
4. **Additives:** Goodles has vitamins and ingredients that aren't tested for dogs — potential for adverse effects.

---

### ✅ If You Want to Share:
- A tiny bite **without cheese powder or seasoning** (just plain pasta) might be okay **as a rare treat**.
- Always monitor for signs of allergy or upset stomach.

### Best Practice:
Stick to **dog-safe treats** and **vet-approved food.** If you want to give them something fun, go with small amounts of:
- Plain cooked chicken or turkey
- Plain green beans or carrots
- Small bites of cooked sweet potato
