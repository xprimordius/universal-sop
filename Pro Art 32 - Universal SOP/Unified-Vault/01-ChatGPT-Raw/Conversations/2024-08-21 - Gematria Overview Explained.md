WHAT IS GEMATRIA
Understanding the Ancient Practice of Gematria

Gematria is an ancient numerological system that assigns numerical value to letters, words, and phrases based on their underlying numerical values. Originating from the Jewish mystical tradition known as Kabbalah, gematria has been used for centuries to uncover hidden meanings and connections within texts, names, and events. By converting words into numbers, gematria offers insights that can help decode various aspects of life, from personal names to significant timelines.

How Does Gematria Work?

Gematria operates on the principle that every letter in the alphabet corresponds to a specific number. In the Hebrew alphabet, for instance, the first ten letters are assigned numbers from 1 to 10, the next eight letters are assigned numbers from 20 to 90, and so on. This system can be applied to other alphabets, including Greek and English, with each letter being given a numerical equivalent.

For example, in Hebrew gematria:
- The letter Aleph (א) corresponds to 1
- Bet (ב) corresponds to 2
- Gimel (ג) corresponds to 3
- And so forth

In English gematria:
- A corresponds to 1
- B corresponds to 2
- C corresponds to 3
- and so on

Decoding Names with Gematria

One of the most fascinating applications of gematria is the decoding of names. By translating a person's name into its numerical value, gematria can reveal hidden connections and insights about their personality, destiny, and life path.

For instance, let's decode the name "John Doe" using English gematria:
- J = 10
- O = 15

- N = 14
- D = 4
- O = 15

Adding these values together, we get:
10 + 15 + 8 + 14 + 4 + 15 + 5 = 71

The numerical value of 71 can then be interpreted using various gematria interpretations to uncover deeper meanings about John Doe's character and life journey.

### Understanding Life Events and Timelines

Gematria can also be used to decode significant life events and timelines. By converting important dates and events into numerical values, gematria can help identify patterns and correlations that may not be immediately apparent. For example, by examining the numerical value of a birth date or a major life event, individuals can gain insights into the underlying energies and influences at play during those times.
