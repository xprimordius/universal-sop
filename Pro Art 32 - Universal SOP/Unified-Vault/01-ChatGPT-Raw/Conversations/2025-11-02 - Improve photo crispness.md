### 🔹 What ISO Really Does
ISO controls your camera sensor’s **signal amplification** — it *doesn’t* make more light; it **boosts** the light your sensor already captured.

- **Low ISO (100–800):** clean, pure color and dynamic range.  
- **High ISO (1600–6400+):** brighter image but at a cost — more noise, less detail, reduced color accuracy, and blown highlights.

---

### 🔹 Why 1600–3200 Is the Sweet Spot for Concerts
Your Canon R5 Mark II has **incredible low-light ability**, but the balance between brightness and image quality peaks here:

| ISO | Pros | Cons |
|------|------|------|

| **1600–3200** | Ideal concert balance — clean enough for pro edits, bright enough for strobes and LED light shows | Minor grain that edits out easily |
| **6400+** | Exposes dark venues without flash | Grain, color shifts, and muddy blacks — fine for emergency shots, not crisp editorial quality |
