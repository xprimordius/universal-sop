# 🔑 Core Insights on Presuppositions, Belief, and Sales Psychology

## 1. **Tense & Language Shapes Reality**
- Past, present, and future tense reveal patterns and beliefs.  
- **Presuppositions** (the assumptions hidden in language) are the most powerful tool in linguistics.  
- Example:  
  - “Here’s why you should buy” → assumes you don’t want to buy.  
  - “We both know you want this, here’s why today” → assumes desire, makes timing the only issue.  

## 2. **Three Sales Frames**
1. **Convince Frame (Weakest):**  
   - “Here’s why you should buy from me.”  
   - Creates resistance → Mount Everest to climb.  

2. **Assumptive Frame (Strong):**  
   - “I know you want this, let’s do it.”  
   - Removes resistance but still lacks full leverage.  

3. **Qualifying Frame (Strongest):**  
   - “Why should I let you have this?”  
   - Shifts power. Prospect now works to *earn* access.  
   - Each question increases investment → by close, they must buy.  

## 3. **Law of Assumption**
- What you assume, you get.  
- Others adopt your assumptions unconsciously.  
- Example: Confidence → others see confidence → treat you as confident → reinforces belief.  

## 4. **Self-Fulfilling Prophecy Loop**
- Belief → Actions → Others’ Beliefs → Others’ Actions → Reinforces Belief.  
- If belief = “I’m not worthy of money” → timid asks → rejection → reinforced unworthiness.  
- If belief = “I’m extremely valuable” → conviction in close → clients pay → reinforced worth.  

## 5. **Lottery Ticket Metaphor**
- Everyone has a “lottery ticket” (their dream).  
- If you think it’s losing, you won’t act.  
- If you believe it’s winning, you’ll relentlessly do what it takes until it pays out.  
- Giving up = deciding not to cash in your winning ticket.  

## 6. **Consistency = Breakthrough**
- The moment you want to quit is usually the brink of transformation.  
- Patience + consistency = fastest path.  
- Impatience → quitting → decades of delay.  

## 7. **Unconscious vs. Conscious Goals**
- What you *consciously* want must align with what your *unconscious* assumes.  
- Goal: make success so unconscious that even if you tried to fail, you’d succeed.  

---

# 🎯 Application for You
- **Sales:** Always use the **qualifying frame**. Never convince, always filter.  
- **Beliefs:** Audit current self-assumptions. Are they serving your outcomes? If not, reprogram.  
- **Execution:** Treat your dream like the winning lottery ticket. Show up relentlessly.  
- **Alignment:** Move goals into the unconscious through repetition, environment, and consistent action.
