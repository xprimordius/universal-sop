## ⚠️ Colors to be cautious of (for work / shirt pairing)

- Pure **white** dress pants are high-maintenance (stains, transparency). Use only when situation allows.
- **Black pants** you said feel weird — fair — they can feel monotonous with black shirts. Use them sparingly (textured fabrics or tonal difference).
- Very bright / saturated colors (e.g. electric red, bright orange) are risky in formal settings.
- Patterns can help, but only if the base colors stay in the neutral palette.

1. **Khaki / Tan / Beige chinos or trousers**

3. **Navy trousers**  

Then optionally add one accent pair — e.g. **olive** or **cream/off-white** — for dress-up or creative mix.

With those, any black or light-sky blue shirt in your wardrobe will always have a solid match.
