## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Fisheye lens = ultra-wide-angle lens** designed to capture an extremely wide, hemispherical field of view — typically **180°** or more.  

### Key traits
- **Curved distortion:** Straight lines (except those through the center) appear curved or bent outward.  
- **Field of view:** 100°–180° depending on the model.  
- **Focal length:** Usually **8–16 mm** on full-frame cameras.  
- **Look:** Exaggerated, bubble-like perspective; subjects close to the lens appear huge, edges stretch dramatically.

### Uses
- Creative photography (skateboarding, concerts, interiors, astrophotography).  
- Immersive shots (VR, 360°, action cams like GoPro).  
- Tight spaces where you need to show everything.

### Two main types
| Type | Shape | Typical FOV |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **Circular fisheye** | Produces a circular image with black edges | ~180° |
| **Full-frame fisheye** | Fills the sensor; cropped edges | ~100–150° |

**In short:** a fisheye lens captures *everything in view*, bending space for a bold, dramatic effect.
