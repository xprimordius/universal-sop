If she just doesn’t inform the IRS, here’s the reality:  

**1. Tax obligations don’t vanish.**  
- The IRS will still expect final returns (business and employment tax) even if the business has no activity.  
- If nothing is filed, they can send notices, assess penalties, and keep the business tax account open in their system.  

**2. EIN stays “active” forever.**  
- The IRS never cancels an EIN automatically. Even if unused, it will stay tied to her and could trigger letters asking for missing returns.  

**3. Penalty exposure.**  
- If they think she owes returns (e.g., 941 payroll taxes, excise, etc.), the IRS can assess failure-to-file penalties—even if no tax is due.
