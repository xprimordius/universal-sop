## **Pre-Draft Setup**

   - Draft Type: Snake (fair), Auction (strategy-heavy).  
   - Time per pick: 60–90 seconds.  
3. **Draft Date:** Poll league members early, finalize with majority.  
4. **Draft Order:** Randomize live (use online randomizer) or reveal ahead.  

---

## **Commissioner Duties**
1. **Communication:** Send rules & draft info clearly.  
2. **Fairness:** No mid-season rule changes without league vote.  
3. **Draft Execution:** Keep draft moving, manage timers, resolve tech issues.  
4. **Record Keeping:** Track trades, waivers, payouts, and disputes.  
5. **Engagement:** Weekly updates, fun awards, trash talk encouragement.  

---

## **Best Practices**
- **Transparency:** Share rules, scoring, and payouts before draft.  
- **Backup Plan:** Auto-pick settings for no-shows.  
- **Draft Board:** Use live draft board (online or physical if in-person).  
- **Payouts:** Collect entry fees before the draft.  

---

## **Draft Day Flow**
1. Welcome, confirm rules.  
2. Randomize/reveal draft order.  
3. Begin draft, enforce time per pick.  
4. Handle missed picks via auto-draft.  
5. Celebrate with recap/trash talk.
