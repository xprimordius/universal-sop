**Quick answer:** Not all Amex business cards are charge cards.  

### 1. **Charge cards (must pay in full, net ~30 days)**  
These are traditional Amex charge cards, although many allow "Pay Over Time" on eligible purchases:  
- **Business Platinum Card®**  
- **Business Gold Card**  
- **Business Green Rewards Card**  
- **Plum Card®** (has unique flexibility: you can take up to 60 days to pay without interest or get 1.5% back for paying early)  

### 2. **Credit cards (can carry a balance, some have 0% intro APR)**  
These act like standard revolving credit cards:  
- **Blue Business Cash™ Card** (0% intro APR for 12 months)  
- **Blue Business® Plus Credit Card** (0% intro APR for 12 months)  
- **Delta SkyMiles® Business Cards (Gold, Platinum, Reserve)**  
- **Marriott Bonvoy Business® Card**  
- **Hilton Honors Business Card**  
- **Amazon Business Prime Card**  
- **Amazon Business Card**  

### Key points  
- **Charge cards:** No preset spending limit, must generally pay in full, but Business Platinum/Gold/Green offer "Pay Over Time" for eligible purchases.  
- **Credit cards:** Have a credit limit and allow carrying a balance (at interest). Some offer **0% intro APR (up to 12 months)**—mainly the Blue Business Cash and Blue Business Plus.  
- **Plum Card:** hybrid – pay in full within 60 days, no interest, or pay early for a discount.  

➡️ If your priority is **0% intro APR and carrying a balance**, go for:  
1. **Blue Business Cash™**  
2. **Blue Business® Plus**
