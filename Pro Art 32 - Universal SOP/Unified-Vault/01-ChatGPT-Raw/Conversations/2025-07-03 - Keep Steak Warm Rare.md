**How to Keep Rare Steak Warm Without Overcooking (Oven Method):**

**1. Set Oven to Low (130°F–150°F / 54°C–65°C):**  
This keeps it warm without pushing it past rare.

**2. Use a Wire Rack + Pan:**  
Place steak on a wire rack over a baking sheet—this avoids sogginess.

**3. Cover Loosely with Foil (Optional):**  
Lightly tent with foil to retain moisture without steaming.

**4. Time Limit:**  
Max 15–20 mins to prevent texture loss.

**Pro Tip:**  
For longer holds, use a **warm water bath (Sous Vide at 120°F / 49°C)** instead—it keeps perfect rare without drying.
