---

### **Base Pay Reality**
- **$850/week gross** ≈ **$44,200/year**
  - ($850 × 52 weeks)

So **base alone ≠ $85K**.

---

### **Bonuses Math**
Quarterly bonuses:
- Low end: **$3,500 × 4 = $14,000**
- High end: **$7,500 × 4 = $30,000**

Add to base:
- **Low total:** $44,200 + $14,000 = **$58,200**
- **High total:** $44,200 + $30,000 = **$74,200**

Still **not $85K**.

---

### **How $85K *could* be claimed**
Only makes sense if **one or more** of these are true:

1. **$85K is “OTE” (On-Target Earnings)**  
   → assumes *maximum* bonuses she doesn’t consistently hit.

2. **$850 is net, not gross**  
   → If that’s after taxes, gross might be ~$1,150–$1,250/week  
   → That *could* support an $80–85K claim.

3. **Irregular / annual bonus not mentioned**  
   → Year-end, retention, commission accelerators, etc.

4. **Benefits counted as “comp”**  
   → Employers sometimes inflate salary by adding health insurance value (misleading but common).

---

### **Bottom Line**
- **$850/week + stated bonuses = ~$58K–$74K**
- **$85K only works if:**
  - $850 is **net**, or  
  - $85K is **OTE / theoretical max**, not actual take-home
