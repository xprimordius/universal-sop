Is ChatGPT owned by Microsoft and open source?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

No, **ChatGPT is not owned by Microsoft** and **it is not open source**.

### Ownership:
- **ChatGPT is developed and owned by OpenAI**, an independent AI research and deployment company.
- **Microsoft is a major investor and partner** of OpenAI, and it has integrated OpenAI's models (like GPT-4) into its own products (e.g., Copilot in Word, Excel, and Azure OpenAI Service).
- However, **Microsoft does not own ChatGPT or OpenAI**.

### Open Source Status:
- **ChatGPT (GPT-4, GPT-3.5, etc.) is proprietary and closed-source.**
- OpenAI has not open-sourced its most advanced models due to safety and commercial concerns.
