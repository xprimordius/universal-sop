# Kasa Smart Power Strip Issue

---

## 👤 You

TP Link power strip Kasa smart SYS light blinking red , why?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

The blinking red light on your TP-Link Kasa Smart power strip usually indicates one of the following issues:

1. **Wi-Fi Connection Issues**: The power strip may not be able to connect to your Wi-Fi network. Check if your router is working properly and that the power strip is within range of the Wi-Fi signal.
   
2. **Setup Mode**: If the device was recently reset or is new, the red blinking light may indicate it is in setup mode, waiting for configuration via the Kasa app.
   
3. **Firmware Update Required**: A blinking red light can sometimes indicate the need for a firmware update. Try updating the device firmware via the Kasa app.
   
4. **Power Issue**: There could be a problem with the power supply to the device. Check the outlet and ensure the power strip is properly plugged in and receiving power.

5. **Device Malfunction**: If none of the above seem to be the issue, it might indicate a malfunction in the power strip. In this case, try resetting it by holding the reset button for 10 seconds or contacting TP-Link support.
