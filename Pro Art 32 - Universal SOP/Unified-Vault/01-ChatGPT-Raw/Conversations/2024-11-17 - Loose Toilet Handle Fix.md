To fix the loose toilet handle so it functions properly and stays in place, follow these steps:

### Tools Needed:
- Adjustable wrench or pliers
- Screwdriver (if applicable)

---

### Steps to Fix:

1. **Inspect the Handle Mechanism:**
   - Remove the toilet tank lid and place it safely aside.
   - Look inside to check if the handle is connected to the flush lever properly.
   - Check the nut securing the handle on the inside of the tank.

2. **Tighten the Nut:**
   - The white plastic or metal nut inside the tank holds the handle in place.
   - Use your hand or an adjustable wrench to gently tighten the nut. 
   - **Important:** This nut is reverse-threaded, so turn it **counterclockwise** to tighten.
   - Ensure it’s snug, but don’t overtighten to avoid cracking the porcelain or damaging the mechanism.

3. **Align the Handle:**
   - Make sure the handle is positioned correctly on the outside of the tank before fully tightening.

4. **Check the Flush Lever Arm:**
   - Inspect the flush lever (the metal or plastic arm connected to the handle inside the tank).
   - Ensure it is securely connected to the chain or strap leading to the flapper.
   - Adjust the chain to have slight slack; it should not be too tight or too loose.

5. **Test the Handle:**
   - Flush the toilet and observe the handle’s operation.
   - Make sure it doesn’t come loose, and the flapper closes fully after flushing.

---

### If the Handle or Lever Is Broken:
- Replace it with a new toilet handle kit from a hardware store.
- To install:
  - Unscrew the old handle by loosening the nut (reverse-threaded).
  - Insert the new handle through the hole in the tank.
  - Secure it with the nut (turn **counterclockwise** to tighten).
  - Attach the flush lever and chain.

This repair should stop the handle from coming loose and ensure proper flushing. Let me know if you need further guidance!
