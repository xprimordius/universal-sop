Does lime juice kill parasites like pineapple juice does

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**No**, lime juice does **not** kill parasites in the same way or as effectively as **pineapple juice**.

### Key Differences:

- **Pineapple juice** contains **bromelain**, a powerful enzyme that can **break down protein structures** in parasites and disrupt their outer layers. It’s particularly hostile to intestinal worms.

- **Lime juice** is **acidic (citric acid)** and **antimicrobial**, which may help create an unfavorable environment for some pathogens, but it **does not contain proteolytic enzymes** like bromelain. Its effect on parasites is minimal.

### TLDR:
- **Pineapple juice**: Disrupts parasites via **enzymatic breakdown**.
- **Lime juice**: Antimicrobial, but **not strong against parasites**.

For antiparasitic effect, **pineapple**, **papaya seeds**, and **wormwood/black walnut/clove protocols** are more effective.
