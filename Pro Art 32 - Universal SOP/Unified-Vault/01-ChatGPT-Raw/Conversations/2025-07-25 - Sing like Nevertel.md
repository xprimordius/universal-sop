**Beginner Guide: Learn to Sing Like Nevertel (Even If You're Out of Tune)**

---

### 🧭 PHASE 1: CORE FOUNDATIONS (Weeks 1–4)

**1. Train Your Ear (Pitch First)**
- 🧠 *Goal*: Recognize and match notes correctly.
- 🎯 *Exercise*:
  - Use **apps like**: *Vanido*, *Toned Ear*, *Vocal Pitch Monitor*
  - Sing single notes → match piano or app tone
  - Record yourself and correct what’s off

**2. Breath Control = Power**
- 🧘‍♂️ *Goal*: Support every note from your diaphragm, not your throat.
- 🎯 *Exercise*:
  - Lay on your back, place a book on your belly → breathe and raise the book only
  - Then apply same breath control while singing long “ah” or “oo” tones

**3. Daily Vocal Warm-Ups**
- 🧼 *Clean tone = easier pitch*
- 🎯 *Warm-Ups*:
  - Lip trills (brrrr)
  - Sirens (ng → ee)
  - Scales with “mum” or “noo”

---

### 🎤 PHASE 2: EMOTIONAL EXPRESSION (Weeks 4–8)

**4. Learn One Song Line-by-Line**
- 🎯 *Pick*: Nevertel – *Some Things*
- Break into **8-second phrases**
  - Speak the line with emotion
  - Sing it on one note
  - Add melody back slowly
  - Record + compare to original

**5. Practice Dynamics**
- Whisper → speak → belt
- Use soft vocal tone on verses, build intensity gradually into chorus

**6. Focus on Feeling, Not Just Notes**
- Channel real emotion while you sing.
- Practice “talk-singing” the lyrics like a monologue.

---

### ⚡️ PHASE 3: POWER + STYLE (Weeks 8–12)

**7. Build Your Chest & Mixed Voice**
- 🎯 *Exercise*:
  - “Hey!” (chest voice)
  - “Nay-nay-nay” (bratty sound) for mixed
  - Blend low and high for belted choruses

**8. Add Controlled Rasp (Optional)**
- 🎯 Start with **vocal fry** ("uhhh") gently
- NEVER push — learn **false cord technique** over time

**9. Vocal Lessons or Feedback**
- Use **YouTube coaches** (Eric Arceneaux, Madeleine Harvey, Ramsey Voice)
- Or find a **$25/mo coach on Fiverr** to check your tone weekly
