### ⚖️ General Commercial Eviction Process (U.S.):

1. **Notice to Vacate or Pay Rent**  
   - Usually **3 to 30 days**, depending on state.
   - In Minnesota, for commercial leases **without a grace period**, a **Notice to Quit** or **Demand for Possession** may be served **immediately after default**.

2. **Court Filing (Eviction Lawsuit / Unlawful Detainer)**  
   - If the tenant doesn't comply with the notice, the landlord files an eviction lawsuit.
   - Court hearing is typically **within 7–14 days** after filing.

3. **Court Hearing & Judgment**  
   - If the landlord wins, a **Writ of Recovery** (eviction order) is issued.

4. **Writ Execution (Forced Removal)**  
   - In Minnesota: Sheriff's office typically gives **24–72 hours' notice** before forcibly removing the tenant.

---
