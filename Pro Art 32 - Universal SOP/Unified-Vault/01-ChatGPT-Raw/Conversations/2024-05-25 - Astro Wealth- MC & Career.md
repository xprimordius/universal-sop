My Kerry gold grass fed cheese has clumps and specs of what appears to be white mold on the edges of it. I just opened it. What is it? And is it safe to eat?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

The clumps and specks of white mold on the edges of your Kerrygold grass-fed cheese are likely mold growth. Mold can develop on cheese due to exposure to air and moisture, even if the cheese was sealed. 

Here are some considerations regarding safety and mold on cheese:

### Identifying Mold
1. **Appearance**: White mold can be common on cheese, but it can also appear in other colors like blue, green, or black. 
2. **Texture**: Mold might look fuzzy or powdery.

### Safety Considerations
- **Hard and Semi-Hard Cheeses**: For hard and semi-hard cheeses like cheddar, it is generally considered safe to cut off the moldy part and eat the rest. Cut at least 1 inch (2.5 cm) around and below the mold spot to ensure you remove all the affected areas.
- **Soft Cheeses**: For soft cheeses (like cream cheese, cottage cheese, or cheese slices), it's usually recommended to discard the entire product if you see mold, as it can penetrate deeper into the cheese and spread more easily.

### Steps to Take
1. **Examine the Cheese**: Check if the mold is just on the surface or if it has penetrated deeper.
2. **Cutting Away Mold**: If it's a hard or semi-hard cheese, use a clean knife to cut away at least 1 inch around and below the moldy spots.
3. **Check Smell and Taste**: If the cheese smells off or has an unusual taste, it's better to err on the side of caution and discard it.
4. **Storage**: Ensure proper storage to prevent future mold growth. Wrap the cheese tightly in parchment or wax paper and store it in the refrigerator.

### Final Decision
- If you are unsure about the safety or extent of the mold, it’s safer to discard the cheese to avoid any potential health risks.
- If the cheese is within its expiration date and you believe it was stored properly, you may also consider contacting Kerrygold or the place of purchase for a refund or replacement.

### Prevention Tips
- **Proper Storage**: Store cheese in an airtight container or wrap it properly to minimize exposure to air and moisture.
- **Cleanliness**: Ensure that the cutting board and knife used for cheese are clean to avoid cross-contamination.

While some molds on cheese can be harmless, it's always best to be cautious, especially if the mold is unexpected or if you're unsure of the cheese's condition.

If there’s mold in an open and sealed pizza sauce container; can I just scrape off the top and eat the rest safely?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

If there is mold in an open and sealed pizza sauce container, it is not safe to just scrape off the top and consume the rest. Here’s why:

### Mold on Pizza Sauce
1. **Penetration**: Mold can have roots that penetrate deeply into the sauce, which are not visible on the surface. This means that scraping off the visible mold does not remove all the mold and its spores.
2. **Toxins**: Some molds produce mycotoxins, which are harmful substances that can cause illness. These toxins can spread throughout the sauce, even if the mold itself is not visible in other areas.

### Health Risks
- **Food Poisoning**: Consuming moldy food can lead to food poisoning, causing symptoms like nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and stomach cramps.
- **Allergic Reactions**: Mold can cause allergic reactions in some people, including respiratory issues and skin irritation.
- **Mycotoxins**: Some molds produce mycotoxins, which can be particularly harmful and potentially carcinogenic.

### Recommendations
- **Discard the Sauce**: It is safest to discard the entire container of pizza sauce if you see any mold. This ensures that you avoid consuming any harmful mold or toxins.
- **Proper Storage**: Store opened pizza sauce in the refrigerator and use it within the recommended time frame. Always use clean utensils to scoop out the sauce to prevent contamination.

### Conclusion
For your safety, do not consume the pizza sauce if you find mold in it. Mold can spread and produce harmful toxins that are not removed by scraping off the visible parts. Discard the entire container to avoid any health risks.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

A block of cheese that is wet on all the edges can be a cause for concern, depending on the type of cheese and the nature of the moisture. Here are some considerations to help determine if the cheese is still safe to eat:

### Potential Concerns
1. **Excess Moisture**: Cheese should generally not be wet. Excess moisture can indicate improper storage or spoilage, as moisture can promote the growth of bacteria and mold.
2. **Texture and Smell**: Wet edges might lead to changes in texture and smell, which can be indicators of spoilage.
3. **Packaging Issues**: If the cheese was not sealed properly, it could have absorbed moisture from the environment, leading to potential spoilage.

### Steps to Assess the Cheese
1. **Check Smell**: Give the cheese a sniff. If it smells sour, off, or has a strong ammonia-like odor, it’s likely spoiled and should be discarded.
2. **Examine Texture**: Check if the texture of the cheese has changed. Slimy or excessively soft edges can be a sign of bacterial growth.
3. **Look for Mold**: Inspect the cheese for any visible signs of mold. If you see mold, especially if it’s spreading, it’s safer to discard the cheese.

### What to Do
- **Cut Off the Edges**: If the cheese smells normal and the texture is otherwise fine, you can try cutting off the wet edges and see if the interior of the cheese looks and smells okay.
- **Reevaluate**: After cutting off the wet edges, if the remaining cheese still seems off in any way, it’s best to discard it.
- **Proper Storage**: Ensure that you store cheese properly in the future. Wrap it tightly in wax paper or cheese paper and then place it in a sealed plastic bag or container to reduce exposure to moisture.

### Conclusion
While a bit of moisture on cheese isn’t always a sign of spoilage, wet edges are unusual and can indicate a problem. If the cheese smells fine and the interior texture is good after trimming the edges, it may still be safe to eat. However, if there are any doubts about its safety, it’s best to discard the cheese to avoid the risk of foodborne illness.

> # Selfic Orienting Paintings or Spheroself
> 
> The overall coordination of the “Tree Orientation” project is coordinated by the School of Meditation of Damanhur. If you are interested in participating in orienting trees using a Selfic painting or your Spheroself, it is necessary for us to gather some data on the operations that are being performed (procedure, place, number of trees).
> 
> Please contact the School directly at the following email address: globaltreenet@damanhur.it when you acquire your painting or add the Triad layer to your spheroself and let us know:
> 
>   * Name
>   * How you acquired your device for orienting.
>   * General area (city, state, country) where you will be orienting trees
> 
> You only have to do this once. If you travel and want to orient trees in a new area, send an email before you begin and let us know your new location. You may also ask where trees have already been oriented in a particular area of the world before you choose exactly where to orient.
> 
> Every time you go out and orient trees, when you are done send us an email with specific information about your work. You can 【11†find here the details】.
> 
> Working with a Selfic Orienting Painting or a Spheroself
> 
> You can operate on your own by using a 【12†spheroself †sel-et.com】or an 【13†orienting painting†www.quadriselfici.it】, which are not necessarily owned by you. The spheroself musthave the program for orienting trees. Take either the spheroself or the painting around each tree between 1 and 3 times, either on your own or with the help of others, in the latter case by passing them from one person to the other. The tools can be kept inside baskets, boxes, bags, or rucksacks, if necessary. When working in a group and you have both tools at your disposal, you can make them go around different trees at the same time.
> 
> 【5†Selfic pendulums】 can be connected to an orientating painting, which can belong to a different person (as ‘ownership’ of a selfic painting we mean the unique, direct relationship built by that painting with its owner).
> 
> It is possible to connect up to 12 pendulums to an orientating painting.
> 
> The painting must be placed within a range of 10 kms (6 miles) from the location where the orienting is taking place. To operate in different conditions it is necessary to first ask the 【14†Coordination Team】.
> 
> When you have orientated 6000 trees, either on your own or with a group, you can start orientating groups of about twenty trees at the same time, by surrounding an area of about 80 sqm and walking around it.
> 
> Afterwards, for the groups working with the cabin, these numbers can change depending on conditions that have to be discussed each time with the Coordination team. After a few thousand orientations you can use some means of transport in order to surround the territory, for example a bicycle, a car or a boat (like it has been done around a Hawaiian island), provided that you go slowly. It is very important, though, that you contact the 【14†Coordination Team】 in order to get their approval.
