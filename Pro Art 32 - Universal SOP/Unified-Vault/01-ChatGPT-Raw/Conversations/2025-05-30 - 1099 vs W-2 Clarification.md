### ⚠️ Why It *Can* Be a Red Flag:
1. **Terminology Confusion** – Saying "employees" without W-2s looks like **misrepresentation** or lack of financial precision.
2. **Lender Preferences** – Some lenders view **W-2 staff** as a sign of stability, infrastructure, and payroll obligations (i.e. "real business").
3. **Payroll-Driven Programs** – Programs like **ERC, SBA**, or **payroll-based underwriting** require W-2s.
4. **Risk Signals** – All-1099 staff may signal higher risk, lack of commitment, or potential **worker misclassification** (which is legally risky).

---

### ✅ Not Always a Dealbreaker:
- **Many funders** (especially fintechs, alt lenders, business credit card issuers) **don’t care** as long as your **revenues, margins, and account balances** are strong.
- Just don’t claim “employees” if they’re 1099s—frame it correctly.
