Here’s how to cook **beef back rib slices** on a **Blackstone griddle** for best flavor and tenderness:

---

### **Quick Method (for pre-cooked or thinner rib slices):**
1. **Preheat Blackstone** to medium-high (~375°F–400°F).
2. **Prep ribs:** Pat dry. Lightly coat in oil and season (salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika).
3. **Sear ribs:** Place sliced ribs **meat-side down**. Cook 3–4 minutes to brown.
4. **Flip & cook:** Flip and cook other side 3–4 mins. Press down for crust.
5. **Optional steam finish:** Add a splash of water, cover with dome to tenderize for 2–3 mins.
6. **Rest 5 mins.**

---

### **Slow & Tender Method (for raw, thick slices):**
1. **Season ribs** generously. Let sit at room temp 20–30 mins.
2. **Preheat to medium (~325°F)**.
3. **Griddle cook:** Start bone-side down. Flip every 4–5 mins. Total: 25–35 mins.
4. **Check internal temp:** Target 185°F–200°F for fall-off-the-bone.
5. **Finish with BBQ sauce (optional):** Last 5 mins, glaze and cover to caramelize.

---

### **Tips:**
- Use **two-zone heat** if possible: hot side for sear, cooler side for slow finish.
- A **griddle press** helps develop crust.
- Rest before slicing/serving to lock in juices.
