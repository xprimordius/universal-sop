To firm up stools if they are too soft, focus on eating the following foods:

### 1. **High-Fiber Foods (Soluble Fiber):** 
   - Soluble fiber absorbs water and adds bulk to stools.
   - **Examples:**
     - Bananas (ripe)
     - Applesauce
     - Oatmeal
     - White rice
     - Potatoes (without skin)

### 2. **Low-Fiber, Binding Foods:**  
   - These help firm up stools by slowing digestion.
   - **Examples:**
     - Toast (preferably white bread)
     - Crackers (plain, like saltines)
     - Cooked carrots
     - Boiled or baked chicken (without skin or added fat)

### 3. **Probiotic-Rich Foods:**
   - Balance gut bacteria and promote stool consistency.
   - **Examples:**
     - Yogurt with live cultures
     - Kefir

### 4. **Foods Rich in Tannins:**
   - Tannins can help tighten up intestinal lining.
   - **Examples:**
     - Black tea
     - Blueberries

### 5. **Hydration (but in Moderation):**
   - Drink small amounts of water frequently to avoid dehydration, which can worsen diarrhea but don't overhydrate as it may keep stools soft.

### Foods to Avoid:
   - High-fat or greasy foods (can loosen stools).
   - Excessive fruit or fruit juices (especially prunes, pears, and cherries).
   - Dairy (if lactose intolerant).

Adjust your diet gradually, and if the issue persists, consult a healthcare provider.
