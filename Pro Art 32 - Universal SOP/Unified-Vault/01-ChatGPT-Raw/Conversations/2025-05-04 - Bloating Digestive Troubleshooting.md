## 👤 You

Why am I bloating from everything I eat? Organic grass fed beef plain bloating. Now organic chicken broth and organic tofu bloating

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

If you're bloating from clean, simple foods like grass-fed beef, chicken broth, and tofu, likely causes include:

### **1. Low Stomach Acid (Hypochlorhydria)**
- Can't properly break down protein → fermentation → gas.
- Especially common with chronic stress, age, or antacid use.

**Fix:** Betaine HCl + pepsin with meals, apple cider vinegar pre-meal.

---

### **2. Gut Dysbiosis / SIBO**
- Bacterial overgrowth in small intestine causes gas from even clean foods.
- Common signs: bloating within 30–90 minutes of eating, worse with protein/fat/fiber.

**Fix:** GI mapping, antimicrobials, low-FODMAP diet short-term.

---

### **3. Enzyme Deficiency**
- Insufficient digestive enzymes → incomplete digestion → bloating.

**Fix:** Broad-spectrum digestive enzymes (look for protease, lipase, amylase).

---

### **4. Food Sensitivities**
- Even organic, whole foods can trigger immune response in some (e.g., histamine in broth, soy in tofu, amines in beef).

**Fix:** Elimination diet + reintroduction. Test histamine intolerance.

---

### **5. Sluggish Motility**
- Food moves slowly → fermentation and gas buildup.
- Linked to dehydration, poor vagal tone, stress.

**Fix:** Daily walking, hydration, ginger, vagus nerve exercises.

---

### TLDR Fix Stack:
- **Betaine HCl + enzymes** with meals
- **Low-FODMAP** or elimination diet test
- **GI testing** if symptoms persist >2 weeks
- Support **motility** (movement, hydration, ginger tea)
- Consider **functional gut health eval** if unresolved
