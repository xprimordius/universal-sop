**Cashback vs Points: Which Is Better?**

### TLDR:
- **Cashback = Simplicity and guaranteed value.**
- **Points = Higher upside if redeemed smartly (e.g., travel), but more effort.**

---

### **Choose Cashback If:**
- You want **straightforward rewards** with no mental overhead.
- You value **predictable value** (e.g., 1%–2% cash = $10–$20 per $1,000 spent).
- You don’t travel much or don’t want to learn point systems.

---

### **Choose Points If:**
- You travel frequently and can **redeem at high value** (e.g., 2–5¢ per point on flights/hotels).
- You’re willing to **optimize transfer partners** (Amex, Chase, etc.).
- You want **luxury perks** (e.g., business class flights, upgrades) at steep discounts.

---

### **Optimal Strategy (Hybrid):**
- Use **points cards** (e.g., Amex Gold, Chase Sapphire) for travel/dining.
- Use **cashback cards** (e.g., Citi Double Cash, Wells Fargo Active Cash) for everything else.
- Maximize **intro bonuses** + category multipliers.

---

### **Final Rule of Thumb:**
- **If you’re not optimizing points, cashback is better.**
- **If you want max travel leverage, go points and learn the system.**
