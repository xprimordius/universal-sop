What would the monthly payments be

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

At 0% APR, you’re only required to pay the minimum monthly payment set by the credit card issuer, which typically ranges from 1% to 3% of the balance.

For $600,000 in credit card debt:

1. **1% Minimum Payment**:  
   $600,000 x 1% = **$6,000** per month.

2. **2% Minimum Payment**:  
   $600,000 x 2% = **$12,000** per month.

3. **3% Minimum Payment**:  
   $600,000 x 3% = **$18,000** per month.

These figures would hold as long as the 0% APR remains active and there are no additional fees or charges. Once the 0% APR period ends, any remaining balance would accrue interest at the standard APR, increasing the monthly payment.
