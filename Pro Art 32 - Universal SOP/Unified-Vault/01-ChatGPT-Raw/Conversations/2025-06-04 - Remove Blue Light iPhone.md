## 🤖 ChatGPT

To remove blue light from your iPhone at night:

### **Enable Night Shift (Warms screen tone):**
1. Go to **Settings** > **Display & Brightness** > **Night Shift**  
2. Toggle **Scheduled** → Set from sunset to sunrise or custom time  
3. Drag the **color temperature slider** to **“More Warm”**

### **Enable Reduce White Point (Dims intensity):**
1. Go to **Settings** > **Accessibility** > **Display & Text Size**  
2. Turn on **Reduce White Point** → Adjust slider to preferred level

### **Use Color Filters (Optional deeper filter):**
1. Go to **Settings** > **Accessibility** > **Display & Text Size** > **Color Filters**  
2. Turn on **Color Filters** → Choose **Color Tint**  
3. Set **Intensity** and **Hue** to reduce blue (move hue toward red)

### **Quick Toggle via Control Center:**
- Pull down Control Center  
- Long press **Brightness slider** → Tap **Night Shift** to toggle

Done. Blue light filtered.
