- Streaming at higher resolution (4K / high bitrate) even if visually similar.  
- Devices you forgot about connected to WiFi all day (smart TVs, game consoles).  
- Speed tests or large downloads/uploads.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes — but only under specific loan types.  

---

## Standard Navy / VA / FHA / Conventional
- **Require tax returns and W-2/1099 income verification** if you’re self-employed or have variable income.  
- If you’re salaried with consistent W-2s, sometimes 1–2 years of W-2s + paystubs suffice.  

---

## Alternatives without tax returns
- **Bank Statement Loans** (non-QM): Lenders use 12–24 months of personal or business bank statements to average deposits instead of tax returns.  
- **Asset Depletion Loans**: Large verified assets (cash, stocks, retirement funds) are treated as income.  
- **DSCR Loans (Debt Service Coverage Ratio)**: For investment properties, approval is based on the property’s rental income vs its mortgage payment — no personal tax returns.  
- **No-Doc / Low-Doc (rare today)**: Only offered by certain non-QM/private lenders at higher rates/fees.  
- **Hard Money Loans**: Private short-term financing; asset-based, no tax returns needed. Much higher cost.  

---

## Navy Federal specifically
- Their **Homebuyers Choice / Military Choice / VA** mortgages are **full-doc**: they *do require* income verification (W-2s or tax returns if self-employed).  
- They don’t currently offer non-QM bank-statement or DSCR loans.  

---

## Strategy
- If you want Navy’s 0-down loans → you’ll need standard documentation.  
- If you want **no-tax-return financing** → you’ll need to look at non-QM lenders, DSCR lenders (if buying rental property), or private money. Expect higher rates (often 7–10% vs 6–7% conventional).
