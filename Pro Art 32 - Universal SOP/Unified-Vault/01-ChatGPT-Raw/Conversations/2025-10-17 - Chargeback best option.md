## 🤖 ChatGPT

Best option for your case (merchant promised coaching calls as part of a package but isn’t delivering them):  

✅ **Select:**  
> “I was charged for something that’s faulty, damaged or not as described.”

### Why
- You *did receive* something (the package), but it’s **not as described** — the coaching calls were a core promised component.  
- This phrasing aligns with **“services not rendered as described”** in Visa/Mastercard chargeback codes (common for coaching, consulting, or course programs).  
- It avoids the higher burden of proof attached to “cancelled/returned” (which requires a clear cancellation record) and fits best for **non-delivered or misrepresented service components**.  

### Support tips
- Attach the **original offer or ad** showing that coaching calls were included.  
- Include **proof of communication** where the merchant acknowledges the calls or fails to provide them.  
- Phrase your summary as:  
  > “The merchant sold a program including live coaching calls, but these calls were never provided. The service is not as described and therefore incomplete.”

That will make it easy for the dispute analyst to categorize it correctly and process your chargeback faster.
