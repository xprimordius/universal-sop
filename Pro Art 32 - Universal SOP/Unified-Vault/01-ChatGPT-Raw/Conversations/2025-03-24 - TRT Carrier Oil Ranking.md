### **TRT in Cottonseed Oil – Risks**
Cottonseed oil is one of the **least optimal** carriers for testosterone replacement therapy (TRT) due to:

1. **Inflammatory Potential** – High in omega-6 fatty acids, promotes systemic inflammation.
2. **Pesticide Residue Risk** – Cotton is not classified as a food crop, so it can be heavily treated with pesticides.
3. **Refinement Chemicals** – Often chemically refined, bleached, and deodorized.
4. **Allergy & Sensitivity** – Higher risk of allergic reactions or injection site irritation.
5. **Oxidative Instability** – Prone to oxidation, which may degrade hormone integrity and cause tissue irritation.
