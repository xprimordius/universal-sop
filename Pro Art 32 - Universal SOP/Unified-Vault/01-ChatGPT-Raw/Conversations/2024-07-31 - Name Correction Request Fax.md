### Which is Better: Fax or Mail?

Both fax and mail are viable options for sending your request. Here are the considerations for each method:

- **Fax:** Generally faster and provides immediate confirmation that the document was sent. It’s a good option if you need the correction made quickly.
- **Mail:** More traditional and can be perceived as more formal. Certified mail can also provide proof of delivery and receipt.

Given the urgency and the need for a paper trail, you might consider faxing the letter and following up with a mailed copy sent via certified mail for additional assurance.
