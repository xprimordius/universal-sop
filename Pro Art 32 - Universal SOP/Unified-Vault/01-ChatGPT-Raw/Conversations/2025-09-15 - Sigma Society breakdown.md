## What Works & What’s Hype

**What Seems Solid / Useful:**

- Mindset training: many guys over-value external things (money, looks) and neglect internal confidence/value. Shifting your identity to someone who *deserves* choice is powerful.  
- Triggering high desirability through status cues, mystery, value, dominance in small ways.  
- Using social media properly: letting your lifestyle, adventure, competence show so you become more attractive without talking.  
- Playing scarcity & ambiguity in a natural way: being less available, more selective, having your own mission so women sense you aren’t just “waiting.”  

**What’s Risky / Overpromised:**

- Guarantee of “women chasing you” as end result: people’s responses vary massively. Expect friction.  
- High price tag or coaching costs; the leap from theory to practice often takes time.  
- Some “triggers” may backfire if done poorly (e.g. arrogance vs confidence, manipulation vs authenticity).  
- Over-reliance on scripts / playbooks: can feel fake unless internalized.

## How to Use It to Make Women Chase *You*

Here’s a tactical plan — integrate what works with who you are:

1. **Solidify Identity & Purpose**
   - Know your mission, values, what you want. Be visibly pursuing something bigger than “getting dates.” Makes you magnetic.  
   - Build confidence from discipline: fitness, business, hobbies. Self-mastery shows.

2. **Leverage Social Proof + Visible Value**
   - Use social media: show your lifestyle, skills, network, moments of fun/adventure. Let people see *you living*.  
   - Make your circles, friends, and associations reflect high value.

3. **Use Attraction Triggers Authentically**
   - Mystery / less availability: don’t always be instantly responding or always “free.”  
   - Contrast: show you can be high energy, serious, playful. Varied emotional tone.  
   - Scarcity: not artificially, but genuinely (because you have your own life).  

4. **Text & Approach Strategy**
   - Use well-constructed messages that open curiosity, tease without over-giving, show wit.  
   - Approaches: be playful, challenge a little, don't ask permission for attention.  

5. **Trigger Chasing Mechanisms**
   - Let her invest: ask questions, have her show interest and engagement.  
   - Pull back when she pulls you in; don’t over-pursue.  
   - Be slightly unpredictable: consistency in your mission and core, but not in always being available.

6. **Optimize Physical & Social Presentation**
   - Grooming, fitness, style: at least maintain high standards.  
   - Nonverbal cues: body language, posture, eye contact, voice tone.  

7. **Iteration & Feedback**
   - Track what works vs what doesn’t. Which triggers get responses? Adjust.  
   - Be honest with yourself about outcome; disown what doesn’t feel aligned.
