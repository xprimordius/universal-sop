## 👤 You

What to feed ducks

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

### ✅ Best Foods for Ducks (Safe & Healthy)
1. **Grains & Seeds**  
   - Cracked corn  
   - Barley  
   - Oats  
   - Millet  
   - Bird seed

2. **Vegetables (chopped/small pieces)**  
   - Lettuce (not iceberg), kale, spinach  
   - Peas, corn, cucumber, zucchini

3. **Fruits (limited)**  
   - Berries, grapes (halved), apple slices (no seeds), bananas

4. **Duck Pellets (store-bought waterfowl feed)**  
   - Nutritionally complete and ideal

5. **Insects & Protein**  
   - Mealworms, earthworms, bugs (live or dried)

---

### ❌ Never Feed Ducks:
- Bread (causes malnutrition, pollutes water)
- Chips, crackers, popcorn, cookies
- Onions, garlic, citrus
- Raw beans or uncooked rice
- Avocado, chocolate, caffeine

---

**Pro Tip:** Feed sparingly, ideally on land, not in water. Clean up uneaten food.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

They do love water, but **ducks often nest on land**, especially in quiet, sheltered spots—like next to your chimney.

### Why They Chose Your House:
- **Safe from predators**
- **Dry, protected corner**
- **Within walking distance to water**

### What to Do:
- **Don’t move the nest** — mother duck knows where it is.
- **Avoid disturbing them** — stress can cause her to abandon the eggs.
- **Expect ducklings to hatch in ~28 days**, then walk to water within 24 hours.

### You Can Help By:
- Keeping pets/kids away
- Placing a shallow dish of **water** nearby (no deep bowls)
- Offering safe food like **cracked corn or chopped greens**

If they’re in danger or need relocating, call local wildlife rescue — they’ll guide you.
