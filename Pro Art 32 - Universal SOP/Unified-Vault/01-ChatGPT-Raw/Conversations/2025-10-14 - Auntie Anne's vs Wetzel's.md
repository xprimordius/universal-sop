### 🔬 Inflammation Mechanisms

- **Omega-6 seed oils** (canola, soy) → promote *arachidonic acid* pathway → chronic inflammation.  
- **Refined sugars** → spike insulin, feed gut dysbiosis → bloating + fatigue.  
- **Dense gluten structures** (especially when not fermented) → poor digestion and gas.  

Wetzel’s typically has **lower sugar and fat density**, making it **less glycemically inflammatory**.

---

### 🧠 Best Practice if You Want Minimal Bloat

1. **Eat fresh** (avoid old pretzels held under heat lamps).  
2. **Skip butter glaze & dips** → that’s the biggest inflammatory trigger.  
3. **Pair with lemon water** → helps offset sodium and acidity.  
4. **Eat slowly, chew thoroughly** → reduces fermentation/gas in gut.
