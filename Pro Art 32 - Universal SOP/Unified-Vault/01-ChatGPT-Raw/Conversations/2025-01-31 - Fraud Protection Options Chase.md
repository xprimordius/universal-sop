Yes, if someone has your **account and routing number**, they can create fraudulent checks or attempt ACH debits to withdraw funds from your account. However, you **do have options** besides closing the account.

### **Alternatives to Closing Your Chase Business Account**
1. **ACH Debit Block or Filter**  
   - **Chase allows you to block unauthorized ACH withdrawals** from specific entities or entirely.
   - You can set up an **ACH Debit Filter**, where only pre-approved vendors can withdraw funds.

2. **Check Fraud Protection (Positive Pay)**  
   - **Chase Positive Pay** allows you to pre-authorize checks, meaning only those you issue will be honored.
   - Any fraudulent check attempts will be flagged before processing.

3. **Account Monitoring & Alerts**  
   - Set up **real-time transaction alerts** to catch any suspicious activity instantly.
   - Chase’s **Fraud Department** can flag any unusual withdrawals and help reverse them.

4. **Dispute Unauthorized Transactions**  
   - If fraud occurs, **you can dispute ACH transactions or checks** within a specific timeframe (typically 60 days under Regulation E for ACH).
   - Chase will conduct an investigation and may **reverse** the fraudulent charges.

5. **Restrict Online Access & Add Security Layers**  
   - Enable **two-factor authentication (2FA)** and ensure no unauthorized parties can initiate online payments.

6. **Request a "Hard Hold" on Withdrawals**  
   - You can ask Chase to **block all new withdrawals** while you evaluate fraud risks and decide on a solution.

### **Should You Open a New Account?**
- If fraud has **already occurred**, Chase **may insist on closing** your account for security reasons. 
- If fraud **hasn’t happened yet**, **blocking ACH debits** and enabling **check fraud protection** might be enough.

You **do not have to lose your banking history**—if you do open a new account, **Chase can transfer your history over**, and you can update vendors to avoid disruption.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

To enable **ACH Debit Block/Filter, Positive Pay, and Transaction Alerts** via the **Chase Business app**, follow these steps:

### **1. Enable ACH Debit Block or Filter**
(Prevents unauthorized ACH withdrawals)
1. Open the **Chase Mobile® app**.
2. Tap **"Pay & Transfer"** at the bottom.
3. Select **"ACH Payments"** (if available) or go to **"Security Preferences"**.
4. Look for **"ACH Debit Block"** or **"ACH Debit Filter"**.
5. Follow the prompts to:
   - **Block all ACH debits** (best for max security).
   - **Whitelist specific vendors** (only approved businesses can withdraw).
6. Save your settings.

> **Note:** If this setting isn't available in the app, you'll need to call **Chase Business Customer Support** at **1-877-226-0071** and request them to enable an ACH Debit Block/Filter.

---

### **2. Enable Positive Pay for Checks**
(Prevents fraudulent checks from clearing)
1. Open the **Chase Business app**.
2. Go to **"Security Preferences"** or **"Fraud Protection"** (under account settings).
3. Look for **"Check Protection (Positive Pay)"**.
4. Enable it and **upload a list of authorized checks** (if required).
5. Any check not on this list will be flagged for review before processing.

> **Note:** You may need to activate **Positive Pay** via Chase Business Online first, then manage it in the app.

---

### **3. Set Up Fraud Alerts & Transaction Notifications**
(Instantly alerts you about suspicious activity)
1. Open the **Chase Mobile® app**.
2. Tap **"Profile & Settings"**.
3. Select **"Alerts & Notifications"**.
4. Choose **"Security Alerts"** and enable:
   - **ACH withdrawal alerts** (instant notifications for ACH debits).
   - **Check clearing alerts** (notifies you when a check is processed).
   - **Login alerts** (alerts for logins from new devices).
   - **High-value transaction alerts** (flags large transactions).
5. Set your preferred notification method (**text, email, or app push**).

---

### **4. Restrict Online Payments & Wire Transfers**
1. Go to **"Security & Privacy"** in your Chase app.
2. Enable **"Approval Required for Large Transactions"**.
3. Disable **"Wire Transfers"** if you don't use them frequently.
4. Set up a **two-step approval process** for online transactions.
