# Credit Profile Boost Strategies

---

## 👤 You

best ways and services to get credit backdated to increase age, and to increase available credit on profile no hard inquiry

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

To increase the age of your credit profile and boost your available credit without incurring a hard inquiry, consider the following strategies and services:

### 1. **Authorized User (AU) Tradelines**
   - **Overview:** Becoming an authorized user on an established credit card account allows you to inherit the account’s credit history, effectively backdating your credit profile and increasing your available credit.
   - **Best Services:**
     - **Tradeline Supply Company**: Offers a marketplace for purchasing AU tradelines with specific age and credit limits.
     - **CreditPro**: Specializes in seasoned tradelines, helping to improve your credit profile age.
     - **Boost Credit 101**: Provides a range of tradelines with different ages and limits, with a focus on rapid results.
   - **Tip:** Ensure the tradeline you select has a long history and low credit utilization to maximize the impact.

### 2. **Personal Loans (Soft Inquiry)**
   - **Overview:** Some lenders offer personal loans with soft inquiries, which can be used to consolidate debt and free up credit lines, indirectly increasing your available credit.
   - **Best Services:**
     - **Upgrade**: Offers personal loans with only a soft inquiry during pre-approval, allowing you to see the terms before committing.
     - **Avant**: Similar to Upgrade, Avant provides loans with soft inquiries and reasonable interest rates.
   - **Tip:** Use the loan to pay off high-interest credit cards, which can increase your available credit and improve your credit utilization ratio.

### 3. **Credit Line Increases (Soft Inquiry)**
   - **Overview:** Requesting a credit line increase from your existing creditors can raise your available credit. Some issuers provide increases without a hard inquiry.
   - **Best Practices:**
     - **Amex**: Known for granting credit line increases with soft inquiries. They typically allow requests every 6-12 months.
     - **Discover**: Often increases credit limits without a hard pull, especially if you have a strong payment history.
   - **Tip:** Before requesting, ensure you’ve demonstrated consistent usage and timely payments to increase your chances of approval.

### 4. **Self-Reporting Services**
   - **Overview:** Adding alternative credit data, such as rent, utilities, or streaming services payments, can lengthen your credit history and improve your credit score.
   - **Best Services:**
     - **Experian Boost**: Allows you to add utility and telecom payments to your Experian credit report, potentially increasing your score.
     - **Rent Reporters**: Reports your rent payments to major credit bureaus, which can help increase the age and diversity of your credit profile.
   - **Tip:** These services are most beneficial if you have a limited credit history or are looking to add positive payment history to your report.

### 5. **Secured Credit Cards with Soft Inquiry**
   - **Overview:** Some secured credit cards perform only a soft inquiry during the application process. Over time, these cards can help build your credit profile and increase available credit.
   - **Best Options:**
     - **OpenSky Secured Visa**: No credit check required, making it a solid option to build or rebuild credit.
     - **Applied Bank Secured Visa**: Offers a low-interest rate with no hard inquiry, beneficial for improving credit without affecting your score.
   - **Tip:** Use the card responsibly, keeping your utilization low and making timely payments to maximize the positive impact on your credit profile.

### Key Considerations:
- **Avoid High Utilization**: Keep your credit card utilization below 30% to maintain a strong credit profile.
- **Monitor Your Credit**: Use services like Credit Karma or Experian to keep track of your credit score and any changes resulting from these strategies.
- **Avoid Scams**: Be cautious of services that guarantee dramatic improvements with little effort, as they may be fraudulent.

By strategically using these services and approaches, you can effectively backdate your credit profile, increase your available credit, and improve your overall creditworthiness without hard inquiries.
