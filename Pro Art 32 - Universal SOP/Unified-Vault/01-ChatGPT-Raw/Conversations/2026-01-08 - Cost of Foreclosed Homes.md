## When 30¢ on the Dollar IS Real

**1. Tax Lien / Tax Deed Auctions**
- Distressed municipalities
- Low competition counties
- Often **cash-only**
- High risk (title, redemption periods)

**2. Judicial Foreclosure Auctions (Low Competition)**
- Rust Belt / Midwest / rural markets
- Poor condition properties
- No inspections
- Must handle evictions, liens, rehab
👉 Possible, but not scalable or easy

**3. Bulk / Institutional / Off-Market Distress**
- Buying multiple homes at once
- Banks, hedge funds, or note holders unloading risk
- Requires relationships + capital
👉 Not available to typical retail buyers
