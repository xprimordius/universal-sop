### ⚡ What Are Internet Data Caps & Overage Charges?

### 🔥 How to Braise Beef (Master Technique)

**Braising = Sear + Simmer in Liquid (Low & Slow)**  
This transforms tough cuts into tender, flavorful perfection.

---

### 🧾 Ingredients (Base Template)
- **Beef**: Chuck roast, brisket, short ribs, shank, or oxtail
- **Aromatics**: Onion, garlic, celery, carrots
- **Liquid**: Beef broth, red wine, or a mix
- **Herbs/Spices**: Bay leaf, thyme, rosemary, pepper

---

### 🍳 Step-by-Step (Bulletproof Process)

1. **Preheat** oven to 300°F (150°C)

2. **Sear Beef**
   - Pat dry → season with salt + pepper
   - Sear in hot oil in Dutch oven/pot → **browning = flavor**
   - Remove and set aside

3. **Sauté Aromatics**
   - In same pot: onions, garlic, carrots, celery
   - Scrape browned bits off bottom (fond = gold)

4. **Deglaze**
   - Pour in wine/broth → simmer 2–3 mins to lift flavor

5. **Return Beef & Add Liquid**
   - Add beef back in → liquid should cover ½ to ¾ of meat

6. **Add Herbs & Cover**
   - Toss in bay leaf, thyme, etc.
   - Cover tightly (lid or foil)

7. **Braise in Oven**
   - 2.5 to 3.5 hours @ 300°F
   - Flip meat halfway for even cooking
   - Done when fork-tender

8. **Rest & Serve**
   - Let sit 10–15 mins
   - Skim fat if needed, reduce liquid for sauce if desired

---

### 🥩 Pro Tips
- **Low & Slow Wins**: Don’t rush—tough cuts need time
- **Flavor Boost**: Add tomato paste, balsamic, or miso to broth
- **Storage**: Improves next day—store in sauce for max flavor
