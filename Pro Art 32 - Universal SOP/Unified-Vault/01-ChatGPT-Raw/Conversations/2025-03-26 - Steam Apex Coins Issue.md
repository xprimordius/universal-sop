- **Fix:** Go to EA.com → Account Settings → check your region → match it to Steam’s.

---

### 2. **EA/Steam Account Sync Issue**
- Sometimes EA doesn’t recognize the Steam wallet due to a **link/sync failure**.
- **Fix:**
  - Unlink EA account from Steam (via EA site).
  - Restart Steam and Apex.
  - Relink via prompt in Apex.

---

### 3. **Payment Method Lock**
- If past purchases failed, Steam may **flag the wallet or payment source** temporarily.
- **Fix:** Contact Steam Support → "Purchases" → select recent failed transaction → ask to clear the flag.

---

### 4. **Corrupted Cache/Data**
- Local files or cache may interfere.
- **Fix:**
  - Log out of Steam and EA.
  - Clear Steam’s download cache (Steam → Settings → Downloads → Clear Download Cache).
  - Reboot system.

---

### 5. **EA Server Error**
- Sometimes EA servers reject transactions despite Steam success.
- **Fix:** Try purchasing Apex Coins **from EA.com or Origin** directly (bypasses Steam).

---

### 6. **Wallet Currency Incompatibility**
- Even if you have funds, Apex may **only accept specific wallet currencies** (e.g. USD vs. EUR).
- **Fix:** Check if Steam wallet currency matches game region/currency.
