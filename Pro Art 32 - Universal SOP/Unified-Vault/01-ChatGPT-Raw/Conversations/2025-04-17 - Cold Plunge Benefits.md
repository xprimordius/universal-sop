## 👤 You

Dye eggs with shaving cream

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**How to Dye Eggs with Shaving Cream (Fun & Safe for Kids):**

**Supplies:**
- Hard-boiled eggs  
- Shaving cream (foam, not gel)  
- Food coloring  
- Tray or plate  
- Toothpick or skewer  
- Gloves (optional)  

**Steps:**
1. **Spread shaving cream** in a tray (about 1-inch thick).  
2. **Drop food coloring** (several drops of multiple colors).  
3. **Swirl gently** with a toothpick for a marbled look.  
4. **Roll eggs** in the cream to fully coat.  
5. **Let sit** 10–30 minutes (longer = deeper color).  
6. **Wipe clean** with paper towel. Don't rinse.

**Note:** Not food-safe to eat after due to shaving cream. For edible eggs, use **whipped cream** instead. Want that version too?
