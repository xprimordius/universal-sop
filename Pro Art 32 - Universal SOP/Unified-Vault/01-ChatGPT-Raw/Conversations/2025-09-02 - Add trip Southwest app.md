Here’s how to add a trip in the **Southwest Airlines app**:

1. **Open the Southwest app** and log in to your Rapid Rewards account.  
2. From the home screen, tap **“Trips”** (bottom menu).  
3. Tap **“Add a trip”** or the **“+” icon**.  
4. Enter your **confirmation number** and **passenger’s name** exactly as it appears on your booking.  
5. Tap **“Retrieve reservation.”**  
6. Your trip will now appear in your **Trips** tab for easy access (boarding pass, flight details, etc.).

👉 Tip: If you booked using your Rapid Rewards account, trips usually auto-populate. Adding is only needed if you booked as a guest or under a different account.
