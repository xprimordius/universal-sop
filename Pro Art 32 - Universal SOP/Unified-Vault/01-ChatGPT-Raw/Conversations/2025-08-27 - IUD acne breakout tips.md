Got it. Breaking out after getting an IUD is actually pretty common, especially in the first few weeks. Here’s the quick breakdown:

### Why It’s Happening
- **Hormonal IUDs (Mirena, Kyleena, Skyla, Liletta):** Release progestin, which can trigger androgen-like effects → oilier skin, clogged pores, acne flare-ups.
- **Copper IUD:** Doesn’t have hormones but can indirectly affect your cycle, stress response, and skin via inflammation and immune shifts.

### Timeline
- Breakouts often peak in the **first 1–3 months** as your body adjusts.
- Many people see improvement by **3–6 months**, but for some, acne can persist.

### What You Can Do
- **Short-Term Relief:** Gentle cleanser, non-comedogenic moisturizer, salicylic acid or benzoyl peroxide spot treatment.
- **Internal Support:** Stay hydrated, prioritize sleep, balance blood sugar, consider zinc + omega-3s (both help with hormonal acne).
- **Track Pattern:** If acne is severe or persists beyond 3–6 months, you may want to discuss with your provider whether to switch IUDs or try skin-supporting meds (like spironolactone or topical retinoids).
