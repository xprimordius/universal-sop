## 👤 You

eSIM do you still have your own phone number

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Yes — you keep your own phone number.**

**How eSIM works (TL;DR):**
- 📱 **eSIM = digital SIM** (no physical card).
- 🔢 **Your phone number stays the same** when you convert or activate eSIM with your carrier.
- 📞 Calls, texts, iMessage, WhatsApp — **unchanged**.
- 🌍 You can add **a second number** (travel/work) without losing your main one (Dual SIM).

**Only time the number changes:**
- If you **buy a new plan** (e.g., travel eSIM like Airalo).
- If you **port to a different carrier** and choose a new number.

**Bottom line:**  
Switching to eSIM **does not change your number** unless you explicitly choose a new one.
