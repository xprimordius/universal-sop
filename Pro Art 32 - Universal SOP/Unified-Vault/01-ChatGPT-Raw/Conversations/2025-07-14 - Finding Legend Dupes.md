Both scents share **structural DNA** (lavender, bergamot, geranium, woody base)—but subtle shifts in **note ratios, quality, and dry-down behavior** change the *emotional impact*.

---

### 🔬 1. **Note Composition vs. Balance**

| Note             | Express Legend           | Montblanc Legend            |
|------------------|--------------------------|-----------------------------|
| **Lavender**     | Softer, smoother         | Sharper, more dominant      |
| **Citrus (Top)** | Mandarin-forward, juicy  | Bergamot-heavy, synthetic   |
| **Florals**      | Violet leaf = airy depth | Apple = sweet/clean crisp   |
| **Herbs**        | Sage adds richness       | Lacks herbaceous roundness  |
| **Base**         | Musk + sandalwood/cedar  | Tonka + oakmoss = powdery   |

🧠 **Translation:** Express Legend feels *warmer, rounder, cleaner, more sensual*. Montblanc Legend has *sharper edges, fruitier top, and powdery base*—less cozy, less intimate.

---

### 🎯 2. **Mood & Emotional Vibe**

| Element            | Express Legend                  | Montblanc Legend             |
|--------------------|----------------------------------|------------------------------|
| **Vibe**           | Calm confidence, sensual energy | Fresh, clean, slightly aloof |

| **Dry-down**       | Warm, skin-like woody musk      | Cooler, sweet-tonka finish   |

---

### 🔥 3. **Why Montblanc Legend Misses for You**

- **Less depth and warmth** in dry-down.
- **More synthetic top notes** vs Express’s smoother mandarin/lavender combo.

### 🧪 If You Want That Express Legend Feel…
