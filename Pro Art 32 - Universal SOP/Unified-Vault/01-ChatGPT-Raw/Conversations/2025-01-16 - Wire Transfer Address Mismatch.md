## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes, a wire transfer can typically go through even if the internal address on the bank account differs from what is listed on the wire transfer request. Wire transfers primarily rely on the following key information:

1. **Account Number**: Ensures the funds are routed to the correct account.
2. **Routing Number (or equivalent SWIFT/BIC code)**: Directs the funds to the correct financial institution.
3. **Recipient Name**: Verifies the intended beneficiary.

### However:
- **Discrepancy Risks**: Some banks may flag or delay the transaction if the mismatch raises suspicion, especially for international transfers or large amounts.
- **Verification Process**: For security, banks may contact you to confirm details if there are inconsistencies.

### Recommendation:
To avoid delays:
- Confirm the address mismatch won't affect processing by contacting your bank.
- Ensure all other account details (e.g., account and routing numbers) are accurate.
