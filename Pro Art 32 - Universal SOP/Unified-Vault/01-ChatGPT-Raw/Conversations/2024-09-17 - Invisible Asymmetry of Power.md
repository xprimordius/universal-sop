# Invisible Asymmetry of Power

---

## 👤 You

What is invisible asymmetry of power

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Invisible asymmetry of power refers to a situation where one party holds significantly more influence, control, or advantage over another, often in a way that is not immediately apparent or acknowledged. This imbalance can exist in various contexts such as negotiations, relationships, organizations, or markets, where one side possesses hidden leverage (e.g., access to information, resources, or authority) that the other side may not be aware of or fully understand.

In business, this often occurs when one party has more knowledge, capital, or strategic positioning, allowing them to manipulate outcomes to their advantage without the other party realizing the full extent of their disadvantage. Recognizing and navigating these power dynamics is critical to achieving better outcomes, especially in negotiations or competitive environments.
