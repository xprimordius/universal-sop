If you've tripped a breaker due to excessive electrical draw, follow these steps to restore power safely:

### **1. Check the Breaker Panel**
- Locate your electrical panel (breaker box).
- Identify the **tripped breaker**—it will usually be in a **middle position** (not fully on or off).

### **2. Reset the Tripped Breaker**
- **Turn it fully OFF** first.
- Then **flip it back ON** firmly.

### **3. If the Main Breaker Was Tripped**
- If **multiple areas lost power**, your **main breaker** may have tripped.
- **Turn it off and then back on** to reset.

### **4. Reduce Electrical Load**
- Unplug high-power devices (space heaters, microwaves, etc.) before restoring power to prevent retripping.

### **5. If the Breaker Keeps Tripping**
- There may be **a short circuit, overload, or faulty wiring**.
- If it **trips immediately** after resetting, **do not force it on**—this could indicate a serious issue.

If power is not restored or keeps tripping, you may need to call an electrician to inspect for wiring issues.
