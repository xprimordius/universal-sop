# DDR4 RAM Speed Impact

---

## 👤 You

is there any point in higher mhz speed for low cl ddr4 ram for apex legends gaming?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

For **Apex Legends** or most competitive FPS games, the **benefits of higher MHz RAM speed** combined with **low CL (CAS latency)** are **minimal but present**.

Here’s a breakdown of how it affects your gaming performance:

1. **FPS Boosts**: Higher MHz speed and low CL RAM can provide small **gains in FPS**. The difference, however, is typically marginal (a few FPS) and more noticeable at lower resolutions like 1080p, where the CPU and memory play a bigger role. At higher resolutions (1440p or 4K), the GPU becomes the bottleneck, and the RAM speed makes less of a difference.

2. **Load Times and Responsiveness**: You may see slightly faster load times and smoother texture loading, which can be helpful in open-world environments or maps with a lot of textures, but it won't drastically change gameplay.

3. **CPU-bound Scenarios**: If your system is **CPU-bound** (which can happen in games like Apex Legends), faster RAM can help to some extent. If you have a **high refresh rate monitor (120Hz or above)**, this can make your gameplay slightly more responsive.

4. **Diminishing Returns**: There's a point of diminishing returns with RAM speeds, especially in DDR4. For example, **3200 MHz with CL16** is generally the sweet spot for price-to-performance. Going higher than this (e.g., 3600 MHz or more) with a very low CL (like CL14 or CL15) will give you performance boosts, but they won’t justify the price for most users.

### Conclusion:
While faster RAM with low latency can provide **small FPS improvements** and potentially smoother gameplay in **CPU-bound scenarios**, it’s not a huge game-changer for **Apex Legends** specifically. Prioritize other upgrades like a better GPU or CPU if you’re looking for more significant improvements. **3200 MHz CL16** or **3600 MHz CL16** are solid, balanced choices for gaming setups.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes, **latency** can have a **noticeable impact**, but the key is understanding how **significant** that impact is in real-world gaming performance, particularly in **Apex Legends**.

### Latency (CL14 vs CL16):
- **CAS Latency (CL)** is the delay between when a command is sent to the RAM and when the data starts being processed.
- **Lower latency** (like CL14 vs CL16) means the memory responds slightly faster. The combination of **high frequency (MHz)** and **low latency (CL)** offers the best performance.

### The Impact of Latency in Gaming:
1. **In CPU-Bound Games**: Lower latency RAM can improve performance in **CPU-bound scenarios**, which occur when the CPU is the bottleneck rather than the GPU. **Apex Legends**, particularly at **1080p** with a **high refresh rate**, can be **CPU-bound**, and in such cases, lower latency (CL14 vs CL16) **can help** improve **minimum FPS** and overall smoothness.
  
2. **Real-World Difference (CL14 vs CL16)**:
   - While **CL14** is faster than **CL16**, the real-world performance difference, especially in gaming, is typically **small**. For instance, you might see a **2-5% increase** in performance (a few FPS), depending on your setup and resolution.
   - The biggest benefit will come if you are gaming at **lower resolutions** (like 1080p) with a **high refresh rate** where both the CPU and RAM have a more substantial impact.
  
3. **Price-to-Performance Consideration**: 
   - **CL14 RAM** is usually more expensive than **CL16**, and for most users, the **small performance gain** doesn't justify the significant cost increase, especially in **gaming scenarios**.
   - If you're really pushing for the **best possible responsiveness** and you want every **frame advantage**, then CL14 is better, but for the vast majority of gamers, **CL16** is already excellent.

### Conclusion:
While the **lower latency (CL14)** is **better** and will provide **slightly faster** response times and **smoother performance**, the difference between **CL14 and CL16** for **Apex Legends** or most other games will be **minimal**. It might provide **small FPS boosts** and improvements in **responsiveness**, but these gains are generally not **game-changing**.

If you're prioritizing cost-efficiency, **3200 MHz CL16** RAM will still give you very good performance. **3200 MHz CL14** is better but comes with diminishing returns for the added cost.
