The **QSC K12** speaker's **1000 watts** rating refers to its **peak power** capability, not RMS. Here's the clarification:

1. **1000 Watts Peak Power:**
   - This is the maximum output the amplifier can produce during brief, dynamic bursts.

2. **RMS Power:**
   - The continuous (RMS) power is generally around **500 watts RMS** (400 watts for the low-frequency driver and 100 watts for the high-frequency driver). This is the sustained power it can handle during regular operation.

Manufacturers often advertise the peak power rating for marketing purposes, but the RMS rating reflects the speaker's true, continuous performance capabilities. If you’ve seen references to "1000 watts RMS," it could be a misunderstanding or marketing exaggeration. Always confirm with the official QSC specifications for accuracy.
