No “I want to start someday” people.

**2. Frame outcome as ROI, not spending**
> funding → assets → cashflow → scale

Never:
> funding → lifestyle

**3. Qualify with deployment language**
Ask prospects:
- What would you deploy this capital into?
- What’s your expected return?
- What’s your current ROI skillset?

If they can’t answer → wrong buyer.

**4. Market to identity**
Speak to:
> builders, investors, operators

Not:
> people who want money

You’re not selling money.
You’re selling **amplification of existing competence**.

That naturally repels consumer mindset and attracts capital discipline.
