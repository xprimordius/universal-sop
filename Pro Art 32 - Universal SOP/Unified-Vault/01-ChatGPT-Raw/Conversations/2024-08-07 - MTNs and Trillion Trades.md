> ## What is the trade balance in the US?

> 
> Several factors affect the US trade balance, such as the health of the global economy, the value of the dollar compared to the currencies of its trade partners, and the demand for goods and services at home and abroad.
>

>

Alright, let’s break it down simply:

### Making a Promise
Imagine you promise to buy a toy from your friend in 3 months. This promise is like a Medium-Term Note (MTN). You agree now, but the actual buying happens later.

### How It Affects You (the Buyer)
1. **Commitment**: Once you make the promise, you are committed to buying the toy in 3 months. You can’t change your mind easily.
2. **Price Lock**: You agree on the price today, so even if the toy becomes more expensive or cheaper in 3 months, you still pay the price you agreed on.
3. **Planning**: You need to make sure you have the money saved up to buy the toy when the time comes. This helps you plan your spending.

### How It Affects Your Friend (the Seller)
1. **Security**: Your friend knows they will sell the toy in 3 months. This gives them confidence that they will get the money.
2. **Price Certainty**: Your friend also knows the exact amount of money they will get, even if the price of the toy changes.
3. **Waiting**: Your friend has to wait for 3 months to get the money. They can’t sell the toy to someone else in the meantime.

### Real-Life Example
In the real world, companies use MTNs to borrow money. Instead of toys, they deal with money. They promise to pay back the money they borrow after a certain time, like 3 years. Investors buy these MTNs to earn interest, just like your friend waits to get paid for the toy.

### Summary
- **Buyer (You)**: Promise to buy, lock in the price, need to save money.
- **Seller (Friend)**: Secure sale, know the price, wait for payment.

So, an MTN helps both sides plan and feel secure about their deal.
