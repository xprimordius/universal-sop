# CPU Overheating AIO Cooler

---

## 👤 You

Why is my cousins PC cpu hitting 105C when he just applied new thermal paste to his AIO cpu cooler

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

If your cousin's CPU is reaching 105°C despite applying new thermal paste and using an AIO (all-in-one) CPU cooler, several issues could be causing this. Here are the main things to check:

1. **Improper Cooler Mounting**: Ensure that the AIO cooler's pump is mounted correctly and firmly on the CPU. If there’s poor contact between the cooler and the CPU, it won’t transfer heat efficiently, leading to high temperatures.

2. **Air in the AIO System**: If there’s air trapped in the AIO system, it can hinder the coolant from circulating properly. This can result in poor cooling performance. Try tilting the PC to see if you can move air pockets out of the pump area, or check if the radiator is mounted with the tubes at the bottom (which helps prevent air pockets in the pump).

3. **Pump Failure**: Ensure the pump is working. You can check this through the BIOS or with software monitoring tools. If the pump is not circulating coolant properly, temperatures will spike.

4. **Fan Configuration**: Check if the fans on the radiator are correctly oriented and functioning. Fans should either pull cool air in or exhaust hot air out effectively. Poor airflow around the radiator can cause overheating.

5. **BIOS Settings**: Review the BIOS settings to ensure the fan and pump curves are set correctly. A fan or pump running at too low of an RPM under load could be the culprit.

6. **Thermal Paste Application**: Double-check the application of the thermal paste. Too much or too little thermal paste can lead to inadequate heat transfer. Make sure there’s a thin, even layer covering the CPU surface.

7. **Radiator Placement**: Ensure that the radiator is positioned in a way that allows good airflow. Common placements are at the front or top of the case, and it’s essential that the airflow around the radiator is not obstructed.

If after checking these steps the issue persists, the AIO cooler itself could be defective, and replacing it or trying an air cooler might be necessary to troubleshoot further.
