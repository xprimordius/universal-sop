## 🤖 ChatGPT

The **Affordable Care Act (ACA)** — also known as **Obamacare** — is a U.S. federal law passed in **2010** designed to make health insurance more affordable and accessible.  

### Core Goals
1. **Expand Coverage:** Increase the number of Americans with health insurance.
2. **Reduce Costs:** Make health care and insurance premiums more affordable.
3. **Improve Quality:** Ensure better care and patient protections.

### Key Features
- **Health Insurance Marketplace:** Lets people compare and buy private health plans, often with government subsidies based on income.
- **Medicaid Expansion:** Extended eligibility for low-income individuals in participating states.
- **Individual Mandate (repealed in 2019):** Originally required most Americans to have health insurance or pay a penalty.
- **Pre-Existing Conditions Protection:** Insurers can’t deny coverage or raise prices based on health history.
- **Essential Health Benefits:** All marketplace plans must cover services like preventive care, maternity, mental health, and prescriptions.
- **Children on Parents’ Plans:** Allows dependents to stay on their parents’ insurance until age 26.
