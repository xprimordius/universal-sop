## 📤 Truist External Transfer Fees  
- **Standard outbound transfers**: $3 each  
- **Next‑day outbound transfers**: $6 each  
- **Inbound transfers**: free citeturn0search3  

Chase allows **free ACH transfers both ways**, with no fees or delays.

---

## 🛠 How to Move Money Between Banks  

### 1. **Zelle — Fast & Free**  
- Send instantly within the U.S.  
- No Truist fees (not included in external transfers) citeturn0search31turn0search3  
- Requires recipient also enrolled on Zelle.

### 2. **ACH External Transfer**  
- Link accounts via routing/account number in Truist app → 3-day standard or next-day option.  
- Fee: $3 or $6 outbound; inbound is free.

### 3. **Wire Transfer**  
- Domestic wire: $20 (outgoing), $15 (incoming)  
- International: $65 (outgoing), $20 (incoming) citeturn0search3  
- Use only for large/urgent transfers — expensive.

### 4. **Bill Pay “push”**  
- Free.  
- Works like ACH but for vendors/other accounts.  
- Plan ahead — delivery may take a few days.

## 🎯 Recommended Strategy  

- **For personal accounts** → use **Zelle**: instant, free, simple.  
- **For your own external bank money** → use **ACH standard** unless fast, then next‑day.  
- **Avoid wires** unless absolutely necessary.

## ✅ Take Action
