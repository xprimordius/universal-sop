## 🤖 ChatGPT

For a flight, you want to keep it simple and travel-friendly. Trim the stems, wrap the flowers in damp paper towels to keep them hydrated, and then loosely cover them with plastic or a floral sleeve. Secure the bundle in a sturdy box or a hard-sided carry-on bag. Make sure you check airline guidelines—most allow flowers in the cabin, and it’s best to keep them with you to avoid damage. Once landed, refresh the stems in water.
