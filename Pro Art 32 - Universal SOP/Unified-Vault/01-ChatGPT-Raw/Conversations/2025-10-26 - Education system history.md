## 🤖 ChatGPT

Formal education systems date back to ancient civilizations. In Mesopotamia and Egypt, schools existed around 2000 BCE. The ancient Greeks formalized education around 400 BCE, focusing on philosophy, math, and physical training. In China, the imperial examination system was established during the Han Dynasty (around 200 BCE) to select government officials. The modern education system, including public schooling, began to take shape in the 17th to 19th centuries, particularly with the rise of industrial societies and the need for educated citizens.
