### Key Principles:
1. **Skills and Belief:**
   - **Skills:** Acquire necessary business skills (e.g., general business knowledge, marketing, sales, team management).
   - **Belief:** Cultivate a mindset that believes in the ability to achieve $100k/month.

2. **Practical Skill Acquisition:**
   - Gain skills through practical experience, not just passive learning.
   - Seek opportunities that allow you to get paid while learning and practicing new skills.

3. **Choosing a Business Model:**
   - Focus on service-based businesses, specifically B2B (Business to Business).
   - Avoid models with high upfront costs, tech barriers, or those that don't facilitate skill acquisition (e.g., crypto, e-commerce).

4. **Target Market:**
   - Sell services to businesses, not consumers.
   - Choose businesses with significant financial resources to ensure they can afford your services.

5. **Business Strategy:**
   - Fill the gap between internal teams and external providers (agencies/consultants).
   - Offer services that help businesses grow, focusing on generating demand and traffic.
   - Long-term contracts are preferred over short-term ones.
   - Include performance-based fees to align incentives and ensure you are motivated to deliver results.

6. **Client Acquisition:**
   - Use LinkedIn to connect with potential clients.
   - Reach out to multiple contacts within your niche.
   - Focus on securing one significant client to start with.

7. **Sales and Closing Deals:**
   - Emphasize long-term contracts and performance fees.
   - Offer a "work for free" guarantee instead of a money-back guarantee.
   - Present a detailed growth plan to potential clients to demonstrate the logical steps you will take to help them grow.

By following this structured approach, you can build a business that not only reaches $100k per month but also has the potential to scale beyond that level.
