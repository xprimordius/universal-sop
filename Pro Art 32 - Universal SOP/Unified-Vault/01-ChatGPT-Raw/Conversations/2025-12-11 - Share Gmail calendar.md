## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here’s how to share your **entire Google Calendar** (or all calendars) with someone else:  

---

### 🔹 Option 1: Share One Calendar (repeat for each)
1. Go to **[Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com)** on a computer.  
2. On the left, hover over the calendar you want to share → click **⋮ (three dots)** → **Settings and sharing**.  
3. Under **“Share with specific people or groups”**, click **Add people**.  
4. Enter their email address.  
5. Choose the permission level:  
   - **See only free/busy (hide details)**  
   - **See all event details**  
   - **Make changes to events**  
   - **Make changes and manage sharing**  
6. Click **Send**.  
   They’ll receive an email invitation.

---

### 🔹 Option 2: Share All Calendars At Once
Google doesn’t have a single “share all calendars” button, but you can:  
1. Repeat the above for each calendar under **“My calendars.”**  
2. Or, create a **new shared calendar** and copy all your events into it:
   - Create → “+” next to “Other calendars” → “Create new calendar.”  
   - In each calendar, select all events → **Duplicate to** → the shared calendar.

---

### 🔹 Option 3: Give Full Account Access (Advanced)
If you trust the person completely (e.g. assistant or partner):
1. Go to **[Google Account Delegation](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#settings/accounts)** → under **“Grant access to your account”** → add their email.  
2. This gives them full Gmail and Calendar access.

Got it — you want to **share your Google (Gmail) calendar using the Apple Calendar app** on iPhone or Mac.  
Here’s how, step by step:

---

### ✅ **Step 1: Make sure your Google Calendar is synced**
**On iPhone/iPad:**
1. Open **Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Google.**
2. Sign in with your Gmail and turn **“Calendars” ON.**
3. Open the **Apple Calendar** app — your Google calendars will appear automatically.

**On Mac:**
1. Open **System Settings → Internet Accounts → Google.**
2. Sign in → enable **Calendars.**

---

### ✅ **Step 2: Share your calendar**
⚠️ **Important:** Apple Calendar can *show* Google calendars, but **sharing permissions must be changed in Google Calendar**, not from the Apple Calendar app.

So:
1. Go to [**Google Calendar**](https://calendar.google.com) → gear icon ⚙️ → **Settings.**
2. Under “My calendars,” select the one you want to share.
3. Choose **“Share with specific people.”**
4. Add the person’s Gmail → set permission (View / Edit / Manage).
5. Once shared, that calendar will also show up in their **Apple Calendar** (if they’ve synced their Google account).

---

### ✅ **Step 3 (Optional): Share via iCloud instead**
If you prefer to manage sharing **directly in Apple Calendar**:
1. Create or move your events into an **iCloud calendar**.
2. In Apple Calendar → click the **calendar name → Share Calendar → Add Person.**
3. Add their Apple ID → choose View or Edit access.
