# Affidavit Notarization Guidelines

---

## 👤 You

is it okay if i'm getting an affidavit notorized for legal purposes; but 6 pages are pictures taken of a form that i filled out with pen, and 2 pages are done digitally

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes, it's generally acceptable to have a mix of handwritten and digitally completed pages in an affidavit, as long as all the content is clear, legible, and relevant to the affidavit. When having the document notarized, the notary public will likely focus on the following:

1. **Content Consistency:** Ensure that the handwritten and digital portions are consistent with each other and that the information flows logically.

2. **Legibility:** Both the handwritten and digital sections should be easy to read.

3. **Signature:** The notary will need to witness your signature, which should be present on the affidavit itself or on an accompanying declaration that confirms the authenticity of the document, including the handwritten portions.

4. **Document Integrity:** Ensure that all pages are securely bound together, so they can't be tampered with or altered after notarization.

Before proceeding, it might be a good idea to check with the notary or a legal professional to confirm that this format will be acceptable for your specific legal needs.
