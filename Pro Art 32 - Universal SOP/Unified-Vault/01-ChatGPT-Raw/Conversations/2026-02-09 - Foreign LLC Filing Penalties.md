**Short answer:** Ignoring it doesn’t make it go away. California keeps billing and can suspend the LLC **until you formally exit**.

### What happens if you don’t file (Foreign LLC – CA)
- **$250 penalty** for failing to file the Statement of Information (Form LLC-12).
- **Franchise Tax Board (FTB) keeps the account open** → you can still be assessed:
  - **$800 minimum franchise tax** for later years if CA thinks you’re still registered.
- **Suspension / forfeiture** by the CA Secretary of State.
- While suspended:
  - You **can’t sue or defend** in CA courts.
  - You **can’t get good standing or close cleanly** later without paying back fees.
- Penalties and notices **continue to accrue**, even if you’re not operating.

### Key misconception (important)
> “I don’t do business there anymore” **≠** “CA knows I’m done.”

California only stops obligations when you **formally withdraw** the foreign LLC.

- Risk of **retroactive $800/year bills**.
- More expensive and messy to fix later.
