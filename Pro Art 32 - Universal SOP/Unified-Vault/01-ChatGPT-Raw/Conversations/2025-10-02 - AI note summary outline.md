Here’s the **best AI-powered note summary format** I recommend for audio recordings—optimized for clarity, speed, and action:  

---

## 🔹 **AI Note Summary Framework**

### 1. **Title / Context**  
- Meeting / Call / Recording Name  
- Date & Time  
- Attendees / Speakers  

### 2. **Executive Summary (TL;DR)**  
- 3–5 bullet points capturing the core outcome(s).  
- Keep it under 60 seconds of reading.  

### 3. **Key Topics / Outline**  
Organized chronologically or by theme:  
- **Topic A:** Short summary of discussion + main takeaways  
- **Topic B:** Key insights, decisions, or blockers  
- **Topic C:** Supporting details if needed  

### 4. **Decisions & Agreements**  
- What was finalized or agreed upon  
- Who owns it  

### 5. **Action Items (Most Important Section)**  
Format:  
- **[Owner] → [Task] → [Deadline]**  
Example:  
- John → Draft proposal outline → Oct 5  
- Sarah → Send updated financial model → End of week  

### 6. **Open Questions / Follow-Ups**  
- Items left unresolved  
- Clarifications required  

---

## 🔹 **AI Workflow for Audio Recordings**
1. **Transcribe**: Use a high-quality transcription tool (e.g., OpenAI’s Whisper, Otter, Fireflies).  
2. **Summarize**: Feed transcript into AI with the above outline.  
3. **Action Extraction**: AI highlights verbs + assignments to auto-generate tasks.  
4. **Sync**: Push action items into task managers (Notion, Asana, ClickUp, etc.).  

---

✨ This structure guarantees:  
- **Instant clarity** for anyone who didn’t attend.  
- **Direct accountability** with owner + deadline.  
- **Seamless handoff** into project management systems.
