## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Yes — Convection Bake is better for frozen meatballs.**

**Why**
- Fan = even heat → no cold centers  
- Faster cooking  
- Better browning without flipping as much

**How to set it (LG)**
- Mode: **Convection Bake**  
- Temp: **375°F** (or 25°F lower than package bake temp)  
- Rack: **Middle**  
- Pan: Sheet pan, spaced out  
- Time: ~**15–20 min**, shake/flip once halfway

**Exception**
- If they’re **sauced** → use **Regular Bake** (prevents splatter + drying)
