Prolonged or “strange” thunder usually points to **atmospheric and acoustic conditions** rather than anything supernatural. Here are the main reasons:  

### Causes of Long, Rolling Thunder
1. **Multiple Lightning Strikes** – If bolts fire in quick succession, the sound waves overlap, stretching out the rumble.  
2. **Distance & Terrain** – Thunder from far away bounces and refracts through valleys, buildings, and clouds, making it echo.  
3. **Atmospheric Layers** – Temperature inversions and humidity trap sound, causing it to roll and linger much longer.  
4. **Cloud Structure** – Large, tall storm systems (supercells or squall lines) produce bigger resonating chambers, amplifying thunder.  
5. **Low-Frequency Energy** – Some strikes release more bass-heavy sound waves, which travel farther and feel deeper or “strange.”  

### Why It Feels “Different” Lately
- **Climate Shifts** – Warmer, wetter air masses hold more energy, fueling stronger storms with more lightning.  
- **Storm Scale** – Widespread, slow-moving storm complexes naturally give longer, continuous rumbles.  
- **Psychological Factor** – If skies already look ominous, thunder may feel exaggerated and unnatural.  

⚡ **Bottom line:** Strange, prolonged thunder is usually normal physics of sound + storm scale, not necessarily a warning of catastrophe.
