### 1. **Tongkat Ali (Eurycoma longifolia)**

**Mechanism:**  
Tongkat Ali is believed to increase testosterone by reducing sex hormone-binding globulin (SHBG) and increasing luteinizing hormone (LH), which signals the testes to produce more testosterone. It also may reduce cortisol levels, helping maintain a better testosterone-to-cortisol ratio.

**Typical Dosage:**  
- 200-400 mg per day of a standardized extract (e.g., 100:1).

**Expected Results:**  
- **Short-term (1 week):** Minor improvements in mood and energy due to cortisol reduction.
- **Mid-term (4-8 weeks):** Measurable increases in testosterone, ranging from 10% to 20%, depending on the individual.
- **Long-term (12+ weeks):** Consistent improvements in libido, muscle mass, and strength. In some studies, testosterone increases by up to 30%.

**Studies:**  
- A 2012 study showed an increase in testosterone levels in 76% of participants after one month of taking 200 mg of Tongkat Ali daily.  
- In a 2013 study, individuals taking 300 mg/day experienced a 37% increase in free testosterone over 12 weeks.

### 2. **Forskolin (Coleus forskohlii)**

**Mechanism:**  
Forskolin activates adenylate cyclase, which increases cyclic AMP (cAMP) levels in cells. This pathway is linked to increased testosterone production and fat loss.

**Typical Dosage:**  
- 250 mg of Coleus forskohlii (standardized to 10-20% forskolin) twice daily.

**Expected Results:**  
- **Short-term (1-2 weeks):** Minor fat loss, but not significant testosterone changes.
- **Mid-term (4-8 weeks):** Increases in free testosterone, ranging from 16% to 33%.
- **Long-term (12 weeks+):** Noticeable improvements in body composition (fat loss and muscle gain). Testosterone levels can increase up to 35% according to some studies.

**Studies:**  
- A 2005 study demonstrated that men taking 250 mg of a 10% forskolin extract twice daily for 12 weeks saw a 16% increase in testosterone levels compared to the placebo group. There was also a significant reduction in body fat and an increase in lean body mass.
