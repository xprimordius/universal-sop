## ⚙️ Strategy: How to Use B-Roll Like a Pro

1. **Define your narrative first**  
   Map out A-roll: what message, what points. Identify spots where you need reinforcement (e.g. product shots, environment, reaction shots).

2. **Use AI for detection & match**  
   Use tools above that auto-scan your speech or script to suggest visuals. Saves you time.

3. **Balance AI + manual tweak**  
   Even when AI proposes B-roll, review it: ensure style, color, tempo match the rest of video. Replace or adjust clips that feel off.

4. **Mind pacing & cuts**  
   Don’t just throw B-roll in; use it to bridge between ideas, cover jump cuts, smooth transitions, or maintain viewer interest in long talking segments.

5. **Consistency of visual style**  
   Use similar color grading, shot composition, lighting style so B-roll doesn’t feel jarring. AI helps, but human touch wins here.

6. **Sound + motion**  
   Add ambient sound / effects that correspond to the B-roll. Let the movement (camera pans, zooms) feel natural or intentional.
