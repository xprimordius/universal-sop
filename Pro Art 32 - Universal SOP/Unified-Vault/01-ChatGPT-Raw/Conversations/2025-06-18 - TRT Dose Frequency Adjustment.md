### ✅ Frequency Options  
You can increase **frequency** (not dose) to improve stability, reduce estrogen spikes, and minimize sides:

| Frequency        | Weekly Dose (same) | Typical Use                             |
|------------------|--------------------|------------------------------------------|
| 2x/week (Mon/Thurs) | 200mg (0.5mL x2)  | Baseline high-frequency TRT              |

| Daily microdose     | ~28.5mg/day       | Ultra-stable; used to reduce estrogen/DHT |

**You can split 200mg into:**
- 3x/week → **0.33mL per shot**
- Daily → **0.071mL per shot**

---

### 🧠 Effects of Increased Frequency
**Pros:**
- More stable T levels
- Lower estrogen conversion (less mood swings, bloating, gyno risk)
- Fewer spikes → less RBC/thick blood risk
- Improved libido/mood/stamina for some

**Cons:**
- More injections
- Daily may feel like a chore
- No extra T benefit unless dose increases (not just frequency)

---

### 🧬 If Dose Continues to Rise (200mg+)
- Estrogen may rise (need AI?)
- Hematocrit may climb (donate blood/check labs)
- Risk of **testicular atrophy**, **fertility loss**, **acne**, **irritability**, etc.

---

### 🔬 Optimal Approach
- Stay at 200mg/week max unless bloodwork shows otherwise
- Increase **frequency**, not dose, if mood/symptoms fluctuate
- Recheck labs: **TT, FT, E2, SHBG, HCT** every 8–12 weeks after change
