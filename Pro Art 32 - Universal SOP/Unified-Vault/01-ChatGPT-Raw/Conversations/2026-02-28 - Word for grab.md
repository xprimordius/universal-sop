## 🤖 ChatGPT

**LDL Chol Calc = Calculated LDL cholesterol.**

It means your **LDL (“bad”) cholesterol** level was **estimated using a formula**, not directly measured.

### What LDL Is
- **LDL = Low-Density Lipoprotein**
- Carries cholesterol to tissues
- High levels are associated with increased risk of atherosclerosis and heart disease

### Why “Calculated”?
Most labs use the **Friedewald equation**:
> LDL = Total Cholesterol − HDL − (Triglycerides ÷ 5)

It’s accurate **unless triglycerides are high (≥400 mg/dL)** — then a direct LDL test is needed.

### What “High” Means (U.S. guidelines)
- **<100 mg/dL** = Optimal  
- **100–129** = Near optimal  
- **130–159** = Borderline high  
- **160–189** = High  
- **≥190** = Very high
