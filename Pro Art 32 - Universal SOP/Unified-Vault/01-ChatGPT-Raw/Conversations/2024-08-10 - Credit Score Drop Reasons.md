# Credit Score Drop Reasons

---

## 👤 You

My credit score went down from 735 to 705 for paying my 25% utilization card down to 5% why?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

A drop in your credit score after paying down a credit card balance could occur due to several factors, even if the utilization rate improved. Here are some possible reasons:

1. **Recent Hard Inquiries:** If you recently applied for new credit (e.g., a loan, mortgage, or credit card), the hard inquiry could temporarily lower your score.

2. **Changes in Credit Mix:** If there were changes to your overall credit profile, such as closing an account or changes in the types of credit you're using, this could impact your score. 

3. **Age of Credit Accounts:** The average age of your credit accounts could decrease if you opened a new account, which could lead to a score drop.

4. **Credit Reporting Timing:** Credit scores can fluctuate based on the timing of when creditors report to the credit bureaus. If another card reported a higher balance around the same time, it could offset the positive impact of reducing your utilization on the other card.

5. **Negative Information:** If any negative information (e.g., late payments, collections) was added to your credit report, it could have a significant impact on your score, even if you improved utilization.

6. **Credit Limits Reduction:** If your credit limit was reduced on one of your accounts, your overall utilization rate may have increased, even though you paid down a balance.

7. **Score Rebalancing:** Credit scoring models periodically update and recalibrate, sometimes causing unexpected shifts in scores based on new data patterns.

To pinpoint the exact cause, you should review your latest credit reports for any changes or new entries that might have contributed to the score drop.
