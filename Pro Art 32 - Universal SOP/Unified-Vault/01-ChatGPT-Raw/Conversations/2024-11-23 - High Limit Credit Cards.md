### **Immediate Actions for Optimization**
1. **Reduce Low-Tier Cards:** Close subprime cards (e.g., Open Sky Bank, Mission Lane, Merrick, etc.) gradually to avoid a sudden drop in average account age.  
2. **Clean Inquiries:** Focus on removing hard inquiries, especially from TransUnion and Equifax, to bring the total below three per bureau. Use dispute letters or third-party services like *InquiryBusters*.  
3. **High-Limit Primary Accounts:** Leverage your excellent score to add high-limit accounts that dilute the impact of lower-limit cards.  
4. **Utilization Management:** Maintain utilization under 5%. Pay off balances on small-limit cards before statement dates to avoid reporting unnecessary balances.  
5. **Authorized User Accounts:** Retain AUs that strengthen your profile. Consider adding an AU account with over $30k limits and 10+ years of history.

### **Business Funding Readiness**
To qualify for significant business funding, creditors look for strong personal credit profiles that demonstrate financial stability.
