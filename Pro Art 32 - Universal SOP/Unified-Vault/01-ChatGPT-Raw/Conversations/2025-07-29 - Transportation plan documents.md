Which public documents can help you understand a city’s transportation plans? (Select All That Apply)

A
Capital Improvement Plans (CIP).

B
Long-Range Transportation Plans (LRTP).

C
Commercial property appraisal reports.

D
Regional Mobility Plans.

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Correct answers:**

✅ **A. Capital Improvement Plans (CIP)**  
✅ **B. Long-Range Transportation Plans (LRTP)**  
✅ **D. Regional Mobility Plans**

---

### Explanation:

- **Capital Improvement Plans (CIP):**  
  Detail planned infrastructure investments, including roads, bridges, transit, and related transportation projects.

- **Long-Range Transportation Plans (LRTP):**  
  Outline a region’s transportation priorities over 20+ years, including future investments, goals, and policies.

- **Regional Mobility Plans:**  
  Focus on improving regional transportation systems, often integrating multiple modes (transit, bike, road) across jurisdictions.

- ❌ **Commercial property appraisal reports:**  
  These are used for real estate valuation and do **not** provide insight into transportation planning.
