## 💡 Best Practice Tips

- **Transcription-first approach**: Let AI transcribe first. Then editing by text is easier (delete or select lines) → cuts video/audio accordingly. Tools like Vizard, Wisecut do this.
- **Clip in layers**: Always keep original in one track. Create a “rough cut” layer, then a “fine adjust” layer.
- **Lock boundaries**: For tricky segments (e.g. punchlines, musical cues), lock start/end and prevent AI from crossing over.
- **Use markers / cues**: While recording, drop markers (claps, verbal cue) to ease AI detection.
- **Train custom models / prompts**: Wherever possible, feed the system examples of your ideal cuts, so it learns your style.
- **Manual review always**: No AI is perfect yet—always scan the final for miscuts.
