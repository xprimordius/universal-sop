## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Chipotle Vinaigrette (like Chipotle’s dressing)**  
Quick, bold, smoky, and tangy.

### **Ingredients**  
- 1–2 **chipotle peppers in adobo** (adjust to heat preference)  
- 2 tsp **adobo sauce** (from the can)  
- 2 tbsp **red wine vinegar**  
- 2 tbsp **honey** (or agave)  
- 1 tsp **dijon mustard**  
- 1/2 tsp **oregano** (preferably Mexican)  
- 1/2 tsp **salt**  
- 1/4 tsp **black pepper**  
- 1/2 tsp **garlic powder** or 1 small clove  
- 3/4 cup **vegetable oil** (or olive oil for more richness)  
- Optional: a **squeeze of lime** for freshness

### **Instructions**  
1. Add everything **except the oil** to a blender or food processor.  
2. Blend until smooth.  
3. Slowly stream in the oil while blending until emulsified.  
4. Adjust salt, acid, or sweetness to taste.

### **Storage**  
- Store in a sealed jar for up to **1 week** in the fridge. Shake before use. 

Want a thicker version for bowls or dipping? Add a spoon of Greek yogurt or mayo.
