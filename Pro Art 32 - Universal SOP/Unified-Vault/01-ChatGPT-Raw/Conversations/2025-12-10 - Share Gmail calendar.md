## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here’s how to share your Google (Gmail) Calendar:  

---

### 🔧 **On Desktop**
1. **Open:** [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).  
2. On the left, under **“My calendars”**, hover over the calendar you want to share.  
3. Click the **⋮ (three dots)** → **Settings and sharing**.  
4. Scroll to **“Share with specific people or groups.”**  
5. Click **+ Add people and groups.**  
6. Enter their Gmail address.  
7. Choose permissions:  
   - **See only free/busy (hide details)**  
   - **See all event details**  
   - **Make changes to events**  
   - **Make changes and manage sharing**  
8. Click **Send** to share.

---

### 📱 **On Mobile (Gmail/Calendar App)**
The mobile app doesn’t allow full sharing setup.  
Instead:
- Go to the **desktop version** (via Safari/Chrome → “Request Desktop Site”), or  
- Use the **Google Calendar website** on your computer to add people.

---

### 🌍 **Public Sharing (optional)**
If you want to share a calendar publicly:
1. In **Settings**, go to **Access permissions for events**.  
2. Check **Make available to public**.  
3. Choose whether to show all details or just free/busy.

## 🖥 On Computer (Best / Easiest)

1. Go to **Google Calendar**:  
   👉 <https://calendar.google.com>

2. On the left under **“My calendars”**, hover over the calendar you want to share  
   (e.g. **Your Name**, **Family**, **Work**, etc.).

3. Click the **⋮ (three dots)** → **`Settings and sharing`**.

4. Scroll to **“Share with specific people or groups”**.

5. Click **`+ Add people and groups`**.

6. Type the person’s **Gmail / Google email**.

7. Choose what they can do with your **entire calendar**:
   - **See only free/busy** – they only see blocks of time.
   - **See all event details** – they see everything.
   - **Make changes to events** – they can edit events.
   - **Make changes and manage sharing** – full control (admin).

8. Click **`Send`** → they’ll get an email invite and can add your **whole calendar**.

---

## 🌍 If You Want Anyone With a Link to See It

1. Same **Settings and sharing** page.  
2. Under **“Access permissions for events”**:
   - Check **“Make available to public”** or **“Make available for [organization]”**.
   - Choose:  
     - **See only free/busy**  
     - or **See all event details**

3. Copy the **Shareable link** there if shown, or grab the **Public URL / iCal link** under **“Integrate calendar”** to send to others.
