## 👤 You

Will fabric shaver remove the lint from my clothes? Not the big clumps but like the normal fuzzy stuff that you can get off with a sticky roller

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes — but with nuance:  

- **Fabric shaver (defuzzer):** Best for pilling and fuzz that sticks tightly to the fabric (the little balls and worn fuzz). It actually shaves the surface and leaves clothes smoother.  
- **Lint roller:** Best for loose lint, hair, dust, and fibers sitting on the surface.  

So if it’s the **fuzzy surface buildup** (not just hair or dust), the fabric shaver will remove it more effectively than a sticky roller. For maximum smoothness, many people use **both**: shaver first for fuzz/pills, roller after for loose lint/hair.
