## 🤖 ChatGPT

Possible reasons your body feels itchy **after going to the beach (even without swimming):**

1. **Salt + Sand Residue** – Wind can carry fine salt and sand that cling to your skin, causing micro-abrasions or irritation. If not fully rinsed off, this can dry the skin and make it itchy the next day.  
2. **UV Exposure** – Even mild sun exposure can dry or slightly burn the skin, leading to itchiness during healing (a mild “sun itch”).  
3. **Wind Burn / Dry Air** – Ocean wind and salt air strip moisture from the skin’s surface, leaving it tight and itchy later.  
4. **Allergic or Irritant Contact** – Beach grass, sunscreen ingredients, or airborne seaweed/marine particles can trigger irritation or mild allergic dermatitis.  
5. **Histamine Response to UV (“Sun Allergy”)** – Some people’s skin releases histamine after UV exposure, even without a visible burn.  
6. **Sweat + Sunscreen Reaction** – Residue from sunscreen mixed with sweat can clog pores and cause delayed itching.

**Quick Relief / Prevention:**
- Shower with mild soap immediately after the beach to remove salt and residue.  
- Moisturize with aloe vera or fragrance-free lotion.  
- Use mineral sunscreen (zinc/titanium) to avoid chemical irritants.  
- Stay hydrated and avoid hot showers right after exposure.

If it persists more than 1–2 days or includes a rash or hives, it could be **photo-contact dermatitis** or **sun-induced histamine reaction**—worth checking with a dermatologist.
