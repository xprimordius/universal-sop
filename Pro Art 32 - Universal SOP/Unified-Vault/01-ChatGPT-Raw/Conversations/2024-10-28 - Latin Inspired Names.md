What are good / best fundable NAICS / SIC codes for an Airbnb arbitrage business? Is marketing ok?

## 🤖 ChatGPT

For an Airbnb arbitrage business, the best fundable NAICS and SIC codes would ideally align with real estate, property management, or leasing rather than marketing. This is because financing entities typically view businesses involved in real estate services and short-term rentals as more stable, with a more straightforward revenue model. Here are some top NAICS and SIC codes that can enhance fundability for an Airbnb arbitrage model:

### NAICS Codes
1. **531110 - Lessors of Residential Buildings and Dwellings**  
   - Primary code for businesses involved in renting residential real estate, including short-term leases, which aligns well with Airbnb arbitrage.
   
2. **721199 - All Other Traveler Accommodation**  
   - Suitable for businesses offering lodging services outside of traditional hotels, including vacation rentals and short-term rentals.
   
3. **531311 - Residential Property Managers**  
   - Ideal if you manage properties on behalf of owners in an arbitrage model, including leasing, upkeep, and marketing of rental units.

4. **531390 - Other Activities Related to Real Estate**  
   - Broad category covering various real estate-related services, helpful if you engage in activities like property setup and rental management.

### SIC Codes
1. **6513 - Operators of Apartment Buildings**  
   - Covers the rental of apartments or similar properties, a straightforward match for the short-term rental or arbitrage model.
   
2. **7011 - Hotels and Motels**  
   - While broader, this SIC code can apply to short-term rentals and vacation stays, making it more familiar for lenders accustomed to hospitality.
   
3. **6531 - Real Estate Agents and Managers**  
   - Relevant if your arbitrage business involves managing and listing properties, which can encompass managing short-term rentals.

### Why Marketing Codes Are Less Optimal
Marketing or advertising-focused NAICS/SIC codes may seem relevant if you’re promoting listings, but they may not be ideal for funding purposes. Financial institutions and investors generally prefer codes tied directly to real estate, leasing, or property management, as these are viewed as assets or services with more consistent, tangible revenue streams compared to marketing businesses.
