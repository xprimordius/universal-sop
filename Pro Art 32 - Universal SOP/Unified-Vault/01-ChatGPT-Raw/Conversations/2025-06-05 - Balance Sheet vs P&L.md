## 🤖 ChatGPT

**TLDR: Here's a quick breakdown of a Business Balance Sheet vs. Profit & Loss (P&L) Statement:**

---

### 📊 **1. Balance Sheet (Snapshot of Financial Health)**  
**Purpose:** Shows what the business owns vs. owes at a **specific point in time**.

| **Section**       | **What It Includes**                                |
|-------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| **Assets**        | Cash, Accounts Receivable, Inventory, Equipment     |
| **Liabilities**   | Loans, Credit Card Debt, Accounts Payable           |
| **Equity**        | Owner’s Capital, Retained Earnings                  |

**Formula:**  
`Assets = Liabilities + Equity`

---

### 📈 **2. Profit & Loss Statement (P&L)**  
**Purpose:** Shows business performance **over a period of time** (e.g., month, quarter, year).

| **Section**        | **What It Includes**                           |
|--------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| **Revenue**         | Total income from sales/services               |
| **COGS**            | Direct costs to produce goods/services         |
| **Gross Profit**    | Revenue - COGS                                 |
| **Operating Expenses** | Rent, Payroll, Marketing, Utilities        |
| **Net Profit (Loss)**| Gross Profit - Operating Expenses             |

**Formula:**  
`Net Profit = Revenue - COGS - Expenses`

---

### 🧠 Use Case Difference:
- **Balance Sheet** = Health check (Can I pay my bills today?)
- **P&L** = Performance check (Am I making money over time?)
