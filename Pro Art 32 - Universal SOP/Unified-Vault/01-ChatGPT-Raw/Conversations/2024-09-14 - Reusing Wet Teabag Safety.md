# Reusing Wet Teabag Safety

---

## 👤 You

Is it safe if I drank a tea cup with a teabag in it, or just a regular glass cup, and then I left that teabag dry afterwards or sitting in the cup last night, it's now 2 p.m. the next day, can I refill this with hot water, or do I have to worry about it being moldy since it was already wet?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

It's generally safe to reuse a teabag within a few hours after brewing, but leaving a wet teabag out overnight can promote bacterial growth, especially if it's been sitting in a warm environment. Even though mold may not be visibly present, it's better to err on the side of caution. I would recommend using a fresh teabag instead of refilling it with hot water after leaving it out since last night.
