### TDLR (Too Long Didn't Read)

**Billion Dollar Copywriting Formula Overview:**

**NUEEPH: New, Unique, Exciting, Easy, Predictable, Huge**

1. **New:** 
   - Your offer must present something never seen before to capture attention and combat skepticism.

2. **Unique:**
   - Avoid categorization; offer something truly unique that stands out.

3. **Exciting:**
   - Make your offer and its presentation exciting to keep the audience engaged.

4. **Easy:**
   - Provide easy, quick-fix solutions to appeal to the audience's desire for minimal effort.

5. **Predictable:**
   - Reduce skepticism by ensuring predictable outcomes, and increase perceived value.

6. **Huge:**
   - Highlight the enormous potential and urgency of the opportunity to create FOMO (Fear of Missing Out).

**3 Reasons Why People Don’t Buy:**

1. They don’t want what you offer.
2. They don’t believe your product/solution will work for them.
3. They don’t trust/feel safe ordering from you.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

### TLDR (Too Long Didn't Read)

**9 Questions Your Sales Message Must Answer:**

1. **Is this of personal interest to me?**
   - Capture the prospect's interest immediately with a relevant headline.

2. **Why should I pay attention to this right now?**
   - Create urgency for the prospect to engage with your message immediately.

3. **Is this exactly the solution that I need?**
   - Clearly explain why your offer is the perfect solution for the prospect's needs.

4. **What is exactly new, unique, exciting, easy, predictable, and huge about this?**
   - Highlight the NUEEPH elements (New, Unique, Exciting, Easy, Predictable, Huge) of your offer.

5. **What undeniable proof do you have?**
   - Provide undeniable proof and evidence that your product/service delivers as promised.

6. **How does this work?**
   - Explain the mechanics and facts of how your offer works.

7. **What are other people saying about it?**
   - Use testimonials and reviews to show social proof and validate your offer.

8. **What guarantee is there that this will work for me?**
   - Offer a strong guarantee to reduce the perceived risk for the prospect.

9. **What do you want me to do next?**
   - Be clear and explicit about the next steps you want the prospect to take.

**Bonus Question: Why should I do it right now?**
   - Provide a compelling reason for the prospect to act immediately, including the consequences of inaction.
