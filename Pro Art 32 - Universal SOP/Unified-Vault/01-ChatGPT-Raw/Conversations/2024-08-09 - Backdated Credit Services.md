When seeking backdated credit services like Kikoff Premium, which are intended to help build or improve your credit score, it's essential to choose reputable options that report to major credit bureaus. Below are some alternatives that function similarly:

### 1. **Self (formerly Self Lender)**
   - **Overview**: Self offers a credit builder account, which works like a secured loan. You make monthly payments into a certificate of deposit (CD), and the payments are reported to the three major credit bureaus (Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion).
   - **Key Feature**: At the end of the term, you receive the money you paid in, minus interest and fees.
   - **Backdating**: Self does not offer traditional backdating but helps build credit through consistent payments reported over time.

### 2. **CreditStrong**
   - **Overview**: CreditStrong offers a similar service to Self, where you make monthly payments into an account, and these payments are reported to the credit bureaus.
   - **Key Feature**: They offer various plan options, including longer terms and higher amounts, which might result in higher credit-building potential.
   - **Backdating**: CreditStrong does not offer backdating but builds credit history over time through the payment plan.

### 3. **Grow Credit**
   - **Overview**: Grow Credit provides a credit-building service by allowing you to pay for your subscriptions (e.g., Netflix, Spotify) through their platform, and they report those payments to the credit bureaus.
   - **Key Feature**: This service is unique in that it builds credit using your everyday subscription payments.
   - **Backdating**: Grow Credit does not offer backdating; it builds credit from the time you start using the service.

### 4. **Chime Credit Builder**
   - **Overview**: Chime offers a secured credit card that allows you to build credit by making everyday purchases. You deposit money into a secured account, and this balance acts as your credit limit.
   - **Key Feature**: No fees, interest, or minimum security deposit required, and reports to all major credit bureaus.
   - **Backdating**: Chime does not provide backdating but supports building credit with regular use and on-time payments.

### 5. **Experian Boost**
   - **Overview**: Experian Boost allows you to add positive payment history from utilities, phone bills, and streaming services to your Experian credit report.
   - **Key Feature**: Boosts your Experian credit score instantly by including payment history that typically wouldn't be reported.
   - **Backdating**: Experian Boost can take into account past payments, effectively "backdating" them, but only for the period you’ve been paying those bills.

### 6. **Rental Kharma**
   - **Overview**: Rental Kharma reports your rent payments to the credit bureaus, helping to build your credit history.
   - **Key Feature**: You can add up to 24 months of previous rental payments to your credit report.
   - **Backdating**: Rental Kharma offers a form of backdating by adding past rental payments.

### 7. **eCredable Lift**
   - **Overview**: eCredable Lift allows you to link utility accounts and have those payments reported to TransUnion.
   - **Key Feature**: Similar to Experian Boost but reports to TransUnion.
   - **Backdating**: eCredable Lift can backdate up to 24 months of payments.

These services provide various ways to build or improve credit, with some offering the ability to backdate payments or add past payment history to your credit report. Always ensure the service is legitimate and aligns with your financial goals before enrolling.

For building credit with backdating specifically for both credit history age and available credit, the options are more limited. However, there are a couple of services and methods that can help you achieve this:

### 1. **Tradeline Services**
   - **Overview**: Tradeline services involve adding you as an authorized user to an existing credit account with a long, positive history. This can instantly increase your credit history age and available credit.
   - **Key Feature**: By being added to a seasoned tradeline (an account that has been open for a long time), you benefit from the account's age and credit limit.
   - **Backdating**: The credit history from the tradeline (including account age and available credit) is reported to the credit bureaus as part of your credit profile. This effectively backdates your credit history.

   **Popular Tradeline Providers**:
   - **BoostCredit101**: Specializes in seasoned tradelines that can add years of credit history and significant available credit.
   - **Tradeline Supply Company**: Offers a marketplace of tradelines with detailed information on credit history length and credit limits.
   - **Credit Pro**: Focuses on helping clients improve their credit scores by adding them to tradelines with high credit limits and long histories.

   **Considerations**: Tradeline services can be expensive, and their impact may vary depending on the credit bureau and the existing structure of your credit report. Some lenders may also discount the impact of authorized user tradelines, so it's essential to use this strategy as part of a broader credit-building plan.

### 2. **Primary Tradelines with Backdating**
   - **Overview**: Some services offer primary tradelines that are backdated to show an older account age on your credit report. These are typically more expensive and less commonly available than authorized user tradelines.
   - **Key Feature**: Primary tradelines reflect as your own credit accounts (not as an authorized user), which may be viewed more favorably by lenders.
   - **Backdating**: The service provides you with a line of credit that is reported with an established history, which includes an older account opening date.

   **Providers**:
   - **New Coast Direct**: Offers a line of credit for purchasing from their online store, and it reports as a primary tradeline. Some services associated with these types of accounts may offer backdating options.
   - **Hutton Chase**: Similar to New Coast Direct, Hutton Chase offers a store credit account that is reported as a primary tradeline. Look for services that may have backdating as an additional feature.

   **Considerations**: While primary tradelines can be powerful in boosting credit scores and history, you must thoroughly vet the provider to ensure they are legitimate and the backdating will be correctly reflected on your credit report.

### 3. **Adding Authorized Users to Existing Accounts (DIY Approach)**
   - **Overview**: If you or someone you trust has an existing credit account with a long history and high available credit, adding yourself or another individual as an authorized user can achieve similar results as purchasing tradelines.
   - **Key Feature**: The history and credit limit of the account are typically reported on the credit report of the authorized user.
   - **Backdating**: The account's full history, including age and available credit, will appear on your credit report as though you were a part of it from the beginning.

   **Considerations**: This method is often the most cost-effective but requires access to an account that meets the necessary criteria. Not all creditors report authorized user information to all three credit bureaus, so it’s essential to verify this before proceeding.

### Key Considerations
- **Legitimacy**: Ensure that any service or provider you use is legitimate, as there are many scams in the credit repair and enhancement industry.
- **Impact**: Understand that while these methods can significantly boost your credit profile, they are not a substitute for building long-term credit habits and may be viewed differently by lenders.
- **Cost**: These services, particularly primary tradelines with backdating, can be expensive, so weigh the cost against the potential benefit to your credit score and history.

Using a combination of these methods can help you build a more robust credit profile by increasing both the age of your credit history and your available credit.
