## 👤 You

How many times would you have to double $1000 to get to $1M

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

To find how many times you'd need to double $1,000 to reach $1,000,000:

Use the formula:  
**Final = Initial × 2^n**  
Solve:  
**$1,000,000 = $1,000 × 2^n**  
Divide both sides:  
**1,000,000 / 1,000 = 2^n**  
**1,000 = 2^n**

Now solve for **n**:  
**n = log₂(1000) ≈ 9.97**

**Answer: 10 times** (since you can’t double a partial time, round up).

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Each double is an independent 50/50 (0.5) chance of success. To successfully double **10 times in a row**:

**P(success) = 0.5^10 = 1 / 1024 ≈ 0.0009765625**

**Answer: ~0.0977% chance** (less than 1 in 1,000).
