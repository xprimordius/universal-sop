### ⚡️Laser vs. Non-Laser Projection — Quick Breakdown  

**1. Laser Projection (Premium Experience)**  
- **Light Source:** Uses high-powered laser diodes instead of traditional xenon bulbs.  
- **Brightness & Clarity:** Up to **2–3× brighter**; maintains contrast even in dark scenes.  
- **Color:** Wider color gamut (richer reds, deeper blacks, cleaner whites).  
- **Sharpness:** Noticeably crisper 4K-level detail, especially on large screens.  
- **Consistency:** No dimming over time like bulbs; every showing looks pristine.  
- **Formats:** Marketed as *Regal Laser*, *IMAX with Laser*, or *RPX Laser* — all premium.  
- **Cost:** Usually **+$2 – $6** surcharge per ticket.  

---

**2. Non-Laser (Standard Projection)**  
- **Light Source:** Older xenon lamp.  
- **Brightness & Color:** Dimmer, less vivid. Whites may appear yellowish after bulb wear.  
- **Sharpness:** Standard 2K or basic 4K projectors; less crisp.  
- **Experience:** The “normal” 2D experience — fine, but not premium.  
- **Cost:** Standard ticket (no surcharge).  

---

| Ticket cost | +$2–$6 surcharge | Regular price |

Laser = higher brightness, richer color, sharper picture — a “premium” cinematic experience.  
Non-laser = standard theater projection.
