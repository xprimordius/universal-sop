### Compensation Consideration

The compensation amount you request should reflect the financial losses and distress caused by the inaccuracies. A lawyer can help determine an appropriate amount based on similar cases and the extent of your damages.

### Legal Action

If Upgrade does not respond or rectify the situation, consider taking legal action. A consumer rights attorney can help you file a lawsuit for damages under the FCRA, which may include:

- Actual damages (financial losses due to credit denials, increased interest rates, etc.).
- Statutory damages (up to $1,000 per violation).
- Punitive damages (in cases of willful noncompliance).
- Legal fees and costs.

Following these steps will ensure you have taken all necessary actions to address the issue and seek appropriate compensation.
