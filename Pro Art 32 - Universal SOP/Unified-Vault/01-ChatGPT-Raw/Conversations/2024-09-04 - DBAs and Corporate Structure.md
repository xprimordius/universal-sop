# DBAs and Corporate Structure

---

## 👤 You

How do DBAs work underneath the parent corp?

For example:

Parent Corp: Skyward

Subsidiary LLC / C Corps: named Prismatic LLC,

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

In this scenario, a **DBA (Doing Business As)** allows the subsidiary (such as Prismatic LLC) or the parent company (Skyward Corp) to operate under a different name without forming a new legal entity. Here’s how DBAs work in relation to a parent corporation and its subsidiaries:

### 1. **Parent Corporation (Skyward Corp) Using DBAs:**
   - **Skyward Corp** could file for a **DBA** to conduct business under a different name for various purposes (e.g., branding, different product lines). For example, Skyward Corp might operate as “Skyward Solutions” or “Blue Horizons” depending on the DBA filing.
   - The **parent corporation** is still the legal entity responsible for contracts, liabilities, and taxes under these names. The DBA does not create a new company but allows flexibility in branding or public-facing operations.

### 2. **Subsidiary (Prismatic LLC) Using DBAs:**
   - If **Prismatic LLC** is a subsidiary of **Skyward Corp**, it can also file for a DBA to operate under a different name. This allows Prismatic LLC to diversify its operations or product lines without creating separate legal entities.
   - The DBA names are tied directly to Prismatic LLC, meaning contracts signed or business conducted under the DBA name are legally the responsibility of Prismatic LLC.

### 3. **Control and Branding:**
   - **Skyward Corp** as the parent company can control and manage the operations of its subsidiaries like **Prismatic LLC**, but each entity remains legally distinct.
   - **Prismatic LLC** may choose to use a DBA if it wants to present different brands or products without revealing the connection to the parent company.
   - However, **Skyward Corp** may file DBAs under its own name or manage the DBAs used by its subsidiaries to streamline operations, depending on its strategy.

### 4. **Legal and Tax Implications:**
   - **DBAs** don’t affect the legal or tax status of the entity. For example, **Prismatic LLC** remains a separate legal entity with its own tax filings, even if it uses a DBA.
   - **DBAs** are registered at the state or county level, and the legal name of the parent company or subsidiary must be used for tax purposes, while the DBA is used for branding and marketing.

### Key Points:
- **DBAs** allow flexibility in branding and public-facing business operations without creating new entities.
- **Prismatic LLC** would still be a separate legal entity under **Skyward Corp**, but it can operate under different names using DBAs for marketing or operational purposes.
- **Skyward Corp** can also hold DBAs for its own operations if desired.

This structure provides legal simplicity while allowing for operational diversity.
