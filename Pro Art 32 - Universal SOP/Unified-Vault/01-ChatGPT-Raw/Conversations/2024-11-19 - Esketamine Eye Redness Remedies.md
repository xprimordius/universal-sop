### Types of Metabolites:
1. **Primary Metabolites:**
   - Essential for basic life processes.
   - Examples: Amino acids, glucose, and fatty acids.

2. **Secondary Metabolites:**
   - Not essential for survival but can play specialized roles (e.g., in defense or signaling).
   - Examples: Alkaloids, pigments, or specific drug breakdown products.
