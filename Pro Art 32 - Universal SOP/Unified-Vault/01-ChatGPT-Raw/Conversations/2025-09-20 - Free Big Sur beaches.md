## 🔍 Strategy to access for free / minimize cost

1. **Walk or hike from accessible public land**  
   If you can park on public roads or forest service lands without a fee, hike in to beaches that are within reach. Sometimes that avoids the parking fee at the beach’s lot.  

2. **Use early mornings / off-peak / weekdays**  
   Fees might still apply, but lots fill up, guards show up, and restricted access is more likely during peak times.  

3. **Look for “public access” maps**  
   There are coastal commission / forest service maps that show legal public access points — roads or trails legally open (even if not always obvious) alongside private property. citeturn0search2turn0search21  

4. **Know vehicle vs. pedestrian rules**  
   Sometimes the road is limited to vehicles and the fee is for parking; walking in from a farther parking spot might avoid fees. But sometimes roads are closed to pedestrians, etc.  

5. **Check Forest Service vs State Parks rules**  
   Different agencies have different fees and rules. Forest Service lands tend to have more lenient public access but sometimes still charge for parking.
