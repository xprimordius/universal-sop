4. **Heat + Movement**  
   - Heating pad on flank, hot shower.  
   - Light walking increases ureter movement and speeds passing.

5. **Avoid high-oxalate foods temporarily**  
   - Spinach, almonds, beets, nuts, tea, chocolate.  
   - Cuts off fuel for most stones while healing.

6. **Electrolytes**  
   - Add magnesium + potassium citrate.  
   - Helps relax ureter muscles + reduce spasms.

7. **Medical interventions if needed**  
   - **Tamsulosin (Flomax)** from a doctor can enlarge the ureter → faster passage.  
   - CT scan if pain is severe or stone >5–6mm.

**ROOT CAUSES (WHY THEY FORM)**  
Most kidney stones are caused by:

1. **Low hydration**  
   - #1 cause. Concentrated urine = crystal formation.

2. **High oxalate foods + low calcium combo**  
   - Paradox: *not enough* calcium → more free oxalate absorbed → more stones.

3. **High salt intake**  
   - Increases calcium in urine → more crystals.

4. **Too much animal protein**  
   - Meat increases uric acid + lowers urine pH = stone-forming environment.

5. **High sugar / soda intake**  
   - Fructose increases uric-acid stones.  
   - Cola has phosphoric acid → higher stone risk.

6. **Chronic magnesium deficiency**  
   - Magnesium prevents calcium from binding with oxalate.

7. **Genetics (20–30% influence)**  
   - But lifestyle is the main driver.

8. **Supplements taken incorrectly**  
   - High vitamin C → increases oxalate.  
   - Calcium supplements taken *away* from meals → kidney stones.
