**Target RedCard rewards/refunds on returns — short answer:**  
If you return an item you bought with a Target RedCard, the **5% RedCard discount is refunded** and any **purchase rewards (like Target Circle earnings)** tied to that purchase can be **removed or reversed** when the return is processed. citeturn0search4turn0search15

**Key facts (precise):**  
- **RedCard 5% discount:** Returned with the item. You don’t “lose” it permanently — the refund amount is adjusted accordingly.  
- **Circle Rewards (earned or used):**  
  - Rewards *earned* on that purchase can be **deducted from your account** if the purchase is returned. citeturn0search15  
  - If you already *spent* rewards on that purchase, Target may take those back and your balance can go negative until it’s cleared. citeturn0search15

**Timeline:**  
Refunds go back to your card based on Target’s processing times (often 1–5 business days depending on method). citeturn0search35

**Bottom line:**  
You don’t lose the ability to get your money/refund back for RedCard purchases — but the rewards tied to that purchase **can be reversed** when you return the item.
