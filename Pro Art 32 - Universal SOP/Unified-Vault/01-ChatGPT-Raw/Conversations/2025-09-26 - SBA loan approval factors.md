People are getting six-figure SBA loans without “perfect credit” by leveraging **SBA’s guarantee, strong documentation, and alternative underwriting factors**.  

---

### Core Factors That Override Credit
1. **SBA Guarantee:**  
   SBA covers 50–85% of lender risk, so banks can approve loans that otherwise wouldn’t pass strict conventional credit guidelines.  

2. **Business Financials > Personal Credit:**  
   Lenders weigh cash flow, profitability, and business tax returns more than just FICO. A business with solid revenue and margins can qualify even if personal credit is average.  

3. **Collateral & Assets:**  
   Real estate, equipment, or other assets pledged reduce lender risk and unlock higher approvals.  

4. **Experience & Plan:**  
   A strong, detailed business plan and management experience can outweigh weaker credit. Lenders need confidence the borrower can operate profitably.  

5. **Alternative Credit Factors:**  
   - Proven repayment history on similar loans.  
   - Strong business checking account balances.  
   - Existing relationship with the bank.  

---

### Programs Used
- **SBA 7(a):** Up to $5M, flexible use (working capital, equipment, refinance).  
- **SBA 504:** Fixed assets (real estate, equipment).  
- **SBA Microloan:** Up to $50K, often easier credit terms.  
- **EIDL (during COVID):** Looser standards let many get $100K+ despite mediocre credit, though this program is closed.  

---

### How People Actually Get “Hundreds of Thousands”
- They **stack SBA loans** with lines of credit.  
- They **partner with consultants/brokers** who know which lenders are flexible.  
- They **document income aggressively** (e.g., showing contracts, recurring revenue, tax returns).  
- They **use co-signers or guarantors** with stronger profiles.  

---

👉 Bottom line: It’s not “credit doesn’t matter.” It’s that **SBA allows lenders to approve based on business strength, collateral, and guarantee coverage**—so borrowers can get large approvals without flawless credit.
