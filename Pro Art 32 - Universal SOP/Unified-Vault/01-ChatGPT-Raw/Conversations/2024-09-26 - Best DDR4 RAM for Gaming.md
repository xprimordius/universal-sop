# Best DDR4 RAM for Gaming

---

## 👤 You

best ddr 4 low CL timings for apex legends

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

For **Apex Legends**, where responsiveness and speed matter, lower **CAS Latency (CL)** timings on DDR4 RAM can slightly improve performance, particularly in frame consistency (minimum frame rates). Here’s a general recommendation for DDR4 memory with low CL timings suitable for gaming, including Apex Legends:

### Ideal DDR4 Specs:
1. **Frequency**: Aim for **3200 MHz to 3600 MHz** as the sweet spot.
2. **CAS Latency (CL)**: 
   - **CL14** for 3200 MHz is considered very low and highly performant for gaming.
   - **CL16** for 3600 MHz provides a good balance between latency and frequency.
3. **Capacity**: At least **16 GB** (preferably dual-channel) for smooth gameplay and multitasking.

### Example DDR4 Kits:
- **G.Skill Trident Z RGB (3200 MHz, CL14)**
- **Corsair Vengeance LPX (3600 MHz, CL16)**

While higher frequencies (e.g., 4000+ MHz) are available, the gains in gaming performance, including Apex Legends, tend to diminish beyond 3600 MHz, and lower latency generally provides more real-world benefit than sheer frequency in gaming scenarios.

Yes, **CL12** does exist, but it is extremely rare for DDR4 RAM, especially at higher frequencies. CL12 is generally seen at **lower frequencies** (like 2400 MHz or 2666 MHz) because it's hard to maintain such low latencies at higher speeds.

### Key Points About CL12:
- **Availability**: DDR4 kits with CL12 timings are usually found in **lower frequency** ranges (2400-2666 MHz), which may not be ideal for modern gaming like **Apex Legends** where higher frequencies (3200-4000 MHz) typically yield better results.
- **Performance**: While CL12 is incredibly low latency, the trade-off with **lower frequency** makes these kits less attractive for gaming performance compared to something like **3600 MHz CL14** or **4000 MHz CL16**.
  
### Example of CL12 RAM:
- **G.Skill Ripjaws V (2400 MHz, CL12)**:
   - This kit offers CL12 at 2400 MHz, but its lower frequency might bottleneck performance in demanding games, even though it has excellent latency.

### Why CL12 Isn't Common at High Frequencies:
- **Latency vs. Speed Trade-Off**: Achieving CL12 at high frequencies like 3200 MHz or 3600 MHz requires high-quality components and very precise tuning, making it both expensive and less common. Most users will get better performance from a higher-frequency kit with slightly higher latency, like **3600 MHz CL14** or **4000 MHz CL15**.

In conclusion, while CL12 exists, it's not commonly found at the higher frequencies needed for gaming performance in **Apex Legends**. For best results, you should look for **3600 MHz CL14** or **4000 MHz CL15/16** for an optimal combination of low latency and high speed.
