To properly thaw salmon (including *sashimi/susamii*-grade), follow safe, texture-preserving methods:

### 🧊 **1. Refrigerator Method (Best for Safety & Texture)**
- **Time:** 8–12 hours (overnight)
- **Steps:**
  1. Keep salmon in its **vacuum-sealed package** or wrap tightly in plastic.
  2. Place on a plate or tray in the **lowest shelf** of the fridge to avoid cross-contamination.
  3. Once thawed, **use within 24 hours**.

> ✅ Preserves flavor, texture, and prevents bacterial growth.

---

### 💧 **2. Cold Water Method (Faster, Still Safe)**
- **Time:** 30–60 minutes
- **Steps:**
  1. Keep sealed in airtight packaging.
  2. Submerge in **cold—not warm—water**.
  3. Change the water every 15–20 minutes.
  4. Use immediately after thawing.

> ⚠️ Never use warm or hot water — it causes uneven thawing and bacteria growth.
