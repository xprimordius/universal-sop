   - *Cause:* Raw broccoli shares proteins with pollen (especially ragweed, birch).  
   - *Symptoms:* Itchy mouth, throat bumps, swelling, tingling.  
   - *Why now?* Can suddenly appear or worsen during allergy season.

2. **Histamine Response / Mild Food Sensitivity**  
   - *Cause:* Hummus (especially tahini, garlic, lemon) can trigger histamine or inflammatory reactions.  
   - *Symptoms:* Mouth tingling, irritation, minor swelling.

3. **Lectins or Sulfur Compounds (from Broccoli)**  
   - *Cause:* Broccoli has sulfur and lectins, which may irritate mucosa in sensitive individuals.  
   - *Symptoms:* Throat tightness, bloating, mouth discomfort.

4. **Saponins or Oxalates (in Broccoli)**  
   - These natural compounds can sometimes irritate the oral lining, especially when raw.

5. **Fungal or Bacterial Contamination (on raw produce)**  
   - Even organic foods can carry microbial irritants if not washed thoroughly.
