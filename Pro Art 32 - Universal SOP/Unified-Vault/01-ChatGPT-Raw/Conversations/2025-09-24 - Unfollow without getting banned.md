## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here’s the **clear breakdown** so you can choose fast:  

---

# 🔹 Tri-Tip
- **Cut:** Bottom sirloin, triangular muscle.  
- **Texture:** Leaner, moderately tender, steak-like chew.  
- **Flavor:** Beefy but mild; often Santa Maria–style seasoned with garlic, salt, pepper.  
- **Best for:** Slicing like steak, sandwiches, healthier option (less fat).  
- **Cook:** Grilled / smoked to medium-rare or medium.  

---

# 🔹 Brisket
- **Cut:** Breast / lower chest (point + flat muscles).  
- **Texture:** Very fatty (esp. point end), pulls apart when cooked long.  
- **Flavor:** Rich, smoky, buttery from rendered fat.  
- **Best for:** Traditional BBQ, platters, sandwiches; heavy, filling.  
- **Cook:** Low & slow smoked (12–16 hrs) until tender.  

---

# 🔹 Burnt Ends
- **Cut:** Cubed brisket point, double-smoked.  
- **Texture:** Crispy outside bark, juicy fatty interior, caramelized edges.  
- **Flavor:** Intense, smoky, sweet-savory — BBQ candy.  
- **Best for:** Small bites, indulgence, max flavor punch.  
- **Cook:** Brisket point cubed + sauced + returned to smoker.  

---

# ⚖️ Quick Decision
- **Want steak-like, lean, sliceable?** → Tri-Tip  
- **Want classic BBQ, tender + fatty?** → Brisket  
- **Want rich, bite-sized, indulgent treat?** → Burnt Ends
