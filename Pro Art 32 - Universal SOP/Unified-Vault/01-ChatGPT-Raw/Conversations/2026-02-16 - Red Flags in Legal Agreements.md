## 🚩 MAJOR RED FLAGS

### 1. **Very High Contingency Fee (40%–50%)**
- **40% pre-filing** is already high.
- **50% after filing / arbitration / verdict** is **top-end aggressive**.
- Many states flag **50%** as excessive unless heavily justified.

👉 Ask: *Why not 33⅓% / 40% max?*

---

### 2. **Costs Come Out of YOUR Pocket First**
> “Client shall pay all costs… including pre-suit medical records, experts, filing fees, travel…”

- This means:
  - Costs are **deducted before** the contingency is calculated (likely).
  - You could win and still receive **far less** than expected.
- No cap. No estimate. No approval threshold.

👉 Ask for:
- Written clarification: **fee calculated after or before costs**
- A **cost-approval requirement** (e.g., anything over $500 requires consent)

---

### 3. **Attorney Can Withdraw — You Still Owe**
> “Attorney may withdraw… Client agrees to pay reasonable value of services…”

- If they drop the case:
  - You may owe **hourly-equivalent fees**
  - PLUS they keep a **lien on your claim**
- “Reasonable” is undefined = dangerous.

👉 This is one of the **biggest risks**.

---

### 4. **Lien on Your Case**
> “Attorney has a lien on Client’s claim”

- Even if you fire them:
  - They can block settlement until paid
  - New attorney must negotiate with them
- This reduces your leverage significantly.

---

### 5. **30-Day ‘Risk-Free’ Is Not Truly Risk-Free**
- Exception:
  > “If Attorney has obtained a settlement offer during first 30 days…”
- They can:
  - Generate a **low early offer**
  - Lock in fee rights
- Also requires **written notice before day 30** — easy to miss.

---

### 6. **Medical Records Authorization Is Broad**
- Covers:
  - **Mental health**
  - **Substance use**
  - **All billing & insurance**
- Valid **560 days**
- Waives provider time limits

👉 Normal for PI cases — but **very broad**. No restriction to accident-related care.

---

### 7. **No Clear Case Strategy or Scope**
- No mention of:
  - Litigation vs settlement plan
  - Arbitration risk
  - Estimated timeline
  - Minimum settlement authority
- Agreement is **one-sided boilerplate**.

---

### 8. **Arbitration Language (Fee Disputes)**
- Fee disputes go to **mandatory arbitration**
- Limits your ability to challenge excessive billing in court.

---

If they **refuse to modify anything**, that’s another red flag.
