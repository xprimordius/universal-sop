## 🤖 ChatGPT

**L-Theanine** is a non-dietary amino acid found primarily in green tea. Key points:

- **Effects**: Promotes relaxation without sedation, reduces stress and anxiety, smooths out caffeine jitters.
- **Dosage**:  
  - Alone: 100–400 mg/day  
  - With caffeine: 100–200 mg L-theanine per 100 mg caffeine for synergy (calm focus).
- **Timing**:  
  - For sleep: 30–60 min before bed.  
  - For focus: With morning caffeine.
- **Safety**: Very well-tolerated; no known toxicity at normal doses.

Use for:  
- **Calm focus** (especially paired with caffeine)  
- **Reduced anxiety/stress**  
- **Improved sleep quality** (not sedative but relaxing)
