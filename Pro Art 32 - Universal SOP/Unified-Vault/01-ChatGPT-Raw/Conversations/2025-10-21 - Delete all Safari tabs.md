Here’s how to **delete all Safari tabs on iOS 17 / iOS 18 / iOS 26 (same layout)**:  

---

### 🔹 **Delete All Tabs at Once**
1. **Open Safari.**  
2. Tap the **two-square icon** in the bottom-right corner (the tab overview).  
3. Tap and **hold** the **“Done”** button.  
4. Tap **“Close All [number] Tabs.”**  
5. Confirm.

---

### 🔹 **Auto-Close Tabs Automatically**
1. Go to **Settings → Safari.**  
2. Scroll to **“Tabs.”**  
3. Tap **“Close Tabs.”**  
4. Choose **After One Day, One Week, or One Month.**

---

### 🔹 **Private Tabs Too**
If you’re in **Private Browsing mode**, repeat the same steps — private tabs are closed separately.
