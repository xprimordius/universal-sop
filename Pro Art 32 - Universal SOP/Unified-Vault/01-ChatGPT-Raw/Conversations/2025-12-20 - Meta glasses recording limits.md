## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Short answer: No.**

**Why:**  
Meta (Ray-Ban) smart glasses **cannot retroactively record**. They only capture **from the moment you say the command or press the button forward**.

**Key facts (clear + accurate):**
- ❌ No “rewind” or “last 10 seconds” buffer  
- ❌ No always-on rolling memory (privacy + battery limits)  
- ✅ Recording starts **only after** you say *“Hey Meta, record a video”* or press the capture button  
- ✅ Photos/videos are **intentional, forward-only**

**Workarounds (if this matters to you):**
- Keep short rolling clips manually (start/stop often)  
- Use a body cam / dash cam with loop recording (different device class)  
- Anticipate moments → pre-arm recording
