1. **Viral Infection (Most Likely)**

   - Influenza (flu)

2. **Postnasal Drip**
   - Caused by **allergies, dry air, or sinus irritation**.
   - Triggers a **throat tickle, coughing, and irritability**.

3. **Acid Reflux (Silent GERD)**
   - Acid reaching the throat **(LPR - laryngopharyngeal reflux)** can cause:
