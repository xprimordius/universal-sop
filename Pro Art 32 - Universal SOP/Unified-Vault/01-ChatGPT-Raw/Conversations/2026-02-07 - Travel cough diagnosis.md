**Why this fits your symptoms**
- **Dry cough, no sniffles** → common after viral URIs (COVID, flu, RSV) or from **air pollution/smoke**, **dry air**, **reflux**, or **airplane irritation**.
- Vietnam travel adds exposure to **pollution**, **AC**, **crowds**, and **new viruses**—not necessarily anything novel.

**Most common causes (ranked)**
1. **Post-viral cough** (can last 3–8 weeks after a mild illness)
2. **COVID-19** (often dry cough without runny nose)
3. **Influenza / RSV**
4. **Airway irritation** (pollution, smoke, dust, AC)
5. **Silent reflux (LPR)**

**What to do now (efficient)**
- **Test today:** COVID rapid (repeat in 24–48h if negative).
- **Supportive care:** hydrate, honey/tea, humidifier, throat lozenges.
- **Avoid irritants:** smoke, cold air, heavy workouts for a few days.
- **Night reflux check:** avoid late meals, elevate head if cough worsens at night.

**Get medical care urgently if ANY of these appear**
- Fever ≥101°F (38.3°C)
- Shortness of breath, chest pain
- Coughing blood
- Symptoms worsening after day 7–10
- Night sweats + weight loss

**Bottom line:** This pattern is common after travel and **not evidence of a new virus**. Test, rest your airways, monitor for red flags.
