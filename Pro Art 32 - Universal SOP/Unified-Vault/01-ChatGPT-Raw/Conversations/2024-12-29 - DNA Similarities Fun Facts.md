Genetically, humans and apes (particularly chimpanzees) share roughly 98–99% of their DNA, but there are key differences in:

1. **Chromosome Fusion:** Humans have 46 chromosomes, while other great apes have 48. Two ancestral ape chromosomes fused into what’s now human chromosome 2.

2. **Gene Regulation & Expression:** Even if the same genes are present, the way they turn “on” or “off” (i.e., regulatory sequences) varies and significantly influences traits like brain development and language abilities.

3. **Specific Genes & Mutations:** Small but critical variations in genes (e.g., FOXP2 for speech) lead to complex changes in cognition and behavior.

Overall, these minor genetic and regulatory differences create the major physiological, cognitive, and behavioral distinctions between humans and other great apes.
