To troubleshoot the issue of undetected video/audio signal, here are some steps you can take:

1. **Check Device Settings:**
   - Ensure that your camera and microphone are connected and operational.
   - Test the devices in other applications to confirm functionality.

2. **Browser Permissions:**
   - Verify that your browser has permission to access the camera and microphone.
   - In your browser’s settings, go to the Privacy and Security section and check the camera and microphone permissions for the website you're using.

3. **Restart Browser or App:**
   - Close the browser or app and restart it.
   - Sometimes reloading the page or restarting the application can fix minor glitches.

4. **Update Software:**
   - Ensure your browser or video conference app is up to date.
   - Check if your camera and microphone drivers are up to date.

5. **Disable Other Applications:**
   - Close other applications that might be using your camera or microphone as they may interfere with the detection.

6. **Try a Different Browser or App:**
   - If the problem persists, try switching to a different browser or video conference application to rule out browser-specific issues.

By following these steps, you should be able to troubleshoot and resolve the issue with your video/audio signal.
