Ground lamb from Walmart's Market Side brand is generally considered safe if proper food handling and cooking guidelines are followed. However, here are some potential concerns to be aware of:

1. **Bacterial Contamination**: Like any ground meat, ground lamb can be susceptible to bacteria such as E. coli or Salmonella if not properly stored or cooked. It's essential to cook the lamb to an internal temperature of at least 160°F (71°C) to kill harmful bacteria.

2. **Storage Concerns**: Ground lamb should be stored in the refrigerator and used within 1-2 days of purchase, or it should be frozen if not used immediately. Make sure it is refrigerated promptly after purchasing to reduce the risk of spoilage.

3. **Fat Content**: Ground lamb typically has a higher fat content than other ground meats, which might not be ideal for those on low-fat diets or with cholesterol concerns.

4. **Sourcing and Welfare**: Walmart sources meats from various suppliers, so the animal welfare practices can vary. If animal welfare is a concern, it's recommended to check for certifications like "grass-fed" or "organic," as these often adhere to stricter welfare standards.

5. **Allergens or Additives**: While ground lamb itself is not a common allergen, always check for any additives or preservatives that might be included in packaged products, as these could pose health concerns for those with sensitivities.

By ensuring proper handling, storage, and cooking, ground lamb from Market Side should pose minimal health risks.
