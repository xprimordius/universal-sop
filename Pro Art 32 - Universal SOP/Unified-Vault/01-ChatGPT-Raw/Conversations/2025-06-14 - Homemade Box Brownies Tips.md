**Top tip to make box brownies taste homemade and elite:**

➡️ **Replace water with coffee or milk**, **oil with melted butter**, and **add an extra egg** for richness.  
➡️ **Mix in chopped dark chocolate or chocolate chips** and a pinch of **salt**.  
➡️ Finish with a **sprinkle of flaky sea salt** on top before baking.

**Result:** Fudgy, rich, bakery-tier brownies that no one will guess came from a box.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Best way to cut brownies cleanly without sticking:**

1. **Cool completely** — even chill in the fridge for 1–2 hours.  
2. **Use a plastic knife** (nonstick miracle) or a metal knife **sprayed with cooking spray** or **wiped with warm water and paper towel** between cuts.  
3. **Wipe after each cut** for clean edges.

Optional: Line pan with parchment so you can lift and slice on a flat surface.
