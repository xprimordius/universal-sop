Coconut oil itself **does not have a pH** because it is an oil and does not contain water. pH is a measure of hydrogen ion concentration in aqueous (water-based) solutions, and since coconut oil is purely lipid-based, it does not have a measurable pH value.

- **Alternative pH-Balanced Lubes:** If you're concerned about vaginal pH, a **water-based, pH-balanced lubricant** (around 4.0-4.5) would be safer for frequent use.
