No—your mom cannot legally pick up and mail your Adderall from Minnesota to you in California.  

**Why:**
- **Controlled Substance:** Adderall is a Schedule II controlled substance under federal law. Mailing it—even by a family member—is illegal (federal drug trafficking laws apply).
- **Pharmacy Rules:** Prescriptions can only be dispensed directly to the patient or an authorized pickup person at the prescribing pharmacy. They cannot be shipped across state lines except through licensed mail-order pharmacies that follow DEA regulations.
- **Risk:** If caught, both you and your mom could face serious legal consequences.

**What you can do instead:**
1. **Transfer prescription:** Ask your doctor or current pharmacy to transfer the prescription to a California pharmacy.  
2. **Mail-order pharmacy:** Many insurance plans offer DEA-compliant mail-order services that can legally ship Adderall to your California address.  
3. **New prescription:** If transfer isn’t possible, ask your doctor to send a new prescription to a California pharmacy.
