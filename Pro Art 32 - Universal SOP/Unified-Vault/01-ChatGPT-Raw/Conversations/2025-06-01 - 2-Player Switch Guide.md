To play 2-player games on a **Nintendo Switch**, follow this streamlined guide:

---

### ✅ **Split-Screen (Local Multiplayer on 1 Switch):**

**1. Open the game.**  
**2. From the title/menu screen, select "Multiplayer" or "2-Player Mode".**  
**3. Detach both Joy-Cons.**  
**4. Hold each Joy-Con sideways (SL/SR buttons face up).**  
**5. On the controller pairing screen:**
   - **Player 1:** Press **SL + SR** on one Joy-Con.  
   - **Player 2:** Press **SL + SR** on the second Joy-Con.  
**6. Confirm and start the game.**

---

### ✅ **Using Two Consoles (Wireless Local Play):**

**1. Each player launches the same game on their own Switch.**  
**2. From the main menu, select **Local Play** or **Wireless Play**.  
**3. One player creates a room; the other joins.**  
**4. Follow on-screen prompts to begin.**

---

### ✅ **Online Multiplayer (Over Internet):**

**1. Both players need:**
   - Nintendo Switch Online membership.  
   - Internet connection.  
**2. Open the game > Choose "Online Play."**  
**3. Add friend via **Friends List** or **Lobby Code**.  
**4. Invite or join session.**
