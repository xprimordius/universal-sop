---

### What determines the exact loss
**Lowest → Highest drain order**

1. **Parked, Sleep Mode ON, no Sentry**  
   → **~1–2%**  
2. **Parked, Sentry Mode ON**  
   → **~3–5%**  
3. **Frequent app checks / climate preheat / cabin overheat on**  
   → **5%+ possible**

---

### Why it happens
- Cold slows lithium-ion chemistry → **temporary range loss**
- Battery may use small energy to **protect itself**
- Loss is **mostly reversible** once the pack warms

---

### How to minimize loss (best practice)
- Turn **Sentry Mode OFF**
- Avoid checking the Tesla app repeatedly
- If possible, **plug in** (even 120V helps)
- Precondition **while plugged in**, not on battery

---

At **20°F for 4 hours**, expect **~2–4%** in real-world use.  
Anything higher usually means **Sentry or climate activity**.
