- Wealthy suits are built on **fine wool** (Super 120s–150s).

**2) Satin & viscose are accents — not foundations**
- **Satin (silk or viscose blend):** Adds **shine** but can easily look glossy in a *cheap* way if overused.
- **Viscose:** Feels smooth, has sheen, but **wears faster** and can look synthetic without wool.

✅ **Wool–Silk (best overall)**  
- Smooth, soft sheen → *natural* luxury light play

- Subtle gloss, crisp lines, wrinkle resistance

✅ **Wool–Silk–Cashmere (premium)**  
- Very soft, slight shine, rich feel  
- Best for evening/formal and executive presence

⚠️ **Viscose dominant suits**  
- Smooth, sometimes glossy  
- More synthetic feel

⚠️ **Full satin suits**
