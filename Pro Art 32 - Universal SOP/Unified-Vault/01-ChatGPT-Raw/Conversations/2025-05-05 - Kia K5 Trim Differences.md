- **Luxury-focused**

- Leatherette seating (ventilated + heated front)
- 10.25" touchscreen standard
- Panoramic sunroof standard
- 12-speaker Bose audio (optional)
- More soft-touch surfaces

---

### **4. K5 GT (Top Trim)**
- **Performance-focused**
- **Engine**: 2.5L Turbo I4 (290 hp, 311 lb-ft)
- **Drivetrain**: FWD only (no AWD)
- 8-speed DCT (dual-clutch)
- Sport-tuned suspension, larger brakes
- Sport seats, GT badging
- Optional GT1 Package: HUD, surround view, ventilated seats, Bose audio
