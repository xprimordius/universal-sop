## 🔑 Master Tips for Compliance

- **Choose tint film with VLT ratings ≥ 70%** for front doors.

- **Avoid windshield tint altogether** to satisfy Minnesota law.

- **Medical exemptions** exist (MN, OR, WA)—must carry physician’s note complying with state rules.
