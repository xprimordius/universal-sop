# Finding Defaulting Individuals

---

## 👤 You

How to find people that are going into default with their car and property titles

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

To find individuals who are on the verge of defaulting on their car or property titles, you can use a combination of strategies focused on public records, data services, and strategic networking. Here’s how you can approach this:

### 1. **Public Records and Court Filings:**
   - **Foreclosure Notices:** These are typically public records available at the county courthouse or through online databases. Properties facing foreclosure are often listed here.
   - **Lien Records:** Liens are often placed on properties and vehicles when payments are overdue. You can search for lien records through local government offices or online databases that aggregate such information.
   - **Notice of Default (NOD):** This is a public record filed by lenders when a borrower fails to make payments. NODs are available through county records and can be a reliable indicator of someone in financial distress.

### 2. **Subscription to Data Services:**
   - **Real Estate Data Providers:** Services like RealtyTrac, PropertyRadar, or Foreclosure.com provide detailed listings of properties in pre-foreclosure, foreclosure, or that have recently been repossessed.
   - **Vehicle Data Services:** Companies like VINAudit or Carfax can provide information on vehicles with liens or repossession notices, often indicating potential defaults.

### 3. **Networking with Industry Professionals:**
   - **Realtors and Real Estate Agents:** They often have early knowledge of distressed properties before they hit public records.
   - **Auto Dealers and Repo Companies:** Building relationships with these professionals can give you insights into individuals who are behind on their car payments.
   - **Banking and Loan Officers:** These professionals may know of clients who are struggling with their payments and are at risk of default.

### 4. **Direct Marketing and Lead Generation:**
   - **Targeted Mail Campaigns:** Use lists of individuals with high debt-to-income ratios or recent financial setbacks to send targeted mail offers for assistance or buying their property/vehicle.
   - **Online Advertising:** Create ads targeting people searching for loan modifications, debt relief, or selling their assets quickly due to financial distress.

### 5. **Social Media and Online Forums:**
   - **Social Media Monitoring:** Use social listening tools to monitor conversations about financial difficulties, foreclosures, or car repossessions.
   - **Online Forums and Groups:** Engage in online communities where individuals might discuss their financial struggles, offering solutions or assistance.

### 6. **Public Auctions and Trustee Sales:**
   - **Attend Auctions:** Properties and vehicles in default often end up in public auctions. Attending these auctions can provide leads on potential sellers.
   - **Trustee Sales:** These sales occur after a foreclosure and can provide leads on individuals who recently defaulted.

These strategies, when combined, will help you identify and connect with individuals who are facing defaults on their car and property titles.
