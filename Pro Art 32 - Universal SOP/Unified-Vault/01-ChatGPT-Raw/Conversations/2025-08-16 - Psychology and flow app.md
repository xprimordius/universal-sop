**Key Insight:** Emotional bandwidth is reduced post-cycle → less energy for accountability → responsibility lapses can show up as immaturity.

## 2. Flow App (Psychology of Flow)
- **Flow State**: Coined by Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi. It’s the “zone” where you’re fully immersed, highly focused, and lose track of time.  
- **Triggers**: Clear goals, immediate feedback, balance between challenge and skill.  
- **Why Flow App Exists**: Apps like *Flow* (there are a few by this name) track habits, time-block tasks, or coach you into a flow state through focus techniques, music, or guided structure. They’re built on the psychology of behavioral reinforcement—turning micro-tasks into achievable wins.  

**Practical Use Here:** Helping her (or yourself) re-ground by breaking responsibilities into small, trackable commitments → easier to stay accountable without overwhelm.
