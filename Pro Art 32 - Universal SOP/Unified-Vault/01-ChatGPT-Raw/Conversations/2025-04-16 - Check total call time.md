## 🤖 ChatGPT

iPhone doesn’t show total call time per contact natively. Here are the only current methods:

---

### ✅ **Option 1: Manual Check in Call Log**
1. Open **Phone app** → **Recents**.
2. Tap the **(i)** next to the contact.
3. Add up the durations of each call manually.

> ⚠️ iOS only stores a limited number of call entries, so older call history might be missing.

---

### ✅ **Option 2: Carrier Call Logs**
- Log into your **carrier account** (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, etc.).
- Check your **billing or call detail** section.
- Export or review time per number (some let you filter/sort by contact).

---

### ✅ **Option 3: Third-Party Apps**
- Apps like **Truecaller**, **Callistics**, or **Phone Analytics** (Android-heavy, limited iOS support) may provide usage insights, if allowed.
- Most iOS apps are limited due to Apple restrictions.

---

### 🧠 Pro Tip (Manual Tracking Hack)
- Start logging durations in **Notes**, **Shortcuts**, or **an Excel sheet** if recurring tracking is needed.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Fastest & easiest reliable way on iPhone:**  

### ✅ **Use the "Recents" Tab**
1. Open **Phone** app → tap **Recents**  
2. Find the person → tap the **(i)** icon  
3. Scroll and **manually add up durations** of each call  

> 🔁 Tap “See All” to view older calls if available  
> 📉 Limit: iPhone only shows **last ~100 calls total**, not per contact  

---

### 🚀 BEST for full history:  
**Log into your carrier’s website or app** (e.g., AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile)  
- Go to **Billing → Call logs**  
- Filter by contact number  
- See **total duration**, date/time, and export as needed

### ✅ How to Check Call Time per Person via PC:
1. **Go to your carrier's website**:
   - [AT&T](https://www.att.com/my/#/usage)
   - [Verizon](https://www.verizon.com/my-verizon/)

2. **Log in to your account**

3. Go to:
   - **“Usage”** or **“Call Details”**
   - View or **download PDF/CSV** of recent call activity  
   - Filter or **search by number/contact**  
   - **Total call time** is usually shown per call — you can sum it up
