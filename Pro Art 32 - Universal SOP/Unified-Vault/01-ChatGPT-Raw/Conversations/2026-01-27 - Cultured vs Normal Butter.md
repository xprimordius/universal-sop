- **Process:** Cream is **fermented** before churning

- **Process:** Fresh cream, **no fermentation**  
- **Taste:** Clean, rich, mild

- Grass-fed cows → **higher omega-3s, beta-carotene**

## Sourdough vs Other Breads — Gluten, Bloating, Digestion (Clear + Accurate)

image_group{"query":["sourdough bread crumb","commercial white bread sliced","sourdough fermentation process","bread bloating comparison"]}

### 🍞 **Sourdough — Why It’s Easier on the Gut**
- **Gluten is partially broken down** during long fermentation (lactic acid bacteria + wild yeast)  
- **Lower FODMAPs** → less gas, bloating, pressure  
- **Phytic acid reduced** → better mineral absorption (iron, zinc, magnesium)  
- **Slower blood sugar spike** → steadier energy, less inflammation  
- **Result:** Many people with *non-celiac gluten sensitivity* tolerate it well

> ⚠️ Not gluten-free. **Celiac = avoid.**

---

### 🍞 **Most Commercial Breads (White, Wheat, “Multigrain”)**
- **Rapid yeast fermentation** → gluten stays intact  
- **Higher FODMAPs** → more bloating, gas  
- **Added sugars, dough conditioners** → gut irritation  
- **Refined flours** → insulin spikes + inflammation  
- **Result:** Common trigger for bloating and brain fog

---

### 🧠 **Why Sourdough “Feels Better”**
Fermentation = **pre-digestion**  
Your gut does less work → less pressure, less inflammation.

---

### 🥇 **Best → Worst for Bloating (Typical)**
1. **True long-fermented sourdough** (12–48 hrs)  
2. **Sprouted grain bread**  
3. **Artisan yeast bread**  
4. **Commercial sandwich bread** ❌

---

### **Bottom Line**
- **Bloating + gluten sensitivity:** choose **real sourdough**  
- **Avoid:** fast-rise, ultra-processed breads  
- **If still bloated:** try **sourdough + butter** before cutting bread entirely
