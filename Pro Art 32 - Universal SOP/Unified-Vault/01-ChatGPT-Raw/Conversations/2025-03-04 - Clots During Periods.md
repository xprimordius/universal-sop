Are clots normal for a period

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes, small blood clots during a period are normal. They typically occur when menstrual blood pools in the uterus or vagina before being expelled, allowing it to coagulate. Clots are usually dark red and range from the size of a pea to a quarter.

However, **large clots (bigger than a quarter), frequent clotting, or excessive bleeding** (soaking through a pad or tampon every hour) may indicate an underlying issue such as:
- **Heavy menstrual bleeding (menorrhagia)**
- **Hormonal imbalances**
- **Fibroids or polyps**
- **Endometriosis or adenomyosis**
- **Blood clotting disorders**

If you notice large or frequent clots along with severe cramps, prolonged bleeding, or other unusual symptoms, consider consulting a healthcare provider.
