### Tactical Breakdown:

   - You cannot outthink a trigger—you must *feel through* it.

   - See them as a child. Or a soul in training.  
   - Ask: *“How would Love treat them if Love had my voice right now?”*

   - Your identity = *the one who transmutes chaos into clarity, stress into grace.*

6. **Set Clear Boundaries After Responding Kindly**  
   - Kindness ≠ tolerance of dysfunction.  
   - Radiate calm presence *first* → then redirect, delegate, or exit.
