## 🤖 ChatGPT

**TLDR: Use Google Voice for simplicity unless you need advanced CRM-level automation. Use Smarter Contact if you are doing **high-volume SMS lead gen** and want better deliverability, automation, and tracking.**

### ✅ Google Voice (Best for Beginners / Low Volume)
- **Free** (or $10–$30/month with Google Workspace).
- **Basic call + SMS support**.
- **Simple interface**.
- **No automation**.
- **Limited deliverability** (often flagged as spam in bulk outreach).
- **No CRM or skip tracing integration**.

> 🔹 **Best for**: Solopreneurs, low volume outreach, general communication.

---

### ✅ Smarter Contact (Best for High-Volume Lead Gen)
- **Paid platform** (~$100+/month).
- **High SMS deliverability** with 10DLC compliance.
- **Skip tracing**, **auto drip campaigns**, and **lead routing**.
- **CRM integration** and full **campaign analytics**.
- **Designed for real estate investors**.

> 🔹 **Best for**: Scaling land flipping with mass SMS campaigns, follow-ups, and automation.

---

### Recommendation for Land Flipping:
| Situation | Recommendation |
|----------|----------------|
| <100 leads/month | **Google Voice** |
| 100–1,000+ leads/month | **Smarter Contact** |
| Need CRM, skip tracing, drip campaigns | **Smarter Contact** |
| Just calling/texting leads manually | **Google Voice** |

Want a **hybrid setup**? Use Google Voice for 1-on-1 follow-ups and **Smarter Contact** to warm leads at scale.

**Key Advantages of Smarter Contact over Google Voice (manual texting):**

---

### 🔄 **1. Automation at Scale**
- **Google Voice:** Manual texting = 10–30 leads/hour.
- **Smarter Contact:** 1,000+ leads in a single SMS blast with automated follow-ups (drip sequences).

> **Result:** Saves 10–20+ hours/week.

---

### 🚀 **2. Deliverability**
- **Google Voice:** Texts often flagged as spam, especially with cold outreach.
- **Smarter Contact:** Uses 10DLC-registered numbers for **higher delivery + lower spam flag rate**.

> **Result:** More leads actually receive your message.

---

### 🧠 **3. Smart Lead Management**
- Built-in **CRM**, tagging, auto-responses, and campaign filters.
- Instantly identify:
  - Hot leads
  - Responses
  - Opt-outs

> **Result:** Faster sorting, follow-up, and conversion.

---

### 🕵️‍♂️ **4. Built-In Skip Tracing**
- Append missing phone numbers or emails with one click.
- Can auto-verify contact data.

> **Result:** No need for separate skip tracing tools.

---

### 📊 **5. Tracking + Metrics**
- See delivery rates, response rates, opt-outs, and ROI per campaign.

> **Result:** Data-driven adjustments for max effectiveness.

---

- Small list (under 50 people).

- You’re not sending cold messages at volume.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

In land flipping, you source **thousands of phone numbers** by first pulling property owner records, then running **skip tracing** to get contact info.

### ⚙️ Step-by-Step Process:

---

### 1. **Pull a Property List (Land Leads)**  
Use platforms like:

| Source           | Purpose                           |
|------------------|------------------------------------|
| **PropStream**   | Pull landowners by filters         |
| **BatchLeads**   | Filter by equity, ownership date   |
| **REIPro**       | Target absentee or vacant land     |
| **DataTree**     | Bulk download property records     |

**Filter by**:  
- Vacant land  
- County  
- Equity (optional)  
- Ownership > 5 years  
- Out-of-state owners

➡ Export **Name + Mailing Address + Parcel Info**

---

### 2. **Skip Trace for Phone Numbers**  
Upload your list to skip tracing tools like:

| Tool               | Cost (per match) | Notes                                  |
|--------------------|------------------|----------------------------------------|
| **BatchSkipTracing** | ~$0.12–$0.20     | Most common, good match rate           |
| **LeadSherpa**       | ~$0.15–$0.18     | Also offers DNC scrubbing              |
| **REISkip**          | ~$0.10–$0.15     | Affordable, works for high volume      |
| **Smarter Contact**  | $0.10–$0.15      | Integrated into platform               |

➡ Get **Phone #, email, other addresses** back.

---

### 3. **Upload to SMS Platform (Smarter Contact)**  
Import the skip-traced list and start:

- Mass SMS campaigns
- Auto-drip sequences
- Tagging & follow-up

---

### ⚡️ End Result:
From one 5,000-record land list, you may get:
- ~3,000 mobile numbers
- ~2,000 landlines
- ~1,000–2,500 valid SMS numbers
