### 🌱 What You Have:

---

### 💧 How to Regrow in Water (Hydro Start)

1. **Cut Base Flat:** Trim the bottom of the stalk clean and flat.
2. **Submerge the Base:** Place 1 inch of the stalk in a glass with clean, filtered water.
3. **Keep It Lit:** Place it in a **sunny window** (6–8 hours of indirect light).
4. **Change Water Daily:** Prevents mold and root rot.
5. **Watch for Growth:** Tiny roots or shoots may emerge from the lower nodes or side buds.
6. **Transition to Soil (Optional):** Once roots form, transplant to a small pot with rich organic soil to continue growth more robustly.
