Below is a quick step-by-step process to find your home’s square footage data via Hennepin County’s online resources. If it’s not listed directly, you may need to check City of Bloomington records or contact the assessor’s office.

---

## 1. Hennepin County Property Lookup
1. Go to [Hennepin County Property Information](https://www16.co.hennepin.mn.us/pins/addrresult.jsp).  
2. Enter your address (or PIN if known).  
3. Click on the parcel result to see the property summary.  
   - **Note**: The summary often shows lot dimensions and some tax/valuation info, but *may* omit finished square footage.

## 2. View or Request the Property Record Card
1. Look for a link labeled “Property Tax” or “Valuation Notice.”  
2. If not shown, there may be an option to download/request the “Property Record Card” (an official PDF).  
3. The Record Card often includes:  
   - **Above-grade sq ft**  
   - **Basement sq ft (finished vs. unfinished)**  

## 3. Check City of Bloomington Building Permits
- Visit the [City of Bloomington’s permits/inspections page](https://www.bloomingtonmn.gov/permitcenter).  
- Search by address or ask about existing permits that might show added finished spaces, remodels, or expansions.

## 4. Call or Email the Assessor’s Office
- **Hennepin County Assessor’s Office**: (612) 348-3011  
- Explain that you need the official finished square footage or “gross living area” (GLA) for your parcel.
