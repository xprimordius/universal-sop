## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes — but there are specific rules.  

### 1. **Eligibility**
You must have a **diagnosed anxiety disorder** (like generalized anxiety, panic disorder, PTSD, etc.) that **substantially limits** major life activities. Normal stress or mild anxiety doesn’t qualify.

### 2. **Service Dog Criteria**
To be legally a *service dog* (under the ADA), the dog must:
- Be **individually trained** to perform specific tasks that directly help your anxiety (not just give comfort).  
  Examples:  
  - Interrupting panic attacks.  
  - Providing deep pressure therapy.  
  - Creating space in crowds.  
  - Reminding you to take medication.

### 3. **Training**
You can:
- **Self-train** the dog (legal under ADA), or  
- Hire a **professional trainer** or organization.

### 4. **Documentation**
- No federal certification or ID is legally required.  
- Landlords or airlines **can’t require “proof”**, only ask if it’s a service animal and what tasks it performs.

### 5. **Emotional Support Animal (ESA) vs Service Dog**
| Type | Requires Task Training? | Public Access Rights | Legal Coverage |
|------|--------------------------|----------------------|----------------|
| **Service Dog** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Full access (ADA) | ADA, FHA |
| **ESA** | ❌ No (comfort only) | 🚫 No public access | Housing (FHA) only |
