## 🤖 ChatGPT

**TLDR: Best Internet for Traveling – Ranked**

### 🥇 **Starlink Roam (Mobile RV plan)**  
- **Speed:** 50–250 Mbps  
- **Pros:** Works anywhere with sky view (remote + rural)  
- **Cons:** High upfront cost ($599+), $150/mo; bulky hardware  
- **Best For:** Rural/off-grid, national parks, no signal zones  

### 🥈 **T-Mobile Magenta MAX / Go5G Plus**  
- **Speed:** Up to 100+ Mbps (varies by location)  
- **Pros:** True unlimited data, includes hotspot (50GB+ high-speed)  
- **Cons:** Slower in rural/dead zones without towers  
- **Best For:** Cities, highways, well-covered areas  

### 🥉 **Verizon Unlimited + Jetpack / Inseego 5G hotspot**  
- **Speed:** Very fast in 5G UW zones; great fallback  
- **Pros:** Better rural coverage than T-Mobile  
- **Cons:** Hotspot limits (typically 50GB), expensive  

---

### ✅ Best Combo Strategy:
- **Urban Areas:** T-Mobile Go5G Plus (unlimited + hotspot)
- **Remote/Rural:** Starlink Roam  
- **Backup:** Verizon prepaid 5G hotspot for redundancy  

---

### Other Options (Less Optimal):
- **AT&T:** Similar to Verizon but with stricter hotspot caps  
- **Nomad Internet / RV Plans:** Often MVNOs (resell major networks) — mixed reviews, throttling issues  
- **Mobile routers (Peplink, MOFI):** Great for bonding multiple networks, but costly/setup-heavy  

---

### Final Rec:
If you're serious about working remotely while traveling full-time:
- **Starlink Roam + T-Mobile Go5G Plus** = best blend of **coverage**, **speed**, and **redundancy**.
