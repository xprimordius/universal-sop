## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here are some potential liabilities or concerns in this agreement that you should be aware of:

1. **Non-Refundable Payments**:
   - Clause 6 explicitly states that all services are non-refundable after a two-day "Cooling Off Period," and even deposits up to $2,000 are non-refundable. This leaves no recourse if services are not delivered as expected.

2. **Service Guarantee Disclaimer**:
   - The company provides services on a "best-effort" basis (Clause 4.1.1) and does not guarantee profits or specific outcomes. This limits their accountability if the results are unsatisfactory.

3. **Client’s Responsibility for Accuracy**:
   - Clause 1.2 places the onus on you to provide accurate and timely information, as the company's performance depends on it. Any inaccuracies could lead to subpar services, with limited recourse for you.

4. **Unilateral Termination Rights**:
   - The company can terminate the agreement unilaterally with 30 days' notice, while the client (you) can terminate anytime but forfeits any refunds (Clause 3.3).

5. **Limitation of Liability**:
   - Clause 7 significantly limits the company’s liability for damages, including direct and indirect losses, which might result in little to no compensation for substandard services or disputes.

6. **Dispute Resolution**:
   - Disputes must be resolved in Delaware courts, with Florida law governing the agreement (Clause 9). This could add complexity and cost if you are based in a different state.

7. **Force Majeure**:
   - The company is not liable for delays or failures to perform due to "Force Majeure" events, which includes a broad range of circumstances (Clause 8). This could delay deliverables without penalty.

8. **Scope of Services**:
   - The scope of services (Schedule 1) includes mentorship, community access, and pre-built TikTok shops. However, the quality and specifics of these deliverables are not detailed, which could lead to mismatched expectations.

9. **Payment Obligations**:
   - Payment must be made upfront, and invoices are due within five business days (Clause 2). Late payments can lead to termination without a refund.

### Recommendations:
- **Clarify Deliverables**: Seek a detailed breakdown of the services and their expected outcomes.
- **Negotiate Refund Terms**: Attempt to include partial refunds if services are not delivered as promised.
- **Legal Review**: Consult an attorney to assess whether the terms, especially limitations of liability and jurisdiction, align with your interests.
- **Document Accuracy**: Ensure all provided information is precise to avoid disputes over service quality. 

This agreement heavily favors the company, so due diligence and potential renegotiation of key terms are advisable.
