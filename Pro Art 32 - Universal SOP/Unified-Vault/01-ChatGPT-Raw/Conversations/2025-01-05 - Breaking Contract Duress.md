## 👤 You

Can I break a contract due to psychological duress if i was pressured to sign something to move forward when my money was already taken

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes, a contract may be voidable if it was signed under **psychological duress**. Duress occurs when a party is unlawfully pressured into signing a contract, such as through coercion or undue influence, depriving them of free will. For a valid claim of duress, you typically need to prove:

1. **Illegitimate Pressure or Threat**: You were pressured in a way that left you no reasonable alternative but to sign.
   
2. **Lack of Free Consent**: Your ability to make a voluntary decision was compromised by the pressure.

3. **Causal Link**: The pressure directly caused you to enter the contract.

### Steps to Take:
1. **Gather Evidence**: Document emails, messages, or verbal exchanges showing coercion or pressure.
2. **Send a Notice**: Inform the other party that you are disputing the contract based on duress.
3. **Seek Legal Counsel**: An attorney can help assess whether your claim meets the legal threshold for duress and guide you through voiding the contract or negotiating a resolution.
