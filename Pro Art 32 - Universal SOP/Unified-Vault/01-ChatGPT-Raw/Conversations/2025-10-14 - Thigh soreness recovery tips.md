## 🤖 ChatGPT

### Likely Causes  
1. **Delayed Onset Muscle Soreness (DOMS):** Microscopic muscle fiber damage from eccentric contractions (downhill, deceleration). Peaks 24–72 hrs post-run.  
2. **Dehydration/Electrolyte Loss:** Insufficient sodium, potassium, magnesium during the race.  
3. **Glycogen Depletion + Inflammation:** Energy stores burned out; inflammatory response drives stiffness.  
4. **Minor Muscle Strain:** Overuse or improper pacing form.

---

### Rapid Recovery Protocol  
**1. Immediate (First 24 hrs):**  
- **Cold-water immersion / ice bath:** 10–15 min to reduce inflammation.  
- **Rehydrate + electrolytes:** Aim for ~1 L/hour for first 4 hrs post-race; include sodium + magnesium.  
- **High-protein + carb meal:** Rebuild glycogen (3:1 carb:protein ratio).  

**2. Days 1–3:**  
- **Active recovery:** Gentle walking, cycling, or mobility work 30 min/day—keep blood flow.  
- **Compression:** Wear compression tights 6–8 hrs/day.  
- **Sleep:** 8–9 hrs minimum—most healing occurs here.  
- **Anti-inflammatory nutrition:** Turmeric, omega-3s, cherries, pineapple, ginger.  

**3. After Day 3:**  
- **Contrast showers:** Alternate hot/cold 1–2×/day.  
- **Massage or foam roll:** Flush residual lactic acid and adhesions.  
- **Stretch lightly post-heat:** Warm muscle first; no aggressive stretching cold.  

---

### Red-Flag Check (See Doctor If):  
- Sharp localized pain (possible tear).  
- Swelling, bruising, or warmth > 3 days.  
- Pain impairs walking > 5 days.

---

**Summary:**  
You’re feeling classic **DOMS from eccentric overload**. Hydrate, refuel, rest, and use gentle motion + cold therapy. Full recovery usually ≈ 5–7 days.
