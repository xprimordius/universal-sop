### **TRT (Testosterone Replacement Therapy)**
1. **Mechanism and Efficacy**: TRT involves directly supplementing testosterone, so it’s more efficient at achieving high levels, especially for those starting at a low baseline.

2. **Timeline for Effects**:
   - **Initial Benefits**: Users generally notice an increase in energy, libido, and mood within a few days to two weeks, with steady improvements in strength, muscle mass, and fat loss over 4–6 weeks.

3. **Maintenance and Monitoring**: Regular check-ins with a medical provider are needed for dose adjustments and to manage potential side effects. 

### **In Summary**
- **Tongkat Ali** may offer a moderate boost but likely won’t increase levels as dramatically or as quickly as TRT. Expect subtle improvements over several weeks, but achieving a substantial jump like 300 to 1000 ng/dL would be unusual.
- **TRT** delivers faster and more predictable results, making it the preferred option if hitting and maintaining high testosterone levels (near 1000 ng/dL) is the primary goal.
