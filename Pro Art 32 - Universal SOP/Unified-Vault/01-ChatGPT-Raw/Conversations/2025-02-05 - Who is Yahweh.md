## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yahweh is the personal name of God in the Hebrew Bible (Old Testament), specifically in Jewish and Christian traditions. The name is derived from the Tetragrammaton **YHWH (יהוה)**, which was revealed to Moses in Exodus 3:14 when God declared, **"I AM WHO I AM."** This name signifies God's eternal and self-existent nature.

In Jewish tradition, Yahweh is considered too sacred to be spoken aloud, so substitutes like **Adonai ("Lord")** or **HaShem ("The Name")** are used instead. In Christianity, Yahweh is often associated with God the Father, though some also link the name to Jesus Christ as part of the Trinity.

Yahweh is regarded as the Creator, the God of Israel, and the supreme being who governs all existence. The name emphasizes God's sovereignty, faithfulness, and covenant relationship with humanity.
