Can you play music in Tesla via the usb c

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes — you can play music in a Tesla via USB-C. Here’s how it works:  

- **File Types Supported:** Tesla supports common formats like MP3, AAC, FLAC, and WAV.  
- **Drive Setup:** Use a USB-C flash drive or SSD formatted to **exFAT** or **FAT32**. Create a folder named `Music` and put your files inside.  
- **Port to Use:** The **front USB-C ports** (in the center console) handle both **data + charging**. Rear USB-C ports are **charging only** — they won’t play music.  
- **Accessing Music:** Plug in your drive, then on the Tesla screen, go to **Media → USB** to browse and play.  

⚡ Tip: If you also use USB storage for Sentry/Dashcam, you’ll need either **separate partitions** or a **multi-partition setup** (one for Dashcam, one for Music).

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Directly **streaming music live from your phone over USB-C in a Tesla does *not*** work — the USB-C ports don’t handle live audio input from phones (they’re for charging or storage devices like flash drives/SSDs).  

If you want to play music from your phone live:  
- **Bluetooth:** Standard way, pairs instantly, streams anything from your phone.  
- **Spotify / Apple Music / Tidal apps (built-in):** If your Tesla has Premium Connectivity or Wi-Fi, you can stream straight from the car without your phone.  
- **USB-C (workaround):** Only works if the phone is acting as a **mass storage device** with pre-downloaded files (rare on iOS/Android now). Not live streaming.  
- **No CarPlay / Android Auto:** Tesla doesn’t support those natively.  

👉 So: **live music from your phone → Bluetooth only.**
