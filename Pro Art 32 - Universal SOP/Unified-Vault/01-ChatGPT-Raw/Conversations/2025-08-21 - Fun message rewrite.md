### How to “Bring Ahi Back to Life” After Grocery Store Purchase  
**1. Trim & Pat Dry**  
- Cut off any browned/oxidized edges.  
- Pat with paper towel to remove excess moisture.  

**2. Quick Acid Rinse (Chef Trick)**  
- Lightly splash with **lemon or lime juice** (or a quick dip in rice vinegar + cold water).  
- Brightens flavor, removes any fishy note.  

**3. Ice Bath Refresh**  
- A **2–3 minute dunk** in ice water firms texture and wakes it up.  
- Pat dry immediately.  

**4. Marinade / Season**  
- **For poke:** soy sauce + sesame oil + green onion + sesame seeds.  
- **For sashimi:** brush lightly with ponzu, or drizzle sesame oil + Maldon salt.  

**5. Serve Cold & Fresh**  
- Keep on ice or chilled plate until eating.  
- Pair with crisp sides (cucumber, pickled ginger) to enhance freshness.
