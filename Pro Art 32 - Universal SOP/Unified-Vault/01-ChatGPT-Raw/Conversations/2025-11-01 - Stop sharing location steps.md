## 👤 You

If you delete someone from fine my iPhone can they see your location

---

> [!note]- Reasoning recap
> Thought for 8s


---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Short answer — No. Deleting their **contact** doesn’t stop them from seeing you. You must **stop sharing** or remove their access.

### Quick checklist (do this now)
1. **Stop sharing** — Open **Find My → People → Select person → Stop Sharing Location**.  
2. **Remove from Family** (if applicable) — **Settings → [your name] → Family Sharing → Select person → Remove**.  
3. **Change Apple ID password + enable 2FA** — **Settings → [your name] → Password & Security → Change Password**.  
4. **Sign out suspicious devices** — **Settings → [your name] → Scroll to device list → Remove any unknown devices**.  
5. **Turn off Location Sharing entirely** (if needed) — **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Share My Location → toggle off**.

### Extra security note
If they have your Apple ID/password or physical access to your device, they can still track you — change your password and revoke devices. Deleting their contact alone is not enough.

## 👤 You

If I select person and delete can they still see location

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

If you **tap the person in Find My → Delete** (or “Remove [Name]”), that **only removes them from your list** — it does **not** automatically stop location sharing.  

✅ **To fully stop them from seeing your location:**  
1. Open **Find My → People tab.**  
2. Tap their name.  
3. Tap **Stop Sharing My Location** (or **Remove Friend** if that’s the option).  

Only once you see they’re **no longer listed under “People Who Can See Your Location”** are they fully blocked from viewing it.  

**Summary:**  
- “Delete” = just removes them from your list.  
- “Stop Sharing My Location” = actually cuts off their access.
