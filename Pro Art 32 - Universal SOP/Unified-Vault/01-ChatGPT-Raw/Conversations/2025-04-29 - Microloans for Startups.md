  → Proof of landlord permissions/sublease clauses.  
  → Startup costs breakdown (security deposits, furnishing, marketing).  
  → Conservative cash flow projections (cover 6–12 months).  
- **Personal Credit Score:** 640+ recommended (some leniency depending on lender).
- **Collateral:** Small assets (savings, equipment, furniture, personal guarantee) often needed.

**3. Find the Right Intermediary Lender:**  
- SBA microloans are not direct from SBA; **local nonprofits and CDFIs** issue them.  
- Search here: [SBA Microloan Intermediaries](https://www.sba.gov/funding-programs/loans/lender-match).

**4. Application Strategy:**  
- Highlight **low-risk nature**: month-to-month leases, multiple units diversification.  
- Show **buffer reserves**: cash to cover rent if slow months occur.  
- Demonstrate **demand proof**: Airbnb occupancy rates, comps in the target city.  
- Be upfront: use funds for **furnishing, deposits, marketing**, **NOT** property ownership.

**5. Pre-approval Checklist:**  
- Business entity (LLC recommended).  
- EIN number.  
- Business checking account.  
- Lease or LOI (Letter of Intent) samples ready.  
- Business insurance quote (optional but strengthens).
