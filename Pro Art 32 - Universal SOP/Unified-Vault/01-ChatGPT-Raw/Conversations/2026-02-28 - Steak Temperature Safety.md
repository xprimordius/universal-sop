## 👤 You

Is pulling off a steak and measuring it after internally at 117 safe

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Short Answer: Yes — if done correctly.**

Pulling a steak at **117°F internal** can be safe **depending on final temp and rest.**

### 🔥 What Matters

1. **Target Final Temp (after rest)**
   - Steak will rise **5–10°F** while resting.
   - 117°F pull → ~122–127°F finish  
   - That’s **rare to low-medium-rare**.

2. **Safety Standard (Whole Cuts)**
   - Per **USDA**: whole cuts of beef are safe at **145°F with 3-min rest**.
   - However, intact muscle steak (not ground) is generally considered safe at lower temps because bacteria are on the surface and are killed by searing.

3. **Critical Requirement**
   - You must have:
     - **Hard sear on all sides**
     - No internal needle tenderizing
     - Fresh, properly stored meat

### 🎯 Practical Reality
Restaurants commonly serve rare steak at **120–125°F final**.  
Pulling at **117°F** and resting properly is normal for rare.

### 🚫 Not Safe If:
- It’s mechanically tenderized
- It’s ground beef
- It wasn’t properly stored
- Surface wasn’t fully seared

### Bottom Line
**Yes — safe for a whole, intact steak with proper sear and rest.**  
Expect rare. If you want safer margin → pull at 120°F.
