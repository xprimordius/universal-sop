# Combine 2.4 and 5GHz

## 🤖 ChatGPT

To combine the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks into a single network name (SSID) on your Comcast Business Gateway, you can follow these steps:

1. **Access the Gateway Admin Tool:**
   - Open a web browser and enter `10.1.10.1` in the address bar.
   - Log in with the default username and password:
     - **Username:** `cusadmin`
     - **Password:** `highspeed` (or your customized password if you have changed it).

2. **Navigate to WiFi Settings:**
   - Once logged in, go to the **Connection** menu on the left sidebar.
   - Select **WiFi** under the Connection section.

3. **Configure WiFi Networks:**
   - In the WiFi settings, you will see options for the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks.
   - To combine these networks, you need to give both networks the same **SSID** (network name) and the same **Password**.

4. **Set the Same SSID and Password:**
   - For both the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands, ensure that:
     - **SSID (Network Name):** Set the same name for both the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands.
     - **Password:** Use the same password for both bands.
     - **Security Mode:** Ensure both bands have the same security settings, typically WPA2.

5. **Save Your Settings:**
   - After making these changes, click the **Save** button to apply the settings.

6. **Reboot the Gateway:**
   - It’s a good idea to reboot your gateway after making these changes to ensure the settings take effect.

By combining the SSIDs and passwords, your devices will automatically connect to the best available band (2.4 GHz or 5 GHz) depending on their compatibility and the signal strength.

If you encounter any issues, you may need to adjust the settings for specific devices or disable band steering if it's causing connectivity problems.
