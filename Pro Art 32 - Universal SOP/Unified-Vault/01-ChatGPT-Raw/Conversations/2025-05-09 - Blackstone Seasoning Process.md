## 👤 You

How long to keep Blackstone on while initial seasoning with conditioner

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

For initial seasoning of a Blackstone griddle using their branded conditioner:

1. **Apply thin layer** of Blackstone Griddle Conditioner after heating the surface.
2. **Leave it on high heat** until it **stops smoking** — this takes about **15–30 minutes** per layer.
3. Repeat the process **3–5 times** for proper seasoning.

**Key:** Do not wipe off between rounds. Let each layer polymerize (burn in) completely before reapplying.

## 👤 You

I only waited 15 mins in between is that okay

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes, 15 minutes is generally fine **if the smoke had fully stopped** before applying the next layer. The key is **waiting until all smoking stops**, meaning the oil has fully polymerized. If it was still smoking, the layer may not have set properly, which can lead to uneven seasoning.

**What to do now:**  
- If your surface looks dark and even: you're good.  
- If it’s sticky, patchy, or flaking: reheat it on high until it stops smoking, then apply another thin coat and repeat properly.
