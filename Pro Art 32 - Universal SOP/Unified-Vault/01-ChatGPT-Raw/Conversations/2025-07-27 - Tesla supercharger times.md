### 🔋 Tesla Supercharger Charge Times – What’s Normal?

**Yes**, Tesla Superchargers **can** charge up to 80% in **15–25 minutes**—**under optimal conditions.**  
However, the **charge time shown in your route planner (35–70 minutes)** is **not abnormal** and depends on the following:

---

### ⚙️ Why It Shows Longer Times:

1. **Battery State of Charge (SoC):**  
   If you're arriving with a low battery, Tesla will plan **longer charges** to ensure you reach the next stop safely.

2. **Charger Speed (V2 vs. V3):**  
   - **V2 Superchargers:** Max 150 kW  
   - **V3 Superchargers:** Max 250 kW  
   Most rural or older stations are **V2** → Slower = longer charge times.

3. **Shared Stalls = Slower Speeds:**  
   If you're sharing a charger pair (e.g. 2A + 2B), you’ll **split power** and charge slower.

4. **Battery Preconditioning:**  
   Tesla will try to warm up the battery en route to superchargers. If it doesn't complete, charging will be slower.

5. **Trip Planning = Conservative:**  
   Tesla often **overestimates charge times** for safety and to avoid range anxiety.

---

### 🧠 TL;DR:

- 15–25 min = best case (V3, warm battery, low SoC)
- 35–70 min = common on long trips (cold battery, shared chargers, conservative planning)
- You **can skip early** if you're confident in the next leg and battery level.
