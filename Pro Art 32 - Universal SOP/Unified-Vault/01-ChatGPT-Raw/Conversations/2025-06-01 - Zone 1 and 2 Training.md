## 👤 You

What is zone 1 and zone 2 trainjng

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Zone 1 and Zone 2 training** refer to heart rate (or effort-based) zones used in endurance training to optimize aerobic development and recovery.

---

### **Zone 1 (Active Recovery)**
- **Effort:** Very easy
- **Heart Rate:** ~50–60% of max HR
- **Feel:** You can easily hold a conversation
- **Purpose:** Recovery, circulation, joint mobility  
- **Usage:** Between hard workouts, cooldowns

---

### **Zone 2 (Aerobic Base)**
- **Effort:** Easy, steady
- **Heart Rate:** ~60–70% of max HR
- **Feel:** Breathing deeper but still conversational
- **Purpose:** Builds aerobic capacity, fat metabolism, endurance  
- **Usage:** Foundation for runners, cyclists, endurance athletes; most efficient zone for long-term cardiovascular gains

---

**Summary:**
- **Zone 1 = recovery**
- **Zone 2 = endurance/aerobic base-building**

Train primarily in **Zone 2** for longevity, fat adaptation, and endurance; use **Zone 1** for recovery days.
