## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here’s how to **see the original FPS (frames per second)** of a video clip in **CapCut**:  

---

### **🧭 Option 1: Check via Clip Info (Desktop / PC)**
1. Import your clip into the **timeline**.  
2. **Right-click** the video clip → select **"Properties"** or **"Information"**.  
3. A small panel will appear showing:
   - **Resolution (e.g. 1920×1080)**  
   - **Frame rate (e.g. 29.97 fps or 60 fps)** ← this is your original FPS.  

---

### **📱 Option 2: Check FPS on CapCut Mobile**
CapCut mobile doesn’t directly display clip FPS, so do this instead:
1. Tap the **project settings (gear icon)** in the top-right.  
2. Look for the **current project FPS** (usually defaults to 30).  
3. To check the original clip’s FPS, **export** or **check file details**:
   - Go to your **phone’s gallery → video file → Details / Info**.  
   - It will list “Frame rate” (e.g. 60 fps).

---

### **💡 Pro Tip (Verification)**
If you want to confirm CapCut didn’t automatically change the FPS:
- In **PC version**, go to the top-right → **Project Settings → Frame Rate** → choose **“Match Source”** to ensure it stays at the clip’s original FPS.
