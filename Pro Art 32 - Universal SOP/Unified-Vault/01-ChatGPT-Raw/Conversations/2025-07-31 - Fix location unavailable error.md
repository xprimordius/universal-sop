The message **"Share My Location Unavailable – Unable to connect to server. Please try again later."** on your iPhone's *Find My* screen means your device is unable to connect to Apple's servers. Here's how to fix it:

### **1. Check network connection**
- Make sure Wi-Fi or cellular data is working.  
- Toggle **Airplane mode ON and OFF**.  
- Try switching between Wi-Fi and cellular.

### **2. Restart device**
- Restart your iPhone to reset connections.

### **3. Verify Apple ID**
- Go to **Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Find My iPhone** and ensure you're signed in.  
- Sign out of Apple ID and sign back in if necessary.

### **4. Update iOS**
- Go to **Settings → General → Software Update** and install the latest version.

### **5. Check Apple System Status**
- Apple's servers might be down. Visit [Apple System Status](https://www.apple.com/support/systemstatus/) and confirm "Find My" is available.

### **6. Reset Location & Privacy settings**
- Go to **Settings → General → Reset → Reset Location & Privacy** (you'll need to re-enable permissions after).
