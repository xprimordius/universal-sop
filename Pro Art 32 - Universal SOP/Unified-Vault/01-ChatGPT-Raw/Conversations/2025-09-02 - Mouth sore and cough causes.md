What you’re describing — sore in mouth/gums + throat tickle/cough — isn’t from the rice/quinoa or Icelandic water alone. Likely causes:

### 1. Oral Irritation / Canker Sore
- Mouth sores can be triggered by **minor trauma** (biting cheek, sharp food edge, toothbrush abrasion).  
- Nutrient limitations from such a restrictive diet (missing B12, folate, zinc, iron, vitamin C) can make mouth tissue more fragile.

### 2. Post-Nasal Drip / Early Viral Infection
- A “tickle” cough in the back of the throat often comes from **mucus drip** during the start of a cold or allergy flare.  
- Your sore could be coincidental, starting around the same time as mild viral symptoms.

### 3. Acid or Oral Microbiome Shifts
- A very plain carb-heavy diet (just rice/quinoa) may alter your oral flora and salivary pH → higher irritation risk.  
- Low dietary variety can lower immune resilience.

### 4. Other Triggers
- Stress, fatigue, or recent immune dips can cause both mouth ulcers and cough onset.  
- Less likely but possible: mild allergic reaction to storage residues or dust on grains.
