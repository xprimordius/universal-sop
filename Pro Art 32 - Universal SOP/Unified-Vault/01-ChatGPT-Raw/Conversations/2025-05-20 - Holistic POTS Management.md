---

### 🧠💓 What is Dysautonomia / POTS, Simply?
Your **autonomic nervous system (ANS)** runs things automatically — like heart rate, blood pressure, digestion, breathing.

With **POTS**, the system glitches.  
When you **stand up**, your body **fails to push enough blood to your brain**, so your heart **overcompensates** (racing heartbeat), and you feel dizzy, tired, nauseous, or even faint.

---

### 🧩 Core Problems Behind It — In Simple Terms

#### 1. **Blood Pooling in Your Legs**
- Your blood falls to your feet when you stand up  
- Your body **can’t push it back up fast enough**  
- Result: brain lacks oxygen → dizziness, rapid heart rate, fatigue

Why?  
- Veins are too stretchy (common in people with hypermobility or EDS)  
- Muscles that help pump blood (calf muscles) are weak  
- Nerves that tell your vessels to squeeze aren't working right

---

#### 2. **Nervous System on Edge**
- Your body thinks it’s in **constant fight-or-flight**  
- Nerves get overstimulated → heart races, blood vessels don’t respond properly  
- Small nerves that manage blood pressure and temperature **may be damaged or inflamed**

---

#### 3. **Salt & Water Imbalance**
- You’re **low on sodium or fluids**, so you don’t have enough **blood volume**  
- Less volume = less pressure to circulate blood  
- Like trying to water a garden with a half-full hose

---

#### 4. **Hidden Triggers & Root Causes**
Something **underneath** is often causing the dysfunction:
- **Viral leftovers** (e.g., long COVID, EBV) confuse your immune system  
- **Mast cells** release histamine and cause weird allergic-like reactions  
- **Toxic buildup** from mold or poor detox  
- **Gut issues** make it harder to absorb nutrients for nerve and blood pressure regulation  
- **Hormone imbalances** or adrenal fatigue weaken your ability to stabilize under stress

---

### 🧪 Simple Analogy

> Imagine your body is a house:  
> - The **plumbing** (blood vessels) is too loose  
> - The **electric system** (nerves) is glitchy and overfiring  
> - The **thermostat** (brain-ANS) can’t regulate the temperature (heart rate, BP)  
> - The **basement** (gut/immune system) has mold, leaks, or wiring issues

---

### ✅ What Needs to Be Fixed?
1. **Restore fluid & salt levels** (add water + electrolytes)
2. **Re-train your nervous system** to calm down and regulate
3. **Strengthen your blood vessels & muscle pump**
4. **Fix hidden root causes** like infections, toxins, gut damage, or trauma
5. **Support your body with nutrients** it’s missing

**Top Shen Tonics:**

**Practices:**

- Reflective journaling or poetry  
- Connecting with beauty (music, art, nature) to nourish spirit
