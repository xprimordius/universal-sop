## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Somnambulism**, more commonly known as **sleepwalking**, is a sleep disorder where individuals perform various activities while in a state of partial consciousness, typically during the deeper stages of non-REM sleep. Here are key aspects:

1. **Behavior**: People with somnambulism may walk around, perform complex tasks, or even leave the house, with little to no memory of these actions upon waking.

2. **Causes**: It’s often linked to genetics, stress, sleep deprivation, alcohol, certain medications, and underlying conditions like sleep apnea or epilepsy.

3. **Age Factor**: More common in children and often fades as they reach adulthood, though adults can experience it too.

4. **Risks**: Injuries can occur since the individual isn’t fully aware of their environment. It's recommended to keep the sleepwalker safe by ensuring doors are locked and hazardous objects are out of reach.

5. **Treatment**: Generally, improving sleep hygiene and reducing stress helps manage it. In more severe cases, therapy or medication may be required.

If the sleepwalking behavior is frequent, disruptive, or potentially dangerous, consulting a sleep specialist can help in managing the condition effectively.
