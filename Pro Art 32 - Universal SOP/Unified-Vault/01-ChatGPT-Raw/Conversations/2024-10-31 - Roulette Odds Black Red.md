In roulette, the **double-zero (00)** and **triple-zero (000)** wheels are variations that affect the game’s house edge and player odds:

1. **Double-Zero (00) Roulette (American Roulette):**
   - This wheel has **38 pockets**: numbers 1 to 36, a single zero (0), and a double zero (00).
   - The addition of the 00 pocket gives the house a **higher edge** of about **5.26%** compared to single-zero European roulette, where the edge is closer to 2.7%.
   - Common in American casinos, the 00 increases the likelihood that a bet on red/black, odd/even, or other even-money bets will lose.

2. **Triple-Zero (000) Roulette:**
   - This wheel has **39 pockets**: numbers 1 to 36, plus the 0, 00, and an additional 000.
   - Adding a third zero increases the house edge further to **7.69%**, making it the least favorable version for players.
   - Triple-zero tables are mostly seen in Las Vegas, especially in high-traffic areas where casino operators take advantage of the added edge to maximize profits.

Choosing between these options, players benefit most from tables with fewer zeros, as each zero reduces the odds of winning even-money bets by creating extra losing outcomes【21†source】【22†source】【23†source】.
