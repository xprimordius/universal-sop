- **Very safe**: CBX is modern, secure, has U.S. and Mexican customs/immigration built in, cameras, lighting, and staff 24/7.  
- It’s used by families, tourists, and business travelers daily.  

---

## 🔹 Walkability
- You can **park at CBX lots** (about $15/day) and **walk across the enclosed bridge** directly into the airport.  
- But you **cannot** use CBX just to enter Tijuana city or walk to downtown — it only connects into TIJ airport.  

---

✅ **If your plan is to fly out of Tijuana Airport:** CBX is the safest, most convenient option.  
❌ **If your plan is to walk into Tijuana without flying:** CBX won’t work — you’ll need **San Ysidro (PedEast/PedWest)** or **Otay Mesa pedestrian crossing** instead.
