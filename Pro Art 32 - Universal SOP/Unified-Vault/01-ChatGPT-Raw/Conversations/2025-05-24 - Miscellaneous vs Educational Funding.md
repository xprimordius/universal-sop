## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Miscellaneous Services** is **generally better** for business funding compared to **Educational Services**, and here’s why:

---

### 🔹 **Miscellaneous Services**
- **Broad + Flexible Classification**: Banks/underwriters often see this as lower risk and more adaptable.  
- **Easier to Justify Revenue**: Can be applied to consulting, admin support, personal services, etc.  
- **Fewer Regulatory Barriers**: No special licensing or compliance usually needed.  
- **Low Perceived Risk**: Especially if not medical, financial, or legal in nature.

---

### 🔹 **Educational Services**
- **Higher Scrutiny**: Can be flagged for risk due to refund rates, regulatory oversight (e.g., tutoring, online courses).
- **Tied to Performance Claims**: Banks may ask for curriculum, outcomes, or refund policies.
- **Longer Approval Path**: Might need documentation or proof of legitimacy if offering training or coaching.

---

### ✅ **Verdict**:  
Use **Miscellaneous Services** unless your model is clearly education-focused and has clean documentation, licensing (if required), and proven performance.

**Pro tip**: When in doubt, file under **Business Support Services** or **Administrative Consulting** to stay generic and fundable.
