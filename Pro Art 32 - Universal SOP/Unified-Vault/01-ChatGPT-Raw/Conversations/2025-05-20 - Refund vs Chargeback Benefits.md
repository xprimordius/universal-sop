### 🧠 **Incentive for Merchant to Refund Instead of Letting Chargeback Happen:**

- **Preserve merchant account (prevent termination/blacklisting)**
- **Maintain lower dispute ratios (staying below 1% is crucial)**
- **Prevent being labeled “high risk”**
- **Retain customer goodwill if caught early**

### TL;DR:
Merchants are *highly incentivized* to issue refunds proactively to avoid the financial and reputational damage of chargebacks.
