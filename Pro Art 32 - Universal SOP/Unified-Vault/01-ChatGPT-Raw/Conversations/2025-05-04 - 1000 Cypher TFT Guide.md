---

#### **1. Choose Cypher Augment Early (Ideally 2-1 or 3-2)**
- **Look for:** “Cypher” augment — it allows you to gain **Intel** through losses and kills.
- If it's not offered early, **restart** or **pivot** strategies.

---

#### **2. Initiate a Lose-Streak Strategy**
- **Lose on purpose** (but don’t open-fort). Place weak units in smart formation to:
  - **Get kills** (more Intel per kill)
  - Maintain a **5-loss streak** for bonus gold and Intel

---

#### **3. Maintain Healthy Economy**
- **Always stay above 50 gold** for maximum interest.
- **Don’t level up or reroll** early — you’re playing for loss streak + Intel farm.
- Buy **key Cypher units** on bench (e.g. Qiyana, Bard, Twitch).

---

#### **4. Prioritize Kill Potential**
- Use early-game units that can **snipe backline** or get at least **1-2 kills per loss** (e.g. Kled, Ziggs, Twitch).
- Items: Stack **damage** on early carry like Ziggs or Twitch.

---

#### **5. Delay Cashout Until 1000 Intel**
