What you’re seeing is **fabric pilling** — tiny fiber balls forming when the outer knit rubs against friction. It’s not dirt or damage at first; it’s loose microfibers tangling together.

This is common on soft, stretchy knit covers like the one on the **entity["company","Tempur-Pedic","mattress company"] Adapt Pro Hi**, especially if:

- the pillow rubs against sheets/headboard/walls
- facial stubble or rough skin brushes it
- dry skin flakes increase friction
- you twist or compress it heavily while sleeping
- washing agitation was too strong

Pulling pills is what causes the damage — you’re yanking live fibers out of the knit structure.

### How to fix it safely

**Do NOT pull them.**

Best options:

✅ Fabric shaver / depiller  
Gently shave pills off — removes only loose fibers  
(looks scary, but it’s safe when done lightly)

✅ Fine scissors  
Snip pills flush with the surface

✅ Lint roller (light pressure)  
Removes loose fuzz before it pills

Avoid razors — they cut structural threads.

### How to prevent more pilling

- Wash inside-out on **gentle / cold**
- Air dry or tumble low
- No fabric softener (weakens fibers)
- Avoid rough sheets or textured blankets
- Keep pillow from rubbing against hard surfaces

The cover isn’t “ruined” yet — pilling is cosmetic. But if you keep pulling fibers, it *will* thin and stretch.

If you want to preserve the original casing long-term without hiding it:

👉 Use a **smooth silk or sateen pillowcase only when sleeping**, remove it during the day. That protects the knit from friction while keeping the original look intact.

## 👤 You

What is piling

## 🤖 ChatGPT

image_group{"aspect_ratio":"1:1","query":["fabric pilling close up sweater lint balls","pilling on cotton fabric macro","fabric pills lint balls extreme close up","textile pilling before after surface"],"num_per_query":1}

**Pilling** is when fabric grows tiny lint balls on the surface.

It happens when:

1. fibers loosen from the weave  
2. friction rubs them together  
3. they tangle into little knots  
4. the knots stay stuck instead of falling off

So those fuzzy spheres you see are literally **broken fibers trapped in a ball**.

It’s cosmetic at first, but if you pull them:

👉 you rip out healthy threads  
👉 fabric thins  
👉 holes eventually form

Soft stretchy knits pill more because they use short fibers.  
Silk pills far less because its fibers are long and smooth.

In simple terms:

> **pilling = friction damage showing on the surface**
