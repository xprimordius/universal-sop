**Short answer:**

1. **Sibling Sponsorship (Green Card)**:  
   A U.S. citizen (not green card holder) can petition for a sibling **only if**:
   - They are **21 or older**
   - The sibling is **outside the U.S.** or in legal status  
   - Wait time: **15+ years** (varies by country)

   **Siblings of green card holders cannot sponsor.**

2. **If the sibling is undocumented or in a “blackout” status** (e.g., pending asylum or removal):  
   - **Sponsorship alone won’t protect them.**
   - They may **not adjust status** unless:
     - They qualify for **245(i)** grandfathering (rare)
     - They **entered legally** and can **adjust via another route**
     - They get **asylum, U visa, T visa, or SIJS** protection

3. **If under government protection** (asylum, U visa, etc.):  
   - They may eventually apply for a green card and **later** sponsor siblings (if they become citizens)  
   - But **protection does not extend to siblings automatically**

**Conclusion:**
