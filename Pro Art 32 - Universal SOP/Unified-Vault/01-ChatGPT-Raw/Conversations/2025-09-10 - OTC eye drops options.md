In the U.S., **antibiotic eye drops are prescription only**. You can’t buy them “over the counter” (OTC).  

**OTC options you *can* get for eye irritation/infection symptoms:**  
- **Lubricating/artificial tears** – soothe dryness and flush irritants.  
- **Antihistamine drops** (e.g., ketotifen/Zaditor) – for allergic eye symptoms.  
- **Redness relievers** (e.g., naphazoline) – short-term only, not for infection.  

**For bacterial conjunctivitis (pink eye) or a true infection:**  
- Requires prescription antibiotic drops such as **moxifloxacin, ofloxacin, or erythromycin ointment**.  
- You’d need to see a doctor, urgent care, or use a telehealth service to get these prescribed.  

⚠️ **Caution:** Many eye infections look similar (viral, bacterial, allergic, fungal). Using the wrong treatment can delay healing or worsen it. If you have pain, discharge, light sensitivity, or vision changes, get checked quickly.
