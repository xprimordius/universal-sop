4. **Fraudulent Misrepresentation (if applicable)**

This included false claims of affiliation with state programs and utility companies, misleading homeowners about the financial terms of [Product/Service].

5. **Housing Violations (if applicable)**

   As a direct result of [Company Name]’s actions, I have suffered considerable emotional distress, requiring therapy, and have incurred significant financial hardship, including increased debt and damage to my credit.

9. **Intent to Settle in Good Faith**  
   I am submitting this affidavit in good faith with the intention of resolving these matters amicably and avoiding the need for formal legal action. However, I reserve the right to pursue all available legal remedies in the United States District Court for the District of Utah or Minnesota, as per the terms of the contract, if this settlement is not reached within the specified timeframe.

10. **Conclusion**  
    I affirm that the contents of this affidavit are accurate and truthful to the best of my knowledge and belief. I sincerely hope that [Company Name] will recognize the validity of these claims and settle this matter swiftly to avoid further legal complications.
