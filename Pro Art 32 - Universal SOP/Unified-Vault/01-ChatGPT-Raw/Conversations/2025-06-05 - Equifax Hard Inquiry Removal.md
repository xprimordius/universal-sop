## 🤖 ChatGPT

**"605B"** refers to **Section 605(b) of the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA)**. It's used to dispute **unauthorized or fraudulent hard inquiries** on your credit report.

### 🔒 **What 605B Does:**
It requires credit bureaus (like Equifax) to **block and remove any information resulting from identity theft** within **4 business days** *if valid proof is submitted.*

---

### ✅ **To Use 605B Effectively (for hard inquiry removal):**
You must submit:

1. **Identity Theft Report**  
   – Usually an **FTC affidavit** from [identitytheft.gov](https://www.identitytheft.gov) or a **police report**.

2. **Proof of Identity**  
   – Government ID + utility bill, lease, or bank statement.

3. **Letter requesting removal under FCRA Section 605B**  
   – Specifically cite the inquiries you did *not* authorize.

4. **Send to Equifax:**
   - Upload via Equifax portal, OR  
   - Call 1-888-378-4329 (can sometimes get faster results), OR  
   - Mail to:  
     **Equifax Information Services LLC**  
     **P.O. Box 740256**  
     **Atlanta, GA 30374-0256**

---

### ⚠️ Important:
- Only **unauthorized** inquiries qualify.  
- Don’t misuse 605B for inquiries you knowingly initiated (that’s considered fraud).
