### 1. **Quantum Wave Function Collapse (Literal Physics)**  
**Context:** Quantum Mechanics  

- A particle (like an electron) exists in a *superposition* — many possible states (positions, energies, etc.) at once.  
- The *wave function* represents all those probabilities.
- When **observed** (measured), the wave function *collapses* — it "chooses" one outcome.
  
**Example:**  
An electron is *everywhere* on a ring. You measure it — suddenly, it's in one exact spot. The other possibilities vanish.

> **Key Concept:** Observation or measurement forces reality to "decide."
