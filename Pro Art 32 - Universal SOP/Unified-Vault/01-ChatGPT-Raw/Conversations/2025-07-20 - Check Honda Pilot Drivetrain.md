## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Quickest Ways to Tell if Your 2011 Honda Pilot EX-L Is 4x4 or 4x2:**

### 1. **Look for “4WD” Badge**
- **Rear of the vehicle (trunk/liftgate)** – If it says **“4WD”**, it’s 4x4 (all-wheel drive system).
- If no badge or it says **“2WD”**, it’s 4x2 (front-wheel drive).

---

### 2. **Check VIN (Vehicle Identification Number)**
- Go to [Honda VIN decoder](https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/decoder/) or use the Honda owner's site.
- Look for drivetrain in results:
  - **4WD = 4x4 (AWD system)**
  - **FWD = 4x2**

---

### 3. **Check Owner’s Manual or Window Sticker (if available)**
- The original Monroney sticker will list “Drivetrain: 4WD” or “FWD”.

---

### 4. **Under the Vehicle – Driveshaft Check**
- Crawl under and look:
  - **4x4:** Long driveshaft to rear wheels + rear differential
  - **4x2:** No driveshaft to rear wheels

---

### 5. **Dashboard / Center Console**
- **4x4 models** may have a **“VTM-4” button** (Variable Torque Management – AWD system) near the gear shifter or dash.
  - This button engages lock mode for rear wheels at low speeds.
- **FWD models** don’t have this.
