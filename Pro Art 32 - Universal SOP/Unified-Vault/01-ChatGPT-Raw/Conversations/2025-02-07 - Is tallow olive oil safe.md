## 🤖 ChatGPT

It depends on several factors:  

### **1. Breakdown by Ingredient**  
- **Tallow (Beef Fat):** Generally stable at room temperature, especially if rendered properly. However, exposure to air and moisture can lead to oxidation.  
- **Olive Oil:** More prone to oxidation, but generally stable at room temp for a while.  
- **Butter:** The most perishable of the three. If salted, it resists bacterial growth better. If unsalted, it's more susceptible to spoilage, especially if the environment was warm or humid.  

### **2. Key Considerations**  
- **Was it exposed to direct sunlight or heat?** If yes, there's a higher risk of oxidation and rancidity.  
- **Was it fully covered or partially open?** Partially open means more air exposure, increasing the risk of oxidation and contamination.  
- **Was the room cool or warm?** If above 70°F (21°C), butter and olive oil can degrade faster.  

### **3. Safety Check**  
- **Smell:** If it has an off or sour smell, discard it.  
- **Texture & Appearance:** If it looks separated, has mold, or a slimy texture, discard it.  
- **Taste (as a last resort):** If it tastes off, don’t consume it.  

### **Conclusion**  
If it was in a cool room and mostly covered, it's *likely* safe, but quality may have degraded. If it was in a warm environment, it’s best to discard it, especially due to the butter component.
