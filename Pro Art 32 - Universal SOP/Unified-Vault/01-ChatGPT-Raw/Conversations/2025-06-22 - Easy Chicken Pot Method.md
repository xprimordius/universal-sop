## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Fastest + Safest Way to Cook 4 lbs of Chicken in a Pot (Minimal Effort):**

### ✅ Method: **Boiled/Shredded Chicken (Pot Method)**  
**Best for meal prep, shredding, seasoning later.**

---

### 🔥 Steps:

1. **Place chicken in pot** (whole breasts or thighs, fresh or thawed).
2. **Cover with water or broth** — just enough to submerge chicken fully.
3. **Add salt** (1–2 tsp) + optional bay leaf, garlic, onion for flavor.
4. **Bring to a boil** on high heat (lid on).
5. **Once boiling, reduce to low** → cover → simmer gently for:
   - 🕒 **Breasts:** ~12–15 min  
   - 🕒 **Thighs/Legs:** ~20–25 min  
6. **Check internal temp** = ✅ **165°F (74°C)**
7. **Let rest 5–10 min**, then shred/slice/store.

---

### 💡 Benefits:
- Zero mess.
- No oil/smoke.
- Easy to batch cook and portion.
- Stays moist and easy to flavor post-cooking.
