🔥 **Creamy Protein Pasta with Pork Chops**  

**Quick recipe (20 min):**  
1. **Sear pork chops** — salt, pepper, garlic powder; cook in butter until golden. Remove.  
2. **Sauce base** — same pan → add a bit more butter + a spoon of cream cheese. Stir until melted and smooth.

5. **Optional** — top with herbs (parsley or basil), black pepper, and a touch of Parmesan if you have it.
