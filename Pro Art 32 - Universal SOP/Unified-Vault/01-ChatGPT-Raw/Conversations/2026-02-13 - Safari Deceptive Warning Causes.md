## Why Safari shows this warning

Safari uses **Google Safe Browsing + Apple heuristics**. A site is flagged when **one or more** of these are true:

### 1. **Domain reputation is weak or tainted**
Common causes:
- New domain or subdomain (`go.` subdomain is a red flag pattern)
- Recently repurposed domain
- Shared hosting with previously flagged sites
- Past phishing/malware association (even indirect)

👉 Very common with **ClickFunnels / GoHighLevel / tracking subdomains**

---

### 2. **Phishing-style patterns detected**
Even if the site is legit.

Triggers include:
- VSL + “thank you” + tracking-heavy URLs
- Long URLs with **fbclid, fbc_id, utm chains**
- Immediate redirects after ad click
- Email / phone / payment requests later in funnel
- Scarcity / income / finance language patterns

**Important:**  
This is *pattern-based*, not judgment-based.

---

### 3. **SSL / HTTPS trust issues**
Examples:
- Recently issued SSL cert
- Mismatch between root domain and subdomain
- Free or auto-renewed certs on aggressive funnels
- Mixed content (HTTP scripts inside HTTPS page)

Safari is **stricter than Chrome** here.

---

### 4. **Ad network spillover**
If:
- Other advertisers using the same funnel platform
- Similar templates
- Same redirect structure

…get flagged, **your domain can inherit risk**.

This is called **collateral reputation poisoning**.

---

### 5. **User reports**
Only a **few manual reports** can trigger a temporary block:
- “This looks like a scam”
- “This site asks for personal info”
- “This redirects unexpectedly”

Competitors *do* abuse this.

## Immediate fixes (high leverage)

### 1. **Move funnels off “go.” subdomains**
Use:
- `secure.yourdomain.com`
- `apply.yourdomain.com`
- or root domain paths

---

### 2. **Shorten URLs**
Strip unnecessary params on thank-you pages.
Keep tracking **server-side** where possible.

---

### 3. **Strengthen trust signals**
Add **above-the-fold**:
- Clear company name
- Privacy policy + terms (visible, not footer-only)
- Contact info
- No exaggerated income claims

---

### 4. **Check Google Safe Browsing status**
Submit review if clean:
https://transparencyreport.google.com/safe-browsing/search

Apple pulls from this.

---

### 5. **Separate ad click → VSL → capture**
Avoid:
Ad → immediate long tracking URL → VSL

Use a **clean intermediary page**.
