## 👤 You

Crispy salmon skin in toaster oven

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Quick Guide: Crispy Salmon Skin in Toaster Oven**

**1. Prep the Skin:**
- Pat salmon skin very dry (moisture = soggy).
- Optional: Sprinkle light salt (draws moisture).
- Let it air-dry 5–10 min more if time allows.

**2. Oil + Seasoning:**
- Light coat of neutral oil (avocado, olive).
- Season simply (salt, pepper) or add spices.

**3. Cooking:**
- Line toaster tray with parchment or foil.
- Lay skin flat, *skin side up*.
- Weight down with another tray if it curls.

**4. Temperature & Time:**
- **375°F to 400°F (190°C to 200°C).**
- Bake for **8–15 min**, depending on thickness.
- Watch closely—remove when golden and crisp.

**5. Finish:**
- Rest 2 min to finish crisping.
- Eat as chips or crumble onto dishes.

**Pro Tips:**
- Thicker skin → longer time.
- Thin, even layer = max crisp.
- Store leftovers open-air; reheating softens them.

Is old fat on tin foil from steak on aluminum fo from last week okay to recook on? It was just closed inside the oven

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**No—Do Not Reuse Old Fat on Foil.**

**Why:**
- Old fat oxidizes → rancid, toxic compounds.
- Sitting a week = bacterial risk, even if sealed inside the oven.
- Foil traps moisture → potential mold/microbial growth.

**Safe Move:**  
Toss the foil + fat. Start fresh.

**Rule:** Always discard old fats after cooking, especially after days or weeks.

**Better Practice:**  
Next time, store reusable fat separately in glass/jar in fridge if you want to save it intentionally.
