## 🤖 ChatGPT

No, **wild ducks should not eat this**.

The canned **cream-style sweet corn** in your image contains **added salt, sugar, and possibly dairy or thickeners**, which are **not safe for wild ducks**. Ducks have sensitive digestive systems, and processed human foods can:

- Disrupt their natural diet
- Cause malnutrition or digestive issues
- Lead to a condition called “angel wing” due to poor nutrition

**Best foods for wild ducks:**
- Cracked corn (plain, unseasoned)
- Oats
- Rice (cooked or uncooked, plain)
- Birdseed
- Chopped lettuce or greens
- Peas or corn (plain, defrosted frozen or fresh—not canned)

**Conclusion:** Do **not** feed them this canned corn. Stick to plain, natural foods.
