### 🧠 What It Means:
- **Support** = A price level where the market tends to stop falling and bounce back up (a *floor*).
- **Resistance** = A price level where the market tends to stop rising and fall back down (a *ceiling*).

### 🔧 How to Spot Them:
- Price **bounces 2–3 times** off the same level → Draw your line.
- Look for **obvious turning points** where price reverses with strength.

> ✅ **Pro Tip:** Don’t draw 100 lines. Focus only on the **levels closest to where price is right now**.

### ✅ When They Flip:
- If price **breaks above resistance**, that level often becomes **new support**.
- If price **breaks below support**, that level often becomes **new resistance**.

## 🔹 2. How to Draw Them (Clean, Smart & Confident)

### 🛠 Simple Drawing Rules:
- **Higher timeframes = stronger levels**

- **Use the candle bodies or wicks**, just be consistent.

> 🎯 **Best Practice:** Use a line chart first to see clean levels. Then switch back to candles.

## 🔹 5. Wyckoff Simplified — Understanding Market Phases

### 🧠 Market Moves in 3 Phases:
1. **Markdown** = Downtrend
2. **Accumulation** = Sideways range (buyers loading up)
3. **Markup** = Uptrend

(Then the cycle repeats in reverse: Markup → Distribution → Markdown)

### 👁 How to Trade It:
1. Spot a **sideways range** (price moves flat).
2. Wait for a **fakeout (liquidity sweep)** below support or above resistance.
3. Enter **after price breaks structure** (new higher high or lower low).
4. Place trade from a **demand/supply zone**.
5. Take profit at next zone left behind.

---

## 🧩 6. Final Execution Blueprint (All Strategies)

| Situation           | Entry Trigger                                          | Exit Plan                    |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------|
| **Support**        | 3 bounces + confirmation candle/MA                     | Take profit at next high     |
| **Resistance**     | 3 rejections + reversal signal                         | Take profit at next low      |
| **Trendline Touch**| Pullback to trendline + engulfing or structure break   | Next swing high/low          |
| **Breakout Trade** | Strong candle + higher high/low formed                 | Ride new trend               |
| **Wyckoff Setup**  | Sideways → sweep → break → enter on retest zone        | Next supply/demand zone      |

---

## 🔑 Beginner Quick Rules Recap:

- Start with **higher timeframes** → work your way down
- Draw **clean zones**, not messy lines
- Don’t guess: **Wait for confirmation**
- Be patient. **Only trade clean setups**
- Use **fewer, stronger lines** – not 20 random ones
- Always **protect with stop loss**, and know your **exit zone** ahead of time
