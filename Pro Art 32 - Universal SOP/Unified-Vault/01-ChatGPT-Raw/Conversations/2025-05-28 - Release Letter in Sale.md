## 👤 You

Release letter as part of a company sale mean

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

A **release letter** in the context of a **company sale** is a formal document used to:

### TLDR — **Purpose:**
It **releases a party (person or entity)** from **claims, obligations, or liabilities** related to the company being sold.

---

### Common Scenarios:

1. **Employee Release Letter:**
   - Confirms termination or resignation as part of the sale.
   - May waive future claims (e.g. severance, lawsuits).

2. **Founder/Shareholder Release:**
   - Waives the right to sue the company or buyer after sale.
   - May include release from prior liabilities or guarantees.

3. **Creditor/Lender Release:**
   - Releases claims on company assets (e.g. lien release).
   - Required to transfer a clean title to the buyer.

4. **General Liability Release:**
   - Protects buyer from inherited legal exposure from seller.

---

### Key Contents:
- Identity of releasing party.
- Description of released claims or obligations.
- Effective date (often closing date of sale).
- Governing law.

---

### Strategic Implication:
Buyers usually require these to ensure they’re acquiring the business free from hidden liabilities or disputes. It protects the deal integrity and reduces post-sale risk.
