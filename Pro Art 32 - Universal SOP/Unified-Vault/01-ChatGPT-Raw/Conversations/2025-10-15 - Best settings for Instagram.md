## 🤖 ChatGPT

For **Instagram**, the best setting depends on balancing **quality vs. file size and workflow efficiency** — since Instagram heavily compresses video after upload.  

### 🔹 Recommended Recording Format
**XF-HEVC S YCC420 10bit**

### 🔹 Why:
1. **10-bit depth** → preserves richer color gradation for color grading before export.  
2. **HEVC (H.265)** → modern codec that gives **smaller file sizes** with **higher quality** than AVC (H.264).  
3. **YCC420** → matches Instagram’s compression pipeline (it re-encodes to 8-bit 4:2:0 anyway).  
4. **Best balance** → high dynamic range, efficient storage, smooth editing workflow.  

### 🔹 When to Use RAW:
Only for cinematic projects where you’ll color grade deeply — not for quick Instagram clips. RAW is huge and overkill for social uploads.  

### 🔹 Workflow Summary:
- **Record:** XF-HEVC S YCC420 10bit  
- **Edit & Grade:** In Final Cut / Premiere  
- **Export for Instagram:**  
  - Codec: H.264  
  - Bitrate: ~20–30 Mbps  
  - Resolution: 4K or 1080p  
  - Frame rate: Match source (usually 24/30/60 fps)
