## 3) The **3R Virality Filter**
Before posting, ask:  
**Relatable:** “Do most people instantly *get* this and see themselves in it?”  
**Shareable:** “Can a viewer think: *This is so [friend’s name].*” (Big hashtags, big tribes.)  
**Repeatable:** “Can I run **50 videos** with this same spine?”

> **Ship only if it’s a YES to all three.**

---

## 4) Structure That Retains

**Skeleton (Shorts/Reels/TikTok):**  
1. **Hook (0–3s):** Big promise + curiosity + specificity.  
2. **Set Stakes (3–7s):** Why this matters / what we’re proving.  
3. **Tension Escalation (to ~90% mark):** Fast, punchy steps, pattern interrupts, open loops.  
4. **Final Payoff (last 5–10%):** Close the promise you opened.  
5. **Soft CTA:** “Comment ‘PLAYBOOK’ and I’ll DM the template.” (Or push to next video in the series.)

**Never** reveal the full payoff mid-video. You’ll nuke retention.

---

## 5) Editing Rules That 2–3x Retention
- **Cut every second you can.** No filler, no greetings, no “context chunks.”  
- **First frame = visual hook.** (On-screen result, bold text, transformation, shocking stat.)  
- **Subtitles:** High-contrast, key words emphasized, kinetic when possible.  
- **Jump cuts > zooms > punch-ins** to reset viewer attention.  
- **B-Roll that advances the story** (not wallpaper).  
- **Looming open loops** (“…but the real problem showed up at 0:14”).  
- **Music under  -14 LUFS** and never overpowering voice (optional, but helps pace).  
- **End on the payoff + tease the sequel.**

## 6) Analytics → Iteration Loop
**Post → Pull → Patch → Push.**

- **3s hold, 50% watch, full watch %, rewatches, shares/save rate.**

- **Title/Hook A/B**: Same body, two hooks. Kill the loser in 1 hour.
