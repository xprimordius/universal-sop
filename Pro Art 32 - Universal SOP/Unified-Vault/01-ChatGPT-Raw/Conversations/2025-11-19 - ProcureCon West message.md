## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Contingent staffing = temporary, non-permanent workers hired on demand.**

TLDR:

**Contingent staffing** is when a company brings in workers who are **not full-time employees**. They usually come through agencies or contracts and are used to **fill gaps, handle workload spikes, or add specialized skills** without long-term commitments.

**Includes:**
- Contractors  
- Temps  
- Freelancers  
- Consultants  
- Gig workers  

**Why companies use it:**
- Flexibility  
- Lower overhead  
- Faster hiring  
- Scale teams up/down quickly  

**Not W-2 employees** → no long-term benefits, usually time-boxed engagements, and often managed through a staffing vendor or MSP.
