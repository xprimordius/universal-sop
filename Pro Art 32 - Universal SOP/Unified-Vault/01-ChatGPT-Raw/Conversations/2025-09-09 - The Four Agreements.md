1. **Be Impeccable With Your Word**  
   - Speak with integrity.  
   - Say only what you mean.  
   - Use words to uplift, not to harm.  

2. **Don’t Take Anything Personally**  
   - Nothing others do is because of you.  
   - Their words and actions reflect their reality, not your worth.  

3. **Don’t Make Assumptions**  
   - Ask questions and communicate clearly.  
   - Avoid creating stories in your head.  

4. **Always Do Your Best**  
   - Your “best” changes moment to moment.  
   - Doing your best keeps you free of regret and self-judgment.
