---

## 📍 Two Setups Compared

**1) Directly Behind Camera**  
- Light points straight at you through the lens.  
- *Result:* flat, shadow-free lighting — but also *flat* dimensionless look.  
- Can cause **harsh reflections** in glasses and a “washed-out” feel.

**2) ~45° Off Axis (to Your Left or Right)**  
- Light aimed at you from ~45° to camera’s axis.  
- *Result:* soft, natural modeling with gentle shadows that give depth.  
- More dynamic, dimensional, and professional on video.

---

## 📸 Why 45° Works Best

**✔ Adds Shape & Depth**  
- Light sweeping across your face creates subtle shadows — looks *real* vs flat.  
- You avoid the “on-set photo booth flash” effect.

**✔ Controls Hot Spots**  
- Reduces glare on glasses/sweet spots on skin.

**✔ Mimics Natural Light**  
- Similar to window light — eyes get *catchlights* that look alive.

---

## 👌 Quick Setup Steps

1. Raise key light to **eye level or slightly above**.  
2. Aim at ~**45°** toward your face.  
3. Use light intensity to balance exposure — not blow out highlights.  
4. Add **fill light opposite** side if shadows too dark.

---

## 🚀 Pro Tip

- If you wear glasses: move light higher and *slightly more off-axis* to avoid reflections.  
- Offset by just 5–10° can markedly reduce glare.
