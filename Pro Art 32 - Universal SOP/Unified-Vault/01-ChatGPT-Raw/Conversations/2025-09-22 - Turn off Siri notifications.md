How to turn off Siri reading notifications

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here’s how to turn off Siri reading notifications on your iPhone/Apple Watch:

### On iPhone
1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Notifications**.
3. Select **Announce Notifications**.
4. Toggle **Announce Notifications** off (or disable per app under the list below).

### On Apple Watch
1. Open the **Watch app** on your iPhone.
2. Tap **Notifications**.
3. Scroll down to **Announce Notifications**.
4. Toggle it off (or manage specific apps).

👉 If you only want Siri to stop reading while headphones/CarPlay are connected, turn off **Headphones** or **CarPlay** under the same Announce Notifications menu.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Got it — you want Siri to announce notifications only in your headphones, not through your iPhone or Watch speaker.  

Here’s the fix:  

### On iPhone  
1. **Settings** → **Notifications** → **Announce Notifications**.  
2. Make sure **Headphones** is toggled **ON**.  
3. Toggle **Speaker** (or **CarPlay**, if you don’t want it there) **OFF**.  

### On Apple Watch  
1. Open the **Watch app** → **Notifications** → **Announce Notifications**.  
2. Toggle **Headphones** **ON**.  
3. Toggle **Speaker** **OFF**.  

This way, Siri will read alerts only when you have AirPods/Beats (or other supported headphones) connected, not aloud on your device speaker.

You’re right — there isn’t a separate **speaker toggle** in iOS. Siri announcing out loud happens only in these cases:  

- **Headphones** (AirPods/Beats, etc.)  
- **CarPlay**  
- **Apple Watch**  

👉 By default, iPhone **does not** announce via the built-in speaker. If you’re hearing it out loud, it’s most likely your **Apple Watch speaker**.  

### To stop that but keep headphones on:  
1. Open the **Watch app** on your iPhone.  
2. Go to **Notifications → Announce Notifications**.  
3. Toggle **Announce Notifications** **OFF** for Apple Watch.  
4. Keep **Headphones** turned **ON** in iPhone settings.  

This way, Siri will read notifications in headphones only, never from your wrist speaker.
