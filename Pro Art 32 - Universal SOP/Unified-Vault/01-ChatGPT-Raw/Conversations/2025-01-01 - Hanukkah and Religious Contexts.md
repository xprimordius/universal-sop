Hanukkah is a Jewish festival commemorating the Maccabees’ victory and rededication of the Second Temple. It’s not a traditional holiday for Christians or Latter-day Saints (Mormons), though some Christians note Jesus attending the “Feast of Dedication” (John 10:22). Mormonism similarly acknowledges its historical and cultural significance but does not traditionally observe it.
