Excellent question — here’s the short, technical truth:  

---

### 🔹 With Apple Vision Pro  
**AirPods Pro 2 (USB-C)** activate a *proprietary ultra-low-latency 20-bit / 48 kHz* wireless link.  
- It’s not Bluetooth AAC.  
- It uses a custom high-bandwidth codec over the **2.4 GHz + 5 GHz** radio channels shared by Vision Pro.  
- The Vision Pro handles the audio encoding natively and transmits it directly to the AirPods with no lossy compression.  
- This is why Apple calls it “Lossless Audio with Vision Pro.”  
- It’s **hardware-dependent** — the chip and radio stack inside Vision Pro and the USB-C AirPods Pro 2 are specifically built for it.

---

### 🔹 Without Apple Vision Pro  
Even those same AirPods Pro 2 revert to **standard Bluetooth AAC** when used with iPhone, iPad, or Mac.  
- AAC = 256 kbps lossy compression.  
- You still get Spatial Audio and Adaptive EQ, but **not** lossless fidelity.  
- No iPhone, iPad, or Mac currently supports the special 20-bit link.

---

### 🔹 Bottom Line  
| Setup | Codec / Path | Lossless? |
|-------|---------------|-----------|
| AirPods 2 (any) | Bluetooth AAC | ❌ No |
| AirPods Pro 2 (Lightning) | Bluetooth AAC | ❌ No |
| AirPods Pro 2 (USB-C) + iPhone/iPad/Mac | Bluetooth AAC | ❌ No |
| AirPods Pro 2 (USB-C) + Vision Pro | Proprietary 20-bit/48 kHz link | ✅ Yes (Lossless) |
