# Credit Score Utilization Tips

---

## 👤 You

Can you get a 800 credit score with 27% utilization? Or what utilization is needed

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Achieving an 800 credit score with a 27% utilization rate is possible but less likely, as credit utilization is a significant factor in credit scoring models. Typically, keeping your credit utilization below 10% to 30% is advisable for a good credit score, but for an optimal score like 800, lower is better. 

**Ideal Utilization Rates:**
1. **1-10%**: Best range for achieving the highest possible credit score.
2. **11-20%**: Still very good and can support a strong credit score.
3. **21-30%**: Acceptable, but may not yield an optimal score.

To aim for an 800 credit score, keeping your utilization below 10% is generally recommended. However, other factors such as your payment history, length of credit history, types of credit, and recent inquiries also play crucial roles.
