**Quick breakdown:**  

- **log** → means **log base 10** (common logarithm)  
  → Example: log 100 = 2  (since 10² = 100)  

- **log₂** → means **log base 2** (binary logarithm)  
  → Example: log₂ 8 = 3  (since 2³ = 8)  

- **ln** → means **log base e ≈ 2.718** (natural logarithm)  
  → Example: ln e = 1  

**In Apple calculators / math functions:**  
- `log(x)` = base 10  
- `ln(x)` = base e  
If you need **base 2**, use: `log(x)/log(2)`  

**Summary:**  
| Notation | Base | Example | Usage |
|-----------|------|----------|--------|
| log x | 10 | log 100 = 2 | Standard math / engineering |
| log₂ x | 2 | log₂ 8 = 3 | Computer science / info theory |
