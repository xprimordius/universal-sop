## 🤖 ChatGPT

No — Discord doesn’t let you “collapse” chats the way you might collapse threads in Slack or folders in email. A few clarifications:

### How It Works
- **Servers → Categories → Channels**  
  - Only **categories** (the folders that group channels) can be collapsed.  
  - Individual **channels** always light up (bold text) and reorder only if the server has sorting or unread settings affecting them.

- **Unread Messages**  
  - Channels with new messages show up bold and may auto-scroll you if you’re clicking around.

### Ways to Control It
1. **Collapse categories** – right-click the category name and collapse it; unread badges will still show but it won’t expand.
2. **Mute channels or categories** – this prevents them from lighting up unless you’re mentioned.
3. **Notification settings** – set a channel to “Only @mentions” or mute entirely.
4. **Mark as read** – right-click the server icon or category to clear bolding quickly.

👉 Bottom line: only **categories** are collapsible, not individual chats. To reduce clutter, collapse categories and tune notifications/mutes.
