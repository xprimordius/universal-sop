## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes, your iPhone can **read PDFs aloud** using built-in features. Here's how:

### Option 1: **Speak Screen**
1. Go to **Settings** > **Accessibility** > **Spoken Content**.
2. Turn on **Speak Screen**.
3. Open your PDF in **Books**, **Files**, or a browser.
4. Swipe down with two fingers from the top of the screen.
5. The iPhone will begin reading the PDF aloud.

### Option 2: **VoiceOver**
1. Go to **Settings** > **Accessibility** > **VoiceOver**.
2. Turn it on.
3. Navigate through the PDF using gestures, and VoiceOver will read what you tap.

Both methods support adjusting **speech speed** and **voice options**.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

To have your iPhone **read a PDF or eBook aloud** in the **Apple Books app**, follow these steps:

---

### **Enable “Speak Screen”**
1. Go to **Settings** > **Accessibility** > **Spoken Content**.
2. Turn on **Speak Screen**.
3. (Optional but recommended) Turn on:
   - **Speak Selection**
   - **Highlight Content**
   - Adjust **Voices** and **Speaking Rate** as needed.

---

### **Use It in Apple Books**
1. Open the PDF or eBook in the **Books** app.
2. Swipe down with **two fingers** from the **top of the screen**.
3. A speech controller will appear and begin reading aloud.

---

**Tips:**
- Tap the **turtle or rabbit** icons on the controller to adjust speed.
- Use the **play/pause/skip** buttons to control reading.
- Works with both PDFs and ePubs (though ePubs have better formatting).
