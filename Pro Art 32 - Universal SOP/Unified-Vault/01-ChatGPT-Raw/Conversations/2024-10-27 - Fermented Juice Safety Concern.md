---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

The pressurization and white vapor in your beet turmeric ginger juice are signs of fermentation. When juice is left at room temperature, especially in a sealed container, natural sugars can ferment due to the presence of bacteria or yeast, producing carbon dioxide and possibly alcohol. This fermentation causes the pressure build-up and the formation of vapor (likely CO2 or other fermentation gases). 

Given these signs, **it is not safe to drink** for the following reasons:

1. **Uncontrolled Fermentation:** The process may have produced harmful bacteria, such as *Clostridium botulinum*, which can be dangerous.
2. **Unknown Contaminants:** Since the fermentation happened spontaneously, there could be contaminants that pose a health risk.

It’s best to discard the juice rather than consume it. To avoid this in the future, store fresh juices in the refrigerator and consume them within a few days.
