### 1. **Let Base Cure First**
- Wait **at least 12–24 hours** (ideally longer)
- Don’t layer too soon or it may **trap solvents**, causing streaks or hazing

### 2. **Surface Prep Again**
- Use **Surface Prep Spray or diluted IPA** before layering
- Ensures bonding by removing dust, oils, or curing residue

### 3. **Apply Extremely Thin**
- Use **less than half** what you used the first time  
- Work in **small sections**, short flash time (30–60 sec), and immediately level

---

### ❌ Don’t:
- Use too much  
- Layer multiple times in one session  
- Apply over dirty or streaked surfaces hoping to "cover them"

---

**Bottom line:**  
Yes, you can layer graphene—but only if base coat is cured, surface is clean, and application is ultra-thin. Otherwise, you’ll amplify defects, not hide them.
