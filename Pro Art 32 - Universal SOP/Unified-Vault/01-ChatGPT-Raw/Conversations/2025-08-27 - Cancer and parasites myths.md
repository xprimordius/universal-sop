## 🤖 ChatGPT

Not true. Here’s the breakdown:  

### Cancer and Parasites  
- **Cancer is not caused by a parasite.**  
  Mainstream science shows it’s uncontrolled growth of the body’s own cells due to DNA mutations and disrupted regulation.  
- **Parasites and cancer are distinct.** Parasites can stress the immune system, cause chronic inflammation, or in rare cases increase cancer risk (e.g., *Schistosoma* linked to bladder cancer), but they are not “the cause” of all cancers.  

### Herbs (wormwood, clove, black walnut)  
- These herbs are traditionally used for **anti-parasitic or digestive support.**  
- Some lab studies show compounds in them have **antimicrobial or antiparasitic activity.**  
- There’s **no clinical evidence** that they “starve and kill cancer” or eliminate tumors.  

### Why This Idea Circulates  
- It comes from **alternative medicine theories** (e.g., Hulda Clark) that claim “all cancer is caused by parasites.” These theories are **not supported by scientific evidence.**  
- People often report feeling better after cleanses—but this can be due to diet changes, improved digestion, or placebo effect, not curing cancer.  

### Bottom Line  
- **Cancer ≠ parasite overgrowth.**  
- Wormwood/clove/black walnut can support gut health and fight certain parasites, but they are **not proven cancer cures.**  
- Ignoring medical treatment for cancer and relying only on herbs would be unsafe.
