Quick fix for **water marks on a couch** depends on fabric type:  

---

### 🧽 **For Fabric or Microfiber**
1. **Lightly dampen** a clean white cloth with **distilled water** (tap water may worsen stains).  
2. Gently **blend the water ring outward** in circular motions to even it out.  
3. Let it **air dry** or use a **hair dryer on low** to prevent new rings.  
4. If stain remains, mix **½ cup white vinegar + ½ cup distilled water**, dab lightly, and repeat step 3.

---

### 🛋️ **For Leather**
1. Wipe with a **slightly damp microfiber cloth** (distilled water).  
2. Dry immediately with a **soft towel**.  
3. Apply a **leather conditioner** afterward to restore oils and sheen.

---

### 🧴 **For Suede**
1. Let it **fully dry** first.  
2. Brush gently with a **suede brush or clean toothbrush**.  
3. For stubborn marks, lightly rub with a **white vinegar–dampened cloth**, then brush again once dry.
