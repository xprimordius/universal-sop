### 1. **Navy Federal Credit Union (NFCU)**
- **Membership Requirements:**
  - Primarily serves members of the military, veterans, and their families.
  - Department of Defense civilians, contractors, and their family members are also eligible.
- **Ease of Membership:**
  - Joining NFCU is straightforward if you meet the membership criteria.
  - Membership can be applied for online.
- **Loan Products:**
  - Offers a wide range of products, including personal loans, mortgages, auto loans, and credit cards.
  - **Loan Amounts:** Personal loans up to $50,000; higher limits available for mortgages and auto loans.
  - **Ease of Approval:** Generally known for competitive rates and accommodating loan approvals, especially for those with military ties.

### 2. **Digital Federal Credit Union (DCU)**
- **Membership Requirements:**
  - Open to anyone who joins an eligible organization (some have low fees, like $10).
  - Also open to employees and retirees of certain companies and residents of specific communities.
- **Ease of Membership:**
  - Relatively easy to join due to broad eligibility criteria.
- **Loan Products:**
  - Offers personal loans, auto loans, mortgages, and credit cards.
  - **Loan Amounts:** Personal loans up to $25,000; mortgages and auto loans vary widely.
  - **Ease of Approval:** Offers competitive rates, with an online application process. Easier for members with good credit, but options available for those with less-than-perfect credit.

### 3. **NIH Federal Credit Union (NIHFCU)**
- **Membership Requirements:**
  - Primarily serves employees and retirees of the National Institutes of Health and select health organizations.
  - Family members of eligible members are also welcome.
- **Ease of Membership:**
  - Requires employment or affiliation with NIH or a select health organization.
- **Loan Products:**
  - Offers a range of personal loans, auto loans, mortgages, and credit cards.
  - **Loan Amounts:** Personal loans up to $50,000; competitive rates for auto and home loans.
  - **Ease of Approval:** Generally favorable for those affiliated with NIH, offering competitive rates and terms.

### 4. **NASA Federal Credit Union**
- **Membership Requirements:**
  - Open to NASA employees, contractors, and their families.
  - Membership also available to members of the NASA Federal Credit Union Alumni Association and members of select organizations.
- **Ease of Membership:**
  - Fairly easy to join if you qualify through employment or by joining an eligible organization.
- **Loan Products:**
  - Offers a wide range of financial products, including personal loans, auto loans, mortgages, and credit cards.
  - **Loan Amounts:** Personal loans up to $30,000; higher limits for auto loans and mortgages.
  - **Ease of Approval:** Known for competitive rates, particularly favorable for those with ties to NASA or its partners.

### **Summary**
- **Ease of Membership:**
  - **Easiest to Join:** DCU (with its broad eligibility) and NFCU (for military families).
  - **Moderate:** NASA FCU (through eligible organizations).
  - **Narrower Membership Base:** NIHFCU (specific to health organizations).
  
- **Loan Amounts & Ease of Access:**
  - **Highest Loan Amounts:** NFCU and NIHFCU offer the largest personal loan limits, up to $50,000.
  - **Ease of Loan Approval:** NFCU and DCU are generally known for competitive rates and accommodating loan approvals.

For the easiest access and broadest membership, **DCU** stands out. For high loan amounts with a solid reputation, **NFCU** and **NIHFCU** are strong contenders, especially if you meet their membership criteria.
