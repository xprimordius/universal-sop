### 3. **Authorized User Strategy**
   - **Why:** Leverages someone else's established credit history.
   - **How:**
     - Ask a trusted family member or friend to add you as an **authorized user** on a card with low utilization, no late payments, and long history.
     - Ensure the card issuer reports authorized user activity to all bureaus (e.g., Amex, Chase, Discover).

### 4. **Retail Store Cards**

   - **Why:** Credit utilization under 10% can significantly improve your score.
   - **How:** Pay balances multiple times a month to keep reported utilization minimal.

### 6. **Add Rental or Utility Payment Reporting**
   - **Why:** Boosts score with additional positive payment history.
   - **How:**
     - Services like **Experian Boost** or **eCredable Lift** can add utility/rent payments to your report.

   - **Why:** Errors can lower your score unnecessarily.
