# Auto Loan Denial Reasons

---

## 👤 You

Is it normal for me to get denied for an auto loan considering I have a 748 Experian FICO-8 score and low utilization, like 5%,

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

With a 748 Experian FICO-8 score and low credit utilization, it might seem unusual to get denied for an auto loan. However, there are several reasons this could happen:

1. **Credit History Length**: If you have a short credit history, lenders might view you as a higher risk despite your good score.
2. **Income and Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI)**: Lenders consider your ability to repay the loan. If your income doesn't align well with your existing debt obligations, they might deny the loan.
3. **Employment Stability**: A recent job change or inconsistent employment history can be a red flag for lenders.
4. **Credit Mix**: If your credit profile lacks diversity (e.g., mostly credit cards and no installment loans), this might affect your application.
5. **Recent Credit Inquiries**: If you've applied for other credit recently, too many inquiries could make lenders wary.
6. **Specific Lender Requirements**: Some lenders have stricter criteria, including internal scoring models or additional requirements beyond the FICO score.

It could be beneficial to check the specific reasons for denial with the lender to address any potential issues.
