**If it still smells after carpet cleaning**, that means the urine *soaked into the padding* or *subfloor* — not just the carpet surface. Regular carpet cleaners **cannot fully remove** it.

**Elite solution:**
1. **Enzymatic cleaner** — Use a *real enzymatic* pet odor remover (e.g., Nature’s Miracle, Rocco & Roxie). It breaks down the uric acid crystals that cause the smell.
   - *Saturate deeply* (soak the carpet and padding underneath).
   - Let it sit 12–24 hours, then blot dry.
2. **Baking soda + vinegar (optional after enzymes)** — Sprinkle baking soda generously over the spot after enzyme treatment, lightly spray vinegar, let it fizz, then dry and vacuum.
3. **Hydrogen peroxide mix (only if needed)** — For lingering smells:
   - Mix: 1 part hydrogen peroxide (3%) + 1 part water + a squirt of dish soap.
   - Soak the area, leave for a few hours, blot and dry.

**If smell persists:**  
- You must **pull up carpet** > treat padding or replace padding.
- Worst case: treat subfloor with enzymatic cleaner or Kilz primer (odor-sealing paint).
