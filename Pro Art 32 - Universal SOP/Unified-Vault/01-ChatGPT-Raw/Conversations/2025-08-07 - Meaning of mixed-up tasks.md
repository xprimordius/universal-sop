## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Love bombing** is a manipulation tactic where someone overwhelms you with excessive affection, praise, gifts, or attention—**fast and intensely**—to gain control or influence over you.

### Key Traits:
- **Too much, too soon:** Constant texts, "I love you" early, big promises.
- **Feels flattering at first**, but it’s often **used to lower your guard**.
- **Phase two:** Once you're attached, the person may shift to control, gaslighting, or emotional withdrawal.

### Healthy vs. Love Bombing:
- **Healthy love** builds gradually and respects boundaries.
- **Love bombing** feels intense, rushed, and often conditional.

**Common in narcissistic or abusive patterns.**
