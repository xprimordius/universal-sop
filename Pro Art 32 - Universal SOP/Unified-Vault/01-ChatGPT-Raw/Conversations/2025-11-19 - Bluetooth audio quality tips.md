**1. Use a codec that preserves detail.**  
- **LDAC** (Android, Sony devices) – highest data rate.

- **AAC** (iPhone) – solid but **not** audiophile-grade; you can only optimize it, not upgrade it.  
*Bluetooth cannot transmit FLAC/WAV losslessly unless using aptX Lossless.*

**2. Upgrade the weakest link.**  
You need:  
- A **clean DAC** → to USB-C/Lightning dongle (AudioQuest DragonFly, iFi Go Bar).  
- **Wired headphones/IEMs** → instant leap in clarity, separation, dynamic range.  
This bypasses Bluetooth compression entirely.

**3. If you want to stay Bluetooth:**  
- Use **LDAC at 990 kbps** (Android Developer settings → enable highest codec).  
- On iPhone:  
  - Turn **EQ off**.  
  - Turn **Sound Check off**.  
  - Disable **Volume Limit**.  
  - Use **Lossless files** → iPhone downconverts to AAC but starts with a better signal.  
- Use **headphones that actually resolve detail** (Focal Bathys, B&W PX8, Sony XM5 in wired mode, Sennheiser Momentum 4).

**4. Minimize Bluetooth degradation:**  
- Keep device **within 1–2 feet**, no obstructions.  
- Avoid **Wi-Fi interference** (2.4 GHz gives the worst).  
- Turn off nearby Bluetooth devices.  
- Ensure both earbuds/headphones support **the same high-end codec** (many don’t).

**5. Hard truth:**  
If you want **true audiophile quality**, you won’t get it over Bluetooth.  
You need:  
- **Wired DAC → wired headphones**, OR  
- **Bluetooth transmitter that supports LDAC/aptX Lossless** → receiver/DAC → wired headphones (Knasen, Qudelix 5K).
