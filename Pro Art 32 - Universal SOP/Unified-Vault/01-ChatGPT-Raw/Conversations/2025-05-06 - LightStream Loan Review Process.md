## 🤖 ChatGPT

Not necessarily bad—but at **$235K stated income**, LightStream may:

- **Trigger manual review** if your credit file doesn’t support that level (e.g., no matching tradelines, thin file, no high-limit cards or loans).
- **Ask for documentation** (pay stubs, W-2s, bank statements), especially for large unsecured loans like $50K.

### When $235K is fine:
- You have 750+ score  
- Thick file (10+ tradelines, >7 years history)  
- Past $25K+ loans or cards  
- Low DTI and utilization  

### When it raises flags:
- Thin or young credit file  
- No prior large loans  
- High stated income with low financial footprint  
- High inquiries or recent credit-seeking activity

**Tactical tip:** Consider stating $120K–$160K if your file doesn’t fully support $235K. It’s often enough for approval but avoids triggering review.
