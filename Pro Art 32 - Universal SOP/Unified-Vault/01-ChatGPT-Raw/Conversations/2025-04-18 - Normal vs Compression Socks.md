Can you wash compression socks like normal

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**No — compression socks require more care than normal socks.**

**Key washing tips:**

| **Aspect** | **Compression Socks** | **Normal Socks** |
|------------|------------------------|------------------|
| **Machine Wash** | Yes, *gentle cycle only* | Yes, any cycle |
| **Water Temp** | *Cold or lukewarm* | Any temperature |
| **Detergent** | Mild only (no bleach or fabric softener) | Any detergent |
| **Drying** | *Air dry only* (no heat) | Tumble dry OK |
| **Stretch/Elastic** | Can be damaged by heat or rough wash | Less risk |

**TLDR:** Wash compression socks gently in cold water, no heat drying, and avoid fabric softeners or bleach — otherwise, you’ll ruin the compression.
