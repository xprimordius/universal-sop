The belief that sex before marriage and cohabitation are considered sins in the sight of God within **Mormonism** (The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, LDS Church) stems from:

### 1. **Scriptural Basis**  
   - **Bible:**
     - **1 Corinthians 6:18-20**: "Flee from sexual immorality... you were bought with a price. So glorify God in your body."
     - **Hebrews 13:4**: "Marriage should be honored by all, and the marriage bed kept pure, for God will judge the adulterer and all the sexually immoral."
     - **Matthew 19:4-6**: Jesus reinforces that sexual relationships are to be within the bounds of marriage.
   - **Book of Mormon**: 
     - **Alma 39:3-5**: Condemns sexual sin, describing it as "most abominable above all sins save it be the shedding of innocent blood or denying the Holy Ghost."
   - **Doctrine and Covenants 42:22-24**: Commands fidelity in marriage and chastity before marriage.

### 2. **Law of Chastity**  
The LDS Church teaches a specific commandment called the **Law of Chastity**, which prohibits:
   - Any sexual relations outside the bonds of a legal marriage between a man and a woman.
   - Viewing or participating in pornography.
   - Homosexual behavior (distinct from orientation, per recent LDS guidelines).

Violating this law is considered a serious sin because it misuses the power of procreation, which is viewed as sacred.

### 3. **Cohabitation Before Marriage**
   - The LDS Church teaches that cohabitation before marriage is problematic because it:
     1. Increases the likelihood of sexual relations outside of marriage.
     2. Undermines the sacred covenant of marriage.
     3. Contradicts teachings about moral purity and self-restraint.

This stance is not explicitly stated in LDS scripture but is consistently emphasized in **modern prophetic teachings** and **church policies**. Leaders like Gordon B. Hinckley and Thomas S. Monson have repeatedly taught against cohabitation and stressed the importance of marriage and chastity.

### 4. **Modern Teachings**  
   - **“The Family: A Proclamation to the World”** (1995): Reinforces marriage as ordained of God and teaches that sexual relationships are reserved for marriage.
   - Church leaders frequently warn that societal trends toward accepting cohabitation and premarital sex are contrary to God’s commandments.

### Summary  
This belief stems from a combination of biblical scripture, additional LDS scripture (Book of Mormon, Doctrine and Covenants), and modern teachings by church leaders. These sources collectively emphasize the sanctity of marriage and chastity as divine principles.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Scriptures** are sacred writings regarded as authoritative or inspired by God, forming the foundation of religious beliefs, teachings, and practices. Their origin and weight depend on the faith tradition, but here’s a breakdown applicable to Christianity, including Mormonism (The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints):

---

### **1. What Are Scriptures?**
Scriptures are collections of texts considered holy and divinely inspired, used as guidance for worship, moral behavior, and understanding God’s will. Examples include:
   - **Bible**: Shared by Christians.
   - **Book of Mormon**, **Doctrine and Covenants**, and **Pearl of Great Price**: Unique to Mormonism.
   - Other religions have their own scriptures (e.g., the Quran in Islam, the Torah in Judaism).

---

### **2. Where Do Scriptures Come From?**
Scriptures come from:
   - **Divine Revelation**: Believed to be directly revealed by God to prophets (e.g., Moses, Isaiah, Joseph Smith).
   - **Historical Events**: Accounts of interactions between God and humanity, often recorded by inspired individuals.
   - **Prophetic Writings**: Teachings, visions, or commandments delivered by chosen prophets.

In Mormonism:
   - **Bible**: The King James Version is used, believed to be the word of God as far as it is translated correctly.
   - **Book of Mormon**: Translated by Joseph Smith from golden plates revealed by the angel Moroni.
   - **Doctrine and Covenants**: A collection of modern revelations given to Joseph Smith and other LDS leaders.
   - **Pearl of Great Price**: Includes the Book of Moses, Abraham, and other key writings, revealed to Joseph Smith.

---

### **3. What Gives Scriptures Their Weight?**
Scriptures are given authority or "weight" based on:
   - **Belief in Divine Origin**: They are seen as the literal or inspired word of God.
   - **Prophetic Authority**: Authored or delivered by individuals recognized as prophets or messengers of God.
   - **Religious Tradition**: Accepted and upheld by religious communities over generations.
   - **Moral and Spiritual Guidance**: They resonate with individuals and communities as sources of truth and direction.

In Mormonism:
   - Scriptures gain weight through **divine confirmation** (prayerful study with the promise of receiving spiritual confirmation, see Moroni 10:4-5).
   - The LDS Church emphasizes continuing revelation through modern prophets, so new revelations (e.g., updates to Doctrine and Covenants) also hold scriptural weight.

---

### **4. Why Are Scriptures Revered?**
   - **Connection to the Divine**: They are believed to reveal God’s nature, plan, and commandments.
   - **Guidance**: Provide moral and ethical teachings.
   - **Sacred Authority**: Used in worship, teaching, and personal spiritual growth.

---

### Summary
Scriptures are sacred texts believed to be divinely inspired and authoritative. They come from revelations to prophets, are preserved by religious traditions, and gain their weight through belief in their divine origin, prophetic authorship, and their power to guide faith and morality. In Mormonism, the unique blend of ancient and modern revelation underscores the dynamic, ongoing nature of scripture as God's communication with humanity.
