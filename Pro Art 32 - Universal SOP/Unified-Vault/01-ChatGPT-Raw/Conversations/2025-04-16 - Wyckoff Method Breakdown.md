The Wyckoff Method is a trading strategy that analyzes market cycles to identify optimal buy and sell points. It consists of four phases: Accumulation, Markup, Distribution, and Markdown. Understanding these phases helps traders align with institutional movements.

---

### Phase 1: Accumulation

**Definition**: A period where informed investors (often institutions) buy large quantities of a security at low prices, typically after a downtrend.

**Characteristics**:
- Price moves sideways within a range.
- Volume may increase on up moves and decrease on down moves, indicating buying interest.
- False breakdowns (springs) may occur to shake out weak holders.

**Visual Representation**:
![Wyckoff Accumulation Phase](https://www.tradingview.com/x/accumulation_phase_example/)

**Key Concepts**:
- **Support**: The lower boundary of the trading range.
- **Resistance**: The upper boundary of the trading range.
- **Spring**: A false breakout below support, followed by a quick recovery.

---

### Phase 2: Markup

**Definition**: The phase where the price breaks above resistance, leading to a sustained uptrend as demand outweighs supply.

**Characteristics**:
- Higher highs and higher lows.
- Increased volume on up moves.
- Minor pullbacks or consolidations (re-accumulations) may occur.

**Key Concepts**:

- **Re-accumulation**: Temporary consolidation before the uptrend resumes.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**"Price moves sideways"** means the price stays within a narrow range, not trending up or down, but bouncing between a clear upper (resistance) and lower (support) level. No sustained upward or downward movement—just “flat” or “range-bound.”

**Example:**  
If a stock stays between $9 and $11 for weeks, moving up and down but never above $11 or below $9, that's sideways.
