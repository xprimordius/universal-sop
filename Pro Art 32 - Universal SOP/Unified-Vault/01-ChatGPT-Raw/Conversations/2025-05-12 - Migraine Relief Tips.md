### ⚡️Immediate Relief
1. **Caffeine + Pain Relief Combo**:  
   - *Excedrin Migraine* (acetaminophen + aspirin + caffeine) — works fast if taken early.
2. **Cold Compress**:  
   - Ice pack on the forehead/neck for 15–20 min. Reduces inflammation and nerve pain.
3. **Dark, Quiet Room**:  
   - Eliminate all light and noise. Sleep if possible.
4. **Magnesium Glycinate**:  
   - 200–400mg — especially if triggered by PMS or stress.

---

### ⚠️Avoid These Common Triggers
- Aged cheese, chocolate, alcohol (especially red wine)
- Skipping meals or dehydration
- Hormonal fluctuations (track cycle)
- Bright lights / screen time / lack of sleep

---

### 🧠 Long-Term Prevention (if recurring)
- **Daily Magnesium** (300–400mg)
- **Riboflavin (B2)** (400mg/day)
- **CoQ10** (100–300mg/day)
- **Avoid trigger foods** (track with a migraine diary)
- **Yoga/Stretching** for tension-type triggers

---

### 🚨 When to Seek Medical Help
- Migraine lasts >3 days
- Vision loss, slurred speech, or paralysis
- First-time or “worst ever” headache
