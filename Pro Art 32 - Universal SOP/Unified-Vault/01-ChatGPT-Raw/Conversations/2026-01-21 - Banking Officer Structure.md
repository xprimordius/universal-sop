## Legal Sufficiency (Colorado)
Under **Colorado Revised Statutes (CRS Title 7)**:

- ✔ One person **may legally hold multiple roles** (President, Treasurer, Director).
- ✔ Officers **do not need unique statutory titles** beyond what bylaws authorize.
- ✔ A **single director** corporation is permitted.
- ✔ “Officer” is **legally valid**, but **non-standard** and weak for third parties.

👉 From a **compliance standpoint**, you’re fine.

## Banking & Funding Reality (Where This Breaks)
Banks, lenders, and underwriters look for:
- Clear **segregation of duties**
- Familiar **C-suite titles**
- Reduced **key-person risk**
- Clean **signing authority hierarchy**

- One person = President + Treasurer + Director
- Someone titled just “Officer”
- No CFO / COO / clearly scoped authority

This doesn’t block approval—but it **raises friction**, delays underwriting, and can trigger extra documentation.
