---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**DTI = Debt-to-Income Ratio**

---

### 📊 Step-by-Step Breakdown:

**Monthly Income:**
- $165K annual = $13,750/month gross

---

### **1. Monthly Debt Payments:**
- **Car loan:** $1,100  
- **Rent:** $1,000 (⚠️ *Not included in DTI unless it's a mortgage*)  
- **Student loan:** Estimated at ~$200–$400/month (we’ll use **$300** for this calc)  
- **Credit cards:** Assume **minimum payments = 1% of balance** → 1% of $2,000 = **$20**

---

### **2. Total Monthly Debt (Excluding Rent):**
= $1,100 (car) + $300 (student loan) + $20 (credit cards)  
= **$1,420**

---

### ✅ **DTI Calculation (Front-End & Back-End)**:

- **Back-End DTI** = $1,420 / $13,750 = **10.3%**

---

### 📈 How High Can You Go?

- Lenders typically allow **DTI up to 36–43%** (some up to 50% for FHA or aggressive lenders).

- Max debt allowed at 43% DTI = 0.43 × $13,750 = **$5,912.50/month**

---

### 🚀 Room for More Debt:

- $5,912.50 max – $1,420 current = **~$4,492.50/month headroom**  
- You could **theoretically** take on another $4,492/mo in monthly payments and still qualify under 43% DTI.
