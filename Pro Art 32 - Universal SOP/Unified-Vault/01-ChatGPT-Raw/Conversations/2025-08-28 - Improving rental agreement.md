### 3. **Utilities & Services**
- Specify who pays for **trash, water, sewer, internet, landscaping, HOA fees**.  
- Clarify what happens if a tenant fails to pay utilities.

### 4. **Occupancy & Use**
- Limit occupancy to named tenants + max guest stay (e.g., no guest >14 days without approval).  
- Ban illegal activities, smoking, or nuisance behavior.  
- Prohibit alterations (painting, construction) without written consent.

### 5. **Maintenance & Repairs**
- Spell out responsibilities:  
  - Tenant: light bulbs, clogged drains (caused by tenant), yard upkeep.  
  - Landlord: structural, plumbing, HVAC (except neglect).  
- Require prompt written notice for repairs.  
- Clarify **who pays if damage results from negligence**.

### 6. **Insurance & Liability**
- Require tenant to carry **renter’s insurance** with liability coverage.  
- Add **indemnification clause** (tenant holds landlord harmless for personal injury/damage except landlord negligence).

### 7. **Entry & Access**
- Clarify notice form (written, text, email).  
- Add tenant’s obligation to provide access for inspections/repairs.

### 8. **Default & Remedies**
- Define “default” (nonpayment, violation of terms).  
- Outline landlord remedies: late fees, eviction, recovery of legal costs.  
- State **failure to enforce once doesn’t waive rights**.

### 9. **Dispute Resolution**
- Option to include **mediation/arbitration** before litigation.  
- Clarify **venue** (Clark County).

### 10. **Legal Protections**
- Add **severability clause** (if one part invalid, rest remains valid).  
- Add **joint & several liability** if multiple tenants.  
- Add **modification clause** (changes must be in writing, signed by both).

## 🔑 **What Is an Option Fee?**
- The **option fee** (sometimes called an *option consideration*) is an **upfront, non-refundable payment** the tenant/buyer pays to the landlord/seller.  
- In exchange, the landlord **gives them the exclusive right (but not obligation) to buy** the property later at the agreed price.  
- If they **buy**, the option fee is typically **applied to the purchase price or down payment**.  
- If they **don’t buy** or default → **you keep the option fee**.  

---

## 📊 **Normal Amounts**
- **Percentage:** Usually **1% – 5% of the purchase price**.  
- **Investor / Landlord Favorable Range:** 3% – 7% (higher if tenant has weak credit, low income, or high risk).  
- **Example:**  
  - Purchase Price = $250,000  
  - Option Fee (3%) = $7,500 (non-refundable, applied only if they buy).  

---

## ⚖️ **Louisiana Context**
- Courts in Louisiana will generally **enforce non-refundable option fees** if clearly stated.  
- Important: Spell out that it’s **non-refundable and forfeited if option not exercised**.  

---

## ✅ Landlord Advantage
- Protects you from wasting time.  
- Filters out unserious tenants.  
- Gives you upfront cash.  
- Even if they walk away → you’ve already profited.
