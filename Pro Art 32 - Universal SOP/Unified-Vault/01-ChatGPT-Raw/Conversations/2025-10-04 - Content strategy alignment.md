> Let’s SEE the teacher stealing money from a student’s backpack.
> Let’s SEE something happen.

> SET UP GOOD LIGHTING

> shadows, backlight, and reflections.
> 
> CHOOSE A SIMPLE BACKGROUND
> Avoid cluttered areas, busy wallpaper and outdoor scenes with
> complex backgrounds. You want the subject of your video to
> ‘pop’, and that can only happen when the background is simple.
>

> 
> THE FEATURED OBJECTS (OR PEOPLE) SHOULD FILL UP THE MIDDLE THIRD OF THE FRAME, AT THE LEAST
> When a video plays on Feed, FB cuts off the top and bottom. Besides, our eyes are drawn to the middle of the videos. So don’t put any
