What’s pretext

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

A **pretext stop** = when a cop uses a small, harmless violation as the *official* reason to pull you over, even though they’re really looking for something else.

Example:  
- Missing front plate  
- Slightly tinted windows  
- Wide lane drift  
- License plate light out  
- Rolling a stop sign 1 mph  

They don’t actually care about the tiny violation — it’s just the **legal excuse** to stop you so they can check:  
- license  
- insurance  
- warrants  
- DUI signs  
- drugs  
- whatever they’re suspicious of

**Why it matters:**  
Missing a front plate is one of the easiest “pretext” reasons to stop someone in states that require it.
