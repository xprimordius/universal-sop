## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Somnambulism = Sleepwalking**

- **Definition:** A sleep disorder where a person engages in complex behaviors (like walking, talking, or even eating) while in deep sleep, usually with no memory afterward.  
- **Stage:** Occurs during **non-REM deep sleep** (slow-wave sleep).  
- **Triggers:** Stress, sleep deprivation, alcohol, certain medications, fever, genetics.  
- **Risks:** Injury from walking into objects, leaving the house, driving, or unsafe behaviors while unaware.  
- **Treatment:**  
  - Ensure safety (locks, alarms, removing obstacles).  
  - Improve sleep hygiene.  
  - Reduce stress.  
  - In severe/recurrent cases, **medication or therapy** may be prescribed.  
- **Prognosis:** More common in children (often outgrown); adults may experience it less frequently, but when persistent, it can signal underlying sleep disorders.
