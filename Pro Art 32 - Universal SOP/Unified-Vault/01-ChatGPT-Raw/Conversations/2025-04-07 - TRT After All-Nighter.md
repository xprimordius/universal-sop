**TRT + Dextroamphetamine Salts (Adderall):**  
Can be combined, but requires awareness:

### **Synergy**
- **TRT** boosts **dopamine/testosterone**—can enhance **motivation, drive, energy**.
- **Dextroamphetamine** also increases **dopamine/norepinephrine**—can stack effects.

### **Risks**
- **Irritability / Anxiety:** Both can increase baseline arousal. Together = overstimulation.
- **Blood Pressure / Heart Rate:** Both raise it—monitor vitals.
- **Sleep Disruption:** Amplified risk if taken late.
- **Libido fluctuations:** TRT increases; dextroamp can blunt it in some.

### **Best Practices**
- **Morning only** for amphetamines.
- **Hydration + electrolytes** mandatory.
- **Monitor mood, BP, sleep** weekly.
- Avoid stacking with stimulants (e.g., caffeine) without need.

**TLDR:** Can be combined, but monitor closely for overstimulation, blood pressure, mood shifts. Balance is key.
