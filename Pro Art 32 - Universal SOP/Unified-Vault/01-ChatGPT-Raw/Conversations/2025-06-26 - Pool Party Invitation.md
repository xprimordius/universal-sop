## 👤 You

How to brine pork loin

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Simple Pork Loin Brine (Foolproof & Juicy):**

### 🔹 Ingredients (for 4–8 lb pork loin):
- **1 gallon cold water** (or enough to fully submerge the loin)
- **1/2 cup kosher salt**
- **1/2 cup brown sugar** (or honey/maple syrup)
- **Optional add-ins:**  
  - 1 tbsp peppercorns  
  - 4 smashed garlic cloves  
  - 2 bay leaves  
  - Fresh herbs (rosemary, thyme)  
  - 1/2 tsp chili flakes for kick  

---

### 🔹 Instructions:
1. **Boil 2 cups water** with salt + sugar (and aromatics if using) to dissolve.
2. **Cool completely**: Add ice or cold water to bring temp down.
3. **Submerge pork loin** in brine in a container or large zip bag.
4. **Refrigerate**:
   - **4–6 hours** minimum for smaller loins.
   - **12–24 hours** for larger ones (up to 8 lbs).
5. **Remove + pat dry.** Let rest uncovered in fridge 1–2 hrs before cooking for best crust.

---

### ❄️ Must I refrigerate?
**Yes.** Always refrigerate meat while brining to prevent bacteria growth.

## 👤 You

Do I season after letting it rest

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Yes — always season after brining and resting.**

### 🔹 Why?
Brining seasons *inside* the meat (salt, moisture, maybe aromatics).  
But it doesn’t flavor the *outside* or create a crust — that's what post-brine seasoning does.

---

### 🔹 Steps After Brining:
1. **Remove from brine**  
2. **Pat completely dry** with paper towels  
3. *(Optional but ideal)*: Let rest uncovered in fridge 1–2 hrs for surface dryness  
4. **Season generously** right before cooking:
   - **Salt** (lightly—it's already brined)  
   - **Pepper**  
   - **Garlic/onion powder**  
   - **Smoked paprika, herbs, etc.**  
   - Add **olive oil or mustard** as a binder if desired

---

Let me know how you’re cooking it (Traeger, oven, etc.) for a custom seasoning or rub suggestion.
