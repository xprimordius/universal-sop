### 🔍 Verified Statement:
**"All sales final" clauses do NOT override a consumer’s right to dispute charges under the Fair Credit Billing Act (FCBA), especially when services were not delivered as promised.**

---

### 📜 Legal Basis – FCBA (15 U.S.C. §1666):

The **Fair Credit Billing Act** provides the right to dispute charges for:

- **Goods or services not received**
- **Goods/services not as described or agreed**
- **Billing errors**

Even if a contract says “no refunds” or “all sales final,” **federal law prevails** when:

- The product or service was **never delivered**, or
- The delivery **substantially deviated** from what was represented

🔗 *Reference:* FCBA Dispute Rights – [15 U.S. Code § 1666](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1666)

> “A creditor may not treat a disputed amount as past due... if a consumer has provided written notice of a billing error involving... property or services not delivered.”

---

### 🛡️ Also Supported By:

- **FTC Guidance:** “Merchants cannot enforce refund policies if they violate FTC Act Section 5 (unfair/deceptive).”
- **Visa/Mastercard Rules:** Preserve cardholder dispute rights **regardless of merchant’s stated refund policy**.

---
