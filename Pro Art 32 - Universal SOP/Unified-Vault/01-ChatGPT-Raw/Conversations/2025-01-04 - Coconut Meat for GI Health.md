1. **Rich in Fiber**:

2. **Anti-inflammatory Properties**:  
   It contains medium-chain triglycerides (MCTs) and lauric acid, which have antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory effects, potentially supporting a healthy gut environment.

3. **Promotes Gut Microbiome Balance**:  
   MCTs may help reduce harmful bacteria and promote beneficial bacteria in the GI tract.

### Important Considerations:
- For those with digestive issues (like **IBS** or **SIBO**), high-fat or high-fiber foods like coconut may cause irritation.
- If dealing with specific GI conditions, coconut meat should be consumed in moderation and as part of a balanced diet.

**TLDR**: Coconut meat can support gut health by promoting digestion, reducing inflammation, and balancing gut bacteria, but it’s not a one-size-fits-all solution.
