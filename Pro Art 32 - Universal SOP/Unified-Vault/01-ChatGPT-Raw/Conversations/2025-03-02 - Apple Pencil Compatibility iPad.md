The **Apple Pencil (USB-C)** is its own separate model and is **not considered** the 1st or 2nd generation. It was released in **2023** and is simply called **"Apple Pencil (USB-C)"**.  

Here’s a quick comparison:  

| **Apple Pencil Model**    | **Charging Method** | **iPad Compatibility**                     |
|--------------------------|--------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| **Apple Pencil (1st Gen)**  | Lightning         | Older iPads with Lightning port            |
| **Apple Pencil (2nd Gen)**  | Magnetic         | iPads with flat-edge design (Pro, Air, Mini) |
| **Apple Pencil (USB-C)**   | USB-C Port        | iPads with USB-C (including **10th-gen iPad**) |

For your **10.9-inch iPad (10th-gen)**, you need the **Apple Pencil (USB-C)**.
