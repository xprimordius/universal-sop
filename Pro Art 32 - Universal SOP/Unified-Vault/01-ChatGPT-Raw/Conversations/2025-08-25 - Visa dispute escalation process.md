Visa’s consumer-facing support agents (like Janelle in your chat) are trained to direct all cardholder disputes back to the issuing bank. That’s why she said Visa has “no jurisdiction over cardholder accounts.”  

**Reality of Escalation:**  
- **Visa does not accept disputes directly from cardholders.** Only the issuing bank (Navy Federal in your case) can submit a dispute or compliance case into Visa’s system.  
- If your issuer refuses or mishandles a case, the correct path is escalation via:  
  1. **Visa Global Customer Assistance Center (GCAC):** 1-800-847-2911 (US). They still usually direct you back to your bank, but you can request *escalation to the Visa Dispute Resolution team* and cite issuer non-compliance with Visa Core Rules.  
  2. **Visa Contact Page:** [https://usa.visa.com/support/consumer.html](https://usa.visa.com/support/consumer.html) → “Contact Us” → “File a complaint against a financial institution.” This form lets you submit complaints about a bank’s failure to comply with Visa rules.  
  3. **Regulatory Channels:** File with the **Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)** or your **State Attorney General** citing that your issuer is refusing to process your claim in compliance with Visa’s rules. These complaints get routed to both Visa and the bank’s compliance departments.
