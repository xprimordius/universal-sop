## 👤 You

Is wrapping a land acuistion and and new construction loan into one via DSCR called a perm to home loan or what is it called?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Headline:** It’s **not** called a “perm-to-home” loan.

**Correct term(s):**

1. **DSCR Construction-to-Permanent Loan (aka “Construction-to-Perm”)**  
   - One loan.  
   - Covers **land acquisition + construction**, then **automatically converts** to a long-term DSCR rental loan at stabilization.  
   - Most accurate name.

2. **DSCR One-Time Close Construction Loan**  
   - Same concept, different label.  
   - “One-time close” = no refi at the end.

3. **DSCR Construction Loan with Mini-Perm** (less common)  
   - Short-term construction + short perm period.  
   - Usually worse terms than true construction-to-perm.

**What lenders will recognize fastest:**  
👉 **“DSCR Construction-to-Permanent (One-Time Close) loan including land.”**

## Is a Gap Funder Used?

### **Yes — in advanced stacks**

Common structure:
- **Senior loan:** Construction-to-perm (80%)
- **Gap / Mezz funder:** Covers:
  - Down payment shortfall
  - Soft costs
  - Carry
- **Borrower cash in:** Minimal

This is **developer-style capital stacking**, not retail lending.

Gap funders usually want:
- Second position
- Equity kicker or fixed return
- Clear take-out (refi or sale)
