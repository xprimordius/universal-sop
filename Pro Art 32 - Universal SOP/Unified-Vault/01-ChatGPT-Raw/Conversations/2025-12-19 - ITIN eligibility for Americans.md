## 👤 You

Can you get an ITIN as an american

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Yes — but only in limited cases.**

### The Rule (Clear & Exact)
- **U.S. citizens and permanent residents are *not eligible*** for an ITIN **by default**.
- The IRS issues ITINs **only to people who are *not eligible* for an SSN**.

### The One Exception
You *can* be issued an ITIN **temporarily** **only if**:
- You **do not yet have** an SSN, **and**
- You **must file a federal tax return now**, **and**
- You are **in the process of obtaining an SSN** (e.g., recent immigrant or newborn without SSN yet).

Once the SSN is issued:
- The ITIN is **invalidated**
- All tax records are merged under the SSN

### Bottom Line
- **Americans use SSNs, not ITINs**
- **You cannot choose an ITIN instead of an SSN**
- Any strategy suggesting Americans should “get an ITIN” is **incorrect or misleading**

**Short answer:**  
**Legally, people are *not* allowed to have multiple active SSNs.**

## The **ONLY legal ways** multiple SSNs appear

### 1. **SSA Error (Duplicate Issuance)**
- Clerical or identity error (more common decades ago)
- SSA later **merges and voids one**
- Person does **not** get to keep both

### 2. **Identity Theft / Fraud Victim**
- Original SSN compromised
- SSA may issue a **new SSN**
- **Old SSN is retired**
- Still **one valid SSN at a time**

### 3. **Extreme Harassment / Danger**
- Domestic violence, witness protection–type scenarios
- New SSN issued for safety
- Old one **cancelled**

👉 In **all legal cases**, **only ONE SSN remains valid**

- Federal felony to use or sell

### ❌ “I reset my SSN”
- No such thing legally
