1. **All Sales Final Clause:**  
   - The agreement states that all sales are final once the program is purchased, making it **non-cancellable**. This removes the client's ability to exit the agreement if the program doesn’t meet expectations.

2. **Highly Conditional Refund Policy:**  
   - The "Accepted Offer + Money Back Guarantee" is only valid for 60 days and requires the client to meet multiple strict conditions (e.g., consuming all Masterclass content, making at least 30 offers, attending 100% of accountability calls, weekly group calls, active participation in the network, etc.).  
   - Any missed condition, even minor, forfeits eligibility.

3. **Subjective Control by Company:**  
   - "First right of refusal" on a property that passes their calculator test is entirely at their discretion. This could be a delaying tactic before refunding.

4. **No Performance Guarantee:**  
   - The agreement explicitly disclaims responsibility for your success and makes clear there are no earnings guarantees.

5. **Financing Risk:**  
   - If you financed through a retail installment agreement, you remain liable for the full balance even if you attempt to exit the program or lose access.

6. **No External Validation:**  
   - Any guarantees or promises not in writing within the agreement are invalidated.
