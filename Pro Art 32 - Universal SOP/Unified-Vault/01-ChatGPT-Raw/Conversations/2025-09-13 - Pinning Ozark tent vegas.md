Vegas desert ground is like concrete. Regular tent stakes won’t work. Here’s how to pin down your Ozark tent securely:  

**1. Upgrade Stakes**  
- Replace stock stakes with **12–18” heavy-duty steel or rebar stakes**.  
- Screw-in **lag bolts** with washers are even better (drill them in with an impact driver).  

**2. Hammer / Driver**  
- Bring a **3–5 lb mallet** or small sledge to drive stakes in.  
- For lag bolts, use a **cordless drill/impact driver** with socket attachment.  

**3. Anchor Alternatives**  
- If ground is too hard:  
  - **Sandbags / water jugs** tied to each tent corner.  
  - **Concrete pavers** or **cinder blocks** as tie-down weights.  
  - Bury filled bags of sand/rocks if you can dig a few inches.  

**4. Guy Lines**  
- Use all guy lines. Angle them out and secure to weights or deep stakes for wind resistance.  

**5. Backup**  
- Duct tape + bungees for reinforcement.  
- Always check before leaving camp—Vegas winds are no joke.  

👉 Best combo: **Lag bolts + impact driver + sandbag backups.**
