## 🤖 ChatGPT

Example:  
- “Nearly finished” = almost done, but not fully.  
- “Nearly midnight” = just before midnight.  
- “I nearly fell” = I almost fell, but didn’t.  

It signals something is *close* to happening, but not fully there.
