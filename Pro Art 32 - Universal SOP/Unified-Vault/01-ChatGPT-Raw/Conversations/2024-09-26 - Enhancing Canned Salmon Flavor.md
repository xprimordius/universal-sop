# Enhancing Canned Salmon Flavor

---

## 👤 You

What can I add to canned salmon to simply make it taste better and healthy

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

To enhance the taste and health benefits of canned salmon, you can mix in the following ingredients for a simple yet flavorful dish:

1. **Lemon Juice & Zest**: Adds freshness and balances the richness of the salmon.
2. **Greek Yogurt or Avocado**: Use these as a creamy, healthy base instead of mayonnaise.
3. **Chopped Herbs**: Fresh dill, parsley, or cilantro to brighten up the flavor.
4. **Mustard or Dijon Mustard**: Adds tang and depth of flavor.
5. **Capers or Pickles**: For a salty, tangy bite.
6. **Spices**: Black pepper, paprika, or cayenne for a kick.
7. **Vegetables**: Chopped cucumber, celery, or red onion for crunch and nutrition.
8. **Olive Oil**: A drizzle of high-quality extra virgin olive oil for healthy fats.

Combine these with the salmon and serve on whole grain toast, in lettuce wraps, or over a bed of greens for a simple, healthy meal.
