## Risks / Limitations

- Placebo / expectation effect is strong— “if you believe it will help, you often notice improvements.”  
- Some reported side effects: spotting, possible hormonal or flora imbalance if the product is contaminated or the probiotic strain triggers overgrowth.  
- If you have infections, underlying conditions (yeast, bacterial vaginosis, etc.), these may require medical treatment (antibiotics, antifungals), not just gummies.

- Best used **as a complement**—not replacement—for hygiene, diet, medical care.
