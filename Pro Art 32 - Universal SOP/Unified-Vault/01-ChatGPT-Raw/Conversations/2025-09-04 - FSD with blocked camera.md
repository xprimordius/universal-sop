- **Traffic-Aware Cruise Control (TACC):** Manages speed and following distance, but it won’t steer.

- **Full Self-Driving (Supervised):** Builds on both TACC + Autosteer, handling turns, lane changes, and navigation.

- **TACC alone = no steering.**
