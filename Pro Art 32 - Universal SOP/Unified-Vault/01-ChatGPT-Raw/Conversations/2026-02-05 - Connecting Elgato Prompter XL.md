## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes — **if your tripod head/plate is rated for the total load and the plate is clamped correctly**, the **single 1/4-20 screw connection is the normal/standard way** teleprompters mount and it will hold the Prompter XL + camera.

What determines safety isn’t “number of screws,” it’s **(1) load rating + (2) torque/leverage + (3) anti-twist control**.

### The 3 real failure modes to watch
1. **Over the head’s payload rating**
   - Your head/ballhead is the limiter, not the screw.
2. **Twist/rotation on the plate**
   - Heavy rigs can slowly rotate around a single screw if there’s no anti-twist pin/lip or if it isn’t cinched hard.
3. **Front-heavy torque**
   - Teleprompters add distance from the head → more leverage. Even if weight is “within rating,” it can feel saggy if the head is small.

### Quick safety checklist (fast)
- **Tripod head payload rating ≥ 2× your total rig weight** (prompter + camera + lens + any batteries/mics).
- Plate clamp is tight and the **plate cannot rotate by hand** when you try.
- If your lens is large/heavy, use a **lens support / lens collar** when possible.
- If you notice any rotation creep, add **an anti-twist solution** (see below).

### If you want it “bulletproof”
- Use an **Arca/Manfrotto plate with anti-twist lip**, or
- Add a **camera cage / baseplate** with **anti-twist pins**, or
- Use a **longer plate** so the clamp has more bite (reduces rotation).
