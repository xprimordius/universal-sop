# Credit Line Increase Impact

---

## 👤 You

Why did my score go down for getting a credit line increase? 500 to 3500 with 11% utilization

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Your credit score could have gone down after getting a credit line increase for several reasons:

1. **Hard Inquiry**: Applying for a credit line increase can result in a hard inquiry on your credit report, which can temporarily lower your credit score.

2. **New Credit Impact**: An increase in your available credit could be seen as a potential for higher future debt, which might cause a slight dip in your score initially.

3. **Credit Mix**: If the increase affects the types of credit you have or changes the overall mix, it could impact your score.

4. **Recent Account Changes**: Recent significant changes to your credit accounts can sometimes cause temporary fluctuations in your score as the credit reporting systems adjust.

5. **Other Factors**: There may be other changes in your credit profile, such as payment history, changes in balances on other accounts, or new accounts opened.

Typically, if you maintain low utilization and a good payment history, your score should recover and possibly improve over time with the increased credit limit.
