Here’s a quick, efficient cooking guide for the **Ninja MC1001** so you can handle chicken, steak, salmon, potatoes, and more with minimal guesswork:  

---

## **Chicken Breast or Thighs**
- **Function:** *Sear/Sauté* → *Slow Cook* or *Pressure Cook* (if MC1001 has pressure function)  
- **Steps:**  
  1. Season chicken.  
  2. Sear 3–4 min per side until browned.  
  3. Add a little broth/water, close lid.  
  4. **Slow cook:** 2–3 hrs on high / 4–6 hrs on low.  
     **Pressure cook:** 6–8 min (then quick release).  

---

## **Steak**
- **Function:** *Sear/Sauté* only (MC1001 is not for grilling high-heat crust like cast iron, but works for searing and finishing).  
- **Steps:**  
  1. Pat dry & season steak.  
  2. Preheat on *Sear/Sauté* high with a little oil.  
  3. Cook 3–5 min each side (thickness-dependent) for medium-rare.  
  4. Rest 5 min before slicing.  

---

## **Salmon**
- **Function:** *Sear/Sauté* or *Steam*  
- **Steps:**  
  1. Light oil & season skin side.  
  2. **Sear:** 3–4 min skin-side down, flip 2–3 min.  
  3. **Steam:** Place on trivet over water, steam 8–10 min.  

---

## **Potatoes**
- **Boiled/Mashed:**  
  - *Boil/Steam* 15–20 min until fork-tender, then mash with butter/salt.  
- **Roasted-Style:**  
  - Parboil 10 min, drain, toss in oil/spices, *Sear/Sauté* 10–15 min until browned.  

---

## **Multi-Item Meal (Chicken + Potatoes + Veg)**
1. *Sear/Sauté* chicken.  
2. Add potatoes & broth, cook under *Slow Cook* high (2 hrs) or *Pressure Cook* 8 min.  
3. Add quick-cook veg in last 10 min (slow cook) or quick-release and add veg for 3 min (pressure cook).
