Is creatine good for your brain

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes — creatine is **good for your brain**. TLDR:

### **Key Cognitive Benefits:**
- **Improved short-term memory**  
- **Enhanced mental clarity and focus**  
- **Reduced mental fatigue** (especially under stress or sleep deprivation)  
- **Neuroprotection** (helps preserve brain cells and function)

### **Mechanism:**
Creatine increases ATP availability in brain cells, improving energy supply during demanding cognitive tasks — same way it fuels muscles.

### **Research Support:**
- Studies show **improved cognitive performance**, especially in **vegetarians**, **older adults**, and **sleep-deprived individuals**.
- May support **mood regulation** and reduce symptoms of **depression**.

### **Safe Use:**
- **3–5g/day** is standard and safe for most.
- No major side effects in healthy individuals.

A pound (16 oz) of **Simply Nature 100% Grass Fed Ground Beef** contains roughly:

### **Creatine Estimate:**
- **1.5 to 2 grams** of creatine per pound

### **Why:**
- Beef naturally contains about **0.9–1g per 100g (3.5 oz)**
