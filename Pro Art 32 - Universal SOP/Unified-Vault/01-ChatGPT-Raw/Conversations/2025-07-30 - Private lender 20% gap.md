## 👤 You

will a private money lender lend the remaining 20% of a DSCR fix n flip loan? or the 20% for a new construction build loan?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

### Short Answer: **Yes, but it's uncommon and highly dependent on the lender, deal, and structure.**

---

### **1. DSCR Fix & Flip Loan (20% Gap)**  
- **Most private/hard money lenders expect full skin-in-the-game.**  
  - They finance **70-80% of purchase price** + some rehab; you bring the remaining 20-30%.  
- **Secondary private lenders (gap funders)** sometimes cover the 20% equity, but:  
  - They’ll often require a **second lien** or **equity split.**  
  - Higher cost (18-30% annualized or profit share).  
  - Many primary lenders **prohibit junior liens** unless disclosed.  

---

### **2. New Construction Build Loan (20% Gap)**  
- Same concept: **primary lender funds 80%, you need 20%.**  
- Gap funding is harder here because:  
  - More risk (construction delays, cost overruns).  
  - Many lenders require your 20% cash up front, not borrowed.  
- If allowed, it’s again a second lien or JV partnership.

---

### **Key Considerations**  
1. **Lender Rules:** Most first-position lenders prohibit “borrowed down payment” unless approved.  
2. **Structure:**  
   - Second lien (mezzanine debt).  
   - Equity partner (profit split instead of interest).  
3. **Cost:** Gap funding is expensive because of the risk.  
4. **Full Disclosure:** If hidden, it can trigger default with the primary lender.

---

### **Better Alternative:**  
- **Raise a JV partner** who provides the 20% in exchange for a profit split. This is more common and acceptable to primary lenders.

## 👤 You

profit split makes sense

## 🤖 ChatGPT

### **Best Way: JV / Profit Split for the 20% Gap**

---

### **How It Works**
1. **Primary Lender:** Funds 70–80% of purchase (and sometimes rehab/construction draws).  
2. **JV Partner:** Brings the 20–30% equity you need.  
3. **You:** Handle deal sourcing, management, and exit.  

---

### **Typical Profit Splits**
- **50/50** after capital is returned (common when JV partner is fully passive).  
- **70/30 (You/Partner)** if you contribute some capital or heavy sweat equity.  
- **Preferred Return:** Partner gets their money back first + a fixed return (e.g., 8–12%), then split profits.

---

### **Why This Works**
- Compliant with first lender (it’s equity, not borrowed funds).  
- JV partner is aligned with you—they want the project to succeed.  
- More flexible than mezzanine debt (no strict monthly payments).  

---

### **Key Points to Protect Yourself**
1. **Use a New LLC per deal.** Partner owns % of the LLC (not a lien).  
2. **Operating Agreement:**  
   - Defines capital contributions, profit splits, and exit.  
   - Clear timeline and control rights.  
3. **Transparency:** Send partner monthly updates + lender draws.

### **Yes, but only if structured correctly and pitched well.**  

---

### **Why Most Private Money Lenders Will Listen**  
1. **They’re already comfortable with real estate risk.**  
2. **Equity / JV** means they’re not in second position debt (safer if deal goes south).  
3. Higher potential return vs. standard lending rates.

---

### **Challenges**
- **Deal Quality:** Must show strong ARV, conservative numbers, clear exit.  
- **Trust & Control:** PMLs want proof you can execute; JV docs must give them clear rights.  
- **Timeline:** Flips/new builds have finite timelines; they’ll want capital back <12 months.

---

### **How to Make it Easy**
1. **Offer Preferred Return + Profit Split:**  
   - e.g., 8–12% preferred return, then 50/50 of profits.  
2. **Show They’re Last in Line for Risk:**  
   - First lender is secured by property.  
   - JV gets equity upside, but you’re handling execution and cost overruns.  
3. **Package Professionally:**  
   - Executive summary (1–2 pages).  
   - ARV comps + conservative budget.  
   - Simple LLC structure (they own % of the deal).  

---

### **Bottom Line**  
PMLs are **easier to close as equity partners than second-position lenders** if:  
- You bring strong deals.  
- You make it hands-off and give them a clear, high ROI.
