## 👤 You

Asymmetric bets? Help me understand asymmetric

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Asymmetric bets** = bets where **the upside is much greater than the downside.**

### Simple Breakdown:

- **Symmetric Bet**: Risk $1 to make $1.
- **Asymmetric Bet**: Risk $1 to make $10, $100, or $1,000.

### Core Idea:
- Even if you’re wrong most of the time, **one win can cover all losses and more**.
- You want **high reward with low risk**.

### Examples:
1. **Startup Investing**:
   - Risk $10K → Potential return: $1M+ (asymmetric)
2. **Out-of-the-money options**:
   - Pay $100 for an option → Could return $5,000+ if it hits
3. **Launching a product with little cost**:
   - Spend $500 on ads → If it hits, you make $50K+/mo

### Key Filters:
- **Limited downside** (cap losses)
- **Unlimited or large upside**
- **Low probability of success is OK** if payoff is large

Asymmetry is how venture capitalists, traders, and top entrepreneurs win big without needing to be right often.

What is a grifter

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

A **grifter** is a **con artist**—someone who **manipulates, deceives, or scams people** to make money, usually by pretending to offer value (like help, insight, or opportunity) but delivering nothing or very little.

### Key traits of a grifter:
- **Charismatic and persuasive**
- Sells **false hope or fake promises**
- Often **plays on emotions** (trust, urgency, fear, greed)
- Disappears or shifts blame when questioned or exposed

### Common forms:
- Fake gurus/coaches
- Get-rich-quick “mentors”
- Ponzi schemers
- People selling worthless or misleading products/services

**Grifter = manipulator who profits through lies or deception**, not real value.
