what does it mean if the brakes are glazed in the grand turismo movie

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Straight answer —  

In *Gran Turismo* (and in real racing), *glazed brakes* means:

> The brake pads or rotors got so hot they hardened and smoothed over, reducing grip/friction — making braking weaker, slower, and less responsive.

### Why it happens:
- Excessive heat from overuse (hard braking, riding brakes too long).
- Pads lose their bite.
- Surface becomes shiny/glassy (like ceramic).

### Symptoms in racing/driving:
- Brakes feel hard or wooden.
- Car doesn’t slow down as expected.
- Longer braking distances.
- Potential brake fade (loss of braking power).

### Solution:
- Let them cool down.
- Replace pads/rotors if badly glazed.
- Change braking technique (harder, shorter braking vs light dragging).

In *Gran Turismo* (the movie) — it means the driver overheated the brakes by braking incorrectly or too much — reducing stopping power in a critical moment.
