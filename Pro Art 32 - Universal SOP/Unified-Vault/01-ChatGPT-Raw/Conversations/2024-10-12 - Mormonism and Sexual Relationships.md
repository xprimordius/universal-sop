# Mormonism and Sexual Relationships

## 👤 You

What does Mormonism say / believe about non intercourse sexual relationships / encounters / experiences

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Mormonism, officially known as *The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints* (LDS Church), teaches strict standards regarding sexual behavior. While most public focus is on chastity and abstinence from sexual intercourse outside of marriage, the church’s doctrines extend to other forms of sexual behavior as well. Below is a detailed breakdown of LDS teachings regarding non-intercourse sexual encounters:

### 1. **Law of Chastity Overview**
   - The Law of Chastity is a core moral principle in Mormonism, emphasizing that sexual activity is reserved solely for a man and a woman legally married to each other. 
   - The standard includes abstinence from all sexual behavior outside of marriage, not just intercourse.

### 2. **Non-Intercourse Sexual Activities**
   The LDS Church teaches that sexual intimacy is sacred and that any sexual behavior outside of marriage, including non-intercourse activities, is a violation of the Law of Chastity. This can include:
   - **Sexual touching or fondling** (outside of marriage)
   - **Oral sex** (if outside of marriage)
   - **Masturbation** (regarded as a violation of personal purity)
   - **Passionate kissing or heavy petting** intended to arouse sexual feelings

   The church emphasizes avoiding behaviors that "arouse sexual feelings" prematurely, as they are believed to be spiritually harmful and weaken one's resolve to remain chaste.

### 3. **Same-Sex Attraction and Intimacy**
   - The church distinguishes between feelings of same-sex attraction and acting on those feelings. 
   - Any form of romantic or sexual behavior (even non-intercourse encounters) outside of heterosexual marriage is seen as inconsistent with church doctrine.

### 4. **Counsel on Dating and Physical Boundaries**
   - Members are counseled to avoid situations that may lead to sexual arousal, such as being alone late at night or engaging in physically intimate activities.
   - "Modest" dating practices are encouraged, avoiding behaviors like passionate kissing or cuddling that could lead to sexual arousal or temptation.

### 5. **Repentance Process**
   - If members engage in sexual behavior (including non-intercourse activities) that violates the Law of Chastity, they are encouraged to repent by confessing their actions to a bishop and seeking forgiveness.
   - The repentance process can involve personal reflection, prayer, and in some cases, church discipline to help the individual realign with church teachings.

### Summary
In Mormon belief, sexual purity encompasses more than abstaining from intercourse; it includes avoiding all sexually stimulating behavior outside of marriage. Sexual intimacy is reserved exclusively for marriage between a man and a woman, and any non-intercourse sexual encounters are considered sinful and require repentance. The emphasis is on maintaining personal purity, self-discipline, and avoiding situations that could lead to sexual transgressions.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

The term *Latter-day Saints* reflects the belief of members of *The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints* (LDS Church) that they are living in the "latter days" or final dispensation before the Second Coming of Jesus Christ. Here is a breakdown of the meaning and significance of the term:

### 1. **"Saints"**  
In Christian tradition, the word *saints* generally refers to believers who strive to follow Christ. In the LDS context, members of the church are referred to as "saints" because they aim to live righteous lives according to Christ's teachings.

### 2. **"Latter-day"**  
The phrase *latter-day* distinguishes the church’s members from the early Christian saints or believers who lived during New Testament times. The LDS Church teaches that after a long period of apostasy, or a falling away from Christ's original teachings, the gospel was restored through Joseph Smith in 1830. 

- **"Latter-day"** indicates that the restored church exists in these modern or "last days," in preparation for the Second Coming of Jesus Christ.  
- The belief in dispensations (periods of time in which God reveals His will to humanity) is central. The *Latter-day Saints* see themselves as living in the final dispensation, which is marked by the restored gospel.

### 3. **Purpose of the Name**  
The full name, *The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints*, reflects the following:
- **"The Church of Jesus Christ"** emphasizes that the church is centered on Christ.
- **"Latter-day"** distinguishes the modern restoration of the church from early Christianity.
- **"Saints"** refers to those who strive to live according to Christ's teachings.

The name communicates that the members of the church see themselves as followers of Christ in the latter or final period of history leading to His prophesied return.

In the LDS Church, engaging in sexual activity before marriage is seen as a serious sin that requires **repentance** and **reconciliation with God**. Through confession, spiritual healing, and a commitment to change, members believe that they can receive **forgiveness** and **regain lost blessings**.
