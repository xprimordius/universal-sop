# Felony Record Duration

---

## 👤 You

how long does a felony last on your record for

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

A felony conviction typically remains on your criminal record permanently unless it is expunged or sealed through a legal process. Expungement or sealing of a felony record depends on several factors, including the state where the conviction occurred, the nature of the crime, and whether you meet specific eligibility criteria, such as a certain period of time having passed without any further criminal activity.

It's important to consult with a legal professional to understand the options available in your jurisdiction, as the laws and procedures can vary significantly.

what about aggravated robbery enhanced with discharge of a fire arm with seriously bodily harm

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

An aggravated robbery conviction, especially one that involves the discharge of a firearm resulting in serious bodily harm, is considered a very serious felony. This type of conviction would likely carry severe penalties, including a long prison sentence, and it would almost certainly remain on your record permanently.

The possibility of expungement or record sealing for such a conviction is generally very limited and often not allowed due to the violent nature of the crime. In many states, violent felonies involving firearms and causing serious bodily harm are considered ineligible for expungement or sealing.

Again, the exact legal implications and possibilities for record expungement depend on the jurisdiction and the specific details of the case. Consulting with a legal professional who specializes in criminal law in your state is crucial for understanding your options and the long-term impact of such a conviction on your record.
