### Common Causes of Wart-like Lumps in Dogs

- **Sebaceous adenomas:** Common in older dogs; benign tumors from oil glands, often wart-like.

### When to See a Vet
- Rapid growth, change in color, bleeding, or ulceration.  
- Multiple new lumps appearing quickly.  
- If the lump is causing discomfort, itching, or infection.  
- If your dog is older, since benign vs. malignant is harder to tell visually.
