# What the Research Actually Shows

## 1. NMN → Raises NAD⁺ (Human Evidence)
Human trials show **NMN supplementation increases blood NAD⁺ levels**.

Example:  
- Yoshino et al., *Science* (2021)  
- 250 mg/day NMN improved insulin sensitivity and increased NAD metabolites in humans.

Source:  
https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.abe9985

---

## 2. Oral NAD⁺ Absorption Is Unclear
Most studies suggest **NAD⁺ taken orally is broken down in the gut** before reaching cells.

Source review:  
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7963035/

Meaning:

**NAD⁺ → degraded → nicotinamide → recycled**

Because of this, many researchers prefer **NMN or NR**.

- Human data is limited
