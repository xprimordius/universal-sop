## 🤖 ChatGPT

Sadistic = deriving pleasure from causing pain, suffering, or humiliation to others.

→ Core idea: Enjoyment from others' pain (physical, emotional, psychological).

Levels of use:
- Clinical: Sadistic Personality Disorder (extreme, rare).
- Casual: Describing cruel, harsh, or intentionally hurtful behavior.
- Sexual: BDSM context — consensual power exchange involving pain or domination.

Opposite: Empathy, compassion.

Example:
> "His insults weren’t just mean — they were *sadistic* — he enjoyed watching people break down."

Key Distinction: Sadism = *pleasure* in hurting, not just hurting for a goal or necessity.
