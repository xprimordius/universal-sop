Removing legitimate hard inquiries from your credit report without closing the associated accounts is generally not possible. Hard inquiries occur when you apply for credit, and they remain on your credit report for up to two years, impacting your credit score for about one year. These inquiries are a factual record of your credit activity and cannot be removed unless they are unauthorized or erroneous. citeturn0search9

**Steps to Address Unauthorized or Erroneous Hard Inquiries:**

1. **Review Your Credit Reports:**
   - Obtain your credit reports from the three major credit bureaus—Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion—through [AnnualCreditReport.com](https://www.annualcreditreport.com).
   - Identify any hard inquiries you do not recognize.

2. **Contact the Creditor:**
   - If you find an unfamiliar inquiry, contact the creditor directly to verify its legitimacy. The creditor's contact information is typically listed on your credit report. citeturn0search5
   - If the inquiry was made in error or is fraudulent, request the creditor to send a letter to each credit bureau to remove the inquiry from your report.

3. **Dispute the Inquiry with Credit Bureaus:**
   - If the creditor does not resolve the issue, file a dispute with each credit bureau reporting the unauthorized inquiry.
   - Provide documentation supporting your claim, such as correspondence with the creditor or a police report if identity theft is suspected.
   - The credit bureaus are required to investigate disputes and remove any unverifiable or inaccurate information. citeturn0search2

**Preventive Measures:**

- **Monitor Your Credit Regularly:**
  - Regularly check your credit reports to detect unauthorized inquiries promptly.

- **Place a Fraud Alert or Credit Freeze:**
  - If you suspect identity theft, consider placing a fraud alert or credit freeze on your credit reports to prevent further unauthorized inquiries. citeturn0news15

Remember, legitimate hard inquiries are part of your credit history and cannot be removed unless they are incorrect or fraudulent. Maintaining good credit habits, such as timely payments and low credit utilization, will help mitigate the impact of hard inquiries over time.
