## 🤖 ChatGPT

To convert a **3840 x 2160 (16:9 aspect ratio)** resolution to **16:10 aspect ratio**, you need to adjust either the width or the height while maintaining the desired ratio.

For **16:10**, the aspect ratio formula is:

\[
\text{Width} : \text{Height} = 16 : 10
\]

### Steps:
1. **Fix the width (3840) and adjust the height:**
   \[
   \text{Height} = \frac{\text{Width}}{16} \times 10 = \frac{3840}{16} \times 10 = 2400
   \]
   Resulting resolution: **3840 x 2400**

2. **Fix the height (2160) and adjust the width:**
   \[
   \text{Width} = \frac{\text{Height}}{10} \times 16 = \frac{2160}{10} \times 16 = 3456
   \]
   Resulting resolution: **3456 x 2160**

### Final Resolutions:
- **3840 x 2400** (Maintains width)
- **3456 x 2160** (Maintains height)

Choose the one that best suits your use case!
