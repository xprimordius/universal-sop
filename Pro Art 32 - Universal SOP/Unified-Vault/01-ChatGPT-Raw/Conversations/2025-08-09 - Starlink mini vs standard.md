Yes — the **Starlink Standard will work in your Tesla’s rear 12V outlet**, but with caveats:  

**1. Power draw vs Tesla 12V limit**  
- Rear 12V is ~120W continuous limit.  
- Standard averages **50–75W** but can spike to **90–100+W** during boot/aiming.  
- Spikes are *close* to that ceiling — if anything else shares that circuit, you risk tripping it.  

**2. Plug type**  
- Standard kit is AC-powered. You’d need a **small inverter** (150W+ continuous) plugged into the rear 12V.  
- Keep inverter close to outlet with heavy-gauge wiring to avoid voltage drop.

**3. Heat**  
- Inverter + confined space can build heat. Ventilate or mount where air can circulate.  

**4. Efficiency loss**  
- 12V → inverter → AC adds ~10–15% draw, so expect ~55–85W actual pull from the Tesla battery when running Standard.  

✅ **Bottom line:** Works, but safest if it’s the *only* thing on that outlet and you use a quality low-watt inverter.
