If you're running **20 miles per week**, you should replace your running shoes approximately **every 3 to 4 months**, depending on the model and wear patterns. Most running shoes last between **300 to 500 miles**, so at 20 miles per week:

- **300 miles** → ~15 weeks (about 3.5 months)
- **500 miles** → ~25 weeks (about 6 months)

Signs you need new shoes sooner:
- Increased joint or foot pain
- Worn-out tread
- Compressed or uneven midsole
- Loss of cushioning and support

Rotating between two pairs can extend their lifespan.
