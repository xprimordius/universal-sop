### 🧠 **MS = Multiple Sclerosis**

**Definition:**  
A chronic autoimmune condition where the immune system attacks the **myelin sheath** (the protective covering of nerves in the brain and spinal cord), disrupting nerve communication.

---

### ⚠️ Key Symptoms:
- **Numbness/tingling** on one side of the body or limbs  
- **Muscle weakness** or loss of coordination  
- **Vision problems** (blurry or double vision)  
- **Fatigue**  
- **Cognitive changes**  
- **Balance and walking issues**  

---

### 🧪 Diagnosis:
- MRI (to detect brain/spinal lesions)  
- Lumbar puncture (spinal fluid analysis)  
- Neurological exam

---

### 🧬 Cause:
- Autoimmune—exact cause unknown, but genetics + environment play a role

---

If you're experiencing **nerve damage, numbness, or weakness on one side**, it's important to see a **neurologist** immediately. Other conditions like **stroke, pinched nerves, or neuropathy** can present similarly, so early testing is critical.
