### 4️⃣ Simple Method
1. Preheat air fryer **2–3 min**
2. Season patty (salt/pepper)
3. Place in basket (single layer)
4. Cook **half time → flip**
5. Check temp **160°F**

- Make a **small thumb indent** in center → prevents dome.

- Rest **1–2 min** before eating.
