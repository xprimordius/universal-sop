## 🤖 ChatGPT

In **Minnesota**, an **NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement)** generally remains **legally enforceable** even after being laid off, **unless**:  

1. **Time Limit Expired** – Check if the NDA has a **timeframe** (e.g., "This NDA lasts for 2 years after termination").  
2. **Scope of Confidentiality** – If it only applies while employed, it may not cover post-employment.  
3. **Public Information** – If the info became public or is non-confidential, the NDA may not apply.  
4. **Unenforceable Terms** – Overbroad, unreasonable, or vague NDAs might be invalid.  
5. **Retaliation or Coercion** – If the NDA was signed under **duress** or unfair circumstances, it could be challenged.  

If you’re concerned about **liability or enforcement**, review the NDA terms carefully or consult an employment attorney in MN.
