### 2. Attempt “Substituted Service” / “Alternate Service”  
If personal service fails after diligent efforts, ask the court to allow alternate methods (depending on your jurisdiction) such as:  
- Leaving papers with a responsible person at his last known residence or workplace  
- Mailing to the last known address

### 3. Service by Publication (Last Resort)

### 4. Motion / Court Request  
- File a motion with the court explaining that he purposefully refused to disclose address and that your good-faith efforts failed. 
- Ask the court for *permission* to use alternate service or publication.  
- The court will weigh due process rights — you must show you’ve done everything reasonable.

---

### 5. Keep Detailed Records  
Document every attempt to locate or serve him:  
- Dates, times, addresses, persons checked, calls made  
- Evidence from database searches, investigators, contacts  
- Return receipts, affidavits from server  
This is your shield if he later claims improper service.

---

### 6. Jurisdiction Matters  
Service rules differ by state (and by county). What works in California may differ in Texas, etc.  
Check your state’s family law rules (or ask local counsel) for “service when address unknown,” “publication,” “substituted service,” etc.
