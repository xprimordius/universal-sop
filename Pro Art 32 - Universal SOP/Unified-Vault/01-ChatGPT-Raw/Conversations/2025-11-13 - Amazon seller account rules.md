## **1. Amazon Links People, Not Just Entities**
Amazon’s enforcement is built on **identity linkage**, not “legal ownership.”  
They look for **beneficial control**, which includes:

- Your name  
- Your date of birth  
- Your government ID  
- Your address history  
- Your bank account holder  
- Your credit card holder  
- Your phone number  
- Your IP address  
- Your device fingerprint  
- Your browser cookies  
- Your tax information (SSN/EIN)  
- Shared employees or log-ins  

If *any* of these match, the new account is treated as **circumventing a ban**, even if the paper trail goes:

Offshore Trust → Holding LLC → Operating LLC.

---

## **2. The corporate veil doesn’t matter to Amazon**
Legal layering protects you from **lawsuits**, not from **platform compliance systems**.

Amazon’s position is:

> “If you are the beneficial owner or controlling party of a deactivated account,  
> **you cannot open another seller account** without Amazon’s explicit approval.”

Even if ownership is private legally, Amazon only cares about **internal linkage signals**.

---

## **3. Risk Score Reality**
Even with a trust and multiple entities, the following will immediately link:

- Logging into the new account from a device used before  
- Using the same home/business Wi-Fi  
- Using the same laptop or phone  
- Using the same bank routing (even a different business name)  
- Using the same phone number or email domain  
- Similar document metadata in uploaded PDFs  
- IP history patterns that match the old account’s region  
- Geolocation fingerprinting from your mobile device

Amazon has **extremely aggressive anti-circumvention AI** designed specifically to catch banned sellers opening “new company” accounts.

---

## **4. What *would* work?**
There are only two compliant pathways:

### **A. Appeal and reinstate the original account**  
Amazon *prefers* that.

### **B. Fully independent ownership (you cannot be a beneficiary or controller)**  
This means:

- You cannot be the manager  
- You cannot be a signer  
- You cannot be the beneficial owner  
- You cannot access the account  
- You cannot log in  
- You cannot receive money  
- You cannot control operations  

Anything short of that = **linked and banned**.

A trust where you are the beneficiary is **still considered control** for Amazon’s risk models because money flows back to you.
