| **Ingredient**              | **Amount**         |
|----------------------------|--------------------|
| **Citrus Vodka**           | 1.5 oz (45 ml)     |
| **Cointreau** (or Triple Sec) | 1 oz (30 ml)   |

| **Fresh Lime Juice**       | 0.5 oz (15 ml)     |

#### 🔧 Instructions:
1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.  
2. Shake hard until ice-cold.  
3. Strain into a chilled **martini glass**.  
4. Garnish with a **lime wedge** or **orange peel twist**.

---

### ✅ Flavor Profile  
- Tart  
- Slightly sweet  
- Citrus-forward  
- Clean, crisp finish

### 💡 Tips for Mastery

- Use **citrus vodka** for authenticity (Absolut Citron was the original).  
- Don’t oversweeten — the cranberry juice should be light.
