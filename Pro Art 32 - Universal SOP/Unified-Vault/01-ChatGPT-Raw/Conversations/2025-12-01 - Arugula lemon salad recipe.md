## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Arugula & Lemon Salad (Simple, Elite Flavor)**  

**Ingredients**  
- 4 cups baby arugula (fresh, crisp)  
- Juice of ½–1 lemon (to taste)  
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil  
- ¼ cup shaved Parmesan or Pecorino  
- Sea salt + fresh cracked pepper  
- Optional: toasted pine nuts or walnuts  

**Directions**  
1. Whisk lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper until emulsified.  
2. Toss arugula gently with dressing right before serving.  
3. Top with shaved Parmesan and nuts if desired.  

**Pro Tip:**  
Add thin-sliced pear or avocado for balance. Grilled shrimp or chicken turns it into a perfect light meal.  

Would you like it **sweet (honey + fruit)** or **savory (garlic + cheese)** next?

**Arugula Lemon Power Salad (No Cheese)**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 4 cups arugula  
- Juice of 1 lemon  
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil  
- Sea salt + cracked black pepper  
- ½ avocado, sliced or cubed  
- Optional add-ons: toasted nuts (almond, walnut, or pine nut), cherry tomatoes, cucumber ribbons  

**Instructions:**  
1. Whisk lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper into a bright dressing.  
2. Toss arugula lightly in dressing until just coated.  
3. Add avocado and any extras on top — don’t overdress.  

**Flavor boost options:**  
- Add **1 tsp Dijon mustard** to the dressing for tang.  
- Or add **½ tsp honey or maple syrup** for balance.

Perfect — here’s the **Arugula Lemon Soft-Boiled Egg Salad**:  

**Ingredients:**  
- 4 cups arugula  
- 2 soft-boiled eggs (6½ minutes, jammy center)  
- Juice of 1 lemon  
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil  
- Sea salt + cracked black pepper  
- Optional: sliced avocado or toasted nuts  

**Instructions:**  
1. Whisk lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper for dressing.  
2. Toss arugula lightly until just coated.  
3. Top with halved soft-boiled eggs.  
4. Finish with a sprinkle of salt and a drizzle of extra oil over the eggs.  

**Upgrade options:**  
- Add a pinch of chili flakes or herbs (dill, chives, parsley).  
- For texture, add roasted chickpeas or crushed pistachios.  

Would you like it **warm (slightly wilted arugula with the egg)** or **fresh and chilled**?
