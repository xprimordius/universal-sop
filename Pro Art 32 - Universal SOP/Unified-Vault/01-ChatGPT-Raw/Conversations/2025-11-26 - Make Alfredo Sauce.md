How to make Alfredo sauce

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Simple Homemade Alfredo Sauce**

**Ingredients:**
- ½ cup (1 stick) butter  
- 1 cup heavy cream  
- 1 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese  
- 2 cloves garlic (minced)  
- Salt and pepper to taste  
- Optional: pinch of nutmeg or parsley for garnish  

**Instructions:**
1. **Melt butter** in a saucepan over medium heat.  
2. **Add garlic** and sauté 30 seconds until fragrant.  
3. **Pour in heavy cream** and bring to a gentle simmer (don’t boil).  
4. **Whisk in Parmesan** gradually until smooth and thickened (3–5 min).  
5. **Season** with salt, pepper, and optional nutmeg.  
6. **Serve immediately** over pasta, chicken, or veggies.  

**Tips:**
- Use *freshly grated* Parmesan — pre-shredded won’t melt as smoothly.  
- If too thick, whisk in a bit more cream or pasta water.  
- Sauce thickens as it cools, so serve warm.

**Easy Sugared Cranberries**

**Ingredients:**  
- 2 cups fresh cranberries  
- 1 cup granulated sugar (plus extra for coating)  
- ½ cup water  

**Instructions:**  
1. **Make syrup:** In a saucepan, heat ½ cup water + ½ cup sugar until sugar dissolves. Don’t boil.  
2. **Add cranberries:** Stir in cranberries until coated, then remove from heat. Let sit 5–10 min.  
3. **Drain:** Use a slotted spoon to transfer cranberries to a wire rack. Let them dry about 1 hour — they should be tacky, not wet.  
4. **Coat:** Roll cranberries in remaining sugar until fully covered.  
5. **Dry:** Let sit 30 min to set.  

**Tips:**  
- Use superfine sugar for a more sparkly finish.  
- Store uncovered at room temp up to 2 days (they’ll stay crisp).  
- Great for garnish, charcuterie boards, or holiday desserts.

**Perfect Sparkling Sugared Cranberries (Restaurant-Quality Finish)**  

**Ingredients:**  
- 12 oz (≈3 cups) fresh cranberries  
- 1 cup water  
- 1 cup white sugar (for syrup)  
- ¾–1 cup superfine sugar (for coating — key to sparkle)  

**Instructions:**  
1. **Simmer Syrup:** In a saucepan, heat water + 1 cup sugar over medium until dissolved. Remove from heat before boiling.  
2. **Soak Cranberries:** Add cranberries, stir to coat evenly, then let sit 10 minutes. Don’t burst them.  
3. **Dry Properly:** Using a slotted spoon, transfer to a wire rack over parchment. Let dry 45–60 minutes until tacky (not wet).  
4. **Double-Coat:** Roll in half the superfine sugar, let rest 15 minutes, then roll again for a frosty, diamond-like finish.  
5. **Set:** Let air-dry 1 hour.  

**Pro Tips:**  
- Use *superfine* (caster) sugar — granules cling better than regular sugar.  
- For longer-lasting shine, store uncovered in a cool, dry spot up to 2 days.  
- Add a drop of vanilla or orange extract to syrup for a gourmet twist.  
- Optional: dust lightly with powdered sugar just before serving for a snow-glazed look.
