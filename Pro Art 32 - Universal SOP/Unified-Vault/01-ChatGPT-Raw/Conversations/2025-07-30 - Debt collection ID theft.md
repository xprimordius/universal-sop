## 🤖 ChatGPT

### **1. Is Radius Global Solutions LLC a third-party collector for M&T Bank?**
- **Yes.** Radius Global Solutions LLC is a third-party debt collection agency. They collect debts on behalf of creditors like M&T Bank or sometimes purchase debts outright.  
- If they are collecting on behalf of M&T Bank, it means the debt is likely still owned by M&T Bank but assigned to Radius. If Radius purchased the debt, they would now be the owner.

---

### **2. Should your friend pay?**
- **Do NOT pay until the debt is validated.**
  1. Request a **Debt Validation Letter** (FDCPA §809) within 30 days of first notice. This forces Radius to prove the debt is legitimate and that your friend owes it.
  2. If it's **not your friend's debt (possible ID theft)**, paying would legitimize it and hurt any future disputes.

---

### **3. How to flag as ID theft (FCRA §605B)**  
If your friend believes it’s not his debt:
1. **File an FTC Identity Theft Report:**  
   - [IdentityTheft.gov](https://www.identitytheft.gov/) → “Get Started.”
   - Save the PDF report.
2. **File a police report** (local police or online if available).
3. **Send a Section 605B Block Request** to Radius and the credit bureaus:  
   - Include:  
     - FTC report  
     - Police report  
     - A statement that the account was the result of identity theft and not authorized  
     - A copy of government-issued ID and proof of address  
   - Demand they **block the account from his credit report** and cease collection activity.

---

### **4. Immediate steps:**  
- **Do not call or verbally confirm anything.**  
- **Send written letters via certified mail** (keep copies).  
- If Radius does not respond properly, file CFPB and AG complaints.
