Yes, doctors **can and do remove** small pea-sized lumps under the skin, especially when they’re recurring or painful. What you’re describing sounds consistent with **hidradenitis suppurativa (HS)**, a chronic skin condition that causes inflamed, cyst-like nodules in areas such as the groin, armpits, and thighs.  

**What doctors usually do:**
1. **Diagnosis:** A dermatologist examines the lesions and may confirm HS based on appearance and pattern.  
2. **Treatment options:**
   - **Incision and drainage** for painful cysts (short-term relief).  
   - **Steroid injections or antibiotics** to reduce inflammation and infection.  
   - **Long-term management** with prescription medications (topical clindamycin, oral antibiotics, hormonal therapy, or biologics like Humira).  
   - **Surgical removal** of chronic or scarred areas when lumps repeatedly form in the same spots.  

You should schedule an appointment with a **dermatologist or gynecologist familiar with HS**. Early treatment prevents scarring and helps reduce flare-ups.
