A **NAV Prime Business Score** of 40 is considered below average and may be due to several factors, even with a new trade line. Here's why your score might be low:

1. **New Trade Line, No History:**
   - A newly opened trade line has no spending or payment history yet. Business credit scores rely heavily on established payment behavior and utilization.

2. **Lack of Business Credit History:**
   - If your business credit file is thin (few or no other trade lines or accounts), it might negatively impact your score. Credit bureaus need sufficient data to assess your risk level.

3. **No Payment Activity:**
   - Business credit scores (e.g., D&B PAYDEX or Experian Intelliscore) improve when you make consistent, on-time payments. Without any transactions or payments, there’s no positive payment history to boost the score.

4. **High Risk Category or NAICS Code:**
   - Certain industries are flagged as higher risk, which can affect your business credit score, even if you have no negative activity.

5. **Errors or Inaccurate Data:**
   - Check your report for any inaccuracies, such as incorrect late payments or derogatory marks. This can sometimes happen, even with a new trade line.

6. **Limited Credit Mix:**
   - Having only one trade line limits your credit diversity, which can hinder your score growth.

### Immediate Steps to Improve:
1. **Use the Trade Line:**
   - Make small purchases and pay them off early to build payment history.
   
2. **Open Additional Trade Lines:**
   - Consider adding more accounts, such as vendor accounts, to diversify and strengthen your credit file.

3. **Monitor Your Report:**
   - Regularly check for errors and disputes through NAV or other credit monitoring services.

4. **Utilize Credit Responsibly:**
   - Maintain a low utilization rate if using a revolving credit account. Aim to use less than 30% of your available credit limit.

Building business credit takes time, but consistent and responsible credit behavior will improve your score. If issues persist, review your NAV report or contact NAV support for clarification.
