### **1. Co-Sale / Wholesale**
- **Timeline:** 15–30 days  
- **Profit:** $10K–$20K  
- **Description:**  
  - You secure the property under contract and sell the contract to another investor without closing.  
  - Fastest exit; no renovation required.  
  - Quick cash but smaller profit.

---

### **2. Wholetail**
- **Timeline:** 2–3 months  
- **Profit:** $25K–$40K  
- **Description:**  
  - Buy the property, make minimal repairs to make it marketable (clean-up, light fixes), then list on MLS.  
  - Moderate speed and profit.  
  - Appeals to retail buyers or investors.

---

### **3. BRRR / Rental**
- **Timeline:** Long-term  
- **Profit:** Ongoing mailbox money + property appreciation  
- **Description:**  
  - **Buy → Rehab → Rent → Refinance (BRRR)** to hold as a rental.  
  - Generates **cash flow and equity growth** over time.  
  - Wealth-building strategy rather than quick cash.

---

### **4. Fix & Flip**
- **Timeline:** 6 months  
- **Profit:** ~$50K per property  
- **Description:**  
  - Full rehab to maximize property value and sell for top market price.  
  - Largest short-term profit but requires the most time, capital, and management.

## 1) Backsolve the Numbers (non‑negotiable)
**Required spread:**  
ARV − (Purchase + Reno + Soft/Holding + Refi costs) ≥ **$100K**

**Max Purchase Price (MPP):**  
MPP = **ARV − TargetEquity − Reno − Soft/Holding − RefiCosts**

**Cash‑out check:**  
Cash‑out = (Refi LTV × ARV) − (All‑in basis after rehab)  
→ Must ≥ “payback + next deal seed.”

**Rules of thumb**
- Aim for **25–35%** discount to ARV all‑in.  
- Use **70% MAO variant** when quick-screening: MAO ≈ 0.70×ARV − Reno − Fees − EquityTarget.

**Example (plug + play)**  
ARV $600k; Reno $120k; Soft/Holding/Refi $45k; Target Equity $100k  
→ **MPP ≤ $335k.**  
If refi at 70% LTV: loan = **$420k.**  
All‑in at MPP = 335+120+45 = **$500k** → too high (loan < basis).  
So either: lower price, lower reno, or higher ARV.  
Winning config: drop price to **$300k** → All‑in **$465k**; loan **$420k**; leave-in **$45k** (often covered via private gap or small cash). Equity still **$135k**.

## 2) The Buy Box (only hunt what fits)
- **Zip blocks:** Median ARV $450k–$850k w/ clear comp velocity.  
- **Property:** Ugly in a nice area; PPSF **≤60–65%** of renovated PPSF.  
- **Structure:** 3/1→3/2+ or 2/1→3/2 conversions; garages/basements for SF add; ADU/duplex zoning possible.  
- **Lot/Utility:** ≥5,000 sf lots, good setbacks, no funk (power lines, highways).  
- **Title/HOA:** Clean title, lenient HOA or none.

## 3) Where the $100K Comes From (value levers)

- **ADU / Jr. ADU:** Rent + higher appraisal comps.  
- **Layout fixes:** Open walls, add primary suite, modern kitchen triangle.  
- **Curb + systems:** Roof/HVAC/windows boost appraisal quality rating.  
- **Zoning angle:** Split lots, convert to duplex/STR‑friendly.

**On‑market (speed plays):**

4) Backsolve **MPP** (section 1). If list ≤ MPP + $10k, pursue.
