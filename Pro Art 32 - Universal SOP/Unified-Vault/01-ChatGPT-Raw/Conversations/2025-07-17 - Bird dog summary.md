## 🐦 Bird Dogging Summary (Pace Morby 4-Day Challenge Part 1)

### 🔑 Core Concept:
**Bird dogging** is finding potential real estate deals (distressed properties, motivated sellers, etc.) and passing them to a buyer (usually a wholesaler or investor) for a fee—without needing capital, a license, or experience.

---

### 🚀 Step-by-Step Breakdown:

#### 1. **Mindset & Identity Shift**
- **You're a lead generator**, not a real estate agent.
- Your **value is speed and quality of lead flow**.
- Your job is **not to close**, just to **connect motivated sellers** with buyers.

#### 2. **Find a Buyer's Buy Box**
- Ask investors:  
  - “What are you looking for?”  
  - “What zip codes?”  
  - “What types of properties?”  
  - “How fast can you close?”
- Get clear on:
  - Budget
  - Condition preferences
  - Desired returns (ARV, cash flow, flip potential)

#### 3. **Find Distressed Properties (Opportunities)**
- Drive for dollars (look for boarded windows, tall grass, etc.)
- Zillow FSBO
- Facebook Marketplace
- Craigslist
- Probate or pre-foreclosure lists
- Code violations from city
- Tax delinquent properties
- Vacant land and mobile homes

#### 4. **Qualify the Seller**
Use the **Four Pillars**:
1. **Condition** of the property
2. **Timeline** to sell
3. **Motivation** (why are they selling?)
4. **Price** expectations

Keep the convo natural, use phrases like:
- “What’s going on with the property?”
- “Why are you selling?”
- “What’s the ideal outcome for you?”

#### 5. **Send the Deal to Your Buyer**
- Provide clear, concise info:
  - Address
  - Pictures (if possible)
  - Four Pillars summary
  - Asking price

Text example:  
> “Hey I got a lead in [zip]. 3/2, needs light rehab, tenant leaving in 2 weeks. Seller wants $190K. ARV is around $285K. Interested?”

#### 6. **Get Paid**
- Set up a simple JV agreement or assignment fee.
- Payment typically happens **when deal closes**.

---

### 🧠 Pro Tips:
- Use a **CRM (like Trello, Google Sheets)** to track leads.
- Be consistent. 1 lead a day = 30/month.
- Build long-term buyer relationships—**be reliable.**
- Be honest. Don’t promise what you can’t deliver.
