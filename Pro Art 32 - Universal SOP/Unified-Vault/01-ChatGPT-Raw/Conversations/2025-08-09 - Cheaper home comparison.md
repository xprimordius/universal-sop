What’s cheaper? New construction home or mobile home delivered

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Generally:  

- **Mobile home delivered** is cheaper upfront — often **$50K–$150K** depending on size, quality, and setup (utilities, foundation, permits).  
- **New construction home** (stick-built) is usually **$150K–$500K+**, with higher land, labor, and permitting costs.  

**Bottom line:** A mobile home is typically **50–80% cheaper** than a new construction house for the same square footage, but long-term value, financing, and appreciation differ.

## 👤 You

Why and how are they cheaper

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Why Mobile Homes Are Cheaper**  

1. **Factory-Built Efficiency** – Mobile/manufactured homes are built in bulk in controlled factories, cutting labor and material waste.  
2. **Lower Labor Costs** – No weather delays or large on-site crews; assembly-line methods reduce labor hours.  
3. **Simpler Foundation** – Often placed on piers, slabs, or block foundations instead of full basements.  
4. **Lower Material Standards** – Many use lighter framing, thinner insulation, and cost-effective finishes compared to site-built codes.  
5. **Smaller Land Costs** – Often placed on inexpensive lots or rented land.  
6. **Fewer Regulatory Costs** – HUD code is national and less complex than many local building codes for site-built homes.  

**How They Stay Cheaper**  

- **Mass Production** – Buying materials in huge quantities lowers per-unit cost.  
- **Shorter Build Time** – Weeks instead of months = less financing interest and carrying costs.  
- **Streamlined Transport & Setup** – Delivered in sections, connected on-site, minimal on-location labor.
