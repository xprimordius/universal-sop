## 👤 You

Can I reuse 2 day old olive oil / tallow / butter used to cook eggs and steak the other day? It was covered in the stove and was reused last night

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes, you **can reuse** 2-day-old olive oil, tallow, or butter **if**:

1. **It was covered** (protected from dust/air).
2. **Not overheated** (no smoke point exceeded).
3. **No burning or off smell/taste**.
4. **No food bits left behind** (strain them out if needed).

**Olive oil:** okay 1–2 reuses if not heavily heated.  
**Tallow:** stable, can handle multiple reuses.  
**Butter:** less stable, degrades faster—limit to 1–2 uses max.

**Best practice:** smell it, check for darkening, bitterness, or sourness. If clean, it's usable.
