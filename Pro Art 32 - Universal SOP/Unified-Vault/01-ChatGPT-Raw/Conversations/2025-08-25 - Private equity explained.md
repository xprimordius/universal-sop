## 👤 You

What is private equity

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Private Equity: Simple Breakdown**

- **Definition:** Private equity (PE) is investment capital raised from wealthy individuals and institutions to buy, invest in, or restructure private companies (not publicly traded) — or to take public companies private.  

- **How It Works:**  
  1. **Investors (LPs):** Pension funds, endowments, wealthy individuals contribute capital.  
  2. **PE Firm (GPs):** Manages the money, identifies opportunities, and makes investments.  
  3. **Target Companies:** Typically underperforming, undervalued, or high-growth potential.  
  4. **Value Creation:** Improve operations, cut costs, restructure, or scale growth.  
  5. **Exit:** Sell the company via IPO, merger, or sale — aiming for high returns.  

- **Key Traits:**  
  - Long-term horizon (5–10 years per investment).  
  - High risk, high reward.  
  - Heavy use of **leverage (debt financing)**.  
  - Focused on **control and active management**, unlike passive stock investors.  

- **Main Strategies:**  
  - **Buyouts (LBOs):** Acquiring companies mostly with borrowed money.  
  - **Growth Equity:** Injecting capital into fast-growing firms to scale.  
  - **Venture Capital (a subset):** Early-stage investments in startups.  
  - **Distressed/Turnaround:** Buying struggling companies and restructuring them.  

👉 In short: **Private equity = pooled money + professional managers + buying/transforming companies → exiting for big returns.**

---

## 1. **Position Yourself as the Operator**
Wealthy people don’t want to babysit projects — they want **trusted operators**.  
- **Your role:** The developer/investor who finds deals, runs numbers, manages contractors, and ensures compliance.  
- **Their role:** Passive capital provider earning predictable returns.

---

## 2. **Investor Pools to Target**
- **High-Net-Worth Individuals (HNWIs):** Business owners, doctors, lawyers, executives.  
- **Family Offices:** Small firms managing $10M–$500M for ultra-wealthy families.  
- **Private Lenders/Hard Money:** Wealthy individuals already lending into real estate.  
- **Private Equity / Real Estate Syndicators:** Already structured to pool money into developments.  
- **Local Wealth Networks:** Country clubs, angel networks, YPO/Vistage groups, real estate investor associations (REIAs).

---

## 3. **Where to Find Them**
- **Networking:** REIAs, developer conferences, Urban Land Institute, local builder associations.  
- **Capital Aggregators:** CrowdStreet, RealtyMogul, Fundrise (online syndication platforms).  
- **Private Bankers/CPAs/Attorneys:** Gatekeepers to wealthy clients — build trust, ask for introductions.  
- **LinkedIn / Investor Groups:** Target accredited investors directly.  

---

## 4. **Structure the Deal**
- **Preferred Return:** 8–12% annually (investors get paid first).  
- **Equity Split:** 70/30 or 80/20 (investors get majority of profits, you keep the promote).  
- **Secured Note:** Offer collateral (property/land) for lower risk.  
- **Exit Timeline:** Show clear liquidity events (refi, sale, condo sell-off).  

---

## 5. **Credibility Materials**
Wealthy people invest based on **trust + clarity**:  
- **Pitch Deck / One Pager:** The deal, timeline, projected returns.  
- **Pro Forma:** Conservative financial model.  
- **Track Record:** If no track record → borrow credibility via your GC, architect, or mentor with experience.  
- **Legal Structure:** Use LLC + Private Placement Memorandum (PPM) to stay compliant.  

---

## 6. **Leverage SEC Rules (Important)**
- **Reg D 506(b):** Raise from people you already know (no public solicitation).  
- **Reg D 506(c):** You can publicly market, but investors must be verified “accredited.”  
⚠️ Always run raises through securities attorneys — non-compliance = big penalties.
