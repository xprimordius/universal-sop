## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here’s the **concealed carry (CCW/CHL) overview** for the states you listed:  

---

## **Minnesota (MN)**
- Called *Permit to Carry a Pistol* (PCP).  
- Required for concealed or open carry.  
- Must be 21+, complete training, background check.  
- Reciprocity: Recognizes some out-of-state permits, but not all.  

---

## **Colorado (CO)**
- Concealed carry requires a *Concealed Handgun Permit (CHP)*.  
- Must be 21+, resident, pass background + training.  
- Open carry is legal in most areas (Denver restricts).  
- Reciprocity with many states.  

---

## **Nevada (NV)**
- Requires a state-issued *Concealed Firearm Permit (CFP)*.  
- Must be 21+, complete approved training, pass background.  
- Reciprocity with many states (not all).  
- Open carry is legal without a permit.  

---

## **California (CA)**
- Strictest of your list.  
- *CCW permit* issued by county sheriff or city police.  
- "Shall-issue" under Bruen ruling, but still limited, varies widely by county.  
- Requires training, background, good moral character.  
- Reciprocity: **No out-of-state permits recognized.**  

---

## **Washington (WA)**
- Requires a *Concealed Pistol License (CPL)*.  
- Must be 21+, fingerprinting, background check.  
- No training requirement.  
- Reciprocity: recognizes some states’ permits.  
- Open carry legal without permit (with conditions).  

---

## **Oregon (OR)**
- Requires *Concealed Handgun License (CHL)*.  
- Must be 21+, complete training, background.  
- Reciprocity: only recognizes a few states.  
- Open carry legal, but cities like Portland can restrict without a CHL.  

---

## **Texas (TX)**
- *Permitless carry* (Constitutional Carry) for 21+.  
- You can carry concealed or openly without a license.  
- *License to Carry (LTC)* still available (useful for reciprocity with other states).  
- Reciprocity: TX LTC widely recognized nationwide.  

---

⚠️ **Key Notes:**  
- Reciprocity is inconsistent. For example, a Texas LTC works in many states, but not CA or OR.  
- Federal law: you can transport firearms across state lines if unloaded + locked case (FOPA).  
- Always check **local/county laws** (Denver, Portland, Los Angeles, etc. have tighter restrictions).
