Can I bring scissors on airplane

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes, but with limits.  

- **TSA rules (U.S.):** Scissors are allowed in carry-on bags if the blades are **shorter than 4 inches** (measured from the pivot point).  
- **Longer blades:** Must go in checked baggage.  
- **Pointed scissors:** Same 4-inch rule applies.  
- **Child or safety scissors (blunt tip):** Generally fine in carry-on.  
- **International flights:** Other countries may have stricter rules—always check the airline and airport security site.  

⚠️ If in doubt, put them in checked luggage to avoid confiscation at security.
