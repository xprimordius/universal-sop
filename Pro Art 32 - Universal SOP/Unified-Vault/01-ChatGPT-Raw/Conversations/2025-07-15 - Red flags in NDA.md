## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes — here are the **main red flags** and issues in the ACE DEALS NDA you uploaded:

---

### ⚠️ **1. Irrevocable and Non-Cancelable (Red Flag)**
- **Clause:** The agreement is labeled *“irrevocable and non-cancelable”* for **3 years**.
- **Risk:** You cannot exit the agreement even if the business becomes unethical or harmful.
- **Normal?** No — most NDAs allow mutual termination.

---

### ⚠️ **2. $150,000 Circumvention Penalty (Excessive and Possibly Unenforceable)**
- **Clause:** $150,000 penalty if you “circumvent, avoid, or bypass.”
- **Problem:** Broadly defined and doesn’t specify how damages are calculated.
- **Risk:** Could be used punitively without real proof of harm.

---

### ⚠️ **3. Ambiguous Scope of “Introductions”**
- **Clause:** Any “individual or entity introduced” is off-limits unless permission is granted.
- **Risk:** If someone casually mentions a contact or sends an email CC, you could be contractually restricted from ever working with them.
- **No time limit per contact** — this can be abused.

---

### ⚠️ **4. Dispute Resolution Location: Denver, CO (Jurisdiction Risk)**
- **Clause:** All disputes go to arbitration in **Denver, Colorado**, regardless of where you live or operate.
- **Risk:** Inconvenient and costly to you if you're in another state (e.g., Minnesota or Texas).

---

### ⚠️ **5. Implied Gag Clause / Confidentiality**
- **Clause:** Overly broad non-disclosure language — includes “negligent or inadvertent” disclosures.
- **Risk:** Could be used to silence fair reviews or whistleblowing.

---

### ⚠️ **6. No Clarification of What Constitutes a “Violation”**
- **Clause:** No clear examples or proof burden. If they *think* you bypassed them, they could claim breach.
- **Risk:** Open to abuse and threat of legal action.

---

### ⚠️ **7. Fax Signatures and Overbroad Binding**
- **Clause:** Fax signatures accepted; agreement binds all “associated companies, individuals, and entities... whether named or not.”
- **Risk:** Could be stretched to include future entities, partners, or businesses unrelated to this deal.

---

### ✅ **Legitimate Uses (Contextually)**
- It's structured like a **non-circumvention agreement** commonly used in brokered introductions — to protect against deal theft.  
- But the **rigidity, scope, and penalty** terms are far more aggressive than standard.

---

### 🛡️ Recommendation:
- **Do not sign** unless redlined heavily.
- If already signed, **seek legal review immediately.**
- You can **negotiate these terms** to include:
  - Mutual termination clause.
  - Reduced circumvention penalty or damages based on actual loss.
  - Clear definition of "introduction" and scope.
  - Jurisdiction in your state or mutually agreed neutral venue.
