# Multiple C Corps Ownership

Yes, you can have multiple C Corporations (C Corps) with the same three people as shareholders, directors, or officers. There are no legal restrictions on the number of C Corps a group of individuals can own or manage. Each corporation is treated as a separate legal entity, regardless of the individuals involved, allowing for distinct businesses or ventures to be operated independently under different C Corps.

However, it's important to ensure that each C Corp is properly registered and maintained according to state and federal regulations. Additionally, it's advisable to consult with a legal or financial advisor to ensure that the structure aligns with your business goals and to avoid potential conflicts of interest or other legal issues.
