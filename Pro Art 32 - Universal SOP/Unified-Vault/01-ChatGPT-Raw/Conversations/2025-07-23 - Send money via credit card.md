To send money from a **credit card to your friend's Cash App**, follow these steps carefully:

---

### ⚠️ Important:
**Cash App does not let you send money directly from a credit card to another user for free.** You *can* link a credit card, but there’s a **3% fee** to send money using it.

---

### ✅ Steps:

1. **Link your credit card to Cash App:**
   - Open Cash App.
   - Tap the **profile icon** > **Linked Banks** > **Link Credit Card**.
   - Enter your card info.

2. **Send money:**
   - Tap **$**, enter amount.
   - Tap **Pay** > enter your friend’s $Cashtag.
   - Select the **credit card** as payment method.
   - Tap **Pay** to confirm (3% fee will apply).

---

### 💡 Alternative (No Fee):  
To avoid fees:

1. Use the **credit card to send money to your bank or debit card** via a service like:
   - **Plastiq**
   - **PayPal (Send to self or trusted account)**

2. Then **transfer that money to your bank**, link that bank account to Cash App, and send to your friend **for free**.
