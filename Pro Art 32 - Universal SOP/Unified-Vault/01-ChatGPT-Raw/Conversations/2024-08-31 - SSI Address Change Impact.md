## 🤖 ChatGPT

When you change your address from Nevada to Minnesota, your Supplemental Security Income (SSI) benefits may be affected due to the differences in state supplement payments. Here's what you need to consider:

1. **Federal SSI Amount**: The federal SSI payment for an individual is the same across all states, which is the amount you currently receive ($943 in your case). However, states can offer additional supplementary payments.

2. **State Supplement**: Some states offer a state supplement on top of the federal SSI payment. Nevada has its own state supplement, which might differ from Minnesota's.

   - **Nevada**: Nevada has a state supplement program that might contribute to your total SSI payment.
   - **Minnesota**: Minnesota also has a state supplement, but the amount may differ from Nevada's. The actual effect on your total SSI benefits would depend on the difference between the Nevada and Minnesota state supplements.

3. **Impact on Your Payment**: If Minnesota's state supplement is less than Nevada's, your total SSI payment might decrease when you change your address. Conversely, if Minnesota's supplement is higher, your payment could increase.

To get an exact answer:

- **Contact the SSA**: Reach out to the Social Security Administration (SSA) directly to confirm how moving from Nevada to Minnesota would impact your specific SSI payment. They can provide details on state supplements and any potential changes to your benefits.
