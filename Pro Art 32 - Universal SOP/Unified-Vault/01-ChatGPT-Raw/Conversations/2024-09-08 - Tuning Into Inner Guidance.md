Jeffrey Allen, known for his teachings on energy work and intuition, emphasizes the importance of trusting your inner guidance by tuning into your own energy and practicing discernment. Here’s how he teaches about tuning into and following inner guidance:

1. **Clarity Through Stillness**: Allen often suggests that stillness and quieting the mind are crucial for tuning into your inner guidance. When the mind is noisy or filled with doubt, it’s harder to hear your intuition. He recommends meditation and mindfulness practices to help calm the internal chatter and create space for clarity to emerge.

2. **Energy Awareness**: According to Allen, much of our inner guidance comes from our energy field. He teaches how to become aware of your energy and emotions, helping you distinguish between fear-based thoughts and intuitive insights. Understanding where your energy is blocked can also reveal why you’re experiencing doubt.

3. **Intuitive Calibration**: Allen highlights the importance of practicing and building confidence in your intuition. He suggests starting small with decisions, noticing the outcome, and gradually increasing trust in your inner guidance over time.

4. **Removing Doubt by Clearing Energy**: He often talks about doubt being a manifestation of stuck or unclear energy. Allen advises regular energy clearing techniques to release negative emotions, limiting beliefs, or confusion, which can cloud your intuition.

5. **Following Resonance**: Allen teaches to follow what feels energetically “right” or in alignment with your inner self. When you're in doubt, tune into how a decision makes you feel energetically—whether it feels expansive (which often signifies alignment) or constrictive (indicating misalignment).

6. **Trust and Practice**: Allen emphasizes that building trust in your inner guidance takes consistent practice and self-compassion. When doubts arise, instead of resisting them, gently acknowledge them, clear your energy, and re-center on the intuitive insights that feel most aligned.

To address doubt, he recommends regular tuning into your energy, meditative stillness, and practicing following your inner resonance to build confidence in your inner guidance.
