**Issue:** You can’t jump (scrub/seek) to certain parts of a song in Spotify on your Tesla Model X.

**Quick Diagnosis:**
1. **You're likely using the built-in Tesla Spotify app.**
2. Tesla’s Spotify integration **does not allow scrubbing** for certain types of tracks:
   - **Podcasts** often restrict seeking.
   - **Tracks not fully buffered** (due to slow connection or streaming rules).
   - **Free Spotify accounts** may be limited (Tesla uses account-linked access but with some quirks).

---

### 🔧 Fixes & Workarounds:

**1. Use Spotify from Your Phone via Bluetooth:**
   - Full control including scrubbing.
   - Drawback: No album art or advanced UI on the Tesla screen.

**2. Use Apple Music or Tidal (Better Integration):**
   - Tesla’s native Apple Music and Tidal apps **allow scrubbing**.
   - Better lossless and high-res audio support.

**3. Try Spotify Connect (From Your Phone):**
   - Open Spotify on your phone.
   - Tap the “device” icon and select your Tesla.
   - This often **enables full playback control**, including seeking.

**4. Premium Plan Check:**
   - Make sure your Spotify account is **Premium**, not Free.
   - Log out and log back in within the Tesla to refresh privileges.

**5. Restart Media App in Tesla:**
   - Hold down both scroll wheels on the steering wheel to reboot the infotainment system.
   - Reopen Spotify and check again.
