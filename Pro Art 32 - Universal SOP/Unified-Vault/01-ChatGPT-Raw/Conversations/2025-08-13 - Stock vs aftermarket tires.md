**Stock Juniper AWD Tires (20" Helix wheels)**  
- **Type**: Tesla equips the Model Y Juniper AWD with **all-season** tires from the factory (usually Pirelli P Zero All Season, Michelin Pilot Sport All Season 4, or Continental ProContact RX, depending on supply).  
- **Performance Balance**: Tuned for Tesla’s weight, torque, and regen braking—gives good year-round grip, but is a compromise between summer performance and winter capability.  
- **Winter Performance**: Adequate for light snow/cold, but not optimal for deep snow/ice—Tesla still recommends true winter tires in harsh climates.  
- **Noise & Efficiency**: Acoustic foam in many OEM tires reduces cabin noise; compound is optimized for efficiency to preserve range.  

**Aftermarket All-Season Tires**  
- **Flexibility**: Wider choice of compounds—can prioritize longer tread life, quieter ride, or better wet/snow grip.  
- **Potential Gains**: Some aftermarket all-seasons (e.g., Michelin CrossClimate2, Continental ExtremeContact DWS06+) outperform Tesla OEM in snow and wet handling while maintaining strong dry grip.  
- **Potential Trade-Offs**: May slightly reduce range if rolling resistance is higher; may lack Tesla-specific acoustic foam (affects cabin quietness).  

**Key Differences**  
| Feature | Stock Tesla Juniper AWD Tires | Premium Aftermarket All-Seasons |
|---|---|---|
| Compound | Balanced for range, grip, and low noise | Can be tailored for grip, life, or comfort |

| Noise | Low (acoustic foam) | Varies; foam optional |
| Range Impact | Optimized for Tesla efficiency | May be slightly higher rolling resistance |
| Longevity | Moderate tread life | Can get longer-wear options |
| Cost | Higher (OEM replacement pricing) | Often cheaper per tire, wider choice |
