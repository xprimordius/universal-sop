## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Roasted Peeled Garlic in Oven-Safe Tray — Quick Instructions**

**Ingredients:**
- 1–2 cups peeled garlic cloves  
- 2–4 tbsp olive oil  
- Optional: salt, herbs (e.g. rosemary, thyme)

**Instructions:**

1. **Preheat Oven:** 325°F (160°C)
2. **Prep Tray:** Use an oven-safe dish or foil tray. Add peeled garlic.
3. **Add Oil:** Drizzle olive oil to coat cloves evenly.
4. **Optional Seasoning:** Lightly salt and/or add herbs for flavor.
5. **Cover:** Use foil or a lid to trap steam (prevents burning).
6. **Bake:** 35–45 minutes, until cloves are golden, soft, and spreadable.
7. **Cool & Store:** Let cool. Store in airtight container with oil in fridge (up to 2 weeks).

**Tips:**  
- Watch closely after 30 min — garlic can burn fast.
- For longer storage: mash and freeze in portions.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Likely Reason:**  
Oil is overheating and vaporizing. Even covered, too much oil or high temp causes smoking.

**Possible Causes:**  
1. **Oven Too Hot:** Above ~375°F, olive oil can smoke.
2. **Too Much Oil:** Excess oil + heat = smoke.
3. **Foil Too Tight:** No venting, steam builds → oil vaporizes → smoke leaks.
4. **Tray Too Close to Heat Source:** Bottom or top rack too near heating element.

**Solution (Right Now):**
1. Lower temp to **275°F–300°F**.
2. Carefully open foil to vent steam (watch for hot vapors!).
3. If garlic looks dark, remove tray immediately.

**Next Time:**  
- Use less oil, lower temp (~300°F), and vent foil slightly.
- Olive oil’s smoke point: ~325°F–375°F (depending on type).
