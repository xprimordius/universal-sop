## 🤖 ChatGPT

### Why You Need the **Receiver** for DJI Mic 2:

The **receiver is essential** because it acts as the **bridge between the mic (transmitter) and your recording device** (camera, phone, laptop, etc.).

---

### 🔌 What the Receiver Does:

| Function | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Audio Bridge** | Captures the wireless signal from the mic and converts it to audio output. |
| **Device Connectivity** | Plugs into your phone/camera via USB-C, Lightning, or 3.5mm jack. |
| **Real-Time Monitoring** | Lets you see audio levels, adjust gain, and monitor battery. |
| **Low-Latency Sync** | Ensures clean, synchronized sound in real-time recording. |

---

### 🚫 Without the Receiver:
- Your mic **still records internally** (32-bit float), but...
- You **can’t live-monitor or transmit to your camera/phone**.
- You’d have to **manually sync audio in post**.

---

### ✅ When You Might NOT Need It:
You **don’t need the receiver** *only* if:
- You're using the **mic as a standalone recorder** and will sync in post.
- Or you're using **another DJI receiver you already own**.

---

### TLDR:
**You need the receiver** for real-time audio transmission to your device.  
**Transmitter-only** = standalone recording or a backup mic.  
**Combo** = everything you need for wireless, plug-and-play audio.
