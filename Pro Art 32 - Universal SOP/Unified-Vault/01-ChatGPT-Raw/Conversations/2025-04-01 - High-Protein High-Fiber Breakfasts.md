## 🤖 ChatGPT

It depends on the specific wording of the **choice of law** and **jurisdiction** clauses in the contract. Here’s the key distinction:  

1. **Choice of Law (MN)** – This means Minnesota law governs the dispute, but it does not necessarily determine where the case is heard.  
2. **Jurisdiction/Venue (UK)** – If the contract specifies that disputes must be resolved in UK courts, then the case must be filed there, even if MN law applies.  

However, if the contract does not explicitly state UK **jurisdiction**, a Minnesota court could still hear the case, applying MN law. Courts may also consider fairness and connections to the parties involved when enforcing foreign jurisdiction clauses.
