- 3 cloves **garlic**, minced

- 1 cup **broccoli sprouts** (or chopped broccoli if unavailable)  
- 1 cup **cauliflower florets**  
- 1 cup **kale** or **bok choy**, chopped  
- 1 medium **carrot**, thinly sliced

- 1 tbsp **dulse flakes** or **wakame seaweed**

**Protein:**  
- 1 lb **wild-caught salmon**, cut into bite-sized chunks  
- 1–2 tbsp **spirulina powder** (optional, for added detox support)

**Herbs & Seasoning:**  
- 1 bunch **cilantro**, chopped (reserve some for garnish)  
- 1 tsp **turmeric powder**  
- 1 tsp **black pepper** (enhances turmeric absorption)  
- 1 tbsp **lime juice**  
- Sea salt, to taste

---

#### **Instructions**  

1. **Sauté the Aromatics:**  
   - Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.  
   - Add diced onion, garlic, and ginger. Sauté for 3–5 minutes until fragrant and translucent.

2. **Build the Base:**

   - Stir in the turmeric and black pepper.

3. **Cook the Vegetables:**  
   - Add the cauliflower, carrots, and broccoli sprouts (or chopped broccoli).

5. **Poach the Salmon:**
