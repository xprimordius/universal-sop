To “become a sap” means to become overly sentimental, emotionally naïve, or easily manipulated—especially in a way that makes you vulnerable or taken advantage of.

**Examples:**
- Falling for someone’s sob story and giving them money even though it’s a scam.
- Letting someone repeatedly use you because you care too much.
- Getting overly emotional or soft in situations that might call for more restraint.

**Core traits of a "sap":**
- Too trusting
- Emotionally over-invested
- Easy to guilt or manipulate

It’s often used pejoratively: “Don’t be a sap” = don’t get played.
