# B2B ACH Transfer Fee

---

## 👤 You

How much is a b2b ACH transfer fee for $4000

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

ACH transfer fees for B2B transactions typically range from $0.20 to $1.50 per transaction, depending on the bank or payment processor. For a $4,000 transfer, the fee would likely fall within this range, meaning it could be anywhere between $0.20 and $1.50. Some banks might charge a flat fee, while others might offer lower fees for higher volumes or specific types of accounts.

To get the exact fee, you would need to check with the specific bank or payment processor handling the transaction.

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

A $50 fee for a Chase-to-Chase ACH transfer seems unusually high, especially for a standard ACH transfer, which typically has much lower fees. Here are a few possibilities:

1. **Wire Transfer Fee**: Chase might be processing this as a wire transfer instead of an ACH transfer. Wire transfers are faster but come with higher fees, often around $25 to $50 for domestic transfers.

2. **Expedited Processing**: If you requested expedited processing or same-day ACH, this might incur higher fees than a standard ACH transfer.

3. **Business Account Specific Fees**: Certain business accounts might have higher fees for specific services. It’s possible that this fee is tied to the type of account or service level you have.

4. **Miscommunication or Error**: There could be a miscommunication or an error in how the transfer is being categorized or processed.

I recommend contacting Chase customer service to clarify why the fee is so high and to ensure that it’s being processed as a standard ACH transfer, which should incur a much lower fee.
