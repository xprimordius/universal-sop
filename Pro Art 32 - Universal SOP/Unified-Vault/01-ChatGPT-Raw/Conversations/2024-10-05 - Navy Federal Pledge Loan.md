# Navy Federal Pledge Loan

---

## 👤 You

are you supposed to pledge loan from checkings or savings for navy federal

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

For a **Navy Federal Pledge Loan**, it is typically secured by funds in your **savings** account, not your checking account. The funds in your savings account are "pledged" as collateral to secure the loan, which means they are frozen and unavailable until the loan is paid off.

If you want to use funds from your checking account, you'd first need to transfer those funds into your savings account before applying for the pledge loan. Always check with Navy Federal to confirm their specific requirements at the time of your application.
