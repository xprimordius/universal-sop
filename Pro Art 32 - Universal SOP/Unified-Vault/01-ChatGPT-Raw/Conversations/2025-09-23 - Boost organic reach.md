---

## 1. Audit Basics
- **Profile Setup:** You already have a clean bio, link, highlights, and verified badge — ✅
- **Posting Consistency:** IG’s algorithm deprioritizes inactive accounts. Minimum **3–5 posts/week + daily story**.
- **Engagement Speed:** First 60 minutes matter most. If no one interacts, IG buries it.

---

## 2. Story Boosting
- **Interactive Stickers:** Polls, questions, sliders → force engagement.
- **Frequency:** 5–10 micro-stories per day (not 1–2 long ones). Each new story bumps you to the front of followers’ bars.
- **DM Hooks:** End stories with calls like “DM me 🔥 if you feel this” → pushes DMs = huge reach boost.

---

## 3. Feed Post Optimization
- **Captions:** Open with curiosity hooks (short, polarizing, or funny first line).
- **Carousels > Single Pics:** IG pushes swipes — they double engagement.
- **B-roll & Reels:** Mix short video with your photos. Reels currently get **3–5x reach vs pics**.
- **Save & Share Incentives:** Drop tips, insights, or memes people *want* to save/share.

---

## 4. Algorithm Signals
- **Engage 15 min before/after posting:** Comment/like on niche accounts so IG tags you as active in community.
- **Hashtags:** Use **5–10 niche-specific**, not 30 generic ones. (e.g., #BigSurViews vs #travel).
- **Music:** IG boosts posts using trending audio (like you did with “Ghost” 🎵).

---

## 5. Growth Hacks
- **Collabs:** Tag locations, brands, or friends → leverages their network.
- **Pinned Reels:** Make 3 “Who I Am / What I Do” reels, pin them to the top.
- **Cross-Pollinate:** Push IG content to TikTok, YouTube Shorts → bring traffic back.
- **Lives:** Even 5–10 min lives spike reach; IG promotes them at the top.

---

## 6. Reset Suppression (if shadowbanned)
- Don’t delete content. Just:  
  - Go 3–5 days light posting.  
  - Come back with **stories + reels flood**.  
  - Engage like crazy.  
  - Within 2 weeks, reach usually rebounds.
