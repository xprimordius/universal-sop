# Credit Inquiry Denial Reasons

---

## 👤 You

why am i getting denied from one main for number of inquiries, when i only have 2 inquires on my account in the past 24 months, and those 2 inquires were 5-6 months ago?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Being denied for a loan due to the number of inquiries, despite only having two inquiries in the past 24 months, could be due to several reasons:

1. **Mistaken Identity or Reporting Errors:** There could be a mistake in the credit report where more inquiries are being reported than what you've actually made. This could happen due to incorrect reporting by creditors or a mix-up with someone else’s credit file.

2. **Soft vs. Hard Inquiries:** Make sure you are distinguishing between soft inquiries (which do not affect your credit score) and hard inquiries (which do). OneMain might be counting inquiries differently or might have specific policies about soft inquiries.

3. **Credit Score Drop:** Even with just two inquiries, if your credit score dropped significantly due to other factors, OneMain might consider you a higher risk.

4. **Multiple Applications:** If you applied for similar types of credit or loans recently, OneMain could be considering that behavior as high-risk, even if those applications didn't result in approved credit.

5. **Specific Lending Criteria:** OneMain might have internal policies that are stricter about inquiries, especially if they see patterns that could indicate financial distress, even if they are legitimate inquiries.

6. **Inquiries on Other Reports:** Some lenders pull multiple credit reports (e.g., from Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion). If one report shows more inquiries than the others, that could be the reason for denial.

### Suggested Actions:

1. **Check Your Credit Reports:** Get a free copy of your credit report from all three major credit bureaus and check for any errors or discrepancies.

2. **Contact OneMain:** Reach out to them directly to ask for a detailed explanation of the denial. They might provide specific insights that are not apparent from your own credit report.

3. **Dispute Errors:** If you find any incorrect inquiries or other errors on your credit report, dispute them with the credit bureaus.

4. **Consider a Reapplication Strategy:** If everything checks out and the denial seems to be a policy issue, you might want to wait a few months before reapplying or look for another lender with different criteria.

If this continues to be an issue, further investigation into your credit report and possibly consulting a credit specialist might be necessary.

## 👤 You

can a bank legally deny you based on the number of soft inquiries? i though the disclaimer for soft inquiries that it does NOT affect your credit??

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Legally, soft inquiries do not affect your credit score, and they are not supposed to be used by lenders as a basis for denying credit. Soft inquiries occur when you check your own credit, when a lender checks your credit for pre-approval offers, or when a company you already have a relationship with checks your credit for account reviews. These inquiries are not visible to other lenders when they check your credit, and they do not impact your credit score.

However, **a bank may use soft inquiries to assess your credit behavior** internally, even though they should not directly impact your creditworthiness or be the sole reason for denial. If a bank is explicitly using the number of soft inquiries to deny you credit, this could be a misunderstanding or a miscommunication, as soft inquiries should not be treated the same as hard inquiries.

### What You Can Do:
1. **Clarify with the Bank:** Contact the bank and request clarification on why your application was denied. Ask specifically if they considered soft inquiries in their decision-making process.

2. **Request a Re-evaluation:** If they confirm that soft inquiries influenced their decision, you can request a re-evaluation of your application, explaining that soft inquiries should not impact your creditworthiness.

3. **Document the Interaction:** If the bank insists that soft inquiries played a role, document this interaction. If you believe the denial was unjust, you can file a complaint with the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) or consult with a consumer rights attorney.

### Legal Perspective:
While banks have discretion in their lending decisions, they must follow certain regulations, and denying credit based solely on soft inquiries could be considered unfair if it contradicts established credit reporting guidelines. You have the right to challenge such decisions and seek a fair evaluation.
