**Parchment Paper vs. Wax Paper – Key Differences:**

| Feature           | **Parchment Paper**                                | **Wax Paper**                                  |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| **Coating**       | Silicone                                            | Wax (usually paraffin)                         |
| **Heat Resistance** | Heat-resistant (up to ~420°F/215°C)               | **Not** heat-resistant (wax melts, can ignite) |
| **Best Uses**     | Baking, roasting, steaming, non-stick surface      | Cold food prep, wrapping, rolling out dough    |
| **Oven Safe?**    | ✅ Yes                                              | ❌ No                                           |
| **Moisture Barrier** | Moderate                                         | Excellent                                      |
| **Stick Resistance** | Excellent                                        | Moderate                                       |

**TL;DR:**  
Use **parchment paper** for **baking/cooking** with heat. Use **wax paper** for **cold food prep or storage**.
