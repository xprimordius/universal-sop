**MidJourney Zoom Levels Explained (1.5 vs 2 vs 2.5) — Visual Framing Comparison for Discord Banners**

### 🧭 What “Zoom” Does:
It **expands the visible canvas** around your original image while preserving its center. The higher the zoom value, the more it “pulls back” — revealing more background while shrinking the original focal subject.

---

### 🔍 Zoom Levels Side-by-Side:

| Zoom Level | Description | Visual Effect |
|------------|-------------|----------------|
| `--zoom 1.5` | Slight pull-back | Subject remains prominent, background slightly expanded. Great for **tight framing** or minor extension. |
| `--zoom 2` | Moderate zoom-out | Subject shrinks a bit, more environment revealed. Ideal for **Discord banners** or widescreen scenes. |
| `--zoom 2.5` | Dramatic pull-back | Subject becomes smaller in frame, much more space and scenery visible. Best for **cinematic wide banners**, breathing room, or text overlay space. |

---
