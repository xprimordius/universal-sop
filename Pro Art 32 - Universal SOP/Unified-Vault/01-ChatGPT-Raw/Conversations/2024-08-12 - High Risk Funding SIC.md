# High Risk Funding SIC

---

## 👤 You

813110 high or low risk sic code for funding

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

The SIC code 813110 corresponds to "Religious Organizations." Generally, this SIC code is considered high risk for funding. Lenders and financial institutions often categorize religious and other non-profit organizations as higher risk due to factors such as inconsistent revenue streams, reliance on donations, and the potential for less predictable financial performance compared to for-profit businesses.

If you are looking to secure funding, it might be beneficial to provide detailed financials, demonstrate stable revenue streams, and possibly explore lenders that specialize in or are more favorable toward non-profit organizations.

3. **IRS and Other Government Databases**: If the SIC code is linked to your business with the IRS or another government database, you might need to file a form or update your business classification through their online system.

4. **Lending Platforms**: If you need to change your SIC code for a specific lender or financial institution, you should contact them directly. They may have an internal process for updating your business classification based on new information you provide.

**Important Note**: Changing your SIC code can have implications for your business's classification, tax status, and how lenders perceive your risk. Ensure that the new SIC code accurately reflects your business activities. Misrepresentation, whether intentional or not, could lead to issues with lenders or regulators.
