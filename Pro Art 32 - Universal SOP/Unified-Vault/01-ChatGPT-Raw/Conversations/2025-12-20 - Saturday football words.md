## 🏛️ **entity["organization","Federal Trade Commission","us consumer protection agency"] (FTC)**

**Primary role:** Federal consumer privacy & data security enforcer

**Authority**
- FTC Act §5 — **Unfair or Deceptive Acts or Practices**
- Enforces **most private-sector privacy** where no sector law displaces it

**Can enforce**
- Deceptive privacy notices
- Unreasonable data security
- Broken promises about data use/sharing
- Children’s privacy (**COPPA**)

**Cannot**
- Enforce HIPAA/GLBA directly (other agencies do)
- Bring criminal cases
- Usually **no private right of action**

**Remedies**
- Injunctions
- Consent decrees
- Civil penalties (where statute allows)
- Ongoing audits / monitoring

---

## ⚖️ **entity["organization","State Attorneys General","us state chief legal officers"] (State AGs)**

**Primary role:** State-level privacy enforcement

**Authority**
- State consumer protection laws
- State privacy statutes (e.g., **CPRA, VCDPA, CPA**)
- Can enforce **federal laws** when authorized (e.g., COPPA, sometimes HIPAA coordination)

**Can enforce**
- State privacy rights violations
- Data breaches affecting residents
- Some federal privacy laws (statute-specific)

**Special powers**
- Civil penalties **per violation**
- Injunctions
- Often act **after notice & cure periods** (varies by state)

---
