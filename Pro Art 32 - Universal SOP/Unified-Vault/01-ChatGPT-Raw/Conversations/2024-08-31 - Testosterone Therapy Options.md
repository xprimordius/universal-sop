## 🤖 ChatGPT

Testosterone Replacement Therapy (TRT) is not typically the first-line treatment for Erectile Dysfunction (ED), but it can be prescribed in cases where ED is linked to low testosterone levels, a condition known as hypogonadism. 

ED can have various causes, including psychological factors, cardiovascular issues, and hormonal imbalances. When low testosterone is identified as a contributing factor to ED, TRT may be considered as part of the treatment plan. However, it's essential to undergo a thorough medical evaluation to determine if low testosterone is the root cause of ED before starting TRT.

In many cases, other treatments like phosphodiesterase inhibitors (e.g., Viagra, Cialis) are commonly prescribed for ED unless low testosterone is specifically identified as a contributing factor.
