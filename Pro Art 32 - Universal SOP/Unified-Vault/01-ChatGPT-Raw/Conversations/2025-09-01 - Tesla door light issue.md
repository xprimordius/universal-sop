## 🤖 ChatGPT

Got it. A Tesla **door pocket light staying on** usually points to one of three issues:

### 1. **Door Sensor / Latch Not Registering**
- Tesla lights stay on if the system thinks the door isn’t fully closed.
- Check if the **door is flush** when shut; push firmly to confirm it’s latching.
- Inspect for debris in the latch or a misalignment.

### 2. **Faulty Door Compartment Light**
- The light module itself may be defective and stuck on.
- Try swapping the driver’s side light with the passenger’s side light.  
  – If the problem moves, it’s the **light module**.  
  – If it stays, it’s **wiring or sensor related**.

### 3. **Software / Body Control Module Glitch**
- Sometimes the car misreads signals.
- Do a **soft reboot**: hold both scroll wheels on the steering yoke/wheel until the screen resets.
- If it persists, try a **full power cycle** (Controls > Safety > Power Off, wait 5 mins, then press brake).
