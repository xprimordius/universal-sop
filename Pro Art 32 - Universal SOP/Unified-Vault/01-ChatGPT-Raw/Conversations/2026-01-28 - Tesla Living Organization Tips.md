## 1) Rear Footwell = Vertical Storage Core (Highest ROI)

**What works best**
- **Stackable soft-sided cubes** (not hard bins)
- **Vertical orientation** > horizontal sprawl
- Target size: ~12–14” W × 10–12” D × 10–14” H

**Why**
- Soft walls conform to Tesla floor contours
- Stack 2–3 high without rattling
- Easy pull-out access from rear doors

**Use-case split**
- Cube 1: Clothing (rolled)
- Cube 2: Food / supplements
- Cube 3: Tech / cables / tools

- Emergency gear
