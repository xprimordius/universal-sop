### 🍣 Sushi Rice Recipe (Perfectly Seasoned)

#### 🔹 Ingredients (for ~2 cups dry rice):
- **2 cups** Japanese short-grain white rice (e.g., *Koshihikari*)
- **2 cups** water (for cooking)
- **2 tbsp** rice vinegar
- **1.5 tbsp** sugar
- **1 tsp** salt

---

### 🔧 Instructions:

1. **Rinse the Rice**  
   Rinse 2 cups rice under cold water until water runs clear. This removes excess starch.

2. **Cook the Rice**  
   - Use a rice cooker or stovetop with **1:1 ratio** (2 cups rice : 2 cups water).
   - Let it steam/rest for 10 min after cooking.

3. **Make Sushi Vinegar** (While rice cooks)  
   - In a small pot, gently heat (don’t boil):  
     - 2 tbsp rice vinegar  
     - 1.5 tbsp sugar  
     - 1 tsp salt  
   - Stir until dissolved. Cool slightly.

4. **Season the Rice**  
   - Spread cooked rice in a wide bowl (ideally a wooden *hangiri*).
   - Gently fold in the vinegar mixture using a rice paddle or spatula.
   - Fan the rice to cool quickly — gives it shine and the right texture.

---

### 🍥 Pro Tips:
- Don’t over-mix or mash the rice.
- Use while slightly warm for best rolling or shaping.
