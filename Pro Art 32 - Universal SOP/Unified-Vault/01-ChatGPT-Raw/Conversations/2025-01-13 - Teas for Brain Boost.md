## 🤖 ChatGPT

Here are some teas known for boosting **cognition** and **mental performance**:

### 1. **Green Tea**  
   - **Key Compounds**: L-Theanine + Caffeine  
   - **Benefits**: Enhances focus, reduces mental fatigue, and improves memory. L-Theanine promotes calm alertness, reducing the jitteriness from caffeine.

### 2. **Matcha**  
   - **Key Compounds**: Higher L-Theanine + Caffeine + EGCG  
   - **Benefits**: Sustained energy, sharper focus, improved reaction time, and reduced stress.

### 3. **Ginkgo Biloba Tea**  
   - **Key Compounds**: Flavonoids + Terpenoids  
   - **Benefits**: Increases blood flow to the brain, enhances memory, and supports long-term cognitive health.

### 4. **Lion’s Mane Mushroom Tea**  
   - **Key Compounds**: Hericenones + Erinacines  
   - **Benefits**: Stimulates nerve growth factor (NGF), supporting neurogenesis and improving mental clarity.

### 5. **Ginseng Tea**  
   - **Key Compounds**: Ginsenosides  
   - **Benefits**: Boosts energy, reduces brain fog, and enhances mental performance under stress.

### 6. **Peppermint Tea**  
   - **Key Compounds**: Menthol  
   - **Benefits**: Improves alertness, focus, and memory recall. Also reduces stress and tension.

### 7. **Gotu Kola Tea**  
   - **Key Compounds**: Triterpenoids  
   - **Benefits**: Enhances cognitive function, improves mood, and reduces anxiety.

### 8. **Rosemary Tea**  
   - **Key Compounds**: Rosmarinic Acid + Carnosic Acid  
   - **Benefits**: Improves memory and concentration by increasing cerebral blood flow.

### 9. **Black Tea**  
   - **Key Compounds**: Caffeine + Theaflavins  
   - **Benefits**: Increases mental alertness and supports sustained focus without overstimulation.

### 10. **Tulsi (Holy Basil) Tea**  
   - **Key Compounds**: Eugenol + Rosmarinic Acid  
   - **Benefits**: Reduces stress, sharpens memory, and enhances mood.

For best results, pair these teas with proper hydration, balanced nutrition, and sufficient rest.
