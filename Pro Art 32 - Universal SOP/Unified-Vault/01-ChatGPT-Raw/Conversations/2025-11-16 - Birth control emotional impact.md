# **HOW A COPPER IUD WORKS**
### **Biology in one sentence**  
Copper creates an environment where sperm **can’t survive, can’t move properly, and can’t fertilize an egg.**

### **Mechanism (simple + accurate)**  
1. **Copper releases ions** → toxic to sperm.  
2. **Sperm mobility drops** → they can’t reach or fertilize an egg.  
3. **Inflammatory immune response inside uterus** (non-hormonal) → makes fertilization nearly impossible.  
4. **If sperm and egg ever meet, implantation is extremely unlikely.**

### **No hormones.**  
Your natural ovulation, emotions, mood, libido, and hormonal cycle stay intact.

---

# **HOW A COPPER IUD IS INSERTED**
Insertion is quick — usually **2–5 minutes** — done by a trained clinician.

### **Step-by-step**
1. **Positioning**
   - You lie on the exam table like a pelvic exam.
   - Speculum goes in to open the vaginal canal.

2. **Cervix preparation**
   - Clinician cleans the cervix with an antiseptic.  
   - Sometimes a local numbing spray or injection may be used (optional/varies).

3. **Measuring the uterus**
   - A small, thin instrument called a *sound* measures the depth of your uterus to place the IUD in the correct spot.

4. **Insertion tube**
   - The IUD (T-shaped) is folded inside a thin plastic tube (applicator).  
   - Tube goes through cervix → into uterus.

5. **Release**
   - Clinician pulls back the plunger → the IUD opens into its T-shape.  
   - The IUD stays; the tube comes out.

6. **Trim the strings**
   - Two thin strings come through the cervix into the vagina.  
   - They’re trimmed short so you can’t feel them but can check placement if needed.

### **Total time:**
**2–5 minutes.**
