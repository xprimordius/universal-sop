## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Likely causes (most common → less common):**
- **Ingrown hair / infected hair follicle (folliculitis):** black dot = trapped hair or oxidized plug.
- **Small boil (furuncle):** deeper infection; tender, swollen, warm.
- **Blackhead that got infected.**

**What to do now (safe, simple):**
- **Do NOT squeeze or pick.**
- **Warm compress** 10–15 min, **3–4×/day**.
- Wash gently; keep clean and dry.
- If you shave there, **stop shaving** until healed.
- Optional: thin layer of **benzoyl peroxide 2.5–5%** once daily (avoid eyes/hairline runoff).

**Get medical help urgently if ANY of these appear:**
- Rapid growth, severe pain, spreading redness
- **Fever**
- Red streaks, significant swelling of face/eye
- **Black center enlarges**, skin turns purple/black, or drainage with foul odor
- No improvement in **48–72 hours**

**Why caution matters:**  
Infections near the face/hairline can spread; early care prevents complications.
