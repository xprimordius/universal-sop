### **Steak Internal Temperature Guide (°F):**

| **Doneness**     | **Internal Temp (Remove at)** | **Final Temp (After Resting)** | **Color & Texture**                      |
|------------------|-------------------------------|----------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Rare**         | 120–125°F                     | 125–130°F                        | Cool red center, very soft               |
| **Medium Rare**  | 125–130°F                     | 130–135°F                        | Warm red center, tender                  |
| **Medium**       | 135–140°F                     | 140–145°F                        | Warm pink center, slightly firm          |
| **Medium Well**  | 145–150°F                     | 150–155°F                        | Slightly pink center, firm               |
| **Well Done**    | 155–160°F+                    | 160°F+                           | Little to no pink, very firm             |

> ✅ **Tip:** Always let steak rest **5–10 min** after cooking to allow juices to redistribute and final temp to rise ~5°F. Use a meat thermometer for precision.
