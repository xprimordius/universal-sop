Yes, you can submerge most Apple Watches in water, but it depends on the model:

- **Apple Watch Series 2 and newer (including SE, Ultra, Ultra 2):**  
  Water resistant up to **50 meters** (Series 2–8, SE) or **100 meters** (Ultra models).  
  Safe for swimming in pools, oceans, and shallow water.  
  **Not** recommended for high-velocity water (jet skiing) or deep diving unless it's an Ultra.

- **Apple Watch Series 0 and Series 1:**  
  **Only splash resistant.**  
  **Not** safe for submersion.

**Important:**  
Water resistance is **not permanent** — it can wear down over time.
