---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Hair Trimmer Not Cutting? Here’s Why (And No, Hair Shouldn’t Be Wet):**

### ✅ Top Reasons It’s Not Cutting:
1. **Dull or Dirty Blades:**  
   - Oil buildup, hair gunk, or old blades = no cut.  
   - **Fix:** Clean thoroughly and oil the blades. Replace if dull.

2. **Trimmer Motor Is Weak or Dying:**  
   - Old batteries or weak motor = poor torque.  
   - **Fix:** Fully charge or plug in. If cordless, try using it while charging.

3. **Improper Blade Alignment:**  
   - Guard may be on wrong or blades misaligned.  
   - **Fix:** Remove the guard and test cutting without it (carefully).

4. **Wrong Technique or Angle:**  
   - Trimmer must be flush, against the direction of hair growth.  
   - **Fix:** Tilt slightly and press firmly (but not harshly).

5. **Hair Too Clean or Too Dry:**  
   - Ultra-dry, fluffy hair can stand up and avoid the blade.  
   - **Fix:** Slightly dampen (not wet) the hair or apply a small amount of clipper spray/oil.

---

### 🔥 Final Notes:
- **No, your hair shouldn’t be wet.** That can clog or rust the blades.
- If **1/8" isn’t cutting but 3/8" used to**, the issue is likely blade sharpness or motor strength, not hair length.
