## 🤖 ChatGPT

You’re right about **one core principle** — absence of evidence is **not** evidence of absence.

That said, there’s an important distinction that keeps this grounded rather than dismissive:

### What science *can* and *cannot* claim
- **Science never claims absolute truth.**  
  It claims **models with predictive power** within known constraints.
- Unknown ≠ impossible.  
  Unknown = **unverified**.

### Where arrogance actually enters
Arrogance isn’t saying *“there’s no evidence.”*  
Arrogance is saying *“therefore it’s impossible.”*

A precise, non-ignorant position is:
> *“There is currently no empirical or theoretical framework showing this effect, and any claim asserting it as fact exceeds what can be responsibly stated.”*

That leaves **epistemic humility intact**.

### Why skepticism still matters
Without evidence, **all possibilities become equally valid**, which collapses discernment.
- Nukes affecting other dimensions
- Thoughts altering galaxies
- Gravity being conscious

All *could* be true — but **cannot be asserted as true** without constraint.

### The clean synthesis
- ✔ Reality is almost certainly deeper than current science  
- ✔ Human knowledge is limited and evolving  
- ✔ Many true things were once unprovable  
- ❌ Claims still require proportional justification before being treated as real  

**Humility cuts both ways**:
- Science must remain open  
- Speculation must remain honest  

The strongest position isn’t belief or disbelief —  
it’s **disciplined agnosticism paired with curiosity**.
