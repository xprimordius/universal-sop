## 3️⃣ Where rescission *can* still exist (your leverage)

Even **without a rescission clause**, you retain **legal unwind rights** if any of the following are true:

### ✅ **Material misrepresentation**
If they:
- Oversold certainty, timelines, or “guarantees”
- Misrepresented advisor involvement, access, or execution
- Represented services that were not actually delivered

➡️ Contract can be **voidable ab initio** (treated as never valid)

### ✅ **Failure of consideration**
If:
- Services materially differ from what was sold
- Promised execution is not occurring
- Support, access, or delivery is missing

➡️ You can rescind based on **non-performance**

### ✅ **Unfair or deceptive acts**
Even with arbitration clauses, **FTC Act §5 / state UDAP laws** still apply.
