**Yes. Melatonin affects hormones.**

Here’s the TLDR breakdown:

---

### 🧠 **What Is Melatonin?**  
A hormone made by your pineal gland that regulates **sleep-wake cycles (circadian rhythm).**

---

### 🧬 **How Melatonin Affects Hormones:**

1. **Suppresses Reproductive Hormones (in high doses):**  
   - **↓ Estrogen & Testosterone** in some cases (especially with long-term or high-dose use).
   - May delay puberty in adolescents.

2. **Interacts with Cortisol:**  
   - **Opposes cortisol.** When melatonin is high, cortisol is low (and vice versa).
   - Affects stress-response timing.

3. **Influences Growth Hormone (GH):**  
   - Sleep (especially deep sleep) boosts GH, and melatonin promotes sleep → **indirectly increases GH.**

4. **Affects Insulin and Glucose Regulation:**  
   - Can **impair insulin sensitivity** if taken during the day or outside normal circadian rhythm.

---

### ⚠️ **Risks with Misuse or Overuse:**

- **Hormonal imbalance** (especially if disrupting natural rhythms)
- **Reduced fertility signals** with chronic high doses
- May interfere with **thyroid** and **pituitary** hormone signaling

---

### ✅ Best Practice:
- Use **low doses (0.3–1mg)** only when needed  
- Take **at night, 30–90 minutes before sleep**  
- Avoid long-term daily use unless under medical guidance
