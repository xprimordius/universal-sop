Here’s a simple, optimized recipe to make burgers similar to MrBeast Burger — juicy, flavorful, and fast-food style:

### **MrBeast-Style Burger Seasoning (per 1 lb of ground beef):**
- **Salt:** 1.5 tsp  
- **Black Pepper (fine ground):** 1 tsp  
- **Garlic Powder:** 1/2 tsp  
- **Onion Powder:** 1/2 tsp  
- **Paprika (optional for color):** 1/4 tsp  
- **MSG (Accent seasoning):** 1/4 tsp *(for fast food umami kick)*

**Tip:** Mix seasoning gently into the surface only or season just the outside — don’t overwork the beef.

---

### **Burger Cooking Steps:**
1. **Use 80/20 ground beef** (high-fat for juiciness).
2. **Form 2 thin patties per burger** (~2.5 oz each).
3. **Smash-style cooking:**
   - Heat cast iron or griddle to high.
   - Place ball of beef, smash flat with spatula or press.
   - Sear 1–2 mins until crust forms, flip once.
   - Add **American cheese** immediately after flipping.

4. **Optional “MrBeast Sauce” Copycat:**
   - 1/4 cup mayo  
   - 1 tbsp ketchup  
   - 1 tsp mustard  
   - 1 tsp relish  
   - 1/2 tsp smoked paprika  
   - Mix and spread on both buns.

5. **Toast buns** in butter until golden.
6. **Assemble:** Bottom bun → sauce → pickles → burger → cheese → second patty → onions (grilled or raw) → top bun.

### **Best-Tasting Ground Beef (Pan-Cooked Crumble Style)**

#### **Ingredients (1 lb ground beef, 80/20):**
- 1.25 tsp fine **kosher salt** (or 1 tsp table salt)  
- 1 tsp **garlic powder**  
- 1 tsp **onion powder**  
- 1/2 tsp **black pepper**  
- 1/2 tsp **smoked paprika** *(adds depth)*  
- 1/4 tsp **MSG** *(optional but elite for umami)*  
- 1–2 tsp **Worcestershire sauce**  
- Optional: 1 tbsp **beef tallow** or **butter** (for richness)

---

### **Step-by-Step Execution:**

1. **Preheat a stainless steel or cast iron pan** on medium-high heat until hot.

2. **Add tallow or butter** if desired. Drop the beef in — **don’t stir yet**. Let it sear untouched for 2–3 minutes to build a crust.

3. Begin breaking it up **into medium crumbles**. **Don’t over-stir** — let it brown in stages.

4. Once halfway browned, **add all dry seasonings** and **Worcestershire**.

5. Cook another 3–5 mins, stirring occasionally until deeply browned and crispy bits form.

6. Optional finish: drizzle a **splash of soy sauce or beef bone broth** and cook down for 1 min to glaze.

---

### **Bonus: Flavor Boost Variations**
- Add **diced onions** or **minced mushrooms** early for added savoriness.
- Finish with **chopped parsley** + squeeze of lemon for brightness.
- For a spicy twist: toss in **chipotle powder** or **red chili flakes**.
