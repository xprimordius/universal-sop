### **Ketamine**
- **Nature:** Dissociative anesthetic; short-acting (45–90 mins).  
- **Mechanism:** Acts on NMDA receptors → induces ego-dissolution, out-of-body states, rapid mood shifts.  
- **Use:** Therapeutic (depression, PTSD), recreational, ceremonial (modern).  
- **Tone of Experience:** Spacious, often detached, sometimes “clinical” but can open mystical doorways with right set/setting.  
- **Integration Need:** High—because of fragmentation risk and the rapid “in/out” feel.  

---

### **Ayahuasca Dieta**
- **Nature:** Traditional Amazonian preparatory regimen for ayahuasca or plant dieta.  
- **Purpose:** Purification of body, mind, spirit → opens sensitivity to plant teachers.  
- **Typical Restrictions:**  
  - No salt, sugar, oil, alcohol, dairy, pork, red meat, spicy/fermented foods.  
  - Abstain from sex, drugs, strong stimulants, overstimulation.  
- **Focus:** Simplicity, discipline, intention → creates a clean vessel for deep medicine work.  
- **Duration:** Weeks before and after ceremony (varies by lineage).  

---

### **Interaction Between Ketamine & Aya Dieta**
- **Philosophical Clash:**  
  - Ketamine = synthetic, dissociative, “Western medicine.”  
  - Ayahuasca dieta = sacred, plant-based, natural, “Amazonian tradition.”  
- **Potential Conflict:**  
  - Dieta is about sensitivity, humility, and spiritual discipline.  
  - Ketamine can disrupt the dieta energetics if used during dieta window.  
- **Safety Concern:** Not chemically toxic if separated by days/weeks, but culturally/spiritually discouraged to mix.  
- **Best Practice:**  
  - Keep dieta pure if working with ayahuasca.  
  - If doing ketamine therapy, complete integration before starting dieta.
