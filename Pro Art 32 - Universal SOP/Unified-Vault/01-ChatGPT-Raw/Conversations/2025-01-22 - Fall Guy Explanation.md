---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

A "fall guy" refers to someone who is made to take the blame for someone else's actions or wrongdoing, often as a scapegoat. 

In the context of "Gary is the fall guy for John," it means that Gary is being set up or expected to bear the responsibility, guilt, or consequences for something John did. This often happens to protect the actual wrongdoer (John) from facing accountability.
