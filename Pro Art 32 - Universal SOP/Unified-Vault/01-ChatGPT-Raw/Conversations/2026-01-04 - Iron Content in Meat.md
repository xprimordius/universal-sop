### Iron in Meat (per ~3 oz / 85 g cooked)

**Heme iron** (from meat) is highly absorbable (~15–35%).

| Meat | Iron (mg) |
|---|---:|
| **Beef liver** | **5–6 mg** |
| **Clams** | **20–24 mg** *(exceptionally high)* |
| **Beef (steak/ground)** | 2.5–3.0 mg |
| **Lamb** | ~2.0 mg |
| **Pork** | ~1.0 mg |
| **Turkey (dark meat)** | ~2.0 mg |
| **Chicken (dark meat)** | ~1.1 mg |
| **Chicken breast** | ~0.9 mg |
| **Fish (salmon, tuna)** | 0.5–1.0 mg |

**Daily needs:**  
- Men: **8 mg/day**  
- Women (19–50): **18 mg/day**

**Key takeaway:** Red meat and organ meats deliver the most *usable* iron. Pair with vitamin C for max absorption.
