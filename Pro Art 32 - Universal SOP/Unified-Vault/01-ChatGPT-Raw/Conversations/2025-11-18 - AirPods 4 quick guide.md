## 🤖 ChatGPT

**AirPods 4 — First-Timer Quick Start**

**1. Open & Pair**  
- Open the case near your iPhone → tap **Connect** → done.  
- They auto-pair to all devices on your Apple ID.

**2. Wear & Fit**  
- Put them in your ears → they auto-detect and turn on.  
- Squeeze the stem to play/pause.

**3. Controls**  
- **Squeeze once:** play/pause or answer call  
- **Squeeze twice:** skip  
- **Squeeze three times:** go back  
- **Press +/– on stem:** volume up/down  
- **Hold:** switch Noise Canceling / Transparency

**4. Noise Control**  
- Swipe down on iPhone → Control Center → press and hold the volume bar → choose **Noise Canceling**, **Transparency**, or **Off**.

**5. Charging**  
- Drop AirPods into the case → they charge automatically.  
- Charge the case with USB-C or MagSafe.  
- Case shows status: **green = full**, **amber = charging**.

**6. Find My**  
- iPhone → **Find My** → Devices → **AirPods**.  
- You can play a sound, see exact location, and locate each earbud separately.

**7. Switching Devices**  
- They auto-switch between iPhone, iPad, Mac.  
- If they don’t: Control Center → Audio → select **AirPods**.

**8. Calls**  
- Answer by squeezing the stem.  
- Switch modes (NC or Transparency) mid-call by holding the stem.

**9. Cleaning**  
- Use a dry cotton swab for the speaker mesh.  
- Never use water or alcohol directly.

**10. Reset (if needed)**  
- Hold case button 15 sec until it flashes orange → white → then reconnect.
