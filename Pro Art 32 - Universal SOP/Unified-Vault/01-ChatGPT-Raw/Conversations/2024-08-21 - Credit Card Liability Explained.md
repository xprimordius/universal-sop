---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

In your Credit Karma TransUnion report:

1. **Flexible Spending Credit Card**: This refers to a type of credit card that doesn't have a fixed credit limit. Instead, your spending limit can be flexible based on various factors, such as your spending patterns, payment history, and creditworthiness. This is different from a traditional credit card, which typically has a set credit limit.

2. **Responsibility - Contractually Liable**: This indicates that you are legally responsible for the debt on this account. The term "contractually liable" is used when you have signed a contract or agreement to pay back the debt. 

   - **Individual Responsibility**: If the report mentioned "individual" instead, it would mean that you alone are responsible for the debt, and it is under your name only.
   
   - **Contractually Liable**: This suggests that while you are the one responsible for the debt, it could be part of a joint account, authorized user account, or some other arrangement where liability is established through a contract.

In summary, the distinction here lies in the type of credit card you have (flexible spending vs. traditional credit card) and the nature of your liability (contractually liable vs. individually responsible).
