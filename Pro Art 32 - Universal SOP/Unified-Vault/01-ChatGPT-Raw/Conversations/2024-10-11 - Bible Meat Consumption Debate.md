# Bible Meat Consumption Debate

---

## 👤 You

What does the Bible say about eating meat if thou shall not kill tldr

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

The Bible allows eating meat despite the commandment "Thou shall not kill," which refers specifically to murder. Genesis 9:3 permits humans to eat animals: "Every moving thing that lives shall be food for you." Additionally, various Old Testament laws regulate the types of animals that may be eaten (e.g., Leviticus 11), and Jesus Himself ate fish and lamb, indicating that meat consumption is acceptable in Christian belief. The prohibition on killing in the Ten Commandments focuses on unlawful taking of human life.

## 👤 You

Define murder

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Murder is the unlawful, intentional killing of one human being by another, typically with malice aforethought. It involves deliberate intent to cause death or serious harm without legal justification or excuse. This distinguishes murder from other forms of killing, such as manslaughter, which may occur without intent, or lawful acts like self-defense or state-sanctioned executions.

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

From a biblical and legal perspective, the concept of murder specifically refers to the unlawful killing of human beings. While the killing of animals is often regulated or subject to moral and ethical considerations, it is not classified as "murder" in most religious or legal frameworks.

In the Bible, humans are given dominion over animals (Genesis 1:26-30) and permitted to use them for food (Genesis 9:3). However, some individuals and religious traditions, such as certain interpretations of Buddhism, Hinduism, or ethical veganism, might consider the killing of animals for food to be morally wrong, but it is not typically referred to as "murder." 

Murder, as defined legally and biblically, remains specific to humans.
