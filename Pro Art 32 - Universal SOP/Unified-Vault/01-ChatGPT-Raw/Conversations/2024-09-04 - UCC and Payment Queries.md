## 🤖 ChatGPT

The **Uniform Commercial Code (UCC)** itself does not directly address late payment reporting in terms of credit reporting. The UCC primarily governs commercial transactions, including sales, secured transactions, and negotiable instruments, but it does not specifically regulate consumer credit reporting or address the legality of late payment reporting to credit bureaus.

Late payment reporting typically falls under **federal laws** like the **Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA)** and related state laws, rather than the UCC. The FCRA regulates how consumer information is reported to credit bureaus and provides consumers with the right to dispute inaccurate or incomplete information, including improperly reported late payments.

If you’re looking to remove late payments from a credit report, the legal arguments are generally rooted in proving that the information is incorrect or that the creditor violated reporting requirements under laws like the FCRA—not the UCC.
