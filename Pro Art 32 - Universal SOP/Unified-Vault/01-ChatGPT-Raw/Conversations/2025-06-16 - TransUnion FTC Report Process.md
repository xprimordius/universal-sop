can I just change the data inside? without submitting a new one

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

No — **you cannot just change the data inside an FTC Identity Theft Report** after it’s submitted. Here's why:

- **It’s a sworn legal affidavit.** Once submitted, it’s locked to preserve its integrity as a legal document.  
- **Any material changes** require submitting a **new report** through [IdentityTheft.gov](https://www.identitytheft.gov/).  
- You can, however, **submit a supplemental police report** or **attach additional documentation** in your dispute with TransUnion (or the other CRAs), clarifying or expanding on the original.

**Bottom line:** You must submit a new FTC report if you need to materially change the data. Don't alter the original—it can invalidate your claim.
