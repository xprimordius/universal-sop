To issue a **Cease and Desist** related to aged domains and emails, particularly for the purpose of domain and email ownership disputes or improper usage of web assets, you can follow this framework:

### Cease and Desist Letter Structure

1. **Introduction:**
   - **Sender’s Details:** Your name, business address, contact information.
   - **Date:** The date of issuing the letter.
   - **Recipient’s Details:** The recipient's name, company, and address.

2. **Subject:**
   - **Subject Line:** “Cease and Desist Notice Regarding Misuse of [Domain Name / Emails]”

3. **Statement of Violation:**
   - Describe the issue clearly, such as misuse or unauthorized usage of aged domains and email addresses. Include:
     - Domain names or email addresses involved.
     - Evidence that proves ownership or original claim to these domains/emails.
     - Mention any contracts, agreements, or laws that have been breached.

4. **Demand to Cease:**
   - Clearly state that the recipient must stop using the domain/emails immediately.
   - Set a deadline by which you expect the misuse to stop (usually 10-15 business days).
   
5. **Legal Consequences:**
   - Highlight the legal consequences of non-compliance, such as penalties under the **Anti-Cybersquatting Consumer Protection Act (ACPA)** or **trademark law** if applicable.
   - Mention that further legal action may be taken, including litigation and possible damages.

6. **Probation Option (Optional):**
   - If applicable, offer a period of probation, during which the recipient must comply with specific conditions, such as verifying the deletion of emails or removal of domain links.

7. **Conclusion:**
   - End with a firm but professional request for confirmation of compliance within a set period.
   - Include a statement that you are prepared to take further legal action if this request is not met.

8. **Signature:**
   - Sign the letter and include any supporting documentation or evidence.
