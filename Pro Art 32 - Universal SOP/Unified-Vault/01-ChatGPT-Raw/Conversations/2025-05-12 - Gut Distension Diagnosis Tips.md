## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes — here’s a master-level diagnostic checklist to identify root causes of a **distended, disproportionate gut** despite being in shape elsewhere and dieting:

---

### 🔍 **Primary Causes to Investigate:**

#### 1. **Visceral Fat Accumulation (Hidden Belly Fat)**
- **Description:** Fat around internal organs (not subcutaneous).
- **Red Flag:** Even if abs are visible, gut may still protrude.
- **Common Causes:** Cortisol/stress, alcohol, insulin resistance.
- **Test:** Waist circumference >40” (men), fasting insulin or HOMA-IR.

#### 2. **Bloating from Gut Dysbiosis or SIBO**
- **Description:** Excess gas from bacterial overgrowth.
- **Red Flag:** Belly inflates after meals, worsens as day progresses.
- **Common Symptoms:** Gas, belching, fatigue, irregular stools.
- **Tests:** GI-MAP, SIBO breath test, stool test, food sensitivity panels.

#### 3. **Weak Core/Abdominal Wall (Diastasis Recti or Lordosis)**
- **Description:** Ab muscles don't support organs = belly hangs.
- **Red Flag:** Belly sticks out when standing, flattens when lying down.
- **Check:** Try hollow body holds; if unable, it's likely core weakness.
- **Fix:** Transverse abdominis training (dead bugs, planks, vacuums).

#### 4. **Digestive Backup / Constipation**
- **Description:** Waste accumulation in intestines.
- **Red Flag:** 1 or fewer bowel movements/day, straining, dry stools.
- **Check:** Magnesium citrate, psyllium husk, digestive enzymes.
- **Fix:** Ensure hydration, fiber balance, bile flow (ox bile or bitters).

#### 5. **Food Intolerances / Inflammation**
- **Description:** Swelling from undiagnosed sensitivities.
- **Red Flag:** Bloat/belly growth right after eating.
- **Common Triggers:** Gluten, dairy, legumes, seed oils.
- **Fix:** Elimination diet (Whole30 or FODMAP) + reintroduction tracking.

---

### 🧪 **Labs to Run for Precision:**
- **Fasting insulin + glucose → HOMA-IR** (insulin resistance)
- **GI-MAP or Viome** (gut microbiome)
- **SIBO lactulose breath test**
- **Stool panel (Genova, GI Effects)**
- **Cortisol (DUTCH test)**
