## 🤖 ChatGPT

**CAS Latency (CL)** affects memory access time (in nanoseconds), but **real-world input latency** differences are minimal—especially in gaming. Here's the technical and practical breakdown:

---

### 🧠 **CAS Latency Formula (True Latency in ns):**

\[
\text{Latency (ns)} = \frac{\text{CL}}{\text{Memory Speed (MT/s)}} \times 2000
\]

### 🔍 **Examples (DDR5-6000):**
| CAS Latency | True Latency (ns) |
|-------------|-------------------|
| CL26        | 8.67 ns           |
| CL28        | 9.33 ns           |
| CL30        | 10.00 ns          |

---

### 🎮 **Input Lag Impact (Apex Legends / FPS Games)**

- **Difference:** ~1.3 ns total between CL26 and CL30  
- **Input latency effect:** **Negligible to unnoticeable** (sub-0.1ms range)
- **Bigger impact comes from:**  
  - CPU performance (especially 1% lows)  
  - GPU frame rendering time  
  - Monitor refresh rate & latency  
  - System polling rates (mouse, USB, etc.)

---

### ✅ **TL;DR:**
- CL26 is better **on paper**, but gains are **<1.5 ns total**, not impactful in real-world gaming.
- If you can get CL26 with **high speed** (e.g., 6000 MT/s or more) at a good price, go for it.
- Prioritize **stable high frequency** + **tight timings**, not just CL.
