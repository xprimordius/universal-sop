Do owls come out during the day

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Most owls are **nocturnal** (active at night), but some **do come out during the day** — these are called **diurnal** or **crepuscular** species. Examples:

- **Diurnal:**  
  - **Northern Hawk Owl**  
  - **Burrowing Owl**  
  - **Snowy Owl** (especially in Arctic summers)

- **Crepuscular (active at dawn/dusk):**  
  - **Short-eared Owl**  
  - **Barred Owl** (can also be active during the day if hunting)

So yes — while most owls are night hunters, some **naturally appear in daylight.**
