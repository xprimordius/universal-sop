## 👤 You

Why running in zone 2 for 3 hours so exhausting

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Running in **Zone 2 for 3 hours** can feel exhausting due to:

### 1. **Duration, not intensity**
- Even though Zone 2 is low-intensity, **3 hours is a long time**—fatigue accumulates aerobically, mentally, and mechanically.

### 2. **Glycogen depletion**
- After ~90–120 min, your body starts running low on glycogen, relying more on fat—slower energy = fatigue.

### 3. **Muscle fatigue & microtrauma**
- Repetitive, low-level stress over hours = soreness and fatigue even at low intensity.

### 4. **Mental fatigue**
- Zone 2 feels “easy,” but holding steady pace for hours **requires focus**, especially without stimulation or variation.

### 5. **Cardiovascular load**
- Even Zone 2 requires sustained cardiac output. For some, especially if not fully adapted, it’s still a significant load.

### 6. **Not yet fat-adapted**
- If your body isn’t trained to **efficiently use fat for fuel**, Zone 2 work is metabolically harder.

---

**TLDR:** It’s not the intensity—it’s the **duration + energy system fatigue + muscle wear + mental grind**. Gets easier with consistent aerobic base building.
