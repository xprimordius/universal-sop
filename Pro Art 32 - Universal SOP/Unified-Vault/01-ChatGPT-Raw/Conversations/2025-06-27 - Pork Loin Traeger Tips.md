## 👤 You

Pork loin traeger dry seasoning with oil or without

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Dry seasoning pork loin on Traeger: with or without oil?**

► **Use a light coating of oil.**

**Why:**
- **Purpose:** Helps dry rub stick evenly.
- **Bonus:** Promotes better surface browning and crust during smoking.

**Recommended approach:**
1. Pat pork loin dry.
2. Rub with **thin layer of olive or avocado oil**.
3. Apply dry seasoning generously.

Then smoke at 225–250°F until internal temp hits ~145°F, rest 10 min, slice.

## 👤 You

Can you pre season and store in fridge

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Yes – Pre-seasoning and refrigerating pork loin is ideal.**

### ✅ Why It Works:
- **Enhances flavor:** Salt and spices penetrate deeper over time.
- **Better bark:** Seasoning dries the surface, leading to a crispier crust.

### 🔒 Storage Method:
1. Pat dry.
2. Light oil coat.
3. Generous dry rub.
4. Wrap **loosely in foil or uncovered on a rack** over tray (preferred: air-dry effect).
5. Store **12–24 hrs in fridge**.

### ⚠️ Notes:
- Don't exceed 2 days (risk of texture change).
- Pull from fridge 30–60 min before smoking.

Let me know if you want a brine-to-dry rub combo or flavor profile (e.g., sweet heat, herby, smoky).

## 👤 You

Should you trim fat off

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Trim most fat, leave a thin layer.**

### 🔪 Why Trim:
- **Excess fat = rubbery, blocks smoke + seasoning.**
- **Too much = greasy texture, flare-ups.**

### ✅ Optimal Trim:
- Remove **thick fat cap** down to about **1/8 inch**.
- Leave a **thin, even layer** to keep moisture and flavor during smoking.

### 🧠 Pro Tip:
- If fat is silver skin (shiny/tough): **trim it all** – it doesn’t render.
- Clean, even surface = better seasoning penetration and crust.
