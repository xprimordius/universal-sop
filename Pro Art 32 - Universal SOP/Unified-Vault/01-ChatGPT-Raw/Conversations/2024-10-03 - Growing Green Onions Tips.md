# Growing Green Onions Tips

---

## 👤 You

When growing cut green onions in a cup and water, am I supposed to replace the water every day? 

And am I supposed to but it in lots of sunlight?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Yes, you should replace the water every day to prevent bacteria buildup and ensure the green onions stay fresh while growing. As for sunlight, placing the green onions in a location with lots of indirect sunlight is ideal. Direct sunlight can be too intense, but bright, indirect light will help them grow effectively. A sunny windowsill is often a good spot.
