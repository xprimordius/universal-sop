# Account Deletion Request Letter

---

## 👤 You

Please help me write a letter to each of the bureaus that request that my accounts be deleted. Including the free account; as I do  NOT agree to their terms and agreement.

Include in the letter to only contact me by mail, as well as provide documentation affirming that I do not have an account. and have opted out of any type of agreements / disclosures / sharing of my private information, as well as expressing that I do NOT consent to the sharing or selling of my personal information

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Certainly! Below is a template you can use to write a letter to each credit bureau requesting the deletion of your accounts and expressing your concerns regarding their terms and agreements.

---

**[Your Name]**  
[Your Address]  
[City, State, ZIP Code]  
[Email Address]  
[Phone Number]  
[Date]

**[Credit Bureau Name]**  
[Credit Bureau Address]  
[City, State, ZIP Code]

Subject: Request for Account Deletion and Removal from All Agreements

Dear [Credit Bureau Name],

I am writing to formally request the deletion of any and all accounts associated with my name and personal information in your system. I do not agree to your terms and agreements, and I wish to terminate any existing relationship with your bureau immediately.

Please ensure the following actions are taken promptly:

1. **Deletion of All Accounts:**  
   Remove all accounts, both paid and free, associated with my name, Social Security number, or any other identifying information.

2. **Opt-Out of All Agreements and Disclosures:**  
   I do not consent to any agreements, disclosures, or sharing of my personal information with any third parties. Please confirm that I have been opted out of all such arrangements.

3. **No Consent for Information Sharing or Selling:**  
   I do not consent to the sharing, selling, or any form of distribution of my personal information. This includes any information that may have been shared prior to this request.

4. **Written Confirmation:**  
   Please provide me with written documentation affirming that my accounts have been deleted and that I am no longer bound by any agreements with your bureau. This confirmation should also verify that my personal information has been removed from your system and will not be shared or sold in the future.

5. **Contact by Mail Only:**  
   I request that all future communications regarding this matter be conducted through postal mail. Do not contact me via phone, email, or any other electronic means.

Please address this matter with the urgency it requires. I expect to receive confirmation of the deletion of my accounts and the termination of all agreements within 30 days of this notice.

Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter.

Sincerely,

[Your Name]

---

You can customize the letter with your personal details and send it to each of the three major credit bureaus (Experian, Equifax, and TransUnion). Be sure to keep a copy for your records and send the letters via certified mail to ensure they are received and processed.
