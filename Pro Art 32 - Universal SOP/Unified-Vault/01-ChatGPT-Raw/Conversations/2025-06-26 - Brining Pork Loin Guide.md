**Quick Brine for 8 lb Pork Loin**  
✅ Juicy. Flavorful. Tender. Done right.

---

### 🧂 **Basic Brine Formula**
- **Water:** 1 gallon (16 cups)
- **Salt:** 1 cup kosher salt (or ¾ cup table salt)
- **Sugar (optional but ideal):** ½ cup (brown or white)
- **Aromatics (optional but great):**
  - 4 crushed garlic cloves  
  - 1 tbsp peppercorns  
  - 2 bay leaves  
  - Fresh herbs: rosemary, thyme, or sage  
  - Citrus peel (lemon/orange)

---

### 🕒 **Steps**
1. **Heat 2 cups of water** to dissolve salt + sugar. Add aromatics.
2. **Add remaining cold water** (to bring total to 1 gallon). Ensure brine is fully cooled.
3. **Place pork loin in a large container** or 2-gallon Ziploc bag. Pour in brine.
4. **Refrigerate 12–24 hours.**
5. **Remove, rinse**, and **pat dry** before cooking.
6. Let sit at room temp 30–60 min before roasting or smoking.

---

### 📝 Notes:
- Do **not** over-brine: max 24 hours or it’ll get too salty/mushy.
- You can **halve the brine** if you vacuum seal it or use less water to cover.

## 🤖 ChatGPT

**Yes — refrigeration is essential** during brining. Here's why:

---

### ❄️ **Why You MUST Refrigerate Brine:**
- **Prevents bacterial growth** (especially in pork).
- Room temp = danger zone (40°F–140°F) = risk of foodborne illness.
- Even salty brine doesn't kill all bacteria.

---

### ✅ Safe Options:
1. **Refrigerator** – Best and safest.
2. **Cooler + Ice Packs** – If fridge space is tight, submerge pork in brine inside a cooler with enough ice to keep below 40°F. Use a thermometer to monitor.
3. **Vacuum Seal + Brine + Chill** – Vacuum brining in the fridge with half the brine volume can save space.

---

⚠️ **Bottom line:** Always keep pork cold while brining. Never leave out.
