# Apex Legends Server Selection

---

## 👤 You

Why do apex legends servers always auto select St. Louis servers when I live in Minnesota and Iowa is closer?

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT

Apex Legends servers are likely auto-selecting St. Louis over Iowa for several reasons:

1. **Server Performance and Load Balancing:** The game may prioritize the St. Louis server due to lower current server load, higher performance, or more optimal latency for matchmaking.
   
2. **Routing and ISP Paths:** The path your internet service provider (ISP) takes to the servers may have a more efficient or direct route to St. Louis rather than Iowa, even if Iowa is geographically closer.

3. **Server Availability:** There might be times when Iowa servers are under maintenance or experiencing high traffic, leading to St. Louis being selected as the next best option.

You can manually select the Iowa servers in the data center options to override the auto-selection if you prefer that location.
