## 🎯 What Is This System?

We use AI (specifically, Claude) to help us do high-quality work fast. But AI, by default, is like a brilliant intern who skips steps, forgets things, and overpromises.

We built a **rulebook** (called the "Universal Output SOP") that forces the AI to work like a senior professional — carefully, accurately, with receipts.

Think of it like this:

| 🎯 | 🏷️ Without The Rulebook | 🏷️ With The Rulebook |
|:---:|------------------------|----------------------|
| 📝 | AI gives vague answers | AI gives specific, traceable answers |
| ⚡ | AI rushes and makes mistakes | AI checks its work before showing you |
| 📉 | AI forgets what you said 10 minutes ago | AI saves everything to files so nothing is lost |
| 😒 | You have to repeat yourself | AI flags when it misses something and fixes it permanently |
| 🤷 | "I did the thing" (maybe) | "I did the thing. Here's the proof. Here's what's next." |

## 🧠 Why This Matters For Your Job

You will be using AI to:
- Build documents, reports, plans

If the AI produces sloppy work, YOU look sloppy. If the AI produces world-class work, YOU look world-class.

**The rulebook is how we ensure the AI produces world-class work, every time.**

|:-:|:---:|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | 💬 | **How to ask AI for what you want** | Clear, specific prompts. No ambiguity. Like writing a good email to a contractor. |

Training takes **about 3 hours** spread over your first week. You won't do it all at once.

## 📋 The 6-Step Dance (Every AI Interaction)

This is what every AI interaction looks like:

```
1. 📋 You ask for something
2. ✅ AI repeats it back to make sure it understood
3. 📊 AI estimates how long / how much it'll cost (in "tokens," which are AI's unit of work)
4. ⚙️ AI does the work
5. 🪞 AI checks its own work before showing you
6. 📤 AI shows you the result + a summary + what's next
```

You don't have to memorize this. You just need to know: **when AI skips step 2 or step 5, stop and tell it to slow down.**

## 🛡️ The 10 Rules AI Must Follow (Priority 10)

These are the 10 non-negotiable rules the AI follows in every output. If you see one missing, say so.

| # | 🎯 | 🏷️ Rule | 📝 In Plain English |
|:-:|:---:|---------|--------------------|
| 1 | 📊 | Step tracking | Every step has a cost, time, and description — like a receipt |
| 2 | 📊 | Live counter | After every section, a running total of how much "fuel" has been used |
| 3 | 📋 | Header | Every response starts with: what this is, which rulebook version, how long |
| 4 | ✅ | Relay-back | AI repeats your request in its own words before doing it |
| 5 | 💾 | Backup first | Before changing any file, AI makes a copy of the original |
| 6 | 🎯 | Check the original request | Before sending, AI compares its output to what you asked for |
| 7 | 🧮 | Debt counter | AI shows you: things promised vs things done (must be equal) |
| 8 | 📋 | Rule report | AI lists all 16 rules it was supposed to follow and marks each ✅ or ❌ |
| 9 | 🔮 | Foresight | Every response ends with "here's what could go wrong next + solutions" |
| 10 | 📝 | Spell out acronyms | Every abbreviation has its full meaning the first time it appears |

## 🚨 When Something Goes Wrong

The AI has a rule called "Honest Failure Report." If the AI misses something, it must:

1. Admit it missed the thing
2. Explain WHY it missed (the actual reason, not an excuse)
3. Fix it permanently (not just for this one time)

**Your job:** When AI messes up, point at the specific thing. Don't give vague feedback. "You skipped the backup step" is useful. "This is bad" is not.

---

## 💾 The Save Routine (1 Minute)

At the end of every work session with AI:

1. Tell AI: "End session. Save state."
2. AI creates a "continuation prompt" — copy it
3. Start next session by pasting the continuation prompt first
4. AI picks up exactly where you left off

**Why this matters:** AI starts every new conversation with no memory. This routine is how we make it remember.

## ❓ Common Questions

| 🎯 | ❓ Question | 📝 Answer |
|:---:|----------|-----------|
| 🤔 | "Is this overkill?" | For simple stuff, yes. The AI has a "QUICK tier" for simple tasks — skips most steps. For important work, this overkill is what prevents disasters. |
| 🧠 | "Do I need to memorize all this?" | No. Read once. Reference as needed. The AI enforces the rules — you just need to recognize when they're missing. |
| 📚 | "What if the AI doesn't follow the rules?" | Point at what's missing. The AI fixes it AND prevents it from happening again. The system gets better every time. |
| 💰 | "What does 'tokens' mean?" | It's AI's unit of fuel. Every word AI reads or writes burns tokens. Sessions have a fuel limit (200,000 tokens). The AI tracks this so you don't run out mid-task. |
| 🔐 | "Is my work saved?" | Yes — to specific files in a `cache/` folder. If your laptop dies, the files survive. If you want to transfer work to a teammate, send them the files. |
